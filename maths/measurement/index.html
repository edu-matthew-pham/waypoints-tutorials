Area, volume, circles and Pythagoras

ReferenceRecapPractice

Why the area formulas work (cut and move, halve and double), then area problems; volume as layers of the base; the parts of a circle, π, and the circumference; then Pythagoras' theorem for right-angled triangles; for Year 8, composite and irregular shapes, capacity and prism problems, the area of a circle, and time zones.

  • For Years 7–10
  • Lessons 50
  • Do first Angles and shapes and Number Foundations and Algebra Foundations
