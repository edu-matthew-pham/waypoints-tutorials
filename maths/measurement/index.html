Volume

6Parts of a circleNot started

10Finding a sideNot started

Pythagoras finds any side. For the hypotenuse, add the squares: c² = a² + b². For a shorter side, subtract from the hypotenuse's square: b² = c² − a². Then take the square root, and check the hypotenuse is still the longest.
