Year 9 Number

Number families

Try firstIs √20 rational or irrational? Why?

Check your answer

Irrational: 20 isn't a perfect square.

The natural numbers are inside the integers, the integers are inside the rational numbers, and the rational and irrational numbers together are the real numbers.

Each family answers a question the last couldn't: $2 - 5$ needs negatives, $1 \div 2$ needs fractions, and $x^2 = 2$ needs irrationals.

Year 9 Algebra

Negative powers

Try firstWork out 4⁻² as a fraction.

Check your answer

1/16.

A negative power is a reciprocal: $a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n}$. For example, $2^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{2^3} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

It's not a negative number. $10^{-6}$ is a millionth, a small positive number.

All the index laws

Try firstSimplify 4⁻² × 4⁵ ÷ 4⁻¹.

Check your answer

4⁴ = 256.

The index laws work with any indices. Multiplying adds the indices, dividing subtracts them, and a power of a power multiplies them.

Subtracting a negative adds: $5^6 \div 5^{-2} = 5^{6 - (-2)} = 5^8$.

Two brackets

Try firstExpand (x − 4)(x + 6).

Check your answer

x² + 2x − 24.

Two brackets multiply like the sides of a rectangle. $(x + 4)(x + 3)$ is $x + 4$ across and $x + 3$ down, so the area has four pieces: $x \times x = x^2$, $x \times 3 = 3x$, $4 \times x = 4x$ and $4 \times 3 = 12$. Add them and collect the two strips: $x^2 + 7x + 12$.

Signs come with the numbers: $(x + 4)(x - 3) = x^2 - 3x + 4x - 12 = x^2 + x - 12$.

The pattern

Try firstExpand (x + 4)(x − 9).

Check your answer

x² − 5x − 36.

The two strips join into one term, and the corner is the product. So $(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$.

For example, $(x + 5)(x + 2) = x^2 + 7x + 10$, and $(x + 6)(x - 6) = x^2 - 36$ because the strips cancel.

Factorising quadratics

Try firstFactorise x² + 2x − 15.

Check your answer

(x + 5)(x − 3).

To factorise $x^2 + bx + c$, find two numbers that multiply to $c$ and add to $b$. Then $x^2 + bx + c = (x + p)(x + q)$.

For example, $x^2 + 7x + 12$: 3 and 4 multiply to 12 and add to 7, so it's $(x + 3)(x + 4)$. Check by expanding.

Recognising a quadratic

Check your answer

Year 9 Measurement

Scientific notation

Try firstWrite 0.0072 in scientific notation.

Check your answer

7.2 × 10⁻³.

Scientific notation is a number from 1 up to (but not including) 10, times a power of 10.

Big numbers have a positive power: $45,000 = 4.5 \times 10^4$. Small numbers have a negative power: $0.0072 = 7.2 \times 10^{-3}$. The power says how many places the decimal point moves.

Calculating in scientific notation

Try firstWork out (4 × 10⁶) × (5 × 10⁻²) in scientific notation.

Check your answer

2 × 10⁵.

To multiply, multiply the number parts and add the powers. To divide, divide the number parts and subtract the powers. Then check the number part is from 1 to 10: if it's 10 or more, move a factor of 10 into the power.
