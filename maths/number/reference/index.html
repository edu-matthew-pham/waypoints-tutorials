integer

A whole number, zero, or a negative whole number.

A number below zero, to the left of 000 on a number line: five below zero.

less than, greater than

The wide end of the sign faces the greater number. Further left on the line is less.

The size of a number without its sign. Among negatives, the one further from zero is the lesser: −10<−1-10 < -1−10<−1.

Each place value column is a power of 10. The exponent counts the zeros, and the ones column is 100=110^0 = 1100=1.

The number of digits after the decimal point. To 2 decimal places means to the nearest hundredth.

ratio

Compares quantities in a stated order. The first thing named is the first number.

The marginal rate is the rate on the last dollar; the effective rate is total tax ÷ income.

A rate compares quantities with different units (km per h, $ per kg); a ratio compares the same kind. 1 km/h = 1000 m ÷ 3600 s: divide by 3.6 for m/s.

Every model assumes something (steady speed, no fees, no starting charge). When it disagrees with real life, name the assumption and refine the model.

Start at the first number. Adding a positive moves right; adding a negative moves left.

Subtracting moves the opposite way to adding: subtracting a positive moves left, past zero if it has to.

Adding −3-3−3 would move left 3, so subtracting it moves right 3. It gives the same as adding 333.

HCF: the primes both share. LCM: all the primes, shared ones once. Sharing into equal groups needs the HCF; things lining up again need the LCM.
