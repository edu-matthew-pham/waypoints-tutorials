Learning Waypoints helps teachers unpack curriculum into connected conceptual steps, then provides tools to plan, teach and check understanding. The waypoints are professional interpretations grounded in ACARA descriptors, not a prescribed ACARA teaching sequence.

[Learning Waypoints in 10 Minutes: presentation and facilitator guide](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/teach/teachers/getting-started/) · [Getting Started: self-paced introduction](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/teachers/getting-started/) · [Teachers hub](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/teachers/).

## Choose your starting point

| Offering | A small thing to try |
|---|---|
| [Curriculum: Unpack](https://acara.learningwaypoints.com/hass/year/7) | Inspect one conceptual progression and the prerequisites or hard part behind an idea in your unit. |
| [Planning and preparation: Prepare](https://prepare.learningwaypoints.com/) | Open one slide deck or printable resource, then choose a teaching strategy for that idea. |
| [Tutorials](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/geography/water-7/resource-types/) | Try one prediction, check, feedback explanation or scaffold alongside your existing teaching. |

These are connected but independently useful. You can benefit from any part without using all three. The talk spends three minutes on Tutorials to make the underlying analysis and planning tangible, not because Tutorials matters more.

## Try one thing and reflect

Choose a relevant progression, planning resource or strategy, or tutorial activity. Fit it into an existing unit, try it, then reflect:

1. Did the part you tried reveal something useful about the concept, its prerequisites or student understanding?
2. How helpful were the curriculum explanation, planning resources or strategies, or tutorial feedback and scaffolds that you used?
3. What would make you more likely to use Learning Waypoints again?

Feedback on any one offering is useful. Keep notes for a conversation with the pilot facilitator; the feedback collection route is still to be decided. No account, form or upload of student information is needed.
