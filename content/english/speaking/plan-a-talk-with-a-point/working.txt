# Nib's plan for a talk to Year 5 about the new library beanbags, one line each.

# region: nib
Opening: Ask who has ever read in a really uncomfortable chair.
Point 1: The new beanbags are for reading, not for sleeping.
Detail: There are twelve, and they sit in the quiet corner.
Point 2: You can book a beanbag for lunchtime.
Detail: The booking sheet is on the library door.
Close: Say it again: read, book, relax. Then ask them to try one this week.
Close: Finish by telling them the library now has a new printer for homework.
# endregion
