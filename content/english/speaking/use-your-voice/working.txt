# Nib's delivery notes for a talk about the wet steps, one line each.

# region: nib
Opening: warm tone, medium pace, a smile.
Point 1: steady pace, say 'the steps get wet when it rains'.
Warning: 'Do not run on the wet steps.' Say it fast and quietly so it takes less time.
Close: pause, then a slower, warm 'Please walk.'
# endregion
