# region: nib
Good morning, Year 5. Today I want to talk about our school bubblers.
Three of them are broken, and that is a problem.
The bubblers by the oval, which were fitted a long time ago before the new courts were built, are among the three that we cannot use, so students line up at the single working tap.
So here is my idea: fix them this term.
In short, three broken bubblers means a long queue for water.
