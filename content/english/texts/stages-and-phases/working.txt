A seed is a tiny plant with a store of food inside a tough coat.
The shoot pushes up through the soil towards the light.
When the seed is in damp, warm soil, it soaks up water and swells.
The coat splits, and a root grows down into the soil.
Once its first leaves open, the plant makes its own food from sunlight.
