### For independent use or a longer staff session

The three [Getting Started lessons](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/teachers/getting-started/) stand alone, about 15–20 minutes in total. Their individual guides below provide generated slides, Print worksheets and facilitator notes. Allow extra time if colleagues explore their own subjects. Completion is optional; do not collect their planning notes.

Use the same Geography example throughout. A conceptual check is one piece of evidence. Discussion, local knowledge, investigation, extended writing and assessment remain teacher decisions. All Humanities pathways are previews and have not been tried with classes.

### Invite teachers to try one useful part

Invite teachers to choose whichever part meets a real need: look at how ideas build in a topic, try a teaching resource or strategy, or use a tutorial check. Fit that into an existing unit and discuss the three reflection questions on the [pilot companion](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/humanities-pilot/). Ask what helped and what got in the way, whether teachers used one part or several. Teachers can keep their own notes for a conversation; no student information is needed.
