# Learning Waypoints in 10 Minutes

Unpack curriculum into connected conceptual steps, then use tools to **plan, teach and check understanding**.

- **Curriculum · Unpack:** conceptual progressions and prerequisites.
- **Planning and preparation · Prepare:** resources and strategies.
- **Tutorials:** checks, feedback and scaffolding in practice.

Connected, independently useful. Start wherever it helps you.
