Your primer has one idea, an opening question that brings out the misconception, and checks that test understanding. In this lesson you'll review it against the checklist every published lesson goes through, share it, and send it in.

## The idea

```step
stage: investigate
insight: "Each share option does one job; choosing is about who it's for."
```

Your work lives in Create, in this browser, until you share or download it. Create's **Share** row:

- **Copy student link:** the lesson as students see it, without the editor. For your class. Recorded videos aren't in the link.
- **Copy edit link (text only):** for a colleague to edit and re-record. Text and clip instructions only.
- **Download lesson (.zip)** or **Download course (.zip):** everything, videos included. Keep it as your backup; it opens offline, and imports back into Create.
- **Download videos:** the videos and captions as plain files, for your learning platform.
- **Request publishing:** downloads the lesson and opens the request form; attach the ZIP there. A person reviews every lesson before it goes on the site.

**The review checklist** (what a reviewer checks):

1. **One idea**, a sentence a student could say back.
2. **Students predict or try before being told.**
3. **A step brings the misconception into the open** and deals with it.
4. **The checks match the goals**, and can't be passed by guessing or recall alone.
5. **No copied material**: your own words and examples, or made-up sources. No pages, pictures or worksheets you don't own.
6. **Lesson format checks pass**, and someone has done it as a student.


### Your primer

Swap student links with a colleague. Do each other's primer as a student, then run the checklist on it and tell them what failed. If you're on your own, do it on yours, a day later if you can.

### Share it

Choose what fits:

- **Use it with your class:** **Copy student link**, and post it where your students find links. Use **Download videos** if it has any.
- **Send it in to be published:** **Request publishing**, then attach the ZIP in the form that opens. Say which year and subject it's for.
- **Keep it:** **Download lesson (.zip)**. Your browser's copy can be lost if site data is cleared.

### Become a reviewer

The site needs teachers who know their subject to review lessons before they're published: the same checklist, plus whether it's accurate and right for the year level. Many lessons start from a curriculum waypoint, so if a lesson's goals or misconception look wrong for your students, say so: that's how the curriculum data behind the site gets corrected.

If you'd review lessons in your subject, say so in the [request form](https://forms.gle/CoRAXJWHnVQ8mXpPA): your subject, the year levels you teach, and how to reach you.
