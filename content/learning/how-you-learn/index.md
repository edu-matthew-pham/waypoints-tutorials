title: How you learn best
summary: "An optional introduction to self-regulated learning for Years 6 to 8, as five short discussion workshops. Students look at what they believe about learning, learn to be their own learning coach whoever chose the task, think about why they do schoolwork (marks included), see why study that feels easy can fool them, and plan to use one strategy on a real task in another subject. The people in the lessons are made up; students apply each idea to themselves and share only what they're comfortable with. The strategy cards on the Reference page are for using afterwards, in any subject."
