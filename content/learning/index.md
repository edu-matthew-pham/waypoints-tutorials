    about: "Self-regulated learning means being your own learning coach: understanding and adjusting your own thinking, motivation, feelings and actions as you learn, by planning, checking how it's going, and looking back, whoever chose the task. It is a skill to keep practising, like spelling, rather than a topic to finish. This course is a short, optional, discussion-led introduction in five workshops: beliefs about learning, the learning coach, reasons and goals (marks included), why easy can fool you, and knowing a strategy versus using it. Discussion stays on made-up learners; students apply each idea to themselves privately. The last workshop asks students to plan one strategy for a real task from another subject. The **strategy cards** (on the course's Reference page) are the part to keep using: general strategies for before, during and after a task, plus subject cards for maths, science, English and Code. It loosely supports the Personal and Social Capability (self-management) and Critical and Creative Thinking general capabilities."
