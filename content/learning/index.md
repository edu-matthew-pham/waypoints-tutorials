    about: "Self-regulated learning means steering your own learning on a task: planning it, checking how it's going, and looking back. It is a skill to keep practising, like spelling, rather than a topic to finish. This course is a short, optional, discussion-led introduction: each workshop starts with a made-up task that has a trap where planning or checking pays off, the class compares approaches, and the idea is named afterwards. The last workshop asks students to try one strategy on a real task from another subject. The **strategy cards** (on the course's Reference page) are the part to keep using: general strategies for before, during and after a task, plus subject cards for maths, science, English and Code. It loosely supports the Personal and Social Capability (self-management) and Critical and Creative Thinking general capabilities."
