# The journey of a purchase, in order (Make). Plain text: students put the lines in order.

# region: order
You press Buy, and your browser encrypts the card number (HTTPS).
The encrypted data is split into numbered packets.
The packets cross the Wi-Fi to your home router.
They travel by cable and fibre to your internet provider.
Routers pass each packet on, by different routes.
The shop's computer confirms each packet; a lost one is sent again (TCP).
The packets are put back in order by number.
The shop decrypts the card number with its key.
# endregion

# region: sam
I type my card number and press Buy.
My laptop splits it into packets.
My home router encrypts the packets.
The packets travel across the internet to the shop.
# endregion
