    summary: Read an excavation as a sequence of layers. Use the position of a find and the evidence beside it to explain what may have changed, while keeping relative age separate from an exact date.
  - id: science
    title: DNA and dating answer different questions
    summary: Describe how ancient DNA can reveal biological relationships. Distinguish that question from dating organic material, and read a date range as an estimate rather than an exact year.
  - id: writing
    title: Reading ancient writing alongside other evidence
    summary: Identify inscriptions as ancient written sources and explain translation and context problems. Compare a written claim with physical evidence instead of ranking writing as automatically more reliable.
  - id: conservation
    title: Keeping the evidence for the next question
    summary: Explain why destroying archaeological evidence closes off future questions. Identify how governments and international bodies help conserve sites, and weigh a made-up development proposal against clear evidence criteria.
