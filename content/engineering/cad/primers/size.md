title: Designing to size in 5 minutes
---
## The idea in plain words

A design is a set of **dimensions**. A box has an outside size and a **wall** on every side, so the inside is the outside minus **two** walls. Something that goes inside needs a **gap** around it.

Parts that fit together need **clearance**: a hole slightly bigger than its peg. Drawn the same size, they won't fit once printed, because a 3D printer squashes plastic outwards: holes come out about 0.2 mm smaller and pegs a little bigger. About 0.2–0.4 mm of clearance gives a sliding fit; less is a tight press fit. Every printer differs, so print a **tolerance test card** once and record what fits.

## What students get wrong

- **One wall, not two.**
- **Same size means it fits.**

## The words

**dimension**, **wall thickness**, **clearance**, **tolerance**, **press fit**, **sliding fit**.
