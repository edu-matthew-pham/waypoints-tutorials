## The idea in plain words

A printer builds a part in **layers**. Each new layer needs something underneath it: an **overhang** more than about 45° from vertical needs **supports**, which waste plastic and leave rough surfaces. Layers bond less well than the plastic within a layer, so a part is **weakest across its layer lines**: turn it so the load runs along the layers.

The **slicer** turns the model into layers and settings. **Layer height** sets the finish and the time (halve it, double the time). **Infill** fills the inside; past about 20–30% it adds lots of time and plastic for little strength. **Walls** (perimeters) add more strength for less.

## What students get wrong

- **A part is equally strong every way.**
- **More infill is always better.**

## The words

**layer height**, **overhang**, **supports**, **infill**, **walls (perimeters)**, **slicer**.
