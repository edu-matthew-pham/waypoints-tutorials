# The charging routine, for the ordering task. Plain text, display only.

# region: charge
Check the pack: no swelling, dents, torn wrap or damaged wires.
Put the pack in a fire-safe LiPo bag on a non-flammable surface.
Plug both the main lead and the balance lead into the balance charger.
Set the charger to LiPo, the right cell count and a current no more than the pack's capacity (1 C).
Stay with it while it charges.
Unplug it when the charger says it is full, and let it cool before use.
# endregion
