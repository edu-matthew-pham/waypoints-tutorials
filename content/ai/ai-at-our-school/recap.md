## The course in four products

A school AI committee makes four things, and each one is tested against the first:

1. **A charter:** five to seven principles about what we want from AI, each with a way to check it's working.
2. **A recommendation:** for a tool someone proposes, adopt, trial, change something first, or don't use it, with conditions and what would change our minds.
3. **A response plan:** for AI that arrived without anyone choosing it, a response for each item, and a cycle for reviewing it all.
4. **A policy:** what students and staff do. A broad version 1 first, then a version 2 refined with evidence: specific enough to decide, with fast-changing detail in guidelines and tasks, and working through task design and honesty rather than trying to catch people.

The emphasis is the principles, not any one decision. Before you check your answers below, ask of one AI feature you met this week: which principle does it test?
