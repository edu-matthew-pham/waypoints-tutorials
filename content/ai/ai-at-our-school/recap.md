## The course in three products

A school AI committee makes three things, and each one is tested against the first:

1. **A charter:** five to seven principles about what we want from AI, each with a way to check it.
2. **A recommendation:** for a tool someone proposes, adopt, trial, change something first, or don't use it, with conditions and what would change our minds.
3. **A response plan:** for AI that arrived without anyone choosing it, a response for each item, and a cycle for reviewing it all.

The emphasis is the principles, not any one decision. Before you check your answers below, ask of one AI feature you met this week: which principle does it test?
