## The course in five questions

Every lesson asked the same questions about a different system:

1. **Which kind of AI is it?** Rules written by people, learns to predict or sort, makes new content, or takes actions.
2. **Who gains?**
3. **Who loses, or could be harmed?**
4. **Who decides?**
5. **What's the catch?**

Before you check your answers below, try them on one system you use every day.
