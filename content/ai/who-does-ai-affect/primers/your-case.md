title: Unit 5 — Your own case
---
## Purpose

The course challenge: students choose a case and judge it with the kind of AI, the lens, the course's questions and one rule with a check. Marks, if any, go to reasons, not verdicts.

## Running it

One or two lessons. Model the lens on the Refunds case together, then students choose. A gallery walk with sticky notes (a gain, a loss or a catch someone missed) works offline. Real cases from the news are fine once checked with the teacher; avoid individual tragedies and cases students are personally involved in.
