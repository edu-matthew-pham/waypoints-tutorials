## Purpose

Two forward-looking questions: what AI is doing to jobs, and how people make rules about it.

## The lessons

1. **Will AI take jobs?** A job is many tasks; exposed is not lost; so far far more augmenting than automating, and no widespread displacement in Australia (Jobs and Skills Australia, 2025). Typical: call-centre novices gained most. Wider: the contested junior-jobs evidence.
2. **Making rules:** students write a class rule about AI with the lens, then meet real rules (the national schools framework, reviewed yearly; the government's own AI policy; wider, the EU AI Act's high-risk category for hiring). A rule existing isn't the same as it working.

## Everyday picture

Calculators changed maths tasks without ending maths; school rules about calculators changed too, and were reviewed as they changed.

## Where the picture breaks

AI changes faster than calculators did, and the evidence on jobs is still arriving.

## Cautions

Some students' families work in jobs named as exposed. Keep it about tasks, not people.
