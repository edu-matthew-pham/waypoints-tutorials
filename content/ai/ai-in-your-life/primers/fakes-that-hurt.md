## Purpose

When AI fakes are about real people: a fake of a classmate, and a voice clone of a parent.

## The lessons

9. **When a fake is about someone you know** ⚑ A made-up, non-sexual case. The harm is about consent, not realism; bystanders don't share, support, tell and report (esafety.gov.au/report). **Flagged for wellbeing review.**
10. **Is that really Mum?** A few seconds of voice can be enough to clone it (Scamwatch); spot urgency and secrecy, and call back on a known number. Links to Cyber safety › Spot the phish.

## Cautions

Never ask students whether this has happened to them or their family. Have the wellbeing team, Kids Helpline (1800 55 1800) and the school's reporting process ready. Statistics about deepfake image-based abuse are for teachers only.
