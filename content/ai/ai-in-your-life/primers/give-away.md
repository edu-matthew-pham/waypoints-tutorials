## Purpose

What happens to what you type into AI apps, and how to find the real choice on a consent screen. Narrowed to AI and your data (decision, 18 Oct 2026): the digital footprint and the Australian Privacy Principles are taught in Digital systems › Cyber safety and privacy, and these lessons link there.

## The lessons

5. **Where does your chat go?** Follow one question through Club Helper, a made-up AI feature in the Coding Club app (`consent` board, trace mode): sent, stored, reviewed, trained on. Leave out what isn't needed; check the training switch.
6. **Read the screen:** a made-up consent screen (`consent` board, screen mode): find the line that matters, the choice behind the small link, what Agree switches on. Most people don't read (OAIC 2023: 21%).

## Everyday picture

Posting a letter: you know who will read it. Typing into an app is more like posting it to a company that may keep a copy, show it to staff and learn from it.

## Cautions

The screens are made up but modelled on common patterns, so they don't date and don't promote a product. Re-check the Children's Online Privacy Code after 10 Dec 2026.
