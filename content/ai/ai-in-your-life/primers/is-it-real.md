## Purpose

People spot AI-made media at about chance, so the course teaches checking where something came from, not hunting for glitches (decision, 18 Oct 2026).

## The lessons

7. **Can you spot a fake?** Predict your score, then the evidence: faces 48.2%; across 56 studies, not clearly above chance; training only to about 65%; glitch tips go stale.
8. **Where did it come from?** Trace a made-up viral photo back to its source; Content Credentials help but are optional and removable; provenance isn't proof of truth.

## Everyday picture

A rumour: you don't judge it by how convincing the teller sounds; you ask who said it first.

## Cautions

No real-versus-fake image quizzes: we don't host fakes, and quizzes teach glitch-spotting.
