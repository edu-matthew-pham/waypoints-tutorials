## Purpose

Name the kind of AI, then see how everyday apps are built around a goal: your time and attention.

## The lessons

1. **Which kind of AI is it?** A short recap of AI Foundations › What kind of AI is it?, with this course's cases.
2. **How does a feed choose?** Be the algorithm on a made-up feed (`feed` board, rank mode, or paper cards): what a feed counts decides what rises. With the app's goal, posts the user asked for less of reach the top.
3. **Designed to keep you:** switch features on a made-up app (`feed` board, design mode); most sticky features (autoplay, endless scroll, streaks) are design, not AI. Typical: the 'addiction' evidence is real but small and causal claims are argued. Wider: Australia's minimum age, the EU's design case.
4. **Is a chatbot a friend?** ⚑ Companion chatbots are designed to feel human and keep you talking; a friend is different. **Flagged for wellbeing review.**

## Everyday picture

A shop puts sweets at the checkout: the layout serves the shop's goal. Apps have layouts too.

## Where the picture breaks

Apps learn and change for each person, millions of times a day; a shop layout doesn't.

## Cautions

Keep it non-personal: Sam, Pip and Ava are made up. Don't ask students how long they use apps, which ones, or whether they use companions.
