## The course in one habit

Every lesson asked two questions about something in your apps:

1. **Which kind of AI is it?** Rules written by people, learns to predict or sort, makes new content, or takes actions.
2. **How will I check?** What a feed counts, which features keep me there, where my chat goes, what Agree switches on, where a picture came from, and who is really calling.

Before you check your answers below, choose one app you know and ask both questions about it.
