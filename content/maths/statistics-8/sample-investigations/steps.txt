# The stages of an investigation to put in order for "Investigating with samples". Plain text: students order the lines.

# region: plan
Write a question about a population: "How long do Year 8s at our school spend on homework?"
Plan a fair sample: 40 Year 8s chosen at random from the roll, with their permission.
Collect the data anonymously, in the same way for everyone.
Display the data and describe its shape, centre and spread.
Make an inference about the population, and say how sure you can be.
# endregion
