## The idea in plain words

**Area** counts unit squares: cm², m². A rectangle is rows of squares, so its area is length × width.

A **parallelogram** is a rectangle pushed over. Cut the triangle off one end along the **perpendicular height** and move it to the other end: it becomes a rectangle with the same base and height. So $A = bh$. Two copies of a **triangle** make a parallelogram with the same base and height, so a triangle is half: $A = \tfrac{1}{2}bh$. Sliding the top along a line parallel to the base changes the shape but not the area.

Real problems need three moves: **choose** the base and the height at a right angle to it (ignore slanted sides), **convert** every length to one unit, and **split** a shape into rectangles and triangles (or subtract a missing part).

## Why it matters

Paint, carpet, tiles, land and fabric are bought by area. The same "base times height" idea gives volume next, and the area of a circle and trapezium in Year 8.

## What students get wrong

- **Using the slanted side as the height.** Base 7, slanted side 5, height 4 worked out as 7 × 5. Find the ∟.
- **Forgetting the half for a triangle.** ½ × 8 × 5 given as 40. Two triangles make that parallelogram.
- **Mixing units.** 50 cm × 2 m = 100. Change to 0.5 m × 2 m = 1 m².
- **Writing cm for an area.** Area counts squares: cm².

## Year 8: composite and irregular shapes

A composite shape is cut into pieces you know (or seen as a rectangle with pieces taken away); each piece uses its own lengths, and a missing side is the difference of two others. An irregular shape is estimated on a grid: full squares, plus half the part squares, times one square's area; a finer grid is closer. A curved perimeter is estimated with straight pieces (always a little short). The common slips: giving a piece the whole shape's height, and counting part squares as whole.

## The words

**base**, **perpendicular height** (at a right angle to the base), **parallelogram**, **composite shape** (made of simpler shapes), **square units** (cm², m²; 1 m² = 10 000 cm²), **irregular shape**, **estimate**.
