Maya works out how many times more energy a magnitude 7 earthquake releases than a magnitude 4.
Each step up the scale multiplies the energy by about 32.
From 4 to 7 is 3 steps.
So the energy goes up by 3 × 32 = 96 times.
That is about 96 times as much energy.
