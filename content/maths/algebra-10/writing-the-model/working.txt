An account pays 5% a year. I start with $1,000. What is the model?
5% is 0.05, so each year the balance is multiplied by 0.05.
So A = 1000 x 0.05^n, where n is the number of years.
After 1 year that gives 1000 x 0.05 = 50.
Fifty dollars is less than I started with, but that can't be right. Maybe I rounded.
