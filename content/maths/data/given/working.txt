The question: what is the chance of a cloudy morning, given that it rained?
The days with both rain and a cloudy morning: 30.
"Given" means I look at the cloudy mornings, which is 50 days.
30 ÷ 50 = 0.6.
So the chance of a cloudy morning, given rain, is 0.6.
