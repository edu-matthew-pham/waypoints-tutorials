Your teacher discusses the ethics of data in class, so these questions check the basics: what makes a report unfair even when its numbers are right, and what you owe the people you collect data from. Every report and number here is made up for the task.

## Fair reporting

```step

insight: "Node 3: a report can be accurate and still be unfair, because of what it leaves out or how it frames the numbers."

  - check: {"type":"question","q":"A company reports: \"Our drink has no added sugar!\" Another company's report on its own drink says: \"Cuts sugar by 40%!\" Both figures are true.
```
