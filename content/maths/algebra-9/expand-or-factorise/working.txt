Factorise x² + x − 6.
Two numbers that multiply to −6: 2 and −3.
So x² + x − 6 = (x + 2)(x − 3).
Check: (x + 2)(x − 3) = x² − 3x + 2x − 6 = x² − x − 6.
That matches, so it's right.
