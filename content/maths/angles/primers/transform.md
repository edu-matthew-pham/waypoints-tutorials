title: Transformations in 5 minutes
---
## The idea in plain words

A **transformation** moves a shape on the coordinate plane. The original is the **object** and the result is the **image**; A becomes A′. The size and shape do not change.

- **Translation**: slide. Every point moves the same distance in the same direction. Right and up add; left and down take away: $(x, y) \to (x + a, y + b)$.
- **Reflection**: flip over a mirror line. In the x-axis, $(x, y) \to (x, -y)$; in the y-axis, $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$. A point and its image are the same distance from the mirror.
- **Rotation**: turn about a centre by an angle in a direction. About the origin, 90° anticlockwise is $(x, y) \to (-y, x)$ and a half turn is $(-x, -y)$.

## Why it matters

Moving shapes by coordinates is how computers draw games and animations, and it leads to symmetry, tessellations and (later) matrices.

## What students get wrong

- **Left or down added** to a coordinate.
- **Changing the wrong coordinate** in a reflection: in the x-axis, y changes sign.
- **Swapping the coordinates for a quarter turn** and forgetting the sign.
- **Forgetting the centre and direction** when describing a rotation.

## The words

**transformation**, **object**, **image**, **translation**, **reflection**, **rotation**, **centre of rotation**, **clockwise**, **anticlockwise**.
