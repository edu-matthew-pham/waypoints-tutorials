## The idea in plain words

**Congruent** shapes are the same shape and size: one fits exactly on the other. Triangles do not need all six parts checked. Each **congruence test** fixes the triangle: **SSS** (three sides), **SAS** (two sides and the angle between them), **ASA** or **AAS** (two angles and a matching side) and **RHS** (a right angle, the hypotenuse and another side). Two sides and an angle that is not between them (**SSA**) can make two different triangles, so it is not a test.

**Similar** shapes are the same shape, maybe a different size: every length is multiplied by the same **scale factor** k = copy ÷ original, and the angles stay the same. Triangles are similar by AA, by SSS (sides in one ratio) or by SAS (two sides in one ratio, the angle between them equal). For other shapes, check angles and side ratios: all squares are similar, but not all rectangles.

Congruent triangles prove properties of **quadrilaterals**. Split the shape with a diagonal, show two triangles are congruent, and matching parts are equal: a rectangle's diagonals are equal, a parallelogram's opposite angles are equal and its diagonals cut each other in half. Those properties then solve problems, with a reason for each step.

## Why it matters

Builders and manufacturers check parts are identical with a few measurements; maps, models and shadows use similar shapes to measure what can't be reached. Proof by congruent triangles is the first real proof most students write.

## What students get wrong

- **SSA called SAS.** Ask where the angle is.
- **Adding instead of multiplying** to enlarge (4, 6 to 7, 9).
- **Equal angles called congruent.** They give similar; a side is needed too.
- **One drawing as a proof.** A proof gives reasons that work for every shape.
- **Opposite angles of a parallelogram adding to 180°.** Neighbours do; opposites are equal.

## The words

**congruent** (≡), **similar**, **scale factor**, **enlargement**, **SSS**, **SAS**, **ASA**, **AAS**, **RHS**, **included angle**, **hypotenuse**, **diagonal**, **bisect**, **proof**.
