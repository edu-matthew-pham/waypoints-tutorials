Priya's ruler has marks at 10 m and 1000 m. She finds the number halfway between them.
The ruler has equal steps of ×10 between its marks.
Halfway between 10 and 1000 is the average of the two.
(10 + 1000) ÷ 2 = 505.
So the halfway mark is 505 m.
