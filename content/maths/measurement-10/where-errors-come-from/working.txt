# Working for the "find the mistake" tasks. Plain text: students click the wrong line, nothing runs.

# region: dev
Dev's group measures a table six times with a cloth tape: 1.52, 1.53, 1.51, 1.52, 1.53, 1.52 m.
The teacher's laser says the table is 1.60 m long.
The readings are all within 0.02 m of each other.
So the readings agree, which means the tape must be accurate.
We will report the table as 1.52 m.
