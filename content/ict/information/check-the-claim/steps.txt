# The steps for checking a claim, in order. Plain text: students put them in order.

# region: sideways
Say the claim in a few words.
Leave the page and open a new tab.
Search for the claim or for who made the page.
Find places that would know, such as the people who keep the records.
See if they agree without copying each other.
Decide how far to trust it, and say what you could not check.
