Australia combined features from two systems. **Westminster** means the British parliamentary tradition. **Washington** means the US federal model. This lesson connects the ideas you have already met: the Constitution, responsible government, checked power and federation.

The founders wanted a national government without simply abolishing the colonies’ own governments. The colonies became states. Smaller colonies wanted protection from being outvoted just because they had fewer people.

The House represents people through electorates, with representation broadly based on population. The Senate gives each state equal representation. Together, these helped make federation acceptable to the smaller colonies. Ministers still come from Parliament and answer to it: the Westminster connection remains.
