# AC9M7M01 — Area of triangles and parallelograms

**Year 7**
**Strand:** Measurement

## ACARA Descriptor

solve problems involving the area of triangles and parallelograms using established formulas and appropriate units

## Prior Knowledge

Students can establish and apply the area formula for a rectangle (from AC9M6M02). Students understand area as the measure of a 2D region and can use appropriate units.

## Progression Endpoint

Students solve problems involving the area of triangles and parallelograms using established formulas with appropriate units.

## Waypoint Sequence (2 waypoints, 1 hinge)

#### 1. Deriving the triangle area formula ⚑

Students understand why the area of a triangle is half the area of a related rectangle or parallelogram, and can state the formula A = ½bh.

**⚑ Hinge:** Students who believe the formula only applies to right-angled triangles misidentify the height in Node 2 problems — applying A = ½bh with the slant side instead of the perpendicular height produces systematically wrong answers for all non-right-angled triangles.

**Response modes:**
- ↔ Widen contexts: Students who derive the formula only for right-angled triangles often believe the formula changes for acute or obtuse triangles. Multiple triangle types resolve this.

**Minimum width:** Explain using a diagram why A = ½bh for a triangle, connecting to the rectangle formula.

**Typical width:** Derive the formula using multiple triangle types (right-angled, acute, obtuse) and verify the connection to the enclosing rectangle in each case.

**Wider:**
- Investigate using dynamic geometry software: slide the apex of a triangle at a fixed altitude and observe that the area is constant — explain why.
- Explore how the formula applies when the height is outside the triangle (obtuse case).

**Success criteria:**
- States the area formula for a triangle and explains its derivation.
- Correctly identifies the base and corresponding perpendicular height.
- Does not confuse the slant side with the perpendicular height.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7M02

#### 2. Applying area formulas to triangles and parallelograms ★

Students calculate the area of triangles and parallelograms using established formulas, selecting appropriate units.

**Response modes:**
- ↔ Widen contexts: Area formula application errors are common in non-standard orientations and composite shapes. Varied practical contexts with different orientations are necessary.

**Minimum width:** Calculate the area of a given triangle and a given parallelogram using their formulas.

**Typical width:** Solve problems involving composite shapes (decompose into triangles and rectangles); select appropriate units; convert between units if needed.

**Wider:**
- Solve practical problems: floor plans, sail areas, land parcels — requiring decomposition into triangles and parallelograms.
- Investigate First Nations design elements that use triangular and parallelogram forms — calculate areas in context.

**Success criteria:**
- Correctly calculates the area of a triangle using A = ½bh.
- Correctly calculates the area of a parallelogram.
- Uses appropriate units and converts if necessary.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7N06
- AC9M7M06
