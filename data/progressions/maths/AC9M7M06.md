# AC9M7M06 — Mathematical modelling with ratios

**Year 7**
**Strand:** Measurement

## ACARA Descriptor

use mathematical modelling to solve practical problems involving ratios; formulate problems, interpret and communicate solutions in terms of the situation, justifying choices made about the representation

## Prior Knowledge

Students can recognise, represent and solve ratio problems (from AC9M7N08). Students have used mathematical modelling in numerical contexts (from AC9M7N09).

## Progression Endpoint

Students use mathematical modelling to formulate and solve practical problems involving ratios of measurement quantities, communicating and justifying solutions in context.

## Waypoint Sequence (2 waypoints, 0 hinges)

#### 1. Recognising ratio structure in measurement problems

Students identify when a practical measurement problem involves a ratio relationship and represent it using ratio notation.

**Response modes:**
- ↔ Widen contexts: Ratio problems in measurement span a wider variety of contexts than number strand ratio problems — scale, mix, division of space — and students need exposure to multiple contexts to recognise the common structure.

**Minimum width:** Identify the ratio relationship in a given practical measurement problem (e.g. mixing paint, scaling a recipe) and write it in a:b form.

**Typical width:** Distinguish part-part and part-whole ratio structures in measurement problems; represent both using diagrams and notation.

**Wider:**
- Investigate ratio applications in scale drawings and maps — connect scale ratios to measurement.
- Explore ratio in construction and design contexts: concrete mix ratios, fertiliser dilution rates.

**Success criteria:**
- Correctly identifies the ratio structure in a practical measurement problem.
- Represents the ratio using appropriate notation.
- Distinguishes part-part from part-whole ratio in context.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7N08

#### 2. Formulating, solving and interpreting ratio models ★

Students formulate a ratio model for a practical measurement problem, solve it, and interpret and communicate the solution in terms of the original situation.

**Response modes:**
- ⊕ Integration point: Formulating, solving and communicating a ratio model requires simultaneous application of ratio understanding, measurement, unit reasoning, and modelling conventions. This integration is the core learning.

**Minimum width:** Formulate and solve a practical ratio problem (e.g. mixing liquids, dividing a length) and express the answer with appropriate units.

**Typical width:** Solve multi-step ratio problems in measurement contexts; justify representation choices; check reasonableness.

**Wider:**
- Investigate First Nations Australians' applications of ratio in substances and land use — formulate and solve a contextualised ratio problem.
- Model a design problem where a ratio constraint must be satisfied (e.g. designing a scale model at 1:50 ratio).

**Success criteria:**
- Formulates the ratio model correctly from the problem description.
- Solves the problem and expresses the answer with appropriate measurement units.
- Justifies the representation choice and checks the solution for reasonableness.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7N09
- AC9M7N08
