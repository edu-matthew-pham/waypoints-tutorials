# AC9M7ST03 — Statistical investigations

**Year 7**
**Strand:** Statistics

## ACARA Descriptor

plan and conduct statistical investigations involving data for discrete and continuous numerical variables; analyse and interpret distributions of data and report findings in terms of shape and summary statistics

## Prior Knowledge

Students have planned and conducted statistical investigations and communicated findings (from AC9M6ST03). Students can create displays and describe distributions (from AC9M7ST01, AC9M7ST02).

## Progression Endpoint

Students plan and conduct statistical investigations with discrete and continuous numerical variables, and report findings in terms of distribution shape and summary statistics.

## Waypoint Sequence (2 waypoints, 0 hinges)

#### 1. Planning a statistical investigation

Students identify a statistical question, plan data collection (including variable type, measurement method and sample), and anticipate potential sources of bias.

**Response modes:**
- ◎ Scaffold reasoning: Planning requires deliberate reasoning about the investigation process before data is collected. Students who skip planning rarely identify bias and produce poor data.

**Minimum width:** Write a statistical question, identify the variable type and describe how data will be collected.

**Typical width:** Plan a full investigation including sample size, data collection method, and at least one potential source of variability or bias.

**Wider:**
- Compare primary data collection (measurement, survey) with secondary data sources — identify advantages and limitations of each.
- Investigate how sample size affects the reliability of conclusions.

**Success criteria:**
- Writes a clear statistical question that can be answered with data.
- Identifies the variable type (discrete or continuous) and appropriate measurement method.
- Identifies at least one potential source of variability in their data collection.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7ST01

#### 2. Conducting, analysing and reporting ★

Students collect data, display it appropriately, analyse the distribution using summary statistics, and report findings in terms of the statistical question.

**Response modes:**
- ⊕ Integration point: Conducting and reporting a full investigation integrates all statistics strand skills. The integration and contextual communication are the learning at this node.
- ↔ Widen contexts: Statistical investigation only develops as a transferable skill through conducting multiple investigations in varied contexts. A single investigation produces performance on that topic, not transferable investigation skill.

**Minimum width:** Collect data, create an appropriate display, calculate summary statistics, and write a brief report answering the statistical question.

**Typical width:** Conduct a full investigation cycle; choose appropriate displays and statistics for the variable type; report findings including shape, centre, spread and context.

**Wider:**
- Use secondary data from a public source (e.g. Reconciliation Barometer, ABS) to conduct a statistical investigation on a social or cultural question.
- Investigate: does the investigation support or refute a stated claim? Frame the report around evidence for and against.

**Success criteria:**
- Collects, displays and analyses data appropriate to the variable type.
- Reports findings that address the original statistical question.
- Uses distribution language (shape, centre, spread) in the report.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7ST01
- AC9M7ST02
