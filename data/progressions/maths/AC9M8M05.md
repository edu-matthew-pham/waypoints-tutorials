# AC9M8M05 — Rates

**Year 8**
**Strand:** Measurement

## ACARA Descriptor

recognise and use rates to solve problems involving the comparison of 2 related quantities of different units of measure

## Prior Knowledge

Students can solve ratio problems (from AC9M7N08). Students understand proportional reasoning and unit conversion.

## Progression Endpoint

Students recognise rates as comparisons of two different quantity types, apply rates to solve conversion and practical problems, and distinguish rates from ratios.

## Waypoint Sequence (2 waypoints, 1 hinge)

#### 1. Rates as comparisons of different units ⚑ ★

Students recognise that a rate compares two quantities with different units and express rates in standard form (quantity per unit).

**⚑ Hinge:** Students who only recognise speed as a rate cannot identify the rate structure in Node 2 problems involving pay rates, tax rates, or dilution — they cannot set up the calculation because they don't recognise these as rate problems.

**Response modes:**
- ↔ Widen contexts: Rates appear in an enormous variety of contexts. Students who only practise speed calculations fail to recognise pay rates, dilution rates, or interest rates as the same mathematical structure.

**Minimum width:** Identify and write rates from practical contexts (e.g. km/h, $/kg); convert between related rate units.

**Typical width:** Identify constant and average rates in real contexts; apply rates to unit conversion problems; distinguish rates from ratios.

**Wider:**
- Investigate First Nations Australians' land management practices involving rates — e.g. rate of fire spread under different environmental conditions.
- Compare multiple rate units for the same phenomenon (e.g. km/h, m/s, miles/hour) and practise conversion between them.

**Success criteria:**
- Correctly identifies and writes a rate from a practical context.
- Converts between related rate units.
- Distinguishes a rate (different units) from a ratio (same units).

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7N08

#### 2. Applying rates to practical problems ★

Students apply rates to solve practical problems including pay rates, run rates, dilution, taxation, and unit conversions.

**Response modes:**
- ↔ Widen contexts: Rate application problems span a very wide range of real-world contexts and problem structures. Breadth of context is the primary driver of transferable rate reasoning.

**Minimum width:** Solve a practical problem using a given rate (e.g. calculate cost at a given rate per kg, or distance at a given speed).

**Typical width:** Apply rates to varied contexts including required run rates in sport, chemical dilution, income tax using rate tables, and currency conversion.

**Wider:**
- Use taxation rate tables to calculate annual income tax for a given salary — apply marginal rate structure and compute effective rate.
- Investigate currency conversion as a rate problem: plan a budget for overseas travel using current exchange rates.

**Success criteria:**
- Correctly applies a given rate to solve a practical problem.
- Shows the rate calculation clearly with appropriate units.
- Interprets the result in context.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M8M07
- AC9M8N05
