# AC9M8SP02 — Properties of quadrilaterals

**Year 8**
**Strand:** Space

## ACARA Descriptor

establish properties of quadrilaterals using congruent triangles and angle properties, and solve related problems explaining reasoning

## Prior Knowledge

Students can classify quadrilaterals and state their properties (from AC9M7SP02). Students can identify congruent triangles (from AC9M8SP01).

## Progression Endpoint

Students establish properties of quadrilaterals by reasoning with congruent triangles and angle facts, and solve multi-step problems with full geometric justification.

## Waypoint Sequence (2 waypoints, 1 hinge)

#### 1. Establishing quadrilateral properties via congruent triangles ⚑

Students use diagonal decomposition into triangles and congruence arguments to establish properties of specific quadrilaterals.

**⚑ Hinge:** Node 2 requires applying established quadrilateral properties to solve multi-step problems — students who have not established those properties with justification have no valid properties to draw on, reducing Node 2 to guessing or memorisation.

**Response modes:**
- ◎ Scaffold reasoning: Geometric proof via congruent triangles requires an explicit scaffold: decompose the quadrilateral, identify the congruence condition, state matching elements, conclude the property. Students without this structure produce informal and incomplete arguments.

**Minimum width:** Show that the diagonals of a rectangle bisect each other using congruent triangles.

**Typical width:** Establish properties of parallelograms, rhombuses and rectangles using congruent triangles and angle facts; state each property with justification.

**Wider:**
- Establish properties of a kite using congruent triangles — which diagonal is the axis of symmetry? Prove using the congruence argument.
- Apply quadrilateral properties to construction designs: car jacks, scissor lifts, folding umbrellas — identify which property makes the design functional.

**Success criteria:**
- Uses a congruence argument to establish at least one quadrilateral property.
- States each step with a geometric reason.
- Does not assert properties without justification.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M8SP01
- AC9M7SP02

#### 2. Solving problems using quadrilateral properties ★

Students use established quadrilateral properties to find unknown angles and lengths, providing full geometric reasoning.

**Response modes:**
- ⊕ Integration point: Solving multi-step quadrilateral problems requires drawing on the full repertoire of angle properties, congruence conditions, and quadrilateral-specific facts simultaneously.

**Minimum width:** Find an unknown angle or side in a quadrilateral using at least one established property.

**Typical width:** Solve multi-step problems combining quadrilateral properties, parallel line angle facts, and triangle angle sums; give reasons for every step.

**Wider:**
- Investigate which quadrilateral properties are preserved under each transformation type — connect to the congruence and similarity work from AC9M8SP01.
- Design a problem requiring at least three geometric reasoning steps using quadrilateral properties — write the full solution with reasons.

**Success criteria:**
- Finds unknown angles or lengths using quadrilateral properties correctly.
- States a geometric reason for each step.
- Solves at least one multi-step problem without prompting for each step.

**Related standards:**
- AC9M7M04
- AC9M7M05
