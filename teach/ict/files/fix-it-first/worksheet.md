# Fix it first

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** The printer says 'Paper jam'. One student presses every button and the job is printed three times. Another reads the message. Who fixes it?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students learn a short routine for fixing problems: read the message, save their work, change one thing and test, restart, and ask with details. They sort good first moves from ones to avoid, find a mistake in changing everything at once, and learn which problems are not theirs to fix.

**Students should be able to say:** I can follow a routine when something goes wrong. I can write a help message that says what I saw and what I tried. I can tell which problems are not mine to fix.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: the missing sound


:::keep-together

### The routine

1. $\text{Read}$ — What does the message say? What changed just before?
2. $\text{Save}$ — Keep your work somewhere safe before trying anything.
3. $\text{One change}$ — Try one thing, then test. Undo it if it did not help.
4. $\text{Restart}$ — Close the app, then the device.
5. $\text{Ask}$ — Say what you saw and what you tried. Name the app.

Now: what is the best first thing to try?

**Your video has no sound. What is the best first thing to try?**

☐ Reinstall the app

☐ Check one thing, such as whether the volume or mute is on, then test

☐ Change every sound setting

☐ Delete the video

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Check one thing, such as whether the volume or mute is on, then test

Start with the simplest possible cause, change one thing, and test.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Sam's sound is fixed, but he cannot say how, and it breaks again. Click the line where Sam first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Sam's video has no sound.
 2  He wants it fixed quickly.
 3  He changes the volume, the output device and the app settings all at once.
 4  The sound comes back, but he does not know which change fixed it, so when it breaks again he starts from the beginning.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Sam changed the volume, the output device and the app settings all at once. The sound came back, but he does not know which change fixed it, so when it breaks again he starts from nothing.

:::


:::keep-together
