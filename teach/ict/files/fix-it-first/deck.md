# Fix it first

Files and systems · Fix it first

<!-- Speaker note: Students learn a short routine for fixing problems: read the message, save their work, change one thing and test, restart, and ask with details. They sort good first moves from ones to avoid, find a mistake in changing everything at once, and learn which problems are not theirs to fix. About 40 minutes (Predict and Try 8 min · Worked example 10 min · Vary 7 min · Practise 6 min · Make and own system 9 min). -->

- I can follow a routine when something goes wrong.
- I can write a help message that says what I saw and what I tried.

## Why it matters

> The printer says 'Paper jam'. One student presses every button and the job is printed three times. Another reads the message. Who fixes it?

<!-- Speaker note: Most problems have a small cause. A routine helps you find it without making things worse: 1. **Stop and read.** What does the message say? Say it out loud, or write it down. 2. **Save what you can.** If your work is open, save it somewhere safe before you try anything. 3. **Change one thing, then test.** If you change three things and it works, you do not know which one fixed it. 4. **Restart.** Closing the app, or the device, fixes many problems. 5. **Ask, with details.** Say what you saw, and what you tried, so the helper does not start from the beginning. Some problems are not yours to fix: a locked school setting, a broken device, or anything that asks for a password that is not yours. Asking is the right move there. -->

## Think, pair, share

**Why do experts ask 'what changed?' and 'what did you try?' before touching anything?**

- What does 'what changed' tell you?
- What does 'what did you try' save?

## Predict

**A window pops up on your screen with a message you have not seen before. Your work is open behind it. What is the best first step?**

- Read the message
- Click OK quickly to get rid of it
- Close everything
- Restart the computer

<!-- Speaker note: What happens: Read the message. It often says what is wrong and what to do. Clicking OK without reading can lose work, and restarting first may lose what is not saved. Take two or three predictions before anyone runs it. -->

## The routine

Now: what is the best first thing to try?

$$\text{Read}$$

1) $\text{Save}$ — Keep your work somewhere safe before trying anything.
2) $\text{One change}$ — Try one thing, then test. Undo it if it did not help.
3) $\text{Restart}$ — Close the app, then the device.
4) $\text{Ask}$ — Say what you saw and what you tried. Name the app.

## The routine

**Your video has no sound. What is the best first thing to try?**

- Reinstall the app
- Check one thing, such as whether the volume or mute is on, then test
- Change every sound setting
- Delete the video

* Answer: Check one thing, such as whether the volume or mute is on, then test

## Find the mistake

**Sam's sound is fixed, but he cannot say how, and it breaks again. Click the line where Sam first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Sam's video has no sound.
 2  He wants it fixed quickly.
 3  He changes the volume, the output device and the app settings all at once.
 4  The sound comes back, but he does not know which change fixed it, so when it breaks again he starts from the beginning.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: Sam changed the volume, the output device and the app settings all at once. The sound came back, but he does not know which change fixed it, so when it breaks again he starts from nothing. -->

## Asking well

**Which help request is most useful?**

- It's broken
- The video has no sound. The volume is up, headphones are unplugged, and a restart did not help
- Nothing works
- Fix my computer

* Answer: The video has no sound. The volume is up, headphones are unplugged, and a restart did not help

## Match the step

**Match each problem to the best first step.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `A file will not open` | A. Wait a moment, then close the app and open it again |
| `The screen is frozen` | B. Check you are connected, then try again |
| `A page will not load` | C. Read the message, then check the file ending |

* Answer: A file will not open → C. Read the message, then check the file ending; The screen is frozen → A. Wait a moment, then close the app and open it again; A page will not load → B. Check you are connected, then try again
