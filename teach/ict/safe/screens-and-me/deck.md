## Engage: predict

**Noah meant to watch one video and watched for an hour. Why might that happen?**

- Videos are always longer than they look
- Noah has no self-control
- The next video starts by itself and the feed never ends, so there is no natural place to stop

<!-- Speaker note: What happens: Often it's the design: autoplay and endless feeds take away the natural places to stop. That happens to adults too. It isn't a sign that something is wrong with Noah. Take two or three predictions before anyone runs it. -->

## A habit Noah can choose

**Which of these is a habit Noah could choose for himself?**

- Decide one or two videos before he starts, then do something else
- Watch until his dad takes the device
- Feel bad about it
- Never watch videos again

* Answer: Decide one or two videos before he starts, then do something else
