## Amira's mistake

**Amira chats with StarPainter12. Click the line where her thinking first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Amira meets StarPainter12 in a drawing game.
 2  They chat about shading and swap tips for a week.
 3  Amira thinks: she's so nice and likes the same things, so she must be who she says she is.
 4  When StarPainter12 asks which school she goes to, Amira tells her.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: Amira decides that being nice and liking the same things means StarPainter12 is who she says. A friendly profile can be made by anyone. That is the moment to check with a trusted adult before sharing more. -->

## Trusted adults

**Who is a trusted adult?**

- Any adult online who is kind to you
- Whoever has the most followers
- An older player in your game
- An adult you know in real life who looks out for you, such as a parent, carer, teacher or school counsellor

* Answer: An adult you know in real life who looks out for you, such as a parent, carer, teacher or school counsellor

<!-- Speaker note: Someone you know in real life, whose job or role is to look out for you. Most people have more than one. -->

## Secrets

**Why is 'don't tell your parents' a reason to check first?**

- Someone safe doesn't ask you to keep secrets from the adults who look after you
- Parents don't understand games
- It means they don't like your parents

* Answer: Someone safe doesn't ask you to keep secrets from the adults who look after you

<!-- Speaker note: A request for secrecy from a new contact is the clearest sign to check with a trusted adult. Ask on mini whiteboards or by show of hands before the reveal. -->

## Elaborate: older games and chats

**A contact in an online game says each of these to Kai, who is in Year 8. Fine, or check first?**

Groups: Fine · Check with a trusted adult first

'I'll give you 500 game coins if you add me on another app.' · 'You're so mature for your age, not like the others.' · 'GG, that was a close match.' · 'Don't tell anyone about our chats, they wouldn't understand.'

* Answer: 'I'll give you 500 game coins if you add me on another app.' → Check with a trusted adult first; 'You're so mature for your age, not like the others.' → Check with a trusted adult first; 'GG, that was a close match.' → Fine; 'Don't tell anyone about our chats, they wouldn't understand.' → Check with a trusted adult first
