# Who is on the other end?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** In an online game, a player called StarPainter12 says she's 12, loves the same drawing app as Amira, and is really nice. What does Amira actually know about her?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students predict how much a profile tells them, sort a new online contact's messages into fine and check with a trusted adult first (personal details, secrets, photos, moving to a more private app, meeting), find the mistake in treating friendly as trusted, and name their trusted adults. Typical width (Years 7 and 8): gifts and flattery as ways to build trust fast.

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain why a profile does not prove who someone is. I can name requests that mean I should check with a trusted adult first. I can name trusted adults I could ask.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Explain: friendly is not the same as known

You only see what someone chooses to show. Most people online are who they say. But because anyone can make a profile, **friendly is not the same as known**.

Keep for people you know in real life: your full name, school, address, phone number and photos of you. If a new contact asks for any of these, asks you to keep a secret, wants to move to a more private app, or wants to meet, **stop and check with a trusted adult**. You are not in trouble for asking, and it is not rude to say "I need to ask first".

:::keep-together

### Amira's mistake

**Amira chats with StarPainter12. Click the line where her thinking first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Amira meets StarPainter12 in a drawing game.
 2  They chat about shading and swap tips for a week.
 3  Amira thinks: she's so nice and likes the same things, so she must be who she says she is.
 4  When StarPainter12 asks which school she goes to, Amira tells her.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Amira decides that being nice and liking the same things means StarPainter12 is who she says. A friendly profile can be made by anyone. That is the moment to check with a trusted adult before sharing more.

:::

### Trusted adults

**Who is a trusted adult?**

☐ Any adult online who is kind to you

☐ Whoever has the most followers

☐ An older player in your game

☐ An adult you know in real life who looks out for you, such as a parent, carer, teacher or school counsellor

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** An adult you know in real life who looks out for you, such as a parent, carer, teacher or school counsellor

Someone you know in real life, whose job or role is to look out for you. Most people have more than one.

:::
