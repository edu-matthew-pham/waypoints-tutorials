- So he clicks before he stops to think or ask anyone

## Grace's mistake

**Grace gets a message in a game. Click the line where her thinking first goes wrong.**

```
 1  A message in Grace's game says she has won a rare pet, but only if she claims it in 5 minutes.
 2  She thinks: it has a countdown, so it must be real and I have to be quick.
 3  She clicks the link and types in her game username and password.
 4  The next day she can't sign in to her account.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: Grace thinks the countdown means she must act now. Pressure to hurry is the sign to slow down. A real offer would still be there after she asked. -->

## The password

**A message asks Leo for his password 'to send the gems'. What should he do?**

- Give it, because the gems are free
- Never give it; close the message and ask a trusted adult
- Give only half of it
- Give it but change it later

* Answer: Never give it; close the message and ask a trusted adult

<!-- Speaker note: Passwords and codes are only typed into the real app or site, never sent to anyone. Real companies don't ask for them in a message. -->

## Passwords

**Who should you send your password to?**

- Nobody: type it only into the real app or site
- A friend you trust
- The game's support team if they message you

* Answer: Nobody: type it only into the real app or site

## Exit ticket

**A message says you have 1 minute to claim a prize. What do you do?**

* Slow down: stop, check where it's from (did I enter?), and ask a trusted adult before clicking. Never send a password or code.
