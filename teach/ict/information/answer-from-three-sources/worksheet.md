# Challenge: answer it from three sources

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Your class is planting beans next week, and someone asks whether to put the trays somewhere warm. You have three sources and ten minutes. What do you tell them?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** The challenge: students answer a small question (do bean seeds sprout faster in warm soil?) from three made-up sources, rate how far they trust each, separate what the trusted sources agree on from a sales claim, credit all three and write a short answer that says no more than the sources show.

**Students should be able to say:** I can answer a question from more than one source and say how far I trust each. I can write an answer that says no more than my sources show. I can credit my sources with who, where and when.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Explain: what do they agree on?


:::keep-together

### What you can claim

**Which claim can you make from the sources you trust?**

☐ Every bean seed sprouts in 24 hours on a heat mat

☐ Beans will not grow at all in cold soil

☐ Heat makes beans grow taller too

☐ Warm soil, around 20 °C, helps bean seeds sprout faster than cold soil

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Warm soil, around 20 °C, helps bean seeds sprout faster than cold soil

The garden club and the class test agree that warmth helps sprouting. No source you trust says more than that.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Tom's mistake

**Tom's answer says more than the sources show. Click the line where Tom first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Tom reads all three sources about warm soil and bean seeds.
 2  He sees that they all say warm soil helps.
 3  He writes that bean seeds sprout in 24 hours in warm soil, because that is the most exciting number.
 4  He does not say where the number came from.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Tom used the shop's 24 hours as his answer, because it was the most exciting number. It comes from one source, which sells mats and gives no evidence. The sources he could check say about a week.
