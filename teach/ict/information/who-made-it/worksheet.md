# Who made it, and why?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Two pages both say beans grow best in full sun. One is from a garden club, the other from a shop selling grow lamps. Does it matter who said it?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find who made a page, why (to inform, sell, persuade or entertain) and when, by sorting six made-up pages by purpose, finding a planted mistake and deciding when a date matters.

**Students should be able to say:** I can find who made a page, and say why they made it. I can find a page's date and say when it matters. I can explain why a page that sells or persuades is read with care.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Engage


:::keep-together

**Two pages both say beans grow best in full sun. One is from a garden club. The other is from a shop selling grow lamps. Which would you trust more on this?**

☐ The shop: it sells garden things, so it knows

☐ They are the same: the words are the same

☐ I would need to know more about both

☐ The garden club: the shop wants to sell lamps

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** There is no single right answer. Many people trust the garden club more because the shop has something to sell, and that is a fair reason. 'I would need to know more' is a strong answer too: who is in the club, and when was each page written? The words are the same, but the makers want different things.

:::


## Explore

Try it on the board in the tutorial. Was your prediction right? What was different?

## Explain: who, why, when


:::keep-together

### Where to look

**Where is the best place to find out who made a page?**

☐ Its About page, the address and any name on it

☐ How many adverts it has

☐ How long the page is

☐ The colours and pictures

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Its About page, the address and any name on it

The About page, the address and names on the page say who made it. If you cannot find out, that is a clue too.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Mia's mistake

**Mia's teacher asks why she trusted her page. Click the line where Mia first goes wrong.**

```
 1  Mia wants to know what her bean plants need to grow.
 2  She searches bean plant needs and opens the first page that is not an advert.
 3  She copies "every plant needs plant food each week" into her report, without asking who made the page or why.
 4  The page was made by a shop that sells plant food.
 5  Her teacher asks how she knows it is true.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Mia used the page without asking who made it or why. It was a shop selling plant food, so it had a reason to say plants need plant food. It might still be right, but she should have known what it wanted.

:::

## Elaborate: when does the date matter?


:::keep-together

### Does the date matter?

**Does the date matter for each question?**

Groups: The date matters a lot, The date matters less

- When is the next school holiday?: {{field-15}}
- How many legs does a spider have?: {{field-15}}
- What is the price of a bus ticket?: {{field-15}}
- Who won the Riverbend junior netball league?: {{field-15}}
- How do you write a fraction as a decimal?: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** When is the next school holiday? → The date matters a lot; How many legs does a spider have? → The date matters less; What is the price of a bus ticket? → The date matters a lot; Who won the Riverbend junior netball league? → The date matters a lot; How do you write a fraction as a decimal? → The date matters less

:::


:::keep-together

### No name, no date

**You cannot find any name, About page or date on a page. What does that tell you?**

☐ Nothing at all

☐ You cannot check who made it or when, so trust it less and look for another page

☐ It is from the government

☐ It must be true: it has nothing to hide

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** You cannot check who made it or when, so trust it less and look for another page

A page with no maker and no date is hard to check. It might be fine, but find a page you can check.
