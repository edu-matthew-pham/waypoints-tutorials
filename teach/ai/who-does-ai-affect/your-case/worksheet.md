# Your own case

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Choose a case and judge it with everything from the course: which kind of AI it is, who gains, who loses, who decides, what's the catch, and one rule you'd make.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** The course challenge: students choose a case (made-up, real with teacher approval, or one from the course) and judge it with the kind of AI, the lens, the course's questions, one rule, and (wider) a verdict with the other side.

**Students should be able to say:** I can judge a new AI system with the course's questions: which kind, who gains, who loses, who decides, and what's the catch. I can link a worry about a system to the right question from the course. I can make one rule for a system and say who checks it.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Engage

Pick one case (made-up cases are fine; so is a real one from the news, checked with your teacher):

- **Marking:** a school uses AI to mark students' short written answers.
- **Traffic:** a city uses cameras with AI to count cars and change traffic lights.
- **Refunds:** a shop lets an AI chatbot give customers refunds on its own.
- **Study notes:** an app makes study notes from photos of your textbook.
- **Library:** a library uses AI to suggest books to each student.
- **A case from this course** (exam grades, hiring, breast screening, energy, labelling, art, jobs), looked at from a new angle.

## Evaluate: your rule

Finish with **one rule** for your case, and who checks it's working (lesson 10). Share your lens with a partner: did they find a gain, a loss or a catch you missed?

Look back at your prediction: which question was hardest?

**Course complete.** Every new AI system you meet, ask: which kind is it, who gains, who loses, who decides, and what's the catch?
