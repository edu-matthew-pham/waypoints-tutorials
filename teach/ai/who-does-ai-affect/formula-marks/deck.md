# When a formula marks you

Who Does AI Affect? · Who decides?

<!-- Speaker note: England's 2020 A-level grades: a formula adjusted teachers' grades, relied more on teachers for small classes, lowered about 39%, and was reversed with an apology. One rule applied to everyone can be unfair to some groups. About 40 minutes. -->

---

## Today

- I can explain what the 2020 exam formula did, and who gained and lost.
- I can explain why one rule used on everyone can go wrong for a whole group at once.
- I can say what automated decisions about people need: a person who checks, a fair appeal, and someone responsible.

## Make: Elaborate: Robodebt

Australia's **Robodebt** scheme used a computer calculation to send people debt notices. The Royal Commission called it "a crude and cruel mechanism, neither fair nor legal". It used no AI: it was a rule written by people, used on many people at once.

Compare Robodebt with the 2020 exam formula. What did each get wrong? What did each need: a person who checks, a fair appeal, someone responsible? Why can "automated" be dangerous even without AI?

## Exit ticket

**Why can one formula used on everyone be unfair even if it works as designed?**

* The same rule is applied to everyone at once, so if it is unfair to a group, the whole group loses out; people need someone to check and a way to appeal.
