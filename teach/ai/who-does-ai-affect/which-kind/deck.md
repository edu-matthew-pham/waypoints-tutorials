# Which kind of AI is it?

Who Does AI Affect? · Who is affected?

<!-- Speaker note: A short recap of the four kinds of AI from AI Foundations (rules, predict or sort, make new content, take actions), using the cases this course will study. About 15 minutes. -->

---

## Today

- I can say which kind of AI a system is: rules, predict or sort, make new content, or take actions.
- I can say where to look first when each kind goes wrong.

## The exam formula

**The exam-grade formula gave unfair grades. Where would you look first?**

- At the examples it learned from
- At the pictures it made
- At the formula people designed, and the rule it applied to everyone

## Elaborate: not all "AI" is AI

**Australia's Robodebt scheme sent people wrong debt notices worked out by a computer. A Royal Commission called it 'a crude and cruel mechanism, neither fair nor legal'. It didn't learn from examples. Which kind was it?**

- Learns to predict or sort
- Makes new content
- Rules written by people

* Answer: Rules written by people
