# What would we never accept?

AI at our school · What do we want from AI?

## Today

**We are learning to** turn values into principles a decision can be checked against. Draft principles for the made-up school, test each one on quick cases, and (wider) compare them with Australia's national framework.

- I can tell a principle that decides cases from a value that doesn't.
- I can write a principle and test it on a case.
- I can explain why a principle should still work for tools nobody has invented yet.

## Think, pair, share

**Is 'Not using AI is a real option' a principle worth having?**

- What would a school lose if it never used AI?
- What would it lose if it always had to?

## Make: Elaborate: Australia's framework

The **Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools** (2023) sets out six principles:

1. Teaching and learning
2. Human and social wellbeing
3. Transparency
4. Fairness
5. Accountability
6. Privacy, security and safety

Match your draft principles to these six. Which of the framework's principles have you **not** covered? Is one of yours missing from the framework? Then look for how your own school's AI policy uses these (your teacher may have a copy).
