## Why it matters

**Why does it matter how an AI tool arrived?**

- Each route needs a different kind of response: a tool we chose can be dropped; a feature that arrived may only have a setting; a home app can't be switched off at all
- Only tools the school chose are real AI
- It doesn't: AI is AI

* Answer: Each route needs a different kind of response: a tool we chose can be dropped; a feature that arrived may only have a setting; a home app can't be switched off at all

## Make: Elaborate: what kind of AI?

For each item on Westbrook's map, name the kind of AI (from [What kind of AI is it?](../../foundations/kinds-of-ai/)): **rules written by people**, **learns to predict or sort**, **makes new content**, or **takes actions**.

The search answer and "Rewrite with AI" **make new content**: they can be wrong in ways that look right. An AI that **takes actions** (books, sends, submits, buys) needs a person to confirm first, and more tools are adding features that act. How would the kind change your response?
