## Today

**We are learning to** refine the AI lines in each policy, and the map that holds them together, using what the committee learned. Find the balance between too vague and too prescribed, keep policies in step, and rewrite lines that rely on catching people or on what AI can't do yet.

- I can tell a policy line that is too vague from one that is too prescribed.
- I can put detail in the right layer, and keep policies in step with each other.
- I can rewrite a policy line that relies on catching people so it works without it.
- I can spot a policy line that will break when AI gets more capable, and rewrite it around what we value.

## Exit ticket

**Why does someone need to hold the map of AI lines across a school's policies?**

* Policies are owned and reviewed separately, so without someone checking they drift apart and contradict each other, and changes in AI don't get passed along.
