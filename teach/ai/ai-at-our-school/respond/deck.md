## Today

**We are learning to** a search page now shows an AI answer above the results. Blocking isn't the only choice: teach a habit, change the task, change a setting, ask the company, or wait and review. Choose responses for Year 7 and Year 10, and check them against the charter.

- I can choose a response to AI nobody chose, from a menu, not just allow or block.
- I can name the charter principle a response serves, and its cost.
- I can explain why Year 7 and Year 10 might need different responses.

## Make: Elaborate: asking the company

When there's no setting, a school can **ask**. Companies do add controls when enough schools ask, but it takes time, and nothing is promised.

Write a short request to the maker of the made-up search page: what control Westbrook wants (for example, hiding AI answers for younger students), for whom, and which charter principle it serves.

Then: if the company says no, what would Westbrook do instead? What would **restricting** cost (students who rely on the tool, workarounds, students who meet it at home with no habit to use)?
