# Let the computer take over

AI Foundations · How AI generates text

<!-- Speaker note: Compare manual and automated sampling and identify what judgement remains with the learner. About 25 minutes. -->

---

## Today

**We are learning to** compare manual and automated sampling, and decide which work can be delegated without losing understanding.

- I can name the job the computer took over (rolling and counting) and the judgement that stayed with me.
- I can explain why 1,000 rolls get closer to 3 : 2 : 1 than ten rolls do.
- I can choose help from an AI tutor that leaves the thinking I'm practising to me.

## Elaborate: learning mode or performance mode?

**A student has never seen the lookup table but copies 1,000 generated words into an assignment. Which learning check is most useful?**

- Ask them to explain and perform a few selections without the computer
- Ask them to generate 10,000 more words
- Assume the large output proves understanding

* Answer: Ask them to explain and perform a few selections without the computer

## Learning with AI: direct the help you receive

**You asked an AI tutor for hints, but it gave you the complete answer straight away. Your goal is to learn the method. What is the best next step?**

- Ask it for one hint at a time, then try the next step yourself
- Copy the answer: you can learn it from reading later
- Ask it a harder question instead

* Answer: Ask it for one hint at a time, then try the next step yourself
