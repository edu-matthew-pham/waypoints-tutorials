# How can we tell what we have learned?

Does AI Help Us Learn? · Who decides what learning looks like?

<!-- Speaker note: Students match evidence to two stated goals, and see why the same report can be strong evidence for one goal and weak for another. About 40 minutes. -->

---

## Today

**We are learning to** match pieces of evidence to the goal they fit, and see how the same work can mean different things under different goals.

- I can match a piece of evidence to the goal it fits.
- I can explain the difference between feedback for learning (formative) and judging achievement (summative).
- I can design a task that allows AI and still shows what a student learned.

## Make: Elaborate: design a fair task

Design one task for this unit that **allows AI** and still shows what the student learned.

- What is the goal?
- What would students hand in with the work: a log, drafts, a short talk, an unseen question?
- What would your task still miss?

## Exit ticket

**Why can the same report support different conclusions under different goals?**

* Evidence is judged against the goal and how the work was done: a polished report can fit a communication goal but not show independent judging of evidence.
