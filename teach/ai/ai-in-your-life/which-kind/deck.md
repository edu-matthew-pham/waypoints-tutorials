## The made-up fact

**A chatbot tells you a fact that turns out to be made up. Which kind of mistake is that?**

- Takes actions: it acted before checking
- Rules: someone wrote a wrong rule
- Makes new content: it made something convincing that isn't true

* Answer: Makes new content: it made something convincing that isn't true

## The feed

**A feed keeps showing you things you said you didn't want. Where would you look?**

- At a rule about bedtimes
- At what it's predicting: what keeps people watching, not what you asked for
- At a picture it made

* Answer: At what it's predicting: what keeps people watching, not what you asked for
