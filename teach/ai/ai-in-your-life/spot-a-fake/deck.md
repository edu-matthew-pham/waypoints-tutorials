## The blinking tip

**Early deepfake videos didn't blink properly, so people said 'check the blinking'. What happened next?**

- Blinking became the best test
- The next deepfakes learned to blink, so the tip stopped working
- Deepfakes still can't blink

* Answer: The next deepfakes learned to blink, so the tip stopped working

## Make: Elaborate: fakes and bias

A 2023 study found that **AI-made White faces were judged to be human more often than real White faces** (the researchers called it "AI hyperrealism"). The effect was found for **White faces only**.

Why might AI make some groups' faces more convincing than others? (Think back to AI Foundations: what's in the examples it learned from?) Who could be harmed by that?

## Exit ticket

**Why isn't 'look for glitches' good advice for spotting AI-made media?**

* People spot fakes at about chance, they feel surer than they are, and the glitches get fixed, so tips go out of date.
