## Today

- I can name the warning signs of a voice-clone scam: urgency, secrecy, money to a new account, a new number.

## Why it works

**Why does calling back on a number you already know work, even if the voice was perfect?**

- It slows the internet down
- The scammer can't answer Mum's real phone
- It makes the clone sound different

* Answer: The scammer can't answer Mum's real phone

## Elaborate: it happens to companies too

**In 2024, a worker at the company Arup joined a video call with what looked and sounded like senior colleagues. They were fakes, and about US$25 million was sent to scammers. Which check would have helped?**

- Looking harder at the faces on the video
- Making the call longer
- Confirming the request another way, with a person they could reach directly

* Answer: Confirming the request another way, with a person they could reach directly

## Make: Elaborate: phishing by voice

In [Cyber safety › Spot the phish](../../../systems/cyber/phishing/), the signs were: someone pretending to be trusted, pressure, a request for money or a password, and a link or number that isn't what it seems. A voice clone is **phishing with a voice**.

Make a table: the signs of a phishing email and the signs of a voice-clone call. Which defences work for both? Which only work for one?
