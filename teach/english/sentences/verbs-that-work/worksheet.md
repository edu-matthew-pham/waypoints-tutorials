# Verbs that do the work

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** 'Drank very quickly' or 'gulped'. Which one is the writer's best choice, and why?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students learn that precise verbs, the right tense and adverb groups make a sentence more exact. They choose a one-word verb for a wordy phrase, match phrases to verbs, catch a plain verb with an adverb, choose the tense a report needs, choose verb forms from time clues, colour adverb groups by what they add and write a match report in their own document.

**Students should be able to say:** I can replace a plain verb and an adverb with one precise verb. I can choose past, present or future for what I am telling. I can say what an adverb group adds: how, where, when or how much. I can write a match report that uses all three well.

## Worked example: verbs, tense and adverbs


:::keep-together

### Three ways to sharpen a sentence

1. $\text{Look at the verb}$ — Is there one verb that says what the verb and the adverb say together?
2. $\text{Check the tense}$ — When does this happen: past, present or future?
3. $\text{Add an adverb group only if it helps}$ — How, where, when or how much? Does the reader need it?

Now match the phrases to verbs.

## Adverb groups


## Make: a match report

In your own document, write a match report of at least five sentences (at least 50 words) about a match or game you know, or one you make up. Tell what happened in the order it happened. Use at least two precise verbs and two adverb groups. Keep to the past tense.

Here is one example: "Our team played Hillside on Saturday morning. The striker **sprinted** down the wing and **crossed** the ball. Hillside's keeper **dived** but missed it. The crowd roared as the ball hit the net. Our captain lifted the trophy, and everyone cheered **very loudly**."

Then swap with a partner. Underline each precise verb and each adverb group in each other's report. Is every verb in the past tense? Does each adverb group help?

Work in the tutorial. Plan first: what will you change, and how will you test it?
