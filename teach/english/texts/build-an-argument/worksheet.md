# Challenge: build an argument

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** You want your school to change one thing. You get one page to convince the principal. How do you build it?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** The course challenge, built on the genre teaching and learning cycle. Students take a model argument apart (position, reasons, restated position), put Nib's jumbled argument back in order, choose the joining words that carry an argument from reason to reason, and build their own argument on the board, then check it with a partner against a short checklist.

**Students should be able to say:** I can name the stages of an argument and what each one does. I can put an argument's stages in order and link its reasons. I can build my own argument with a position, reasons with evidence and a restated position.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Make: Part 4: build your own

The board checks that each stage is there and labelled. With a partner, check the rest:

- Is the position one clear sentence?
- Does each reason have a piece of evidence?
- Does the restated position sum up the reasons, without adding a new one?
