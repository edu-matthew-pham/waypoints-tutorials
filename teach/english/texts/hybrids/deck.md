## Why it matters

> A letter asking for donations starts: 'Last July, floodwater filled Jun's house in the night.' Is it a story, or something else?

<!-- Speaker note: Writers often **mix** text types on purpose. The result is a **hybrid**. - A charity letter opens with a **true story**, so the reader cares before the reasons arrive. - A novel is told as **diary entries**, so the reader learns things day by day. - A recipe starts with a **memory**, so the reader wants to make it. A hybrid works when each borrowed part does a job for the **main purpose**, and the story links back to the point. -->

## Borrowing for a purpose

Now: why start an argument with a story?

$$\text{Find the main purpose}$$

1) $\text{Find the borrowed part}$ — Which part comes from another text type?
2) $\text{Ask what job it does}$ — Does it hook, make the reader care, or make the steps easier to follow?
3) $\text{Check the link}$ — Does the borrowed part connect back to the main point?

## Borrowing for a purpose

**Why might an argument start with a short true story?**

- So the writer doesn't need to give any reasons
- Because every argument has to start with a story
- So the reader cares before the reasons arrive
- To fill space before the real argument begins

* Answer: So the reader cares before the reasons arrive

<!-- Speaker note: A story makes the issue real. The reasons and the call to act then have someone to be about. -->

## Find Nib's slip

**Here is Nib's argument, one sentence per line. Nib opens with a story. Click the first line where the reader finds out the story has nothing to do with the argument.**

```
 1  Last Friday, my friend Ava found a baby bird under the slide.
 2  We put it in a shoebox, and Ms Lin called the wildlife rescue.
 3  Our school should make lunch half an hour longer.
 4  Longer lunches would give students more time to eat and play.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: the argument is about longer lunches, but the baby bird story never connects to it. Nib needs to link it ('We missed lunch helping the bird. With a longer break...') or cut it. -->
