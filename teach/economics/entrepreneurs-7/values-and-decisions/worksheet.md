# Values and the decisions entrepreneurs make

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Leo could sell cheap new gadgets, or fix up old laptops for students who can't afford new ones. Fixing laptops earns him less on each sale. Why might he choose it anyway?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students analyse how values shape entrepreneurs' decisions in three areas: taking an opportunity, negotiating with stakeholders, and complying with the law and regulations. This is the hinge for AC9HE7K03: lesson 3 asks students to explain business success, and an explanation that leaves out values and stakeholders will be thin. The planted mistake is confusing stakeholders with shareholders.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what a value is and give an example of one shaping a business decision. I can explain who a business's stakeholders are, and how they differ from shareholders. I can say what a value might cost or gain a business.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: who counts as a stakeholder?


:::keep-together

### Leo's Laptop Repairs

1. $\text{Value}$ — Less waste, and fair access for students.
2. $\text{Taking an opportunity}$ — Refurbish old laptops instead of selling cheap new gadgets.
3. $\text{Value}$ — Fairness to suppliers.
4. $\text{Negotiating with stakeholders}$ — Agrees to pay Sunita within a week.
5. $\text{Value}$ — Honesty.
6. $\text{Complying with the law}$ — Registers his business name and keeps records for tax, and does it properly.
7. $\text{So}$ — Each value can be traced to a decision. Some cost him; some may help.

Now: is Sunita a stakeholder?

**Leo buys parts from Sunita's Parts Supply. Is Sunita a stakeholder in Leo's business?**

☐ Yes, because Leo's decisions about paying and ordering affect her business

☐ No, because she doesn't own any part of Leo's business

☐ Only if Leo makes a profit that year

☐ No, because stakeholders are only the customers

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Yes, because Leo's decisions about paying and ordering affect her business

A stakeholder is anyone affected by a business's decisions. Sunita is affected by how and when Leo pays her.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**Nib is working out who Leo needs to think about. Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Leo buys parts from Sunita's Parts Supply and sells refurbished laptops to students.
 2  Leo owns the business himself, so it has no shareholders.
 3  Nib says: "Stakeholders are the people who own shares in a business."
 4  Nib says: "So Leo's business has no stakeholders, and he doesn't need to think about Sunita or his customers."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: that is what a shareholder is. A stakeholder is anyone affected by the business's decisions: Sunita, Leo's customers, his neighbours and Leo himself. So line 4 is wrong too.

:::


:::keep-together

### Values that cost something

**Leo wipes every laptop's old files before he sells it, which takes him about an hour each. He does it because he values people's privacy. What does this show?**

☐ Values are bad for business, because they waste time

☐ Leo is only doing it because a law forces him to

☐ Values are personal opinions and don't change what a business does

☐ A value can cost a business time or money and still shape the decision

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A value can cost a business time or money and still shape the decision

Values show up in decisions, even when they cost something. Whether they pay off is a separate question.

:::

## Trace a value


:::keep-together

### The bakery's flour

**Riverbend Bakery buys flour from a local mill that costs more than a big supplier, because the baker values supporting local farms. What is one likely result?**

☐ The bakery must close, because any extra cost makes a business fail

☐ The mill becomes a shareholder in the bakery

☐ Nothing changes, because values don't affect a business's costs

☐ Her costs go up a little, and the mill keeps a steady customer

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Her costs go up a little, and the mill keeps a steady customer

A value led to a decision with a cost and a benefit: higher flour costs, and a steady local supplier.

:::


:::keep-together

### Leo's stakeholders

**Which list contains only stakeholders in Leo's Laptop Repairs?**

☐ Leo, his customers, Sunita the supplier and his neighbours

☐ Only Leo, because he owns the business

☐ Only his customers, because they pay the money

☐ Only people who own shares in Leo's business

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Leo, his customers, Sunita the supplier and his neighbours

Stakeholders are everyone affected by the business's decisions.
