# Rights and responsibilities

Consumer rights and responsibilities · Rights and responsibilities

## Today

**We are learning to** distinguish rights from responsibilities and list both for a customer and a business in a sale.

- I can explain the difference between a right and a responsibility.
- I can list rights and responsibilities for both a customer and a business.
- I can show how a right on one side pairs with a responsibility on the other.

## Why it matters

> Mia buys a kettle at Riverbend Appliances. What can she expect from the shop? And what can the shop expect from her?

<!-- Speaker note: Riverbend is a made-up town, and so are its people and shops. A **consumer** is someone who buys goods or services for their own use. - A **right** is something you can **claim**: something you are entitled to. - A **responsibility** is something you **must do**: your part of the deal, which you owe to someone else. In a sale, both sides have both. Often they pair up: what one side can claim is what the other side must do. -->

## Find Nib's mistake

Now: where does Nib go wrong?

$$\text{Mia can claim}$$

1) $\text{Mia must}$ — Pay for it, and use it the way the instructions say.
2) $\text{The shop can claim}$ — Payment for the kettle.
3) $\text{The shop must}$ — Sell safe kettles and tell the truth about them.
4) $\text{So}$ — Both sides have rights and responsibilities, and many of them pair up.

<!-- Speaker note: Model this live on the board before revealing anything: write each line and say why, pausing to ask the class what comes next. Then click to reveal the lines and compare. Script. Line 4: write So and say "Both sides have rights and responsibilities, and many of them pair up." -->

## Rights that pair up

**Rights and responsibilities often pair up. Match each right to the responsibility on the other side.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `Mia's right to a safe kettle` | A. Mia's responsibility to pay the agreed price |
| `The shop's right to be paid` | B. The shop's responsibility not to mislead her |
| `Mia's right to honest information` | C. The shop's responsibility to sell safe kettles |

* Answer: Mia's right to a safe kettle → C. The shop's responsibility to sell safe kettles; The shop's right to be paid → A. Mia's responsibility to pay the agreed price; Mia's right to honest information → B. The shop's responsibility not to mislead her

## Right or responsibility?

**Which of these is a right, not a responsibility?**

- Getting a product that matches how it was described
