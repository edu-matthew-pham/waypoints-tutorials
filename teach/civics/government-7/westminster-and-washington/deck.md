## Exit ticket

**Name two features from each tradition and explain why the founders combined them.**

* Westminster: responsible government and constitutional monarchy. Washington: written federal constitution and an equal-state Senate. The combination joined parliamentary accountability with national cooperation and a protected voice for smaller states.
