# Four-bar linkages

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A windscreen wiper swings back and forth, but its motor turns round and round. Four bars do it. Why can't they be any lengths you like?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students turn a four-bar linkage, find that a longer crank jams, learn the rule for whether the crank can turn fully (shortest + longest ≤ the other two, with the crank shortest), design a wiper with a swing of at least 100°, and search for a linkage whose swing falls in a range.

**Students should be able to say:** I can name the four bars of a four-bar linkage and what each one does. I can use the rule to say whether a crank can turn all the way round. I can change bar lengths to meet a swing criterion.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: the rule


:::keep-together

### Check 30, 80, 60, 70, then 55, 80, 60, 70

1. $\text{sorted: } 30, 60, 70, 80$ — List the four lengths, shortest to longest.
2. $30 + 80 = 110$ — Add the shortest and the longest.
3. $60 + 70 = 130$ — Add the other two.
4. $110 \le 130 \;\checkmark$ — No more than the other two: one bar can go all the way round. The crank is the shortest, so it is the crank.
5. $55 + 80 = 135 > 60 + 70 = 130 \;\times$ — With a 55 mm crank: more than the other two, so no bar can go all the way round. It jams.

**Bars 40, 90, 60 and 70 mm (crank 40). Can the crank turn all the way?**

☐ Yes: 40 + 90 = 130 is no more than 60 + 70 = 130

☐ No: 40 + 90 is more than 60 + 70

☐ Yes: 40 + 60 = 100 is less than 90 + 70

☐ No: the coupler is too long

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Yes: 40 + 90 = 130 is no more than 60 + 70 = 130

Shortest + longest = 40 + 90 = 130, the other two = 130. Not more, and the crank is the shortest, so it turns (only just).

:::


:::keep-together

## The rule


:::keep-together

### Yes or no

**Bars 20, 50, 40 and 45 mm (crank 20). Can the crank turn all the way?**

☐ Yes

☐ No

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Yes

20 + 50 = 70, no more than 40 + 45 = 85.

:::


:::keep-together

### Again

**Bars 35, 100, 50 and 70 mm (crank 35). Can the crank turn all the way?**

☐ Yes

☐ No

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** No

35 + 100 = 135, more than 50 + 70 = 120: it jams.

:::


:::keep-together

### A limit

**Coupler 90, rocker 70 and ground 80 mm. What is the longest crank (shorter than all of them) that still turns all the way, in mm?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 60

crank + 90 ≤ 70 + 80 = 150, so the crank can be up to 150 − 90 = 60 mm.

:::
