# Parking assistant

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Reversing cars beep faster as they get closer to a wall. Can you build one, and prove it warns at the right distances?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** A Years 8–9 design project: a parking assistant with an ultrasonic sensor and three warning bands, planned with band-edge tests, prototyped on the board, built in Tinkercad and optionally on hardware, tested with a ruler and evaluated.

**Students should be able to say:** I can plan tests for every edge of a set of bands. I can read a distance sensor and act on it. I can evaluate my design against each criterion with evidence.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Develop: two ideas first


:::keep-together

### Two ways to do it

**Two ideas for the beeping. A: one steady beep rate from 30 to 100 cm. B: beeping that speeds up as the car gets closer. B is nicer to use. Does the brief ask for it?**

☐ It doesn't matter what the brief says if the family likes it

☐ No: it's extra, and it still has to pass the brief's tests at 30 and 100 cm

☐ Yes: beeping means it has to speed up

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** No: it's extra, and it still has to pass the brief's tests at 30 and 100 cm

Extras are welcome, but they don't replace the criteria: whatever the beep does, it still has to start at exactly 100 cm and turn steady below 30.

:::


:::keep-together

**Now two ideas of your own. For each one, say which criterion in the brief it's strongest on and which it's weakest on. Then say which you'll build, and why.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** One way to write it: Idea 1: one beep rate from 30 to 100 cm. Strongest on the tests: three bands with clear edges. Weakest on use: it doesn't tell you how close you're getting inside the band. Idea 2: the beep speeds up as the distance drops. Strongest on use. Weakest on testing: the edges are harder to hear when the rate keeps changing. I'll build idea 1 first, pass every test, then add idea 2 as an improvement and run the same tests again.
