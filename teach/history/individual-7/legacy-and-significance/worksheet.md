# What lasted, and how was he remembered?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** His dynasty lasted about fifteen years. The way of running China that he set up lasted about two thousand. How can both be true?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Describe what ended with the Qin and what the Han and later dynasties kept, compare how his inscriptions, Han writers and historians today saw him, and explain that significant means important to history, not admirable.

**Students should be able to say:** I can describe one thing the First Emperor set up that lasted after his dynasty fell. I can explain why his inscriptions and Han writers saw him so differently. I can explain that a person can be significant without being good.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

A. Many historians today, weighing both sides

**Answer:** Brought peace, order and one set of laws → C. His own inscriptions, made to show his power; A harsh ruler whose cruelty brought his dynasty down → B. Han writers, working for the dynasty that replaced his; Built a lasting system, at a terrible cost → A. Many historians today, weighing both sides

### Find Nib's mistake

**Nib is writing about whether the First Emperor was significant (Nib's notes are made up for this lesson). Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Han writers said the First Emperor was harsh and cruel.
 2  Many labourers suffered building his walls, roads and tomb.
 3  Nib says: "A cruel ruler like that can't be significant."
 4  Nib says: "So his system lasting for centuries doesn't count."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Nib thinks a cruel ruler cannot be significant. Significant means important to history, not admirable. His harsh rule and his lasting system both changed many lives, so he is significant whatever we think of him.

:::


:::keep-together

### Why so harsh?

**Why might Han writers have described the First Emperor so harshly?**

☐ Because they had all met him and disliked him in person at court

☐ They worked for the Han, so a cruel Qin made the Han look better

☐ There was no reason at all; they were simply recording the facts

☐ Because his own stone inscriptions said that he was cruel to people

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** They worked for the Han, so a cruel Qin made the Han look better

Who wrote it, and when, shapes how a person is remembered. That is why historians compare views.

:::

## Legacy and significance


:::keep-together

### Why significant?

**Which statement best explains the First Emperor's significance?**

☐ His empire changed millions of lives, his system lasted, and people still argue about him

☐ He is significant because he alone made all of China's later history

☐ His dynasty only lasted fifteen years, so he is not very significant

☐ He was a good and wise ruler, so he must be significant to history

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** His empire changed millions of lives, his system lasted, and people still argue about him

Use the tests: many lives, lasting effects, still argued about.

:::


:::keep-together

### Good or significant?

**Is each sentence a judgement about whether he was good, or about whether he was significant?**

Groups: Good or bad?, Significant?

- He was cruel to the people who worked for him: {{field-15}}
- His system of officials lasted for centuries: {{field-15}}
- He deserves praise for uniting the kingdoms: {{field-15}}
- Writers still disagree about him: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** He was cruel to the people who worked for him → Good or bad?; His system of officials lasted for centuries → Significant?; He deserves praise for uniting the kingdoms → Good or bad?; Writers still disagree about him → Significant?
