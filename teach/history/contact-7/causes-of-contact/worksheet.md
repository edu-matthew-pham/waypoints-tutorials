# Why did societies meet and fight?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** In 138 BCE an emperor sent about a hundred men walking west into lands no one at his court had mapped. Why would he do that?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Describe the causes of the Han–Xiongnu conflict, Zhang Qian's mission and the war for Ferghana's horses, sort them into power, land, security, and trade and resources, and explain how a cause shaped the contact that followed.

**Students should be able to say:** I can describe at least two causes of a contact or conflict. I can sort causes into kinds, such as power, land, security and trade. I can explain how one cause shaped what happened next.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

**The Han and the Xiongnu clashed, made peace and clashed again for well over a hundred years. What do you think kept them in contact for so long?**

☐ The Xiongnu wanted to rule all of China themselves

☐ Fighting was just what people did in ancient times

☐ Several reasons at once: raids, horses, goods and power

☐ The Han wanted to take the Xiongnu's grassland for farms

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** Several reasons at once. Raids (as Han writers tell it) made security a cause, the Han wanted horses and the Xiongnu wanted Han goods, and each ruler wanted to be the stronger one.

### Find Nib's mistake

**Nib is explaining why the Han fought the Xiongnu (Nib's notes are made up for this lesson). Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  The Han and the Xiongnu clashed again and again for more than a hundred years.
 2  Xiongnu horsemen raided Han farms, and the Han wanted Xiongnu horses.
 3  Nib says: "The Han fought the Xiongnu because they wanted more land. That's the reason."
 4  Nib says: "So the horses and the raids are not really part of the story."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Nib gives one cause, land, and stops. The Han also wanted to stop the raids (security), wanted horses (resources), and Emperor Wu wanted to be the stronger ruler (power). One cause on its own cannot explain why the conflict went on so long, or why the Han wanted horses and allies.

:::


:::keep-together

### How a cause shapes events

**The Han wanted protection from Xiongnu horsemen. How did that cause shape what Emperor Wu did next?**

☐ It changed nothing, because the Han already had enough horses

☐ He gave up on the north and moved the capital far to the south

☐ He looked for allies against the Xiongnu, which is why he sent Zhang Qian west

☐ He asked the Xiongnu to protect the Han from other raiders

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** He looked for allies against the Xiongnu, which is why he sent Zhang Qian west

A security cause led to a search for allies. The news about Ferghana's horses came only when Zhang Qian returned, and it led to a new contact. Causes do not just sit in a list: they shape the contact that follows.

:::

## Causes and kinds


:::keep-together

### The strongest explanation

**Which explanation of the war for Ferghana's horses is strongest?**

☐ Ferghana attacked the Han first, so the Han had to fight back

☐ The Han wanted horses, so that is the whole reason for the war

☐ The Han wanted horses, and Emperor Wu wanted to show his power

☐ The Han wanted Ferghana's farmland, as in every ancient war

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The Han wanted horses, and Emperor Wu wanted to show his power

The strongest explanation names more than one cause, of different kinds.

:::


:::keep-together

### Cause or result?

**Is each one a cause of the Han–Xiongnu conflict, or something that happened because of it?**

Groups: Cause, Result

- Xiongnu horsemen raided Han farms: {{field-15}}
- Han armies brought horses back from Ferghana: {{field-15}}
- The Han wanted bigger, stronger horses: {{field-15}}
- Zhang Qian brought home news of lands to the west: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Xiongnu horsemen raided Han farms → Cause; Han armies brought horses back from Ferghana → Result; The Han wanted bigger, stronger horses → Cause; Zhang Qian brought home news of lands to the west → Result
