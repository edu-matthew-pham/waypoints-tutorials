# Why was it made, and what was going on?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** The First Emperor had words cut into stone on mountains. Why would a ruler do that? What was going on that made it worth the trouble?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students learn the next two questions to ask of any source: context (what was going on when it was made, not just the place) and purpose (why it was made). The second hinge lesson on this skill: students who answer 'ancient China' for context and 'it shows the emperor' for purpose cannot combine the four questions in the next lesson.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what was going on when a source was made, beyond its place and period. I can say why a source was made, without mixing that up with what it shows. I can write a context and a purpose for a source in my own words.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Predict


:::keep-together

**Which is the best description of the context of the inscription in Source C?**

☐ It is made of stone

☐ It praises the emperor

☐ The Qin had just joined six rival kingdoms into one empire

☐ It was made in ancient China

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** The Qin had just joined six rival kingdoms into one empire. That is what was going on. 'Ancient China' is only the setting, stone is how it was made, and praise is what it says.

:::

## Worked example: Source C


:::keep-together

### Find the context

**Which context explains why the First Emperor might want an inscription like Source C?**

☐ He liked carving stone

☐ China is a big country

☐ Stone lasts a long time

☐ He had just joined rival kingdoms into one empire and wanted people to accept his rule

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** He had just joined rival kingdoms into one empire and wanted people to accept his rule

He had just united rival kingdoms. Many people in them had been Qin's enemies, so he had reason to tell them the new empire was good and here to stay.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**Nib is working out the context and purpose of Source C. Click the line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Nib is looking at the stone inscription (Source C).
 2  Nib asks "What was going on when it was made?" and writes: "Ancient China."
 3  Nib asks "Why was it made?" and writes: "To show the emperor's power."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: Nib has written 'ancient China'. That is the setting, not the context. The context is what was going on: the Qin had just defeated rival kingdoms and joined them into one empire.

:::


:::keep-together

### Say the purpose

**Which is the best statement of the purpose of Source C?**

☐ To show the emperor

☐ To show the emperor's power and win people's support for his rule

☐ To be made of stone

☐ To say the emperor brought peace

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** To show the emperor's power and win people's support for his rule

Purpose is what the maker wanted it to do. Showing power and winning support is a reason. 'It shows the emperor' only says what is in it.

:::

## Both questions


:::keep-together

### The tomb warriors

**The clay warriors were made for the First Emperor's tomb. Which is the best purpose?**

☐ To be in a museum

☐ To be found by farmers

☐ To guard the emperor and honour him after his death

☐ To show soldiers in armour

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** To guard the emperor and honour him after his death

The tomb was built for the emperor. The army was made to guard and honour him.

:::


:::keep-together

### Ravi's answer

**Ravi says: 'The purpose of the inscription is that it shows the emperor.' What is the best reply?**

☐ That is what it shows. The purpose is why it was made

☐ That is right, it shows the emperor

☐ Inscriptions have no purpose

☐ The purpose is to be made of stone

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** That is what it shows. The purpose is why it was made

What a source shows is its content. Its purpose is what the maker wanted it to do.
