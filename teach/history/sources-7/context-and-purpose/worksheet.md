# Why was it made, and what was going on?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** The First Emperor had words cut into stone on mountains. Why would a ruler do that? What was going on that made it worth the trouble?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students learn the next two questions to ask of any source: context (what was going on when it was made, not just the place) and purpose (why it was made). The second hinge lesson on this skill: students who answer 'ancient China' for context and 'it shows the emperor' for purpose cannot combine the four questions in the next lesson.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what was going on when a source was made, beyond its place and period. I can say why a source was made, without mixing that up with what it shows. I can write a context and a purpose for a source in my own words.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Predict


:::keep-together

**Which is the best description of the context of the inscription in Source C?**

☐ It was carved into stone on a mountain

☐ It was made in ancient China, in Qin times

☐ The Qin had just joined six rival kingdoms into one

☐ It praises the emperor as a ruler without equal

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** The Qin had just joined six rival kingdoms into one empire. That is what was going on. 'Ancient China, in Qin times' is only the setting, stone on a mountain is how and where it was made, and the praise is what it says.

:::

## Worked example: Source C


:::keep-together

### Find the context

**Which context explains why the First Emperor might want an inscription like Source C?**

☐ The inscription says he had brought peace and order to everyone

☐ He was travelling on a tour of the mountains when it was carved

☐ He had just joined six rival kingdoms, many of them old enemies, into one empire

☐ It was set up in ancient China, during the time of the Qin emperors

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** He had just joined six rival kingdoms, many of them old enemies, into one empire

That is what was going on: a new empire full of people who had been Qin's enemies. Why he made the stone (its purpose) comes next, and this context helps explain it.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**Nib is working out the context and purpose of Source C. Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Nib is looking at the stone inscription (Source C).
 2  Nib asks "What was going on when it was made?" and writes: "Ancient China."
 3  Nib asks "Why was it made?" and writes: "To show the emperor's power."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: Nib has written 'ancient China'. That is the setting, not the context. The context is what was going on: the Qin had just defeated rival kingdoms and joined them into one empire.

:::


:::keep-together

### Say the purpose

**Which is the best statement of the likely purpose of Source C?**

☐ Probably to show the emperor and the laws he made for everyone

☐ Probably to show the emperor's power and win people's support for his rule

☐ To record the date of the emperor's tour of the mountains

☐ To say that the emperor brought peace and order

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Probably to show the emperor's power and win people's support for his rule

Purpose is what the maker wanted it to do. No one wrote down the purpose, but historians think showing power and winning support is the likely reason. 'It shows the emperor' only says what is in it.

:::

## Both questions


:::keep-together

### The tomb warriors

**The clay warriors were made for the First Emperor's tomb. No Qin text says why. Which is the best statement of their purpose?**

☐ As practice pieces for craftsmen learning to work clay

☐ Probably to guard and serve the emperor after his death

☐ To show soldiers in armour with bronze weapons

☐ Probably to show the Qin army to visitors to the tomb

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Probably to guard and serve the emperor after his death

The tomb was built for the emperor. Most historians think the army was made to guard or serve him after death, reasoning from where it was placed and what it shows. That is an inference, not something a Qin source tells us.

:::


:::keep-together

### Ravi's answer

**Ravi says: 'The purpose of the inscription is that it shows the emperor.' What is the best reply?**

☐ That is what it shows. The purpose is why it was made

☐ That is right: what it shows is its purpose

☐ Nearly: its purpose is that it shows the emperor's laws

☐ Nearly: its purpose is that it is on a mountain

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** That is what it shows. The purpose is why it was made

What a source shows is its content. Its purpose is what the maker wanted it to do.
