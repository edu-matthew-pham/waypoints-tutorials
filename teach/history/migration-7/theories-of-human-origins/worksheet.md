# What is a theory of human origins?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Someone says, 'Out of Africa is just a theory.' Is that a reason to doubt it? It depends what they mean by theory.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Identify the main theories about where Homo sapiens began, describe what the Out of Africa theory proposes, and use the word 'theory' the way historians and scientists do.

**Students should be able to say:** I can describe what the Out of Africa theory says. I can explain what 'theory' means in history and science. I can say how a new find can refine a theory without throwing it out.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: Out of Africa


:::keep-together

### What does it say?

**What does the Out of Africa theory propose?**

☐ No kind of human ever lived outside Africa until our species did

☐ People came from Africa only in the last 10,000 years

☐ Our species first evolved in Africa and later spread to the rest of the world

☐ Our species evolved separately on every continent at the same time

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Our species first evolved in Africa and later spread to the rest of the world

Out of Africa is about where Homo sapiens began and how it spread. Other kinds of humans had travelled before.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**In a made-up classroom discussion, Nib is answering a question about Out of Africa. Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Question: why do scientists think our species began in Africa?
 2  The oldest Homo sapiens fossils have been found in Africa.
 3  Nib says: "But Out of Africa is only a theory, so it's really just a guess."
 4  Nib says: "So my guess, that people came from somewhere else, is just as good."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: Nib uses 'theory' in its everyday sense, a hunch. A theory in history and science is built on evidence, so a guess with no evidence is not as good as it.

:::


:::keep-together

### Why accept it?

**Why do most scientists accept the Out of Africa theory today?**

☐ A famous scientist suggested it, so everyone agreed

☐ Nobody has ever found any human fossils outside Africa

☐ Separate kinds of evidence, like fossils and DNA, agree

☐ It is the oldest theory, so it is most likely right

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Separate kinds of evidence, like fossils and DNA, agree

When separate kinds of evidence agree, an explanation is much stronger.

:::

## Vary: a new find changes the date

Scientists used to think our species first left Africa about 100,000 years ago. Then, in Misliya Cave in Israel, archaeologists found part of a modern human jawbone dated to about 177,000 to 194,000 years ago (Tel Aviv University reported it in 2018).

Work in the tutorial. Plan first: what will you change, and how will you test it?
