## Why it matters

> Someone says, 'Out of Africa is just a theory.' Is that a reason to doubt it? It depends what they mean by theory.

<!-- Speaker note: Nobody wrote anything down when the first people of our species lived, so historians and scientists work from what has survived: fossils (bones and teeth), stone tools, the layers they were found in, and the DNA of people living now and long ago. From that evidence they build **theories**. In everyday talk, a theory can mean a hunch ("I have a theory about who ate my lunch"). In history and science, a **theory** is an explanation built on evidence, tested against new finds, and changed when the evidence calls for it. The main theories about human origins: - **The theory of evolution** explains how living things, people included, change over very many generations. It is the starting point for the others. - **The Out of Africa theory** says our species, *Homo sapiens*, first evolved in Africa, about 300,000 years ago, and later spread to the rest of the world. Most scientists accept it today because fossils and DNA point the same way. - **The multiregional theory**, an older idea, said modern humans developed in several parts of the world at once from earlier kinds of humans already living there. Few scientists hold it in that form now, but one part of the debate lives on: when our species spread out, it met other kinds of humans, such as Neanderthals, and some interbred with them, so a little of their DNA survives in many people today. -->

## What does it say?

**What does the Out of Africa theory propose?**

- No kind of human ever lived outside Africa until our species did
- People came from Africa only in the last 10,000 years
- Our species first evolved in Africa and later spread to the rest of the world
- Our species evolved separately on every continent at the same time

* Answer: Our species first evolved in Africa and later spread to the rest of the world

<!-- Speaker note: Out of Africa is about where Homo sapiens began and how it spread. Other kinds of humans had travelled before. -->

## Why accept it?

**Why do most scientists accept the Out of Africa theory today?**

- A famous scientist suggested it, so everyone agreed
- Nobody has ever found any human fossils outside Africa
- Separate kinds of evidence, like fossils and DNA, agree
- It is the oldest theory, so it is most likely right

* Answer: Separate kinds of evidence, like fossils and DNA, agree

<!-- Speaker note: When separate kinds of evidence agree, an explanation is much stronger. -->

## Vary: a new find changes the date

Scientists used to think our species first left Africa about 100,000 years ago. Then, in Misliya Cave in Israel, archaeologists found part of a modern human jawbone dated to about 177,000 to 194,000 years ago (Tel Aviv University reported it in 2018).
