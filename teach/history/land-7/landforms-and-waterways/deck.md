Shaped by the land · Where it grew

## Today

**We are learning to** describe the landforms, waterways, climate and resources of ancient China's heartland and explain why people could settle there.

- I can name the main landforms, rivers and climate of the place an ancient society grew.
- I can say what each feature gave people, or how it got in their way.
- I can explain why people could settle in a place, using its geography.

> Look at a map of China today and most people live in the east, on the plains and along the rivers. More than 2,000 years ago, it was much the same. Why there, and not out in the desert?

## Why there?

**Historians often point out that the Wei River valley was a strong base for the state of Qin. Which reason uses the geography best?**

- It was close to the sea, so the Qin could trade by ship
- It was the warmest and wettest part of China, so rice grew easily
- It was in the middle of the Gobi Desert, far from any enemies
- It had river water, good farmland and mountains that slowed attackers

* Answer: It had river water, good farmland and mountains that slowed attackers

<!-- Speaker note: Farmland and water fed the people, and the mountains around the valley helped protect them. Both are reasons the place worked. -->
