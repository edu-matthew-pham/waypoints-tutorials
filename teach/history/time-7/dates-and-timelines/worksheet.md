# BCE, CE and putting events in order

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Confucius died in 479 BCE. The Qin unified China in 221 BCE. Could Confucius have met the First Emperor?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Use BC/AD, BCE/CE and BP correctly, work out spans of time (including across the BCE/CE line, where there is no year 0), and sequence events on a timeline.

### Find Nib's mistake

**Click the first line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  The Qin unified China in 221 BCE.
 2  Confucius died in 479 BCE.
 3  Nib says: "479 is bigger than 221, so Confucius died after the Qin unified China."
 4  Nib says: "So Confucius could have taught the First Emperor."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: in BCE, a bigger number is earlier, not later. 479 BCE was 258 years before 221 BCE, so Confucius died long before the Qin unified China.

:::


:::keep-together

### BC or BCE?

**Match each date to the same year written the other way.**

A. 479 BCE

B. 105 CE

C. 9 CE

D. 221 BCE

{{rows 1}}

| Part | Letter |
|---|---|
| `221 BC` | |
| `AD 9` | |

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 221 BC → D. 221 BCE; AD 9 → C. 9 CE; 479 BC → A. 479 BCE; AD 105 → B. 105 CE

BC and BCE use the same numbers; so do AD and CE. Only the letters change, and AD usually goes before the number.

## Vary: across the line

Back to the hook: 479 BCE is **before** 221 BCE, by 479 − 221 = 258 years. Confucius died more than 250 years before the Qin unified China, so he could never have met the First Emperor. His ideas, though, were still being taught and argued about.

The trap gets harder when a span crosses from BCE into CE.

Work in the tutorial. Plan first: what will you change, and how will you test it?

## Vary: BP, years before 1950

For times long before writing, scientists and archaeologists often write **BP**, *Before Present*. "Present" is fixed at the year **1950**, so the number never changes as years go by. 3,000 BP means about 3,000 years before 1950, which is about 1050 BCE. BP is used mostly for dates found by radiocarbon dating and for deep time, such as the first farming of millet in northern China, about 7,800 years ago.

## Make: Typical: gaps in the timeline

Timelines often have gaps: long stretches where we know very little.

ACARA's curriculum defines **history** as the period that begins when there are named people and written records; the time before is sometimes called *prehistory*. That is a convention. Many people point out that groups without writing still passed on their past in other ways, so "no writing" does not mean "no history".
