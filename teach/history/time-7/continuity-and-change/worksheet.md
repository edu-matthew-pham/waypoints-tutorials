# Qin to Han, what changed and what stayed?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** By 206 BCE the Qin dynasty had fallen, and soon a new family took the throne. Did everything start again?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Explain continuities and changes across the Qin to Han turning point, using sources put in date order, and recognise that continuity and change coexist.

**Students should be able to say:** I can name something that changed and something that stayed the same across a turning point. I can back each one up with a dated source or event. I can explain why both can be true of the same period.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

:::teacher-note

**What happens:** Some things changed (the ruling family, and later the ideas used to choose officials), but much stayed: officials running commanderies, the written script, standard measures, the empire's borders, and most people's lives as farmers.

## Worked example: dated sources in order

Historians back up continuity and change with **dated evidence**, put in order. Here are three kinds, described in our own words.

| Date | Source | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| before 217 BCE | Qin legal texts on bamboo slips, found at **Shuihudi** | Detailed Qin rules for officials: grain stores, labour, punishments |
| about 186 BCE | Han legal texts on bamboo slips, found at **Zhangjiashan** | Han laws; historians report that many follow Qin rules, with changes |
| about 124 BCE | Records of Emperor Wu's academy for training officials in Confucian learning | A new way of choosing and training officials |

Put in order, the sources show **continuity** (Han laws built on Qin ones) and then a **change** (a new idea about what officials should know). Both are true of the same period.

## Both at once


:::keep-together

**Which sentence best describes the change from Qin to Han?**

☐ The Han threw out everything the Qin had built and began again

☐ A new family ruled, but it kept much of the Qin way of running the empire

☐ Nothing changed, because the Han and Qin were the same dynasty

☐ Everything changed at once in 206 BCE and then stayed the same

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A new family ruled, but it kept much of the Qin way of running the empire

Historians describe periods as a mix: some things continue while others change.

:::
