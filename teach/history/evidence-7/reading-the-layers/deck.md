## Check Nib’s claim

**Nib writes: “B is deeper than C, so B is exactly 500 years older.” What needs changing?**

- Keep the order but remove the exact time gap
- Reverse the order because deeper means newer
- Keep the time gap because three layers prove it

* Answer: Keep the order but remove the exact time gap

## A fresh case

**At a second made-up, undisturbed site, a floor lies below flood mud and a rebuilt wall lies above it. Which account is supported?**

- The wall proves why the flood happened
- A period of use was followed by flooding and rebuilding
- Flooding came before any floor existed

* Answer: A period of use was followed by flooding and rebuilding
