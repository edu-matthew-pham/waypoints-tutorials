## Today

**We are learning to** describe what ancient DNA comparison and radiocarbon dating can each tell us, and read a date range as an estimate.

- I can describe one thing ancient DNA comparison can reveal.
- I can distinguish a biological relationship from a dating estimate.

## Why it matters

> One report says two ancient people were close biological relatives. Another gives an estimated age range for charcoal. Have the labs answered the same question?

<!-- Speaker note: **DNA** is the information passed down from parents to children in the body. Sometimes DNA survives in ancient bones or teeth. Scientists can compare DNA from two people to see whether they were likely to be related. DNA does not tell you a person's name, or what they believed. **Radiocarbon dating** estimates when something that was once alive (wood, charcoal, bone, seeds) died. It cannot date stone, so it cannot date a stone wall directly. The answer always comes as a range, such as "between 300 and 200 BCE", because there is uncertainty in the measurement. The lab dates the sample. Whether that sample tells you when an event happened depends on where it was found. These two reports are **made up** for this lesson. They are not about real people or a real lab. - **Report A:** DNA from two ancient people suggests they were close biological relatives. - **Report B:** a piece of charcoal most likely dates to between 300 and 200 BCE. -->

## Which evidence?

**You want to find out whether two ancient people were biological relatives. Which is the best evidence to start with?**

- How deep each was buried, compared with the other
- Similar pots buried with each of them in their graves
- A comparison of their DNA, plus the dig records

* Answer: A comparison of their DNA, plus the dig records

## Exit ticket

**What could DNA comparison tell us about two ancient individuals, and what could it not tell us by itself?**

* It could support an inference about biological relationship. It would not by itself give their names, beliefs or an exact event date.
