# DNA and dating answer different questions

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** One report says two ancient people were close biological relatives. Another gives an estimated age range for charcoal. Have the labs answered the same question?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Describe how ancient DNA can reveal biological relationships. Distinguish that question from dating organic material, and read a date range as an estimate rather than an exact year.

**Students should be able to say:** I can describe one thing ancient DNA comparison can reveal. I can distinguish a biological relationship from a dating estimate.

## Worked example: follow the reasoning

Report A adds information that layer depth cannot give: a biological relationship. Report B gives a time estimate. Read the whole range, not just its middle. Neither report proves why people lived together or whether a charcoal sample belongs to the event you are investigating.

:::keep-together

### What follows from the evidence?

**Which conclusion combines the two reports carefully?**

☐ The people were certainly named in a royal family tree

☐ The DNA gives an exact date for the charcoal

☐ There is evidence of a biological relationship and a separate estimated sample date

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** There is evidence of a biological relationship and a separate estimated sample date

The reports contribute different evidence. Their connection to the settlement still depends on the find records.

:::


:::keep-together

### Check Nib’s claim

**Nib records Report B as “the settlement was built in exactly 250 BCE”. What is the problem?**

☐ Charcoal cannot ever be dated

☐ The range should be read backwards

☐ The range is an estimate for a sample, not an exact construction date

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The range is an estimate for a sample, not an exact construction date

250 BCE is inside the range, but the report does not select that year or directly date construction.

## A fresh case


:::keep-together

**You want to investigate whether two ancient individuals were biological relatives. Which is the best starting evidence?**

☐ Similar pots alone

☐ The depth of their finds alone

☐ A comparison of surviving DNA, alongside the context records

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A comparison of surviving DNA, alongside the context records

DNA comparison can test biological relationship; the archaeological records help interpret the result.
