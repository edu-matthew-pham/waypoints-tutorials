# Earth spins, Earth orbits

Before Year 7 science · Forces and the Earth

<!-- Speaker note: Students separate the Earth's daily spin (day and night) from its yearly orbit round the sun, and drop the idea that going round the sun gives day and night. About 17 minutes (Think first 3 min · Try 4 min · The idea 5 min · Check 3 min · Apply 2 min). -->

---

## Today

- I can say how long the Earth takes to spin and to orbit the sun.
- I can say what gives day and night.
- I can say what the Earth's orbit gives us.

## Why it matters

> It is day in Sydney when it is night in London. What is going on?

<!-- Speaker note: The Earth does two things at once: it **spins** and it **goes round the sun**. They take different amounts of time and they cause different things. Mixing them up is the most common mistake in this topic. -->

- The Earth spinning on its axis once a day
- The sun moving round the Earth

## Spin and orbit

**Match each movement to how long it takes.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `The Earth spins once on its axis` | A. About one month |
| `The Earth goes once round the sun` | B. About one year |
| `The moon goes once round the Earth` | C. About one day |

* Answer: The Earth spins once on its axis → C. About one day; The Earth goes once round the sun → B. About one year; The moon goes once round the Earth → A. About one month

<!-- Speaker note: The spin takes a day, the trip round the sun takes a year, and the moon goes round the Earth in about a month. -->

## The other side of the Earth

**It is midday in Canberra. What is it like on the other side of the Earth?**

- Day, because the sun lights the whole Earth
- Dusk everywhere
- Nobody can tell
- Night, because that side faces away from the sun

* Answer: Night, because that side faces away from the sun

<!-- Speaker note: The sun lights half the Earth at a time. The other half is in the dark, which is night. -->

## A year

**How long does it take the Earth to go once round the sun?**

- About one year
- About one week
- About one month
- About one day

* Answer: About one year

<!-- Speaker note: One trip round the sun is one year. -->

## A claim about night

**Someone says: 'We have night because the Earth goes round the sun.' What would you say?**

- The sun goes round the Earth
- Night happens because clouds block the sun
- That's right
- It's the spin that gives night. Going round the sun takes a year

* Answer: It's the spin that gives night. Going round the sun takes a year

## Spin or orbit

**Does it come from the spin (a day) or the orbit (a year)?**

Groups: The spin · The orbit

Day and night · A whole year going by · The sun seeming to rise in the east · The same seasons coming back every year

* Answer: Day and night → The spin; A whole year going by → The orbit; The sun seeming to rise in the east → The spin; The same seasons coming back every year → The orbit

## Exit ticket

**What gives us a day, and what gives us a year?**

* The Earth's spin gives a day. The Earth's trip round the sun gives a year.
