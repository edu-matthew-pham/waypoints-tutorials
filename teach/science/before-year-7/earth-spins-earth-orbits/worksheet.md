# Earth spins, Earth orbits

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** It is day in Sydney when it is night in London. What is going on?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students separate the Earth's daily spin (day and night) from its yearly orbit round the sun, and drop the idea that going round the sun gives day and night.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say how long the Earth takes to spin and to orbit the sun. I can say what gives day and night. I can say what the Earth's orbit gives us.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea

The Earth **spins** (rotates) on its axis once a day. That is what gives us **day and night**. The Earth also goes round the sun, which is its **orbit**, once a year. Gravity from the sun keeps it on this path.

:::keep-together

### Spin and orbit

**Match each movement to how long it takes.**

A. About one month

B. About one year

C. About one day

{{columns 60% 40%}}

{{rows 1}}

| Part | Letter |
|---|---|
| `The Earth spins once on its axis` | |
| `The Earth goes once round the sun` | |
| `The moon goes once round the Earth` | |

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The Earth spins once on its axis → C. About one day; The Earth goes once round the sun → B. About one year; The moon goes once round the Earth → A. About one month

The spin takes a day, the trip round the sun takes a year, and the moon goes round the Earth in about a month.

:::


:::keep-together

### The other side of the Earth

**It is midday in Canberra. What is it like on the other side of the Earth?**

☐ Day, because the sun lights the whole Earth

☐ Dusk everywhere

☐ Nobody can tell

☐ Night, because that side faces away from the sun

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Night, because that side faces away from the sun

The sun lights half the Earth at a time. The other half is in the dark, which is night.

:::


:::keep-together

### A year

**How long does it take the Earth to go once round the sun?**

☐ About one year

☐ About one week

☐ About one month

☐ About one day

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** About one year

One trip round the sun is one year.

:::
