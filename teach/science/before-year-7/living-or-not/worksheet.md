# Living or not?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A flame moves, grows and uses up fuel. So why isn't it alive?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students decide whether things are living, once living or non-living by checking a short list of what living things do, and drop the idea that moving means alive.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what a living thing does. I can sort things into living, once living and non-living. I can say why a flame or a car isn't alive.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Think first

Lock in a guess. A wrong guess is fine, because the next step shows what to look for.

:::keep-together

**A candle flame moves, grows bigger when it gets more air, and uses up the wax. Is it alive?**

☐ No, because it doesn't do everything that living things do

☐ Yes, because it moves and grows

☐ Yes, because it needs air

☐ You can't tell

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** No. A flame moves, grows and needs air, but it doesn't have young or react to its surroundings the way a living thing does. To be alive, a thing needs to do all of them together.

:::


## The idea

A living thing needs **food or energy**, **grows**, **has young** and **reacts to what is around it**. It does all of these together. Things that are **once living** (wood, paper, a dry leaf) came from something alive. Things that are **non-living** (a rock, a car, a flame) were never alive.

Now try the three questions below.

:::keep-together

### A car

**A car moves along, uses fuel and makes noise. Is it alive?**

☐ Yes: it uses fuel like food

☐ Yes: it moves by itself

☐ Only when the engine is running

☐ No: it doesn't grow or have young

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** No: it doesn't grow or have young

A living thing needs food or energy, grows, has young and reacts to what is around it. A car does only one of those.

:::


:::keep-together

### A seed

**A dry seed sits still on a shelf. It doesn't seem to be doing anything. Is it alive?**

☐ Yes, but only after it has grown

☐ No: it isn't moving

☐ No: it doesn't eat

☐ Yes: it can grow when it gets water and warmth

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Yes: it can grow when it gets water and warmth

A seed is alive but waiting. It is a living thing that can grow when it gets water and warmth.

:::


:::keep-together

### A wooden spoon

**A wooden spoon was made from a tree. Which group is it in?**

☐ Living: it's made of tree

☐ Once living: it came from a living thing but is not alive now

☐ Non-living: it never had anything to do with living things

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Once living: it came from a living thing but is not alive now

Things like wood, paper and dry leaves are called once living. They come from living things but are not alive now.

:::
