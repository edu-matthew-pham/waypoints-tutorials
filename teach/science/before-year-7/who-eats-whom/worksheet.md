# Who eats whom? A first look

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Grass doesn't eat anything, but a kangaroo eats grass. So where does the grass get its energy?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students build a short food chain, name the producer, and say which way the arrows point and why.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what a producer and a consumer are. I can draw a food chain with the arrows the right way round. I can say what an arrow in a food chain means.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea

Every food chain starts with a **producer**: a plant that makes its own food from sunlight. A **consumer** is an animal that eats other living things. Read the arrows as "gives energy to": the arrow goes from the food to the one that eats it.

:::keep-together

### Where a chain starts

**Which one makes its own food, so it starts a food chain?**

☐ Lizard

☐ Kookaburra

☐ Grasshopper

☐ Grass

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Grass

Grass is a producer. It makes its own food using sunlight, and the rest of the chain gets its energy from it.

:::


:::keep-together

### Reading an arrow

**In the chain grass → grasshopper → lizard → kookaburra, what does the arrow from lizard to kookaburra mean?**

☐ The lizard is bigger than the kookaburra

☐ The lizard eats the kookaburra

☐ The kookaburra and lizard are friends

☐ The kookaburra eats the lizard, and energy goes to the kookaburra

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The kookaburra eats the lizard, and energy goes to the kookaburra

An arrow goes from the food to the eater: the kookaburra eats the lizard, so the energy moves from the lizard to the kookaburra.

:::


## Check


:::keep-together

### Which way?

**Which food chain has its arrows the right way round?**

☐ Seaweed → fish → seal

☐ Seal → fish → seaweed

☐ Fish → seaweed → seal

☐ Seaweed → seal → fish

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Seaweed → fish → seal

Seaweed is the producer, a fish eats it, and a seal eats the fish. Each arrow goes from the food to the eater.

:::


:::keep-together
