# Transfer or transformation?

Year 8 Physics · Energy transfer and transformation

<!-- Speaker note: Students distinguish energy transfer (same type, new place) from transformation (type changes), describe common transformations, and identify by-product heat (AC9S8U05, nodes 3 and 4; node 4 is a hinge). ## Today

**We are learning to** in a transfer, the same type of energy moves from one place to another (heat from a hot drink into your hands). In a transformation, energy changes type (chemical energy in a battery becomes electrical, then light). Almost every transformation also makes some heat that isn't wanted: a by-product.

- I can tell an energy transfer from a transformation.
- I can describe the transformations in a torch or a speaker.
- I can name the by-product heat in a transformation.

## Transfer or transformation

**Transfer (same type, new place) or transformation (type changes)?**

Groups: Transfer · Transformation

Heat from a hot pan into the handle · A moving cue ball hitting another ball and making it move · Sound travelling from a speaker to your ear · A battery lighting a bulb · A ball falling from a shelf · Burning wood

* Answer: Heat from a hot pan into the handle → Transfer; A moving cue ball hitting another ball and making it move → Transfer; Sound travelling from a speaker to your ear → Transfer; A battery lighting a bulb → Transformation; A ball falling from a shelf → Transformation; Burning wood → Transformation

## Not destroyed

**Is the by-product heat from a bulb destroyed?**

- Yes: by-products are destroyed
- No: it spreads into the room, where it is hard to use
- It turns back into electricity

* Answer: No: it spreads into the room, where it is hard to use

## Drawing it back

**Why does a bow need to be drawn back before the arrow can fly?**

- To aim it
- To warm it up
- Drawing it stores elastic potential energy, which becomes the arrow's kinetic energy

* Answer: Drawing it stores elastic potential energy, which becomes the arrow's kinetic energy

<!-- Speaker note: The archer's chemical energy is stored as elastic potential in the bow, then released as the arrow's kinetic energy. -->

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Transfer**: the same type of energy moves to a new place (heat into your hands, sound to your ear).
- **Transformation**: energy changes type (chemical → electrical → light in a torch).
- Almost every transformation also makes **by-product** heat (and sometimes sound) that isn't the useful output.
- By-product energy isn't destroyed: it spreads into the surroundings.

## Exit ticket

**A wind-up toy runs across the floor. Is 'spring to moving wheels' a transfer or a transformation? Name a by-product.**

* A transformation (elastic potential to kinetic). By-products: heat (friction) and sound.
