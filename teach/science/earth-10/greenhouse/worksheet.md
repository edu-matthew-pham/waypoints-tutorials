# How do greenhouse gases warm the Earth?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** The moon is the same distance from the sun as Earth, but its average surface temperature is far below freezing. Earth's thin blanket of gases makes the difference.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students explain how greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit outgoing infrared, distinguish the natural and enhanced greenhouse effect, and locate the mechanism in the energy-balance model (AC9S10U04, node 3, a hinge node).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain that greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared from the surface. I can distinguish the natural and enhanced greenhouse effect. I can explain how more greenhouse gases upset the energy balance and warm the Earth.

**How do greenhouse gases warm the Earth?**

☐ They absorb infrared given off by Earth's surface and send some back down, slowing the energy going out

☐ They trap incoming sunlight

☐ They make the sun hotter

☐ They let more sunlight in through a hole in the ozone layer

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** They absorb outgoing infrared from the surface and send part of it back down, so energy leaves more slowly. Sunlight passes through them easily. The ozone hole is a different problem.

## Explain: natural and enhanced


:::keep-together

### Which flow

**Greenhouse gases slow which flow of energy?**

☐ Heat from Earth's core

☐ Wind

☐ Infrared going out from Earth to space

☐ Sunlight coming in

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Infrared going out from Earth to space

:::


:::keep-together

### Natural or enhanced

**Natural greenhouse effect or enhanced greenhouse effect?**

Groups: Natural, Enhanced

- Keeps Earth about 33 °C warmer than it would be with no greenhouse gases: {{field-15}}
- Extra CO₂ from burning fossil fuels: {{field-15}}
- Water vapour and CO₂ that were in the air before 1800: {{field-15}}
- Methane from farming and landfill adding to the air: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Keeps Earth about 33 °C warmer than it would be with no greenhouse gases → Natural; Extra CO₂ from burning fossil fuels → Enhanced; Water vapour and CO₂ that were in the air before 1800 → Natural; Methane from farming and landfill adding to the air → Enhanced

:::


:::keep-together

### Rise and level off

**When you raised CO₂, why did the temperature rise and then level off?**

☐ Less infrared escaped, so it warmed until out matched in again

☐ CO₂ is hot, so it heated the air

☐ More sunlight got through to the surface

☐ The ozone layer got thinner and let in heat

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Less infrared escaped, so it warmed until out matched in again

With less infrared escaping, energy in was more than energy out, so the surface warmed. The warmer surface then gave off more infrared until out equalled in again.

## Elaborate: methane


:::keep-together

**There is about 200 times less methane than CO₂ in the air, yet it causes about a quarter as much extra warming. Why?**

☐ Kilogram for kilogram, methane absorbs far more infrared than CO₂

☐ Methane molecules are hotter than CO₂ molecules

☐ Methane stays in the air far longer than CO₂

☐ Methane lets more sunlight into the atmosphere

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Kilogram for kilogram, methane absorbs far more infrared than CO₂

Over 20 years, a kilogram of methane traps about 80 times as much heat as a kilogram of CO₂, so a little methane goes a long way.

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- Earth's surface gives off **infrared**. **Greenhouse gases** (water vapour, CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide) absorb some and send part back down: they slow energy **going out**, not sunlight coming in.
- **Natural greenhouse effect:** keeps Earth about 15 °C on average instead of about −18 °C.
- **Enhanced greenhouse effect:** extra greenhouse gases from human activity reduce the outgoing infrared, so energy in exceeds energy out and Earth **warms** until balance is restored.
- It is not the ozone hole.
