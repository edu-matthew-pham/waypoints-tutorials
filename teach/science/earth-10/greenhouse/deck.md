# How do greenhouse gases warm the Earth?

Year 10 Earth and space · Earth's energy balance

<!-- Speaker note: Students explain how greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit outgoing infrared, distinguish the natural and enhanced greenhouse effect, and locate the mechanism in the energy-balance model (AC9S10U04, node 3, a hinge node). ## Today

**We are learning to** earth gives off infrared. Greenhouse gases (water vapour, CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide) absorb some of it and send part back down, slowing the energy going out. Without them Earth would average about −18 °C. Adding more upsets the balance, and Earth warms until it is restored.

- I can explain that greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared from the surface.
- I can distinguish the natural and enhanced greenhouse effect.
- I can explain how more greenhouse gases upset the energy balance and warm the Earth.

## Why it matters

> The moon is the same distance from the sun as Earth, but its average surface temperature is far below freezing. Earth's thin blanket of gases makes the difference.

## Elaborate: methane

**There is about 200 times less methane than CO₂ in the air, yet it causes about a quarter as much extra warming. Why?**

- Kilogram for kilogram, methane absorbs far more infrared than CO₂ (it traps about 80 times as much heat over 20 years)
- Methane lasts forever
- Methane lets more sunlight in
- Methane is hotter

* Answer: Kilogram for kilogram, methane absorbs far more infrared than CO₂ (it traps about 80 times as much heat over 20 years)

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- Earth's surface gives off **infrared**. **Greenhouse gases** (water vapour, CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide) absorb some and send part back down: they slow energy **going out**, not sunlight coming in.
- **Natural greenhouse effect:** keeps Earth about 15 °C on average instead of about −18 °C.
- **Enhanced greenhouse effect:** extra greenhouse gases from human activity reduce the outgoing infrared, so energy in exceeds energy out and Earth **warms** until balance is restored.
- It is not the ozone hole.
