## Smallest to largest

**Which list is in order from smallest to largest?**

- Galaxy, star, planet, universe, solar system
- Star, planet, galaxy, solar system, universe
- Universe, galaxy, solar system, star, planet
- Planet, star, solar system, galaxy, universe

* Answer: Planet, star, solar system, galaxy, universe

<!-- Speaker note: A planet is part of a solar system around a star; billions of stars make a galaxy; the universe holds billions of galaxies. (A star is larger than a planet.) -->

## Units

**A light-year is a unit of what?**

- Speed
- Time: one year
- Brightness
- Distance: how far light travels in a year

* Answer: Distance: how far light travels in a year

<!-- Speaker note: Light travels about 9.5 trillion kilometres in a year. The distance, not the time, is the unit. -->

## Which unit?

**Which unit suits the distance from Earth to the Sun?**

- Kilogram
- Astronomical unit (AU)
- Metre
- Light-year

* Answer: Astronomical unit (AU)

<!-- Speaker note: One AU is the average distance from Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km. Light-years are for distances between stars. -->

## The glow

**The cosmic microwave background is a faint radiation coming from every direction. What does it show?**

- Stars are cooling
- Earth is surrounded by a hot shell
- Space is full of hot gas
- The early universe was hot and dense, and has been cooling as it expanded

* Answer: The early universe was hot and dense, and has been cooling as it expanded

<!-- Speaker note: This radiation was released when the universe was young and hot. It has cooled and stretched into microwaves. -->

## What matter is made of

**About how much of the mass of ordinary matter in the universe is hydrogen and helium?**

- Mostly oxygen and carbon
- Mostly heavier elements, made in stars
- Almost all of it: by mass, about three-quarters hydrogen and one-quarter helium
- Equal parts of every element

* Answer: Almost all of it: by mass, about three-quarters hydrogen and one-quarter helium

<!-- Speaker note: The big bang model predicts roughly this mix from the first few minutes, and observations match it. -->

## Independent evidence

**Is it evidence for the big bang that does not depend on redshift?**

Groups: Independent evidence · Not independent

The cosmic microwave background · The proportions of hydrogen and helium · Distant galaxies moving away from us (redshift)

* Answer: The cosmic microwave background → Independent evidence; The proportions of hydrogen and helium → Independent evidence; Distant galaxies moving away from us (redshift) → Not independent

## Match the evidence

**Which line of evidence is it?**

Groups: Redshift · CMB · Matter proportions

Distant galaxies are moving away from us · A faint glow reaches us from every direction · About three-quarters of the mass is hydrogen, almost all the rest helium · The universe is expanding · The early universe was very hot and has cooled · Hydrogen and helium nuclei formed in the first few minutes

* Answer: Distant galaxies are moving away from us → Redshift; A faint glow reaches us from every direction → CMB; About three-quarters of the mass is hydrogen, almost all the rest helium → Matter proportions; The universe is expanding → Redshift; The early universe was very hot and has cooled → CMB; Hydrogen and helium nuclei formed in the first few minutes → Matter proportions

## Why it is strong

**Why does it strengthen the big bang theory that redshift, the CMB and matter proportions all fit?**

- They are all one measurement in three forms
- They disprove each other
- They are independent lines of evidence that agree on the same model
- They prove the theory can never change

* Answer: They are independent lines of evidence that agree on the same model

<!-- Speaker note: When different kinds of evidence from different methods point the same way, the model is hard to explain away. -->

## Still open

**Which is still an open question in cosmology?**

- What dark matter and dark energy are
- Whether other galaxies exist
- Whether there is a CMB
- Whether the universe is expanding

* Answer: What dark matter and dark energy are

<!-- Speaker note: Observations suggest most of the universe is dark matter and dark energy, but scientists do not yet know what they are. -->
