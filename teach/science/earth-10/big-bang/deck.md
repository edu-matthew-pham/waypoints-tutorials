# How did the universe begin?

Year 10 Earth and space · The big bang

## Today

**We are learning to** the big bang theory: space, time, matter and energy began about 13.8 billion years ago in an extremely hot, dense state, and the universe has expanded and cooled ever since: from particles to atoms, stars, galaxies and planets.

- I can describe what the big bang theory says.
- I can outline at least three stages from the big bang to galaxies.
- I can explain that the universe has been expanding and cooling.

## Atoms

**Why could atoms only form after about 380 000 years?**

- Gravity was too weak
- There were no electrons
- Stars hadn't made them yet
- Before then the universe was too hot: electrons moved too fast to stay attached to nuclei

* Answer: Before then the universe was too hot: electrons moved too fast to stay attached to nuclei

## Elaborate: stars as element factories

**The very first stars were made of hydrogen and helium only. The sun also contains carbon, oxygen and iron. What does that tell us?**

- The sun is older than the first stars
- Carbon was made in the big bang
- The sun formed from material that earlier stars had made and spread out
- The sun has already made its own carbon, oxygen and iron by fusion

* Answer: The sun formed from material that earlier stars had made and spread out

## Elaborate: open questions

**Measurements show the universe's expansion is speeding up, and galaxies hold far more mass than we can see. How do scientists treat these findings?**

- They throw out the big bang theory
- They prove the universe is shrinking
- They add new ideas (dark energy, dark matter) to the model and test them with new observations, from telescopes such as ASKAP in Western Australia
- They ignore them

* Answer: They add new ideas (dark energy, dark matter) to the model and test them with new observations, from telescopes such as ASKAP in Western Australia

**Revision summary**

- The **big bang theory**: space, time, matter and energy began about **13.8 billion years ago**, extremely hot and dense; the universe has been **expanding and cooling** ever since.
- Stages: inflation → protons and neutrons → hydrogen and helium nuclei (3 min) → atoms and free light (380 000 years) → first stars (about 200 million years) → galaxies → the sun and Earth (9.2 billion years).
- Heavier elements were made **in stars**.
