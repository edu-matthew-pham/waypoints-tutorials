# How do we know the universe is expanding?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** In 1929 Edwin Hubble found that almost every galaxy is moving away from us. Does that put us at the centre of the universe?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students explain redshift as evidence that galaxies are moving away, describe Hubble's distance–speed pattern, and connect an expanding universe to the big bang model (AC9S10U03, node 3).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain what redshift tells us about a galaxy's motion. I can describe how speed depends on distance (Hubble's law). I can explain how an expanding universe supports the big bang theory.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Explain: redshift and Hubble's law


:::keep-together

### Redshift

**What does a redshift in a galaxy's light tell us?**

☐ The galaxy is red

☐ The galaxy is moving away from us: its light is stretched to longer, redder wavelengths

☐ The galaxy is hot

☐ The galaxy is moving towards us

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The galaxy is moving away from us: its light is stretched to longer, redder wavelengths

:::


:::keep-together

### The pattern

**On your graph, how does speed depend on distance?**

☐ Nearer galaxies move faster

☐ All galaxies move at the same speed

☐ There's no pattern

☐ Farther galaxies move away faster, in proportion: double the distance, double the speed

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Farther galaxies move away faster, in proportion: double the distance, double the speed

:::


:::keep-together

### Back in time

**How does an expanding universe support the big bang theory?**

☐ It doesn't

☐ It proves the universe is shrinking

☐ Run the expansion backwards and all galaxies were once together, in a hot, dense state

☐ It shows the universe has a centre

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Run the expansion backwards and all galaxies were once together, in a hot, dense state

:::

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Redshift:** light from distant galaxies is shifted to longer wavelengths: they are moving **away**.
- **Hubble's law:** farther galaxies move away **faster**, in proportion to distance.
- This is what an **expanding universe** looks like from any galaxy (no centre). Run backwards, everything was once together: evidence for the **big bang**.
