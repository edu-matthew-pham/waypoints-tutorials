# What keeps Earth's temperature steady?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** In about an hour, the sun pours as much energy onto Earth as humanity uses in a year. Yet, year to year, Earth's average temperature barely changes. Where does it all go?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students explain energy balance as incoming absorbed solar energy equalling outgoing infrared over time, describe energy flows between the spheres, and identify the ocean as a heat store (AC9S10U04, node 2, a hinge node).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain what energy balance means. I can explain how a temperature change restores the balance. I can describe energy flows between spheres and the ocean's role as a store.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** It gives off energy to space as infrared radiation. When the energy out equals the energy in, the temperature stays steady: energy balance.

## Explain: energy in, energy out


:::keep-together

### Balance

**What does energy balance mean?**

☐ Energy absorbed from the sun equals energy given off to space, over time

☐ No energy is moving

☐ Earth stores all the energy it receives

☐ The sun and Earth are the same temperature

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Energy absorbed from the sun equals energy given off to space, over time

:::


:::keep-together

### Restored

**After you increased the sunlight, why did the temperature stop rising?**

☐ The sunlight ran out

☐ The ocean got full

☐ A warmer surface gives off more infrared, until energy out matched energy in again

☐ The warmer Earth reflected more of the sunlight

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A warmer surface gives off more infrared, until energy out matched energy in again

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Energy balance:** energy absorbed from the sun = energy given off to space as infrared, over time. Then temperature is steady.
- Upset it (more in or less out) and Earth warms or cools until **out = in** again.
- Energy moves between the spheres by **radiation, conduction and convection**; the **oceans store** heat and carry it around the world.

## Exit ticket

:::keep-together

**What is energy balance, and what happens if more energy comes in than goes out?**

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Energy in equals energy out over time. If more comes in, Earth warms until it gives off enough extra infrared to balance again.

:::
