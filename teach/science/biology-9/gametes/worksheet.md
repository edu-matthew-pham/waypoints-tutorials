# What makes a sex cell special?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A human egg is just big enough to see without a microscope. A sperm is one of the smallest cells in the body. Together they make one cell that grows into a person.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students explain that gametes have half the chromosome number and relate the features of sperm and egg cells to their jobs (AC9S9U02, node 1, a hinge node).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain why gametes have half the usual number of chromosomes. I can link three features of sperm and egg cells to their jobs. I can describe how a plant's male nucleus reaches the egg.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::


## Engage


:::keep-together

**Your body cells have 46 chromosomes. How many does a human sperm or egg cell have?**

☐ 92: double

☐ 46, like every other cell

☐ 23: half

☐ None: gametes have no nucleus

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** 23: half. When a sperm (23) fertilises an egg (23), the new cell has 46 again. If gametes had 46, the number would double every generation.

:::

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Gametes** are sex cells: sperm and egg in animals; the male nucleus in pollen and the egg in the ovule in flowering plants.
- Gametes have **half** the chromosomes (23 in humans), so **fertilisation** restores the full number (46).
- **Sperm:** tail, midpiece of mitochondria, acrosome. **Egg:** large, food store, a coat that lets in only one sperm.

## Exit ticket

**Why do sperm and egg cells have 23 chromosomes, not 46?**

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** So that when they fuse at fertilisation, the new cell has 46, the full number, again.
