> **Key question:** Carbon has been moving between air, living things, water and rock for billions of years. What have we changed?

## The whole cycle


:::keep-together

### Between spheres

**Which flow moves carbon from the atmosphere into the biosphere?**

☐ Decomposition

☐ Photosynthesis

☐ Combustion

☐ Respiration

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Photosynthesis

Plants take carbon dioxide from the air and build it into sugars. Respiration, combustion and decomposition move carbon the other way.

:::


:::keep-together

### The human change

**Why has burning fossil fuels changed the carbon cycle's balance?**

☐ It moves carbon that was stored for millions of years into the air in a few centuries, faster than natural processes remove it

☐ It has no effect, because natural flows are much bigger

☐ It makes plants stop photosynthesising

☐ It destroys carbon

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** It moves carbon that was stored for millions of years into the air in a few centuries, faster than natural processes remove it

The natural flows nearly balance each other. The extra flow from fossil fuels is not balanced, so carbon builds up in the air.

:::
