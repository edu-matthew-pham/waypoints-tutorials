> Carbon has been moving between air, living things, water and rock for billions of years. What have we changed?

## Between spheres

**Which flow moves carbon from the atmosphere into the biosphere?**

- Decomposition
- Photosynthesis
- Combustion
- Respiration

* Answer: Photosynthesis

<!-- Speaker note: Plants take carbon dioxide from the air and build it into sugars. Respiration, combustion and decomposition move carbon the other way. -->

## Where from, where to

**Match each flow to where it moves carbon.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `Photosynthesis` | A. Geosphere to atmosphere |
| `Respiration by animals` | B. Atmosphere to hydrosphere |
| `Burning coal` | C. Atmosphere to biosphere |
| `The ocean dissolving carbon dioxide` | D. Biosphere to atmosphere |

* Answer: Photosynthesis → C. Atmosphere to biosphere; Respiration by animals → D. Biosphere to atmosphere; Burning coal → A. Geosphere to atmosphere; The ocean dissolving carbon dioxide → B. Atmosphere to hydrosphere

## What drives the flow?

**Is the flow biological, geological or human-driven?**

Groups: Biological · Geological · Human-driven

Photosynthesis · Respiration · Decomposition · A volcano releasing carbon dioxide · Burning petrol in cars · Clearing and burning a forest

* Answer: Photosynthesis → Biological; Respiration → Biological; Decomposition → Biological; A volcano releasing carbon dioxide → Geological; Burning petrol in cars → Human-driven; Clearing and burning a forest → Human-driven

## The human change

**Why has burning fossil fuels changed the carbon cycle's balance?**

- It moves carbon that was stored for millions of years into the air in a few centuries, faster than natural processes remove it
- It has no effect, because natural flows are much bigger
- It makes plants stop photosynthesising
- It destroys carbon

* Answer: It moves carbon that was stored for millions of years into the air in a few centuries, faster than natural processes remove it

<!-- Speaker note: The natural flows nearly balance each other. The extra flow from fossil fuels is not balanced, so carbon builds up in the air. -->
