# What are Earth's spheres?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A single bushfire involves all of Earth: burning trees, smoke in the air, ash washed into rivers and scorched soil. Scientists use four 'spheres' to keep track of it.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students name and describe Earth's four spheres, give examples of matter and processes in each, and recognise that spheres overlap and interact (AC9S9U03, node 1, a hinge node).

**Students should be able to say:** I can name the four spheres and say what each contains. I can give an example of matter in each sphere. I can explain that the spheres interact, with examples where they meet.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

**What happens:** More than one. Soil is mostly broken rock (geosphere) full of living things and their remains (biosphere), with water and air in its gaps. The spheres overlap and interact.

## Elaborate: managing interactions


:::keep-together

**For tens of thousands of years, many First Nations peoples have used small, cool fires to manage Country. Which spheres does a cool burn involve?**

☐ Only the biosphere

☐ All four: plants burn (biosphere), smoke and CO₂ enter the air (atmosphere), soil is warmed and enriched (geosphere), and later rain and regrowth (hydrosphere, biosphere)

☐ None: fire isn't part of the spheres

☐ Only the atmosphere

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** All four: plants burn (biosphere), smoke and CO₂ enter the air (atmosphere), soil is warmed and enriched (geosphere), and later rain and regrowth (hydrosphere, biosphere)

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Atmosphere:** air. **Biosphere:** living things. **Hydrosphere:** water. **Geosphere:** rock, soil, magma.
- Many things sit where spheres meet (soil, clouds), and processes move matter between spheres.
- Earth works as one system of interacting spheres.
