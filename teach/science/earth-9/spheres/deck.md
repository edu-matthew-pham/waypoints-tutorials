# What are Earth's spheres?

Year 9 Earth and space · The carbon cycle

<!-- Speaker note: Students name and describe Earth's four spheres, give examples of matter and processes in each, and recognise that spheres overlap and interact (AC9S9U03, node 1, a hinge node). ## Today

**We are learning to** earth can be described as four interacting spheres: the atmosphere (air), biosphere (living things), hydrosphere (water) and geosphere (rock, soil and magma). Many things sit where spheres meet.

- I can name the four spheres and say what each contains.
- I can give an example of matter in each sphere.
- I can explain that the spheres interact, with examples where they meet.

## Why it matters

> A single bushfire involves all of Earth: burning trees, smoke in the air, ash washed into rivers and scorched soil. Scientists use four 'spheres' to keep track of it.

## Contents

**Match each sphere to what it contains.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `Atmosphere` | A. All living things |
| `Biosphere` | B. Rock, soil, minerals and magma |
| `Hydrosphere` | C. All water: oceans, rivers, ice, vapour |
| `Geosphere` | D. The layer of gases around Earth |

* Answer: Atmosphere → D. The layer of gases around Earth; Biosphere → A. All living things; Hydrosphere → C. All water: oceans, rivers, ice, vapour; Geosphere → B. Rock, soil, minerals and magma

## Interactions

**A tree takes in carbon dioxide and water to grow. Which spheres does this connect?**

- Only the atmosphere
- Geosphere and atmosphere
- Atmosphere (CO₂), hydrosphere (water) and biosphere (the tree)
- Only the biosphere

* Answer: Atmosphere (CO₂), hydrosphere (water) and biosphere (the tree)

## Elaborate: a rain event

**A storm rolls in over farmland. Which sphere is each part mainly about?**

Groups: Atmosphere · Biosphere · Hydrosphere · Geosphere

Wind and falling air pressure · Rain filling the creek · Topsoil washed off a paddock · Crops growing faster after the rain

* Answer: Wind and falling air pressure → Atmosphere; Rain filling the creek → Hydrosphere; Topsoil washed off a paddock → Geosphere; Crops growing faster after the rain → Biosphere

## Elaborate: managing interactions

**For tens of thousands of years, many First Nations peoples have used small, cool fires to manage Country. Which spheres does a cool burn involve?**

- Only the biosphere
- All four: plants burn (biosphere), smoke and CO₂ enter the air (atmosphere), soil is warmed and enriched (geosphere), and later rain and regrowth (hydrosphere, biosphere)
- None: fire isn't part of the spheres
- Only the atmosphere

* Answer: All four: plants burn (biosphere), smoke and CO₂ enter the air (atmosphere), soil is warmed and enriched (geosphere), and later rain and regrowth (hydrosphere, biosphere)

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Atmosphere:** air. **Biosphere:** living things. **Hydrosphere:** water. **Geosphere:** rock, soil, magma.
- Many things sit where spheres meet (soil, clouds), and processes move matter between spheres.
- Earth works as one system of interacting spheres.

**Name the four spheres and give one example of something where two of them meet.**
