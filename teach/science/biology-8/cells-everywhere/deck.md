# Is everything alive made of cells?

Year 8 Biology · Cells

## Today

**We are learning to** cell theory: all living things, including plants, fungi and bacteria, are made of cells. Some organisms are a single cell; others are made of many.

- I can state cell theory.
- I can explain that plants, fungi and bacteria are made of cells too.
- I can give examples of single-celled and multicellular organisms.

## Cell theory

**Which statement is cell theory?**

- Big organisms have big cells
- All living things are made of one or more cells, and the cell is the smallest unit of life
- Cells are found in living and non-living things
- Only animals are made of cells

* Answer: All living things are made of one or more cells, and the cell is the smallest unit of life

## Doing everything

**A single-celled yeast cell and one of your skin cells are both cells. What's the big difference?**

- The yeast cell does everything to stay alive on its own; your skin cell is one of trillions, each doing a job
- Your skin cell has no membrane
- The yeast cell isn't really alive

* Answer: The yeast cell does everything to stay alive on its own; your skin cell is one of trillions, each doing a job

## Elaborate: the smallest living thing

**A virus is far smaller than a bacterium. It has DNA (or RNA) in a protein coat, but no membrane or cytoplasm of its own, and it can only reproduce inside a living cell. Using cell theory, is a virus a living thing?**

- Yes: it has DNA
- Yes: it is very small
- By cell theory, no: it isn't a cell, so scientists usually call it non-living

* Answer: By cell theory, no: it isn't a cell, so scientists usually call it non-living

<!-- Speaker note: Viruses sit on the boundary. Most biologists say they are not alive, because they are not cells. What this step is for: Asking what the minimum for life is uses cell theory to reason about a boundary case. -->

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Cell theory:** all living things are made of one or more cells; the cell is the smallest unit of life; cells come from other cells.
- That includes **plants, fungi and bacteria**, not only animals.
- **Single-celled** organisms (bacteria, yeast) are one cell doing everything; **multicellular** organisms are many cells working together.
