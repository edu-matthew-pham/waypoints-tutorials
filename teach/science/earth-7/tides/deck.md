# Why are there two high tides a day?

Year 7 Earth and space · The moon

## Why it matters

> At a beach the water creeps up the sand and back down about every 12 and a half hours, and the biggest tides of the month always arrive near a new or full moon.

<!-- Speaker note: You know where the moon is through its phases, and it turns out that has a lot to do with what the sea does at the beach. -->

## Spring or neap

**Spring tides or neap tides?**

Groups: Spring tides · Neap tides

New moon · Full moon · First quarter · Last quarter · The sun and moon pull in line · The sun pulls at right angles to the moon · Smallest difference between high and low tide

* Answer: New moon → Spring tides; Full moon → Spring tides; First quarter → Neap tides; Last quarter → Neap tides; The sun and moon pull in line → Spring tides; The sun pulls at right angles to the moon → Neap tides; Smallest difference between high and low tide → Neap tides

## The name

**Do spring tides happen only in spring?**

- Only in the Northern Hemisphere's spring
- Only once a year
- Yes, only in September to November
- No: they happen about every two weeks all year, at new and full moon ('spring' as in springing up)

* Answer: No: they happen about every two weeks all year, at new and full moon ('spring' as in springing up)

## Elaborate: the best low tide

**Reef walking and gathering shellfish is best at the lowest low tides. When should you plan to go?**

- At a quarter moon
- Near a new or full moon, at low tide: spring tides give the lowest lows
- When the moon is high overhead
- Any day: low tides are always the same

* Answer: Near a new or full moon, at low tide: spring tides give the lowest lows

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- The moon's **gravity** pulls the oceans into **two bulges** (towards the moon and on the far side); the Earth spins through both: **two high tides a day** on most coasts.
- **Spring tides** (biggest range): new and full moon, sun and moon **in line**. **Neap tides** (smallest range): quarter moons, sun at **right angles**.
- Spring and neap tides each come about every two weeks, all year.

## Exit ticket

**When do spring tides happen, and why are they bigger?**

* At new and full moon, when the sun and moon are in line, so their pulls on the oceans add together.
