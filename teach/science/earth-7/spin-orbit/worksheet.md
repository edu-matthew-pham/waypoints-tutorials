# Spinning and orbiting

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Right now you are spinning at about 1400 km/h (in Canberra) and flying round the sun at about 107 000 km/h. You don't feel either.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students distinguish the Earth's rotation from its revolution, link each to day and night and the year, and describe the tilted axis that keeps its direction (AC9S7U03, node 1, a hinge node).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain the difference between rotation and revolution. I can link rotation to day and night, and revolution to the year. I can describe the Earth's tilted axis and how it points.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (Engage, Explore, Explain, Evaluate). Typical and Wider sections add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- **Rotation:** the Earth spins on its axis once a day: day and night.
- **Revolution:** the Earth goes round the sun once a year (about 365¼ days, about 365 spins).
- The **axis is tilted 23.4°** and always points the same way in space, so each hemisphere leans towards the sun for half the year.
