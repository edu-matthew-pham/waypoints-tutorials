# What's inside the Earth?

Year 8 Earth and space · Plate tectonics

## Today

**We are learning to** earth has layers: a thin solid crust, a thick solid mantle that flows very slowly, a liquid outer core and a solid inner core. Temperature and pressure rise with depth. The lithosphere (crust plus the rigid top of the mantle) is the rigid outer shell that breaks into plates.

- I can name Earth's layers and say whether each is solid or liquid.
- I can explain that temperature and pressure increase with depth.
- I can explain that the lithosphere is the rigid outer shell (crust and top of the mantle).

## Elaborate: how do we know?

**One kind of earthquake wave (S-waves) can't travel through liquids. On the far side of the Earth from a big earthquake, no S-waves arrive. What does that suggest?**

- The mantle is liquid
- Part of the Earth's interior (the outer core) is liquid
- The core is too hot for the waves to pass through
- The waves get tired

* Answer: Part of the Earth's interior (the outer core) is liquid

**Revision summary**

- **Crust** (thin, solid) → **mantle** (thick, solid but flows very slowly) → **outer core** (liquid iron and nickel) → **inner core** (solid iron and nickel).
- **Temperature and pressure increase** with depth.
- The **lithosphere** (crust + rigid top of the mantle) is the rigid outer shell, broken into plates; it rests on the soft, slowly flowing **asthenosphere**.
