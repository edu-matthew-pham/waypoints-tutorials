**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Australia is moving north about 7 centimetres a year, nearly twice as fast as your fingernails grow. A whole continent. What could possibly push it?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students explain that the lithosphere is divided into moving plates and describe mantle convection, driven by Earth's inner heat (from the core and from radioactive decay in the mantle), as the mechanism (AC9S8U03, node 2, a hinge node).

### Why rock rises

**Why does hot rock above the core rise?**

☐ Hot things always go up because heat rises

☐ It is less dense than the cooler rock around it

☐ The core pushes it out

☐ It has melted into liquid magma, which floats up

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** It is less dense than the cooler rock around it

Heated rock expands a little, so it is less dense and rises. Cooled rock is denser and sinks.

:::


:::keep-together

### Plates move apart

**On the board, where did the two plates move apart?**

☐ Above the sinking currents

☐ Above the rising current

☐ Where the board was coolest

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Above the rising current

Rising rock spreads out sideways under the plates, dragging them apart. This is a divergent boundary, like the Mid-Atlantic Ridge.

:::


:::keep-together

The mechanism is the same; the timescale is not. A loop in the beaker takes seconds; in the mantle, tens of millions of years.

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- The **lithosphere** (crust and top of the mantle) is broken into rigid **tectonic plates**, moving a few centimetres a year.
- Earth's inner heat (from the **core** below, and from radioactive elements in the mantle rock) drives **convection currents** in the **mantle**: hot rock is less dense and rises, spreads, cools and sinks.
- The currents carry the plates: apart above rising rock, together above sinking rock.
- The mantle is solid rock that flows extremely slowly, not liquid magma.
