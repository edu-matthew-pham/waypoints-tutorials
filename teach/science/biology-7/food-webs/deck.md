# Who eats whom?

Year 7 Biology · Food webs and ecosystems

## Today

**We are learning to** a food web joins all the food chains in an ecosystem. Arrows point the way energy flows, from the food to the eater. Every organism is a producer, a consumer or a decomposer, and decomposers include microorganisms.

- I can construct a food web from a list of organisms.
- I can draw arrows in the direction of energy flow.
- I can identify producers, consumers and decomposers, including microorganisms.

## Why it matters

> A kookaburra eats lizards, grasshoppers, even small snakes. A food chain can only show one of those meals. How do you show all of them?

## Roles

**What is each organism's role in the bush web?**

Groups: Producer · Primary consumer · Secondary consumer · Decomposer

Grass · Grasshopper · Kangaroo · Lizard · Dingo · Fungi and bacteria

* Answer: Grass → Producer; Grasshopper → Primary consumer; Kangaroo → Primary consumer; Lizard → Secondary consumer; Dingo → Secondary consumer; Fungi and bacteria → Decomposer

## More than one level

**The kookaburra eats both lizards and grasshoppers. What does that make it?**

- A secondary consumer when it eats grasshoppers, and a tertiary consumer when it eats lizards
- A producer
- A primary consumer

* Answer: A secondary consumer when it eats grasshoppers, and a tertiary consumer when it eats lizards

## Decomposers

**Why do decomposers belong in a food web?**

- They don't; only animals that hunt belong
- They make food from sunlight
- They feed on dead organisms and wastes from every level, returning nutrients to the soil

* Answer: They feed on dead organisms and wastes from every level, returning nutrients to the soil

## Mix it up

**Looking back at the kingdoms: a mushroom grows on a dead log. How does it get its food?**

- It absorbs food from the dead log: it is a fungus, so it is a decomposer
- It hunts and eats small animals
- It makes food from the soil, like a plant
- It makes food from sunlight, like a plant

* Answer: It absorbs food from the dead log: it is a fungus, so it is a decomposer

## Elaborate: chain or web?

**The food chain grass → grasshopper → lizard → kookaburra is part of the web. What does the chain leave out?**

- The direction of energy flow
- Other foods each animal eats, and other animals that eat the same food
- Nothing: a chain and a web show the same thing

* Answer: Other foods each animal eats, and other animals that eat the same food

<!-- Speaker note: A chain shows one path. A web shows every path, which is why it can predict what happens when one population changes. What this step is for: Comparing the simpler model with the fuller one shows what each representation keeps and loses. -->

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- A **food web** joins all the food chains in an ecosystem.
- **Arrows point the way energy flows**: from the food to the eater.
- **Producers** make their own food; **consumers** eat other organisms (primary, secondary…); **decomposers** (fungi and bacteria, microorganisms) break down dead matter and wastes.
- One animal can feed at more than one level.
