### A dry year

**In a web, grass is eaten by grasshoppers, which are eaten by lizards. A drought cuts grass growth. What happens to the grasshoppers?**

☐ They increase, because the grass is drier

☐ They turn into lizards

☐ Nothing, because grasshoppers are not plants

☐ They decrease, because they have less food

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** They decrease, because they have less food

A change in an abiotic factor (water) hits the producer first, then the animals that depend on it.

:::


:::keep-together

### Who else?

**Besides the grasshoppers, which population is also affected by the drought?**

☐ The sun

☐ The lizards, because their food supply falls

☐ No other population

☐ Only the grass

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The lizards, because their food supply falls

The effect cascades up the web: fewer grasshoppers means less food for the lizards, and then for whatever eats them.

:::


:::keep-together

### Abiotic means

**What is an abiotic factor?**

☐ Anything that harms living things

☐ A living thing that affects other living things

☐ A non-living part of the environment, such as temperature, water or sunlight

☐ An animal that eats only plants

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A non-living part of the environment, such as temperature, water or sunlight

Abiotic means non-living: physical and chemical conditions such as temperature, water, light and soil.

:::

### A weak prediction

**A web shows a hawk eats lizards. Someone predicts that if lizards disappear the hawks will die out. Why might that be wrong?**

☐ It is right: if lizards disappear, the hawks must die out

☐ Hawks may also eat other animals that the diagram leaves out, so they could switch food

☐ Food webs can't be used to make predictions

☐ Hawks would switch to eating grass

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Hawks may also eat other animals that the diagram leaves out, so they could switch food

Real animals often eat several foods, and a diagram simplifies. A prediction is only as good as the web it comes from.

:::


:::keep-together

### Model limits

**Which is a limitation of a food web model?**

☐ It shows only some organisms and links, so predictions are less certain

☐ It shows how many organisms there are

☐ It only works for plants

☐ It is always completely accurate

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** It shows only some organisms and links, so predictions are less certain

A model simplifies. Knowing what it leaves out tells you how far to trust its predictions.

:::
