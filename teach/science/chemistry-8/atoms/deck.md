# What are particles made of?

Year 8 Chemistry · Elements and compounds

<!-- Speaker note: Students extend the particle model so that particles are atoms with a type, and explain that atom type decides the element (AC9S8U06, node 1, a hinge node). About 20 minutes (Engage and Explore 6 min · Explain 8 min · Evaluate 3 min · Typical adds 5 min, Wider about 4 min). -->

## Today

**We are learning to** the particles of the particle model are atoms. Atoms come in different types, one type for each element, and atom type decides what element a substance is.

- I can explain that the particles in the particle model are atoms.
- I can explain that different elements are made of different types of atom.
- I can explain that atom type decides what element a substance is.

## Why it matters

> Water, oxygen and carbon dioxide are all made of particles. So why is one a drink, one keeps you alive, and one puts out fires?

**What makes oxygen a different element from nitrogen?**

- Its atoms are a different type
- It has more atoms
- It is a different colour
- Its atoms are bigger

## One type, two atoms

**An oxygen particle (O₂) has two atoms. Is oxygen still an element?**

- No: two atoms joined make a compound
- Yes: both atoms are the same type

* Answer: Yes: both atoms are the same type

## Elaborate: how the idea of atoms grew

**Put the ideas about matter in the order they were proposed, earliest first.**

```science
Aristotle: no atoms; everything is earth, water, air and fire, mixed
Dalton: each element has its own type of atom, and atoms join in fixed ratios
Boyle: an element is a substance that cannot be broken down into simpler ones
Democritus: matter is made of tiny pieces that cannot be cut, "atomos"
```

<!-- Speaker note: Democritus (about 400 BCE): atoms as an idea without evidence. Aristotle (about 350 BCE): his four elements won for 2000 years. Boyle (1661): elements defined by experiment. Dalton (1808): atoms explain why elements combine in fixed ratios. In Year 9 the model changes again: atoms have smaller parts inside. Correct order: Democritus: matter is made of tiny pieces that cannot be cut, "atomos" Aristotle: no atoms; everything is earth, water, air and fire, mixed Boyle: an element is a substance that cannot be broken down into simpler ones Dalton: each element has its own type of atom, and atoms join in fixed ratios What this step is for: Ordering the models shows the atom as a model refined by evidence, which prepares Year 9 (atoms have parts inside). -->

## Evaluate: look back

What did you think air was made of? Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- The particles in the particle model are **atoms** (or atoms joined together).
- There are about 118 **types of atom**, one for each **element**.
- All atoms of one element are the same type. **Atom type decides the element.**
- An element can be single atoms (helium) or atoms of one type joined (oxygen, O₂).
