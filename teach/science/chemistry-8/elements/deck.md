# What is an element?

Year 8 Chemistry · Elements and compounds

<!-- Speaker note: Students define an element as a pure substance of only one type of atom, tell elements from compounds by their particles, and use chemical symbols (AC9S8U06, node 2). About 24 minutes (Engage and Explore 7 min · Explain 10 min · Evaluate 3 min · Typical adds 3 min, Wider about 4 min). -->

## Today

**We are learning to** an element is a pure substance made of only one type of atom. Its particles can be single atoms (helium) or atoms joined together (O₂), but every atom is the same type. Each element has a symbol of one or two letters, and the periodic table lists them all.

- I can define an element.
- I can tell an element from a compound by the atoms in its particles.
- I can use chemical symbols, including two-letter ones.

## Why it matters

> Everything around you, from your phone to your fingernails, is built from fewer than a hundred kinds of atom. Each kind is an element.

## What an element is

**What is an element?**

- A pure substance made of only one type of atom
- Any substance made of single atoms
- Any pure substance

* Answer: A pure substance made of only one type of atom

<!-- Speaker note: One type of atom, and nothing else. The atoms may be single (helium) or joined (oxygen, O₂). -->

## Element or compound

**Element or compound?**

Groups: Element · Compound

O₂ (oxygen) · He (helium) · N₂ (nitrogen) · O₃ (ozone) · H₂O (water) · CO₂ (carbon dioxide) · NaCl (salt)

* Answer: O₂ (oxygen) → Element; He (helium) → Element; N₂ (nitrogen) → Element; O₃ (ozone) → Element; H₂O (water) → Compound; CO₂ (carbon dioxide) → Compound; NaCl (salt) → Compound

## Co or CO

**Why is the symbol for cobalt written Co, not CO?**

- It doesn't matter how you write it
- A symbol is one capital letter, or a capital and a small letter; CO means a carbon atom joined to an oxygen atom
- CO is the symbol for cobalt gas

* Answer: A symbol is one capital letter, or a capital and a small letter; CO means a carbon atom joined to an oxygen atom

<!-- Speaker note: Each symbol starts with a capital. Co is one element (cobalt); CO is two symbols, C and O: carbon monoxide. -->

## The periodic table

**What does the periodic table show?**

- Only the elements found in living things
- Every substance, including compounds
- Every element, one box each, in order of atomic number, with similar elements in columns

* Answer: Every element, one box each, in order of atomic number, with similar elements in columns

## Reading a box

**A box on the periodic table shows 26, Fe and Iron. What does each part tell you?**

- 26 is the temperature iron melts at
- 26 is the atomic number (its place in the table), Fe is the symbol and Iron is the name
- 26 is the number of atoms in one particle of iron
- Fe is the formula of a compound of iron

* Answer: 26 is the atomic number (its place in the table), Fe is the symbol and Iron is the name

<!-- Speaker note: Each box gives an element's atomic number, symbol and name (often its atomic mass too). Find the name and you have the symbol, or the other way round. -->

## One element, two substances

**Ozone (O₃) and oxygen (O₂) are both made of only oxygen atoms, but ozone protects us from ultraviolet light high in the atmosphere and is harmful to breathe. What does this show?**

- Ozone must be a compound
- One element can form different substances when its atoms join in different ways
- O₃ contains a different type of oxygen atom

* Answer: One element can form different substances when its atoms join in different ways

<!-- Speaker note: Carbon does the same: diamond and graphite are both pure carbon. How the atoms are joined changes the properties. -->

**Revision summary**

- An **element** is a pure substance made of only one type of atom. Its particles can be single atoms (He) or atoms joined together (O₂, O₃).
- A **compound** has two or more types of atom joined (H₂O, CO₂).
- Each element has a **symbol**: one capital letter, or a capital and a small letter (C, Co, Na). Some come from Latin names (Fe, Na, Cu, Au).
- The **periodic table** lists all the elements, about 118.

## Exit ticket

**Nitrogen gas is N₂ and ammonia is NH₃. Which is an element, and how can you tell from the formula?**

* Nitrogen: only one symbol (N), so one type of atom. Ammonia has two symbols (N and H): a compound.
