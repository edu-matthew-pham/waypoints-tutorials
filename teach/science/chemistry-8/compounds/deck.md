# Why isn't salt poisonous?

Year 8 Chemistry · Elements and compounds

<!-- Speaker note: Students define a compound (different atom types chemically joined in a fixed ratio, a new substance with new properties), tell it apart from a mixture, and read formulas (AC9S8U06, node 3, a hinge node). ## Today

**We are learning to** a compound is two or more types of atom chemically joined in a fixed ratio. It is a new substance with new properties, and a formula like H₂O tells you which atoms and how many.

- I can define a compound as two or more types of atom chemically joined in a fixed ratio.
- I can read a formula to find the atom types and how many of each.
- I can explain that a compound has different properties from the elements it is made from.

## Joined or mixed

**Both boards had hydrogen and oxygen atoms. What made water a compound and not a mixture?**

- It has more atoms
- It is a liquid
- Its atoms are closer together
- Its hydrogen and oxygen atoms are chemically joined in each particle

* Answer: Its hydrogen and oxygen atoms are chemically joined in each particle

<!-- Speaker note: In a compound, different types of atom are chemically joined. In a mixture they are just together, not joined. -->

## Reading a formula

**Match each formula to the atoms in one particle.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `H₂O` | A. 1 carbon, 2 oxygen |
| `CO₂` | B. 2 hydrogen, 1 oxygen |
| `NH₃` | C. 1 nitrogen, 3 hydrogen |

* Answer: H₂O → B. 2 hydrogen, 1 oxygen; CO₂ → A. 1 carbon, 2 oxygen; NH₃ → C. 1 nitrogen, 3 hydrogen; CH₄ → D. 1 carbon, 4 hydrogen

## Fixed ratio

**Water is always H₂O, never H₃O or HO. What does that tell you about compounds?**

- Water can be any mix of hydrogen and oxygen
- The atoms are joined in a fixed ratio
- Hydrogen is rarer than oxygen

* Answer: The atoms are joined in a fixed ratio

<!-- Speaker note: Every water particle has exactly 2 hydrogen atoms for each oxygen atom. A mixture of hydrogen and oxygen gases can be in any ratio. -->

## Why salt is safe

**Sodium fizzes in water and chlorine is poisonous, yet sodium chloride is safe to eat. Why?**

- The sodium and chlorine atoms changed into other kinds of atom
- There is only a tiny amount of sodium and chlorine in it
- The sodium and chlorine are mixed in it, and cancel each other out
- It is a new substance: the atoms are joined, so it has new properties

* Answer: It is a new substance: the atoms are joined, so it has new properties

<!-- Speaker note: A compound is a new substance. Its properties belong to the joined particles, not to the elements it was made from. -->

**Hydrogen and oxygen are gases that let things burn. Water puts fires out. What does that show?**

- A compound has different properties from the elements it is made from
- Water is a mixture of hydrogen and oxygen
- The atoms changed type

* Answer: A compound has different properties from the elements it is made from

## Evaluate: look back

Write one sentence: "At first I thought… Now I think… because…"

**Revision summary**

- A **compound** is two or more **types** of atom **chemically joined** in a **fixed ratio**.
- A compound is a **new substance** with new properties (sodium chloride is not like sodium or chlorine).
- In a **mixture**, substances are together but not joined, and each keeps its properties.
- A **formula** gives the atoms in one particle: H₂O is 2 hydrogen and 1 oxygen.
