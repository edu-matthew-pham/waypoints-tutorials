# Your digital footprint

Cyber safety and privacy · Protect your accounts

## Today

**We are learning to** your digital footprint is everything apps record about you: what you give them, and what they collect as you use them (where you are, what you open, when). A good app collects only what its purpose needs. You manage your own footprint by checking settings, which often start open.

- I can name data an app collects without my typing it.
- I can judge whether data is needed for an app's purpose.
- I can set my own privacy settings and explain the trade-off.
