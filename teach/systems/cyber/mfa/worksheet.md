## Explain: something you know, have or are

**Multi-factor authentication** (MFA) asks for two different kinds of proof:

1. $\text{something you know}$ — A password or PIN. This is the part that leaks.
2. $\text{something you have}$ — A phone (an SMS or app code) or a security key you plug in or tap.
3. $\text{something you are}$ — A fingerprint or face, usually to unlock the phone or key.
4. $\text{two kinds} \Rightarrow \text{a leak isn't enough}$ — Someone with the password still needs the thing Ava has.
