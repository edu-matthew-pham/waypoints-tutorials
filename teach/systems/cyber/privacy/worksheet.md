## Explain: five principles

The **Australian Privacy Principles** (in the Privacy Act 1988) say what organisations must do with **personal information**: anything that identifies a person.

1. $\text{APP 3 Collection}$ — Collect only what you need for your purpose.
2. $\text{APP 5 Notice}$ — Tell people what you collect, why, and who to ask.
3. $\text{APP 6 Use and disclosure}$ — Use and share it only for that purpose, unless they agree.
4. $\text{APP 11 Security}$ — Protect it: hash passwords, encrypt data, limit who can see it.
5. $\text{APP 12 Access}$ — Let people see what you hold about them (APP 13: and correct it).
