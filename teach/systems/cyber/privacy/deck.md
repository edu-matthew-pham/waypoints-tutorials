## Today

**We are learning to** the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) say what organisations must do with personal information: collect only what they need (APP 3), tell people what and why (APP 5), use and share it only for that purpose unless people agree (APP 6), keep it secure (APP 11), and let people see and correct it (APP 12). A privacy policy doesn't override them.

- I can say what five of the Australian Privacy Principles require.
- I can judge whether an app meets or breaches a principle, with evidence.
- I can propose a change that would meet it.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Try the board yourself. The club app is made up. They bind agencies and organisations with turnover over $3 million, plus some smaller ones; a school club wouldn't usually be bound itself; independent schools usually are (government schools come under their state's privacy law), and the principles are the standard to design against.
