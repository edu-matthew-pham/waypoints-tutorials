## Explain: the signs

**Phishing** is a message pretending to be someone you trust, to get something from you.

1. $\text{who sent it?}$ — Look at the address, not just the name. A lookalike (schooI with a capital I) is a warning.
2. $\text{pressure?}$ — Deadlines, threats and prizes are there to make you act before you think.
3. $\text{what does it want?}$ — A password, a code, money or gift cards: real organisations don't ask for these by message.
4. $\text{where does the link go?}$ — Check it. If the text and the destination differ, don't click.
5. $\text{unsure?}$ — Go to the site or person yourself (type the address, use the app, ask in person).
