## Today

**We are learning to** phishing messages pretend to be someone you trust to get a password, money or a click. The signs: a sender address that isn't quite right, pressure or a deadline, a request for a password or payment, and a link that goes somewhere other than its text. When unsure, go to the site or person yourself.

- I can spot the signs of a phishing message.
- I can check where a link really goes.
- I can say what to do when I'm not sure.

## Exit ticket

**Give three signs that a message might be phishing, and what to do if you're not sure.**

* A wrong or lookalike address, pressure or a deadline, asking for a password, code or money, a link that goes somewhere else. Go to the site or person yourself; report it.
