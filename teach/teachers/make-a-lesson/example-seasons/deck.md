## The idea: seasons

The Earth's axis is tilted by about 23.5 degrees. As the Earth goes around the Sun over a year, first one half and then the other is tilted towards the Sun. The half tilted towards the Sun has summer: the Sun is higher in the sky, its light is more concentrated, and the days are longer. The half tilted away has winter.

## The idea: phases of the Moon

The Moon does not make its own light. We see the half of the Moon that the Sun lights up, but as the Moon goes around the Earth (about once a month) we see different amounts of that lit half. This gives the phases: new Moon, crescent, first quarter, gibbous, full Moon, and back again.
