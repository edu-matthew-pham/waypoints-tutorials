# Other ways to start

Make a lesson · Make one

## Why it matters

> You don't always start from nothing. Often the quickest lesson is a change to one that exists.

## Think first

**You already teach a lesson on this site almost the way you want, but your class needs a different example. What is the quickest start?**

- Ask an AI to write a lesson from scratch
- Start a new primer from a waypoint
- Open that lesson as a copy and change the example
- Print it and cross things out

## The idea

- **A lesson that is nearly right.** Press **Make your own version** at the bottom of any lesson page. Create opens a copy titled "(my version)", with an "Adapted from" credit you keep when you share it.
- **A finished project** (a program, page or micro:bit project you built). In Create, choose **Start from a project**. It copies a prompt for your own AI chat that asks whether the project was made for students or is a personal one, finds the ideas the site already teaches, and cuts the rest into small steps. It reads Python, HTML/CSS and MakeCode; other languages still get the planning help.
- **Only a topic.** Choose **Start with a prompt**, or start from a curriculum waypoint as in lesson 1.
- **Lessons that exist but in your order.** Use **My path**: pick lessons, put them in order, add a note and share one link. Nothing to write and no account.

Two other places help: **Open existing work** brings back a lesson or course you saved, and **Explore an example** shows a working one.
