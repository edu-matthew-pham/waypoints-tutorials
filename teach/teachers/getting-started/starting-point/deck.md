## Why it matters

> You have a lesson to plan. Which tool helps with the part you need to think through?

<!-- Speaker note: Learning Waypoints helps you work out what students need to understand, what to teach first and how to check their thinking. You can use it to plan a topic, prepare teaching resources or try an activity with your class. A **waypoint** is an idea students need to understand to meet a curriculum expectation. In our Geography example, one waypoint is understanding that a renewable resource can still be used up. That matters when students explain why a river can run low. The waypoints draw on ACARA descriptors. Their sequence, examples and teaching suggestions reflect professional judgement; ACARA does not prescribe them. You can use them alongside your learning intentions and success criteria to think through what students already know and where they might need help. The teacher situations in this lesson are made up. -->

## The idea: three ways in

- [**Curriculum: Unpack**](https://acara.learningwaypoints.com/): see how ideas build on each other and what students need to understand first.
- [**Planning and preparation: Prepare**](https://prepare.learningwaypoints.com/): work with slide decks and printable teaching resources.
- [**Tutorials**](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/geography/water-7/resource-types/): use activities, checks and feedback to explore what students understand, on their own or as a class.

Open each tool and have a look. Use whichever part helps with your planning; you do not have to use all three or follow a set order. The Geography example is a preview, not yet tried with classes.
