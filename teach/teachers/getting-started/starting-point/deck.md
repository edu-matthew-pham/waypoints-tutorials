## Why it matters

> You have a lesson to plan. Which tool helps with the part you need to think through?

<!-- Speaker note: Learning Waypoints helps you work out what students need to understand, what to teach first and how to check their thinking. You can use it to plan a topic, prepare teaching resources or try an activity with your class. A **waypoint** is an idea students need to understand to meet a curriculum expectation. In our Geography example, one waypoint is understanding that a renewable resource can still be used up. That matters when students explain why a river can run low. The waypoints draw on ACARA content descriptions. Their sequence, examples and teaching suggestions reflect professional judgement; ACARA does not prescribe them. You can use them alongside your learning intentions and success criteria to think through what students already know and where they might need help. The teacher situations in this lesson are made up. -->

## The idea: three ways in

- **Curriculum:** [ACARA Progressions](https://acara.learningwaypoints.com/) shows how ideas build on each other and what students need to understand first.
- **Plan:** Planner and Kit on [learningwaypoints.com](https://learningwaypoints.com/) help you shape a unit from the standards.
- **Lessons:** [Tutorials](https://tutorials.learningwaypoints.com/geography/water-7/resource-types/) use activities, checks and feedback to explore what students understand, on their own or as a class; [Prepare](https://prepare.learningwaypoints.com/) turns a lesson into slides and printouts.

These are the same three menus as learningwaypoints.com. Open each tool and have a look. Use whichever part helps with your planning; there is no set order. The Geography example is a preview, not yet tried with classes.

## Check: is there a required order?

**A colleague already has a unit plan and wants one misconception check. Which advice fits?**

- Use ACARA Progressions because its curriculum map is a ready-made slide deck.
- Use the relevant Tutorials check alongside the unit.
- Complete ACARA Progressions and Prepare before opening Tutorials.
- Use Tutorials only for homework; classroom teaching needs another tool.

* Answer: Use the relevant Tutorials check alongside the unit.
