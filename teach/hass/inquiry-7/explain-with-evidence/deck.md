# Explain with evidence

Investigate like a humanities expert · Sharing what you found

## Today

- I can make a claim and name evidence for it.
- I can explain how evidence supports a claim.
- I can keep an explanation within the evidence.

## Why it matters

> A fact beside a claim does not explain it. The reader needs the connection.

## The idea: claim, evidence, explain

**Match each part of a Kilby River explanation to its job.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `Less rain contributed to less water entering the dam.` | A. Evidence: named findings from records |
| `Rain record G1 shows 20 mm this month versus 60 mm in the same month last year; flow record G2 shows mean daily inflow of 3 ML this month versus 8 ML in that same comparison month.` | B. Reasoning: the connection and its limits |
| `Less rain in the catchment could mean less runoff entering storage; the lower inflow fits this, although other causes need checking.` | C. Claim: the answer being offered |

* Answer: Less rain contributed to less water entering the dam. → C. Claim: the answer being offered; Rain record G1 shows 20 mm this month versus 60 mm in the same month last year; flow record G2 shows mean daily inflow of 3 ML this month versus 8 ML in that same comparison month. → A. Evidence: named findings from records; Less rain in the catchment could mean less runoff entering storage; the lower inflow fits this, although other causes need checking. → B. Reasoning: the connection and its limits

## Apply: write and compare

Choose **your current subject’s** task below, using the supplied records or real sources approved by your teacher. Meet its evidence requirements; another case is optional practice on the same move.

- **Economics:** explain why Parkside cannot buy both. Include the cost table, refer to E1 and E2 and use *scarcity* and *opportunity cost*. Compare with the worked example.
- **Civics:** explain how C1 enables participation. Model: *Notice C1 invites comments before a decision, so people can participate by contributing views during consultation.*
- **Geography:** explain how reduced rain could contribute to lower inflow. Use **both G1 and G2**, connect each to the catchment process and acknowledge other possible influences. Compare with the matching example.
- **History:** explain why the mirror's appearance might have changed. Use **both M1 and M2**, name *cause* or *change* and explain rather than list. Model: *M1 records a polished face; M2 identifies wear as a possible cause of change, so wear could explain reduced shine, although the original appearance remains uncertain.*

Real History work needs verified sources and a historical content check. Write in your own book or document; the site does not collect or mark it. For paragraph organisation, try English's [Inside a paragraph](../../../english/cohesion/inside-a-paragraph/).
