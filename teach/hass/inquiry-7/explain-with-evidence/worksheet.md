## The idea: claim, evidence, explain

ML means megalitres, a measure of water volume. G1 and G2 cover the same two months; they show a comparison, not a complete account of the cause.

**Worked example: why can Parkside not buy both?** Invented quote E1 lists a fridge at $600 and an oven at $500. Budget record E2 sets a $600 limit. These are the only two options in this practice decision, so the oven is the next best alternative if the fridge is chosen.

| Item | Cost in E1 |
|---|---:|
| Fridge | $600 |
| Oven | $500 |
| Both | $1,100 |

*Scarcity forces the tuckshop to choose. Quote E1 shows both goods cost $1,100, above the $600 budget in E2. Buying the fridge uses all available funds, so its opportunity cost is the oven it gives up.* The table makes the comparison visible; the explanation says why it matters.

## Apply: write and compare

Choose **your current subject’s** task below, using the supplied records or real sources approved by your teacher. Meet its evidence requirements; another case is optional practice on the same move.

- **Economics:** explain why Parkside cannot buy both. Include the cost table, refer to E1 and E2 and use *scarcity* and *opportunity cost*. Compare with the worked example.
- **Civics:** explain how C1 enables participation. Model: *Notice C1 invites comments before a decision, so people can participate by contributing views during consultation.*
- **Geography:** explain how reduced rain could contribute to lower inflow. Use **both G1 and G2**, connect each to the catchment process and acknowledge other possible influences. Compare with the matching example.
- **History:** explain why the mirror's appearance might have changed. Use **both M1 and M2**, name *cause* or *change* and explain rather than list. Model: *M1 records a polished face; M2 identifies wear as a possible cause of change, so wear could explain reduced shine, although the original appearance remains uncertain.*

Real History work needs verified sources and a historical content check. Write in your own book or document; the site does not collect or mark it. For paragraph organisation, try English's [Inside a paragraph](../../../english/cohesion/inside-a-paragraph/).
