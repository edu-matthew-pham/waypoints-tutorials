Investigate like a humanities expert · Sharing what you found

## Today

- I can introduce an issue for an audience.
- I can use subject terms accurately in context.

## The idea: make the words do work

**Match the term to its meaning in these cases.**

| | |
|---|---|
| `Opportunity cost` | A. The area of land draining towards a river or other water body |
| `Participation` | B. Taking part in a decision or community activity |
| `Catchment` | C. An object made or changed by people |
| `Artefact` | D. The next best option given up when choosing |

* Answer: Opportunity cost → D. The next best option given up when choosing; Participation → B. Taking part in a decision or community activity; Catchment → A. The area of land draining towards a river or other water body; Artefact → C. An object made or changed by people

## Apply: write for a reader

Choose **your current subject’s** case or class topic and write one short opening. Meet that subject’s checklist below; the other cases show the same move and are available for optional practice.

- **Economics:** address Parkside students, name the menu issue and its relevance, and use *scarcity* and *opportunity cost* accurately.
- **Civics:** describe Mill Road using *council*, *consultation* and *participation*, showing what each means here.
- **Geography:** describe Wattle Flat using *catchment*, *upstream* and *downstream*. Explicitly name the concept of *interconnection*.
- **History:** describe the mirror using *artefact* and *source*, name card M1 and what it records. Do not turn an invented record into a fact about Han society.

For your class topic, use the vocabulary and real evidence your teacher has taught. The site does not collect or mark your writing.
