# Find it and sort it

Before Year 7: HASS · Inquiry skills

<!-- Speaker note: Students choose a way to find out to suit the question, pick key words, and read what an address suggests about who made a page. About 20 minutes (Think first and Try 6 min · The idea 7 min · Check 3 min · Make 4 min.). -->

---

## Today

- I can pick a way to find out that suits my question.
- I can choose key words for a search.
- I can say what an address suggests about who made a page.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Year 6 catch-up for AC9HS6S02. Year 7 Where to look, and how to search builds on it. Addresses vary by country; check what your students see. Teacher: Try: ask what you would do if the first source did not have it. -->

---

## Why it matters

> You want to know when the school garden was started. Search, ask, or look something up?

<!-- Speaker note: Pick **how** to find out to suit the question. You can ask a person, look at records, read a book, or search online. When you search, use **key words** and look at the address: `.gov` for a government, `.edu` for a school, `.com` for a business. The pages here are made up and end in `.example`. -->

## Think first

**When was the school garden started? What is the best first move?**

- Search 'garden'
- Ask the teacher who looks after it, or check school records
- Guess
- Ask a stranger online

<!-- Speaker note: What happens: Ask the teacher or check school records. They would know. A search for 'garden' finds gardens everywhere. Take two or three predictions before anyone runs it. -->

## Find Nib's mistake

**Click the line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Nib wants to know when the school garden was started.
 2  Nib types the whole question, with every word, into a search box.
 3  Nib clicks the first result and writes down what it says.
 4  Nib says: "Done. The first result is always right."
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: the first result is not always right. Nib should use key words, and check who made the page. -->

## Key words

**Which are the best key words for 'When was the school garden started?'**

- the
- garden
- when was the school garden started please tell me

* Answer: school garden started
