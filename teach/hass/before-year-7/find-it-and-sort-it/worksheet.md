> **Key question:** You want to know when the school garden was started. Search, ask, or look something up?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students choose a way to find out to suit the question, pick key words, and read what an address suggests about who made a page.

## The idea: key words and the address


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**Click the line where Nib's thinking goes wrong.**

```
 1  Nib wants to know when the school garden was started.
 2  Nib types the whole question, with every word, into a search box.
 3  Nib clicks the first result and writes down what it says.
 4  Nib says: "Done. The first result is always right."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: the first result is not always right. Nib should use key words, and check who made the page.

:::
