# Three levels of government

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A pothole on your street, a new school and the money in your pocket. Is one government in charge of all three?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students name Australia's three levels of government (local, state or territory, federal) and sort everyday services by which level looks after them. In the ACT, the ACT Government does both state and local jobs.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what a parliament, a court and the Governor-General do. I can name the three levels of government. I can give an example of what each level looks after.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea: each level has its own jobs


:::keep-together

### Find Nib's mistake

**Click the first line where Nib goes wrong.**

```
 1  There's a pothole on Nib's street in Wattle Creek.
 2  Nib wants it fixed before someone trips.
 3  Nib says: "The Prime Minister fixes the potholes on my street, so I'll write to the Prime Minister."
 4  Nib says: "The council has nothing to do with roads."
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the Prime Minister leads the federal government, which doesn't fix local streets. The local council does.

:::


:::keep-together

### Why three levels?

**Why does Australia have three levels of government?**

☐ So one level makes all the laws for the other two

☐ So the federal government can run every council's work

☐ So a state can overrule the federal government

☐ So town, state and country matters each get looked after

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** So town, state and country matters each get looked after

Some things matter mostly to a town, some to a state, and some to the whole country.
