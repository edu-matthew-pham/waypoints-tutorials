Select and present visual arts practice in informal and formal settings

AC9AVA6P01 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Visual arts: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select and present documentation of visual arts practice, and display artworks in informal and/or formal settings

Prior knowledge

Students can share and display artworks in informal settings, produce artist statements and make audience-driven decisions about format (AC9AVA4P01). They have curated basic displays and produced written or spoken statements. They have not yet prepared artworks for formal display, created professional exhibition materials, or used digital encoding methods to accompany their work.

By the end

Students select and present documentation of visual arts practice and display artworks in informal and/or formal settings — making deliberate decisions about space, labelling, audience and format.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • preparing artworks for display within the school environment; for example, measuring and making frames or stands for artworks, using repurposed/recycled materials
  • exploring digital methods of encoding information in artworks to share with audiences; for example, by using image coding software or QR codes
  • working collaboratively to create an online exhibition of student work
  • exploring options for sharing work in public spaces; for example, in café galleries, public exhibition spaces or through community arts events
  • selecting and arranging evidence of their learning from their investigations, experiments and reflections in a way that communicates the development of their ideas and understandings
  • considering artworks that represent a spatial awareness in digital or physical forms; for example, experimenting with Augmented Reality to place collage, objects or forms in space
  • creating a set of art labels to accompany artworks in a class exhibition of student work

Waypoints

  • 1. Preparing artworks for displayStudents can prepare artworks for physical display — attending to measurement, framing, labelling and spatial arrangement.
  • 2. Selecting documentation to communicate developmentStudents can select and arrange evidence of their learning — investigations, experiments, reflections — to communicate the development of their ideas and understanding.
  • 3. Exhibition materials and public sharingStudents can create exhibition materials that support audience engagement — labels, introductions, advertising, reviews — and consider options for sharing work in public spaces.
