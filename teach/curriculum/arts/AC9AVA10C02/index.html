Create artworks reflecting personal expression

AC9AVA10C02 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Visual arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select and manipulate visual conventions, visual arts processes and/or materials to create artworks that reflect personal expression, and represent and/or challenge, ideas, perspectives and/or meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can plan and create artworks with coordinated visual choices and can evaluate feedback in their practice. They make deliberate aesthetic decisions. They have not yet developed a sustained personal voice, self-initiated research process, or created artworks that deliberately challenge established perspectives.

By the end

Students select and manipulate visual conventions, processes and materials to create artworks that reflect personal expression and represent or challenge ideas, perspectives and meaning.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating artworks that realise the intentions and communicate ideas developed through their experimentation and planning
  • researching approaches to representing their ideas and interests in the world/subjects or concepts from a particular viewpoint or a range of viewpoints
  • recording and documenting their research into their subject using their own images or images from other sources, written annotations, and comments and evaluations
  • initiating their own ways of resolving ideas and concepts visually, using creative problem-solving throughout the process
  • creating their own artworks in response to a specific subject, theme or idea, using material, techniques and conventions in intentional, interpretative and personal ways
  • applying their knowledge and understanding of visual arts processes and materials to create artworks

Waypoints

  • 1. Researching and developing personal approachesHingeStudents can research approaches to their subject or concept and develop a direction that reflects a personal point of view — not just reproducing a known style.
  • 2. Creative problem-solving and resolutionStudents can initiate and use creative problem-solving to resolve visual and conceptual challenges throughout the making process.
  • 3. Personal expression in completed artworksStudents can create artworks in which personal expression is evident — using materials, techniques and conventions in intentional, interpretative and personal ways to represent or challenge ideas, perspectives and meaning.
