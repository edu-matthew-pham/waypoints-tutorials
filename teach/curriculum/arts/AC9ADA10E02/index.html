First Nations identity and challenge

AC9ADA10E02 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Dance: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate the ways First Nations Australian choreographers and/or performers celebrate and challenge multiple perspectives of Australian identity through dance

Prior knowledge

Students can investigate the diversity of dance choreographed and/or performed by First Nations Australians, considering ICIP rights (Y7-8, AC9ADA8E02). They understand cultural appropriation and can apply culturally responsive approaches to First Nations dance.

By the end

Students investigate the ways First Nations Australian choreographers and/or performers celebrate and challenge multiple perspectives of Australian identity through dance.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • evaluating ways First Nations Australian performers and choreographers work respectfully with communities to explore local histories and identities
  • investigating dance works that challenge understandings about 'first contacts' between First Nations Peoples and colonisers
  • exploring ways First Nations Australian choreographers use their practice to communicate ideas, messages and lived experiences to the broader community

Waypoints

  • 1. Celebrating and challenging Australian identityHingeStudents can investigate how First Nations Australian choreographers and/or performers use dance to celebrate and challenge perspectives of Australian identity.
  • 2. Communicating lived experience through danceStudents can analyse how First Nations Australian choreographers and/or performers communicate lived experiences and ideas to the broader community through dance.
