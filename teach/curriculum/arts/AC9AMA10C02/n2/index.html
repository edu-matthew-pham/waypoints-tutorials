Ethical and collaborative production

AC9AMA10C02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply ethical, legal and culturally safe production practices — including community collaboration approaches — throughout the production process.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student applies at least two ethical, legal or culturally safe production practices and documents them
  • Student can explain how each practice shaped production decisions

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Production to confirm or challenge expectations.

Width

Minimum
Apply at least two ethical, legal or culturally safe production practices during a production (e.g. copyright compliance, ICIP protocol, community consultation) and document these applications.
Typical
Apply ethical, legal and culturally safe practices throughout production; use Viewpoints questions to evaluate communicative intent during post-production; consider community collaboration protocols when representing local histories or identities; evaluate how production intentions are being communicated at each stage.
Wider
  • Collaborate with a community (using school protocols to contact First Nations Australian or other community groups) to produce a media arts work that explores, challenges or celebrates local histories, cultures or identities — documenting the collaboration process and reflecting on how it shaped the production
  • Produce a media arts work on a social or cultural issue — applying full ethical, legal and cultural safety review at each production stage, and reflecting on how the issue's sensitivity shaped production decisions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierEthical production practice is integrated throughout the production cycle, not a separate compliance step — ensure students treat ethical decisions (community consultation, copyright, ICIP) as production decisions, not administrative requirements.
