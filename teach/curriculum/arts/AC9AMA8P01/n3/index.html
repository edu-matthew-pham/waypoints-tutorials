← AC9AMA8P01 Present media arts works

Evaluating presentation and audience reception

AC9AMA8P01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Media arts: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate how presentation choices affected audience reception and what this reveals about the relationship between presentation context and meaning

Success criteria

  • Reflects on how presentation context affected audience reception
  • Identifies at least one specific aspect that worked and one that would change
  • Evaluation addresses the relationship between presentation choice and audience reception

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Considering audience relationships in presentation.

Width

Minimum
Reflect on how the chosen presentation context affected how audiences received the work; identify at least one thing that worked and one that would change
Typical
Evaluate the effectiveness of presentation choices across different audience contexts; reflect on the relationship between presentation decisions and audience reception; document how evaluation would inform future presentation
Wider
  • Present the same work in different contexts and compare audience responses — evaluate how context changes what the work communicates and what relationship it creates
  • Collect audience feedback and evaluate the gap between intended and received meaning — develop criteria for evaluating presentation effectiveness
  • Evaluate how different audiences within the same cohort interpreted the work differently — analyse what the differences reveal about how media works create meaning

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates responsible practice application (Node 1), audience relationship consideration (Node 2), and production work from C01/C02 into a complete evaluation of how a media arts work reached its audience. Students who can present but cannot evaluate the relationship between presentation choices and audience reception have not met the standard's full demand.
