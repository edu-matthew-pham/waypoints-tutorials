Discover possibilities and develop ideas

AC9AMUFD01 · Foundation The Arts · Music: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use play, imagination, arts knowledge, processes and/or skills to discover possibilities and develop ideas

Prior knowledge

No formal prior arts learning assumed. Students bring informal experience with play, voice and movement from early childhood. This is their first formal encounter with arts skill-development in a school context.

By the end

Students use play, imagination and at least one arts process or skill to discover possibilities and develop ideas across at least one arts form.

Waypoints

  • 1. Exploring through playHingeStudents can use play as a genuine tool for discovering musical or arts possibilities — trying things out without a fixed endpoint.
  • 2. Exploring voice and soundStudents can explore their speaking and singing voices and a range of sound sources to discover ways of communicating ideas and feelings.
  • 3. Responding to and interpreting musicStudents can respond to music they hear through movement, image-making or vocalisation, and describe one feeling or idea the music gave them.
  • 4. Developing and extending an ideaStudents can use a discovered possibility to develop a musical or arts idea — taking one thing they found through play and doing something intentional with it.
