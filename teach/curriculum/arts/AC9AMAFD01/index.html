Discover possibilities and develop ideas

AC9AMAFD01 · Foundation The Arts · Media arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use play, imagination, arts knowledge, processes and/or skills to discover possibilities and develop ideas

Prior knowledge

Students have experience with free play and exploration of materials, voice, movement and basic digital devices. They have not yet deliberately used play as a strategy for discovering creative possibilities in media arts.

By the end

Students use play, imagination and arts processes to discover new possibilities and develop ideas for media arts works.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • playing with the tactile qualities of a range of materials (rough, smooth, spikey, gooey) and exploring how they feel about these qualities to develop ideas for a visual artwork, dance or soundscape
  • imagining how the characters in a story they are reading might dress or move, and making still or moving images to communicate ideas about the characters
  • identifying and describing shapes, colours and lines they observe in their environment and exploring ways to use these shapes creatively
  • playing games to develop subject-specific practices such as pitch, beat and/or rhythm recognition; exploring ways that body parts, zones and bases can be moved safely and expressively; taking on the point of view of a fictional character in dramatic play
  • responding to the emotions they experience while listening to different pieces of music and using movement to share their response
  • improvising rhythmic patterns for clapping or stamping, or inventing new movements for familiar action songs, and interpreting these visually using elements of art/design such as shapes, lines and/or colours
  • exploring their speaking and singing voices and discovering ways they can use their voices/vocalisation to communicate ideas and feelings
  • improvising movements to explain the steps in a process, and then using a camera to capture a series of images/photographic story that can be displayed in the classroom to remind everybody about the process

Waypoints

  • 1. Playing to discoverHingeStudents can engage in purposeful play with materials, movement, voice or technology and notice what happens — identifying at least one discovery or surprise.
  • 2. Imagining possibilitiesStudents can use imagination to extend what they observe or know — asking 'what if?' questions to develop new ideas for arts works.
  • 3. Developing and documenting ideasStudents can translate discoveries from play and imagination into a developing idea for an arts work — using a tool, material or device to record or represent the idea.
