Playing to discover

AC9AMAFD01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Foundation The Arts · Media arts: developing practices and skills · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can engage in purposeful play with materials, movement, voice or technology and notice what happens — identifying at least one discovery or surprise.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student can describe or demonstrate something they discovered while playing with a material, tool or technology
  • Student can name at least one way this discovery could be used to communicate an idea

Prior knowledge

Students have experience with free play and exploration of materials, voice, movement and basic digital devices. They have not yet deliberately used play as a strategy for discovering creative possibilities in media arts.

Width

Minimum
Engage with one material, movement quality, or technology in free play — identify one thing they discovered or that surprised them.
Typical
Explore multiple materials and technologies through play (tactile materials, camera, voice, movement); respond to the emotions or discoveries that arise; describe how different qualities (rough, smooth, loud, quiet) could be used to communicate ideas.
Wider
  • Play with the tactile qualities of materials and use movement, voice or digital tools to embody the qualities discovered — connecting sensory exploration to idea generation
  • Respond to music through movement — using scarves, drawing or digital capture to document the response and discussing how the music changed what was discovered

Hinge

If students cannot engage in purposeful discovery through play, the idea-development strand of this standard collapses — play is the primary mechanism for generating ideas at Foundation level.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepVary the play contexts — tactile materials, digital tools, voice, movement — to ensure students discover possibilities across the full range of media arts modalities available at Foundation.
