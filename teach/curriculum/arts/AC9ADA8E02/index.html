Diversity of First Nations dance

AC9ADA8E02 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Dance: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate the diversity of dance choreographed and/or performed by First Nations Australians considering culturally responsive approaches to Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property rights

Prior knowledge

Students have some awareness that First Nations Australians have diverse dance traditions. They have not yet investigated this diversity systematically or engaged with ICIP rights frameworks in dance contexts.

By the end

Students investigate the diversity of First Nations Australian dance with genuine cultural responsiveness, understanding and applying ICIP rights frameworks.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring information about ICIP rights to help dance practitioners make ethical choices
  • using and annotating a map of First Nations Australian language groups to identify where First Nations choreographers and performers are working
  • analysing how linear and non-linear narrative is used in specific First Nations Australian dance works
  • investigating cultural appropriation examples where ICIP rights have been denied and how situations could have been approached respectfully

Waypoints

  • 1. Understanding ICIP rights in dance contextsStudents can explain what ICIP rights mean in dance contexts and describe what culturally responsive engagement with First Nations dance looks like in practice
  • 2. Investigating diversity of First Nations Australian danceStudents can investigate the diversity of First Nations Australian dance practices across regions and contexts with cultural responsiveness
  • 3. Applying culturally responsive approaches in danceundefined
