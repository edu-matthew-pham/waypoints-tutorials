← AC9ADA8C02 Apply skills to communicate meaning

Integrated application of skills across a complete dance

AC9ADA8C02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Dance: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply technical and expressive skills consistently across a complete dance to communicate intended ideas, perspectives or meaning

Success criteria

  • Demonstrates at least two deliberate technical and expressive applications in a complete dance
  • Applications are connected to intended meaning — not just demonstrations of skill
  • Can evaluate whether the application communicated the intended ideas

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Using feedback to refine expressive and technical application.

Width

Minimum
Perform or present a dance that demonstrates at least two deliberate technical and expressive skill applications connected to intended meaning
Typical
Perform or present a dance demonstrating consistent and deliberate application of technical and expressive skills across the whole work; evaluate whether the application successfully communicates the intended meaning
Wider
  • Apply genre- or style-specific techniques with sufficient mastery that they carry the expressive content of the style — evaluate whether technique has moved from imitation to genuine stylistic expression
  • Perform the same dance material with deliberately different technical and expressive choices — evaluate how the changes alter what the dance communicates
  • Reflect on the relationship between technical mastery and expressive freedom — evaluate how technical development has expanded rather than constrained expressive possibilities

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates purposeful technical application (Node 1), feedback-driven refinement (Node 2), and material developed through AC9ADA8C01 into a complete performed work. The coordination of technical and expressive skills across a whole dance is the learning — students who can apply skills in isolation but cannot sustain them across a complete work have not met the standard.
