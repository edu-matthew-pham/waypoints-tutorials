Investigate composers and performers across contexts

AC9AMU8E01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate the ways that composers and/or performers use the elements of music and/or compositional devices in music composed across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students can explore ways that the elements of music are combined in music across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts (AC9AMU6E01). They have explored rather than investigated — they have not yet analysed how elements and devices are used with the specific purpose of informing their own performance or composition decisions.

By the end

Students investigate how composers and/or performers use elements of music and compositional devices in music across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts, using this investigation to describe stylistic features and inform their own practice.

Waypoints

  • 1. Accessing and analysing music across contextsStudents can access and analyse music from a range of cultural, historical or contextual sources, identifying how elements of music are manipulated and how compositional devices are used
  • 2. Describing stylistic features of genres and stylesStudents can describe the stylistic features of a musical style or genre using music terminology, based on analytical listening
  • 3. Analysing performer interpretation of composer intentionsStudents can analyse how performers interpret composers' intentions, identifying specific choices about tempo, instrumentation, feel or articulation and evaluating their effect
  • 4. Using investigation to inform own practiceStudents can use their investigation of composers' and performers' use of elements and devices to inform specific choices in their own performance or composition
