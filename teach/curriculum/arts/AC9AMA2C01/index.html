Construct representations

AC9AMA2C01 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use media languages and media technologies to construct representations

Prior knowledge

Students can create arts works that communicate a recognisable idea and share them informally. They have explored media technologies through play. They have not yet deliberately applied media languages (shot types, composition) or production choices to construct representations with communicative intent.

By the end

Students use media languages and media technologies to construct representations that communicate identifiable ideas or stories.

Waypoints

  • 1. Media languages — what they are and why they matterHingeStudents can identify at least two media language features (e.g. shot types: long, mid, close-up) and explain what each communicates to an audience.
  • 2. Constructing with media technologiesStudents can use a media technology to capture, sequence or edit media elements — making at least one deliberate compositional decision.
  • 3. Representations communicate ideasStudents can construct a complete representation — a media arts work that communicates an identifiable story or idea to an audience.
