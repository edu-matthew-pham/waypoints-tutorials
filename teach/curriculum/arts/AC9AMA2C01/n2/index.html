Constructing with media technologies

AC9AMA2C01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use a media technology to capture, sequence or edit media elements — making at least one deliberate compositional decision.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student uses a media technology to capture or create at least three media elements
  • Student makes at least one deliberate composition decision (selection, sequencing, or captioning) and can explain the reason for it

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Media languages — what they are and why they matter.

Width

Minimum
Use a digital device to capture and sequence at least three images or sounds, making at least one deliberate choice (which image to keep, how to order them, what caption to add).
Typical
Capture and sequence images and text to create a media arts work (comic book, captioned sequence, voice-over story); select, edit and delete elements based on purpose; apply shot type decisions during capture; combine visual and audio elements.
Wider
  • Explore claymation or simple animation by creating a character using images and/or sounds — making deliberate composition decisions at each stage and reflecting on what each choice communicates
  • Experiment with retelling a school day story using a digital camera to capture still or moving images and adding appropriate captions — reviewing and selecting the best images with Viewpoints questions: 'What images will I keep or delete?'

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsThe mechanics of capture, sequencing and selection require practice — students who rush through production without deliberate selection and review produce accidental rather than intentional representations.
