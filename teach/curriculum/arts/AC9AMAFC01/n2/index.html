Making and constructing

AC9AMAFC01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Foundation The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use available materials or technologies to make an arts work that embodies their idea.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student creates an arts work using at least one material or technology
  • Student can describe what their work communicates or what it is about

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Idea to arts form.

Width

Minimum
Create one arts work (e.g. a sequence of captured images, a voice-over recording, a puppet retelling) using at least one available material or technology.
Typical
Create arts works across two or more forms or materials — combining images, sounds, objects or digital tools to communicate ideas; manipulate choices (which images to keep, how to sequence events, which sounds to add).
Wider
  • Repurpose everyday materials (boxes, clothing) as environments or characters, and document the creation using a digital device
  • Devise and record a chant or rap on a digital device that communicates a message or retells a story — sharing via a school-managed platform
  • Explore claymation or stop-motion animation by manipulating objects and capturing a sequence of images

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe making process at Foundation integrates idea, material and tool — support students in connecting their intended communication with the choices they make during construction rather than treating making as separate from meaning.
Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepVary the making modalities — digital capture, voice, object manipulation, repurposed materials — so students who are ready encounter the full diversity of making contexts available at Foundation.
