Comparing across cultures, times and places

AC9AVA4E01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Visual arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can make connections and comparisons between artists working across cultures, times and places — identifying similarities and differences and explaining how context accounts for them.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Compares artworks from at least two different cultures, times or places
  • Identifies at least one similarity and one difference, and suggests how context might explain the difference

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Artist context and approach.

Width

Minimum
Compare two artworks from different cultures, times or places; identify one similarity and one difference and suggest how context might explain the difference.
Typical
Make connections and comparisons across multiple artists and contexts; investigate how the same theme (sustainability, celebration, community) is approached differently; discuss how artworks develop social awareness; explore multiple perspectives on the same event.
Wider
  • Compare artworks addressing sustainability from different cultural contexts — identify how land art, works using recycled materials, and representations of climate effects each reflect their makers' relationships to the environment
  • Explore artworks by First Nations Australian artists that represent connection to Country/Place — compare with artworks by non-First Nations artists representing the same landscapes

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierComparative analysis draws on close observation skills and context understanding to produce a higher-order claim: that differences between artworks reflect differences in context, not just differences in style.
