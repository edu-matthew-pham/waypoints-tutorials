← AC9AMA8C02 Apply production processes and concepts

Applying responsible media practice throughout

AC9AMA8C02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply responsible media practice consistently throughout production — copyright, ICIP rights, ethical representation, safety and legality

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Identifies and applies at least one responsible practice obligation
  • Explains how the obligation is met in specific production decisions
  • Responsible practice is built into the production — not just acknowledged at the end

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Applying media arts concepts to construct representations.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one responsible media practice obligation relevant to own production; explain how it is applied in the work
Typical
Apply responsible media practice throughout the production process; explain how copyright, ICIP rights and ethical representation obligations are met in specific production decisions
Wider
  • Produce a work that explores how people are engaging in social justice or sustainability issues — evaluate how responsible practice principles apply to sensitive social content
  • Construct representations that are safe, legal, ethical and responsible for cultural or social groups represented — develop and apply explicit criteria throughout production
  • Reflect on how responsible media practice has shaped production decisions — evaluate moments where responsible practice constrained or redirected creative choices

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates production pipeline application (Node 1), concept application (Node 2), and ICIP/ethical understanding from E02 into a complete production that maintains responsible practice throughout. Students who can produce technically but treat responsible practice as an afterthought rather than a design principle have not met the standard's full demand.
