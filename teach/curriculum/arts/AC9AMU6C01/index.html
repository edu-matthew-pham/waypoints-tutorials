Manipulate elements and use compositional devices

AC9AMU6C01 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Music: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

manipulate elements of music and use compositional devices to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning when composing and practising music for performance, and notate, document and/or record the music they compose

Prior knowledge

Students can manipulate elements of music to communicate ideas and practise for performance, using notation to document work (AC9AMU4C01). They can use multiple documentation formats and work collaboratively on accompaniments. They have not yet applied formal compositional devices (form, contrast, repetition) to structure compositions, or composed for specific instruments within technical constraints.

By the end

Students manipulate elements of music and apply compositional devices to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning, notating, documenting or recording the work in a format that supports rehearsal and performance.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • working collaboratively to compose music in response to an image, theme or question
  • improvising and experimenting with combinations of sounds and/or using digital tools to create moods and atmospheres
  • rehearsing a range of unison and part music individually and/or in an ensemble
  • using a limited range of pitches to compose melodies for a selected instrument
  • arranging or re-imagining a known work; for example, writing new lyrics and arranging for available instruments
  • composing a melody, manipulating elements of music such as rhythm, pitch, dynamics, texture or timbre, and using a compositional device/s to structure the music
  • using voice/vocalisation and sounds such as body percussion or drumming to develop a song, chant or soundscape for a Welcome to Country or Acknowledgement of Country
  • using digital tools, voices and instruments to compose, notate, document and/or record music

Waypoints

  • 1. What compositional devices areHingeStudents can identify and describe at least two compositional devices (e.g. repetition, contrast, a given form such as verse/chorus or binary) and recognise them when listening.
  • 2. Using compositional devices to structure a compositionStudents can use at least one compositional device to structure a composition with a clear communicative intention.
  • 3. Composing within technical constraintsStudents can compose a melody or instrumental part within a defined technical constraint (e.g. a limited pitch range, specific instruments) and use digital tools to complete the work.
  • 4. Notating, documenting and recording compositionsStudents can notate, document or record a composition in a format appropriate to the type of music and suitable for use in rehearsal and performance.
