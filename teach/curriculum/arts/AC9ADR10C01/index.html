Improvise, devise and interpret

AC9ADR10C01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Drama: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

improvise and devise drama, and interpret scripted drama, using elements of drama and conventions to shape and manipulate dramatic action and convey intended ideas, perspectives and/or meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can improvise, devise and interpret scripted drama, manipulating elements and applying conventions relevant to style/form (Y7-8). They can evaluate and refine dramatic work and develop performance skills for specific styles.

By the end

Students improvise and devise drama, and interpret scripted drama, using elements of drama and conventions to shape and manipulate dramatic action and convey intended ideas, perspectives and/or meaning.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using a teacher-provided framework to develop drama collaboratively
  • considering contexts such as audiences, viewpoints, intentions and style/form when planning interpretations
  • conveying tensions through manipulation of language, movement and symbol
  • devising drama presenting a range of perspectives using non-linear structures
  • scripting scenes from improvisation using play-building techniques
  • using props, set items or digital tools to enhance communication of meaning

Waypoints

  • 1. Working within a framework to develop dramatic intentionHingeStudents can use a teacher-provided or self-generated framework to develop a clear dramatic intention — specifying audience, viewpoint, purpose, style and form before devising.
  • 2. Manipulating elements and conventions to convey meaningStudents can select and manipulate elements of drama and conventions to shape dramatic action that conveys intended meaning — including tension, character relationships and symbolic communication.
