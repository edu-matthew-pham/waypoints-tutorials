Investigate drama to challenge meaning

AC9ADR10E01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Drama: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate use of elements of drama, performance skills and/or conventions to communicate and/or challenge ideas, perspectives and/or meaning in drama across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students can investigate how elements and conventions communicate meaning in drama across cultures, times and places (Y7-8). They can analyse and evaluate use of elements and conventions in drama they experience or participate in.

By the end

Students investigate how elements of drama, performance skills and conventions are used to communicate and/or challenge ideas, perspectives and/or meaning in drama across cultures, times, places and other contexts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • viewing a performance and considering how elements use symbol or movement to make meaning
  • investigating ways drama texts are created and developed collaboratively
  • viewing examples of varied styles within a style to understand shared and differing conventions
  • using Viewpoints to ask questions about social context, cultural movements and historical influences
  • viewing historical texts where artists use contemporary performance conventions to affect meaning

Waypoints

  • 1. Drama that challenges — identifying the 'challenge' moveHingeStudents can identify when and how drama is being used to challenge — not just communicate — ideas, perspectives or meaning, and describe the specific dramatic devices doing that work.
  • 2. Investigating conventions, context and cultural challenge across dramaStudents can investigate how performance conventions, context and cultural position shape whether and how drama communicates or challenges — across diverse cultural, historical and contemporary examples.
