Curating and presenting a coherent exhibition

AC9AVA8P01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Visual arts: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can make deliberate curation decisions about which works to present, how to arrange them, and how documentation supports the exhibition's communicative intent

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Presents artworks with documentation in a chosen context
  • Makes and explains at least two deliberate curation decisions
  • Curation choices are connected to communicative intent — not just aesthetic preference

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Creating documentation for artworks.

Width

Minimum
Present at least one artwork with documentation in a chosen context; explain at least two curation decisions made and why
Typical
Curate a selection of works for presentation; make deliberate decisions about selection, arrangement and documentation; explain how curation choices support the exhibition's communicative intent
Wider
  • Create a themed series of artworks curated for display — use Viewpoints questions to guide decisions about visual narrative, spatial relationships and audience experience
  • Create a digital presentation (webpage, walkthrough) to connect geographically distanced audiences with a visual arts showcase — evaluate the specific curation decisions needed for digital presentation
  • Develop an exhibition that tells a story through the arrangement and relationships between works — document the curatorial thinking behind the visual narrative

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates understanding of curation principles (Node 1), documentation writing skills (Node 2), and artwork creation from AC9AVA8C02 into a coherent presented exhibition. The coordination of selection, arrangement, documentation and communicative intent is the learning — students who can create artworks and write labels but cannot make the curation decisions that connect them into a coherent whole have not reached this node.
