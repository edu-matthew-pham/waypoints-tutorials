Share arts works with audiences

AC9AMAFP01 · Foundation The Arts · Media arts: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

share their arts works with audiences

Prior knowledge

Students have experience sharing work informally with teachers during play or activity time, but have not yet engaged in deliberate presentation to an audience — where the relationship between maker and viewer is conscious and considered.

By the end

Students share arts works with an audience and engage with that audience's responses.

Waypoints

  • 1. Preparing to shareStudents can prepare a work to share — selecting what to present and thinking about who will see or hear it.
  • 2. Presenting to an audienceHingeStudents can share an arts work with an audience and explain what it communicates or how it was made.
  • 3. Responding to audienceStudents can engage with an audience's response to their work — listening to feedback and reflecting on what others noticed or felt.
