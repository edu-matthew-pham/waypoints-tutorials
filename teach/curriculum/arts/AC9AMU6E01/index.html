Elements of music across cultures and contexts

AC9AMU6E01 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore ways that the elements of music are combined in music across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students have explored music for similar purposes across cultures and compared performance and audience expectations (AC9AMU4E01). They can identify how elements are used in a piece and describe purposes. The new demand is specifically on how elements are combined — and on evaluation of personal response and musical preferences using music terminology.

By the end

Students explore ways that elements of music are combined in music across cultures, times, places and other contexts, evaluating personal responses and describing how structural features create effects.

Waypoints

  • 1. How elements are combined in diverse musicStudents can describe how multiple elements of music are combined in a piece and identify the effect of those combinations.
  • 2. Evaluating personal responses with reasonsStudents can evaluate and share personal preferences for music from diverse contexts, using both their own words and music terminology, and giving reasons.
  • 3. Structural features and music in social contextsStudents can identify structural features (riffs, strumming patterns, song forms) and describe how music is used in celebrations, ceremonies or rituals in at least two contexts.
