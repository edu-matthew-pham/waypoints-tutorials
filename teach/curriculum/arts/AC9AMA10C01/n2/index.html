Pre-production design

AC9AMA10C01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use pre-production tools to design and structure a media arts work — making deliberate decisions about how technical and symbolic codes will examine and communicate ideas for an intended audience.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student produces a pre-production document for a planned media arts work
  • Student can explain at least three design decisions in terms of the ideas, perspectives or meaning to be examined

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Critical analysis of genre conventions.

Width

Minimum
Produce a pre-production document (shot list, storyboard, shooting script, treatment or screenplay) for a planned media arts work — including at least three design decisions explicitly linked to the ideas, perspectives or meaning to be examined.
Typical
Use pre-production processes to design and structure media arts works across two or more forms — documenting how camera work, sound, editing, mise-en-scène or media technologies will construct representations; trial ideas for different production approaches before committing; design alternative representations of the same subject.
Wider
  • Design a media arts work using a storyboard and shooting script — then design an alternative version of the same work that challenges the representations constructed in the first version, comparing how each communicates differently to audiences
  • Design a social media campaign or community service announcement using detailed pre-production documentation — including target audience analysis, platform selection reasoning, and representation design rationale

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsPre-production design discipline at Y9-10 requires sustained investment — students who use pre-production tools superficially produce works that are not designed but assembled. Thorough pre-production is the difference between examination and illustration.
