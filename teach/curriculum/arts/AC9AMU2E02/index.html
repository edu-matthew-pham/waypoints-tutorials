First Nations Australian music

AC9AMU2E02 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore examples of music composed and/or performed by First Nations Australians

Prior knowledge

Students have explored how arts are important for communities (AC9AMUFE01) including listening to First Nations Australians talk about arts and Country/Place. They have explored music from diverse cultural contexts (AC9AMU2E01). This is their first focused exploration of music specifically by First Nations Australian composers and performers.

By the end

Students explore examples of music composed and/or performed by First Nations Australians across diverse styles, asking questions about instruments, voices, cultural messages and connections to Country/Place.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying where and how they can experience music composed and/or performed by First Nations Australians, such as via mainstream music platforms, music for ceremonies, or state and national events
  • exploring the diverse music styles represented in music composed and performed by First Nations Australians
  • exploring how the elements of music are used to create specific effects in screen-based or theatrical works that communicate First Nations Australian cultural knowledge

Waypoints

  • 1. Where First Nations Australian music is heardStudents can identify at least two contexts in which they can experience music composed or performed by First Nations Australians.
  • 2. Listening to diverse First Nations Australian music stylesHingeStudents can listen to examples of First Nations Australian music across diverse styles and identify instruments, voices and features of the music.
  • 3. Music communicating messages about Country/PlaceStudents can explore how First Nations Australian music communicates messages about Country/Place, asking questions about how musical elements are used to carry those messages.
