← AC9AVA8C02 Select and manipulate for meaning

Creating artworks that communicate ideas and meaning

AC9AVA8C02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Visual arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create an artwork where visual convention, process and material choices work together to communicate ideas, perspectives or meaning to an audience

Success criteria

  • Creates an artwork that realises at least two intended ideas or meanings
  • Convention, process and material choices are deliberate and connected to intended meaning
  • Can explain what the artwork communicates and how specific choices serve that communication

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Working across media and processes.

Width

Minimum
Create an artwork that realises at least two intended ideas or meanings through deliberate convention, process or material choices
Typical
Create an artwork demonstrating consistent and considered selection and manipulation of conventions, processes and materials; reflect on whether the artwork communicates the intended meaning effectively
Wider
  • Create an artwork that explores personal feelings or emotions — evaluate whether the visual choices successfully externalise an internal experience
  • Create a work (independently or collaboratively) that tells a story or creates awareness about a significant issue — evaluate how visual choices support the communicative intent
  • Develop an extended artistic practice across a unit using a visual diary — reflect on how convention, process and material choices have evolved as the concept has deepened

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates deliberate convention selection (Node 1), purposeful process and material choice (Node 2), and planning/development work from AC9AVA8C01 into a completed artwork. The coordination of all these elements into a coherent communicative whole is the learning — students who can make individual deliberate choices but cannot sustain those choices across a complete work have not reached this node.
