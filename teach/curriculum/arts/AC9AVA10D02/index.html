Reflect on influences on visual arts practice

AC9AVA10D02 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Visual arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

reflect on the way they and other visual artists respond to influences to inspire, develop and resolve choices they make in their own visual arts practice

Prior knowledge

Students have begun identifying an emerging personal style and have investigated artists from diverse contexts. They can make informed choices based on documented experimentation. They have not yet systematically reflected on how personal, cultural and contextual influences shape their own practice, or used investigation of others' influences to understand their own.

By the end

Students reflect on the ways they and other visual artists respond to influences — using this understanding to inspire, develop and resolve choices in their own visual arts practice.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating and reflecting on the role and influence of their own culture, family or personal experiences when developing ideas for artworks
  • investigating a diverse range of visual artists; for example, artists who live and work in a different place, such as a country in the Asia region
  • analysing and reflecting on the ways that artists have structured their artworks to represent ideas
  • reflecting on, evaluating and resolving their choices for representing their ideas as their work progresses
  • exploring current issues that are of importance to them, such as global or local issues, as a starting point for generating and reflecting on ideas for artworks
  • investigating the ways that artists use their visual arts practice to explore, examine, resolve or represent personal experiences and expressions

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying personal influencesHingeStudents can investigate and reflect on the role of their own culture, family and personal experiences as influences on their visual arts practice.
  • 2. Investigating other artists' influencesStudents can investigate how other artists respond to their own influences — examining accounts of their practices to understand the many factors that can shape a visual arts practice.
  • 3. Using influence understanding to resolve choicesStudents can use their understanding of personal and external influences to inspire, develop and resolve choices in their own visual arts practice.
