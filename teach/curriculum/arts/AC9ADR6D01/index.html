Combine elements to communicate

AC9ADR6D01 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Drama: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore ways to combine the elements of drama to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning in improvisations, devised drama and/or scripted drama

Prior knowledge

Students can use elements of drama — voice, movement, language, space — in combination to develop dramatic action in improvisations and devised drama (Y3-4). They can make deliberate element choices and describe their effect.

By the end

Students explore ways to combine elements of drama to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning in improvisations, devised drama and/or scripted drama.

Waypoints

  • 1. Using empathy and perspective to develop combination choicesHingeStudents can use empathy to explore how combining elements differently communicates different perspectives and challenges stereotypes.
  • 2. Combining elements across forms — improvised, devised and scriptedStudents can combine elements of drama purposefully across improvised, devised and scripted forms, evaluating which combinations communicate most effectively.
