← AC9ADR6D01 Combine elements to communicate

Combining elements across forms — improvised, devised and scripted

AC9ADR6D01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Drama: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can combine elements of drama purposefully across improvised, devised and scripted forms, evaluating which combinations communicate most effectively.

Success criteria

  • Student combines elements of drama purposefully in at least two different forms
  • Student evaluates the effectiveness of a specific element combination and suggests an alternative

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Using empathy and perspective to develop combination choices.

Width

Minimum
Student compares how element combinations produce different effects in at least two different dramatic forms.
Typical
Students use improvisation or process drama to compare how improvised and scripted drama create characters and action; they explore how elements combine differently across forms; they consider how cultural understandings shape meaning through element combinations.
Wider
  • Students evaluate drama they experience in live performance, identifying specific element combinations, and consider how to apply those techniques in their own work
  • Students explore drama from another culture or historical period and investigate how element combinations reflect that culture's dramatic conventions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.
