Reflect on media arts works to inform practice

AC9AMA8D02 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Media arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

reflect on their own and others' media arts works and practices to inform choices they make during the production process

Prior knowledge

Students can use feedback to reflect on own media arts practice (AC9AMA6D02). They have not yet investigated others' media arts works and practices systematically as a source of influence, or used structured reflection to document and direct production decisions.

By the end

Students reflect on own and others' media arts works to make and document informed production choices throughout the production process.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • observing and considering potential audience engagement to inform refinements across production processes
  • using Viewpoints to develop investigating questions about representing themes, concepts or ideas
  • documenting and explaining creative choices in relation to representations, genre codes and conventions
  • justifying decisions made by producers in construction of representations through technical and symbolic codes
  • using a storyboarding or editing template to annotate creative thinking when refining production
  • using Viewpoints for reflective questions about representing views and creating audience connection
  • exploring styles and representations from other cultural contexts to inform practice and understand cultural appropriation

Waypoints

  • 1. Documenting and explaining creative choicesStudents can document and explain their creative choices in relation to representations, genre codes and conventions with reference to intended meaning
  • 2. Reflecting on audience engagementStudents can reflect on potential audience engagement and use that reflection to refine production choices
  • 3. Investigating others' practices to inform ownStudents can investigate other media arts works and practices and use insights to inform deliberate choices in own production
