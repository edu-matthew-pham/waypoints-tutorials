Manipulating vs selecting elements

AC9AMU4C01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Music: creating and making · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students understand that manipulating elements means actively changing, shaping and controlling them throughout a composition — not just choosing them at the start.

Success criteria

  • Demonstrates at least one active manipulation of an element within a composition (not just its initial selection)
  • Can describe the effect of the manipulation on the music's mood or meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can select and combine elements of music when composing (AC9AMU2C01) and have practised documentation using signs, symbols and recordings. They can combine two elements for expressive effect and justify their selections. They have not yet manipulated elements strategically to communicate specific ideas, perspectives or meaning across a sustained composition.

Width

Minimum
Demonstrate one manipulation of a musical element during a composition (e.g. making a section get louder over time, or changing a melody's rhythm) and explain what effect the manipulation creates.
Typical
Manipulate elements dynamically within compositions — varying dynamics across sections, extending or shortening rhythmic values, changing tempo for dramatic effect, altering articulation; describe how each manipulation shapes the music's meaning or mood.
Wider
  • Compose a section where one element (e.g. dynamics) gradually changes across the whole section — describe what that arc communicates
  • Take a known song and deliberately manipulate two elements (e.g. tempo and articulation) to create a completely different mood — compare before and after

Hinge

The conceptual leap from 'selecting elements' (Y1-2) to 'manipulating elements' is the core of this standard. Students who remain at selection level — choosing elements but not actively shaping them throughout — cannot meet the descriptor's command term and cannot access AC9AMU6C01 which requires manipulating elements plus compositional devices.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkMake the distinction explicit: 'Selecting means choosing which elements to use. Manipulating means changing how an element behaves during the music — making it grow, shrink, shift, transform.' Use guided analysis of recordings to identify manipulations before students attempt them in their own compositions.
