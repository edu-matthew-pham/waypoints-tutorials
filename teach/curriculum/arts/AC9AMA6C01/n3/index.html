Constructing for specific audiences

AC9AMA6C01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can construct a media arts work using advanced production techniques — applying genre conventions and reflecting on whether the work communicates its intended meaning to the specific audience.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student produces a media arts work for a specific audience applying at least two genre conventions deliberately
  • Student can explain whether and how the work successfully communicates its intended meaning to that audience

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Production planning for audiences.

Width

Minimum
Produce one media arts work for a specific audience, applying at least two genre conventions deliberately, and reflect on how successfully the work communicates to that audience.
Typical
Produce media arts works across two or more genres and forms (genre short film, advertisement, game design, music video section) using chroma key, special effects, editing, sound effects and film language; apply genre conventions deliberately; reflect using Viewpoints questions on whether production choices serve the audience.
Wider
  • Create a short genre film using chroma key, special effects, editing, sound effects and film language — applying all five elements deliberately and evaluating how each serves the intended genre audience
  • Create a section of a music video that brings together music, dance and media arts — considering how the codes and conventions of music videos structure the audience experience, and reflecting on what was successfully communicated

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierConstruction for specific audiences integrates genre knowledge, pre-production planning and production skills — the produced work should be evaluated against the intended audience relationship, not just as an object.
