← AC9ADA10P01 Rehearse and perform with genre skills

Genre/style technique in performance

AC9ADA10P01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Dance: presenting and performing · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply genre- or style-specific techniques with technical and expressive skill in formal performance — communicating ideas, perspectives or meaning authentically within the style.

Success criteria

  • Student applies at least two genre- or style-specific techniques recognisably in performance
  • Student uses at least one production element purposefully — enhancing rather than distracting from the communicative intent
  • Student's performance communicates the intended ideas, perspectives or meaning — evidenced through audience response or reflection

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Performance entry/exit and spatial fluency.

Width

Minimum
Performing in a selected genre/style with at least two identifiable style-specific techniques — demonstrating both technical quality and expressive communication.
Typical
Applying technical and expressive skills and genre- or style-specific techniques when performing learned and/or choreographed dances; using available production elements (music, costume, props) to enhance communication of ideas, perspectives or meaning.
Wider
  • Perform in a culturally specific genre/style with input or guidance from community representatives — demonstrating authentic engagement beyond surface imitation
  • Perform in more than one genre/style context during the year — comparing the performance demands and audience relationships each context creates

Hinge

Genre/style-authentic performance — not just technically competent movement — is the standard for this band. Students who cannot yet apply style-specific technique in performance context have not met the descriptor's demand and are not prepared for post-school performance or arts industry pathways.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierGenre-authentic performance integrates technical skill, expressive skill, style-specific technique, and production element use into a communicative event. The audience relationship is the test: does this read as this style/genre to an informed observer?
