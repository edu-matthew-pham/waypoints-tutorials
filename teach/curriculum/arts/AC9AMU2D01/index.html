Develop listening and performance skills

AC9AMU2D01 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Music: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop listening skills and skills for singing and playing instruments

Prior knowledge

Students have explored their speaking and singing voices through play (AC9AMUFD01) and discovered ways voice can communicate ideas. They have not yet formally practised listening skills for specific musical purposes, ensemble blending, or instrumental technique.

By the end

Students develop listening skills for specific musical purposes and demonstrate developing skills for singing and playing instruments across a range of sound sources.

Waypoints

  • 1. Listening for specific purposesHingeStudents can listen to music for at least one specific purpose (e.g. to identify tempo, match pitch, follow a count-in, or stay with the group's dynamic level).
  • 2. Exploring and varying sound sourcesStudents can explore how sounds from a range of sources (voice, classroom instruments, found objects, digital instruments) can be varied using elements of music.
  • 3. Pitch awareness and matchingStudents can demonstrate developing pitch awareness — attempting to match a given pitch or adjust their singing toward a target pitch.
  • 4. Singing and playing techniqueStudents can demonstrate basic technique for singing songs and playing classroom instruments with developing control.
