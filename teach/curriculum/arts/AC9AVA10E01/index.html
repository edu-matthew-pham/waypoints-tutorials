Artists develop personal expression across cultures and contexts

AC9AVA10E01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Visual arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate the ways that artists across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts develop personal expression in their visual arts practice to represent, communicate and/or challenge ideas, perspectives and/or meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can compare how artists from different contexts communicate ideas and examine multiple perspectives on artworks (AC9AVA8E01, implied from progression). They have developed critical questioning skills. They have not yet produced extended critical analysis, selected from multiple critical viewpoints, or investigated the historical development of a visual arts form.

By the end

Students investigate how artists across cultures, times, places and other contexts develop personal expression to represent, communicate and challenge ideas, perspectives and meaning.

Waypoints

  • 1. Analysing personal expression in contextHingeStudents can analyse how an artist's personal expression is shaped by their cultural, historical and contextual position — and how this shapes what the artwork represents, communicates or challenges.
  • 2. Multiple critical viewpointsStudents can select different critical viewpoints (formal, contextual, socio-political, cultural) to develop different explanations of the same artwork or artist's approach.
  • 3. Extended critical accountStudents can produce an extended written, oral or multimedia account that investigates how an artist or artists develop personal expression to represent, communicate or challenge ideas across cultures, times or places.
