Investigate drama across cultures and contexts

AC9ADR8E01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Drama: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate ways the elements of drama and/or conventions are used to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning in drama created and/or performed across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students can identify how elements of drama and conventions communicate ideas (AC9ADR6E01). They have not yet investigated how cultural, historical and contextual factors shape these choices across diverse dramatic traditions.

By the end

Students investigate how elements of drama and conventions communicate ideas, perspectives and meaning across cultures, times and contexts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying elements and conventions in drama worksStudents can identify specific elements of drama and conventions in a given work and describe how each contributes to meaning
  • 2. Investigating context and meaningStudents can investigate how cultural, historical and contextual factors shape how elements and conventions communicate ideas and meaning in drama
  • 3. Connecting investigation to own drama practiceStudents can use investigation of drama across contexts to inform specific decisions in own devising, interpreting or performing
