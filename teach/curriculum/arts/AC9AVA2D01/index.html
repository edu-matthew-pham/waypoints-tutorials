Experiment and play with visual conventions and materials

AC9AVA2D01 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Visual arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

experiment and play with visual conventions, visual arts processes and materials

Prior knowledge

Students can use play and imagination to discover possibilities with materials (AC9AVAFD01). They have begun noticing visual qualities and developing ideas through experimentation. They have not yet deliberately experimented with visual conventions as named elements.

By the end

Students experiment and play with visual conventions, visual arts processes and materials, reflecting on what they discover.

Waypoints

  • 1. Playing within limitsStudents can work with constraints on materials or processes to focus experimentation and notice what the material can do.
  • 2. Reflecting on experimentationHingeStudents can use questions (Viewpoints-style) to reflect on their arts experiences — naming what was challenging, what was discovered and what failures led to something new.
  • 3. Using arts languageStudents can use visual arts language when describing and documenting their experiments — naming conventions, processes and effects.
