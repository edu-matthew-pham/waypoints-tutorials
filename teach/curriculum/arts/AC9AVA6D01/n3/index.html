← AC9AVA6D01 Experiment, document and reflect on visual arts practice

Informed making choices

AC9AVA6D01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Visual arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can draw on documented experiments and reflection to make informed choices about visual conventions, processes and materials for a specific artwork.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one making choice that is directly informed by a documented experiment
  • Explains the connection between the discovery and the choice

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Multiple approaches to representation.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one choice (convention, material or process) for an artwork that is directly informed by something discovered in a documented experiment.
Typical
Use documented experiments to make and justify multiple informed choices for artworks; connect discoveries about representational approaches, material properties and visual effects to specific making decisions.
Wider
  • Develop a range of 3D hand-building techniques in earthenware or modelling clay — document surface decoration experiments and reflect on how form and surface choices communicate ideas
  • Review a full set of documented experiments and identify a pattern in your choices — describe an emerging preference or approach that could define a personal style direction

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe connection between documented discovery and future making is the core integration at this level — students demonstrate that experimentation is not separate from creating but feeds it.
