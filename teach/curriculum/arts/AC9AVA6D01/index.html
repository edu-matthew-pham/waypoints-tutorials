Experiment, document and reflect on visual arts practice

AC9AVA6D01 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Visual arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

experiment with, document and reflect on ways to use a range of visual conventions, visual arts processes, and materials

Prior knowledge

Students can experiment with a range of materials and processes, document discoveries through annotation, and use visual arts language to reflect (AC9AVA4D01). They have begun investigating composition. They have not yet produced systematic logbook-style documentation, experimented with Augmented Reality or cross-arts visual mapping, or applied observational drawing across multiple representational modes.

By the end

Students experiment with, document and reflect on ways to use a range of visual conventions, visual arts processes and materials, using their documentation as a source for informed making choices.

Waypoints

  • 1. Systematic documentationHingeStudents can document experiments systematically — recording what was tried, what resulted, and what it means for future making.
  • 2. Multiple approaches to representationStudents can use multiple approaches to represent the same subject — observational drawing, gestural drawing, photographic overlay, abstract — and reflect on what each approach can and cannot communicate.
  • 3. Informed making choicesStudents can draw on documented experiments and reflection to make informed choices about visual conventions, processes and materials for a specific artwork.
