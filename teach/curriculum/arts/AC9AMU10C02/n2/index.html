Style-specific compositional devices

AC9AMU10C02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify and apply compositional devices specific to a chosen style (e.g. hooks, riffs, ostinatos, specific chord progressions, characteristic rhythmic patterns) to develop and shape a composition.

Success criteria

  • Applies at least two style-specific compositional devices in an original composition
  • Can identify how each device is characteristic of the style by citing at least one existing example

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Aural understanding of key and tonality.

Width

Minimum
Apply at least two style-specific devices in a composition; explain how each device is characteristic of the style.
Typical
Use style-specific compositional devices to develop compositions — hooks, riffs, ostinatos, characteristic progressions, style-specific forms; analyse how these devices are used in existing works and apply findings in original compositions; experiment with ways to combine and manipulate elements using these devices.
Wider
  • Analyse the compositional devices in two or three works in the chosen style and use the findings to compose a new piece — document the analytical process and the compositional decisions it informed
  • Re-arrange an existing piece in a contrasting style by substituting the original style's devices with the new style's equivalents — compare the effect

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStyle-specific device application is strongest when grounded in multiple examples from the style. Listening analytically to several works before composing — rather than applying devices from memory or instruction — builds the pattern recognition that makes device use feel natural and stylistically authentic.
