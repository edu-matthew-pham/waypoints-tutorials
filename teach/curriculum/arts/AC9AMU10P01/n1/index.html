Planning performances for specific spaces

AC9AMU10P01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can plan and adapt performances for specific physical and virtual spaces — making decisions about staging, presentation format and audience interaction suited to each context.

Success criteria

  • Can identify at least two performance decisions that are specific to the space or platform chosen
  • Adapts at least one aspect of the performance (staging, introduction, or format) in response to the specific context

Prior knowledge

Students can perform in a range of forms in informal and formal settings, use peer feedback systematically, and perform via digital platforms (AC9AMU8P01 — Y7-8). They can adapt performance decisions to context. The Y9-10 step adds: performing in a range of physical and virtual spaces as a distinct requirement, recording and evaluating systematically, and performing own compositions with accompanying scores or visual material.

Width

Minimum
Plan a performance for at least one specific space (physical or virtual); identify at least two decisions that are specific to that space.
Typical
Plan and present performances in multiple physical and/or virtual spaces — making context-specific decisions about staging, introduction format, audience interaction, audio/visual requirements; compare how performing in different spaces (classroom, concert hall, assembly, online platform) changes the performance decisions.
Wider
  • Present the same performance in both a physical and a virtual space — compare how the audience interaction, staging and performance decisions differ between the two contexts
  • Research the specific technical requirements of a venue or platform before performing — use the research to inform preparation decisions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepPhysical and virtual performance contexts each impose genuinely different demands. Students who have only ever performed in one context default to its conventions when placed in another. Each new performance context encountered builds a more flexible and adaptive approach to performance planning.
