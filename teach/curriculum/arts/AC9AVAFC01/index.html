Create artworks that communicate ideas

AC9AVAFC01 · Foundation The Arts · Visual arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create arts works that communicate ideas

Prior knowledge

Students have no formal prior arts education assumed. They bring play-based experience with materials, mark-making and imaginative representation from early childhood contexts.

By the end

Students create artworks in one or more forms that communicate an idea drawn from personal experience, imagination or observation.

Waypoints

  • 1. Materials and imagination as starting pointsHingeStudents can use a material or imaginative prompt as a starting point for making — exploring what happens when they manipulate materials or act out ideas.
  • 2. Making with communicative intentStudents can make a deliberate choice in their artwork — choosing what to include, how to arrange it, or what material to use — because of what they want to communicate.
  • 3. Completing and communicatingStudents can create an artwork in one or more forms that communicates an idea from personal experience, imagination or observation.
