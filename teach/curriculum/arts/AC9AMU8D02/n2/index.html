Evaluating using focused listening

AC9AMU8D02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 4 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Music: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use focused listening and selected aural skills to evaluate how elements of music are used, with the purpose of informing their own interpretive decisions

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Evaluates at least one element or device in a recording with a stated purpose
  • Connects the evaluation to at least one decision about their own work

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Describing music using terminology.

Width

Minimum
Listen to a recording with a specific evaluative focus; identify at least one element or device and explain how the performer or composer has used it; connect the observation to a decision about their own work
Typical
Apply multiple focused listening strategies to evaluate a recording; use the evaluation to identify specific choices to apply or adapt in their own composing or performing
Wider
  • Use focused listening to evaluate alternative interpretations of a piece (two different recordings) — identify which interpretive choices are more effective for the intended stylistic effect and explain why
  • Evaluate a recording of their own performance using the same criteria applied to others — identify at least one choice they would change and explain the musical reasoning

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkEvaluation without a clear purpose easily becomes description — students listen and notice things but don't connect observations to decisions. Scaffolding the evaluative reasoning explicitly (what am I listening for → what do I observe → what does this suggest I should do in my own work) keeps reflection purposeful and productive.
