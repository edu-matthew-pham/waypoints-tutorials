Interpret music in styles using style-specific techniques

AC9AMU10C01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

interpret music in a variety of forms and styles, manipulating the elements of music and/or compositional devices, and using style-specific vocal/instrumental techniques to communicate ideas, perspectives and/or meaning

Prior knowledge

Students have manipulated elements and used compositional devices to communicate ideas (AC9AMU8C01/C02 — Y7-8). They can compose for specific forms, use style-specific devices and notate/document work. The Y9-10 step adds: style-specific vocal/instrumental techniques as a distinct requirement, and accuracy and fluency as explicit performance goals alongside communicative intent.

By the end

Students interpret music in a variety of forms and styles using style-specific vocal/instrumental techniques and compositional devices, demonstrating accuracy, fluency and stylistic understanding in performance.

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying stylistic conventionsHingeStudents can identify the stylistic conventions relevant to music they are learning — including characteristic elements, devices and performance techniques.
  • 2. Applying style-specific techniques for accuracy and fluencyStudents can apply style-specific vocal/instrumental techniques with developing accuracy and fluency, and monitor their own performance against stylistic expectations.
  • 3. Performance program with coherent theme or styleStudents can select, interpret and prepare a performance program around a specific theme, style or the work of a composer/performer.
