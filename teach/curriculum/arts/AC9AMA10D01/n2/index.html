Audience research and production experimentation

AC9AMA10D01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Media arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can research a target audience and use findings to design and produce a media arts work — experimenting with multiple production approaches and selecting the most effective for the intended audience relationship.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student researches a target audience and identifies at least two media conventions specific to that audience
  • Student uses those findings to make and document at least two production decisions

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Auteur, genre and representational language.

Width

Minimum
Research a target audience for a planned media arts work (identifying genre, platform, and representative media conventions for that audience) and use at least two findings to shape production decisions.
Typical
Research target audiences and conventions in successful online media arts works; use research to inform design, production and post-production decisions; manipulate technical and symbolic codes to produce works for identified target audiences; experiment with multiple production approaches and select the most effective.
Wider
  • Research target audiences and conventions in successful online media arts works — use findings to design and produce a work that deliberately constructs audience relationships, documenting at each production stage how research findings shaped decisions
  • Manipulate technical and symbolic codes across multiple production drafts for the same target audience — comparing how different approaches position the audience differently, and selecting the final approach with a documented justification

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyBuild students' repertoire of production experimentation strategies — multiple drafts, audience comparison, approach selection. The more systematic the experimentation, the more informed the final production decisions.
