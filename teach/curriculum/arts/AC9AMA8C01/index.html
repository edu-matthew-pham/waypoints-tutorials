Design and structure media arts works

AC9AMA8C01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Media arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

design and structure media arts works to communicate ideas, perspectives and meaning for an intended audience

Prior knowledge

Students can plan and structure media arts works with basic pre-production tools (AC9AMA6C01). They have not yet made deliberate design decisions about media languages and technical elements with a clear rationale for how each serves the intended representation for a specific audience.

By the end

Students design and structure media arts works where media language and technical element choices are deliberate and connected to intended ideas, perspectives and meaning for a specific audience.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using pre-production templates such as storyboard, shooting script or production schedule to plan production
  • understanding the importance of respectful relationships and empathy when developing characters to convey historical or cultural ideas
  • exploring ways to use media languages and technologies to create representations that perpetuate or challenge relationships or address issues such as stereotyping, bias, identity
  • experimenting with technical capabilities of media technologies and using media languages to inform plans for construction of representations
  • experimenting with technical and symbolic elements such as sound and lighting to construct representations for various genres or media platforms
  • experimenting with audio effects to construct ambient sound for different places or create/enhance atmosphere
  • experimenting with technical and symbolic elements to construct safe, legal, ethical and responsible representations
  • trialling ways to combine established genre conventions to make a hybrid production

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning with pre-production toolsStudents can use pre-production planning tools to design a media arts work with deliberate choices about structure, genre and representational intent
  • 2. Designing representations with media languagesStudents can design representations using technical and symbolic elements — sound, lighting, framing, colour, font — that serve intended meaning for a specific audience
  • 3. Designing for specific audiences with empathyStudents can design media arts works that consider the intended audience relationship and approach representations of characters and cultural content with empathy and responsibility
