First Nations media arts and country/place

AC9AMA4E02 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Media arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore how First Nations Australians use media arts to communicate their connection to and responsibility for Country/Place

Prior knowledge

Students can identify media arts works produced by First Nations Australians and describe how visual, audio and interactive elements communicate cultural knowledge. They have not yet explored specifically how media arts communicates connection to and responsibility for Country/Place.

By the end

Students explore how First Nations Australians use media arts to communicate their connection to and responsibility for Country/Place.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying meaning and describing representations in media arts works created and/or co-created by First Nations Australians, such as how traditional stories of First Nations Australians are being retold using media technologies
  • recognising, through media arts, how First Nations Australians' cultural expressions often contain and communicate connection to and responsibility for Country/Place
  • exploring First Nations Australians' use of media arts to express connection to and responsibility for Country/Place, such as communicating knowledge about the local seasons or using screen-based storytelling to maintain language and unique ways of life
  • exploring how First Nations Australians use media arts concepts to communicate their connection to and responsibility for Country/Place
  • collecting and sharing information about media arts works created and produced by First Nations Australians for occasions such as festivals or ceremonies
  • exploring how stories and ideas can communicate connection to and responsibility for Country/Place
  • exploring a media arts work that includes images, sound and/or text and communicates the diversity of First Nations Australians' cultures, such as an interactive map of First Nations Australian languages

Waypoints

  • 1. Connection to Country/Place in media artsHingeStudents can identify how a First Nations Australian media arts work communicates connection to and responsibility for Country/Place — naming at least one specific media arts concept used (framing, language, sound, symbols).
  • 2. Diversity of First Nations media arts expressionStudents can compare how different First Nations Australian media arts works communicate connection to Country/Place — noting how different media arts concepts, languages and purposes produce different works.
