Arts importance for communities

AC9ADAFE01 · Foundation The Arts · Dance: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore how and why the arts are important for people and communities

Prior knowledge

No formal prior knowledge assumed. Students bring awareness of arts experiences from family and community life.

By the end

Students explore how and why the arts — including dance — are important for people and communities.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring how facial expressions, gestures and other ways of moving communicate emotions and feelings in arts works
  • identifying and/or learning social or cultural dances that have meaning for people and communities, observing relevant protocols
  • identifying arts works and experiences in their lives and sharing ideas and feelings about them with peers and teachers
  • singing songs that are used for celebrations
  • exploring how illustrations in texts communicate narrative about characters, settings and mood
  • listening to First Nations Australians talk about the importance of the arts for connecting to People, Culture and Country/Place

Waypoints

  • 1. Recognising arts in everyday lifeHingeStudents can identify arts works and experiences — including dances and songs — that are present in their own lives and communities.
  • 2. Understanding arts communicate emotion and ideasStudents can describe how facial expressions, gestures, movement and voice in arts works communicate emotions and feelings.
  • 3. Arts bring people togetherStudents can identify that arts experiences — including dances, songs and celebrations — bring people together and have meaning for communities.
