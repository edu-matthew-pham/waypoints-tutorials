← AC9ADA4C01 Choreograph to communicate ideas

Structuring and combining into whole dance

AC9ADA4C01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Dance: creating and making · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can select, combine and structure movement material into a dance that communicates their intended meaning — using choreographic decisions to shape the whole.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student produces a dance with a clear beginning, middle and end
  • Student can explain how their structural choices (how it begins, how it ends) serve the communicative intent
  • Student can identify at least one change they made in response to feedback

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Generating and extending movement material.

Width

Minimum
A structured dance with clear beginning, middle and end; at least two deliberate element choices serving the communicative intent; combined with a partner or group.
Typical
Selecting and combining locomotor and non-locomotor movements into sequences; structuring into a dance with beginning-middle-end; combining with a partner or group to extend; using Viewpoints to evaluate and refine structural choices; following protocols where content draws on First Nations knowledge.
Wider
  • Choreograph a dance that communicates connection to and responsibility for a specific place in their community — with element choices that reflect emotional relationship to place
  • Extend the dance by layering a second structural device (e.g. canon, contrast section, call-and-response) to deepen the communication of meaning

Hinge

Producing a complete structured dance — not just movement material — is the capability milestone of this standard. Students who cannot yet structure and combine at this level will lack the compositional experience to engage with choreographic devices (Y5-6 C strand) and more complex structural forms.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierStructuring integrates intent (N1) with generated material (N2) into a communicative whole. Scaffold with explicit structure prompts and repeated partner/group sharing — hearing 'what did you think my dance was about?' calibrates intent-execution alignment.
