Reflect on own and others' music to inform choices

AC9AMU10D02 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use listening, aural skills and understanding of the information provided in scores, charts or lead-sheets to develop interpretations and reflect on the practices of others to inform their own

Prior knowledge

Students have developed aural skills for tuning/intonation and used recordings and performance comparisons to develop interpretations (AC9AMU8D02 — Y7-8). They can read from scores and lead-sheets during performance. The Y9-10 step requires using focused listening and aural/memory skills to identify, sing/play and notate musical ideas for future use, and developing real-time intonation skills.

By the end

Students use listening, aural skills and score/chart/lead-sheet information to develop interpretations, and reflect on the practices of others to inform their own compositions and performances.

Waypoints

  • 1. Developing interpretations from scores and listeningHingeStudents can use both score/chart/lead-sheet information and listening to develop a personal interpretation — identifying what the notation specifies, what it leaves open, and how listening to other performances informs their choices.
  • 2. Aural and memory skills for capturing musical ideasStudents can use focused listening and aural/memory skills to identify, sing or play, and notate or document musical ideas (pitch patterns, intervals, chord progressions) for future use in composition or performance.
  • 3. Real-time intonation in performanceStudents can monitor and adjust intonation in real time during ensemble performance, demonstrating developing ability to hear and correct pitch discrepancies without stopping.
