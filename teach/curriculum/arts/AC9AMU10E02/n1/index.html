Contributing to contemporary Australian music

AC9AMU10E02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate innovations in the ways First Nations Australian composers and performers are contributing to Australian contemporary music, including inter-cultural collaborations.

Success criteria

  • Can identify at least one innovative contribution by a First Nations Australian artist to contemporary Australian music
  • Can describe what makes the contribution innovative and how it relates to First Nations Australian identity or perspective

Prior knowledge

Students have explored how First Nations Australians use music to continue and revitalise culture — including diverse contemporary styles, language revitalisation, and cultural knowledge-sharing in performance (AC9AMU8E02 — Y7-8). The Y9-10 step specifically concerns Australian national identity — how First Nations Australian artists engage with, celebrate and challenge perspectives on what Australia is and means.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one First Nations Australian artist whose work makes an innovative contribution to contemporary Australian music; describe what is innovative about their contribution.
Typical
Investigate innovations across a range of First Nations Australian contemporary music contributions — artists working in orchestral, chamber, pop, hip-hop, country, and electronic contexts; inter-cultural collaborations that draw on First Nations Australian and non-Indigenous traditions; evaluate what each contributes to Australian contemporary music and what makes each innovative.
Wider
  • Investigate a specific inter-cultural collaboration between a First Nations Australian artist and a non-Indigenous artist or ensemble — describe what each tradition contributes, what is new in the collaboration, and how each artist has talked about the process
  • Compare how two or three First Nations Australian artists in different genres each contribute to Australian contemporary music — what does each do that no other tradition could?

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe breadth of First Nations Australian contributions to contemporary Australian music is the point — students who only encounter one or two examples cannot develop a picture of the full scope of these contributions. Each new genre or collaboration context reveals a different dimension of what First Nations Australian artists bring to Australian contemporary music.
