← AC9AVA6C01 Plan and create artworks that communicate ideas and meaning

Creating with coordinated intent

AC9AVA6C01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 5–6 The Arts · Visual arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create an artwork in which planning, aesthetic choices and communicative intent are coordinated — the work delivers what was intended.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Creates an artwork that realises at least one planned intention
  • Explains how at least two visual choices (convention, material, composition) work together to serve the communicative intent

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Aesthetic choices and material knowledge.

Width

Minimum
Create an artwork that realises at least one planned intention; explain how the visual choices made serve that intention.
Typical
Create artworks using planned visual conventions, materials and processes; make choices that serve both aesthetic quality and communicative intent; combine multiple elements coherently; include digital and analog combinations where appropriate.
Wider
  • Create an artwork that combines at least one digital and one analog material — document in a logbook and explain how each contributes to the final work
  • Form modular patterns using grid paper or digital tools (glyphs, pixels, shapes) — remove the grid and transform into a final form such as a poster or printmaking template
  • Create an artwork in response to a site-specific inspiration — document how the site shaped material and compositional choices

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierPlanning, aesthetic judgment and material execution are coordinated here. The integration is not just technical — it is the student demonstrating that what they made matches what they intended.
