Performing with music skills

AC9AMUFP01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Foundation The Arts · Music: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can perform a song, soundscape or chant using at least one practised music skill (e.g. matching starting pitch, staying in time with the group, following a simple graphic score).

Success criteria

  • Performs a song or chant as part of a group with some awareness of at least one music convention
  • Attempts to match the group's timing or dynamic level during the performance

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Introducing and sharing a work.

Width

Minimum
Perform a song or chant with the group, demonstrating awareness of at least one music convention (e.g. staying in time, or using singing voice rather than speaking voice).
Typical
Perform songs, soundscapes or chants using practised skills — matching pitch, staying in time, following an agreed plan or graphic score; use a digital device to document the performance via images or recording.
Wider
  • Follow a graphic score when performing a soundscape — practise reading the score in rehearsal and apply it in performance
  • Curate a sequence of digital images from a class arts event and add voice narration or soundscape as commentary

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsMusic performance skills at Foundation (staying in time, matching pitch, following an agreed plan) require repeated practice in low-stakes contexts before students can apply them reliably in a shared performance. Fluency here underpins the more deliberate performance decisions required at Y1-2.
