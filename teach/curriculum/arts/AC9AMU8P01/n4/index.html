← AC9AMU8P01 Perform music

Integrated performance with stylistic understanding

AC9AMU8P01 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Music: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can perform music that demonstrates technical control and expressive understanding appropriate to the style, in solo and/or ensemble contexts, for a range of audiences

Success criteria

  • Performs a piece in which at least two expressive or stylistic features are evident
  • Reflects on the performance with at least one specific observation about what worked and what would change

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Planning and rehearsing for performance.

Width

Minimum
Perform a piece for an audience in which at least two expressive or stylistic features are evident; reflect on what worked and what would change in a future performance
Typical
Perform music in solo and/or ensemble contexts that demonstrates consistent technical control and stylistic understanding throughout; seek and use feedback to reflect on the performance
Wider
  • Perform a range of music across different styles or forms, demonstrating that technique and expressive choices are adapted to each style rather than applied uniformly
  • Plan and present a performance that includes their own composed or arranged music alongside other repertoire — evaluate how performing their own work differs from performing existing repertoire

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis is where technique, tonal balance, rehearsal planning and stylistic understanding come together in a single live performance for an audience. The coordination of these components under performance conditions — where real-time adjustment is required — is the learning that only performance itself can provide.
