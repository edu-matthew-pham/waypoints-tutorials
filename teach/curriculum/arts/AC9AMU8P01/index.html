Perform music

AC9AMU8P01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Music: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

perform music using relevant vocal and/or instrumental techniques and performance skills

Prior knowledge

Students can perform music in a range of forms they have learnt and/or composed in informal and/or formal settings (AC9AMU6P01). They have not yet focused on the full range of performance skills — posture, technical control, tonal balance, real-time problem-solving — or on planning and presenting performances that demonstrate stylistic understanding.

By the end

Students perform music using relevant vocal and/or instrumental techniques and performance skills, demonstrating technical control and expressive understanding appropriate to the style and context.

Waypoints

  • 1. Technique for solo and ensemble performanceStudents can apply relevant vocal/instrumental techniques in solo and ensemble performance contexts, maintaining posture and technical control throughout
  • 2. Tonal balance and blend in ensembleStudents can control tone and volume in ensemble performance to create a balanced sound, identifying and responding to imbalances in real time
  • 3. Planning and rehearsing for performanceStudents can plan a performance, identifying expressive skills and stylistic understanding to communicate, and rehearse with attention to those goals
  • 4. Integrated performance with stylistic understandingStudents can perform music that demonstrates technical control and expressive understanding appropriate to the style, in solo and/or ensemble contexts, for a range of audiences
