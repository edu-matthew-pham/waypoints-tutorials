← AC9ADR4C01 Improvise and devise drama

Devising and presenting drama with communicative intent

AC9ADR4C01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Drama: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can work collaboratively to devise a structured drama that communicates an intended idea, perspective or meaning.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student contributes to a devised drama that communicates an identifiable idea or perspective
  • Student can describe what the drama is about and one element choice they made to communicate it
  • Student demonstrates collaborative engagement — offering ideas and accepting others' contributions

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Using elements to shape improvised drama.

Width

Minimum
Student contributes to devising a drama (with at least one other student) that has a clear beginning, development and ending, and communicates a recognisable idea.
Typical
Students work in groups to devise sections of drama and then, working as a class, combine sections using a play-building process; they communicate their connection to Country/Place through Acknowledgement of Country (as appropriate); they shape improvisation to communicate their intentions as drama-makers.
Wider
  • Students devise drama exploring issues and ideas, using empathy to consider multiple perspectives and challenge their own assumptions
  • Students devise drama based on a real or imagined 'miniature world', developing a complex fictional space with consistent roles, rules and relationships

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierDevising integrates all prior moves — identifying intent, using elements, collaborating. Scaffold with clear group roles and regular reflection stops ('Is the meaning clear? What element could you adjust?').
