Arts importance for communities

AC9AMAFE01 · Foundation The Arts · Media arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore how and why the arts are important for people and communities

Prior knowledge

Students have personal experience of arts events (songs at celebrations, animations, dances) but have not yet consciously reflected on why the arts matter to communities or what role they play.

By the end

Students can identify arts works and experiences from their lives and explain how they are important for people and communities.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring how facial expressions, gestures and other ways of moving, and using voice or language, can communicate emotions and feelings in arts works
  • identifying and/or learning social or cultural dances that have meaning for people and communities, taking care to observe relevant protocols
  • using dramatic play to explore ideas or understandings they observe or perceive in a text or image
  • identifying arts works and experiences in their lives, such as songs they sing, dances or drama they watch or perform, animations, computer games or music videos, public art, 2D, 3D or screen-based works they encounter at school or in community settings
  • singing songs that are used for celebrations, such as school songs, anthems, sports teams' songs, birthday songs
  • exploring how illustrations/images in a text help to communicate narrative or information about characters or settings and/or mood
  • listening to First Nations Australians talk about the importance of the arts for connecting to people, culture and Country/Place; for example, using resources created or co-created by First Nations Australians

Waypoints

  • 1. Arts in my lifeHingeStudents can identify at least two types of arts works or experiences from their own life or community (e.g. a song, a dance, a cartoon, a game, public art).
  • 2. How arts communicateStudents can describe how arts works communicate feelings, ideas or cultural knowledge — using examples they have encountered.
  • 3. Why arts matter to communitiesStudents can explain why an arts work matters to a group of people — connecting the work to its community purpose.
