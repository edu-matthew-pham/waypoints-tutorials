Rehearse and perform drama

AC9ADR8P01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Drama: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

rehearse and perform improvised, devised and/or scripted drama to audiences, using performance skills and conventions relevant to style and/or form

Prior knowledge

Students can perform drama for an audience with basic performance skills (AC9ADR6P01). They have not yet used deliberate rehearsal strategies, systematically collected audience feedback, or reflected on the relationship between intention and audience reception.

By the end

Students rehearse and perform drama for audiences using performance skills and conventions appropriate to style and form, reflecting on the relationship between intention and audience reception.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • performing using conventions relevant to style, suiting choices to the underlying dramatic structure
  • collecting and evaluating audience feedback to enhance future performance
  • using Viewpoints to ask reflective performance questions about intentions, meaning and audience engagement
  • respecting ICIP rights when performing drama that explores First Nations Australian perspectives
  • reflecting on how respectful relationships and empathy were applied and interpreted by the audience

Waypoints

  • 1. Rehearsing with performance intentStudents can use the rehearsal process to develop performance readiness — technical skills, conventions and expressive clarity — with a clear sense of intended audience experience
  • 2. Performing with style-appropriate skills and conventionsStudents can perform drama using performance skills and conventions relevant to the style or form with clear communicative intent
  • 3. Evaluating performance and audience receptionStudents can collect and evaluate audience feedback and reflect on whether performance communicated intended meaning
