Generate and develop ideas

AC9AVA8C01 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Visual arts: creating and making

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

generate, document and develop ideas for artworks

Prior knowledge

Students can generate and document ideas for artworks using planning strategies (AC9AVA6C01). They have not yet developed sustained visual inquiry, moved through multiple development stages, or justified compositional decisions in relation to intended meaning.

By the end

Students generate, document and develop ideas for artworks through sustained visual inquiry, making and justifying compositional decisions that connect to intended meaning.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • engaging in visual brainstorming by selecting, organising and combining appropriate images, forms, visual art processes and materials to represent ideas about a particular subject
  • documenting their visual arts practice when developing ideas and processes, including studio practice, correct use of materials and tools, creative thinking and reflection
  • developing a series of compositional sketches as planning for a final artwork that communicates how the visual conventions and planned processes will communicate meaning
  • exploring and researching ideas about their interests in the world/subject matter or concepts from a particular perspective or a range of perspectives
  • recording and documenting their research into a subject or theme using their own images or images from other sources, written annotations and comments, or evaluations
  • exploring ways that artists, designers, architects or craftspeople communicate ideas and meaning in their work

Waypoints

  • 1. Generating ideas through visual brainstormingStudents can generate ideas for artworks through visual brainstorming that explores multiple approaches before committing to a direction
  • 2. Developing ideas through research and investigationStudents can develop ideas through investigation of subject matter, artists and contexts, using research to deepen and refine the artistic concept
  • 3. Making and justifying compositional decisionsStudents can develop a series of compositional decisions for an artwork and justify each in relation to intended meaning
