← AC9AMU8E02 First Nations music and ICIP rights

Applying culturally responsive approaches

AC9AMU8E02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can make ethical choices about how they engage with First Nations Australian music, using protocols for ICIP rights as a guide

Success criteria

  • Applies at least one protocol question to a real or hypothetical music practice scenario
  • Explains the ethical reasoning behind the protocol

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Understanding ICIP rights.

Width

Minimum
Apply at least one protocol question to their own practice (e.g. 'Who wrote this music?', 'May I use ideas from this, and do I need permission?'); explain the reasoning behind the protocol
Typical
Apply multiple protocol questions to their own practice as listeners, composers and/or performers; explain how the protocols guide ethical engagement with First Nations Australian music
Wider
  • Evaluate a scenario in which a creator used First Nations Australian music without following ICIP protocols — analyse the ethical issues raised and consider what the creator should have done differently
  • Develop a set of personal guidelines for culturally responsive engagement with First Nations Australian music — based on the protocols investigated, articulate what they will do as a listener, composer and performer

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkApplying protocols requires students to reason through specific situations — the ethical logic doesn't transfer automatically from principle to practice. Scaffolding the reasoning (who created this → what rights apply → what does that mean for what I want to do) makes the protocol application purposeful rather than checklist-driven.
