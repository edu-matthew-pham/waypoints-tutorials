Share and display artworks in informal settings

AC9AVA4P01 · Years 3–4 The Arts · Visual arts: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

share and/or display artworks and/or visual arts practice in informal settings

Prior knowledge

Students can share artworks in informal settings, choosing audience and format and explaining their work (AC9AVA2P01). They have basic experience of multiple sharing contexts. They have not yet selected documentation of a process to share, curated a display, or produced an artist statement.

By the end

Students share and/or display artworks and visual arts practice in informal settings, selecting documentation and making decisions about appropriate audiences and presentation formats.

Waypoints

  • 1. Selecting and arranging for displayStudents can select examples from their visual arts practice and arrange them to communicate the development of their ideas.
  • 2. Producing an artist statementStudents can produce a written or spoken artist statement that explains the ideas, themes and meanings in their artwork.
  • 3. Choosing audience and formatStudents can make deliberate decisions about appropriate audiences for their work and choose presentation formats suited to each context.
