Reflect on influences to inform practice

AC9AVA8D02 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Visual arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

reflect on the ways that they and other artists respond to influences to inform choices they make in their own visual arts practice

Prior knowledge

Students can use teacher feedback to reflect on their practice (AC9AVA6D02). They have not yet investigated how other artists respond to influences and used that investigation to articulate and inform their own practice choices.

By the end

Students reflect on how they and other artists respond to influences — materials, emotions, cultural context, other artworks — to make informed and documented choices in own visual arts practice.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • developing understanding of the ways that visual conventions can be used to communicate feelings and emotions — exploring compositional sketches to identify how changes communicate different emotions
  • using teacher and student set goals to reflect on and practise specific skills over time, using Viewpoints to develop questions about aims, strengths and areas for improvement
  • exploring and reflecting on the way a particular artist uses materials, techniques and processes to inform own investigations
  • considering sustainable practices when exploring properties of materials
  • reflecting on the work of others and own explorations when developing an understanding of visual conventions and how they might be manipulated to communicate meaning
  • building on understanding of composition to explore multiple ways to represent space and depth — experimenting with proportion, scale, positive and negative space, perspective

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying influences on artistic choicesStudents can identify the influences on an artist's choices — including material properties, emotional responses, cultural context and investigation of other artists — and explain how these shape the work
  • 2. Reflecting on own influences and practice choicesStudents can reflect on the influences on their own practice choices and document how those influences are shaping specific decisions
  • 3. Using reflection to develop visual arts practice over timeStudents can use reflection on influences — their own and other artists' — as a sustained practice that develops over time and produces increasingly informed and distinctive artistic choices
