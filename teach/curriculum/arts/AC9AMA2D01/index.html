Use media technologies responsibly

AC9AMA2D01 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Media arts: developing practices and skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore ways of using media technologies responsibly to capture and organise images, sounds, text and/or interactive elements

Prior knowledge

Students have used digital devices for play and idea exploration. They can capture images or sounds informally. They have not yet explicitly considered responsible use, or deliberately combined visual and audio elements to communicate.

By the end

Students explore ways of using media technologies responsibly to capture and organise images, sounds, text and/or interactive elements.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring ways to use digital tools to capture moving image; for example, using a digital device to capture and manipulate short videos, add sound effects or voice-overs, and considering how best to combine the visual and audio elements to communicate ideas or a narrative
  • exploring how sound can be used to communicate ideas; for example, creating sound effects using found objects or sound recording technology to integrate with a picture book story
  • experimenting with images; for example, retelling the story of a school day using a digital camera to capture still or moving images and adding appropriate captions
  • experimenting with ways that media artists communicate with audiences by combining text, symbols and images; for example, combining headlines, subtitles and digital images to communicate ideas or emotions
  • using Viewpoints to develop questions as they experiment with media technologies, such as, "What happens if I take a photo up close to the object, and how does it change as I move further away?", "How can I change the meaning of this image by adding different text headings?"

Waypoints

  • 1. Responsible use of media technologiesHingeStudents can identify at least one principle of responsible media technology use and apply it when capturing media elements.
  • 2. Capturing and organising media elementsStudents can capture and organise images, sounds and/or text using a digital device — making deliberate choices about what to capture and how to combine elements.
  • 3. Experimenting to change meaningStudents can experiment with how changes to media elements (different text, different sound, different framing) change what a work communicates.
