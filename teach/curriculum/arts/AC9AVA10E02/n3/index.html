← AC9AVA10E02 First Nations artists celebrate and challenge Australian identity

Cultural continuation, development and community voice

AC9AVA10E02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Visual arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can investigate how First Nations Australian visual arts provide opportunities to continue and develop culture, communicate lived experiences, and care for Country/Place and people.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two distinct purposes served by First Nations Australian visual arts (from continuation, challenge, Country care, lived experience communication)
  • Demonstrates understanding that these purposes are not separate but often simultaneous in a single artwork or practice

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Multiple perspectives on Australian identity.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two distinct ways First Nations Australian visual arts serve community or cultural purposes (continuation, challenge, caring for Country, communicating lived experience).
Typical
Explore the full range of ways First Nations Australian visual arts function — cultural continuation, community development, lived experience communication, Country/Place care, identity declaration and political challenge; synthesise understanding across multiple artists and forms.
Wider
  • Work with representatives of the First Nations Australian community to explore how local groups are caring for Country/Place — investigate how visual arts campaigns highlight environmental issues for local and broader audiences
  • Investigate how the digital age has expanded opportunities for First Nations Australian artists and designers to continue and develop culture — explore specific examples of online, social media or digital art contexts

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierActivism, identity challenge and cultural continuation come together in a complete understanding of First Nations Australian visual arts as simultaneously political, cultural and community-directed — serving multiple purposes at once.
