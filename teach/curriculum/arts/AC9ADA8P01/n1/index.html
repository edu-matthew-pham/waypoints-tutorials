Using rehearsal strategies to prepare for performance

AC9ADA8P01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 7–8 The Arts · Dance: presenting and performing

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use deliberate rehearsal strategies to develop confidence, clarity and expressive quality in preparation for performance

Success criteria

  • Uses at least one deliberate rehearsal strategy with a specific goal
  • Explains what the strategy helped develop
  • Rehearsal strategy is targeted — not just 'run it again from the top'

Prior knowledge

Students can perform dance with basic technical and expressive skill for an audience (AC9ADA6P01). They have not yet used deliberate rehearsal strategies, production elements, or audience communication tools to enhance the performance experience.

Width

Minimum
Use at least one rehearsal strategy (e.g. spotting, spatial planning, repetition of key phrases) with a specific preparation goal; explain what the strategy helped develop
Typical
Use a range of rehearsal strategies to develop confidence, clarity of movement, projection, focus and musicality; document how rehearsal strategies address specific performance challenges
Wider
  • Use video review during rehearsal to identify and address spatial placement, articulation and expressive clarity — compare video observation with own sense of the performance
  • Plan effective use of available performance spaces during rehearsals — evaluate how spatial planning changes performer confidence and audience experience
  • Develop a personal rehearsal protocol for preparing a specific aspect of performance (e.g. projection, focus, musicality) — evaluate its effectiveness over multiple rehearsal sessions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsEffective rehearsal — using targeted strategies repeatedly to address specific performance challenges — is a disciplined habit that develops through sustained practice cycles. Students who rehearse without strategy develop performance confidence more slowly and less deliberately. The skill is in the purposeful application of rehearsal strategies, which develops through iteration.
