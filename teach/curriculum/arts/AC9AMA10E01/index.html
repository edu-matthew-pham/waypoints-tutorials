Investigate media artists

AC9AMA10E01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Media arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate the ways that media artists use media arts concepts to construct representations in media arts works and practices across cultures, times, places and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students can compare media representations across contexts and explain how context shapes values and stereotypes. They have analysed power relationships in representations. They have not yet investigated at the level of evaluating social, cultural and ethical implications — or analysing how media platforms use data to shape audience information.

By the end

Students investigate how media artists use media arts concepts to construct representations across cultures, times, places and contexts — evaluating social, cultural and ethical implications and how audiences are positioned.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • analysing and evaluating the social, cultural and/or ethical implications of the changing nature of representations in media arts works over time
  • justifying, in a video commentary, the technical and symbolic code choices they have made in constructing representations
  • analysing and evaluating the way social beliefs and values can be communicated to audiences through the manipulation of technical and symbolic codes
  • exploring how media platforms use data and how they can influence the breadth and depth of information provided to an audience
  • evaluating relationships embedded within media practices
  • researching audiences of specific genres or users of specific media platforms
  • analysing and evaluating the ways technical and symbolic codes have been used to construct stereotypical representations
  • identifying, describing and explaining the way technical and symbolic codes are manipulated to evoke audience responses
  • examining the role of media makers in perpetuating and/or challenging prevailing views

Waypoints

  • 1. Technical and symbolic codes — ideology and positioningHingeStudents can analyse how technical and symbolic codes communicate social beliefs, values and ideology — and evaluate how audiences are positioned to respond.
  • 2. Social, cultural and ethical implicationsStudents can evaluate the social, cultural and ethical implications of representations in media arts works — including how representations have changed over time and how media practices perpetuate or challenge prevailing views.
