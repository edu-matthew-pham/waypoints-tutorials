First Nations media arts

AC9AMA2E02 · Years 1–2 The Arts · Media arts: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore examples of media arts produced and/or distributed by First Nations Australians

Prior knowledge

Students can identify media arts works and explain how they communicate cultural knowledge. They may have encountered First Nations Australian arts works in Foundation but have not yet specifically explored media arts produced and/or distributed by First Nations Australians.

By the end

Students explore examples of media arts produced and/or distributed by First Nations Australians, identifying how visual, audio and/or interactive elements communicate cultural knowledge.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring when, where, how and why First Nations Australians use media arts works to share knowledge about their cultures; for example, during celebrations such as National Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Children's Day, NAIDOC Week, Reconciliation Week or the acknowledgement of significant occasions including Sorry Day
  • exploring children's television programs, cartoons/animations and child-focused print media, such as an advertisement encouraging people to adopt a healthy lifestyle, created and distributed by First Nations Australians
  • observing how visual, audio and/or interactive elements are used in media arts works created by First Nations Australians; for example, exploring colours, symbols and patterns used in media arts works with representatives of the First Nations Australian community or through resources that are created or co-created by First Nations Australians
  • exploring media arts works created and/or co-created by First Nations Australians that communicate cultural narratives

Waypoints

  • 1. First Nations media arts worksHingeStudents can identify at least one media arts work produced and/or distributed by First Nations Australians and describe its form and context.
  • 2. Elements and cultural communicationStudents can identify at least one visual, audio or interactive element in a First Nations Australian media arts work and describe what cultural knowledge it communicates.
