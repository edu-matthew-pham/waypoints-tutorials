Music in times of conflict, change and social context

AC9AMU10E01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can investigate how music is used in times of conflict and social change — analysing what compositional and performative choices carry political, social or historical messages.

Success criteria

  • Can identify the social or historical context of a piece of music and connect it to specific compositional choices
  • Can analyse how those choices carry the work's social or political message, with musical evidence

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Analysing how elements and devices evoke response.

Width

Minimum
Investigate one piece of music connected to a specific historical or social context; analyse at least two compositional choices that carry the work's message.
Typical
Compare how people in a range of times, places and contexts have used music in times of conflict and change — examining how specific compositional and performative choices carry political or social messages; investigating why music might be described as 'experimental' in its response to social context; discussing how music influences personal and social identity.
Wider
  • Investigate how music used in two different historical or political conflicts uses similar or different compositional strategies to carry its message — compare the musical choices and their social contexts
  • Analyse how music influences personal or social identity — use this analysis to inform the creation of a piece designed to communicate a specific social or community message

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepMusical responses to conflict and social change span many different historical and cultural contexts — each adds a new example of how the same musical purpose (communicating protest, grief, solidarity) is realised through different compositional choices. Breadth of examples is what enables genuine analytical comparison.
