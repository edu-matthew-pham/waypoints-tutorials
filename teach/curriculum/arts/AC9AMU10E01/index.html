Investigate composers' and performers' use of elements across cultures and times

AC9AMU10E01 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate and analyse ways that composers and/or performers use the elements of music, compositional devices and/or vocal and instrumental techniques across a range of cultures, times and/or other contexts

Prior knowledge

Students have explored how elements of music are combined across cultures and contexts and evaluated personal responses using music terminology (AC9AMU8E01 — Y7-8). They can describe how structural features create effects. The Y9-10 step shifts from exploration to investigation and analysis — specifically examining how composers/performers use elements to influence emotional response, communicate identity, and respond to social and historical contexts.

By the end

Students investigate and analyse ways composers and/or performers use elements of music, compositional devices and/or vocal/instrumental techniques across a range of cultures, times and contexts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating ways that composers and/or performers use the elements of music and compositional devices to structure compositions or evoke an emotional response
  • investigating how composers and performers use instrumentation or instrumental techniques to communicate specific effects
  • comparing ways that people in a range of times, places and/or other contexts have used music in times of conflict and change
  • reflecting on why music might be described as 'experimental'
  • investigating the way elements of music are manipulated in music or sound designs for installations or site-specific artworks
  • discussing and evaluating the influence of music on the development of personal or social identity
  • using Viewpoints to ask questions about how technology has changed music styles, composition practices, audiences and the music industry

Waypoints

  • 1. Analysing how elements and devices evoke responseHingeStudents can analyse how a composer or performer uses specific elements and compositional devices to structure a work or evoke a particular emotional response.
  • 2. Music in times of conflict, change and social contextStudents can investigate how music is used in times of conflict and social change — analysing what compositional and performative choices carry political, social or historical messages.
  • 3. Experimental music and sound installationsStudents can investigate why music might be described as 'experimental' and analyse how elements of music are manipulated in sound designs for installations or site-specific artworks.
