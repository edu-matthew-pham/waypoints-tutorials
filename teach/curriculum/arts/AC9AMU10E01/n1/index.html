Analysing how elements and devices evoke response

AC9AMU10E01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 The Arts · Music: exploring and responding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in The Arts: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse how a composer or performer uses specific elements and compositional devices to structure a work or evoke a particular emotional response.

Success criteria

  • Can identify at least two specific techniques or devices in a piece under analysis
  • Can explain how each contributes to a specific emotional or communicative effect, with musical reasoning

Prior knowledge

Students have explored how elements of music are combined across cultures and contexts and evaluated personal responses using music terminology (AC9AMU8E01 — Y7-8). They can describe how structural features create effects. The Y9-10 step shifts from exploration to investigation and analysis — specifically examining how composers/performers use elements to influence emotional response, communicate identity, and respond to social and historical contexts.

Width

Minimum
Analyse one piece in depth — identify at least two specific techniques or devices used and explain how each contributes to a particular emotional response.
Typical
Investigate how composers and performers use elements and devices to structure compositions and evoke emotional responses — examining instrumentation choices, dynamic manipulation, structural devices, expressive techniques; connecting specific musical choices to their emotional and communicative effects; using Viewpoints to guide analysis.
Wider
  • Compare how two composers from different times or cultures use elements to evoke a similar emotional response — analyse what is similar in their approach and what is culturally or historically specific
  • Use Viewpoints to ask how technology has changed music styles, composition practices, audiences and the music industry — trace one specific technology's influence on compositional choices

Hinge

The move from 'describing' to 'analysing' — connecting specific musical choices to specific effects with evidence — is the defining cognitive step of Y9-10 Exploring and Responding. Students who describe without analysis cannot access the investigation required by the descriptor, and cannot meet the demands of senior music analytical writing.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkBuild the analysis habit with an explicit evidence-to-claim structure: 'The composer uses [specific technique] at [specific moment] which creates [specific effect] because [musical reasoning].' The move from observation to analytical claim to musical reasoning is the progression to make explicit. Viewpoints questions provide a useful scaffold.
