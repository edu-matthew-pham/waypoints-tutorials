← AC9HC10S02 Locate, select and compare information

Selecting and comparing across sources

AC9HC10S02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can select relevant information from multiple sources and compare them to identify patterns, conflicts and gaps in the civic evidence base.

Success criteria

  • Selects information from at least three sources
  • Identifies at least one point of agreement and one point of conflict across sources
  • Explains what the pattern of agreement and conflict reveals about the issue

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Evaluating sources for credibility and bias.

Width

Minimum
Select information from at least three sources and compare them, identifying at least one point of agreement and one point of conflict.
Typical
Select varying and conflicting information across sources and compare them systematically, identifying points of agreement, conflict and significant gaps — and explaining what these patterns reveal about the civic issue being investigated.
Wider
  • Compare the information available on a civic issue from Australian government sources versus international bodies (e.g. UN reports) and evaluate what each source can and cannot tell you
  • Investigate a civic issue where survey data and media representation conflict and analyse what explains the difference
  • Extend source comparison to include perspectives from First Nations Australians or communities directly affected by the issue and evaluate what their perspectives add

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates source location and evaluation into comparative analysis — students must synthesise across multiple sources rather than treating each individually. Teaching investment is in supporting the move from 'source A says X, source B says Y' to 'these sources conflict because...'.
