Evaluate civic participation methods and outcomes

AC9HC10S04 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate the methods or strategies and outcomes related to making decisions about civic participation

Prior knowledge

Students have evaluated the methods or strategies related to making decisions about civic participation at Year 9 (AC9HC9S04). At Year 10 the descriptor adds 'and outcomes' — students are now expected to evaluate not just the method but what the method achieved.

By the end

Students evaluate both the methods or strategies used in civic participation and the outcomes achieved, drawing conclusions about effectiveness and accountability.

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying civic participation methods and strategiesHingeStudents can identify the methods or strategies used by a government, NGO or campaign group to address an issue at regional or global level or to ensure accountability to international obligations.
  • 2. Evaluating methods and outcomesStudents can evaluate the methods or strategies and outcomes related to civic participation, making a supported judgement about effectiveness and drawing conclusions about accountability.
