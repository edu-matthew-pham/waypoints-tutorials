Analyse civic perspectives

AC9HC8S03 · Year 8 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse information, data and ideas about political, legal or civic issues to identify and explain differences in perspectives and potential challenges

Prior knowledge

Students have analysed civic information at Year 7 level (AC9HC7S03) to identify differences in perspectives. The gap this descriptor fills is the move to analysis that also identifies and explains potential challenges — not just that perspectives differ, but why they differ and what consequences those differences have for civic and democratic life.

By the end

Students analyse information, data and ideas about political, legal or civic issues to identify and explain differences in perspectives and potential challenges, drawing on a range of source types including speeches, award criteria, letters and case studies.

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying differences in perspectiveHingeStudents can identify differences in perspective in information about a civic issue, explaining what the perspectives are and naming the values or interests that ground them.
  • 2. Explaining reasons for perspective differencesStudents can identify and explain possible reasons for differences in perspective about a civics and citizenship issue — connecting perspective differences to underlying values, experiences, roles or positions.
  • 3. Identifying and explaining potential challengesStudents can identify potential challenges arising from perspective differences on civic issues and explain what makes those challenges significant for civic and democratic life.
