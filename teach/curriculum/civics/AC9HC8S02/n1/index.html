Locating civic information using targeted search

AC9HC8S02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 8 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can locate information about a civics issue from a range of print and online sources using advanced search functions or targeted criteria.

Success criteria

  • Can locate information on a civic issue from at least two different source types
  • Can describe the search strategy used to find each source

Prior knowledge

Students have located, selected and organised information from provided sources at Year 7 level (AC9HC7S02). The gap this descriptor fills is the move from provided sources to self-directed source finding — including using advanced search strategies, applying accuracy and reliability criteria, and critically organising sources by identifying bias and selective use of information.

Width

Minimum
Use at least two different types of sources (e.g. an online news article and a government website) to find information on a civic issue, explaining the search strategy used.
Typical
Locate information from a range of print and online sources — including online journal articles, news reports and social media posts — using advanced search functions or targeted criteria (e.g. date filtering, source type specification, Boolean operators) about a citizenship issue such as the nature of the Australian citizenship test.
Wider
  • Investigate how different search strategies (general web search vs database search vs government portal search) yield different types of sources for the same civic issue — evaluating what each strategy is best suited for
  • Practise using advanced search operators and filters to narrow results to specific source types, dates or perspectives on a civic issue
  • Investigate how social media algorithms shape what civic information is surfaced when searching civic topics online — and what this means for source breadth

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyEffective source location requires a repertoire of search strategies. Build range across different search approaches — general search, database, government portals, news archives — before moving to reliability evaluation.
