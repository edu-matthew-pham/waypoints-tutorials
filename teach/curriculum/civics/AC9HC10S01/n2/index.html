Generating connected sub-questions

AC9HC10S01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can develop a range of sub-questions connected to the overarching question that collectively structure a productive investigation.

Success criteria

  • Develops at least three sub-questions connected to the overarching question
  • Sub-questions address different aspects of the investigation (not all asking the same thing)
  • Can explain how each sub-question contributes to answering the overarching question

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Framing an investigable civic question.

Width

Minimum
Develop at least three sub-questions connected to the overarching question that together cover different aspects of the investigation.
Typical
Develop a range of sub-questions connected to the overarching question — for example, addressing representation, equality, accountability, and accessibility — and explain how the sub-questions collectively structure the inquiry.
Wider
  • Develop sub-questions that deliberately seek out different perspectives — including those of groups whose voices are less commonly represented in civic debate
  • Practise generating sub-questions across multiple overarching questions and evaluate which sets of sub-questions best support a structured investigation
  • Design a full inquiry framework showing how sub-questions connect to different evidence sources

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents often generate sub-questions that are essentially restatements of the main question or that only address one dimension. Scaffold the process of mapping different dimensions of an inquiry (e.g. structural, representational, evaluative) before students generate sub-questions independently.
