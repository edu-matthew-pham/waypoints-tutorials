Develop and modify civic questions

AC9HC10S01 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop and modify questions to investigate Australia's political and legal systems, and contemporary civic issues

Prior knowledge

Students have developed and modified questions to investigate Australia's political and legal systems and contemporary civic issues at Year 9 (AC9HC9S01). They can formulate sub-questions and revisit questions based on new information.

By the end

Students develop and modify questions that guide sustained civic inquiry, generating productive sub-questions and revising the inquiry based on evidence gathered.

Waypoints

  • 1. Framing an investigable civic questionHingeStudents can develop an overarching inquiry question about Australia's political or legal system or a contemporary civic issue that is specific, investigable, and productively open.
  • 2. Generating connected sub-questionsStudents can develop a range of sub-questions connected to the overarching question that collectively structure a productive investigation.
  • 3. Revising questions based on evidenceStudents can revisit and modify their inquiry questions based on further information collected, including identifying whether different groups hold particular views that require the question to be refined.
