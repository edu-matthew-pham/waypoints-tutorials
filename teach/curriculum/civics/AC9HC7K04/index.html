Secular democracy and pluralist society

AC9HC7K04 · Year 7 Civics and Citizenship · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

how Australia's secular democracy and pluralist, multi-faith society draws upon diverse cultural origins, including Christian and Western heritage, distinct First Nations Australian histories and cultures, and migrant communities

Prior knowledge

Students understand Australia's system of government and democratic freedoms (AC9HC7K01, AC9HC7K02). From primary HASS they know about migration and Australian society (AC9HS6K03) and diversity in communities. This descriptor extends that to the concept of secular democracy and the religious and cultural dimensions of Australian identity.

By the end

Students explain how Australia's secular democracy and pluralist, multi-faith society draws upon diverse cultural origins including Christian and Western heritage, distinct First Nations histories and cultures, and migrant communities.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • defining the terms 'secular', 'multi-faith', 'diverse society', 'culturally diverse', 'Christian heritage', 'Western heritage' and 'pluralist', and discussing their relevance to Australia today
  • exploring the diversity of First Nations Australian communities and discussing the distinctiveness of a community linked to their Country/Place
  • identifying trends regarding religious observance and/or cultural heritage in Australian society, using the Australian Bureau of Statistics and other data sources
  • exploring the diversity of spiritualities among First Nations Australian communities, from traditional spirituality to the adoption of other religions such as Christianity and Islam
  • appreciating the cultural and historical foundations of Australia's Christian heritage and their impact on Australia's political and legal systems

Waypoints

  • 1. Key concepts: secular, pluralist, multi-faithHingeStudents can define and explain the terms 'secular', 'pluralist', 'multi-faith', and 'diverse society', and discuss their relevance to contemporary Australia.
  • 2. Christian and Western heritageStudents can explain the cultural and historical foundations of Australia's Christian heritage and their impact on Australia's political and legal systems.
  • 3. First Nations Australian histories and culturesStudents can explain the diversity of First Nations Australian communities and their distinct histories and cultures, including the distinctiveness of connections to Country/Place.
  • 4. Migrant communities and contemporary diversityStudents can explain how migrant communities contribute to Australia's pluralist, multi-faith society, and use data to identify trends in religious observance and cultural heritage.
