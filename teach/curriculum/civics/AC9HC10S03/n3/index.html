← AC9HC10S03 Analyse civic perspectives and interpretations

Assessing effectiveness of global governance actors

AC9HC10S03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can investigate a contemporary international event or situation and assess the effectiveness and values positions of global actors, including the Australian Government's response.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two global actors and their values positions in relation to a specific international event
  • Assesses the effectiveness of at least one actor's response with evidence
  • Evaluates the Australian Government's response to the event

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Evaluating perspectives and interpretations.

Width

Minimum
Investigate a contemporary international event or situation and identify the values positions of at least two global actors, including the Australian Government.
Typical
Investigate a contemporary international event or situation — armed conflict, development of an international agreement, or natural or humanitarian disaster — to assess the effectiveness and values positions of global actors such as NGOs, and evaluate the Australian Government's response.
Wider
  • Compare the responses of at least two different global actors (e.g. an NGO, a government, a multilateral body) to the same event and evaluate which response was most effective and why
  • Trace how the Australian Government's response to a specific international event has evolved over time and evaluate what drove the changes
  • Investigate a case where NGO action achieved outcomes that government actors could not and analyse what this reveals about the civic landscape of global governance

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierIntegration node — analysis of global actors, evaluation of perspectives, and assessment of the Australian Government's role are brought together in a specific case study. Teaching investment is in supporting synthesis across the multiple actors and evidence sources.
