International law and policy

AC9HC10K04 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

how Australia's international legal obligations shape Australian law and government policies, including those relating to First Nations Australians, and the issues related to the application of these obligations

Prior knowledge

Students know how individuals and groups participate in civic life and contribute to global citizenship (AC9HC9K05). They understand the court system's role in applying law (AC9HC9K03, AC9HC9K04).

By the end

Students explain how Australia's international legal obligations shape Australian law and government policies, including those relating to First Nations Australians, and analyse issues related to applying those obligations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating how many of the 9 key human rights treaties Australia has signed and ratified, and researching how at least one of these has shaped Australian law or government policy
  • explaining how international conventions and declarations, such as the Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples, have shaped Australian government law and policies with regards to First Nations Australians
  • analysing Australia's legal obligations to the environment under the Paris Agreement (2016) and the World Heritage Convention (1972), and/or to refugees under the Convention Relating to the Status of Refugees (1951) and its optional protocol (1967)

Waypoints

  • 1. Australia's key international treaty commitmentsHingeStudents can identify the key international treaties Australia has signed and ratified, and explain what signing and ratifying a treaty requires of the Australian Government.
  • 2. How international obligations shape domestic law and policyHingeStudents can explain how at least one international convention or declaration has shaped Australian law or government policy, using specific examples.
  • 3. Issues in applying international obligationsStudents can identify and analyse issues related to Australia's application of its international legal obligations, including tensions between international and domestic law, political will, and enforcement.
