Global interconnectedness and Australia's choices

AC9HC10K02 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 10 Civics and Citizenship · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Civics and Citizenship: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how global interconnectedness affects Australia's capacity to address global issues, and evaluate how this shapes Australian identity and policy choices.

Success criteria

  • Explains at least one way global interconnectedness enables and one way it constrains Australia's response to a global issue
  • Connects Australia's global participation to its national identity or values
  • Evaluates the implications for citizens acting in the global interest

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Analysing and evaluating global participation.

Width

Minimum
Explain how global interconnectedness has either helped or hindered Australia's ability to respond to a global issue.
Typical
Identify and explain how the forces of global interconnectedness both exacerbate and enhance Australia's ability to address a key global issue, and evaluate impacts on Australia's identity and citizens' choices to act in the global interest.
Wider
  • Investigate a specific case where Australia's international trade relationships have created tensions with its stated commitment to human rights
  • Examine how diaspora communities in Australia influence Australian foreign policy choices in relation to their countries of origin
  • Evaluate how the concept of global citizenship has evolved and what it demands of Australian citizens specifically

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node brings together participation knowledge, treaty obligations, and evaluative reasoning into a reflective account of Australia's global position and identity. Teaching investment is in supporting synthesis rather than adding factual content.
