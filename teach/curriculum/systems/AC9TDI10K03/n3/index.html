Lossy vs lossless — trade-offs and consequences

AC9TDI10K03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 Digital Technologies (knowledge) · Data representation

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (knowledge): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain the difference between lossy and lossless compression, identify contexts where each is appropriate, and describe the consequences of using each.

Lessons on this site

  • Lossy or lossless

Success criteria

  • Correctly distinguishes lossless (exact recovery) from lossy (permanent data loss) compression
  • Identifies an appropriate context for each type and justifies the choice
  • Names at least one consequence of using lossy compression in an inappropriate context

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Lossless compression — run-length encoding.

Width

Minimum
Explain the difference between lossy and lossless compression and give one example of each; identify one consequence of using lossy compression.
Typical
Explain that lossless compression preserves all original data (decompression recovers the original exactly) while lossy compression permanently discards information; describe appropriate contexts for each (e.g. medical images require lossless; music streaming tolerates lossy); explain why the same file format may not suit both contexts.
Wider
  • Compare audiovisual codecs (MP3 vs WAV, MP4 vs uncompressed video) and explain what information is discarded and why human perception makes this acceptable in most contexts
  • Investigate the consequences of lossy compression in high-stakes domains — medical imaging, legal evidence, archival records — and explain why lossless is required
  • Explore how image compression artefacts arise (e.g. JPEG block artefacts) and explain the algorithmic reason they appear

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe lossy/lossless distinction is abstract without concrete cases. Students need examples where the trade-off is consequential — medical imaging, audio quality, image artefacts — to understand why the distinction matters beyond a vocabulary definition.
