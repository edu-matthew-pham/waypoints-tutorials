Digital systems and peripherals

AC9TDI4K01 · Year 4 Digital Technologies (knowledge) · Digital systems

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (knowledge): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore and describe a range of digital systems and their peripherals for a variety of purposes

Prior knowledge

Students can identify and explore digital systems and their components for a purpose (AC9TDI2K01). They can name hardware and software as components of digital systems. They have not yet explored the specific categories of peripherals (input, output, storage) or described how peripherals expand system functionality.

By the end

Students explore and describe a range of digital systems and their peripherals, explaining how different peripherals serve input, output and storage purposes and how they expand what a digital system can do.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • experimenting with a range of peripherals to perform input, output and storage, for example a keyboard, touch screen, mouse, camera and microphone; a monitor, projector, printer or 3D printer; or speaker; or USB drive
  • adding peripherals to a digital system to expand its functionality, for example connecting a headset to a digital system to participate in an online lesson more effectively
  • exploring how they can use digital systems differently depending on the task, recognising that many digital systems can perform multiple tasks, for example a student can use a tablet to take photos, record audio and find information to create a presentation

Waypoints

  • 1. Peripherals as categoriesHingeStudents can sort peripherals into input, output and storage categories and give an example of each
  • 2. Peripherals expand functionalityHingeStudents can explain how adding a peripheral changes what a digital system can do
  • 3. Choosing peripherals for purposesStudents can describe which digital system and peripherals are appropriate for a given task and explain why
