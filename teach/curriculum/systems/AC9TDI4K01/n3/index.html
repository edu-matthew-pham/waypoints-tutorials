Choosing peripherals for purposes

AC9TDI4K01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 4 Digital Technologies (knowledge) · Digital systems

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (knowledge): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe which digital system and peripherals are appropriate for a given task and explain why

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one peripheral appropriate for a described task
  • Gives a reason for the choice that references the peripheral's function

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Peripherals expand functionality.

Width

Minimum
Given a task (e.g. recording a class presentation), identify the digital system and at least one peripheral needed and explain why they suit the task
Typical
Explain how different tasks require different combinations of digital systems and peripherals; describe how the same digital system (e.g. a tablet) can serve different purposes depending on which peripherals or features are used
Wider
  • Compare how a student in a city school and a student in a remote community might use different digital systems and peripherals to complete the same task — discuss what affects those choices
  • Explore how digital systems used in scientific data collection (e.g. sensors, cameras in field research) combine specialist peripherals for specific purposes

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents often anchor on a single device type (e.g. 'you need a computer'). Exploring varied task contexts — recording, presenting, communicating, storing — surfaces the idea that purpose drives peripheral selection.
Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates the category knowledge from node 1 and the functionality reasoning from node 2 into a purposeful selection decision — the components come together in a task-driven context.
