Wired and wireless networks

AC9TDI8K02 · Year 8 Digital Technologies (knowledge) · Networks and security

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (knowledge): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Investigate how data is transmitted and secured in wired and wireless networks including the internet

Prior knowledge

Students can explain how digital systems form networks to transmit data (AC9TDI6K02). They have not yet compared wired and wireless network properties, explored encryption, or investigated how protocols handle transmission errors.

By the end

Students investigate how data is transmitted across wired and wireless networks including the internet, by comparing network properties (bandwidth, latency, reliability), explaining how protocols handle packet transmission and errors, and explaining why and how encryption secures data in transit.

Waypoints

  • 1. Wired vs wireless network propertiesHingeStudents can describe the properties of wired and wireless networks and compare their bandwidth, latency and reliability
  • 2. Packets and protocolsHingeStudents can explain how data is broken into packets for transmission and how protocols like TCP handle errors when packets are lost
  • 3. Why data needs to be securedHingeStudents can explain why cryptography is necessary for securing data transmitted across networks, connecting the need to how packets travel through shared infrastructure
  • 4. How simple encryption worksStudents can explore how simple encryption algorithms transform data to protect it, and demonstrate an encrypt-decrypt cycle
  • 5. How the internet transmits and secures dataStudents can explain how the internet combines network infrastructure, packet transmission, protocols and encryption to transmit and secure data end to end
