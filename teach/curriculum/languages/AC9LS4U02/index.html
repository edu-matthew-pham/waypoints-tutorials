Grammatical structures and basic syntax

AC9LS4U02 · Years 3–4 Languages · Second language, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise target language conventions, grammatical structures and basic syntax in familiar texts and contexts

Prior knowledge

Students recognise grammatical agreement markers, basic word class features, simple verb forms, affirmative and negative statements (AC9LS2U02). They have not yet worked with plural agreement, expanded possessives, a range of prepositions, question words, or the distinction between different forms of key verbs.

By the end

Students recognise and use target language grammatical structures and basic syntax — including agreement patterns, pronouns, possessives, verb forms, prepositions, conjunctions and adverbs in familiar contexts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Agreement patterns and possessivesHingeStudents can apply agreement patterns in singular and plural forms and use possessive markers in familiar sentence contexts.
  • 2. Verb forms, prepositions and time adverbsStudents can use common verbs in familiar forms, prepositions for location and direction, and time adverbs in modelled sentence contexts.
  • 3. Conjunctions, question words and imperativesStudents can link ideas using conjunctions, use question words in modelled structures, and apply imperative forms in familiar contexts.
