← AC9LS10C05 Create texts for diverse contexts and audiences

Creating for real and community audiences

AC9LS10C05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create and present texts for real community audiences — including target language community peers, First Nations Australian cultural contexts, and public occasions.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Creates and presents at least one text for a genuine audience outside the classroom
  • Reflects on whether the text achieved its communicative purpose for that audience

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Creating across imaginative modes and narrative perspectives.

Width

Minimum
Create and present at least one text for a genuine audience outside the classroom — reflecting on whether it achieved its communicative purpose.
Typical
Create texts for real community purposes — social media, school publications, community events, cultural exchanges with target language community peers; reflect on the impact of a visit to a significant cultural location and present in the target language; compose and perform speeches for real occasions; create cultural ID profiles for exchange with target language community peers.
Wider
  • Create a text for a target language community peer exchange — receive and incorporate feedback about whether the text achieved its communicative purpose for that audience
  • Contribute to a First Nations cultural location text with appropriate permission — reflecting on the protocols followed and what they taught about cultural respect

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierCreating for real community audiences integrates all prior text creation capabilities — audience design (N1) and imaginative mode (N2) — and tests them in a genuine communicative context.
