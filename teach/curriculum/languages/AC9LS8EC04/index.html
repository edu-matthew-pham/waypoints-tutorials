Locate and process information in texts

AC9LS8EC04 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate and process information and ideas in familiar spoken, written and multimodal texts, responding in ways appropriate to cultural context, purpose and audience

Prior knowledge

Students enter the Y7-10 sequence without prior formal target language study. They bring general literacy strategies from English and may have incidental exposure to the target language. They have not yet applied information-locating strategies to target language texts across spoken, written and multimodal modes.

By the end

Students locate and process specific information and ideas from familiar spoken, written and multimodal texts in the target language, and respond in ways that demonstrate awareness of cultural context, purpose and intended audience.

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying key information in familiar textsHingeStudents can identify specific details and key points of information in familiar spoken, written or multimodal texts in the target language.
  • 2. Processing information across text typesStudents can process information from spoken, written and multimodal texts in the target language, summarising or classifying key ideas.
  • 3. Responding appropriately to cultural contextStudents can respond to texts in ways that demonstrate awareness of the cultural context, purpose and audience of the original text.
