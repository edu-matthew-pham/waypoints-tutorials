← AC9LS8EC05 Interpret and convey meaning in the target language

Understanding that translation involves more than word equivalence

AC9LS8EC05 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify that direct word-for-word translation does not always convey accurate meaning, and can explain at least one reason why.

Success criteria

  • Can identify at least one expression in the target language that cannot be translated word-for-word and explain why
  • Can evaluate an automated or peer translation of a short text and identify at least one improvement

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Inferring meaning from context.

Width

Minimum
Identify one expression or phrase in the target language that does not translate word-for-word into English; explain what additional knowledge is needed.
Typical
Recognise the distinction between literal and non-literal translation; identify idioms, fixed expressions and abbreviated forms that require interpretation beyond word equivalence; understand that abbreviations, slang and digital communication conventions vary between languages.
Wider
  • Analyse an automated translation of a familiar text — identify specific errors or awkward renderings and explain what the tool missed
  • Translate a short familiar English text into the target language and compare it with a classmate's or automated version — discuss where choices diverged and why

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierUnderstanding translation as interpretation rather than substitution draws on all prior nodes — dictionary use, cognate awareness, contextual inferencing — and adds the conceptual insight that meaning is shaped by culture and context, not only by language structure. This prepares students for EC05's Y9-10 demands.
