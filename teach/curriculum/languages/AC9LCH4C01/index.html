Initiate exchanges and respond to questions

AC9LCH4C01 · Years 3–4 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

initiate exchanges and respond to questions about home and classroom environments, using familiar expressions

Prior knowledge

Students can interact in classroom routines, personal introductions and collaborative activities using modelled expressions (AC9LCH2C01, AC9LCH2C02). They can make polite requests, express thanks and preferences. They have not yet independently initiated exchanges, maintained multi-turn conversations, asked for clarification, or used digital communication formats in the target language.

By the end

Students initiate exchanges and respond to questions about home and classroom environments using familiar expressions — including initiating and sustaining multi-turn conversations, seeking clarification, and using digital formats for interaction.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • initiating conversations by asking questions in different contexts using modelled responses
  • playing games where students take turns to role-play the teacher and give instructions
  • sharing information about themselves
  • participating in simple spoken exchanges with peers about familiar topics such as daily routines, local places or personal interests
  • initiating and maintaining communication by taking turns, for example, making suggestions
  • asking for clarification, using appropriate formulaic expressions and gestures
  • seeking advice and help from others
  • creating short texts such as emails, letters and text messages to interact with others
  • using emoticons in digital communication to enhance meaning

Waypoints

  • 1. Initiating and sustaining conversationsHingeStudents can initiate a conversation by asking a question in a familiar context and sustain it through at least three turns, using appropriate turn-taking strategies.
  • 2. Asking for clarification and seeking helpStudents can ask for clarification during an exchange and seek advice or help using appropriate formulaic expressions and gesture.
  • 3. Digital and written interaction formatsStudents can create short texts in digital or written formats to interact with others — inviting, congratulating, thanking or sharing information — using appropriate format conventions.
