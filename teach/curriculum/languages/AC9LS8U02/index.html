Apply grammatical structures to compose and respond

AC9LS8U02 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

apply understanding of grammatical structures and expressions to compose and respond to texts

Prior knowledge

Students can apply modelled grammatical structures including tense variation, comparatives, agreement and frequency adverbs (AC9LS6U02). They compose in present, past and near-future. They have not yet conjugated irregular verbs across a tense range, used compound tense forms, expanded cohesive devices, or applied expressions of quantity systematically.

By the end

Students apply grammatical structures to compose and respond to texts — including regular and irregular verb forms across a range of tenses, expanded cohesive devices, expressions of quantity, and adverbial modification.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • conjugating common regular verbs and high-frequency irregular verbs in the present, preterite and present perfect tenses
  • using present, past and immediate future tense verbs followed by infinitive
  • expanding use of cohesive devices to create compound sentences and link ideas
  • using adjectives to describe people and aspects of the environment
  • expressing quantity using a range of quantity expressions
  • using adverbs to modify the meaning of verbs and adjectives
  • indicating possession using grammatical structures appropriate to the target language

Waypoints

  • 1. Irregular verb forms across a tense rangeHingeStudents can use regular verb forms and high-frequency irregular verb forms across present, past and compound tenses, and use modal structures with infinitive.
  • 2. Expanded cohesive devices and connectorsStudents can use an expanded range of cohesive devices to create compound and complex sentences and link ideas across a text.
  • 3. Quantity expressions and adverbial modificationStudents can use a range of expressions of quantity and adverbs to modify verbs and adjectives — adding precision and nuance to composition.
