← AC9LS8U02 Apply grammatical structures to compose and respond

Irregular verb forms across a tense range

AC9LS8U02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, F–10: understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use regular verb forms and high-frequency irregular verb forms across present, past and compound tenses, and use modal structures with infinitive.

Success criteria

  • Uses at least three high-frequency irregular verb forms correctly in at least two different tenses
  • Uses at least one modal structure correctly in a composition context

Prior knowledge

Students can apply modelled grammatical structures including tense variation, comparatives, agreement and frequency adverbs (AC9LS6U02). They compose in present, past and near-future. They have not yet conjugated irregular verbs across a tense range, used compound tense forms, expanded cohesive devices, or applied expressions of quantity systematically.

Width

Minimum
Use at least three high-frequency irregular verb forms correctly in at least two tenses; use at least one modal structure correctly.
Typical
Use regular and high-frequency irregular verbs across present, past and compound tenses; use modal structures with infinitive for varied purposes; apply verb forms for first, second and third person; compose in connected past, present and near-future across varied topics.
Wider
  • Compose a narrative using all three main tense forms correctly — with a margin note explaining the tense choice for each section
  • Identify all irregular verb forms in a given text — categorise by verb and tense, and produce the conjugation pattern for two irregular verbs

Hinge

Irregular verb accuracy across tenses is the grammatical gate for expressing past experience authentically. Students with only present tense or regular past forms cannot produce the narrative and experience-sharing texts central to this band and the next.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsIrregular verb accuracy requires high-frequency production practice — students who know the rules still make errors under production pressure. Repeated composition tasks with varied topics build the automaticity needed.
