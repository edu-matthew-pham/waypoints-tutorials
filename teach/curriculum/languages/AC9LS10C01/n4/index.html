← AC9LS10C01 Initiate, sustain and extend exchanges

Sustaining exchanges about complex and topical subjects

AC9LS10C01 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can initiate and sustain exchanges about complex and topical subjects — including social issues, global topics and shared experiences — drawing on a full range of interaction strategies.

Success criteria

  • Initiates and sustains an exchange on a complex or topical subject for at least six turns
  • Uses a mix of at least three different interaction strategies across the exchange

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Adjusting language across unfamiliar contexts.

Width

Minimum
Initiate and sustain an exchange on a complex or topical subject for at least six turns using a mix of argument, narrative and discussion management strategies.
Typical
Engage in discussion of topical issues, social questions and shared experiences across familiar and unfamiliar subjects; use the full range of interaction strategies developed across prior nodes; sustain extended exchanges with varied interlocutors.
Wider
  • Participate in a sustained exchange on a topic of genuine personal significance — demonstrating that the target language can carry the full weight of the student's thinking
  • Initiate and maintain a target language exchange with a community member on a topic of mutual interest — adapting spontaneously to the interlocutor's responses

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierExtended exchanges on complex subjects integrate all prior interaction capabilities — narrative, argument, management and adjustment — in a sustained communicative performance.
