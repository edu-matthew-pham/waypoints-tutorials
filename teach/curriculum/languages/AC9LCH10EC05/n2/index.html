← AC9LCH10EC05 Create and present texts for diverse contexts

Multimodal community and cultural texts

AC9LCH10EC05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 9–10 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can produce multimodal texts — presentations, exhibitions, cultural performances, community resources — combining spoken, written and visual elements for specific community audiences.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Produces a multimodal text combining at least two modes for a community or cultural audience
  • Demonstrates deliberate mode selection appropriate to audience and purpose

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Evaluative and persuasive texts.

Width

Minimum
Produce a multimodal text combining at least two modes for a community or cultural audience, with deliberate mode selection justified by audience and purpose.
Typical
Produce multimodal presentations using data and statistics; compose texts for events with specific purposes; create cultural exhibitions and performances; write voice-overs and scripts for drama or comedy; create resources for Chinese-speaking community audiences; reflect on co-creating Acknowledgements of Country with First Nations Australians.
Wider
  • Create a multimodal community text for a real audience — evaluate its effectiveness with that audience and revise based on feedback
  • Produce a cultural performance or exhibition that draws on a specific target language cultural tradition — explain the cultural choices made and how they serve the audience

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepMultimodal community text production requires students to understand how different modes serve different purposes for different audiences. Practice across varied production formats — exhibitions, performances, presentations, community resources — builds the flexible multimodal production capacity needed.
