Create and present texts for diverse contexts

AC9LCH10EC05 · Years 9–10 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create and present informative and imaginative texts for diverse contexts and purposes, selecting vocabulary, expressions, sentence structures and textual features and conventions, to engage different audiences

Prior knowledge

Students can create spoken, written and multimodal informative and imaginative texts for purpose and audience (AC9LCH8EC05). They have drafted, revised and produced texts for community audiences. They have not yet created evaluative and persuasive texts at extended length, produced formal procedural or policy-based texts, or created literary texts drawing on specific target language literary traditions.

By the end

Students create and present informative and imaginative texts for diverse contexts — including evaluative, persuasive, procedural and literary forms — selecting language and conventions deliberately to engage different audiences.

Waypoints

  • 1. Evaluative and persuasive textsHingeStudents can produce evaluative and persuasive texts — issue analyses, debate speeches, community presentations — using evidence, balanced perspective and persuasive language structures.
  • 2. Multimodal community and cultural textsStudents can produce multimodal texts — presentations, exhibitions, cultural performances, community resources — combining spoken, written and visual elements for specific community audiences.
  • 3. Literary and formal texts using stylistic modelsStudents can create literary and formal texts using stylistic models — applying target language literary conventions, experimenting with formal language, and producing texts that demonstrate deliberate stylistic choices.
