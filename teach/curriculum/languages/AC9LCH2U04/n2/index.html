Cultural symbols, practices and language

AC9LCH2U04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 1–2 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify cultural symbols and practices associated with the target language and describe how language is used in those contexts.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two cultural symbols or practices associated with the target language
  • Describes at least one piece of language used in connection with a cultural practice

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Language use across communities.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two cultural symbols or practices associated with the target language and describe the language used in connection with each.
Typical
Explore cultural symbols and practices — festivals, art forms, games, crafts, community events — and the language associated with them; compare target language cultural practices with those of other communities in the class; discuss the role of language in cultural expression and identity.
Wider
  • Examine a cultural practice in depth — identify all the language used in connection with it (greetings, wishes, descriptions, instructions) and explain what the language reveals about the cultural values behind the practice
  • Compare a cultural practice from the target language community with a similar practice in another community represented in the class — identify where the practices and their associated language are similar or different

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyCultural symbol and practice knowledge is built through accumulation — students need encounter with varied practices (festivals, arts, games, food, crafts) before the connection between language and cultural practice becomes generalisable. Each encounter adds to the repertoire from which the broader insight emerges.
