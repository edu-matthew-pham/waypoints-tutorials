Component analysis and word building

AC9LCH4U02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 3–4 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify components or morphemes in target language characters or words and use component knowledge to build or analyse new words.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Identifies components of at least three compound characters or words
  • Explains how at least one component contributes to the meaning or sound of the character or word

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Writing system conventions and character formation.

Width

Minimum
Identify the components of at least three compound characters or words and explain how each component contributes to meaning or sound.
Typical
Identify and categorise characters or words by shared components; make connections between component forms and related meanings; mix and match known components to build new words; infer meaning of new words from known components; use digital tools to explore character structure.
Wider
  • Investigate the six types of character formation (or equivalent morphological patterns in the target language) and identify examples of each — explain the logic behind each formation type
  • Use component knowledge to make an educated guess about the meaning of an unfamiliar character or word — then verify using a dictionary and discuss accuracy of the prediction

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkComponent analysis requires students to apply a systematic framework to word/character structure — not just memorise individual items. Explicit instruction on component types and their semantic or phonetic roles builds the analytical capacity that supports vocabulary extension.
