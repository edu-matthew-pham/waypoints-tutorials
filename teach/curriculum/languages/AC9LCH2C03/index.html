Locate key information in texts and respond

AC9LCH2C03 · Years 1–2 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate, with support, key information in spoken and written texts, and respond using gestures, images, words and modelled sentences

Prior knowledge

Students can participate in classroom interactions and collaborative activities using modelled expressions (AC9LCH2C01, AC9LCH2C02). They can follow spoken instructions and respond through action. They have not yet systematically located and extracted key information from texts, made connections between text content and their own knowledge, or responded to texts with structured opinions or retelling.

By the end

Students locate key information in familiar spoken and written texts with support, and respond using gestures, images, words and modelled sentences — including identifying main points, making personal connections, and expressing opinions about characters and storylines.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • making connections between a simple text and self
  • making connections between world knowledge and target language to infer meaning of words
  • identifying details about people and events heard, seen or read in media
  • recognising that gestures and body language are integral to communicating in language for First Nations Australians, and showing examples of how they are also integral to communicating in the target language
  • showing understanding of main points in spoken and written texts
  • listening to imaginative texts and responding through play-acting or movement
  • identifying key words in familiar texts such as stories and rhymes
  • drawing and labelling a character or scene from rhymes or stories
  • participating in shared reading and responding to questions
  • identifying and justifying a favourite character using modelled language
  • expressing opinions about characters or retelling storylines after viewing or listening

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying key words and main points in textsHingeStudents can identify key words and main points in familiar spoken and written texts, demonstrating understanding through gesture, image, ordering or brief response.
  • 2. Making connections between texts and personal experienceStudents can make connections between text content and their own experience or world knowledge, and identify details about characters and events.
  • 3. Expressing opinions and retellingStudents can express a simple opinion about a character or text, and retell or summarise a storyline using modelled language.
