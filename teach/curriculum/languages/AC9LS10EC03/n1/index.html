← AC9LS10EC03 Discuss and reflect with peers

Planning and negotiating collaboratively

AC9LS10EC03 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use the target language to plan and negotiate with peers through a multi-stage process — assigning roles, making decisions and managing adjustments.

Success criteria

  • Participates in at least two planning stages of a collaborative task using target language for proposal, negotiation or decision
  • Manages at least one adjustment or disagreement during the task using target language rather than reverting to English

Prior knowledge

Students can engage in modelled non-verbal, spoken and written exchanges to organise activities in familiar contexts (AC9LS8EC03). They have not yet discussed, planned and reflected on activities and experiences in ways that require extended planning, negotiation and post-activity reflection in the target language.

Width

Minimum
Participate in a collaborative planning task in the target language that involves at least two stages (e.g. proposal, decision, assignment of roles).
Typical
Plan, negotiate and execute a collaborative project or event using spoken, written and digital exchanges in the target language; manage disagreements and adjustments through negotiation; use appropriate language for proposing, agreeing, disagreeing and revising.
Wider
  • Plan and execute a real or simulated event (e.g. a class celebration, a fundraiser, a trip) using the target language as the primary medium throughout — from planning to evaluation
  • Compare how negotiation and planning language conventions differ in formal and informal contexts in the target language

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierMulti-stage collaborative planning integrates discussion strategies (EC02), functional classroom language (EC02 Y7-8) and transactional exchange skills (EC03 Y7-8) into a sustained, purposeful communicative task. The teaching investment is in creating genuine planning contexts, not simulated planning with predetermined outcomes.
