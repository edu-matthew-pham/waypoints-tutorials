Analyse target language texts using metalanguage

AC9LCH10U03 · Years 9–10 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse a range of target language texts, using metalanguage to explain linguistic, textual and cultural elements

Prior knowledge

Students can reflect on and explain similarities and differences between target language and English, support younger learners, and analyse metaphorical meaning (AC9LCH8U03). They have applied comparative metalanguage across text types. They have not yet analysed stylistic devices in target language texts, compared genre organisation across languages, described genres unique to target language literary tradition, or used metalanguage to explain linguistic, textual and cultural elements simultaneously.

By the end

Students analyse a range of target language texts using metalanguage to explain linguistic, textual and cultural elements — including stylistic devices, genre conventions, structural analysis, and culturally-specific literary forms.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • analysing linguistic features using metalanguage such as double negatives and parallel sentences
  • analysing a range of text types and explaining how different language features suit different purposes in cultural contexts
  • analysing and categorising cultural and linguistic elements in target language expressions
  • comparing key features used in formal and informal contexts in target language and English
  • analysing the way content is structured in a range of target language texts
  • analysing the use of stylistic devices in a range of written texts and evaluating their effectiveness
  • reading and comparing how ideas are ordered and substantiated across genres and languages
  • describing the purpose and features of genres uniquely important in target language literature

Waypoints

  • 1. Stylistic devices and rhetorical analysisHingeStudents can identify and analyse stylistic devices used in target language texts — including rhetorical, figurative and structural devices — and evaluate their effectiveness.
  • 2. Genre organisation and content developmentStudents can analyse how content is structured and ideas are developed in different target language genres, including how genre conventions compare across languages.
  • 3. Culturally-specific literary genres and traditionsStudents can describe the purpose and features of genres unique to target language literary tradition, exploring their origins and demonstrating cultural knowledge in text analysis.
