Sustain interactions in varied contexts

AC9LS10EC01 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

initiate and sustain interactions in familiar and some unfamiliar contexts to exchange ideas, opinions and experiences about their own and others' personal worlds

Prior knowledge

Students can interact using modelled language to exchange personal information in familiar contexts and manage classroom language (AC9LS8EC01, AC9LS8EC02). They have not yet initiated and sustained interactions independently or exchanged opinions and personal experiences in unfamiliar contexts.

By the end

Students initiate and sustain interactions in the target language in familiar and some unfamiliar contexts, exchanging ideas, opinions and personal experiences about their own and others' worlds, using strategies to maintain flow and authenticity.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • initiating conversations with peers, family and others using appropriate expressions
  • expressing, comparing and explaining likes, dislikes and preferences
  • interviewing other teenagers to build relationships and share views on aspects of young people's lives
  • developing non-verbal and spoken techniques and strategies to maintain the flow and authenticity of conversations
  • sharing impressions of past experiences and significant events
  • discussing personal views on topics such as bullying, beauty ideals and music
  • using appropriate discussion strategies such as active listening, showing interest, acknowledging others' opinions, responding to and inviting contributions

Waypoints

  • 1. Initiating conversations and topicsStudents can initiate conversations in the target language using appropriate opening strategies and introduce a topic for discussion.
  • 2. Sustaining interaction and managing flowHingeStudents can sustain an interaction in the target language using strategies to maintain flow, show interest and respond to others — including in some unfamiliar contexts.
  • 3. Exchanging opinions and personal experiencesStudents can express and exchange opinions, compare views and share personal experiences in the target language, including on topics of personal and social relevance.
