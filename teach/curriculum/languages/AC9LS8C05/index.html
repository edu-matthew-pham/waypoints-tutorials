Create texts for specific purposes with appropriate vocabulary and conventions

AC9LS8C05 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create and present spoken, written and multimodal, informative and imaginative texts for specific purposes, selecting vocabulary, expressions, grammatical structures, features and conventions appropriate to text type and context

Prior knowledge

Students can create texts using varied structures and text-type conventions (AC9LS6C05). They create for familiar formats and purposes. They have not yet selected vocabulary and structures deliberately for a specific purpose or created biographical and narrative texts integrating multiple text features.

By the end

Students create and present informative and imaginative texts for specific purposes — selecting vocabulary, grammatical structures and conventions appropriate to text type, purpose and context.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • designing a digital poster or electronic brochure to advertise a social event related to lifestyles or cultural events
  • using digital tools to create a slideshow with audio about a planned trip to a target language community region
  • reporting on own and others' experiences of events
  • creating a print or digital poster to promote travel to a significant cultural location on a First Nations Country/Place
  • composing bilingual menus, recipes or programs for target language community-themed events
  • creating subtitles, captions or commentaries for texts that inform the school community
  • creating cartoons, role-plays or short stories about a past event or a dream for the future
  • using voice recording and digital presentation software to create, perform or record imaginative texts
  • preparing a biographical account of an influential figure
  • using up-to-date and emerging terms relating to pop culture, technology and media when creating texts

Waypoints

  • 1. Selecting vocabulary and structures for purposeHingeStudents can select vocabulary, expressions and grammatical structures deliberately to suit the purpose of a text — moving beyond default or most familiar choices.
  • 2. Creating and presenting across formats and audiencesStudents can create and present texts across a range of informative and imaginative formats — using conventions appropriate to each and presenting for real audiences.
