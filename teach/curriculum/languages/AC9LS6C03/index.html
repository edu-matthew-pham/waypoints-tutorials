Locate, process and respond to information in texts

AC9LS6C03 · Years 5–6 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate and process information and ideas in a range of spoken, written and multimodal texts, and respond in different ways to suit purpose

Prior knowledge

Students can locate and respond to key information in familiar texts and categorise it using graphic organisers (AC9LS4C03). They extract information from prepared texts. They have not yet researched independently across multiple sources, processed information for specific purposes, or compared ideas across imaginative texts.

By the end

Students locate and process information and ideas in a range of spoken, written and multimodal target language texts, and respond in ways suited to the purpose — including research, comparison, creative response and procedural tasks.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • researching topics of interest using a range of sources and organising and sharing information in print or digital format
  • listening to, reading and viewing authentic texts and extracting key points relating to aspects of life in target language communities
  • comparing main characters, events and ideas in different types of imaginative texts
  • listening to or viewing First Nations Australian authors' stories in English and responding using words and modelled sentences in the target language
  • adapting an informative or imaginative text by resequencing events, adding a new element, or creating an alternative ending
  • discussing key messages and cultural elements in creative texts
  • responding to artworks and images from target language communities
  • playing interpretation games with peers involving non-verbal cues
  • listening to a simple procedure and extracting keywords to complete a task
  • reading texts about destinations or conditions and making decisions as a group

Waypoints

  • 1. Researching and processing information across sourcesHingeStudents can research topics of interest across multiple target language sources, extracting and organising information for a specified purpose.
  • 2. Responding to texts with personal reactions and comparisonsStudents can respond to target language texts in varied ways — expressing personal reactions, opinions and comparisons about characters, ideas and cultural elements.
  • 3. Adapting texts for new purposesStudents can adapt target language texts for new purposes or audiences — resequencing, adding elements, changing cultural setting or creating alternative versions.
