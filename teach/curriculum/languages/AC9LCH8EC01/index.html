Initiate and sustain exchanges

AC9LCH8EC01 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

initiate and sustain exchanges, related to students' experiences and perspectives

Prior knowledge

Students entering the background language learner pathway at Y7-8 bring significant home and community experience of the target language — spoken fluency in familiar registers, cultural knowledge, and informal literacy to varying degrees. They have not yet participated in structured academic exchanges, used the target language for sustained opinion-based discussion, or navigated formal and digital interaction contexts in an academic setting.

By the end

Students initiate and sustain exchanges related to their experiences and perspectives, using culturally appropriate language across face-to-face and digital contexts, sharing opinions and responding to others.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • initiating and sustaining conversations in a culturally appropriate way to share personal interests such as hobbies and future plans
  • clarifying or paraphrasing the teacher's instructions when needed and responding to questions
  • expressing preferences, stating reasons and making sure other participants are included
  • engaging in class discussion by responding to questions and expressing agreement or disagreement in a culturally appropriate way
  • exchanging emails using appropriate format and modelled language highlighting school, home or social activities
  • responding to invitations to participate in personal celebrations, including accepting and declining appropriately
  • sharing information with peers in target language communities in face-to-face and secure online conversations
  • maintaining contact with friends or peers and recording events in everyday life through a secure online journal or blog
  • exchanging information about special events or community celebrations

Waypoints

  • 1. Culturally appropriate initiation and responseHingeStudents can initiate and sustain exchanges in culturally appropriate ways — including expressing preferences, justifying views, and ensuring others are included.
  • 2. Digital and written interactionStudents can exchange information and share experiences through digital and written formats — emails, online journals, blogs — using appropriate format conventions.
  • 3. Responding to invitations and social scenariosStudents can respond to invitations and participate in exchanges about special events and community celebrations using appropriate social language.
