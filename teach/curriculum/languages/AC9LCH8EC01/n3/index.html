← AC9LCH8EC01 Initiate and sustain exchanges

Responding to invitations and social scenarios

AC9LCH8EC01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can respond to invitations and participate in exchanges about special events and community celebrations using appropriate social language.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Responds to an invitation using appropriate acceptance or decline conventions
  • Exchanges information about a special event or celebration in culturally appropriate target language

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Digital and written interaction.

Width

Minimum
Respond appropriately to at least one invitation — accepting or declining — using appropriate target language conventions, and exchange information about at least one special event or celebration.
Typical
Respond to invitations — accepting and declining in appropriate ways; share information about special events and community celebrations; participate in exchanges about personal and community life with peers in target language communities; share life experiences in face-to-face and online contexts.
Wider
  • Compare invitation and response conventions across different target language community contexts — identify how formality and register vary depending on relationship and occasion
  • Create and respond to an invitation for a culturally significant event — demonstrating full command of the relevant social language conventions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierSocial scenario response integrates culturally appropriate interaction (node 1) and digital interaction skills (node 2) into the full range of social communication contexts. The teaching investment is in creating genuine social scenarios — real invitations, real events — that require authentic target language use.
