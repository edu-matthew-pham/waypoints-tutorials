Plan and negotiate with others

AC9LCH6C02 · Years 5–6 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

participate in activities that involve planning and negotiating with others, using modelled and idiomatic language, to agree, suggest and resolve

Prior knowledge

Students can plan and transact with others in familiar contexts using a range of phrases and modelled sentences (AC9LCH4C02). They can take on roles, give instructions, and participate in cultural presentations. They have not yet used idiomatic language in collaborative planning, negotiated competing preferences to reach resolution, or participated in community-facing collaborative projects.

By the end

Students participate in planning and negotiating activities using modelled and idiomatic language — making suggestions, resolving differences, reaching agreements — across collaborative tasks including community and school events.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • planning and collaborating in shared activities such as running a school assembly in the target language
  • participating in planning and presentation of a role-play with peers, such as an imagined trip, discussing preferences and bargaining
  • listening to or viewing community media and participating in advertised activities
  • collaborating to decide on a menu for a class lunch and negotiating items, prices and delivery
  • contributing to the development of a set of class rules
  • providing feedback about peer presentations using modelled language
  • collaboratively writing a children's story and reading it to younger learners
  • planning a campaign for a school or community event by creating digital posters or pamphlets
  • researching and arranging a visit by an expert to share knowledge and promote language learning in the school community

Waypoints

  • 1. Language for suggesting, agreeing and resolvingHingeStudents can use the language of suggestion, agreement and resolution — including idiomatic expressions — to navigate collaborative decision-making.
  • 2. Idiomatic language in collaborative contextsStudents can identify and use idiomatic expressions appropriate to collaborative and planning contexts, understanding that idiomatic meaning differs from literal meaning.
  • 3. Collaborative planning for real outcomesStudents can collaborate to plan and execute a shared project or event — making decisions, dividing tasks, and resolving differences — using target language throughout the planning process.
