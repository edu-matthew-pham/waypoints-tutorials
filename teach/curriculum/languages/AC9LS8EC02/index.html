Develop language for classroom exchanges

AC9LS8EC02 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop language to interact in exchanges, routines, tasks and responsibilities related to classroom and interests

Prior knowledge

Students can exchange basic personal information in familiar contexts using modelled language (AC9LS8EC01). They have not yet developed the functional classroom language needed to participate in the routines, tasks and responsibilities of the target language learning environment.

By the end

Students interact in the target language to manage classroom routines, ask for help or permission, follow and give instructions, express opinions and participate in classroom tasks and collaborative activities.

Waypoints

  • 1. Following and giving classroom instructionsHingeStudents can follow modelled instructions and commands in the target language and attempt to give simple instructions to peers.
  • 2. Asking for help, information and permissionStudents can use modelled phrases to ask for help, clarification or permission in the target language.
  • 3. Expressing opinions and reactionsStudents can express simple opinions and reactions using set phrases in the target language.
  • 4. Participating in collaborative tasks and activitiesStudents can participate in collaborative classroom tasks and games using target language for sharing, encouraging, taking turns and completing joint activities.
