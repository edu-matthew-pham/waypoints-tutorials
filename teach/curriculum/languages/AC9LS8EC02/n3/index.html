Expressing opinions and reactions

AC9LS8EC02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can express simple opinions and reactions using set phrases in the target language.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Can use at least two different opinion phrases in the target language in a classroom exchange
  • Can respond to a peer's opinion with agreement or a simple contrasting phrase

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Asking for help, information and permission.

Width

Minimum
Use at least two modelled set phrases to express agreement, disagreement or a simple opinion in a classroom context.
Typical
Use a range of set phrases to express opinions, reactions and thoughts; respond to others' opinions with agreement or simple contrast; use spontaneous interjections and expressions to maintain simple interactions.
Wider
  • Participate in a structured classroom debate or 'four corners' opinion activity using target language opinion phrases
  • Collect and compare opinion expressions across different registers — identify which are appropriate in which social contexts

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyOpinion expression at Y7-8 entry is phrase-dependent — students need a bank of set expressions before they can participate authentically in opinion exchanges. Varied low-stakes opinion tasks build this bank.
