Language reflects cultural practices, values and identity

AC9LS6U04 · Years 5–6 Languages · Second language, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise that language reflects cultural practices, values and identity, and that this impacts on non-verbal and spoken communication

Prior knowledge

Students can identify connections between the target language and cultural practices using conceptual vocabulary (AC9LS4U04). They reflect on cross-cultural comparisons. They have not yet explored contact language influences on the target language, examined cultural stereotypes critically, or analysed modern language change.

By the end

Students recognise that language reflects cultural practices, values and identity — including through contact language influences, cultural stereotypes, modern language change, and First Nations Australian cultural expressions.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • understanding that there are indigenous languages spoken in addition to the target language in target language communities, and that these languages influence the target language
  • researching target language communities, inviting special guests or showcasing learning about cultural aspects
  • recognising how the target language is widespread, and that traditions, values and practices may reflect collective identities of individuals from different target language communities
  • exploring how language and culture are expressed through First Nations Australians' song, dance or artworks, considering similarities and differences
  • sharing reactions to cultural elements witnessed in authentic materials
  • becoming aware of cultural stereotypes that can lead to generalisations and misconceptions
  • investigating modern influences on the target language such as pop culture, digital media and migration
  • reflecting on culturally appropriate ways of offering praise, recognition and gratitude, or declining requests
  • understanding that target language communities may have shared history with other languages and cultures
  • identifying visible and invisible elements that contribute to identity across all cultures

Waypoints

  • 1. Contact language influences and cultural stereotypesHingeStudents can explain how contact with other languages has influenced the target language vocabulary, and recognise that cultural stereotypes about target language communities are oversimplifications.
  • 2. Modern language change and identity expressionStudents can explain how modern influences shape target language change, and reflect on visible and invisible elements of identity expressed through language.
