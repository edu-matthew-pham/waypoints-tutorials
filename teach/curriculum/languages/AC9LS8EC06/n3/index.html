← AC9LS8EC06 Create informative and imaginative texts

Applying grammatical structures in text creation

AC9LS8EC06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply familiar grammatical structures accurately enough in a target language text to convey intended meaning to an audience.

Success criteria

  • Produces a text in which grammatical structures are accurate enough for the meaning to be understood by an audience unfamiliar with the student's intent
  • Can identify and self-correct at least one grammatical error in their own draft during revision

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Selecting vocabulary and expressions.

Width

Minimum
Produce a text in which the grammatical structures required for the text type (e.g. present tense verbs, basic agreement, interrogative forms) are applied with sufficient accuracy to convey meaning.
Typical
Apply a range of grammatical structures (tense, agreement, sentence types) in both informative and imaginative texts; self-correct common errors during drafting; connect ideas using basic connectives.
Wider
  • Review and revise a peer's draft text — identify grammatical errors that affect meaning and suggest corrections with explanations
  • Experiment with grammatical variation (e.g. changing sentence type, adding detail with prepositions or adjectives) to observe how it changes the effect of a text

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsGrammatical accuracy in text production is a fluency issue at Y7-8 — students typically understand the structures but apply them inconsistently under the cognitive load of text creation. Focused drafting-and-revision cycles rather than isolated grammar exercises are the most productive practice structure.
