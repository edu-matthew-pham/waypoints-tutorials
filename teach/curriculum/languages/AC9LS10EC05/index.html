Interpret and translate for intercultural understanding

AC9LS10EC05 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

apply strategies to interpret and translate non-verbal, spoken and written interactions and texts to convey meaning and intercultural understanding in familiar and unfamiliar contexts

Prior knowledge

Students have developed and begun to apply interpretation and translation strategies in familiar contexts — including dictionary use, cognate recognition, contextual inferencing and understanding word-for-word limits (AC9LS8EC05). They have not yet applied these strategies in unfamiliar contexts or with the explicit goal of conveying intercultural understanding.

By the end

Students apply a range of interpretation and translation strategies to non-verbal, spoken and written texts in familiar and unfamiliar contexts, conveying meaning and intercultural understanding — including recognising culturally embedded concepts, evaluating translation tools, and managing communication challenges.

Waypoints

  • 1. Translating culturally embedded meaningHingeStudents can identify and interpret expressions or concepts in the target language that are culturally embedded — where meaning cannot be conveyed through word-for-word translation.
  • 2. Evaluating translation tools and strategiesStudents can evaluate the effectiveness and limitations of translation tools and strategies, including digital translation tools, for different contexts and purposes.
  • 3. Managing communication challengesStudents can apply repair and adaptation strategies to manage communication challenges in the target language — including in unfamiliar contexts.
