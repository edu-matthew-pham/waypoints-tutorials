Analyse texts using metalanguage

AC9LCH10EU03 · Years 9–10 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse a range of Chinese texts, using metalanguage to explain linguistic, textual and cultural elements

Prior knowledge

Students can compare language structures and features across target language and English using metalanguage (AC9LCH8EU03). They have applied comparative metalanguage to text types and genre conventions. They have not yet conducted full stylistic analysis of target language texts, examined essay organisation conventions specific to the target language, analysed language in media contexts, or described genres unique to the target language literary tradition.

By the end

Students analyse a range of target language texts using metalanguage to explain linguistic, textual and cultural elements — including stylistic devices, genre organisation, content structure, and culturally-specific literary forms.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using metalanguage in Chinese or English to identify the grammatical differences between languages
  • analysing the sequence of content in different text types such as blogs or news articles
  • explaining the use of data such as census information and surveys to support views
  • incorporating statistical data, quotations and research-based evidence in own texts
  • analysing language used in a range of text types comparing emails for different purposes
  • analysing language and writing techniques used in traditional target language texts
  • analysing the layout of magazines and online texts to identify how images and words reinforce ideas
  • identifying linguistic terms and how world events are reported in target language media
  • comparing literature across languages and recognising different features
  • exploring examples of literary language and how grammatical features differ from everyday text

Waypoints

  • 1. Stylistic and linguistic analysisHingeStudents can identify and analyse grammatical differences between languages using metalanguage, and analyse language in a range of text types for purpose and audience.
  • 2. Genre organisation and essay conventionsStudents can analyse how content is structured in target language text types — including essay organisation conventions — and compare these with English equivalents.
  • 3. Literary and cultural text analysisStudents can analyse stylistic devices in literary texts, describe target language-specific literary forms, and explain the cultural elements embedded in literary and cultural texts.
