Reflect on how identity is shaped by language and culture

AC9LS10U04 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

reflect on and evaluate how identity is shaped by language(s), culture(s), attitudes, beliefs and values, and how these affect ways of communicating

Prior knowledge

Students can explain how language variation signals identity and social relationships, and how co-official languages relate to community identity (AC9LS8U04). They have not yet evaluated critically how language shapes belonging and exclusion, engaged in intercultural projects for real audiences, or reflected on language learning as identity experience.

By the end

Students reflect on and evaluate how identity is shaped by language, culture, attitudes, beliefs and values — including belonging and exclusion, cultural change, and personal intercultural experience.

Waypoints

  • 1. Language, belonging, exclusion and cultural changeHingeStudents can evaluate how language choices create belonging or exclusion and reflect on how cultural change affects language and identity.
  • 2. Intercultural engagement and community projectsStudents can engage in intercultural projects for real community audiences — exploring cultural knowledge and connections through genuine target language interaction.
  • 3. Reflecting on language learning as identity experienceStudents can reflect on their own language learning experience as an identity experience — examining how learning a new language changes who they are and how they communicate.
