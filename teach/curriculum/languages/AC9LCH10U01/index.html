Features and conventions of spoken target language for fluency

AC9LCH10U01 · Years 9–10 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

apply features and conventions of spoken the target language to extend fluency in responding to and creating texts appropriate to contexts, purposes and audiences

Prior knowledge

Students can apply tonal and prosodic knowledge fluently in text response and creation, and comprehend diverse speakers (AC9LCH8U01). They have discriminated subtle tonal and regional features. They have not yet delivered formal speeches to real audiences, analysed tone and rhythm for own composition, or quoted from traditional literature with appropriate intonation.

By the end

Students apply features and conventions of spoken target language to extend fluency in responding to and creating texts — including formal speech, analysis of tone and rhythm patterns for composition, and comprehension strategies for diverse accents.

Waypoints

  • 1. Formal speech deliveryHingeStudents can deliver formal speeches to real or realistic audiences using appropriate intonation, stress, phrasing and cultural conventions.
  • 2. Analysing tone and rhythm for compositionStudents can analyse tone patterns and rhythm in spoken or written target language texts and apply insights to develop their own texts with similar prosodic features.
  • 3. Comprehension strategies for diverse accentsStudents can develop and apply strategies for understanding spoken target language from speakers with diverse regional accents, using contextual inference and accent awareness.
