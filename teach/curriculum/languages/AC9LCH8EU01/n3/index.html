← AC9LCH8EU01 Tone, intonation and phrasing for fluency

Performance and fluency in formal contexts

AC9LCH8EU01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can perform target language texts — poems, speeches, formal presentations — with attention to rhythm, tone, stress and emotional expression for a specific audience.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Performs a target language text demonstrating deliberate attention to at least one prosodic feature
  • Can explain the prosodic choice made and its intended effect

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Self-monitoring and accent awareness.

Width

Minimum
Perform at least one target language text demonstrating deliberate attention to tone, rhythm or stress for communicative or emotional effect.
Typical
Perform Chinese poems with attention to rhythm, voice, emotion and gesture; prepare and rehearse speeches with Chinese-speaking family or community members; perform for specific audiences; use intonation and stress deliberately to convey meaning.
Wider
  • Perform a classical target language poem or literary text — explain the prosodic choices made and how they serve the text's meaning
  • Compare own performance of a text with a published recording — identify specific prosodic differences and evaluate the effect of each

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierPerformance integrates phonological awareness (node 1) and self-monitoring (node 2) into a full communicative act. The teaching investment is in creating genuine performance conditions — real audiences, real texts — where prosodic control serves communicative and artistic purpose.
