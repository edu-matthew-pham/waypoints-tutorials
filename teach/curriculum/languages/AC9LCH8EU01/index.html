Tone, intonation and phrasing for fluency

AC9LCH8EU01 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use tone-syllables and apply intonation, stress and phrasing to enhance fluency, and respond to and create texts in a range of contexts

Prior knowledge

Students entering at Y7-8 have developed spoken target language fluency in home and community contexts, typically with regional or family accent features. They have not yet systematically examined their own pronunciation, compared it with standard forms, applied Pinyin as an analytical tool, or performed for formal or academic audiences.

By the end

Students use tone-syllables, intonation, stress and phrasing to enhance fluency in responding to and creating texts across a range of contexts — including self-monitoring, performance, and awareness of phonological variation.

Waypoints

  • 1. Phonological awareness and Pinyin as a toolHingeStudents can explain key features of target language phonology — tones, Pinyin romanisation, homophone discrimination — and use Pinyin to support pronunciation and digital text generation.
  • 2. Self-monitoring and accent awarenessStudents can compare their own speech patterns with standard spoken target language, identify differences, and make targeted adjustments to pronunciation and fluency.
  • 3. Performance and fluency in formal contextsStudents can perform target language texts — poems, speeches, formal presentations — with attention to rhythm, tone, stress and emotional expression for a specific audience.
