Exchange information in familiar contexts

AC9LS8EC01 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

interact with others using modelled language to exchange information in familiar contexts about self and personal worlds

Prior knowledge

No formal prior study of the target language assumed (Y7-10 Entry sequence). Students bring general communicative competence in English and may have informal or incidental exposure to the target language. They have not yet learned to operate within the conventions and structures of the target language to exchange personal information.

By the end

Students interact with others in the target language using modelled language structures to exchange information about themselves, their families, routines and personal worlds in familiar contexts, making appropriate choices about formality.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exchanging greetings with others using appropriate formal or informal language
  • introducing and describing themselves, others and possessions
  • expressing likes, dislikes and preferences using simple modelled structures
  • interviewing others about their families, pets, likes and dislikes, hobbies and leisure time activities
  • responding to questions about feelings
  • conversing with others about routines, events and leisure activities using language associated with time and place
  • discussing significant or special events in their lives with peers or target language community members

Waypoints

  • 1. Greetings and basic social exchangesHingeStudents can exchange greetings and basic social courtesies in the target language, choosing appropriate formal or informal register.
  • 2. Introducing and describing self and othersHingeStudents can introduce themselves and others and describe personal details (name, age, family, possessions) using modelled structures in the target language.
  • 3. Expressing likes, dislikes and preferencesStudents can express likes, dislikes and preferences about familiar topics using modelled structures in the target language.
  • 4. Exchanging information about routines and daily lifeStudents can interact with others to exchange information about routines, events and daily life activities using modelled language in familiar contexts.
