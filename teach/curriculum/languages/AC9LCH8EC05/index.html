Create informative and imaginative texts

AC9LCH8EC05 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create spoken, written and multimodal informative and imaginative texts, using vocabulary, expressions, sentence structures, textual conventions and appropriate characters and/or Pinyin, to suit purpose and audience

Prior knowledge

Students entering at Y7-8 can produce spoken and written target language in familiar registers from home exposure. They may have informal literacy skills of varying depth. They have not yet created extended texts using academic vocabulary, textual conventions, and deliberate stylistic choices for a range of purposes and audiences in a school context.

By the end

Students create spoken, written and multimodal informative and imaginative texts using vocabulary, expressions, sentence structures and textual conventions appropriate to purpose and audience.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating digital presentations adding sound, voice and music
  • producing a poster or digital brochure for a cultural event
  • creating texts depicting experiences of target language-speaking people in Australia
  • writing a journal entry or contributing to a school newsletter reflecting on a school excursion
  • developing material to explain a procedure or practice such as a recipe or tutorial
  • creating subtitles to match short video clips of intercultural experiences
  • composing songs, jingles or video clips to promote real or imagined products
  • writing and performing role-plays using alternative versions of cultural stories
  • creating an original story based on modelled expressions or idioms from traditional literature

Waypoints

  • 1. Informative texts for real purposesHingeStudents can create informative texts — presentations, posters, brochures, newsletters — using appropriate textual conventions and supporting visual elements.
  • 2. Imaginative and creative textsStudents can create imaginative texts — stories, songs, role-plays, cultural narratives — using descriptive language, cultural references, and deliberate creative choices.
  • 3. Audience-aware multimodal productionStudents can create multimodal texts combining spoken, written and visual elements, making deliberate production choices appropriate to purpose and audience.
