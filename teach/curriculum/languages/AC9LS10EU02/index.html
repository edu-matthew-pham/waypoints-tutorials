Select grammar to enhance meaning

AC9LS10EU02 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select and use structures and features of the target language grammatical and writing systems to enhance meaning and create spoken, written and multimodal texts

Prior knowledge

Students have developed knowledge of and can use key grammatical structures and writing system features of the target language — including agreement, verb conjugation, sentence construction and orthographic conventions (AC9LS8EU02). They have not yet selected and used more complex grammatical structures (comparatives, multiple tenses, complex sentences) to enhance meaning purposefully.

By the end

Students select and use a range of grammatical structures — including comparatives, multiple tense forms, pronoun systems, cohesive devices and complex sentence constructions — to enhance meaning in spoken, written and multimodal target language texts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • knowing when to omit definite and indefinite articles, and adjusting for gender-change cases
  • using collective nouns and noticing the use of singular verbs
  • noticing changes in meaning when adjective position changes
  • using comparatives and superlatives, including some irregular forms
  • understanding and using indefinite adjectives and pronouns
  • using possessive and demonstrative pronouns
  • understanding the use of relative pronouns in familiar contexts
  • using modelled structures with direct and indirect objects
  • recognising different past tense forms and comparing uses with English past tense
  • understanding and using regular and irregular preterite/past forms
  • understanding and using the immediate future
  • recognising future and conditional tenses and their conjugational patterns
  • elaborating on ideas with prepositions
  • understanding the function of impersonal expressions
  • using cohesive devices in complex sentences
  • forming adverbs from adjectives

Waypoints

  • 1. Articles, agreement and determiner refinementStudents can apply article and agreement rules with increased accuracy, including in cases where the target language differs from expected patterns.
  • 2. Comparatives, superlatives and intensifiersStudents can use comparatives and superlatives — including irregular forms — and intensifiers to express and nuance comparisons in the target language.
  • 3. Tense variety and temporal expressionHingeStudents can recognise and use multiple tense forms in the target language — including past tenses and immediate or simple future — to express temporal relationships.
  • 4. Complex sentence construction and cohesive devicesStudents can construct complex sentences and use cohesive devices in the target language to connect and develop ideas across a text.
