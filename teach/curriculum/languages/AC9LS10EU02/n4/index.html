Complex sentence construction and cohesive devices

AC9LS10EU02 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can construct complex sentences and use cohesive devices in the target language to connect and develop ideas across a text.

Success criteria

  • Produces a text with at least three complex sentences using cohesive devices to connect ideas
  • Uses at least two different cohesive devices correctly in the same text

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Tense variety and temporal expression.

Width

Minimum
Produce at least three complex sentences in a target language text using cohesive devices to connect ideas.
Typical
Use a range of cohesive devices to connect and develop ideas; construct sentences with subordinate clauses, relative pronouns and prepositional phrases; use impersonal expressions and discourse markers to add nuance and manage text flow.
Wider
  • Analyse the cohesive structure of a native speaker text — identify all cohesive devices and discuss their effect on the text's coherence and flow
  • Compare complex sentence construction in the target language and English — identify where the target language requires different syntactic choices for the same meaning

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierComplex sentence construction integrates the grammar and vocabulary knowledge from nodes 1–3 into extended, coherent text production. The move from producing individual sentences to constructing cohesive texts is the key integration challenge — tasks requiring multi-paragraph texts with explicit cohesive marking support this.
