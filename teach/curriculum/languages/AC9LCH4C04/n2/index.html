← AC9LCH4C04 Strategies to interpret culturally-loaded language

Untranslatable expressions and cultural symbols

AC9LCH4C04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 3–4 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify words and expressions in the target language that do not translate directly into English, explain their cultural meaning, and recognise cultural symbols and their significance.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two untranslatable expressions and explains their cultural meaning in context
  • Can explain why at least one expression cannot be directly translated into English

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Formal and informal register in context.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two expressions or terms that cannot be directly translated and explain what each means in its cultural context.
Typical
Identify culturally-specific terms and expressions — colloquialisms, culturally-embedded phrases, occasion-specific language — that resist direct translation; compare these with English equivalents or absence of equivalents; recognise cultural symbols and identify the language and values associated with them.
Wider
  • Research a culturally-specific expression or symbol and create a bilingual explanation resource — explaining what it means, when it is used, and what it reveals about cultural values
  • Compare untranslatable expressions across two languages — identify whether they reflect fundamentally different cultural values or can be approximately explained

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepUntranslatable expressions are understood through varied cultural encounters — different occasions, relationships and contexts. Building a repertoire of examples across contexts gives students the cultural framework to approach new untranslatable expressions with strategies rather than confusion.
