Grammar and writing systems

AC9LS8EU02 · Years 7–8 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop knowledge of, and use structures and features of, the target language grammatical and writing systems to understand and create spoken, written and multimodal texts

Prior knowledge

Students enter the Y7-10 sequence without formal prior knowledge of the target language grammatical system. They bring implicit grammatical knowledge from English and may have informal exposure to the target language. They have not yet learned the grammatical structures or writing system conventions of the target language.

By the end

Students develop knowledge of and use key grammatical structures and writing system features of the target language — including word classes, agreement, verb conjugation, sentence construction and orthographic conventions — to understand and create spoken, written and multimodal texts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • recognising the number of consonants and vowels in the target language
  • noticing and applying different rules of capitalisation in the target language and English
  • identifying and applying graphic symbols specific to the target language on keyboards and in writing systems
  • understanding and applying grammatical gender rules
  • developing awareness of additional gender patterns in word endings
  • using appropriate definite and indefinite articles that agree in gender and number
  • understanding that adjectives and possessives agree in number and gender with nouns
  • developing knowledge of number and gender agreement with demonstratives
  • understanding the form and function of subject pronouns
  • understanding and using verb conjugations for the present tense of regular and common irregular verbs
  • recognising multiple versions of the verb 'to be' in some target languages
  • using verb conjugations in affirmative, negative and interrogative forms
  • seeking information using interrogatives
  • using prepositions of place and common contractions
  • linking ideas using connectives
  • recognising the functions of elements such as prefixes and suffixes

Waypoints

  • 1. Writing system and orthographic conventionsHingeStudents can recognise and apply the orthographic conventions of the target language writing system, including any characters, symbols or capitalization rules that differ from English.
  • 2. Grammatical gender and agreementHingeStudents can recognise and apply grammatical gender and number agreement rules of the target language where these are grammatically required.
  • 3. Verb conjugation and subject pronounsHingeStudents can recognise and use subject pronouns and conjugate familiar verbs in the target language, including common irregular forms.
  • 4. Sentence construction and connectivesStudents can construct grammatically coherent sentences in the target language, link ideas using connectives, and apply interrogative forms to ask questions.
