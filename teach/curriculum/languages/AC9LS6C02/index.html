Plan and negotiate with others

AC9LS6C02 · Years 5–6 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

participate in activities that involve planning and negotiating with others, using language that expresses information, preferences and ideas

Prior knowledge

Students can participate in collaborative planning activities using familiar phrases and modelled structures (AC9LS4C02). They allocate tasks and follow sequences. They have not yet used language to express and negotiate preferences and ideas — only to coordinate task allocation.

By the end

Students participate in planning and negotiation activities that require expressing preferences and ideas, checking understanding, delegating and managing decisions in the target language.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • participating in an activity for an assembly, school or class event or performance, and creating promotional materials
  • working with others to create resources for the school community
  • checking on understanding and progress during learning tasks
  • delegating roles and tasks with peers to plan for and participate in an event
  • developing a range of interview questions to ask a real or imagined target language community guest
  • participating in real or imagined transactions involving negotiation, requesting information and considering options
  • creating digital displays, presentations or performances to showcase progress in the target language
  • demonstrating numeracy and decision-making skills by agreeing with a partner about how to spend a set budget
  • working in a team to solve a puzzle or problem using target language clues

Waypoints

  • 1. Expressing preferences and ideas in planningHingeStudents can express preferences and ideas in collaborative planning contexts — contributing personal views and negotiating with others about options and decisions.
  • 2. Problem-solving and decision-making in collaborative tasksStudents can participate in planning tasks that involve decision-making with constraints — budgets, quantities, priorities — using target language to justify choices and reach agreement.
