Recognise and respond to classroom language

AC9LS2C01 · Years 1–2 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise and respond to modelled classroom-related greetings, instructions and routines, and personal introductions

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise meaning and communicate using modelled words, gestures and formulaic phrases with support (AC9LSF01). They have no formal curriculum experience with structured classroom routines, following instructions, or producing personal introductions in the target language.

By the end

Students recognise and respond to modelled greetings, classroom instructions, routines and personal introductions in the target language, and use modelled language to introduce themselves and express simple personal information.

Waypoints

  • 1. Recognising and responding to greetings and farewellsHingeStudents can recognise and respond appropriately to modelled greetings and farewells — selecting responses appropriate to time of day, relationship and context.
  • 2. Following classroom instructions and routinesStudents can follow and respond to modelled classroom instructions, commands and routine language — using action, gesture or target language response.
  • 3. Producing personal introductionsStudents can produce personal introductions and respond to simple personal questions using modelled sentence structures — including personal details and expressions of preference.
