Participating in games and songs

AC9LS2C02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 1–2 Languages · Second language, F–10: communicating · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can participate in structured language games, songs and chants using formulaic expressions and following spoken and visual cues.

Success criteria

  • Participates in at least two activity types using appropriate target language expressions
  • Uses at least one spontaneous expression during a game or activity

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise and communicate using modelled words and formulaic phrases with support (AC9LSF01). They have limited experience using the target language in structured collaborative activity.

Width

Minimum
Participate in at least two different activity types using target language expressions appropriate to each.
Typical
Participate in games involving turn-taking, matching, questioning and responding; perform songs and chants with gesture and movement; use spontaneous expressions during play; use digital tools collaboratively for language activities.
Wider
  • Lead a familiar game or song in the target language — calling out instructions or leading a chant for classmates
  • Use a spontaneous target language expression in an unscripted moment during a game — demonstrating transfer beyond rehearsed contexts

Hinge

Games and songs are the primary interaction context at this band. Students who cannot use formulaic language within structured activities are excluded from the main vehicle for language development, and spontaneous expression in role-play and collaborative tasks is impossible without this base.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFormulaic language in games and songs becomes automatic through repeated performance across varied activity types — the game or song is the practice vehicle.
