Interpret and analyse texts with cultural and critical awareness

AC9LCH8C03 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

interpret and analyse information, ideas and opinions in a range of spoken, written and multimodal texts, and respond appropriately to cultural context, purpose and audience

Prior knowledge

Students can locate and process information in texts analytically and respond for purpose and audience (AC9LCH6C03). They can analyse perspective and language variation. They have not yet evaluated credibility of sources, analysed humour and cultural reference, identified register variation across formal and informal contexts, or responded with empathetic or critical personal stances.

By the end

Students interpret and analyse information, ideas and opinions in a range of spoken, written and multimodal texts — evaluating credibility, analysing cultural reference and humour, and responding appropriately to cultural context, purpose and audience.

Waypoints

  • 1. Source credibility and critical information evaluationHingeStudents can evaluate the credibility of information sources in the target language and apply strategies to assess and compare information across sources.
  • 2. Analysing register, humour and cultural referenceStudents can identify and analyse register variation, humour and culturally-embedded reference in target language texts, and discuss how these features serve the text's purpose.
  • 3. Responding with empathy and critical stanceStudents can respond to imaginative and informative texts with empathy and critical awareness — demonstrating understanding of perspective, cultural context and authorial intention.
