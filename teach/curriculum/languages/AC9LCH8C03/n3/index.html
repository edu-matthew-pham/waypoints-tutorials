Responding with empathy and critical stance

AC9LCH8C03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Languages · Background speakers, F–10: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can respond to imaginative and informative texts with empathy and critical awareness — demonstrating understanding of perspective, cultural context and authorial intention.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Produces a response demonstrating empathy with a perspective or character
  • Includes at least one critical observation about cultural context or authorial choice with textual support

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Analysing register, humour and cultural reference.

Width

Minimum
Produce at least one response to a text that demonstrates both empathy with a character or perspective and a critical observation about the text's cultural context or authorial choices.
Typical
Respond to narratives with empathy — writing or performing from a character's perspective; identify differences in formal and informal register and replicate these in own responses; compare cultural expressions across texts; respond to First Nations Australian stories in English in target language.
Wider
  • Write a response to a text that takes a critical stance — challenging an assumption, identifying a cultural bias, or questioning an authorial choice — with textual evidence
  • Compare responses to the same text across different readers or cultural perspectives — discuss how background knowledge and cultural positioning shape interpretation

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierEmpathetic and critical response integrates source evaluation (node 1) and cultural reference analysis (node 2) into a full interpretive act. The teaching investment is in creating genuine reading and response conditions — real texts, real audiences — that require both engagement and critical distance.
