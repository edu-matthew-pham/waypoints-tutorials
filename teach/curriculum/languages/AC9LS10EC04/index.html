Interpret across a wide range of texts

AC9LS10EC04 · Years 9–10 Languages · Second language, from Year 7: communicating

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Languages: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

interpret information, ideas and perspectives in a wide range of spoken, written and multimodal texts and respond appropriately to cultural context, purpose and audience

Prior knowledge

Students can locate and process information in familiar texts and respond with some awareness of cultural context (AC9LS8EC04). They have not yet interpreted information across a wide range of text types, distinguished fact from opinion, or produced responses that demonstrate nuanced awareness of cultural context and audience.

By the end

Students interpret information, ideas and perspectives in a wide range of spoken, written and multimodal target language texts, and produce responses that are appropriate to the cultural context, purpose and audience of the original text — including texts with divergent perspectives.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • reading tourist brochures and travel websites about diverse locations in target language speaking communities
  • obtaining information about a festival, experience, event or tourist site and writing a series of postcards
  • accessing a range of texts on travel to target language speaking countries and writing a checklist
  • listening to or reading First Nations Australians' stories in English and creating a profile in the target language
  • watching a documentary on global issues and summarising the data
  • analysing and comparing information obtained from a range of sources on topics of social and cultural interest
  • identifying different perspectives in texts such as articles, reports or blogs, by distinguishing between facts and opinions
  • gathering information from a range of sources including personal commentaries by target language speaking peers and adults
  • responding to imaginative texts by modifying key elements
  • researching, presenting and taking a position on issues relating to contemporary topics

Waypoints

  • 1. Interpreting a wide range of text typesStudents can interpret information and ideas from a wider range of text types than encountered at Y7-8, including some unfamiliar formats and longer or more complex texts.
  • 2. Distinguishing perspective and identifying biasHingeStudents can identify different perspectives in target language texts and distinguish between fact and opinion.
  • 3. Responding with cultural and audience awarenessStudents can produce responses to target language texts that demonstrate nuanced awareness of cultural context, purpose and intended audience.
