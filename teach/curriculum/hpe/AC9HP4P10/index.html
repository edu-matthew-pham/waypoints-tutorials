Health and wellbeing behaviours

AC9HP4P10 · Years 3–4 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate and apply behaviours that contribute to their own and others' health, safety, relationships and wellbeing

Prior knowledge

Students have investigated health messages and practices in their community and discussed their purposes (AC9HP2P06) and identified and demonstrated protective behaviours (AC9HP2P05). They understand health messages and basic safety behaviours but have not yet investigated or applied the full range of health behaviours across nutrition, physical activity, mental health and safety.

By the end

Students investigate and apply behaviours that contribute to their own and others' health, safety, relationships and wellbeing.

Waypoints

  • 1. Safe storage of medications and substancesStudents can identify how medications and other substances can be stored safely in the home and at school.
  • 2. Nutrition and healthy eating patternsStudents can explore the benefits of eating patterns that reflect the Australian Guide to Healthy Eating and investigate nutritional information about foods.
  • 3. Mental health, belonging and connectionStudents can discuss the importance of a sense of belonging and connection in promoting mental health and wellbeing.
  • 4. Sustainability and health behavioursStudents can investigate ways to change their behaviours to support both their own health and the sustainability of the Earth's systems.
