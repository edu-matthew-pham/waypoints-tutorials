Regular activity and health benefits

AC9HP4M04 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 3–4 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe how regular physical activity influences sleep quality, concentration and overall wellbeing.

Success criteria

  • Names at least two health benefits of regular physical activity.
  • Explains how at least one benefit connects to a specific body function (e.g. 'activity improves sleep because it helps the body feel tired in a healthy way').
  • Identifies one change they could make to be more regularly active and explains the expected benefit.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Warm-up and preparation for activity.

Width

Minimum
Describe at least two health benefits of regular physical activity, including at least one beyond physical fitness (e.g. sleep, concentration).
Typical
Describe how regular physical activity influences multiple dimensions of health and wellbeing, including sleep, concentration, mood and physical health, using examples from their own participation experiences.
Wider
  • Investigate the Australian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines for Children and Young People and compare their own activity patterns against the recommendations, identifying gaps and proposing changes.
  • Research and present how regular physical activity is linked to mental health and wellbeing, drawing on one real-world example (e.g. a community sport program or physical activity initiative).

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepHealth benefit understanding deepens when students connect it to a range of activity types and personal contexts, not just formal sport. Exploring benefits through classroom activity, outdoor adventure, sport and incidental movement builds a more holistic understanding.
