← AC9HPFM01 Fundamental movement skills

Object manipulation

AC9HPFM01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 4 · Foundation Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can send, control and receive objects (rolling, throwing, catching, kicking) in different ways and at different levels.

Success criteria

  • Demonstrates at least one sending skill and one receiving skill with a manipulable object
  • Attempts to adjust body position to send or receive the object at different heights or distances

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Locomotor skills in play.

Width

Minimum
Practise at least one sending skill (throw or kick) and one receiving skill (catch or stop) using a ball or similar object.
Typical
Practise a range of object manipulation skills — throwing, catching, rolling, kicking — in minor games; send and receive objects at different heights and distances.
Wider
  • Explore sending and receiving objects using non-standard equipment (scarves, beanbags, balloons) to isolate tracking and timing
  • Practise water confidence skills such as floating with a buoyancy aid and receiving objects in/near water
  • Combine object manipulation with locomotion in simple game structures (e.g. carry-and-pass relay)

Hinge

Object manipulation skill is a prerequisite for most minor game and sports contexts in Y1-2. Students with very limited manipulation experience cannot access game structures that form the basis of later FMS progression.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFine and gross motor patterns for throwing and catching require repeated, low-pressure practice. Use varied equipment sizes and distances to build consistency before applying in game contexts.
Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepDifferent equipment types (balloon, beanbag, ball) and environments (pool, outdoor) broaden manipulation experience and reveal transfer gaps.
