Leadership and collaboration

AC9HP8M09 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

practise and apply leadership, collaboration and group decision-making processes when participating in a range of physical activities

Prior knowledge

Students have participated positively in groups and teams by contributing to group activities, encouraging others and negotiating roles and responsibilities (Y5-6). They can participate constructively but have not yet been expected to undertake explicit leadership roles or apply group decision-making processes.

By the end

Students practise and apply leadership, collaboration and group decision-making processes when participating in a range of physical activities.

Waypoints

  • 1. Understanding leadership and collaboration rolesStudents can identify different leadership and collaboration roles, describe what each involves, and explain how the roles contribute to team and group outcomes.
  • 2. Reading group dynamicsHingeStudents can interpret verbal, physical and situational cues from others in a team or group and respond appropriately to support positive group functioning.
  • 3. Practising leadership and collaborationStudents can adopt varied leadership and collaboration roles across physical activities, contributing actively to group decision-making and reflecting on how their actions affected outcomes.
