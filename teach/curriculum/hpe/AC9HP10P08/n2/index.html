← AC9HP10P08 Managing health risk situations

Rehearsing and evaluating risk responses

AC9HP10P08 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 9–10 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can rehearse and evaluate strategies for managing health risk situations, including first aid responses

Success criteria

  • Rehearses a strategy for at least one named risk situation
  • Evaluates its effectiveness — identifies what worked, what was difficult, and what would be changed
  • Evaluation is specific to the scenario context, not generic ('it went well')

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Identifying and planning for risk situations.

Width

Minimum
Rehearse one strategy for a health risk situation and evaluate its effectiveness — what worked, what barriers emerged, what would be done differently
Typical
Rehearse responses to a range of risk scenarios including peer pressure situations and first aid emergencies (including CPR); evaluate the appropriateness and effectiveness of responses; investigate behaviours that constitute safe, legal and ethical conduct in digital environments and identify actions to take when behaviour becomes abusive
Wider
  • Practise and evaluate CPR and basic first aid responses — evaluate own performance against criteria and identify what further practice is needed
  • Rehearse a range of realistic refusal skill scenarios (substance offers, unsafe situations) and evaluate which verbal and non-verbal strategies are most effective across different social contexts
  • Evaluate available digital safety resources and support services for young people — assess which are most accessible and effective for specific risk situations young people in your community face

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyRehearsal across multiple risk scenario types builds a repertoire of response strategies that can be accessed in real situations. A single rehearsed response is insufficient — the elaborations explicitly require rehearsal across peer pressure scenarios, first aid emergencies, and digital safety situations. The evaluation component requires students to assess effectiveness, not just complete the rehearsal.
