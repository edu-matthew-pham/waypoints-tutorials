Group roles and teamwork

AC9HP4M09 · Years 3–4 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

perform a range of roles in respectful ways to achieve successful outcomes in group or team movement activities

Prior knowledge

Students have applied strategies to work collaboratively when participating in physical activities (AC9HP2M05). They have experience collaborating but have not yet taken on differentiated roles within teams or considered how role performance affects group outcomes.

By the end

Students perform a range of roles respectfully to achieve successful outcomes in group or team movement activities.

Waypoints

  • 1. Cooperative skills in movement tasksStudents can use cooperative skills to complete a movement task, such as a partner balance, passing strategy or team strategy.
  • 2. Maintaining possession — passing and listeningStudents can work cooperatively with team members to maintain possession in a game by passing to other players and listening to teammates.
  • 3. Modifying activities for inclusionHingeStudents can modify physical activities to ensure that everyone is included, such as changing equipment, rules or playing space.
