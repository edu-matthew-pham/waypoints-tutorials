← AC9HP2M02 Moving and manipulating objects

Investigating object and space manipulation

AC9HP2M02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 1–2 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use different types of equipment and explore different ways of manipulating objects and space to solve a movement challenge or create a game.

Lessons on this site

Success criteria

  • Uses at least two types of equipment in a movement challenge or game context
  • Describes how the equipment affected what they could do

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Movement variation — speed, direction, level.

Width

Minimum
Use at least two types of equipment to attempt a movement challenge; describe how using different equipment changed what they could do.
Typical
Use a range of equipment to create an original game or solve a movement challenge; compare how different equipment choices affected the movement possibilities and game outcomes.
Wider
  • Invent a new game based on rules and equipment from a familiar game and evaluate whether the rules and equipment provide an interesting and challenging game
  • Use stimuli such as equipment, rhythm and music to create a simple playground game and assess which game they enjoy most
  • Compare moving around different outdoor spaces and identify which types of movement are most appropriate for safe and efficient travel through each space

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents need experience with a range of equipment types before they can genuinely compare effectiveness. Build a catalogue of equipment-movement combinations first, then move to evaluation.
