Community diversity and inclusion

AC9HP8P05 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate strategies that influence how communities value diversity and propose actions they can take to promote inclusion in their communities

Prior knowledge

Students have described and implemented strategies to value diversity in their communities (Y5-6). They can describe and implement strategies but have not yet been asked to investigate the broader mechanisms by which communities come to value diversity, or to propose community-level actions.

By the end

Students investigate strategies that influence how communities value diversity and propose actions to promote inclusion in their communities.

Waypoints

  • 1. Understanding diversity and its valueStudents can explain what diversity means in community contexts, identify key dimensions of diversity relevant to their own community, and describe why valuing diversity matters for health and wellbeing.
  • 2. Investigating strategies that promote community valuing of diversityHingeStudents can investigate specific strategies or initiatives that have influenced how communities value diversity, explaining the mechanisms by which they create change.
  • 3. Proposing actions to promote inclusionStudents can propose specific, realistic actions to promote inclusion in their own communities, with reasoning that connects the proposed action to its intended effect.
