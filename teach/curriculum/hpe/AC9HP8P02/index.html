Changes, transitions and support

AC9HP8P02 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse the impact of changes and transitions, and devise strategies to support themselves and others through these changes

Prior knowledge

Students have investigated resources and strategies to manage changes and transitions, including puberty (Y5-6). They can identify strategies and resources but have not yet been expected to analyse the impact of transitions systematically or devise strategies for supporting others.

By the end

Students analyse the impact of changes and transitions, and devise strategies to support themselves and others through these changes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating the changing nature of peer and family relationships and proposing strategies to manage these changes
  • accessing and assessing health information and services that support young people to effectively manage changes and transitions as they grow older, including sexual health information about sexually transmitted infections, contraception and safe sexual practices
  • examining how ceremonies of First Nations Australians involve sharing of cultural knowledge leading to changes in social standing and responsibilities in communities
  • examining the notion of 'border crossing'; that is, how First Nations Australians live across multiple cultures, and how these transitions can impact on a sense of belonging to culture, family and peer groups
  • evaluating and practising coping, communication and problem-solving skills to manage changes and emotions associated with puberty and getting older

Waypoints

  • 1. Understanding types of changes and transitionsStudents can identify the range of significant changes and transitions relevant to their age group and describe the types of impact — social, emotional, physical — each can have.
  • 2. Analysing the impact of changes and transitionsHingeStudents can analyse the specific impact of one or more transitions on individuals — including emotional, relational and identity effects — and explain what factors influence how significant the impact is.
  • 3. Devising strategies for supportStudents can devise practical strategies to support themselves and others through specific transitions, including accessing relevant health information and services.
