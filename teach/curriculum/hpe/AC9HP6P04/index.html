Respect and empathy in relationships

AC9HP6P04 · Years 5–6 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe and demonstrate how respect and empathy can be expressed to positively influence relationships

Prior knowledge

At Years 3-4, students selected, used and refined personal and social skills to establish, manage and strengthen relationships (AC9HP4P04). They have a repertoire of relationship skills. This descriptor requires specifically demonstrating respect and empathy as relationship-influencing mechanisms, and understanding how language and behaviour directed at others (including First Nations Australians) can affect relationships.

By the end

Students describe and demonstrate how respect and empathy can be expressed to positively influence relationships in a range of contexts.
