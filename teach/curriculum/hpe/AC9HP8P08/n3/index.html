← AC9HP8P08 Protective behaviours and community resources

Refining protective behaviours through scenario practice

AC9HP8P08 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply and refine protective behaviours across a range of help-seeking scenarios, adjusting their approach based on the specific context and available resources.

Success criteria

  • Applies protective behaviours in at least one scenario
  • Identifies at least one way to refine the approach for the specific context
  • Connects at least one protective behaviour to a specific community resource that would support it

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Evaluating community resources for help-seeking.

Width

Minimum
Apply protective behaviours in at least one scenario and describe one way they refined their approach based on the specific context.
Typical
Apply and refine protective behaviours across multiple scenario types; explain how bystanders play a role in ensuring safety; practise strategies for managing online abuse, substance pressure and mental health crises; connect protective behaviours to specific community resources.
Wider
  • Explore strategies for dealing with online abuse and bullying when there is an imbalance of power — including grooming and image-based abuse — and practise reporting situations to appropriate authorities and through online tools
  • Collaborate with peers to suggest strategies for scenarios where a friend is being encouraged to use a substance, evaluating which strategies are most likely to be effective in a real peer context

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsProtective behaviour refinement requires repeated scenario practice — one rehearsal does not build the reliable, habitual responses that protective behaviour depends on in real situations.
Integration: a combining task at the wider tierRefined protective behaviours integrate knowledge of scenarios, resource evaluation and practical strategy — connecting resources to behaviours is essential so students can act, not just plan.
