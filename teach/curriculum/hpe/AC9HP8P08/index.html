Protective behaviours and community resources

AC9HP8P08 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

refine protective behaviours and evaluate community resources to seek help for themselves and others

Prior knowledge

Students have analysed and rehearsed protective behaviours and help-seeking strategies in online and offline situations (Y5-6). They have rehearsed protective behaviours but have not yet been expected to refine them through evaluation or to evaluate the quality and suitability of community resources.

By the end

Students refine protective behaviours and evaluate community resources to seek help for themselves and others.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring different strategies they can use, and resources or help sites they can access or refer others to, if they are experiencing symptoms of anxiety or depression
  • examining scenarios to highlight how emotions, dispositions, social supports, and gender and cultural norms can affect a person's willingness to seek help
  • exploring strategies for dealing with online abuse and bullying when there is an imbalance of power, such as grooming, image-based abuse or negative social interactions, and reporting the situation to appropriate authorities, trusted adults and through online tools
  • collaborating with peers to suggest strategies they could use if they are being encouraged to use a substance such as alcohol, e-cigarettes, cannabis or inhalants
  • analysing how bystanders play a role in ensuring online spaces are positive and safe, and examining how support services such as kidshelpline and the eSafety Commissioner can provide support for young people who feel unsafe, bullied or abused online
  • exploring help-seeking scenarios young people may encounter and sharing strategies for dealing with each situation, including situations linked to substance use, mental health issues, safety and risk-taking, and sexual health

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying protective behaviours and when they applyStudents can identify a range of protective behaviours appropriate to different risk or help-seeking scenarios and explain the reasoning behind each.
  • 2. Evaluating community resources for help-seekingHingeStudents can identify and evaluate community resources available for help-seeking in a range of scenarios, assessing their accessibility, relevance and quality.
  • 3. Refining protective behaviours through scenario practiceStudents can apply and refine protective behaviours across a range of help-seeking scenarios, adjusting their approach based on the specific context and available resources.
