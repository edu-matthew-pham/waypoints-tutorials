Gender stereotypes and societal roles

AC9HP6P03 · Years 5–6 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate how the portrayal of societal roles and responsibilities can be influenced by gender stereotypes

Prior knowledge

At Years 3-4, students described how choices and actions can be influenced by stereotypes (AC9HP4P03). They understand that stereotypes exist and can shape behaviour. This descriptor extends that understanding specifically to gender stereotypes and their influence on societal roles and responsibilities — a more specific and politically situated application.

By the end

Students investigate how gender stereotypes influence the portrayal of societal roles and responsibilities, identifying limiting effects and proposing strategies for gender equality.
