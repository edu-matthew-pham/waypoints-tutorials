← AC9HP8P01 Values, beliefs and identities

Reflecting on personal identity development

AC9HP8P01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can reflect on how their own values and beliefs — and those of the people and communities around them — have contributed to their developing sense of identity.

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one personal, family or cultural value or belief that has influenced their own identity
  • Reflects on at least one specific way that value or belief has shaped their sense of self

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Analysing influence on identity development.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one personal or family value or belief and reflect on one way it has shaped their own sense of identity.
Typical
Reflect on how values and beliefs from multiple sources (personal, family, friends, cultural community) have influenced their own identity development; connect this reflection to the broader analysis completed in prior nodes.
Wider
  • Reflect on how navigating between different cultural value systems (e.g. for students with bicultural or multicultural backgrounds) has shaped their developing identity in distinctive ways
  • Investigate how their own values about identity compare to and differ from those of their family, peer group and cultural community, reflecting on what this reveals about identity negotiation

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierReflection integrates the conceptual analysis of values/beliefs/identity with personal experience — instruction should position reflection as an application of the analytical framework, not as separate personal disclosure.
