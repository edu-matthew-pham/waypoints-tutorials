Movement concepts applied to outcomes

AC9HP6M03 · Years 5–6 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate how different movement concepts related to effort, space, time, objects and people can be applied to improve movement outcomes

Prior knowledge

At Years 3-4, students demonstrated how movement concepts related to effort, space, time, objects and people can be applied when performing movement sequences (AC9HP4M03). They can identify and apply individual movement concepts in structured sequences but have not systematically investigated how manipulating multiple concepts improves outcomes.

By the end

Students investigate how movement concepts related to effort, space, time, objects and people can be applied and combined to improve movement outcomes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • demonstrating an understanding of how to adjust the force and speed of an object to improve accuracy and control
  • creating a movement sequence that demonstrates variations in flow and levels, and involves individuals moving together in time
  • working with a partner to explore pushing and pulling movements and how these can be manipulated to generate and perform counterbalances
  • developing strategies that exploit the playing space to create overlaps and extra attackers
