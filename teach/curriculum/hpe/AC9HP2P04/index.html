Seek, give or deny permission

AC9HP2P04 · Years 1–2 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

practise strategies they can use when they need to seek, give or deny permission respectfully

Prior knowledge

Students have explored how to seek, give or deny permission respectfully when sharing possessions or personal space (AC9HPFP04).

By the end

Students practise strategies for seeking, giving or denying permission respectfully.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • practising strategies they can use when they need to give or refuse consent; for example, saying yes and no in an assertive manner, using non-verbal body cues and gestures
  • practising ways to interact with others in a fair and respectful way in play and other activities, regardless of differences in gender, abilities, race or personality
  • exploring situations where they need to seek, give or deny permission and practising strategies to assert themselves; for example, saying no to inappropriate touching

Waypoints

  • 1. Seeking, giving and refusing consentHingeStudents can practise strategies for giving or refusing consent, including verbal and non-verbal approaches.
  • 2. Fair and respectful interaction regardless of differenceStudents can practise ways to interact with others in a fair and respectful way in play and other activities, regardless of differences in gender, abilities, race or personality.
