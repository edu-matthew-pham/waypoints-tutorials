Fundamental movement in varied situations

AC9HP2M01 · Years 1–2 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

practise fundamental movement skills and apply them in a variety of movement situations

Prior knowledge

Students have practised fundamental movement skills in minor game and play situations (AC9HPFM01). They have experimented with different ways of moving their body safely and manipulating objects and space (AC9HPFM02).

By the end

Students practise fundamental movement skills and apply them in a variety of movement situations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • performing locomotor movements using different body parts to travel in different directions
  • selecting and implementing different movement skills to be successful in a range of games
  • performing fundamental movement skills involving controlling objects with equipment and different parts of the body
  • practising gliding forward and backward in the water using arm and kicking movements
  • demonstrating balances and describing what helps to maintain stable positions

Waypoints

  • 1. Locomotor and non-locomotor movementHingeStudents can perform locomotor movements using different body parts to travel in different directions, and perform non-locomotor balances.
  • 2. Selecting and applying movement skills in gamesHingeStudents can select and implement different movement skills to be successful in a range of games.
  • 3. Controlling objects with equipment and body partsStudents can perform fundamental movement skills involving controlling objects with equipment and different parts of the body.
  • 4. Applying skills across varied movement situationsStudents can practise fundamental movement skills and apply them purposefully across a variety of movement situations, including games, sequences, and object control contexts.
