← AC9HP8P06 Emotional self-management

Devising self-management strategies

AC9HP8P06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Personal, social and community health

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can devise strategies to self-manage emotional responses that are specifically responsive to identified influencing factors, and evaluate their likely effectiveness.

Success criteria

  • Devises at least one strategy for self-managing an emotional response in a specific situation
  • Explains why the strategy addresses the factors influencing that response
  • Considers how the strategy prevents or limits negative impact on others

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Analysing emotional responses in context.

Width

Minimum
Devise at least one strategy for self-managing an emotional response in a specific situation and give a reason why it would be effective.
Typical
Devise a range of self-management strategies responsive to different factor types and situations; evaluate which strategies are most appropriate for specific emotional contexts; ensure proposed strategies prevent negative impact on others.
Wider
  • Recognise and interpret emotional responses to stressful situations and devise strategies that ensure those responses don't negatively impact others, practising strategies in simulated or role-play contexts
  • Design a personal emotional self-management toolkit that includes strategies for both in-person and digital/online contexts, with justification for each strategy's relevance

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierDevising strategies integrates factor analysis, emotional understanding and practical planning — the strategies produced should be visibly connected to the analysis completed in prior nodes, not generated independently.
