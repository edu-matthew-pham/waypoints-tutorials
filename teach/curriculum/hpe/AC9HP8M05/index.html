Community spaces and outdoor settings

AC9HP8M05 · Years 7–8 Health and Physical Education · Movement and physical activity

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Health and Physical Education: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

participate in physical activities that utilise community spaces and outdoor settings, and evaluate strategies to support increased use of these spaces

Prior knowledge

Students have participated in physical activities in natural and outdoor settings and analysed the steps and resources needed to promote participation (Y5-6). They can identify what supports participation but have not yet evaluated management strategies at a community level.

By the end

Students participate in physical activities using community spaces and outdoor settings, and evaluate strategies to support increased use of these spaces.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • evaluating different strategies used to manage and protect environments where physical activities take place, such as bushwalking trails, surf beaches, skate parks and sporting fields
  • investigating different approaches to custodial responsibility for Country/Place used by First Nations Australian communities to support the use of local settings and spaces for physical activity while protecting the environment
  • designing and evaluating physical activity options that reimagine the use of community spaces to encourage more active lifestyles among their peer group
  • promoting an understanding of minimal-impact outdoor recreation in the local area

Waypoints

  • 1. Understanding community and outdoor spaces for physical activityStudents can identify the range of community and outdoor settings used for physical activity in their local area and describe key features that make them suitable or unsuitable for different activity types.
  • 2. Participating in community and outdoor settingsStudents can participate in physical activities in at least one community or outdoor setting, demonstrating safe and appropriate use of the environment.
  • 3. Evaluating strategies for increased useStudents can evaluate strategies designed to support increased or sustainable use of community and outdoor physical activity spaces, weighing their benefits against potential drawbacks.
