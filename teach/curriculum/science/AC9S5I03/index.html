Observing and measuring

AC9S5I03 · Years 5–6 Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use equipment to observe, measure and record data with reasonable precision, using digital tools as appropriate

Prior knowledge

Students can read familiar scaled instruments and record observations systematically (AC9S3I03, 3-4 band). They now extend to selecting equipment for precision, using standard units with prefixes, and recording data electronically.

By the end

Students select equipment appropriate to the precision required, measure and record data using standard units and prefixes, discuss why precision matters for investigation findings, and use digital tools where appropriate.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring which equipment gives the most reasonable precision for the measurements of data required in the investigation
  • exploring the precision of measurements of different equipment such as a cup compared with a measuring jug and discussing why precision is important in measurement
  • recording data using standard units, such as grams, second and metre, and developing the use of standard prefixes for metric units such as kilo- and milli-
  • recording data in tables and diagrams or electronically as digital images and spreadsheets

Waypoints

  • 1. Equipment is selected for the precision the investigation requiresHingeStudents can explain why different equipment gives different levels of precision, select appropriate equipment for a given measurement task, and describe the effect of low precision on investigation findings.
