Culture shapes scientific knowledge

AC9S7H02 · Years 7–8 Science · Human endeavour

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

investigate how cultural perspectives and world views influence the development of scientific knowledge

Prior knowledge

Students can examine why scientific advances depend on collaboration and building on prior work (AC9S5H01). They have not yet investigated how cultural perspectives and world views specifically shape which questions are asked and how knowledge develops.

By the end

Students investigate how cultural perspectives and world views influence the development of scientific knowledge.

Waypoints

  • 1. Scientists bring perspectives to their workStudents can identify that scientists' personal beliefs, cultural backgrounds and societal contexts influence the questions they pursue
  • 2. Cultural knowledge systems contribute to scienceStudents can investigate how knowledge systems from different cultures contribute to scientific understanding
  • 3. Different perspectives on science-society issuesStudents can explain why different people hold different perspectives on science-related issues and why recognising this matters
  • 4. Culture shapes the development of scientific knowledgeStudents can explain how cultural perspectives and world views have influenced the development of scientific knowledge across multiple examples
