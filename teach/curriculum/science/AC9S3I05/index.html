Evaluating

AC9S3I05 · Years 3–4 Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

compare findings with those of others, consider if investigations were fair, identify questions for further investigation and draw conclusions

Prior knowledge

Students can compare observations with predictions with guidance, and identify unexpected results (AC9S1I05, 1-2 band). They have not yet systematically evaluated fairness of investigations, drawn evidence-based conclusions, or posed further questions from findings.

By the end

Students compare their findings with others, evaluate whether their investigation was fair, draw conclusions that are grounded in their data, and identify questions for further investigation arising from their findings or unexpected results.

Waypoints

  • 1. Conclusions are grounded in dataHingeStudents can draw a conclusion from an investigation that is directly grounded in the data collected, and distinguish between a conclusion supported by data and a claim that goes beyond the data.
  • 2. Comparing findings and evaluating fairnessStudents can compare their investigation findings with those of other students or groups, identify differences and suggest reasons for them, and evaluate whether differences are attributable to unfair testing.
  • 3. Identifying questions for further investigationStudents can identify at least one question for further investigation arising from their findings, unexpected results, or limitations of the investigation.
