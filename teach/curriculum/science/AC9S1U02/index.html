Daily and seasonal changes

AC9S1U02 · Year 1 Science · Earth and space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe daily and seasonal changes in the environment and explore how these changes affect everyday life

Prior knowledge

Students can observe and describe features of their local environment (F band). They have not yet described daily and seasonal change patterns or connected those patterns to effects on living things and human decisions.

By the end

Students describe observable daily changes (weather, temperature, day length) and seasonal patterns, and explain how these changes affect plants, animals and human daily life — including clothing, activity planning and habitat use.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • making and recording observations of weather, seasonal plant changes and growth of flowers or fruit
  • noticing how daily and seasonal patterns help us make plans for activities
  • investigating how seasonal changes affect plants and animals including hibernation and migration
  • exploring how people make clothing choices using weather predictions or seasonal knowledge
  • recognising First Nations Australians' extensive knowledges of daily and seasonal changes
  • exploring how First Nations Australians' concepts of time and weather patterns explain the world

Waypoints

  • 1. The environment changes daily and across seasonsHingeStudents can describe observable daily changes (weather, temperature, daylight) and identify seasonal patterns across the year.
  • 2. Daily and seasonal changes affect everyday lifeStudents can explain how daily weather and seasonal changes affect decisions people make and how plants and animals respond to these changes.
