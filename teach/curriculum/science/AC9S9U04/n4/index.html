The wave model of light and electromagnetic radiation

AC9S9U04 n4 · waypoint 4 of 5 · Year 9 Science · Physical

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe light and electromagnetic radiation as waves and explain how the wave model accounts for phenomena including reflection, refraction and the electromagnetic spectrum.

Lessons on this site

  • What is the electromagnetic spectrum?
  • How does light bend?

Success criteria

  • Describes light as a transverse wave and the EM spectrum as a family of waves differing in frequency and wavelength
  • Uses the wave model to explain reflection and refraction
  • Explains how wave frequency relates to energy across the electromagnetic spectrum

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: The particle model of electricity — static charge and current.

Width

Minimum
Students describe light as a transverse wave and use the wave model to explain reflection and refraction.
Typical
Students describe the electromagnetic spectrum as a family of transverse waves varying in frequency and wavelength; explain that all EM waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum; connect wave frequency to energy (radio → microwave → infrared → visible → UV → X-ray → gamma); explain how EM wave properties determine their use in communication technologies; apply the wave model to explain reflection and refraction from AC9S5U03.
Wider
  • Investigate the forms of electromagnetic radiation used in different modern communication technologies — what wave properties make each suitable for its application?
  • Examine how the wave model explains UV damage to skin and X-ray imaging — what wave property is the key variable?

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepApplying the wave model across the full EM spectrum — from radio to gamma — and to real communication and medical technologies builds the model's explanatory range needed for the 'usefulness' evaluation in N5.
