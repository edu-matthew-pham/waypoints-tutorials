Combustion — carbon release from geosphere to atmosphere

AC9S9U03 n4 · waypoint 4 of 5 · Year 9 Science · Earth and space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how combustion of fossil fuels moves carbon from the geosphere to the atmosphere and describe the impact of human activity on this flow.

Lessons on this site

  • What does burning fossil fuels do to the carbon cycle?

Success criteria

  • Explains that combustion of fossil fuels moves carbon from geosphere to atmosphere
  • Describes why this represents a net addition to the atmospheric carbon store (geological timescale of sequestration vs rapid release)
  • Connects increased atmospheric CO₂ to the greenhouse effect and its role in heat retention

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Photosynthesis and respiration — carbon movement through the biosphere.

Width

Minimum
Students explain that combustion of fossil fuels releases carbon stored in the geosphere as CO₂ into the atmosphere.
Typical
Students connect fossil fuel formation to ancient carbon sequestration (geological timescale); explain that combustion rapidly releases carbon that took millions of years to sequester; describe human industrial and transport combustion as the dominant driver; connect to the greenhouse effect elaboration — increased atmospheric CO₂ affects heat retention; calculate or estimate an individual's carbon footprint.
Wider
  • Investigate First Nations Australians' firestick farming as a controlled combustion practice — how does this differ from fossil fuel combustion in terms of carbon cycle impact?
  • Model the carbon cycle perturbation: if combustion adds X tonnes of CO₂ to the atmosphere annually, and photosynthesis + ocean absorption removes Y, what happens to the atmospheric store over time?

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe elaborations name multiple combustion contexts — industrial, transport, firestick farming, carbon capture — each of which illuminates a different aspect of the combustion-cycle interaction. Varied contexts build the system-level understanding required for carbon cycle representation.
