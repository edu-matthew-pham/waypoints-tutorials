Planning and conducting

AC9S3I02 · Years 3–4 Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use provided scaffolds to plan and conduct investigations to answer questions or test predictions, including identifying the elements of fair tests, and considering the safe use of materials and equipment

Prior knowledge

Students can follow instructions to conduct investigations safely and make observations (AC9S1I02, 1-2 band). They have not yet identified the elements of a fair test or used a provided scaffold to plan an investigation, as opposed to following a given procedure.

By the end

Students use provided scaffolds to plan investigations that test a prediction, identify and control the elements of a fair test (variable to change, variable to measure, variables to keep the same), and conduct investigations safely with appropriate materials and equipment.

Waypoints

  • 1. Fair tests control variablesHingeStudents can identify the variable being changed, the variable being measured, and the variables that must be kept the same in a fair test, and explain why controlling variables makes a test fair.
  • 2. Using a scaffold to plan an investigationStudents can use a provided investigation scaffold to plan a fair test that answers a given question, including identifying variables, selecting materials and outlining a procedure.
  • 3. Safe and appropriate use of materials and equipmentStudents can identify safety considerations relevant to a planned investigation and demonstrate safe behaviour when using materials and equipment, including field site considerations.
