Particle theory and properties

AC9S7U05 · Year 7 Science · Chemical

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use particle theory to describe the arrangement of particles in a substance, including the motion of and attraction between particles, and relate this to the properties of the substance

Prior knowledge

Students know that matter exists as solids, liquids and gases and can describe observable properties. They have not used particle theory to explain properties or modelled particle behaviour at a submicroscopic level.

By the end

Students use particle theory to describe the arrangement, motion and attraction of particles in substances and relate this to observable physical properties, including changes of state.

Waypoints

  • 1. Introducing the particle modelHingeStudents can match particle diagrams to states of matter and state the key ideas of particle theory
  • 2. Particle arrangement, motion and attraction in each stateHingeStudents can describe particle arrangement, motion and attraction for each state and explain observable properties using this model
  • 3. Explaining physical properties using particle theoryHingeStudents can use particle theory to explain physical properties of substances including compressibility, viscosity and density
  • 4. Changes of state and energyStudents can explain changes of state using particle theory and correctly identify energy input or output for each change
  • 5. Particle theory as a model: scope and limitsStudents can identify the scope and limitations of the particle model and explain what evidence supports it
