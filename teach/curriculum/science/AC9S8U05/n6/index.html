← AC9S8U05 Energy transfer and transformation

Flow diagrams — representing and analysing energy systems

AC9S8U05 n6 · waypoint 6 of 6 · Year 8 Science · Physical

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can construct and interpret flow diagrams representing energy through a system and use these to identify where by-product outputs occur.

Lessons on this site

  • Follow the energy

Success criteria

  • Constructs a flow diagram for a multi-step system with correctly labelled energy types
  • Includes by-product outputs at each transformation stage
  • Critiques a diagram for completeness — identifies any missing outputs

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 5: Tracing energy through multi-step systems.

Width

Minimum
Students construct a flow diagram for a system with at least two stages, labelling input, useful output and by-product at each stage.
Typical
Students construct flow diagrams using arrows and labelled energy types; represent both transfers and transformations correctly; include by-products at each transformation stage; critique diagrams for completeness — are all outputs accounted for?; explain that flow diagrams are the tool for efficiency analysis in Year 9.
Wider
  • Compare flow diagrams for electricity generation from coal and from solar — what transformation stages differ and where does by-product heat appear in each?
  • Critique a peer's flow diagram for completeness and accuracy — identify missing outputs or incorrectly labelled transformations

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFlow diagram construction requires applying the classification (N1–N2), transfer/transformation distinction (N3–N4) and tracing skill (N5) in a representational form. Diagrams that miss by-product outputs are a systematic error that propagates into AC9S9U05's efficiency calculations. Structured practice with completeness checking is the appropriate response.
