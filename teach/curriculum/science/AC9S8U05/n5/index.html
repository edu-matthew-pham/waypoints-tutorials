Tracing energy through multi-step systems

AC9S8U05 n5 · waypoint 5 of 6 · Year 8 Science · Physical

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can trace energy through a multi-step system, naming the energy type at each stage and identifying all transfers, transformations and by-product outputs.

Lessons on this site

  • Follow the energy

Success criteria

  • Traces energy through a multi-step system naming all types at each stage
  • Identifies all transfers, transformations and by-product outputs in the trace
  • Distinguishes useful energy output from by-product outputs

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 4: Energy transformation — energy changing type.

Width

Minimum
Students trace energy through a two-step system, naming the energy type at each stage and identifying at least one by-product.
Typical
Students trace: roller coaster (gravitational potential → kinetic → gravitational potential → kinetic + heat + sound); Newton's cradle (kinetic → elastic potential → kinetic + heat + sound); battery torch (chemical → electrical → light + heat); First Nations fire-starting (mechanical friction → heat → combustion chemical → light + heat); identify which outputs are useful and which are by-product; explain that tracing all outputs is the prerequisite for understanding efficiency.
Wider
  • Observe a Rube Goldberg machine and construct a complete energy trace — identify every stage, every transfer, every transformation, and every by-product
  • Compare energy traces for two different systems that produce the same useful output (e.g. candle vs electric light) — identify which produces more by-product heat and what this implies

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepEach system context (mechanical, electrical, biological, traditional) exercises the energy tracing skill with a different sequence of types and outputs. Width here builds the generalised tracing capability the descriptor's 'investigate' demand requires.
