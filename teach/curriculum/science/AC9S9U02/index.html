Reproduction and species survival

AC9S9U02 · Year 9 Science · Biological

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe the form and function of reproductive cells and organs in animals and plants, and analyse how the processes of sexual and asexual reproduction enable survival of the species

Prior knowledge

Students can explain how structural features and behaviours are adaptations enabling survival in particular habitats (AC9S5U01) and analyse how physical conditions affect organism survival (AC9S6U01). They can recognise cells as basic units of living things and describe specialised cell structures (AC9S8U01). They have not yet examined reproductive organs and cells as systems with specific forms matched to function, or analysed sexual vs asexual reproduction in terms of variation and species survival trade-offs.

By the end

Students describe the form and function of reproductive cells and organs in animals and plants, and analyse how sexual reproduction generates variation and how asexual reproduction enables rapid colonisation — connecting each strategy to survival in different environmental contexts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • examining how the male and female reproductive organ structures work collectively as a system
  • explaining how the forms of male and female gametes relate to their specific function
  • identifying and comparing sexual and asexual reproductive strategies in plants
  • exploring how sexual reproduction creates a greater rate of variation among offspring compared with asexual reproduction
  • examining how the reproductive strategies of multicellular animals are related to their environment and the complexity of the organism
  • examining how the number of offspring produced by animals is related to the amount of parental care

Waypoints

  • 1. Reproductive cells — form matched to functionHingeStudents can describe the structural features of male and female gametes and explain how each feature relates to the gamete's reproductive function.
  • 2. Reproductive organs — systems supporting gamete production and fertilisationStudents can describe the key structures of male and female reproductive systems in animals and plants and explain how these work collectively to support gamete production, transfer and fertilisation.
  • 3. Sexual reproduction — fertilisation, variation and survivalHingeStudents can explain how sexual reproduction produces variation among offspring and analyse how this variation supports species survival.
  • 4. Asexual reproduction — strategies, speed and survival contextsStudents can describe asexual reproductive strategies in plants and animals and explain when asexual reproduction is advantageous for species survival.
  • 5. Comparing reproductive strategies — analysing survival trade-offsStudents can compare sexual and asexual reproduction and analyse how each strategy enables species survival in different environmental contexts.
