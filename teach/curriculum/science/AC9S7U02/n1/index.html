← AC9S7U02 Food webs and ecosystems

From food chains to food webs

AC9S7U02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 5 · Year 7 Science · Biological · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can construct a food web and identify the roles of producers, consumers and decomposers including microorganisms

Lessons on this site

  • Who eats whom?

Success criteria

  • Correctly places all organisms in a food web into producer, consumer or decomposer roles
  • Arrows show direction of energy flow, not just feeding relationships
  • Includes microorganisms in the decomposer role

Prior knowledge

Students can identify producers, consumers and decomposers and construct simple food chains. They have not studied energy flow quantitatively or modelled multi-factor ecosystem changes.

Width

Minimum
Construct a food web from a given organism list; identify producers, primary and secondary consumers, and decomposers including microorganisms
Typical
Compare a food chain and food web for the same ecosystem — explain what information is lost in the simpler model
Wider
  • Analyse food webs to show feeding relationships including the role of microorganisms in decomposition
  • Critique a published food web diagram — what is it missing, what assumptions does it make?
  • Investigate First Nations Australians' food webs in a specific region and compare with a scientific representation of the same ecosystem

Hinge

Students who cannot construct food webs with correct arrow direction and role assignment cannot represent matter and energy flow in Node 2 — the web structure is the scaffolding all later modelling depends on.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents need to construct food webs across multiple ecosystem types — aquatic, terrestrial, Australian — to build a reliable mental model of web structure. A single ecosystem example may be memorised rather than understood; multiple examples build the structural pattern recognition needed for prediction tasks in later nodes.
