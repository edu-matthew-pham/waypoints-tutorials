← AC9S8U03 Plate tectonics

Divergent boundaries — plates moving apart

AC9S8U03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 6 · Year 8 Science · Earth and space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe the geological features formed where plates diverge and explain the process producing each.

Lessons on this site

  • Quick check: plate boundaries and evidence

Success criteria

  • Explains that diverging plates produce tension and allow magma to rise
  • Describes mid-ocean ridges and seafloor spreading as products of divergent boundaries
  • States that oceanic crust is youngest at ridges and ages with distance

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Tectonic plates and the driving mechanism.

Width

Minimum
Students describe seafloor spreading and mid-ocean ridges as the result of diverging plates.
Typical
Students explain that diverging plates create tension in the crust; magma rises to fill the gap — forming new oceanic crust (seafloor spreading) and mid-ocean ridges; on continents, divergence produces rift valleys; explain that rock at mid-ocean ridges is youngest and age increases with distance from the ridge; describe volcanic activity along ridges.
Wider
  • Investigate Marie Tharp's topographic mapping of the Atlantic Ocean floor — how did her maps reveal the mid-ocean ridge system and what resistance did she face in getting her findings accepted?
  • Model divergent boundary interactions using physical materials — evaluate the model's accuracy and identify what it cannot show

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkEach boundary type is most clearly understood through the plate movement direction → stress type → feature chain. Divergent = tension → crust thins → magma rises → new crust. Making this causal chain explicit for each type builds a transferable reasoning framework rather than a list of boundary features.
