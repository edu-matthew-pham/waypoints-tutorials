Science in daily life

AC9S1H01 · Years 1–2 Science · Human endeavour

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe how people use science in their daily lives, including using patterns to make scientific predictions

Prior knowledge

Students have not yet examined science as a human activity used in daily life or connected the idea of patterns to prediction.

By the end

Students describe examples of how people — including themselves, community members and First Nations Australians — use science knowledge and observed patterns to make decisions and predictions in everyday life.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • learning from farmers, bush care volunteers, gardeners or nursery owners about observing plant needs
  • identifying ways science knowledge is used in care of the local environment
  • investigating how First Nations Australians use science to meet needs including food, water and shelter
  • recognising how First Nations Australians use landscape and sky changes to answer questions about resource gathering
  • learning from ecologists or wildlife carers about observing animal behaviour
  • sharing examples of using science knowledge at home — weather forecasts, seasonal clothing
  • identifying pushes and pulls when preparing meals and tools that help
  • exploring how engineers use force knowledge to create playground equipment or toys

Waypoints

  • 1. People use science knowledge in everyday decisionsHingeStudents can describe at least two examples of how people use science knowledge in their daily lives, explaining what knowledge is being used and why.
  • 2. Patterns help people make scientific predictionsStudents can describe how observing patterns in nature (weather, seasons, animal behaviour) allows people to make predictions that guide decisions.
