Basic needs of plants and animals

AC9S1U01 · Year 1 Science · Biological

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

identify the basic needs of plants and animals, including air, water, food or shelter, and describe how the places they live meet those needs

Prior knowledge

Students can observe and describe features of living things and their environments (AC9SFU01, F band). They have not yet systematically identified needs of living things or described how habitat features meet those needs.

By the end

Students identify the basic needs of plants and animals (air, water, food, shelter) and describe how features of the places they live meet those needs — connecting habitat features to specific survival requirements.

Waypoints

  • 1. Living things have basic needsHingeStudents can name the basic needs of plants and animals — air, water, food and shelter — and explain that all living things must have their needs met to survive.
  • 2. The places living things live meet their needsStudents can describe how features of a named habitat meet the basic needs of a plant or animal that lives there.
