Scientific explanations meet needs

AC9S3H02 · Years 3–4 Science · Human endeavour

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

consider how people use scientific explanations to meet a need or solve a problem

Prior knowledge

Students can describe how science knowledge has changed peoples' lives (AC9S3H02, 3-4 band). They have not yet examined the specific link between a scientific explanation and the design of a solution — the mechanism by which understanding produces application.

By the end

Students examine how scientific explanations about natural phenomena are applied by people to meet needs or solve problems, and identify the explanation-to-application pathway in real-world examples including First Nations knowledge applications.

Waypoints

  • 1. Scientific explanations can be applied to solve problemsHingeStudents can distinguish between a scientific explanation (an account of how or why something happens) and an application (a solution that uses that explanation to meet a need), and identify examples of each.
  • 2. Examining real applications of science knowledgeStudents can examine real-world cases where scientific knowledge was applied to meet a need, identifying the specific knowledge used and the problem or need it addressed.
  • 3. Science knowledge influences decisions and practicesStudents can explain how scientific knowledge has influenced how people make decisions or change their practices — not only in designed solutions but in everyday choices and policy.
