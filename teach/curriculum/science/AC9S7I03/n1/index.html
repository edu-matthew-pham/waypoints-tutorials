Selecting appropriate equipment

AC9S7I03 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 7–8 Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can select equipment appropriate to an investigation's measurement needs and justify the choice

Success criteria

  • Selects equipment appropriate to the measurement task
  • Justifies the choice based on measurement range or precision
  • Does not select equipment that cannot measure the expected values

Prior knowledge

Students can follow safe procedures to investigate questions and use basic measurement tools (AC9S5I02). They have not yet selected equipment based on measurement range or precision requirements, or used digital tools to improve data quality.

Width

Minimum
Select appropriate equipment for a given investigation; explain why the selected equipment suits the measurement task
Typical
Select and use equipment appropriate to the investigation's measurement range; explain why a thermometer, measuring cylinder or force meter was chosen based on the expected measurement range
Wider
  • Select equipment for an investigation where the expected measurement range is not given — estimate the range first, then choose
  • Compare two pieces of equipment for the same measurement task and justify which is more appropriate
  • Design a data collection system for a multi-variable investigation, justifying equipment selection

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents need to accumulate a repertoire of equipment types and their appropriate contexts — thermometers for temperature ranges, measuring cylinders for volumes, force meters for force ranges, digital sensors for abiotic factors — before they can make justified selection decisions in novel contexts. More equipment examples in varied investigation contexts build the selection vocabulary.
