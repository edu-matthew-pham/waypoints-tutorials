← AC9S2U01 Earth in the solar system

Celestial objects show observable patterns

AC9S2U01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 2 Science · Earth and space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe patterns in the changing position of the sun across a day and the changing appearance of the moon across a month, and use these patterns to make predictions.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Describes the pattern of the sun's movement across a day (direction and shadow change)
  • Describes the pattern of moon shape change across a month and makes one prediction based on this pattern

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Earth is a planet in the solar system.

Width

Minimum
Students describe the pattern of the sun's position across a day and describe at least two phases of the moon, using these to make one prediction.
Typical
Students observe and record how shadow length changes with the changing position of the sun; create a moon diary across a month identifying the pattern of changing moon shape; observe and describe short-term and longer-term sky patterns; and make predictions based on identified patterns.
Wider
  • Observe the changing appearance of the moon across a month and make predictions about future phases — connecting pattern observation to scientific prediction
  • View time-lapse video of sun, moon, stars or satellite movement across the sky — using digital tools to observe patterns invisible at normal timescales
  • Explore how First Nations Peoples use patterns in the night sky for navigation and timekeeping — examining practical applications of astronomical pattern knowledge

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents see sky events as random rather than patterned. Making the pattern explicit — sun position changes predictably across each day; moon shape changes predictably across each month — and using those patterns to make and check predictions is the core reasoning scaffold at this year level.
