Earth in the solar system

AC9S2U01 · Year 2 Science · Earth and space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise Earth is a planet in the solar system and identify patterns in the changing position of the sun, moon, planets and stars in the sky

Prior knowledge

Students can describe daily and seasonal changes in the environment (AC9S1U02). They have not yet placed Earth within the solar system or examined patterns in the changing position of celestial objects as a systematic phenomena.

By the end

Students recognise Earth as a planet in the solar system, identify observable patterns in the changing position of the sun, moon and stars across days and months, and make predictions based on those patterns.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying celestial objects visible in space: sun, moon, stars, planets
  • viewing images or video of Earth from space and describing Earth's shape
  • exploring representations of the solar system and identifying Earth and other planets
  • observing day and night sky phenomena
  • investigating how shadow length changes with changing sun position
  • creating a class moon diary across a month identifying patterns and making predictions
  • observing short-term and longer-term sky patterns
  • distinguishing regular and irregular sky events
  • exploring First Nations cultural stories about patterns in the sun, moon and stars

Waypoints

  • 1. Earth is a planet in the solar systemHingeStudents can describe Earth as a planet in the solar system, name other planets and the sun, and describe the shape of Earth using images from space.
  • 2. Celestial objects show observable patternsStudents can describe patterns in the changing position of the sun across a day and the changing appearance of the moon across a month, and use these patterns to make predictions.
