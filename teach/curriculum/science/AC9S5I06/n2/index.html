← AC9S5I06 Communicating

Science texts serve specific purposes with appropriate conventions

AC9S5I06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 5–6 Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can write and create science texts in at least two different forms (scientific report, persuasive text, digital presentation) with appropriate conventions for each form, tailored to a specific purpose and audience.

Success criteria

  • Produces a science text in a given form using appropriate conventions
  • Selects vocabulary appropriate to the purpose and audience
  • Justifies at least one language choice in terms of how it serves the communication purpose

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Language features are selected deliberately to shape meaning.

Width

Minimum
Students produce a science text in a given form using appropriate conventions for that form and with vocabulary suited to the purpose and audience.
Typical
Students produce science texts across multiple forms — a co-authored scientific report with standard conventions (prediction, method, results, conclusion, discussion of errors), a persuasive text for a local authority, and a digital presentation for information sharing — selecting and justifying language choices for each.
Wider
  • Construct a persuasive text for local council arguing for an erosion mitigation strategy — applying scientific knowledge to a genuine civic communication purpose
  • Co-author a scientific report on a light investigation using standard scientific report conventions and appropriate vocabulary, data representations and sentence structures
  • Develop a digital presentation on structural features of animals in a specific habitat — selecting language and visual representations appropriate for the intended audience

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents at this band must produce texts across genuinely different forms — each with different conventions and purposes. Encountering report, persuasive and digital presentation forms in different science contexts builds flexible communication capability rather than a single-genre template.
