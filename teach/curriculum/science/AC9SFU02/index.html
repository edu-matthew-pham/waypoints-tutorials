How objects move

AC9SFU02 · Foundation Science · Physical

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe how objects move and how factors including their size, shape or material influence their movement

Prior knowledge

No formal prior science learning assumed. Students bring everyday experience of moving objects.

By the end

Students describe different ways objects move, observe how size, shape and material affect movement, and make comparisons between objects based on how they move.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • observing how toys move and grouping by movement
  • observing and describing how unusually shaped objects move when rolled down a slope
  • comparing different-sized similar-shaped objects rolling and bouncing
  • exploring how material affects ball movement
  • exploring how size and shape of First Nations instructive toys influence movement

Waypoints

  • 1. Objects move in different waysHingeStudents can observe and describe different types of object movement (roll, slide, bounce, spin, swing) and group toys or objects by their movement type.
  • 2. Size, shape and material affect how objects moveStudents can compare how different-sized, shaped or material objects move and describe at least one factor that affects movement.
