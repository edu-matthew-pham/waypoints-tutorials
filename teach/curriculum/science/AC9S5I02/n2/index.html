← AC9S5I02 Planning and conducting

Variables, risks and permissions are identified in planning

AC9S5I02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 5–6 Science · Inquiry · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can independently decide which variables to change, measure and control in a fair test, describe potential risks, plan safe equipment use, and identify any permissions required for field investigations.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Independently identifies variables to change, measure and control without a provided scaffold
  • Describes at least one potential risk and the planned safety measure to address it

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Repeatable investigations require a recorded, reproducible method.

Width

Minimum
Students independently identify the variable to change, variable to measure and at least two variables to control for a given investigation question, and describe one potential risk.
Typical
Students independently plan fair tests across Y5-6 investigation contexts (light, erosion, survival features); decide all variables without scaffolding; describe multiple potential risks and planned safety measures; identify any permissions required for field investigations on Country/Place.
Wider
  • Evaluate an investigation plan for completeness — identifying any uncontrolled variables, unaddressed risks or missing permissions, and explaining the consequence of each gap
  • Plan for and navigate the practical challenge of conducting field erosion observations — identifying what permissions, safety equipment and observation methods are required
  • Consult with First Nations Australians to guide investigation planning, including safety considerations and culturally appropriate approaches to field sites

Hinge

Independent variable identification is the prerequisite for all subsequent fair test work at Y5-6 — students who cannot decide variables without scaffolding cannot meaningfully engage with I03 precision measurement or I05 error analysis, both of which assume a validly planned investigation.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents at this band must transfer the fair test planning skill to investigation types beyond the classroom bench test — field observations, simulations, research-based investigations. Encountering planning across these varied investigation types builds the flexible, context-independent planning capability the descriptor requires.
