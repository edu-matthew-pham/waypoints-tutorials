← AC9SFI04 Comparing observations with predictions

Predictions are compared with observations

AC9SFI04 n1 · waypoint 1 of 1 · Foundation Science · Inquiry

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can revisit their prediction after an observation and say whether it matched, with teacher guidance.

None yet.

Success criteria

  • States whether their prediction matched their observation
  • Describes at least one way the observation was the same as or different from the prediction

Prior knowledge

No formal prior science learning assumed.

Width

Minimum
Students compare one prediction with one observation and state whether they matched.
Typical
Students revisit predictions and with guidance identify whether they matched observations; use a provided table to draw or dictate their prediction and observation; compare field work observations with pre-field predictions; and describe what was the same and what was different.
Wider
  • Use a provided table to draw or dictate prediction and observation side by side — comparing explicitly
  • Revisit predictions about plant or animal field work observations and compare with what was actually found
  • Explore what to do when a prediction does not match — 'what can we do to find out more?'
