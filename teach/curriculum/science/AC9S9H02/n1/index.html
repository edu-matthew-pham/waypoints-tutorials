Technology enabling science

AC9S9H02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 Science · Human endeavour

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Science: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe cases where advances in technology enabled advances in scientific understanding — explaining what the technology made possible that was not possible before.

Success criteria

  • Can describe at least one example where technology enabled a scientific advance.
  • Can explain what the technology specifically made possible — not just that 'it helped'.
  • Does not treat the relationship as simply 'scientists use tools' — understands that the tool opened a previously inaccessible domain.

Prior knowledge

Students can explain how scientific understanding has developed through people's questions, observations and experiments (AC9S7H03, AC9S7H04). They have not yet examined the two-way relationship between science and technology specifically — where technology enables scientific discovery and scientific understanding enables technological development — as a systematic relationship rather than individual historical examples.

Width

Minimum
Describe one example where a technology enabled a scientific advance (e.g. development of imaging technologies enabling understanding of body system interactions; electron microscope enabling understanding of cell ultrastructure) and explain what the technology specifically made possible.
Typical
Describe two or three cases of technology enabling science across different fields, explaining in each case what question or observation became possible with the new technology — connecting the tool to the knowledge it produced.
Wider
  • Examine how modern technology has enabled exploration of energy conversion processes at scales previously inaccessible — from black holes to atoms to sub-atomic particles — as cases where the limits of scientific knowledge coincide with the limits of available technology.
  • Investigate how technological advances in monitoring greenhouse gas emissions and environmental factors have contributed to the reinstatement of traditional fire management practices — connecting remote sensing technology to both scientific knowledge and First Nations knowledge validation.
