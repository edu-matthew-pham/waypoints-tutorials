Purpose drives organisation

AC9E2LA03 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 2 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify the purpose of a text and connect that purpose to at least one organisational feature.

Success criteria

  • Student can name the purpose of a given text and identify at least one organisational feature that serves that purpose.
  • Student does not claim that all texts of the same type are organised identically — they can acknowledge variation.

Prior knowledge

Students explore how texts are organised differently according to their purpose (AC9E1LA03). They have not yet identified specific structural and language features associated with distinct text types across curriculum areas, or recognised that the same purpose can be expressed in different forms.

Width

Minimum
Identify the purpose of a familiar text type (e.g. narrative, procedure) and name one organisational feature that serves that purpose.
Typical
Identify and compare the purposes of several text types and connect each purpose to its typical organisational features across narrative, recount, information report and procedure.
Wider
  • Compare two texts with the same purpose but different organisations (e.g. a procedure as a list vs as a video) and discuss how the form affects the audience
  • Explore informative texts from Science or HASS and identify how their purpose shapes their organisation

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents often think organisational features are arbitrary rules rather than purposeful choices. Seeing the same purpose realised in different text types, and different purposes in similar formats, resolves the misconception that structure is fixed. Multiple text type examples across curriculum areas are the productive move.
