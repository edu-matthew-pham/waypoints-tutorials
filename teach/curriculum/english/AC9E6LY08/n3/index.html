← AC9E6LY08 Phonic knowledge for complex words

Phonic strategies in independent reading

AC9E6LY08 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 6 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can independently apply phonic knowledge to decode unfamiliar complex words encountered during reading across curriculum areas.

Success criteria

  • Decodes at least one unfamiliar complex word encountered during reading using a phonic strategy
  • Names the phonic strategy applied

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Less common grapheme-phoneme relationships.

Width

Minimum
Encounter and decode at least two unfamiliar complex words in a curriculum-area text using phonic strategies; explain the strategy used for one.
Typical
Independently apply phonic knowledge (including less common grapheme–phoneme relationships) to read unfamiliar technical and scientific vocabulary encountered in curriculum-area reading; use phonic decoding as one strategy alongside context and morphemic knowledge.
Wider
  • Collect unfamiliar words from curriculum-area reading and analyse the grapheme–phoneme patterns in each, building a personal phonic reference
  • Teach a classmate the phonic strategy for decoding a set of complex words from curriculum reading

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierIndependent phonic decoding in real reading contexts integrates common pattern knowledge (Node 1) and uncommon pattern knowledge (Node 2) alongside contextual and morphemic cues — the coordination of multiple word-solving strategies is the integration challenge.
