Symbols in still and moving images

AC9E9LA07 · Year 9 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse how symbols in still and moving images augment meaning

Prior knowledge

Students analyse how vectors, angle and social distance in visual texts create perspective (AC9E7LA07). They have not yet examined how symbols within images augment meaning through cultural or conventional associations that extend beyond what is literally depicted.

By the end

Students analyse how symbols in still and moving images augment meaning, including how symbolic meanings differ across groups and cultures.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating the use of symbols; for example, specific seasons, weather and colours in images, films and picture books, and evaluating their contribution to viewers' understanding, recognising that visual and verbal symbols have different meanings for different groups and cultures
  • understanding the use of symbols by First Nations Australians, where a symbol may have many meanings or have different meanings across First Nations Australian groups; for example, artwork enables First Nations Australians from a particular Country/Place to identify symbols and interpret the artwork

Waypoints

  • 1. Symbols as meaning beyond the literalHingeStudents can identify visual symbols in images and explain the meanings they carry beyond their literal depiction.
  • 2. Symbols augmenting meaning across image typesStudents can analyse how symbols augment meaning differently in still versus moving images, explaining how context and sequence affect symbolic interpretation.
