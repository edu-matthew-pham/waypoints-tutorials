Spelling Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander language words

AC9E8LY08 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 8 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify and use appropriate sources (e.g. the AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia, local First Nations community resources) to access the correct spelling of Aboriginal language and Torres Strait Islander language words.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one appropriate source for the spelling of Aboriginal language or Torres Strait Islander language words.
  • Uses that source to spell at least two words correctly in own writing.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Applying morphemic knowledge to complex words.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one appropriate source for the spelling of Aboriginal language or Torres Strait Islander language words and demonstrate correct spelling of at least two words accessed from that source.
Typical
Apply knowledge of appropriate sources — including the AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia and local First Nations Australian community guidance — to access and use correct spellings of Aboriginal language and Torres Strait Islander language words in own writing.
Wider
  • Investigate how spelling conventions for Aboriginal language words differ from English spelling conventions and what this requires in terms of resource use and cultural respect
  • Explore how local First Nations community members are the authoritative source for the correct spelling and pronunciation of their community's language words — and what this means for respectful practice
  • Examine how the AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia records language names as a resource for identifying and respecting Country/Place naming conventions
