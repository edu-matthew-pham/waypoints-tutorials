Text connectives and cohesion

AC9E4LA04 · Year 4 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

identify how text connectives including temporal and conditional words, and topic word associations are used to sequence and connect ideas

Prior knowledge

Students understand how paragraphs group related information within stages of written texts (AC9E3LA04). They have not yet identified text connectives as the explicit devices that sequence and connect ideas across sentences and sections.

By the end

Students can identify temporal, conditional and additive text connectives, explain their role in sequencing and connecting ideas, and recognise how pronouns and determiners maintain cohesion across a text.

Waypoints

  • 1. Pronouns and determiners as cohesive linksStudents can identify how pronouns and determiners link back to previously mentioned information in a text.
  • 2. Connectives between sentencesStudents can identify text connectives that create logical links between sentences and explain the relationship each signals.
  • 3. Temporal connectives and sequencingStudents can identify temporal connectives and explain how they sequence events or steps through time.
