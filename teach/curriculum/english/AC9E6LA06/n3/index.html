Adverb groups for precision

AC9E6LA06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 6 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how adverb groups expand and sharpen ideas by adding information about manner, place, time or degree, and can use them to improve the precision of their own writing.

Lessons on this site

  • Verbs that do the work

Success criteria

  • Identifies and classifies adverb groups by type (manner, place, time, degree) in provided text
  • Uses at least one adverb group appropriately in own writing to add precision

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Elaborated tenses.

Width

Minimum
Identify adverb groups in at least two provided sentences and explain what type of information each adds (manner, place, time, degree); use one adverb group to improve a provided sentence.
Typical
Analyse how adverb groups work alongside precise verb and tense choices to sharpen ideas across a range of text types; use adverb groups in writing to add precision about when, where, how and to what extent actions occur.
Wider
  • Edit a piece of own writing to evaluate whether each adverb group adds necessary precision or is redundant given a precise verb choice
  • Compare adverb group use in procedure texts vs. narratives and explain how the type and placement of adverb groups differs by purpose

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates verb precision (Node 1) and tense understanding (Node 2) with adverb group selection — the authoring challenge is coordinating all three to produce precise, varied writing. The components come together in the editing act.
