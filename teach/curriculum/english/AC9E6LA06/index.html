Verbs, tense and adverb groups for precision

AC9E6LA06 · Year 6 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

understand how ideas can be expanded and sharpened through careful choice of verbs, elaborated tenses and a range of adverb groups

Prior knowledge

Students understand how noun groups can be expanded in a variety of ways to provide a fuller description (AC9E5LA06). They have not yet examined the precision available through verb selection, tense elaboration, or adverb groups as tools for sharpening ideas.

By the end

Students understand how careful verb choice, elaborated tenses and adverb groups expand and sharpen ideas in texts, and can apply these choices in their own writing.

Waypoints

  • 1. Precise verb selectionHingeStudents can explain how selecting a precise verb sharpens meaning and reduces the need for additional words.
  • 2. Elaborated tensesStudents can explain the different ways English refers to time using simple present, simple past, and future time expressions, and select the appropriate form for a given purpose.
  • 3. Adverb groups for precisionStudents can explain how adverb groups expand and sharpen ideas by adding information about manner, place, time or degree, and can use them to improve the precision of their own writing.
