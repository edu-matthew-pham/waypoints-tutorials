Plurals and suffix effects

AC9E5LY10 · Year 5 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Explore less common plurals, and understand how a suffix changes the meaning or grammatical form of a word

Prior knowledge

Students can use and apply phonological and morphological knowledge to read and spell complex letter combinations (AC9E4LY09). They have not yet examined irregular plurals derived from word origins, or examined how suffixes systematically change the grammatical class of words.

By the end

Students read, spell and use less common plurals derived from word origins, and explain how specific suffixes change the grammatical form or meaning of words — including how '-tion' and '-ment' nominalise verbs.

Waypoints

  • 1. Less common plurals and word originsStudents can spell and explain less common plurals whose forms are derived from their word origins (Greek, Latin, English), explaining why these plurals differ from the regular -s pattern.
  • 2. Suffixes change grammatical formStudents can explain how specific suffixes change the grammatical form of a word and use this knowledge to build and recognise different word classes.
