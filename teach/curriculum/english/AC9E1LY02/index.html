Turn-taking and active listening

AC9E1LY02 · Year 1 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use interaction skills including turn-taking, speaking clearly, using active listening behaviours and responding to the contributions of others, and contributing ideas and questions

Prior knowledge

Students can interact in informal and structured situations by listening while others speak and using some features of spoken language (AC9EFLY02). They have not yet applied the full set of interaction skills systematically — turn-taking, active listening, responding to others' contributions, and contributing ideas and questions as a coordinated set.

By the end

Students use interaction skills including turn-taking, active listening, responding to others' contributions, and contributing ideas and questions in discussions.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using turn-taking, speaking clearly, using active listening behaviours
  • responding to the contributions of others
  • contributing ideas and questions

Waypoints

  • 1. Turn-taking and clear speakingStudents can take turns to speak in structured discussions and speak clearly enough to be understood by the group.
  • 2. Active listening behavioursStudents can demonstrate active listening — attending to the speaker, making eye contact, showing they are following the contribution.
  • 3. Responding to and building on contributionsStudents can respond to what others have said — acknowledging, agreeing, questioning or extending a previous contribution.
