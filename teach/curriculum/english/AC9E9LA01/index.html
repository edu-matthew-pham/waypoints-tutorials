Language empowers relationships and roles

AC9E9LA01 · Year 9 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise how language empowers relationships and roles

Prior knowledge

Students understand how language expresses and creates personal and social identities (AC9E7LA01). They have not yet examined how language functions as a mechanism of power within communities — shaping membership, authority and social roles.

By the end

Students recognise how specific language choices empower or constrain relationships and define roles within communities.

Waypoints

  • 1. Community membership through languageStudents can identify language features that signal membership in specific communities, such as family registers, teen slang, technical jargon and classroom interaction patterns.
  • 2. Language and power in rolesHingeStudents can recognise how language choices define and reinforce roles — such as authority, expertise or solidarity — within and across communities.
  • 3. Language empowerment as a mechanismStudents can recognise how language choices function as mechanisms of empowerment — not just markers of identity — actively shaping relationships and roles rather than simply reflecting them.
