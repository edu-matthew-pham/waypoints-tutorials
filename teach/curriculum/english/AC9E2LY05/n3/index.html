← AC9E2LY05 Build and monitor meaning

Monitoring, summarising and questioning

AC9E2LY05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 2 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can monitor their comprehension, identify key information for summarising, and generate questions during and after reading.

Success criteria

  • Student can identify the main idea of a text.
  • Student generates at least one question during or after reading that goes beyond what was explicitly stated.
  • Student identifies when their comprehension has broken down and takes a monitoring action.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Making inferences.

Width

Minimum
Identify the main idea of a text and generate at least one question about something they want to know more about.
Typical
Apply monitoring, summarising and questioning strategies across informative and persuasive texts — identifying main idea, tracking comprehension, generating questions before, during and after reading.
Wider
  • Use a graphic organiser to represent connections between characters, order of events or sequence of information after reading
  • Compare summaries made by different students of the same text and discuss what was included or omitted and why

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node requires students to deploy multiple comprehension strategies simultaneously — monitoring while reading, identifying key ideas for summarising, generating questions throughout. Coordinating these as a set of active reading behaviours is the integration demand.
