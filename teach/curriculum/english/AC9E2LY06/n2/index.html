Constructing sentences with noun and verb groups

AC9E2LY06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 2 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use simple and compound sentences with extended noun groups and verb groups correctly and flexibly in their writing.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student produces at least one compound sentence with two independent clauses correctly joined.
  • Student uses at least one extended noun group (noun + article + adjective) in their writing.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Selecting structure for purpose.

Width

Minimum
Write at least one sentence with an extended noun group and one compound sentence in a short piece of writing.
Typical
Use simple and compound sentences flexibly across a short text, including extended noun groups and verb groups where appropriate to express ideas precisely.
Wider
  • Review a draft by identifying places where noun groups could be extended to add precision or where compound sentences could be used to show relationships between ideas
  • Compare a draft with simple sentences only to a revised version with compound sentences and noun groups, discussing the improvement

Hinge

Errors in compound sentence construction and noun group extension produced at this node carry into Node 3 — the editing node requires a draft with these features present to review; without them there is nothing to improve and the editing task collapses to surface punctuation only.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsCorrect and flexible use of compound sentences and extended noun groups in produced text requires repeated practice with self-checking. Students who understand the concepts in isolation still produce errors in extended writing. Practice in writing and reviewing is the productive investment.
