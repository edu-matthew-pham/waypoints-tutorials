← AC9E9LE05 Complex literary devices and intertextuality

Intertextual references

AC9E9LE05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 9 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify intertextual references in literary texts and explain how they augment meaning by invoking associations from another text or tradition.

Success criteria

  • Student identifies an intertextual reference and names the source text or tradition being invoked
  • Student explains the specific meaning-effect that the intertextual reference creates in this text

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Symbolism and metonymy.

Width

Minimum
Identify an intertextual reference in a literary text and explain what meaning it imports from the referenced text or tradition.
Typical
Analyse how intertextual references — literary, cultural, mythological or historical — contribute to meaning in literary texts, explaining both what is imported and what effect the invocation has on the reader's interpretation.
Wider
  • Examine how intertextual references create a two-tier reading experience — accessible to readers who recognise the reference, different for those who don't — and evaluate the implications for audiences
  • Analyse how a text subverts or ironises an intertextual reference, creating meaning through the gap between original and adaptation

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyRecognising intertextual references requires a repertoire of cultural, literary and mythological knowledge. Students with wider reading can identify more references — this is a strand where the width of prior reading directly enables the analytical work.
Integration: a combining task at the wider tierIntertextuality integrates all the complex device analysis — extended metaphor, allegory, symbol, metonymy can all be deployed intertextually. The final node coordinates the range of complex devices as a system of meaning-making tools that operate across texts.
