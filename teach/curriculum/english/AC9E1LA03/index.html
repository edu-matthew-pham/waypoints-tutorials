Texts organised by purpose

AC9E1LA03 · Year 1 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore how texts are organised according to their purpose, such as to recount, narrate, express opinion, inform, report and explain

Prior knowledge

Students can understand that texts can take many forms and be used for different purposes (AC9EFLA03). They have not yet explored how organisation (stages and structure) differs systematically across text types such as recount, narrative, and information text.

By the end

Students explore how texts are organised differently according to purpose, identifying the typical stages of familiar text types including recount, narrative, opinion, information, report and explanation.

Waypoints

  • 1. Texts have a purposeStudents can identify the purpose of a familiar text (e.g. to tell a story, to give information, to give instructions).
  • 2. Text stages differ by typeHingeStudents can identify the typical stages of at least two text types and explain how those stages serve the text's purpose.
  • 3. Words and images both organise textsStudents can recognise that text organisation includes both words and visual/graphical elements, and that these work together to serve the text's purpose.
