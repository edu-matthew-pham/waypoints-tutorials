Personal response and subsequent analysis

AC9E9LE02 · Year 9 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

present a personal response to a literary text comparing initial impressions and subsequent analysis of the whole text

Prior knowledge

Students form and justify opinions about texts, identifying agreement and difference with others (AC9E7LE02). They have not yet compared their initial impressions with a developed whole-text analysis — the dialectic between first response and considered interpretation.

By the end

Students present a personal response to a literary text that compares initial impressions with subsequent analysis of the whole text.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • interrogating and making judgements about a text, comparing others' ideas against the student's own and reaching an independent decision or consensus about the interpretations and ideas expressed

Waypoints

  • 1. Initial response as legitimate readingHingeStudents can articulate an initial response to a literary text and recognise that first impressions are a valid starting point for analysis.
  • 2. Whole-text analysisStudents can develop an analysis of a complete text that interrogates and makes judgements about its ideas and interpretations.
  • 3. Comparing initial and developed responseStudents can present a personal response that explicitly compares their initial impressions with their subsequent whole-text analysis, explaining what changed and why.
