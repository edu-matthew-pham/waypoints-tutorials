Beyond assertions to evidence

AC9E5LA02 · Year 5 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Understand how to move beyond making bare assertions by taking account of differing ideas or opinions and authoritative sources

Prior knowledge

Students can identify subjective language of opinion and feeling, and objective language of factual reporting (AC9E4LA02). They have not yet examined the rhetorical mechanisms for tempering assertions — distancing language, sourcing, generalising support — or how these choices affect a claim's persuasive weight.

By the end

Students explain how bare assertions can be strengthened by using distancing language, recruiting support, or citing authoritative sources, and identify how these moves shift the persuasive weight and reliability of a claim.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • recognising that a bare assertion often needs to be tempered by: using the impersonal 'it' to distance oneself; recruiting anonymous support; indicating a general source of the opinion; specifying the source of the opinion; and reflecting on the effect of these different choices

Waypoints

  • 1. What makes a bare assertionHingeStudents can identify a bare assertion and explain why it is insufficient as persuasive or informative language in contexts that require evidence or qualification.
  • 2. Tempering moves: distancing and recruiting supportHingeStudents can identify and explain how distancing language (impersonal it, modal verbs) and recruited support (anonymous or general sourcing) change the persuasive character of an assertion.
  • 3. Citing authoritative sourcesStudents can explain how specifying an authoritative source strengthens a claim, and identify the difference in persuasive weight between general and specific sourcing.
