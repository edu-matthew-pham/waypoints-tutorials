Plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts

AC9E6LY06 · Year 6 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts whose purposes may be imaginative, informative and persuasive, using paragraphs, a variety of complex sentences, expanded verb groups, tense, topic-specific and vivid vocabulary, punctuation, spelling and visual features

Prior knowledge

Students can plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts across imaginative, informative and persuasive purposes, using paragraphs, complex sentences, expanded noun and verb groups, tense, vocabulary choices, punctuation and spelling (AC9E5LY06). They have not yet specifically deployed embedded clauses, elaborated tenses, adverb groups, or explicitly rhetorical devices in their writing.

By the end

Students plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts for imaginative, informative and persuasive purposes, using paragraphs, complex sentences (including embedded clauses), expanded verb groups, tense, topic-specific and vivid vocabulary, appropriate punctuation and spelling, and visual features.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating informative texts for 2 different audiences that explore a topic using text structure and language features for the identified audiences, noting the changes required to appeal to each audience
  • using rhetorical devices, images and modal verbs for persuasive effects, for an identified audience
  • selecting and combining digital tools to create texts
  • re-reading and editing their own and others' work using negotiated criteria for text structure, meaning and accuracy of grammar, spelling and punctuation, and explaining editing choices as required

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning for purpose and audienceStudents can plan a written or multimodal text that identifies the purpose, audience, text structure and key language choices to be made.
  • 2. Creating with varied complex sentences and precise vocabularyStudents can create written texts that deploy a variety of complex sentences (including embedded clauses), expanded verb groups, tense choices, and topic-specific and vivid vocabulary.
  • 3. Editing for structure, meaning and accuracyStudents can re-read and edit their own and others' work against negotiated criteria for text structure, meaning, grammar, spelling and punctuation, and explain editing choices.
