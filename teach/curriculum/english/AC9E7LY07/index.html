Deliver purposeful presentations

AC9E7LY07 · Year 7 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Plan, create, rehearse and deliver presentations for purposes and audiences in ways that may be imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive and/or analytical, by selecting text structures, language features, literary devices and visual features, and using features of voice including volume, tone, pitch and pace

Prior knowledge

Students can plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations that develop a theme or idea using precise vocabulary, pitch, tone, pace, volume and visual and digital features (AC9E6LY07). They have not yet worked at the level of selecting text structures, language features and literary devices deliberately for presentation purposes, or combining print, visual and audio elements to interpret ideas.

By the end

Students plan, create and deliver presentations selecting structures, devices and voice features for purpose and audience.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • preparing a presentation combining print, visual and audio elements to explore and interpret ideas, drawing on knowledge and research about ideas and opinions different from students' own
  • selecting features of voice, such as tone, volume, pitch and pace, for different audiences and purposes, recognising the effects these have on audience understanding and engagement
  • monitoring ideas developed at each stage of creating a presentation in a blog or journal
  • sharing feedback with a peer while planning, creating and rehearsing a presentation

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning a presentation for purpose and audienceStudents can plan a presentation by making deliberate choices about structure, content and mode based on purpose and audience
  • 2. Voice features for audience engagementStudents can select and use voice features including volume, tone, pitch and pace to serve the purpose and engage the audience
