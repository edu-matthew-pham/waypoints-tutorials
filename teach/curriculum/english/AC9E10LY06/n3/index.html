← AC9E10LY06 Plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts

Editing and publishing with reflection

AC9E10LY06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can review, edit and refine texts for accuracy and purpose, and reflect on choices made and how they might develop further.

Success criteria

  • Makes at least two specific editing revisions with a stated reason
  • Reflects on at least one choice made in the text and how it might be developed further

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Creating texts with developed ideas.

Width

Minimum
Edit a text making at least two specific revisions to grammar, spelling, vocabulary or structure, and reflect on at least one choice made.
Typical
Review and edit a written or multimodal text for accuracy of grammar, spelling and punctuation and to achieve specific purposes and address particular audiences, reflecting on how choices may be changed or developed in future texts.
Wider
  • Conduct a structured self-editing process using an editing checklist that covers grammar, vocabulary precision, structural effectiveness and audience impact
  • Share a draft with a peer and use their response to guide a revision round, reflecting on how reader feedback informed editing decisions
  • Publish a final text to a real audience (school publication, competition) and reflect on how the publishing context shaped final editing decisions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierEditing integrates the planning and creation phases — students must coordinate their original intentions with what the text actually achieves; this phase collapse risk is real if students treat editing as merely correcting errors rather than purposeful revision.
