← AC9E10LY06 Plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts

Creating texts with developed ideas

AC9E10LY06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 10 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create written and multimodal texts that develop and expand ideas through deliberate use of text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Creates a text that develops ideas through deliberate feature choices
  • Demonstrates experimentation with at least one text structure, language feature or multimodal element

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Planning for purpose and audience.

Width

Minimum
Create a text that develops ideas through at least two deliberate feature choices (structural, language or multimodal), with evidence of experimentation.
Typical
Create a written or multimodal text that organises, expands and develops ideas through deliberate experimentation with text structures, language features, literary devices and/or multimodal features for a specific purpose and audience.
Wider
  • Create a written text that compels readers to empathise with ideas and emotions expressed or implied, using deliberate literary and structural choices
  • Design a webpage that combines navigation, text, sound, and moving and still images for a specific audience, making deliberate structural choices about how the modes work together
  • Create a multimodal text that addresses a complex real-world issue for a specific audience, experimenting with how different modes contribute to an analytical or critical purpose

Hinge

Node 3 (editing and publishing) requires students to have a created text to review, revise and reflect on — without a substantive creation, editing is reduced to surface error-correction rather than purposeful revision.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents who only create in one mode or for one purpose develop a narrow repertoire — experimentation across written and multimodal contexts builds transferable understanding of how features serve different purposes.
