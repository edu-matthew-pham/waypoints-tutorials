Plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts

AC9E10LY06 · Year 10 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts, organising, expanding and developing ideas through experimenting with text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features for specific purposes and audiences in ways that may be imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive, analytical and/or critical

Prior knowledge

Students plan, create and publish texts developing ideas critically, selecting features for complex purposes and audiences (AC9E9LY06). They have not yet planned, created, edited and published written and multimodal texts that experiment with text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features across the full range of text purposes.

By the end

Students plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts, organising, expanding and developing ideas through experimenting with text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features for specific purposes and audiences.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • presenting a structured argument by providing a statement of the major arguments or concerns relating to an issue, previewing the structure of arguments, structuring the text to provide a major point for each paragraph with succinct elaboration, and concluding with a summary of the main issues or recommendations in an argument
  • creating written and multimodal texts that compel readers to empathise with the ideas and emotions expressed or implied
  • designing a webpage that combines navigation, text, sound, and moving and still images for a specific audience
  • reviewing, editing and refining their own and others’ texts for accuracy of grammar, spelling and punctuation, and to achieve particular purposes and address specific audiences through control and organisation of content, sentence structures, vocabulary choices and visual features
  • reflecting on the effect of choices made in a written or multimodal text and how these choices may be changed or developed in future texts

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning for purpose and audienceStudents can plan a written or multimodal text by identifying purpose, audience and key ideas, and selecting appropriate text structures and features for those choices.
  • 2. Creating texts with developed ideasHingeStudents can create written and multimodal texts that develop and expand ideas through deliberate use of text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features.
  • 3. Editing and publishing with reflectionStudents can review, edit and refine texts for accuracy and purpose, and reflect on choices made and how they might develop further.
