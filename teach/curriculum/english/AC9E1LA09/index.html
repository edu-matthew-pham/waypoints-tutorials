Learning area vocabulary

AC9E1LA09 · Year 1 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise the vocabulary of learning area topics

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise and develop awareness of vocabulary used in familiar contexts related to personal experiences (AC9EFLA08). They have not yet systematically recognised topic-specific vocabulary tied to learning area disciplines, or understood the cultural and contextual dimension of vocabulary such as Acknowledgement of Country.

By the end

Students recognise vocabulary associated with learning area topics, including topic-specific terms used in classroom discussions and protocols such as Acknowledgement of Country.

Waypoints

  • 1. Topics have their own wordsStudents can identify that different learning areas use their own topic-specific vocabulary and can name examples from a familiar topic.
  • 2. Using topic vocabulary in contextStudents can use appropriate topic-specific vocabulary when discussing a learning area topic.
  • 3. Cultural and community vocabularyStudents can use appropriate vocabulary in cultural and community protocols, including Acknowledgement of Country, and understand these as a specific vocabulary context.
