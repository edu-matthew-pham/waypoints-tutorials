Images, figures, tables, diagrams, maps and graphs

AC9E6LA07 · Year 6 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

identify and explain how images, figures, tables, diagrams, maps and graphs contribute to meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can explain how the sequence of images in print, digital and film texts has an effect on meaning (AC9E5LA07). They have not yet examined how a range of non-narrative visual representations (tables, diagrams, maps, graphs, flowcharts) contribute to meaning in informational and procedural texts.

By the end

Students can identify and explain how a range of visual representations — images, figures, tables, diagrams, maps and graphs — contribute to meaning in different text types.

Waypoints

  • 1. Sequential visual representationsStudents can identify how sequential visual representations (timelines, flowcharts, procedure diagrams, life-cycle diagrams) represent order and process.
  • 2. Conceptual visual representationsStudents can explain how tables, maps, graphs and diagrams represent concepts, information and relationships visually.
  • 3. Cultural sensitivity in visual textsStudents understand that images and maps may hold cultural significance or sensitivity for First Nations Australians, and can apply appropriate judgement when using or sharing such materials.
