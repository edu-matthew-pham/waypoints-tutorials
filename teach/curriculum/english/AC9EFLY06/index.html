Create short written texts

AC9EFLY06 · Foundation English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create and participate in shared editing of short written texts to record and report ideas and events using some learnt vocabulary, basic sentence boundary punctuation and spelling some consonant–vowel–consonant words correctly

Prior knowledge

Students have beginning letter-sound and word knowledge (AC9EFLY09–15). They have not yet been asked to compose and edit connected written text — integrating spelling, vocabulary, punctuation and ideas into a coherent short text.

By the end

Students can create short written texts to record or report ideas and events — using learnt vocabulary, CVC spelling and basic sentence punctuation — and can participate in shared editing to improve a text.

Waypoints

  • 1. Writing communicates an ideaStudents can produce a short text (drawing and/or beginning writing) that communicates one idea or event.
  • 2. CVC spelling, vocabulary and punctuationHingeStudents can apply CVC spelling, use some learnt vocabulary, and include a capital letter at the sentence start and a full stop at the end.
  • 3. Reading back and shared editingStudents can 'read' their own text back and participate in shared editing — identifying at least one thing to change or correct.
