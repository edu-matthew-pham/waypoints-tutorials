Acknowledge and extend in discussion

AC9E4LY02 · Year 4 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

listen for key points and information to carry out tasks and contribute to discussions, acknowledging another opinion, linking a response to the topic, and sharing and extending ideas and information

Prior knowledge

Students use interaction skills to contribute to conversations and discussions, sharing information and ideas (AC9E3LY02). They have not yet practised acknowledging another's opinion and linking responses to the topic while extending ideas.

By the end

Students can listen for key points in a discussion, acknowledge another's opinion, link their response to the topic, and share ideas that extend or build on what others have contributed.

Waypoints

  • 1. Listening for key pointsHingeStudents can identify key points and information in what others say in a discussion and use these in their own contribution.
  • 2. Acknowledging and extendingStudents can use language that acknowledges another's opinion and links their own response to the topic while extending or building on what was said.
