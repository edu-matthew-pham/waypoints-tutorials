Building on others' ideas

AC9E3LY02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 3 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can build on and connect to ideas and opinions expressed by others in a discussion.

Success criteria

  • Student makes a contribution that acknowledges and builds on a previous speaker's point
  • Student demonstrates active listening by responding to specific content from earlier in the discussion

Prior knowledge

Students use interaction skills to actively listen, extend ideas, express and respond to opinions and give instructions (AC9E2LY02). They have not yet focused on the specific skills of contributing as a group member across different roles — building on others, listening actively for specific information, and adjusting language to context.

Width

Minimum
In a discussion, make at least one contribution that explicitly connects to or builds on a previous speaker's idea.
Typical
Build on and connect ideas expressed by others; listen actively including for specific information; recognise the value of others' contributions; use language appropriately in different group contexts.
Wider
  • Practice specific group roles (chairperson, recorder, reporter) and discuss how each role requires different interaction skills
  • Compare how the language used in a formal group discussion differs from casual conversation, and practise adjusting to each context

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsBuilding on others is a turn-taking skill that requires real-time listening under social pressure — it develops through repeated practice in genuine discussion contexts, not through more conceptual instruction.
