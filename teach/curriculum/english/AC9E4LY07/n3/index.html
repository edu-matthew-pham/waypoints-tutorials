← AC9E4LY07 Structured oral and multimodal presentations

Delivering the presentation

AC9E4LY07 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 4 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can deliver a structured presentation using appropriate language, complex sentences, visual features and deliberate voice control for the purpose and audience.

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Presentation has a clear structure with introduction and conclusion
  • At least one complex sentence is used in delivery
  • At least one deliberate voice feature is evident — pace, volume, pitch or tone varied for effect

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Rehearsing — voice features and feedback.

Width

Minimum
Deliver a structured presentation on a topic using at least one complex sentence and at least one deliberate voice feature (e.g. slowing pace for emphasis).
Typical
Deliver presentations across a range of purposes — report, argument, recount — with appropriate language register, complex sentences, integrated visual features and deliberate voice control (tone, pace, pitch, volume) suited to purpose and audience.
Wider
  • Deliver the same content in two different presentation contexts (e.g. formal classroom vs informal small group) and reflect on how delivery changes across contexts
  • Deliver a presentation incorporating First Nations Australian content with attention to respectful language choices and appropriate acknowledgement conventions
