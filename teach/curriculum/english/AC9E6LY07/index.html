Plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations

AC9E6LY07 · Year 6 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations that include information, arguments and details that develop a theme or idea, organising ideas using precise topic-specific and technical vocabulary, pitch, tone, pace, volume, and visual and digital features

Prior knowledge

Students can plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations that include relevant information, facts, arguments and details using appropriate vocabulary, pace, volume and visual features (AC9E5LY07). They have not yet specifically developed a sustained theme or idea across a presentation, deployed pitch and tone deliberately, or used technical vocabulary for persuasive effect.

By the end

Students plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations that develop a theme or idea with information, arguments and details, using precise vocabulary, voice delivery features, and visual and digital elements.

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning and organising ideasStudents can plan a spoken or multimodal presentation that organises information, arguments and details to develop a theme or idea, selecting appropriate vocabulary and structural choices.
  • 2. Voice delivery for effectStudents can deliberately use pitch, tone, pace and volume in spoken delivery and explain the effect on audience understanding.
  • 3. Delivering a complete presentationStudents can deliver a spoken and/or multimodal presentation that develops a theme or idea using precise vocabulary, deliberate voice features, and effective visual and digital elements.
