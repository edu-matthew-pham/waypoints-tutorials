Planning and organising ideas

AC9E6LY07 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 6 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can plan a spoken or multimodal presentation that organises information, arguments and details to develop a theme or idea, selecting appropriate vocabulary and structural choices.

Lessons on this site

  • Plan a talk that has a point
  • From script to cue cards

Success criteria

  • Produces a plan that identifies a theme or idea and organises at least three supporting points logically
  • Identifies at least two key vocabulary choices appropriate to the topic and audience

Prior knowledge

Students can plan, create, rehearse and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations that include relevant information, facts, arguments and details using appropriate vocabulary, pace, volume and visual features (AC9E5LY07). They have not yet specifically developed a sustained theme or idea across a presentation, deployed pitch and tone deliberately, or used technical vocabulary for persuasive effect.

Width

Minimum
Produce a plan for a spoken or multimodal presentation that identifies the theme or idea, organises at least three supporting points logically, and notes key vocabulary choices.
Typical
Plan a report or argument that sequences ideas logically and provides supporting detail; select precise topic-specific and technical vocabulary for key concepts; plan how graphics, sound and visuals will enhance audience engagement and understanding.
Wider
  • Plan an argument on a curriculum content issue, identifying claims, evidence and persuasive techniques appropriate to the audience
  • Plan and set goals for a presentation in a written or digital journal, and monitor progress against those goals
