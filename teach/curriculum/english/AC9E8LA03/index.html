Text structure, purpose and hybrid genres

AC9E8LA03 · Year 8 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explain how texts are structured depending on their purpose and how language features vary, recognising that some texts are hybrids that combine different genres or elements of different genres

Prior knowledge

Students can understand how different text types are structured to achieve different purposes and identify how language features vary across text types (AC9E7LA03). They have not yet examined how texts combine multiple genre structures or analysed hybrid texts that deliberately blend conventions.

By the end

Students explain how text structures serve specific purposes and how language features vary accordingly, including recognising and explaining hybrid texts that combine elements from different genres.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • recognising how texts such as critical responses to texts, expositions, text interpretations and discussions are typically structured to achieve their purpose
  • discussing how the placement of images and written text in a linear or non-linear way, such as online texts, is used differently in a variety of texts for a purpose
  • explaining the structure and language features of texts such as narratives, literary recounts, memoirs, drama scripts, types of poems, formal speeches, comparisons and creative responses, discussions and debates

Waypoints

  • 1. Structure and language features serve purposeHingeStudents can explain how the structural choices and language features of a specific text type are designed to achieve its communicative purpose.
  • 2. Hybrid genresStudents can recognise and explain how hybrid texts combine elements from different genres, identifying what purposes or effects this combination achieves.
