Hybrid genres

AC9E8LA03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 8 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can recognise and explain how hybrid texts combine elements from different genres, identifying what purposes or effects this combination achieves.

Success criteria

  • Identifies specific elements from two different genres present in one hybrid text.
  • Explains how the combination of genre elements serves a purpose or creates an effect that neither genre alone would achieve.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Structure and language features serve purpose.

Width

Minimum
Identify that one provided text combines elements from two different genres and explain what effect this combination creates for a reader.
Typical
Recognise and explain hybrid texts that deliberately combine genre elements — such as a narrative that embeds exposition, or an opinion piece that uses memoir structure — explaining how the combination serves the text's overall purpose.
Wider
  • Analyse how a specific hybrid text (e.g. creative nonfiction, a personal essay, a docudrama) exploits the conventions of multiple genres to position the reader in complex ways
  • Investigate how digital and multimodal texts (e.g. interactive journalism, video essays) create new hybrid forms by combining text, image, audio and structural elements from different genres
  • Create a short hybrid text that deliberately combines elements from two genres and annotate the structural and language choices made

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe concept of 'hybrid genre' is non-obvious — students expect texts to be cleanly categorisable. Encountering a range of hybrid examples (memoir-as-argument, narrative nonfiction, docudrama) dissolves the expectation of clean genre categories and builds the flexible reading lens the descriptor requires.
