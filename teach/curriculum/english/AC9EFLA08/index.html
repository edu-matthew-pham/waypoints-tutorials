Vocabulary in familiar contexts

AC9EFLA08 · Foundation English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise and develop awareness of vocabulary used in familiar contexts related to everyday experiences, personal interests and topics taught at school

Prior knowledge

Students bring oral vocabulary from home and community contexts. They have not yet been asked to consciously attend to vocabulary — to notice, collect and develop awareness of words in relation to specific topics and contexts.

By the end

Students can recognise and use vocabulary relevant to everyday experiences, personal interests and school topics — drawing on multiple sources to expand their word knowledge and applying vocabulary in both imaginative and informative contexts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying learning area–specific words when learning new topics or engaging in imaginative play; for example, role-playing a shopkeeper and a customer
  • identifying words for a topic studied at school; for example, words relevant to the topic of families
  • expanding vocabulary through informal interactions and planned experiences with adults and peers, texts, images, and artefacts or objects

Waypoints

  • 1. Topics have wordsStudents can identify that topics and contexts have specific vocabulary — particular words that go with particular subjects or activities.
  • 2. Vocabulary grows through experienceStudents can identify multiple ways of encountering and learning new vocabulary — through interactions, texts, images, objects and play.
