Language features vary with audience and purpose

AC9E7LY03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 7 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse how language feature choices vary when the audience or purpose of a text changes

Lessons on this site

  • Features that shape meaning

Success criteria

  • Identifies a language feature that varies between two texts with different audiences or purposes
  • Explains why the feature changes with the audience or purpose

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Language features shape meaning.

Width

Minimum
Compare two texts on the same topic with different audiences or purposes; identify at least one language feature that differs and explain why it changes with the audience or purpose
Typical
Analyse how language features vary systematically across texts with different audiences and purposes — how formality, vocabulary, modality and sentence complexity shift — and explain the relationship between feature choices and context
Wider
  • Examine how the same event or subject is represented differently in texts for different audiences — e.g. a news article vs a social media post vs a children's explanation — and trace how language feature choices differ and why
  • Analyse how language features in texts about First Nations Australians vary depending on who the text is for and what its purpose is — and what this reveals about the relationship between language, audience and power

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe audience-purpose-language relationship only becomes visible through comparison. Students need to see language feature variation across genuinely different audience-purpose combinations — not just formal vs informal — to understand the principle rather than observing a single instance. Breadth across varied contexts builds the analytical pattern.
