Historical and cultural contexts

AC9E5LE01 · Year 5 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Identify aspects of literary texts that represent details or information about historical, social and cultural contexts in literature by First Nations Australian, and wide-ranging Australian and world authors

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise similar storylines, ideas and relationships in different contexts in literary texts (AC9E4LE01). They have not yet examined how specific literary details — images, symbols, dialogue, character descriptions — represent historical, social and cultural contexts, or explored texts by First Nations Australian authors in relation to the events that shaped their lives.

By the end

Students identify and explain how specific literary details — including images, symbols, dialogue and character descriptions — represent historical, social and cultural contexts in texts by First Nations Australian, Australian and world authors.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying aspects of literature; for example, images, symbols, dialogue and character descriptions, that can convey information about a context
  • exploring and discussing texts written by First Nations Australian authors about the events that shaped or had an impact on their lives
  • exploring aspects of literature that represent historical context in texts by First Nations Australian authors
  • exploring characters and concepts in ballads from different times by wide-ranging Australian authors

Waypoints

  • 1. Literary details carry contextHingeStudents can identify specific literary details — images, symbols, dialogue, character descriptions — and explain what historical, social or cultural information those details convey.
  • 2. First Nations Australian literary contextsStudents can identify aspects of First Nations Australian literary texts that convey information about historical context, cultural practice, or the impact of events on community life.
  • 3. Ballads and historical context across Australian literatureStudents can identify aspects of Australian literary texts — including ballads from different times — that convey historical and social context, and discuss how those contexts are represented through literary choices.
