Segment phonemes in words

AC9E1LY09 · Year 1 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

segment words into separate phonemes (sounds) including consonant blends or clusters at the beginnings and ends of words (phonological awareness)

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise and generate rhyming words, identify syllables and segment single-syllable CVC words into onset and rime at Foundation level (AC9EFLY15). They have not yet segmented words fully into individual phonemes, particularly those containing consonant blends or clusters.

By the end

Students segment words into all separate phonemes including consonant blends and clusters at word beginnings and ends.

Waypoints

  • 1. Phoneme segmentation in simple wordsHingeStudents can segment single-syllable words into all individual phonemes in sequence.
  • 2. Segmenting words with initial consonant blendsHingeStudents can segment words that begin with consonant blends or clusters (e.g. sp-, st-, bl-, cr-) into their individual phonemes.
  • 3. Segmenting words with final consonant blendsHingeStudents can segment words with final consonant blends or clusters into their individual phonemes.
