← AC9E1LY09 Segment phonemes in words

Segmenting words with final consonant blends

AC9E1LY09 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 1 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can segment words with final consonant blends or clusters into their individual phonemes.

Success criteria

  • Student correctly segments a word with a final consonant blend
  • Student does not merge the final blend consonants into a single sound

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Segmenting words with initial consonant blends.

Width

Minimum
Segment at least two words with final consonant blends (e.g. 'fist', 'lamp') into all phonemes.
Typical
Segment words with final consonant blends — including common patterns such as -st, -nd, -mp, -nk — into individual phonemes.
Wider
  • Segment words with both initial and final consonant blends (e.g. 'stamp', 'blend')
  • Discuss how segmenting words with final blends helps with spelling

Hinge

Complete phoneme segmentation — including final blends — is prerequisite for AC9E1LY10 phoneme manipulation; students cannot delete, substitute or add phonemes within blend positions they have not learned to isolate.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFinal consonant blends are harder to segment than initial blends for most students — the end of the word receives less articulatory attention. Repeated practice with final blends before moving to both-ends blending is important.
