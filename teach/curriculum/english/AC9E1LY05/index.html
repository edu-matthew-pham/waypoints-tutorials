Build literal and inferred meaning

AC9E1LY05 · Year 1 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use comprehension strategies such as visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising and questioning when listening, viewing and reading to build literal and inferred meaning by drawing on vocabulary and growing knowledge of context and text structures

Prior knowledge

Students can use comprehension strategies such as visualising, predicting, connecting and summarising at Foundation level (AC9EFLY05). They have not yet explicitly applied inferencing, questioning as a deliberate strategy, or drawn on vocabulary and text structure knowledge to build beyond literal meaning.

By the end

Students use comprehension strategies — visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising, questioning — to build literal and inferred meaning when listening, viewing and reading.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying information and details from spoken informative texts
  • building topic knowledge and learning new vocabulary before and during reading
  • making predictions from the cover, from illustrations and at points in the text
  • drawing inferences and explaining inferences using clues from the text
  • making connections with existing knowledge and personal experiences

Waypoints

  • 1. Literal meaning: what the text saysStudents can identify and retell information stated explicitly in a text — what the text directly says.
  • 2. Predicting and connectingStudents can make predictions before and during reading and connect text content to their own knowledge or experiences.
  • 3. Inferring meaningHingeStudents can draw inferences — identify meaning that is implied but not directly stated — using clues from the text.
  • 4. Visualising, questioning and summarisingStudents can use visualising, questioning and summarising strategies to build and consolidate meaning.
