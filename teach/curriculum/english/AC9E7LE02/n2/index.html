← AC9E7LE02 Form and justify opinions

Engaging with others' opinions

AC9E7LE02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 7 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify areas of agreement and difference between their own and others' opinions about a text and respond to those differences

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one point of agreement or difference with another's opinion
  • Justifies their own position in response to the difference, with reference to the text

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Forming and justifying a personal response.

Width

Minimum
Given another person's opinion about a character, setting or event, identify whether you agree or disagree and explain why — reference both your own response and what the text says
Typical
Participate in a discussion of a literary text where multiple opinions are present; identify specific points of agreement and difference, justify your own position, and respond to at least one point of difference by engaging with the other person's reasoning
Wider
  • Establish forums for discussing the relative merits of characters, settings and events — practise structured literary discussion where opinions are presented, challenged and refined
  • Compare opinions on a text where characters exhibit complex behaviours (integrity, loyalty, moral ambiguity) — discuss how different readers can reach different justified conclusions from the same text

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepEngaging with interpretive difference requires genuine interpretive diversity — students need to encounter opinions that differ from their own in non-trivial ways to develop the habit of identifying and responding to difference rather than dismissing it. A range of text types and discussion contexts provides the varied perspectives that make this learning possible.
