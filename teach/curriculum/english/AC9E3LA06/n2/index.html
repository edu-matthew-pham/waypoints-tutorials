← AC9E3LA06 Clauses with subject-verb agreement

Subject-verb agreement

AC9E3LA06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 3 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify when a singular subject requires a singular verb form and a plural subject requires a plural verb form.

Success criteria

  • Student identifies whether a subject is singular or plural
  • Student selects or confirms the correct verb form to match the subject

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Identifying clause elements.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two examples of subject-verb agreement (one singular, one plural) and explain why the verb form matches the subject.
Typical
Understand and apply subject-verb agreement: singular subjects take singular verbs, plural subjects take plural verbs — recognise and correct agreement errors.
Wider
  • Identify tricky agreement cases (e.g. collective nouns, compound subjects) and discuss why they are harder to get right
  • Find subject-verb agreement errors in student-level writing samples and explain the correction

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsSubject-verb agreement errors are common in student writing and persist without targeted practice. This is a precision skill that requires repeated application across varied sentence types, not just comprehension of the rule.
