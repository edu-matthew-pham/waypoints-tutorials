Images and words make meaning

AC9EFLA07 · Foundation English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore the contribution of images and words to meaning in stories and informative texts

Prior knowledge

Students have been read picture books and interacted with illustrated texts. They have not yet been explicitly asked to separate and compare what words contribute versus what images contribute to meaning.

By the end

Students can describe what images add to a text beyond the words, and what words add beyond the images — recognising that words and images work together in different ways across different texts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Images carry meaningHingeStudents can identify that images in a text communicate meaning — that looking at images alone tells us something.
  • 2. Words and images work differentlyStudents can identify that words and images each contribute different things to a text's meaning — what each mode does distinctively.
