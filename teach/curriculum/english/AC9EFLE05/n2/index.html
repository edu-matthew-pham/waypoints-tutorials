← AC9EFLE05 Retell and adapt familiar texts

Role-play brings characters to life

AC9EFLE05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Foundation English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can take on a character role from a familiar text through play or performance, enacting key events.

Success criteria

  • Student can enact at least one scene from a story in role.
  • Student maintains the character's identity and the event's logic during play or performance.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Retelling maintains sequence.

Width

Minimum
Role-play or act out at least one scene from a familiar story, taking on a character role.
Typical
Retell familiar literary texts through play and performance; draw and role-play characters or events; maintain character identity and event logic during dramatic play.
Wider
  • Perform a familiar story as a class drama, allocating roles and rehearsing key events
  • Role-play the same story with different students in each role across multiple performances — discussing how different portrayals affect the story
  • Dramatise scenes from First Nations Australian literary texts, attending to cultural context and meaning
