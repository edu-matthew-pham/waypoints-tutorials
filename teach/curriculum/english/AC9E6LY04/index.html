Select, navigate and read texts for purpose

AC9E6LY04 · Year 6 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select, navigate and read texts for a range of purposes, monitoring meaning and evaluating the use of structural features; for example, table of contents, glossary, chapters, headings and subheadings

Prior knowledge

Students can navigate and read texts for specific purposes, monitoring meaning using strategies such as adjusting reading rate and re-reading (AC9E5LY04). They have not yet systematically evaluated structural features (table of contents, glossary, headings) for their effectiveness in serving navigation and comprehension purposes, or applied research skills to locate and evaluate multiple texts.

By the end

Students can select, navigate and read texts for a range of purposes, monitoring meaning and evaluating how structural features serve those purposes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • bringing subject and technical vocabulary and concept knowledge to new reading tasks, selecting, evaluating and using texts for their pertinence to the task and the accuracy of their information
  • using knowledge of text structure to navigate the text to locate specific literal content for a purpose
  • using research skills including locating texts, gathering and organising information, evaluating and using information

Waypoints

  • 1. Using structural features to navigateStudents can use structural features of texts (table of contents, glossary, chapters, headings, subheadings) to locate specific content for a purpose.
  • 2. Evaluating structural featuresHingeStudents can evaluate the use of structural features in a text — assessing how well they serve navigation, comprehension and the reader's purpose.
  • 3. Selecting and evaluating texts for researchStudents can select, evaluate and use texts for their relevance to a task and the accuracy of their information, applying research skills including locating, gathering and organising information.
