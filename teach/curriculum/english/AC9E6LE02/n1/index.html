← AC9E6LE02 Comparing literary texts on similar topics

Identifying shared and different features

AC9E6LE02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 6 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify similarities and differences in the use of structural and language features between two texts on a similar topic, theme or plot.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one similarity and one difference in the structural features of two compared texts
  • Identifies at least one similarity and one difference in the language or visual features of two compared texts

Prior knowledge

Students can present an opinion on a literary text using specific terms about literary devices, text structure and language features (AC9E5LE02). They have not yet systematically compared texts on similar topics or themes, examining how narrator, narrative structure, voice, and language choices differ and influence readers.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two similarities and two differences between two provided texts on a similar topic or theme, using at least one structural feature and one language feature.
Typical
Compare at least two texts on a similar topic or theme across a range of features — topic, theme, narrative structure, characterisation, language choices, visual features — and organise comparisons systematically.
Wider
  • Compare two fantasy quest novels or two realistic novels on a specific theme from different authors or cultural contexts
  • Compare texts on the same theme with very different styles — for example, a picture book and a chapter book on the same theme — to identify what stylistic differences do to the reader experience
