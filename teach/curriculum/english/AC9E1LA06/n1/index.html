← AC9E1LA06 Simple sentences

A sentence expresses one event or idea

AC9E1LA06 n1 · waypoint 1 of 4 · Year 1 English · Language · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify that a sentence expresses one complete event or idea and recognise when a sentence is complete.

Success criteria

  • Student correctly identifies at least three complete sentences in a mixed set
  • Student explains why a given fragment is not a complete sentence

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise that sentences are key units for expressing ideas (AC9EFLA05). They have not yet understood the internal structure of a simple sentence — that it consists of a single independent clause with a process (verb), participants (noun groups) and optional circumstances (adverbs).

Width

Minimum
Identify whether a given group of words is a complete sentence (expresses a complete event or idea) or is incomplete.
Typical
Identify complete sentences and explain what makes them complete — they express a whole event or idea. Distinguish complete sentences from fragments and from sequences of multiple events.
Wider
  • Sort a set of word groups into complete sentences and fragments, explaining the decision for each
  • Find examples of single-event sentences in a familiar picture book and discuss what each one expresses

Hinge

Students who cannot reliably identify a complete sentence cannot identify the participant and process structure in N2 — the completeness concept is definitionally prior to analysis of what a complete sentence contains.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsRecognising a complete sentence requires repeated exposure to both examples and non-examples. Students need practice judging completeness across varied sentence types before moving to internal structure.
