Commas and apostrophes

AC9E5LA09 · Year 5 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Use commas to indicate prepositional phrases, and apostrophes where there is multiple possession

Prior knowledge

Students can understand that punctuation signals dialogue through quotation marks and that different types of punctuation including commas signal meaning to a reader (AC9E4LA12). They have not yet applied commas to prepositional phrases or apostrophes in multiple-possession contexts.

By the end

Students use commas to signal prepositional phrases at the beginning of sentences, and use apostrophes correctly for plural possession including regular plural nouns, irregular plural nouns, and proper nouns.

Waypoints

  • 1. Commas with fronted prepositional phrasesStudents can identify a prepositional phrase at the start of a sentence and use a comma to signal its boundary from the main clause.
  • 2. Apostrophes for plural possessionHingeStudents can apply apostrophes correctly for plural possession, including regular plural nouns ending in -s, and explain the difference between singular and plural possession apostrophe placement.
  • 3. Multiple possession and joint ownershipStudents can apply the apostrophe rule for multiple ownership (apostrophe on the last owner) and explain why the rule is placed as it is.
