← AC9E6LA05 Embedded clauses in complex sentences

Using embedded clauses to elaborate ideas

AC9E6LA05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 6 English · Language · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can construct complex sentences using embedded clauses to elaborate, extend and explain ideas, and explain why they have used an embedded clause in preference to other constructions.

Lessons on this site

  • A clause inside a clause

Success criteria

  • Constructs a grammatically accurate sentence with an embedded clause
  • Identifies the embedded clause and names the noun group it expands

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: What an embedded clause is.

Width

Minimum
Construct at least one sentence using an embedded clause to expand a noun group (e.g. 'Hamish studied the rock samples that he had collected on the excursion'); identify the embedded clause and what noun group it expands.
Typical
Create and use complex sentences with embedded clauses in writing, selecting conjunctions that enable the clauses to elaborate, extend or explain; revise sentences to evaluate whether an embedded clause improves precision and variety.
Wider
  • Experiment with stacking multiple clauses in a sentence and evaluate where embedded clauses create clarity vs. awkwardness
  • Edit a piece of own writing to introduce embedded clauses where they add precision, and reflect on the effect

Hinge

Students who cannot produce accurate embedded clauses cannot deploy them in extended writing (Node 3 and AC9E6LY06) — the production skill must be established before the integration task is attempted.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsConstructing embedded clauses accurately requires mechanical precision — students need practice producing them in varied sentences before they can deploy them fluently in extended writing. Fluency here enables transfer to AC9E6LY06.
