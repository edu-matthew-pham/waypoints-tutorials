Semicolons and dashes

AC9E8LA09 · Year 8 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

understand and use punctuation conventions including semicolons and dashes to extend ideas and support meaning

Prior knowledge

Students can understand and use a range of punctuation conventions including colons and brackets (AC9E7LA09). They have not yet examined semicolons and dashes specifically — marks used to extend ideas within sentences and to create effects such as interruption, aside and pause in both formal and creative writing.

By the end

Students understand and use semicolons and dashes to extend ideas and support meaning in their own writing.

Waypoints

  • 1. Semicolons and dashes — function and effectHingeStudents can identify semicolons and dashes in texts and explain the function each performs (linking closely related independent clauses; marking interruption, aside or pause).
  • 2. Using semicolons and dashes in own writingStudents can use semicolons and dashes accurately and purposefully in their own writing to extend ideas and create intended effects.
