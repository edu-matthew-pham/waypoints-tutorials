Editing and publishing for purpose

AC9E7LY06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 7 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can edit a draft text by making purposeful revision decisions, and publish in a form appropriate to the audience and purpose

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one revision decision in editing a draft and explains how it better serves the purpose
  • Selects a publication form appropriate to the audience and purpose

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Creating for purpose.

Width

Minimum
Given a draft text, identify at least one revision decision — something changed, added or removed — and explain why the change better serves the purpose; identify an appropriate form or platform for publishing
Typical
Edit a written or multimodal text by making deliberate revision decisions — removing unnecessary repetition, reordering for logic or effect, varying sentence structure, refining language choices — and explain how each decision improves the text; select a publication form appropriate to the audience and purpose
Wider
  • Edit for meaning using digital tools (tracked changes, collaborative editing) to make the revision decision-making process visible and discussable
  • Compile a portfolio of texts for a shared concept or audience, editing across the collection for consistency of structural and language choices

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents commonly treat editing as error-correction rather than purposeful revision. The reasoning scaffold — what was changed, why was it changed, how does the change better serve the purpose — shifts editing from a mechanical check to a meaning-making process and builds the metalinguistic awareness that transfers across all writing contexts.
