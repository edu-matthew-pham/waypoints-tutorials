Texts reflect time and place

AC9E5LY01 · Year 5 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Describe the ways in which a text reflects the time and place in which it was created

Prior knowledge

Students can compare texts from different times with similar purposes and audiences to identify similarities and differences (AC9E4LY01). They have not yet examined how vocabulary, idiomatic expressions, and social expectations within texts reveal the time and place of their creation.

By the end

Students describe how vocabulary choices, idiomatic expressions, social expectations, and cultural values in a text reflect the time and place of its creation.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • describing how ideas in texts are conveyed by vocabulary, including idiomatic expressions, and that these can change according to time and place
  • describing how ideas in texts reflect the social expectations of the time and place in which they were created

Waypoints

  • 1. Vocabulary marks time and placeStudents can identify vocabulary in a text that signals when and where it was created, and explain how vocabulary choices reflect the language use of a particular time and place.
  • 2. Social expectations in textsHingeStudents can identify how the social expectations of the time and place of a text's creation are reflected in its content, characters, or events.
  • 3. Describing how texts reflect their contextStudents can bring together vocabulary and social expectation observations to provide a coherent description of how a text reflects the time and place of its creation.
