Language to justify preferences

AC9E1LA02 · Year 1 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

explore language to provide reasons for likes, dislikes and preferences

Prior knowledge

Students can explore different ways of using language to express preferences, likes and dislikes (AC9EFLA02). They have not yet used connective language such as 'because' to provide reasons, or comparative adjectives to express degrees of preference.

By the end

Students explore language that provides reasons for likes, dislikes and preferences, including connective and comparative language.

Waypoints

  • 1. Stating a preferenceStudents can express a like, dislike or preference using appropriate language (e.g. 'I like...', 'I prefer...', 'My favourite is...').
  • 2. Giving reasons with 'because'HingeStudents can use 'because' and similar connectives to link a preference to a reason.
  • 3. Comparative language for degreeStudents can use comparative adjectives to express the degree of a preference (e.g. 'better', 'faster', 'more exciting').
