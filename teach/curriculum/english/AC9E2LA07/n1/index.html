Extending a noun into a noun group

AC9E2LA07 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 2 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify that articles and adjectives extend a bare noun into a noun group that provides more specific information.

Success criteria

  • Student can identify the head noun and at least one extending element (article or adjective) in a noun group.
  • Student can build an extended noun group from a bare noun by adding at least an article and one adjective.

Prior knowledge

Students understand the major word classes — nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs (AC9E1LA07). They have not yet examined how these elements combine into extended noun groups and verb groups that carry richer meaning.

Width

Minimum
Identify the noun, article and at least one adjective in a given noun group (e.g. 'the longest side').
Typical
Identify noun groups in sentences from a range of texts, naming the head noun and the articles and adjectives that extend it; build extended noun groups from bare nouns by asking 'how many?', 'what type?', 'what's it like?'
Wider
  • Compare noun groups across different text types — sparse noun groups in procedures vs rich noun groups in narratives — and discuss why each text type uses them differently
  • Build noun groups from bare nouns in cross-curriculum texts (e.g. 'the ancient Egyptian artefact') and discuss how each element adds information

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents often treat adjectives as decoration rather than as group-builders. Seeing noun groups across different text types — where some texts deliberately minimise them and others maximise them — helps students understand that noun group length is a purposeful choice, not random. More text-type examples is the productive move.
