Noun groups and verb groups in text

AC9E2LA07 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 2 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify both noun groups and verb groups in sentences, and explain how they work together to create more precise and expressive sentences.

Success criteria

  • Student correctly identifies both the noun group and verb group in a given sentence.
  • Student can expand a simple sentence by extending at least its noun group or its verb group.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Verb groups.

Width

Minimum
In a given sentence, identify the noun group and the verb group and name their key elements.
Typical
Analyse sentences across several texts for noun group and verb group complexity; revise simple sentences by expanding noun groups and extending verb groups.
Wider
  • Compare two versions of a sentence — one with simple nouns and verbs, one with extended groups — and discuss how the expanded version changes the image or meaning
  • Write a short descriptive paragraph deliberately using extended noun groups and verb groups, then annotate what was added and why

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node requires students to coordinate noun group and verb group knowledge simultaneously within sentences — the two concepts reinforce and interact. Coordinated identification and production is the learning demand.
