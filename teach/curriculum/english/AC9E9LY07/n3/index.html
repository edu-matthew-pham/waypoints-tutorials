Delivering with integrated multimodal and language choices

AC9E9LY07 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 9 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can deliver a spoken or multimodal presentation that integrates language features, literary devices, voice features and multimodal elements for purpose and audience.

Success criteria

  • Student delivers a presentation that demonstrates deliberate integration of at least two of the four element types
  • Student can evaluate the success of at least one integrative choice — did it serve the purpose and audience?

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Rehearsing voice features for effect.

Width

Minimum
Deliver a presentation that integrates at least two of the four elements (language features, literary devices, voice features, multimodal elements) in ways that serve the purpose.
Typical
Deliver a spoken or multimodal presentation integrating language features, literary devices, voice features and multimodal elements — including graphics, text animations or images to accompany and influence viewer response.
Wider
  • Deliver a news-style current affairs presentation that uses images with spoken text to influence viewer response, then evaluate how effectively the multimodal combination achieved the purpose
  • Collaborate with peers to deliver an imaginative recreation of part of a text — evaluating how the group's delivery decisions collectively represent the key idea

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierDelivery integrates all four elements simultaneously under performance conditions. Coordinating language choices, device deployment, voice features and multimodal elements while maintaining audience connection is the fullest expression of the descriptor's demand.
