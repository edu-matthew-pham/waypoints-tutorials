Plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts

AC9E8LY06 · Year 8 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts, organising and expanding ideas, and selecting text structures, language features, literary devices and visual features for purposes and audiences in ways that may be imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive and/or analytical

Prior knowledge

Students can plan, create, edit and publish texts for a range of purposes and audiences, making deliberate text structure and language feature choices (AC9E7LY06). They have not yet focused on expanding and organising ideas through the full planning-to-publishing cycle with academic accuracy goals, or on the deliberate use of vocabulary for positioning and persuasion.

By the end

Students plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts, organising and expanding ideas with deliberate structural, language and visual feature choices suited to purpose and audience.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • experimenting with and editing text structures and language features
  • selecting vocabulary to position and persuade the reader
  • editing for accuracy of grammar, spelling and punctuation, and for meaning
  • using conceptual maps or journals to plan and reflect on each stage of creating a written or multimodal text

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning and organising ideasHingeStudents can plan a written or multimodal text by organising and expanding ideas — using conceptual maps, journals or equivalent tools — and selecting appropriate text structures for the text's purpose and audience.
  • 2. Creating with deliberate language and feature selectionHingeStudents can create written or multimodal texts that make deliberate selections from text structures, language features, literary devices and visual features to serve their purposes and audiences.
  • 3. Editing for accuracy and meaningStudents can edit own texts for grammatical accuracy, spelling and punctuation, and for clarity and precision of meaning — using experimentation with sentence structure, literary devices and vocabulary to clarify ideas.
