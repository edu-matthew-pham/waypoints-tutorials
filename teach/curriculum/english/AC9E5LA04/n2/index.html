← AC9E5LA04 Starting point and prominence

Theme position creates cohesion

AC9E5LA04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 5 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how consistent or shifting sentence opener choices create cohesion and guide the reader through the development of a text.

Lessons on this site

  • Where a sentence starts

Success criteria

  • Traces the sentence opener choices through a passage and explains how they contribute to cohesion.
  • Identifies at least one place where an opener choice creates a shift or break in topic continuity and explains the effect.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Sentence openers signal topic.

Width

Minimum
Identify in a sample text how the sequence of sentence openers creates a consistent thread or marks a shift in the text's focus, and explain the cohesive effect.
Typical
Analyse how a writer uses sentence opener choices across a paragraph or passage to build cohesion — tracking how the starting points connect sentences into a coherent flow. Identify where opener choices create or break cohesion and explain the effect.
Wider
  • Analyse how paragraphs use their opening sentence to signal to the reader what the paragraph is about, and how this creates cohesion at the paragraph level as well as the sentence level
  • Compare a cohesive text with a rewritten version where sentence openers have been randomised — discussing what is lost and why opener sequencing matters
