Sentences express ideas

AC9EFLA05 · Foundation English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise that sentences are key units for expressing ideas

Prior knowledge

Students use spoken sentences naturally but have not yet reflected on what makes a sentence. They have not yet been asked to distinguish a sentence from a fragment or to identify word order as meaningful.

By the end

Students can identify a sentence as a complete unit that expresses a single idea, distinguish it from a fragment, and recognise that word order affects meaning.

Waypoints

  • 1. A sentence is completeHingeStudents can identify that a sentence expresses a complete idea — it makes sense on its own.
  • 2. Word order carries meaningStudents can demonstrate that changing word order in a sentence changes — or destroys — its meaning.
