Building inferred meaning

AC9E6LY05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 6 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use prior knowledge and textual information to make inferences, going beyond what is explicitly stated.

Success criteria

  • Makes an inference and names the textual evidence that supports it
  • Distinguishes the inference from what is explicitly stated in the text

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Building literal meaning.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one inference made during reading and explain what textual information and prior knowledge supported it.
Typical
Use prior knowledge and textual information to make inferences; connect information in print and images to construct inferred meaning; ask and answer questions that require inference rather than literal retrieval.
Wider
  • Identify a point in a text where inference is required due to ambiguity or implied meaning, and evaluate two different possible inferences against the textual evidence
  • Compare inferences made by different readers of the same text and discuss what prior knowledge or experience shaped different interpretations

Hinge

Students who cannot make supported inferences cannot connect and compare across sources in Node 3 — multi-source comparison relies on inferred meaning as much as literal retrieval, and without the inference skill, cross-source synthesis collapses to surface matching.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkInference requires students to articulate the two-part reasoning move: 'the text says X; I know Y; therefore Z'. Students who have not had the inference process made explicit often guess rather than infer — scaffolding the distinction between evidence and guessing is productive here.
