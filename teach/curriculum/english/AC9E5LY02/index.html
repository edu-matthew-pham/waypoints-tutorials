Paraphrase and justify opinions

AC9E5LY02 · Year 5 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Use appropriate interaction skills including paraphrasing and questioning to clarify meaning, make connections to own experience, and present and justify an opinion or idea

Prior knowledge

Students can listen for key points and information and contribute to discussions by expressing opinions and making judgements (AC9E4LY02). They have not yet applied paraphrasing as a clarification strategy, or developed systematic approaches to questioning for meaning-making in discussion.

By the end

Students use paraphrasing and purposeful questioning to clarify meaning in discussions, make connections between what they hear and their own experience, and present and justify opinions with supporting reasons.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • participating in pair, group, class and school speaking and listening situations, including informal conversations, discussions and presentations
  • asking specific questions to clarify a speaker's meaning, making constructive comments that keep conversation moving
  • using strategies for discussion, such as speaking clearly, pausing, asking questions and linking students' own responses to the contributions of others
  • choosing vocabulary and sentence structures for particular purposes including formal and informal contexts

Waypoints

  • 1. Paraphrasing to clarifyStudents can paraphrase what a speaker has said to check understanding and signal attention, and use this as a clarification strategy in discussion.
  • 2. Purposeful questioning for meaningStudents can ask specific questions to clarify a speaker's meaning, and use questioning to move a discussion forward rather than just to gather information.
  • 3. Present and justify opinionsStudents can present an opinion clearly and support it with reasons, making connections to their own experience where relevant and choosing vocabulary suited to the context.
