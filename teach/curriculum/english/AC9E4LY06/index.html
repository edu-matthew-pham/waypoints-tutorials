Plan, create and publish texts

AC9E4LY06 · Year 4 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal imaginative, informative and persuasive texts, using visual features, relevant linked ideas, complex sentences, appropriate tense, synonyms and antonyms, correct spelling of multisyllabic words and simple punctuation

Prior knowledge

Students plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts using visual features, paragraphs and topic vocabulary (AC9E3LY06). They have not yet planned and created texts using complex sentences, linked ideas, synonyms/antonyms, multisyllabic word spelling and simple punctuation across all three text types.

By the end

Students can plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts across three text types — imaginative, informative and persuasive — using complex sentences, linked ideas, appropriate tense, precise vocabulary and correct spelling and punctuation.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using research to gather ideas for writing and integrating information from a range of sources which may include those found online
  • selecting text structure and planning how to group ideas into paragraphs to sequence content
  • using topic-specific, precise and varied vocabulary
  • using grammatical features including different types of verb groups, noun groups and adverb groups/phrases for effective descriptions and details according to purpose
  • revising written texts to improve the selection of words used to connect ideas and improve the cohesion of the text

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning — structure and contentHingeStudents can plan a written or multimodal text by selecting appropriate structure, grouping ideas into logical sections and deciding on key vocabulary.
  • 2. Creating — complex sentences and linked ideasHingeStudents can create texts using complex sentences and text connectives to link ideas coherently within and across paragraphs.
  • 3. Editing — vocabulary, spelling and punctuationStudents can edit their texts to improve vocabulary choices (synonyms, precise terms), correct multisyllabic spelling and ensure consistent punctuation.
