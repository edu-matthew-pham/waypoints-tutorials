Create critical written and multimodal texts

AC9E9LY06 · Year 9 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts, organising, expanding and developing ideas, and selecting text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features for purposes and audiences in ways that may be imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive, analytical and/or critical

Prior knowledge

Students plan, create and publish texts selecting structures, language features and literary devices for purpose (AC9E7LY06). They have not yet developed analytical and critical text types, or focused on multi-element selection across structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features simultaneously.

By the end

Students plan, create, edit and publish written and multimodal texts selecting text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features for purposes and audiences across imaginative, reflective, informative, persuasive, analytical and critical modes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • developing an argument that has a series of sequenced and linked paragraphs, beginning with a contention, followed by a series of supported points that develop a line of argument, and a conclusion that summarises the main line of argument
  • creating informative texts that explain and analyse complex phenomena using well-chosen facts and precise language
  • reviewing and editing their own and others' texts, which may involve using online applications, for accuracy of grammar, spelling and punctuation, and to achieve particular purposes and address specific audiences by improving clarity and control of content through organising, developing, extending and linking ideas
  • discussing choices of literary devices used in a literary text with a peer, and evaluating the potential effect of each choice on an audience

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning and structuring for analytical/critical purposesHingeStudents can plan a written text with an argument structure: contention, sequenced supporting points and conclusion.
  • 2. Creating with selected language and device choicesStudents can create texts selecting appropriate text structures, language features, literary devices and multimodal features for their specific purpose and audience.
  • 3. Editing and publishing for accuracy and effectStudents can edit written and multimodal texts for grammatical accuracy, spelling and punctuation, and to improve clarity and organisation for specific purposes and audiences.
