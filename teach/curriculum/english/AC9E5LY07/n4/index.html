Integrating visual and digital features

AC9E5LY07 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 5 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can plan and use visual and digital features in a multimodal presentation to support and extend the spoken content, explaining how each feature contributes to audience understanding.

Lessons on this site

  • Visuals that help

Success criteria

  • Includes at least two visual or digital features and can explain how each contributes to audience understanding.
  • The visual/digital features support the spoken content — they are not just slides with repeated text.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Vocal features for engagement.

Width

Minimum
Include at least two visual or digital features in a presentation and explain how each contributes to the audience's understanding of the ideas presented.
Typical
Plan and deliver a multimodal presentation that integrates spoken content with visual and digital features — graphics, sound, visuals — selected to enhance audience engagement and understanding. Explain how the visual/digital and spoken channels work together, not independently.
Wider
  • Compare how visual and digital features function differently in a live presentation versus a recorded or asynchronous presentation — discussing how audience and delivery context shape feature choices
  • Evaluate a peer's use of visual/digital features in a presentation and provide feedback on whether each feature enhanced or distracted from the spoken content

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node requires students to integrate planning (node 1), language choice (node 2), and vocal delivery (node 3) with visual/digital feature selection into a complete multimodal presentation — the full delivery demand of the descriptor.
