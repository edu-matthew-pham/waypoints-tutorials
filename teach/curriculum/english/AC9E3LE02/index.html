Connect personal and character experiences

AC9E3LE02 · Year 3 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

discuss connections between personal experiences and character experiences in literary texts

Prior knowledge

Students identify features of literary texts and give reasons for personal preferences (AC9E2LE02). They have not yet focused specifically on drawing connections between their own experiences and those of characters — using prior knowledge and personal experience as a reading resource.

By the end

Students discuss connections between their own experiences and the experiences of characters in literary texts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Drawing on prior knowledge to connectStudents can draw on relevant prior knowledge and personal experience to make meaningful connections to characters' situations in texts.
  • 2. Preferences grounded in experienceStudents can select and discuss favourite texts, explaining their preferences in ways that connect to personal experience and feelings.
