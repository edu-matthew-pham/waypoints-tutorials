How layers interact

AC9E7LE06 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 7 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how the layers of meaning created by a literary device reinforce or complicate each other

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two layers of meaning created by a literary device
  • Explains whether and how the layers reinforce or complicate each other

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Devices operate on multiple levels simultaneously.

Width

Minimum
In a given text, identify a literary device operating on at least two levels; explain whether the layers reinforce each other or create tension, and why that relationship matters for the text's meaning
Typical
Examine how multiple literary devices in a poem or narrative create layers that interact — where layers reinforce each other to deepen meaning, and where they complicate or create ambiguity; explain how the interaction between layers contributes to the text's overall meaning
Wider
  • Explain the layers of meaning created by imagery in First Nations Australian films, plays and poetry — attending to how visual, cultural and symbolic layers interact with and complicate the literal meaning
  • Compare how the same literary device (e.g. imagery of light and dark) creates different interacting layers in different texts — what does the relationship between layers reveal about each text's concerns?

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents can identify multiple levels of meaning without being able to explain the relationship between them. The reasoning scaffold — do these layers point in the same direction or pull against each other, and what does that tension or reinforcement contribute — is the analytical move that meets the descriptor's 'explain' command term.
Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe relationship between layers is not always reinforcement — some devices create productive ambiguity or cultural complexity. Seeing layer interaction across genuinely different texts and traditions, including First Nations Australian poetry, builds a flexible understanding of how layers can relate rather than assuming they always point the same way.
