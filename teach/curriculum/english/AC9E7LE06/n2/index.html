← AC9E7LE06 Literary devices create layers

Devices operate on multiple levels simultaneously

AC9E7LE06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 7 English · Literature · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify that a literary device creates more than one level of meaning at the same time

Success criteria

  • Identifies a literary device in a given text
  • Names at least two levels of meaning the device creates simultaneously

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Literary devices and their effects.

Width

Minimum
In a given text, identify a literary device and name at least two levels of meaning it creates simultaneously — e.g. the literal image and the figurative or emotional meaning
Typical
Examine how literary devices in a poem or narrative each operate on multiple levels at once — how an image works literally and figuratively simultaneously; how alliteration creates both sound effect and emotional emphasis at the same time; identify the levels present in each case
Wider
  • Compare how a single device (e.g. a recurring image) accumulates levels of meaning across a whole text — how does its meaning expand as the text develops?
  • Examine devices in First Nations Australian poetry and identify the levels of meaning present — including cultural and symbolic layers alongside literal and figurative

Hinge

If students cannot hold two levels of meaning simultaneously, Node 3's work on how layers interact is inaccessible — examining the relationship between layers requires first being able to identify that multiple layers exist.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkThe concept that a device simultaneously means more than one thing is not intuitive for students who are used to looking for one right meaning. The reasoning scaffold — what does this device mean literally, and what else does it mean at the same time — establishes the multi-level reading capacity that the next node builds on.
