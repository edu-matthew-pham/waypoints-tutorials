Deliver structured presentations

AC9E1LY07 · Year 1 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create and deliver short oral and/or multimodal presentations on personal and learnt topics, which include an opening, middle and concluding statement; some topic-specific vocabulary and appropriate gesture, volume and pace

Prior knowledge

Students can create and deliver short spoken texts to report ideas and events to peers (AC9EFLY07). They have not yet structured presentations with explicit opening, middle and concluding statements, or deliberately used topic-specific vocabulary and paralinguistic features (gesture, volume, pace) as presenting tools.

By the end

Students create and deliver short oral and/or multimodal presentations with an opening, middle and concluding statement, topic vocabulary, and appropriate gesture, volume and pace.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • reporting the results of group activities
  • explaining how to do or make something
  • giving short oral presentations about areas of interest or content area topics, speaking clearly and with appropriate volume
  • experimenting with volume and pace for particular purposes
  • giving reasons why the class should learn a particular game

Waypoints

  • 1. Structuring a presentationHingeStudents can organise a short presentation with an identifiable opening, middle and concluding statement.
  • 2. Topic vocabulary in presentationsStudents can use topic-specific vocabulary in their presentations to communicate precisely about the topic.
  • 3. Gesture, volume and paceStudents can use appropriate gesture, volume and pace when delivering a presentation to support meaning and engage the audience.
