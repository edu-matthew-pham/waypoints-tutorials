← AC9E10LE05 Text features and intertextuality shape interpretations

How language devices layer meaning

AC9E10LE05 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 10 English · Literature · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse how language devices in a text layer meaning and influence the responses of listeners, viewers or readers.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two language devices in a text
  • Analyses how the devices interact to layer meaning or shape audience response

Prior knowledge

Students analyse the effect of complex literary devices including extended metaphor, allegory, symbolism and intertextuality (AC9E9LE05). They have not yet analysed how text structure, language features, literary devices and intertextual connections combine to shape interpretations of texts.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two language devices in a text and analyse how they work together to layer meaning or shape a reader's response.
Typical
Analyse how multiple language devices interact across a text to create layered meaning, examining how combinations of devices produce effects that no single device would achieve.
Wider
  • Compare how the same literary device (e.g. extended metaphor) operates differently across poetry, prose and film, examining how medium shapes the device's effect
  • Examine how language devices in a satirical text shape the reader's interpretation of the issue being satirised
  • Analyse how First Nations Australian authors use language devices to layer cultural knowledge and meaning that may not be immediately visible to all readers

Hinge

Node 2 requires students to analyse how intertextual connections shape interpretation — a move that depends on understanding how devices layer meaning. Students who cannot coordinate multiple devices will treat intertextual references as isolated facts rather than meaning-shaping connections.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierStudents need to coordinate multiple device identifications into a coherent analysis of how they interact — the challenge is not identifying individual devices but understanding how they compound and layer.
