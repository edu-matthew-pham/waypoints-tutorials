Retell using plot and language

AC9E1LE05 · Year 1 English · Literature

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

orally retell or adapt a familiar story using plot and characters, language features including vocabulary, and structure of a familiar text, through role-play, writing, drawing or digital tools

Prior knowledge

Students can retell and adapt familiar literary texts through play, performance, images or writing at Foundation level (AC9EFLE05). They have not yet deliberately drawn on plot, character, vocabulary choices and text structure as resources for retelling — the Year 1 expectation makes these elements explicit as tools.

By the end

Students orally retell or adapt a familiar story using plot and characters, appropriate vocabulary and text structure, through role-play, writing, drawing or digital tools.

Waypoints

  • 1. Retelling the plot in orderStudents can orally retell the main events of a familiar story in sequence, including the key characters, problem and resolution.
  • 2. Using language features in retellingStudents can use language features from the original text — vocabulary, dialogue, descriptive phrases — in their retelling.
  • 3. Adapting the storyStudents can adapt a familiar story — changing a character, setting or event — while maintaining the recognisable plot structure and using appropriate vocabulary.
