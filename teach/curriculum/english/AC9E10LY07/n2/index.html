← AC9E10LY07 Plan, create and deliver spoken and multimodal presentations

Creating and rehearsing the presentation

AC9E10LY07 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 10 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create and rehearse a spoken or multimodal presentation, using rhetorical devices and refining delivery through rehearsal.

Success criteria

  • Creates a presentation that includes at least two rhetorical devices
  • Shows evidence of rehearsal in the delivery — timing, delivery choices, responsiveness to audience

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Planning presentations with rhetorical awareness.

Width

Minimum
Create and rehearse a spoken presentation that uses at least two rhetorical devices, with evidence of rehearsal reflected in delivery.
Typical
Create and rehearse a spoken or multimodal presentation that develops and organises ideas for a specific purpose, using rhetorical devices with evidence of deliberate preparation and rehearsal.
Wider
  • Create a multimodal presentation that manipulates rhetorical devices across visual and spoken modes to compel the audience to act
  • Create and rehearse a reflective character monologue from a studied text, experimenting with how rhetorical choices support both characterisation and argument
  • Create a spoken text that incorporates the rhetorical strategies of First Nations Australian oratory or storytelling conventions

Hinge

Node 3 requires students to deliver the presentation — without sufficient rehearsal, the rhetorical choices planned and created cannot be executed effectively in real time and the delivery fails to achieve its purpose.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsDelivery of rhetorical devices in real time requires rehearsal — students who have not practised sufficiently cannot execute their rhetorical choices effectively; rehearsal fluency is the threshold capability here.
