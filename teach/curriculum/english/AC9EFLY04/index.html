Read using phonic knowledge

AC9EFLY04 · Foundation English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

read decodable and authentic texts using developing phonic knowledge, and monitor meaning using context and emerging grammatical knowledge

Prior knowledge

Students are developing letter-sound knowledge (AC9EFLY11, AC9EFLY12). They have not yet been asked to apply phonic decoding strategies within connected text, or to monitor for meaning breakdown and self-correct using context.

By the end

Students can read decodable and authentic texts using developing phonic knowledge — navigating correctly, attempting unknown words using phonics, and monitoring for meaning using context and grammatical knowledge.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • navigating a text correctly, starting at the right place and reading in the right direction, returning to the next line as needed and matching one spoken word to one written word
  • attempting to work out unknown words by using phonic decoding and knowledge of high-frequency words
  • pausing or asking for support when meaning breaks down

Waypoints

  • 1. Navigate a text correctlyHingeStudents can navigate a text from left to right, top to bottom, matching one spoken word to one written word.
  • 2. Use phonic knowledge to decodeHingeStudents can attempt to decode unknown words using their developing letter-sound knowledge and high-frequency word knowledge.
  • 3. Monitor meaning and self-correctStudents can recognise when their reading does not make sense and pause or seek support — using context and emerging grammatical knowledge as a check.
