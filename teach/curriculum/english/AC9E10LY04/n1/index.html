Analysing organisation of ideas

AC9E10LY04 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 10 English · Literacy · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse how ideas are organised in a text and explain the purpose and effect of the organisational choices.

Success criteria

  • Identifies how ideas are organised in a text
  • Explains the purpose the organisational structure serves for the audience

Prior knowledge

Students analyse idea organisation in paragraphs and extended texts (AC9E9LY04). They have not yet analysed and evaluated how authors organise ideas in texts to achieve a specific purpose.

Width

Minimum
Identify how ideas are organised in a text and explain the purpose of that organisation.
Typical
Analyse how the organisation of ideas in a range of texts — print, digital and multimodal — achieves different purposes, examining how structure guides the reader's attention and understanding.
Wider
  • Compare how ideas are organised in texts designed for different platforms (print versus web page) and analyse how the platform shapes organisational choices
  • Examine how the organisation of ideas in political pamphlets or advocacy texts achieves persuasive purposes
  • Analyse how First Nations Australian authors organise ideas in ways that reflect non-linear or culturally specific narrative or argumentative structures

Hinge

Node 2 requires students to evaluate the effectiveness of idea organisation — a judgement that depends on first being able to identify and explain the organisational structure and its intended purpose. Without this, evaluation has no object.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepOrganisational patterns vary substantially across print, digital and multimodal texts — students who have only analysed one text type may not recognise how purpose shapes organisation in other formats; breadth across text types builds reliable identification.
