← AC9E6LA01 Language formality and social distance

Applying register appropriately

AC9E6LA01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 6 English · Language

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can select and use language appropriate to a given level of formality and social distance, and evaluate their choices.

Success criteria

  • Produces spoken or written language that consistently fits the specified register level
  • Explains at least one specific language choice (vocabulary, sentence structure, or tone) and how it signals the intended register

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Social distance and audience.

Width

Minimum
Present or write an idea at one specified register level appropriate to a given audience; identify one choice made and explain why it suits the context.
Typical
Present ideas or opinions in at least two contrasting register contexts (e.g. peer discussion and a formal presentation); reflect on choices made and how they serve different audiences.
Wider
  • Analyse a formal public speech or ceremonial address and identify specific language features that signal institutional formality
  • Draft the same argument for two different audiences (e.g. a class debate and a letter to a local council) and compare register choices

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node requires students to coordinate understanding of continuum, social distance, and language features into a production and evaluation act — the components from Nodes 1 and 2 come together in a situated, purposeful language choice.
