Comprehension strategies for interpretation and evaluation

AC9E8LY05 · Year 8 English · Literacy

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in English: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use comprehension strategies such as visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising, monitoring, questioning and inferring to interpret and evaluate ideas in texts

Prior knowledge

Students can use a range of comprehension strategies to construct meaning from texts and synthesise ideas across texts (AC9E7LY05). They have not yet applied these strategies specifically to interpretation and evaluation — using them to assess the credibility, effectiveness and significance of ideas in texts rather than merely to build comprehension.

By the end

Students use comprehension strategies to interpret and evaluate ideas in texts, including assessing credibility and effectiveness of content.

Waypoints

  • 1. Comprehension strategies for deeper readingHingeStudents can apply comprehension strategies (visualising, predicting, connecting, summarising, monitoring, questioning, inferring) purposefully to interpret ideas in texts — reading for what is implied and inferred, not only what is stated.
  • 2. Comprehension strategies for evaluationStudents can use comprehension strategies to evaluate ideas in texts — assessing the credibility, effectiveness and significance of content, including evaluating the selection and composition of images for their effect on credibility.
