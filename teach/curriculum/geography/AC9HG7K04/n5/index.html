← AC9HG7K04 Hydrological and atmospheric hazards

Integration: causes, impacts and responses

AC9HG7K04 n5 · waypoint 5 of 5 · Year 7 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students explain the causes and impacts of an atmospheric or hydrological hazard and responses from communities and governments — integrating process analysis, impact factors and response framework.

Success criteria

  • Produces an integrated explanation of a hazard event that addresses causes, impact factors and at least one response, using accurate geographical terminology throughout.
  • Explicitly connects at least one response to a prevention, mitigation or preparedness principle.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 4: Prevention, mitigation and preparedness framework.

Width

Minimum
Students produce an explanation of a selected hazard that addresses causes, at least two impact factors, and at least one community or government response.
Typical
Students produce an integrated explanation of a hazard case that traces: the environmental process causing the hazard, how environmental/social/economic factors shaped its impact, specific community and government responses, and how prevention, mitigation or preparedness principles inform those responses.
Wider
  • Develop a hazard management recommendation for a specific Australian place, applying all four elements of the integrated framework and drawing on evidence from the case study
  • Compare two hazard cases (one atmospheric, one hydrological) to examine whether the causes, impact factors and response types share common patterns or differ fundamentally

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe four nodes in this standard cover distinct analytical components (process, impact factors, response examples, response framework). The integration node practises synthesising these into a coherent geographic explanation rather than four separate answers.
