← AC9HG10S06 Create geographical descriptions, explanations and responses

Incorporating geographical knowledge, tools and concepts

AC9HG10S06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 10 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can present conclusions using geospatial technologies and digital tools, incorporating geographical knowledge, concepts and terms that reflect and acknowledge research findings.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Students incorporate at least one geospatial or digital tool output accurately into a response, explaining what it shows
  • Students use at least two geographical concepts or terms accurately and acknowledge research findings that support their claims

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Structural features and purposeful organisation.

Width

Minimum
Include at least one geospatial or digital tool output as part of a response, and use at least two geographical concepts or terms accurately, with acknowledgement of research sources.
Typical
Present conclusions using geospatial technologies and digital tools (e.g. HDI trend graphs, wellbeing maps, GIS outputs); use geographical concepts and terms accurately and consistently; acknowledge research findings that support each major claim.
Wider
  • Create a response that integrates multiple data representations (map, graph, table) into a coherent argument — explaining what each representation contributes to the overall explanation
  • Evaluate the data representations in a published geographical report for their accuracy, relevance and argumentative function, then produce an improved version
  • Produce a response where geospatial data visualisations are specifically designed to reveal a spatial relationship or trend that supports the central claim

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyBuilding a repertoire of geospatial and digital tool outputs that students can accurately incorporate in responses requires practice across multiple tool types — each tool type has conventions for integration into written responses.
