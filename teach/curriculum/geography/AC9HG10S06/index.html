Create geographical descriptions, explanations and responses

AC9HG10S06 · Year 10 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create descriptions, explanations and responses, using geographical knowledge and geographical tools as appropriate, and concepts and terms that incorporate and acknowledge research findings

Prior knowledge

At Y9, students created descriptions, explanations and responses using geographical knowledge, tools, concepts and terms (AC9HG9S06). At Y10 the same descriptor applies with added complexity: explicit use of tone appropriate to purpose and audience (including authoritative tone), formal use of structural features, and integration of geospatial data representations as part of the response.

By the end

Students create descriptions, explanations and responses that use appropriate structural features, incorporate geographical knowledge and tools, deploy concepts and terms accurately, acknowledge research findings, and apply tone appropriate to purpose and audience.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating a description for a purpose, using structural features to organise ideas, findings, impacts and actions; for example, sub-headings
  • presenting conclusions using geospatial technologies and digital tools to create representations of data (for example, the trends in Human Development Index [HDI] over time in a selected country or region) and research findings (for example, how a person's wellbeing is influenced by where they live) to explain causes and effects of a geographical phenomenon or challenge, and reinforcing understanding of the interconnections between people, places and the environment
  • developing an explanation, applying tone appropriate to purpose and audience; for example, using an authoritative tone, and referring to representations of data and information when explaining a strategy to improve the sustainability of an identified environment or action to improve human wellbeing

Waypoints

  • 1. Structural features and purposeful organisationStudents can create a description or explanation for a purpose using structural features to organise ideas, findings, impacts and actions.
  • 2. Incorporating geographical knowledge, tools and conceptsStudents can present conclusions using geospatial technologies and digital tools, incorporating geographical knowledge, concepts and terms that reflect and acknowledge research findings.
  • 3. Applying tone appropriate to purpose and audienceStudents can develop an explanation using an authoritative tone appropriate to purpose and audience, referring to data representations and research findings when explaining a strategy or phenomenon.
