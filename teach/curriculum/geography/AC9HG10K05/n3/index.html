Trends in wellbeing at global scale

AC9HG10K05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can interpret and explain trends in human wellbeing in a developed and a developing country over time, applying wellbeing indices at the global scale.

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Students interpret wellbeing data for two countries over time, identifying at least one trend
  • Students explain the trend with reference to at least one economic, social or political factor

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Measurement methods: GDP, HDI, PQLI and their trade-offs.

Width

Minimum
Interpret a graph or table of wellbeing data for two countries over time and describe at least one trend, offering an explanation for that trend.
Typical
Interpret and explain trends in human wellbeing for Australia and a country in Asia or the Pacific over time, identifying convergence or divergence patterns and explaining the economic, social and political factors driving the trends.
Wider
  • Examine global wellbeing trend data to identify regions showing fastest improvement and those showing stagnation or decline, explaining the structural factors behind divergent trajectories
  • Investigate the relationship between aid flows, foreign investment and wellbeing trends in a specific developing country — evaluating which interventions correlate with sustained improvement
  • Research how global shocks (pandemics, financial crises, climate events) differentially affect wellbeing trajectories in developed vs developing countries

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates conceptual understanding of wellbeing, index literacy and data interpretation into a coherent trend analysis. Students need to connect what the index measures to what the trend means — not just read the data but explain it in terms of the dimensions the index captures.
