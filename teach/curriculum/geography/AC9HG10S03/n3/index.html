Inferring relationships between indicators

AC9HG10S03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 10 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can infer relationships between key environmental or wellbeing indicators at the national scale, using geospatial technology applications where appropriate.

Success criteria

  • Students identify a relationship between two indicators and explain the basis for inferring it from the data
  • Students evaluate the strength of the inferred relationship, acknowledging alternative explanations or confounding factors

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Explaining patterns and trends.

Width

Minimum
Identify a likely relationship between two geographical indicators (e.g. air quality and population density, adult literacy and HDI) and explain the basis for inferring the relationship.
Typical
Infer relationships between multiple environmental or wellbeing indicators at national scale; use geospatial technology applications to create maps showing the relationship spatially; evaluate the strength of the inferred relationship with reference to data evidence.
Wider
  • Distinguish between correlation and causation in a geographical relationship — identifying a case where two indicators correlate but are both caused by a third factor rather than each other
  • Use a scatterplot or bivariate map to visualise a relationship between two indicators and evaluate what the visualisation reveals and conceals about the relationship
  • Investigate a relationship between an environmental indicator and a wellbeing indicator at national scale — tracing the mechanism that connects them

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierRelationship inference integrates generalisation, pattern explanation and data visualisation (from S02) into a coherent analytical conclusion. Students should explicitly connect the relationship they infer to the patterns they have explained and the data they have used.
