Evaluate data to generalise, explain patterns and infer relationships

AC9HG10S03 · Year 10 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate geographical data and information to make generalisations and predictions, explain patterns and trends and infer relationships

Prior knowledge

At Y9, students evaluated geographical data to make generalisations and predictions, explain patterns and trends and infer relationships (AC9HG9S03). At Y10 the same descriptor applies with the implicit expectation that students apply it to more complex data sets — including geospatial technology outputs, multi-variable data and cross-national comparisons — that match the Y10 content strand complexity.

By the end

Students evaluate geographical data and information to develop generalisations, explain patterns and trends in complex datasets including geospatial outputs, and infer relationships between key environmental or wellbeing indicators at the national scale.

Waypoints

  • 1. Developing generalisations from dataHingeStudents can develop generalisations from geographical data and information, critically analysing text and images for meaning and significance.
  • 2. Explaining patterns and trendsStudents can explain geographical patterns and trends in data, including trends in complex and multi-variable datasets.
  • 3. Inferring relationships between indicatorsStudents can infer relationships between key environmental or wellbeing indicators at the national scale, using geospatial technology applications where appropriate.
