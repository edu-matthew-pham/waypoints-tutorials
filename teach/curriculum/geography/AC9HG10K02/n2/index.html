World views and causes of environmental issues

AC9HG10K02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 10 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can compare differences in people's views about the causes of an environmental issue, explaining how world view shapes the identification of causes.

Success criteria

  • Students explain how two stakeholders with different world views identify different causes for the same environmental issue
  • Students use the terms 'human-centred' and 'earth-centred' correctly when analysing cause attribution

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Human-centred and earth-centred world views.

Width

Minimum
Compare two different perspectives on the causes of one environmental issue, tracing how each perspective reflects a different world view.
Typical
For at least one environmental issue of personal, national and global importance, compare perspectives from human-centred and earth-centred stakeholders; explain how each world view leads to a different identification of causes and responsible parties.
Wider
  • Analyse media coverage of a single environmental issue from outlets reflecting different world views, identifying how framing choices (headline, quoted sources, language) encode particular world views
  • Investigate how scientific consensus on an environmental issue (e.g. climate attribution) intersects with or conflicts with different world view frameworks
  • Compare how communities in different countries assign cause and responsibility for the same global environmental issue based on historical, economic and cultural factors

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepAnalysing cause attribution across multiple issues and stakeholder groups prevents students from applying the world view framework mechanically to a single case.
