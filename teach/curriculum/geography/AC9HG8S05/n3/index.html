Evaluating strategy effectiveness

AC9HG8S05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 8 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate the effectiveness of a strategy in relation to environmental, economic and social factors.

Success criteria

  • Evaluates the effectiveness of a strategy using at least two criteria across different dimensions.
  • Reflects on how personal values influence the evaluation of the strategy's outcomes.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Explaining potential impacts.

Width

Minimum
Students evaluate one strategy's effectiveness using at least two criteria.
Typical
Students evaluate the effectiveness of a strategy by considering how well it achieves its goals across environmental, economic and social dimensions, reflecting on personal values and attitudes and how these influence their explanation of potential outcomes.
Wider
  • Compare the effectiveness of two different strategies addressing the same issue.
  • Examine how values and assumptions embedded in a strategy shape what counts as 'effective'.
  • Investigate how effectiveness is measured in practice — what indicators are used and who sets them?

Response modes
