Local trade: businesses selling and buying across places

AC9HG9K07 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 9 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify products and services that businesses in their local area sell to or buy from other places

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two products/services local businesses sell to other places
  • Identifies at least two products/services local businesses buy from other places
  • Distinguishes between businesses trading locally, nationally and globally

Prior knowledge

Students understand that places are interconnected through movement of people, goods and ideas (Y7-8). They have not examined the mechanisms and scale variations of trade interconnections systematically, nor traced how these change over time at local, national and global scales.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two products or services that local businesses sell to other places and at least two they buy from other places
Typical
Examine a range of local businesses — identify what they sell to and buy from other places; explain what factors determine whether a business sells locally, nationally or globally
Wider
  • Map the trade connections of three local businesses at different scales — compare the geographic extent of their interconnections
  • Investigate how local farmers' market products connect to local, national and global supply chains simultaneously
  • Examine how digital platforms (e.g. Etsy, eBay) have changed the scale at which local producers can trade

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStarting with local examples makes the abstract concept of trade interconnection concrete. Examining multiple local businesses — not just one — gives students a range of scale experiences to compare.
