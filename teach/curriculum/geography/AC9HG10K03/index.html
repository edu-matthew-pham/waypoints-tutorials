First Nations custodial responsibility and environmental management

AC9HG10K03 · Year 10 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

First Nations Australians' approaches to custodial responsibility and environmental management in different regions of Australia

Prior knowledge

Students understand the spiritual and aesthetic value of landscapes for First Nations Australians (AC9HG8K03) and have engaged with environmental world views including distinctions between anthropocentric and other frameworks (AC9HG10K02). They have developed awareness of Country/Place as a concept in earlier years.

By the end

Students explain First Nations Australians' approaches to custodial responsibility and environmental management in different regions of Australia, including the influence of cultural values, the role of First Nations Park Rangers, and First Nations models of sustainability.

Waypoints

  • 1. Custodial responsibility and cultural valuesHingeStudents can explain what custodial responsibility means for First Nations Australians and identify the influence of cultural values — including continuity of cultural practices, management of Country/Place, and land tenure — on how environments are managed.
  • 2. First Nations Park Rangers and cultural knowledge in practiceStudents can explain the role of First Nations Australian Park Rangers and how their cultural knowledge and practices contribute to the management of Country/Place and environments.
  • 3. First Nations models of sustainabilityStudents can explain First Nations Australians' models of sustainability, including specific practices such as fire management, land care, species protection and community capacity building, and explain how these contribute to broader conservation.
