Recommending a strategy and predicting impacts

AC9HG9S05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Year 9 Geography · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can recommend a strategy and explain its predicted outcomes and impacts

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • States a clear strategy recommendation
  • Justifies the recommendation with reference to the evaluation criteria from Node 2
  • Explains at least two predicted outcomes or impacts of the recommended strategy

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Evaluating strategy effectiveness.

Width

Minimum
Recommend one strategy with a reasoned justification; explain at least two predicted outcomes or impacts of implementing the strategy
Typical
Recommend a strategy for a geographical challenge (e.g. improving food quality and cultural acceptability, reducing global hazardous waste movement) and explain its predicted outcomes and impacts across environmental, economic and social dimensions; connect the recommendation to the evaluation from Node 2
Wider
  • Address likely counterarguments to the recommended strategy — explain why the recommendation stands despite the limitations identified in evaluation
  • Predict second-order impacts — what might change as a result of the strategy's first-order effects?
  • Compare two possible recommendations and explain why one is preferable given the evaluation criteria from Node 2

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierRecommendation integrates the strategy repertoire from Node 1 and the criterion-based evaluation from Node 2 into a justified choice. The recommendation must be explicitly grounded in the evaluation — a recommendation that ignores the evaluation has not integrated the prior nodes.
