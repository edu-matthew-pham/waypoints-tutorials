Reflecting on personal values and predicted outcomes

AC9HG9S05 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 9 Geography · Skills

Checkpoint

Students can reflect on the influence of personal values and attitudes on predicted outcomes and impacts

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one personal value that influences a predicted outcome
  • Explains how a different value would produce a different prediction
  • Connects the values reflection back to the strategy recommendation from Node 3

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Recommending a strategy and predicting impacts.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one personal value or attitude that influences a predicted outcome; explain how a different value or attitude might produce a different prediction
Typical
Reflect on how personal values (e.g. preference for locally produced food, commitment to recycling) influence predicted outcomes and impacts; explain how different values lead to different predictions about the same strategy
Wider
  • Examine how cultural background or geographic position might shape what values are held and what outcomes are predicted — connect to S04 perspective reflection
  • Identify where a value judgement is embedded in an apparently objective predicted impact — explain what would change if the value were different
  • Reflect on how the values implicit in a government or organisational strategy differ from personal values — explain what this reveals about whose interests are prioritised

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkValues-reflection requires metacognitive awareness — students must step back from their prediction and ask what value it assumes. Students who cannot identify the embedded value cannot reflect on how different values produce different predictions. Scaffolding the identification step — 'what do I have to believe is important for this prediction to follow?' — builds the reflective capacity.
