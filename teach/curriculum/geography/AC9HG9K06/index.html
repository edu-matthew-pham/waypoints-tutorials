Tourism, leisure and place impacts

AC9HG9K06 · Year 9 Geography · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Geography: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the effects on places of people's travel, recreational, cultural or leisure choices, and the strategies for managing the impacts on these places

Prior knowledge

Students can describe how people's activities affect the characteristics of places (Y7-8). They have not examined tourism and leisure as a structured driver of environmental, economic and social change, nor examined management plans that integrate cultural and scientific knowledge to address these impacts.

By the end

Students explain the causes of global tourism growth and its environmental, economic and social impacts on places, predict how future trends may affect places, and examine management strategies that integrate cultural and scientific knowledge to manage these impacts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Causes and growth of global tourismStudents can discuss the causes of global tourism growth
  • 2. Environmental, economic and social impacts of tourismHingeStudents can explain the environmental, economic and social impacts of tourism on places and predict how future trends may affect these impacts
  • 3. Management strategies integrating cultural and scientific knowledgeStudents can examine how management plans integrate cultural and scientific knowledge and experience to manage the effects of people's travel and leisure choices
