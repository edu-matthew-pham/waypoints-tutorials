Developing historical inquiry questions

AC9HH10S01 · Year 10 History · Historical skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop and modify a range of historical questions about the past to inform historical inquiry

Prior knowledge

Students can develop and modify historical questions to inform inquiry at Year 9 level — scoping questions for a specific investigation and adjusting them based on source availability.

By the end

Students develop a range of historical questions requiring historical argument, modify them in response to source suitability and scope, and evaluate whether questions are appropriately calibrated for the investigation.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying, planning to investigate and investigating a specific historical question or issue
  • developing a range of questions about aspects of the past that require historical argument
  • modifying a key question or related questions in an inquiry, depending on the suitability of the sources available
  • determining whether a key question or related questions are too broad or narrow given the requirements of the investigation

Waypoints

  • 1. Developing questions that require historical argumentHingeStudents can develop a range of historical questions — a key question and related sub-questions — about an aspect of the past that require historical argument rather than factual retrieval.
  • 2. Modifying and scoping questionsStudents can modify historical questions in response to source suitability and evaluate whether questions are appropriately scoped for the investigation.
