Significant individuals and groups in promoting ideas

AC9HH9K18 · Year 9 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the role of a significant individual or group such as agricultural and factory workers, inventors and entrepreneurs, landowners, politicians and religious groups in promoting and enacting some of the ideas that emerged during the Industrial Revolution

Prior knowledge

Students can describe the role of significant individuals and groups in historical change (AC9HH8K05, AC9HH8K10, AC9HH8K15) and understand the ideas that emerged during the Industrial Revolution (AC9HH9K17). They have not yet applied the concept of historical agency — the role of individuals in promoting and enacting ideas — to the Industrial Revolution context, or examined how responses to ideas shaped the trajectory of industrial society.

By the end

Students explain responses to particular ideas — including how religious groups responded to Darwin's ideas, and how workers responded to capitalism or socialism — and investigate the role played by a specific individual or group in promoting a key idea, including figures such as Adam Smith, Florence Nightingale, Pope Leo XIII, and William Cuffay.

Waypoints

  • 1. Historical agency — how individuals and groups promote ideasHingeStudents can explain what it means for an individual or group to promote an idea — the distinction between holding an idea and actively working to advance it — and describe the range of strategies available (writing, organising, campaigning, legislating).
  • 2. Responses to ideas — Darwin, capitalism and socialismStudents can explain how different groups responded to specific ideas — including how religious groups responded to Darwin's evolutionary theory, and how workers responded to capitalism and socialism.
  • 3. Investigating a significant individual — role in promoting a key ideaStudents can investigate the role played by a specific individual in promoting a key idea from the Industrial Revolution period — describing what they did, what idea they promoted, and what they achieved.
