Locate primary and secondary sources

AC9HH7S02 · Year 7 History · Historical skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate and identify primary and secondary sources to use in historical inquiry

Prior knowledge

Students can locate, collect, and organise information from primary and secondary sources (AC9HS6S02). They have not yet applied the primary/secondary distinction specifically to historical inquiry — including the disciplinary definition of primary sources as sources from the time of the event being investigated, and the practical skills of locating sources in digital repositories and research databases.

By the end

Students locate and identify primary and secondary sources to use in historical inquiry, demonstrating understanding of the primary/secondary distinction, ability to use search strategies to find sources in repositories and databases, and the capacity to sort and organise sources by type.

Waypoints

  • 1. Primary and secondary sources — the disciplinary distinctionHingeStudents can explain the distinction between primary and secondary sources as used in historical inquiry — primary sources are from the time of the event being investigated; secondary sources are later interpretations — and correctly classify a given set of sources.
  • 2. Locating sources — repositories and search strategiesStudents can locate primary and secondary sources using digital repositories, archives, and research databases, applying appropriate search strategies to find relevant evidence for a historical inquiry question.
  • 3. Sorting and organising sources for inquiryStudents can sort and organise a set of primary and secondary sources by type, identify categories relevant to their inquiry, and explain why different source types serve different purposes in a historical investigation.
