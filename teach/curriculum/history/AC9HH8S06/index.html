Identify perspectives and values in sources

AC9HH8S06 · Year 8 History · Historical skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

identify perspectives, attitudes and values of the past in sources

Prior knowledge

Students identify perspectives, attitudes and values of the past in sources (AC9HH7S06). They can identify the perspective in a source. They have not yet used additional sources to show how an individual account is broadly representative of or contrasts with wider societal values, or analysed how historians' own contexts shape their interpretations.

By the end

Students identify perspectives, attitudes and values of the past in sources — describing what a source reveals and using additional sources to assess how representative it is of broader societal values — and explain why historians interpret the same sources differently based on their own perspectives and contextual factors.

Waypoints

  • 1. Values and attitudes in individual sourcesHingeStudents can identify the values and attitudes revealed by an individual source and describe what these suggest about the society or individual that produced it.
  • 2. Representativeness and broader societal valuesStudents can use additional sources to assess whether an individual account's values and attitudes are broadly representative of the society, or contrast with it — and explain the significance of this for the source's usefulness.
  • 3. Historians' perspectives and interpretationsStudents can explain why historians have different interpretations of sources — including how access to source material, personal views, and the contextual factors of the historian's own time have influenced their conclusions.
