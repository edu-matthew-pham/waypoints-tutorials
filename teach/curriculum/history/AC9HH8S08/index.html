Write sourced historical arguments

AC9HH8S08 · Year 8 History · Historical skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create descriptions, explanations and historical arguments, using historical knowledge, concepts and terms that reference evidence from sources

Prior knowledge

Students create descriptions, explanations and historical arguments using historical knowledge, concepts and terms that reference evidence from sources (AC9HH7S08). They can construct basic historical writing with source references. They have not yet applied structural elements for argumentative effect (introduction with explicit argument, conclusion that restates and summarises), presented findings across multiple formats, or applied historical concepts and terms with precision in their appropriate historical context.

By the end

Students create descriptions, explanations and historical arguments — using structural elements for argumentative effect, applying historical terms and concepts in their appropriate context, referencing source evidence, and presenting findings in formats appropriate to audience and purpose.

Waypoints

  • 1. Historical terms and concepts in contextHingeStudents can apply historical terms and concepts in their appropriate historical context — using the vocabulary of the discipline accurately to describe, explain and argue about the past.
  • 2. Structural elements for argumentative effectStudents can use structural elements — an introduction that outlines an argument and a conclusion that summarises and restates it — to communicate historical explanations or analysis with argumentative effect.
  • 3. Presenting findings across formatsStudents can present historical findings or knowledge in a range of formats — such as a podcast, oral presentation or essay — appropriate to the audience and purpose, with reference to evidence.
