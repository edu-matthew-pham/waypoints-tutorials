The Kokoda commemoration controversy

AC9HH10K06 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 10 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse the debate about the commemoration of Kokoda — including the appropriateness of commercial trekking — from multiple perspectives including Papua New Guinean communities.

Success criteria

  • Explains what Kokoda Trail trekking involves as a contemporary commemoration practice
  • Identifies at least two arguments in the debate — representing more than one stakeholder perspective
  • Explains the perspective of at least one non-Australian stakeholder, including what they see as at stake in the debate

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: The Battle for Australia debate.

Width

Minimum
Students explain what Kokoda Trail trekking involves as a contemporary practice; identify at least two arguments in the debate representing more than one perspective.
Typical
Students examine Kokoda's place in Australian national memory (built on N2's content); discuss the growth of commercial trekking as a commemoration practice; analyse arguments for appropriateness (personal connection to sacrifice, physical experience of terrain, income for local communities) and against (commercialisation trivialises sacrifice, environmental impact, questions about whose story is being centred); examine the perspectives of Papua New Guinean communities, families of veterans, and the descendants of the Fuzzy Wuzzy Angels in the debate.
Wider
  • Examine the perspectives of Papua New Guinean communities on how Kokoda is commemorated — analysing whether Australian commemoration adequately recognises the contribution and experience of Papua New Guineans in the campaign
  • Compare the Kokoda debate with other contested commemoration practices — battlefield tourism at Gallipoli, Anzac Day alcohol consumption debates — analysing what the pattern reveals about how societies navigate the tension between commemoration and consumption

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepThe debate is analytically richer when examined from multiple stakeholder perspectives — Australian veterans' families, commercial operators, Papua New Guinean communities, historians, environmentalists. Using multiple perspectives prevents students from treating only one side as legitimate and builds the nuanced analysis the descriptor demands.
