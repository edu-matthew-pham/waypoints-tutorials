Analyse different and contested historical interpretations

AC9HH9S07 · Year 9 History · Historical skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse different and contested historical interpretations

Prior knowledge

In Year 7 students explained historical interpretations about significant events, individuals and groups (AC9HH7S07). In Year 8 they continued with explanation (AC9HH8S07). Y9 lifts the demand to analysis — students must now not only identify and explain different interpretations but analyse how and why they differ, including how interpretations change over time as new sources are discovered.

By the end

Students analyse different and contested historical interpretations, examining how and why interpretations differ and change over time.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring debates about the historical period or topic studied
  • comparing similarities and differences in historians' views about colonial society
  • identifying different interpretations of specific events
  • analysing how historians have changed the way they interpret the event under investigation over time

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying and comparing historical interpretationsStudents can identify and compare different historical interpretations of an event, individual, group, or period, explaining the specific claims each interpretation makes.
  • 2. Analysing how and why interpretations changeStudents can analyse how historians have changed their interpretations of events over time, examining why interpretations change as new sources are discovered, social values shift, or new analytical frameworks emerge.
