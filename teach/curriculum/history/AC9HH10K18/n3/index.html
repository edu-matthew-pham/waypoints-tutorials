Changing religious landscape

AC9HH10K18 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Year 10 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can examine the changing nature of religion in Australia — including the declining practice of mainstream Christianity, the growth of non-Christian religions, and the emergence of non-traditional churches.

Success criteria

  • Describes at least two changes in Australia's religious landscape since the post-war period
  • Explains the causes of each change identified
  • Connects religious change to the broader theme of changing values and beliefs

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Challenges to traditional values — Vietnam, feminism, and the Whitlam dismissal.

Width

Minimum
Students describe at least two changes in Australia's religious landscape since the post-war period and explain the causes of each.
Typical
Students examine the post-war dominance of mainstream Christian denominations in Australian public and private life; trace the decline in practice of mainstream Christianity from the 1960s; examine the growth of non-Christian religions through successive migration waves (Buddhism, Islam, Hinduism, Sikhism); examine the emergence of Pentecostal and non-traditional Christian churches; explain the causes of each change — secularisation, migration, generational change, the loss of institutional authority following abuse scandals; connect to the broader theme of changing values and beliefs.
Wider
  • Examine the Royal Commission into Institutional Responses to Child Sexual Abuse (2013–17) as a major event in the declining authority of mainstream Christian institutions — analysing its findings and their effects on public attitudes to religion
  • Investigate how Australia's religious diversity compares with other comparable countries — examining whether Australia's trajectory of secularisation and diversification is typical or distinctive

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepExamining multiple threads in religious change — declining mainstream practice, migration-driven diversification, non-traditional church growth — gives a fuller and more accurate picture than focusing on secularisation alone. Each thread has its own causes and each contributes differently to the changing religious landscape.
