Economic and environmental features

AC9HH8K07 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 8 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe the economic activities and environmental context of the chosen group and explain how these shaped their expansion and interactions with other societies.

Success criteria

  • Describes at least one economic activity of the chosen group and explains its connection to their expansion or wider influence
  • Identifies how an environmental feature shaped the group's economic or expansionist capacity

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Religious and cultural features.

Width

Minimum
A description of at least one economic activity and its relationship to the group's expansion or influence.
Typical
Economic and environmental features across trade, agriculture, resource use and technology. Examples: Mongol trade networks connecting Europe and Asia; Ottoman bazaars and trade routes; Viking longboat technology enabling trade and raiding; Aztec agriculture and resource management in Tenochtitlan; Spanish conquest driven by access to American commodities.
Wider
  • For the Ottomans: examine inventions and developments in the Islamic world (astrolabe, public hospitals and libraries) and their adoption in the Western world, showing how economic and intellectual expansion intersected
  • For the Vikings: investigate the construction of longboats — innovations in keel and sail design — as a case study in how technological development enabled economic and military expansion

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents need to see the link between economic capacity (trade, technology, agriculture) and the ability to expand. Scaffold the reasoning: what resources or technologies did this group have, and how did these enable or constrain their expansion?
