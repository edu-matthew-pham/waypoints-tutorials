Causes and effects of the industrial revolution

AC9HH9K13 · Year 9 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the social, economic, political, technological and/or environmental causes and effects of the Industrial Revolution on Europe in the late 18th and 19th century

Prior knowledge

Students can describe significant developments and their causes and effects in earlier periods (AC9HH8K03, AC9HH8K07). They have not yet examined the Industrial Revolution as a multi-causal, multi-domain transformation — the specific role of technology, the Agricultural Revolution preconditions, the spread of industrialisation across Europe, or how to evaluate the relative significance of multiple causes.

By the end

Students explain how changes in technology led to the development of factories and industrialised cities, identify the spread of innovations across Europe and beyond, analyse the multiple factors that caused the Industrial Revolution, and evaluate the most significant effects across economic, social, political and environmental domains.

Waypoints

  • 1. Preconditions for industrialisation — why Britain firstHingeStudents can identify the preconditions that enabled industrialisation to begin in Britain and explain why industrialisation began there rather than elsewhere.
  • 2. Technology and the factory system — steam, iron and textilesStudents can explain how key technological changes — steam-driven machinery, iron and steel production, railways — led to the development of factories and industrialised cities.
  • 3. The spread of industrialisation — Europe, USA and JapanStudents can describe how industrialisation spread from Britain to Europe, the USA and Japan, and identify the spread of key innovations (steam power, iron and steel, transport, new chemicals).
  • 4. Effects of the Industrial Revolution — evaluating significance across domainsStudents can describe the range of effects of the Industrial Revolution — economic, social, political, environmental — and evaluate which effects were most significant.
