European imperial expansion and movement of peoples

AC9HH9K01 · Year 9 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the causes and effects of European imperial expansion and the movement of peoples in the late 18th and early 19th centuries, and the different responses to colonisation and migration

Prior knowledge

Students can describe the features of societies and the effects of contact and change in earlier periods (AC9HH8K07, AC9HH8K08, AC9HH8K12). They have not yet examined the global scale of European imperial expansion in the modern period, the push and pull factors driving mass population movements, or the range of responses by colonised peoples to European contact.

By the end

Students identify and describe the causes of European imperial expansion and the movement of peoples — including push and pull factors — describe the effects of those movements on colonised peoples, and describe the different responses to colonisation and migration across affected societies.

Waypoints

  • 1. European imperial expansion — scale and driversHingeStudents can describe the scale of European imperial expansion in the late 18th and early 19th centuries and identify the main drivers — economic, political, technological and ideological — that enabled and motivated it.
  • 2. Push and pull factors — causes of population movementStudents can identify and describe the push factors that drove people to leave their homelands and the pull factors that attracted them to new destinations, across voluntary and forced migration.
  • 3. Effects on colonised peoplesStudents can describe the effects of European expansion and population movement on colonised peoples — including dispossession, disease, destruction of traditional society and culture — and explain how these effects operated.
  • 4. Different responses to colonisation and migrationStudents can describe the different responses of colonised and migrant peoples to European expansion and movement — including resistance, accommodation, adaptation, and contribution — and explain what shaped each response.
