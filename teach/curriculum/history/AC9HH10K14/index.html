Changing perspectives on modern life

AC9HH10K14 · Year 10 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

changing historical perspectives over time in relation to the developments in technology, public health, longevity, standard of living in the 20th century, and concern for the environment and sustainability

Prior knowledge

Students understand continuity and change as historical concepts from earlier study. They have examined post-war reconstruction and the long boom in K05 and K16. They have not yet examined technological development, public health, or the environmental movement as distinct historical threads with their own periodisation and changing perspectives.

By the end

Students identify key developments in technology, public health, and standard of living in the 20th century, and explain how perspectives on environmental sustainability have changed over time.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying key developments in technology since 1945 that have changed the world in the following areas: the household, such as television, appliances, central heating and cooling; travel and trade, such as shipping and passenger jets; globalisation, mass consumption and communications, such as the microchip, satellites and digital systems
  • discussing the growth in the world’s population during the 20th century, including life expectancy changes in different parts of the world, and the depletion of natural resources
  • identifying how the rise of the environmental movement around the world has changed people’s perspectives on things such as developments in renewable energy technology and sustainability measures such as recycling

Waypoints

  • 1. Changing perspectives as a historical conceptHingeStudents can explain that historical perspectives on the same developments change over time, and can apply this to at least one 20th-century development.
  • 2. Technological development and its effectsStudents can identify key technological developments since 1945 and explain how each changed the world — in households, travel and trade, and communications.
  • 3. Public health, longevity, and standard of livingStudents can explain the major improvements in public health and standard of living during the 20th century and discuss how perspectives on population growth and resource depletion changed.
  • 4. The environmental movement — changing perspectives on sustainabilityStudents can explain the rise of the environmental movement and analyse how perspectives on sustainability and environmental protection have changed since the 1960s.
