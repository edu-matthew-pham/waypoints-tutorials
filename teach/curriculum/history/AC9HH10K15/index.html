The Universal Declaration of Human Rights

AC9HH10K15 · Year 10 History · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in History: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the origins and significance of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, including Australia's involvement in the development of the declaration

Prior knowledge

Students have examined the Holocaust's scale and significance and its role in motivating international human rights frameworks (AC9HH10K03, N5). They have examined Australia's role in founding the United Nations (K05, N3). They have studied international human rights frameworks in K13. They have not yet examined the UDHR's specific content, drafting history, or present significance in detail.

By the end

Students explain the origins and significance of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, including Australia's involvement in its development and its present significance.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • describing the causes of the development of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, such as the atrocities of the Holocaust, the immense scale of destruction and displacement because of Second World War
  • investigating the present significance of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights

Waypoints

  • 1. Origins — the post-war human rights momentHingeStudents can explain the causes of the development of the UDHR, connecting the Holocaust, WW2's destruction and displacement, and the founding of the UN as the conditions that made the declaration possible and necessary.
  • 2. Australia's involvement — HV Evatt and Jessie StreetStudents can explain Australia's specific involvement in the development of the UDHR, including the roles of HV Evatt and Jessie Street.
  • 3. The UDHR's present significanceStudents can investigate the present significance of the UDHR — examining how it is used, what it has achieved, and what limits it faces.
