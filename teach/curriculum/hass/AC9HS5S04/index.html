Evaluate source purpose and perspective

AC9HS5S04 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate primary and secondary sources to determine origin, purpose and perspectives

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS4S04 (Analyse data and perspectives): Students analyse data and identify different perspectives in sources. Students can identify viewpoints in sources, but at Year 4 level do not yet evaluate origin and purpose systematically.

By the end

Students evaluate primary and secondary sources to determine their origin, purpose and the perspectives they present, identifying bias, stereotypes and differences between accounts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Origin and purpose of sourcesHingeStudents can identify the origin of a source (who created it, when, where) and explain its likely purpose (to inform, persuade, entertain, record), and explain how origin and purpose might affect the information a source contains.
  • 2. Identifying bias and stereotypesStudents can identify inferred messages, stereotypes and over-generalisations in historical and contemporary sources, explaining how these reflect the perspective and possible bias of the source's creator.
  • 3. Comparing accounts and perspectivesStudents can compare primary and secondary sources on the same topic to identify similarities and differences in their accounts, explaining why different sources present different perspectives.
  • 4. Evaluating sources for accuracy and perspectiveStudents can evaluate the accuracy of sources, identify the perspectives they present, and explain how origin, purpose and perspective together affect a source's usefulness for inquiry.
