Asia region diversity and location

AC9HS6K04 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the geographical diversity and location of places in the Asia region, and its location in relation to Australia

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5K04 (People shape places): Students understand how people influence the characteristics of places. Students can describe and locate places at a general level.

By the end

Students explain the geographical diversity and location of places in the Asia region, describe its location in relation to Australia, and use latitude and longitude to describe absolute location.

Waypoints

  • 1. Location of the Asia region in relation to AustraliaHingeStudents can describe the location of the Asia region in relation to Australia, including the main sub-regions (North-East, South-East, South Asia) and Australia's proximity to and connections with each.
  • 2. Absolute location using latitude and longitudeStudents can describe the location of places in countries of the Asia region in absolute terms, using latitude and longitude.
  • 3. Geographical and cultural diversity of the Asia regionStudents can describe the geographical and cultural diversity of the Asia region, including diversity of environments, settlement types, population, languages and ways of life.
  • 4. Asia region: location, diversity and Australia's connectionsStudents can describe the geographical diversity and absolute location of places in the Asia region, and explain its location in relation to Australia, drawing on population, environment and life expectancy data.
