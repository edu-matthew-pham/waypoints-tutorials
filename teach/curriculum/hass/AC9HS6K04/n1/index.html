Location of the Asia region in relation to Australia

AC9HS6K04 n1 · waypoint 1 of 4 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe the location of the Asia region in relation to Australia, including the main sub-regions (North-East, South-East, South Asia) and Australia's proximity to and connections with each.

Success criteria

  • Students identify the Asia region and its main sub-regions on a map.
  • Students describe Australia's location in relation to the Asia region.

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5K04 (People shape places): Students understand how people influence the characteristics of places. Students can describe and locate places at a general level.

Width

Minimum
Describe where the Asia region is in relation to Australia and name at least two sub-regions.
Typical
Describe the Asia region's location in relation to Australia, identify the main sub-regions (North-East, South-East, South Asia), and explain how Australia's location in the Asia-Pacific region shapes its relationships and connections.
Wider
  • Use latitude and longitude to describe the absolute location of at least three countries in the Asia region, and compare these with Australia's coordinates
  • Investigate how Australia's geographic proximity to South-East and South Asia has shaped historical and contemporary connections (trade, migration, diplomacy)

Hinge

Without a secure geographical foundation for the location and structure of the Asia region, students cannot develop meaningful understanding of diversity, connections or comparisons within the region.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsLocation knowledge requires repeated map practice — students need to build a reliable mental map of the Asia region through multiple encounters with atlases, digital maps and coordinate systems.
