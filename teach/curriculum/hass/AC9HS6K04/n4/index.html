Asia region: location, diversity and Australia's connections

AC9HS6K04 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe the geographical diversity and absolute location of places in the Asia region, and explain its location in relation to Australia, drawing on population, environment and life expectancy data.

Success criteria

  • Students describe the Asia region's location in relation to Australia.
  • Students use latitude and longitude for at least one location in the region.
  • Students describe geographical and cultural diversity across at least two countries or sub-regions.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Geographical and cultural diversity of the Asia region.

Width

Minimum
Describe the Asia region's location in relation to Australia, use latitude/longitude for at least one location, and describe the diversity of at least one country or sub-region.
Typical
Describe the geographical diversity and location of places in the Asia region; use latitude and longitude to describe absolute locations; compare population size and life expectancy across selected countries; describe the range of environments and ways of life in the region; explain its location in relation to Australia.
Wider
  • Analyse population and development data for countries across the Asia region, identifying patterns and explaining factors that account for differences
  • Evaluate how Australia's geographic position in relation to the Asia region has shaped its trade relationships, migration patterns and foreign policy

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis integration node draws together location (coordinates), diversity (environment, culture, population) and Australia's relationship to the region. Use a structured comparison matrix or annotated map to support synthesis.
