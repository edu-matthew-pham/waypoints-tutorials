← AC9HS6K04 Asia region diversity and location

Absolute location using latitude and longitude

AC9HS6K04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 4 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe the location of places in countries of the Asia region in absolute terms, using latitude and longitude.

Success criteria

  • Students correctly use latitude and longitude to describe the location of at least two places in Asia.
  • Students explain what latitude and longitude coordinates mean (which hemisphere, how far from equator/prime meridian).

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Location of the Asia region in relation to Australia.

Width

Minimum
Use latitude and longitude to describe the absolute location of at least two places in the Asia region.
Typical
Describe the location of places in the Asia region using latitude and longitude; apply this to compare locations, explain hemispheric positions, and understand why latitude relates to climate and seasonal patterns.
Wider
  • Use a digital geospatial tool to plot locations in the Asia region using coordinates, and explain what the coordinates reveal about relative position and climate
  • Investigate how latitude affects climate across the Asia region, comparing countries at different latitudes

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsReading and using latitude and longitude is a precision geographic skill requiring practice. Errors in coordinate notation (reversing lat/long, confusion about hemispheres) need targeted correction.
