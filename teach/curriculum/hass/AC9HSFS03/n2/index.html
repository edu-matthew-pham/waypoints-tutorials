Sharing my own perspective

AC9HSFS03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Foundation HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can share their own perspective on a significant event or special place, explaining why they feel that way and using a source to support their view.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Shares a personal view about a significant event or special place
  • Gives at least one reason for that view

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Recognising that perspectives differ.

Width

Minimum
Share a personal view about a special place or significant event and give one reason for that view.
Typical
Share perspectives on stories about significant events and special places using provided sources; explain why the place or event is important from their own point of view; connect their perspective to personal experience.
Wider
  • Share perspectives about a place that is important to past generations of their family, explaining why those events and places are special
  • Listen to a First Nations Australian story about a special place and share a response, comparing it with their own experience of special places

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepA range of significant events and places gives students more contexts to form and share views — class diversity is the primary resource here.
