← AC9HS3S06 Propose actions or responses

Proposing and justifying responses

AC9HS3S06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 3 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can develop a plan of action for a community challenge and argue for their proposal using persuasive reasoning.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Proposes at least one action for a community challenge
  • Supports the proposal with at least one persuasive reason

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Considering effects of actions.

Width

Minimum
Propose at least one action for a community challenge and give at least one reason why it would help.
Typical
Develop plans of action for community challenges (recycling, protecting endangered species, preserving a heritage place, community festival participation); argue a point of view using persuasive language ('I think', 'I dis/agree that').
Wider
  • Develop a group response to a community challenge that considers the perspectives of at least two different community groups
  • Present a proposal to a real audience (school council, local community member) and respond to questions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.
