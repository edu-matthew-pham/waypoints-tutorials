← AC9HS2S02 Collect and organise observations

Collecting from historical and geographical sources

AC9HS2S02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 2 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can collect relevant information from a range of sources appropriate to both historical and geographical inquiry, identifying relevant information for their purpose.

Success criteria

  • Collects information from at least two source types
  • Identifies which information from each source is relevant to the inquiry question

Prior knowledge

From Y1 (AC9HS1S02): Students collected information from a range of sources and recorded on unscaled timelines and labelled maps.

Width

Minimum
Collect information from at least two different source types relevant to a Y2 inquiry (e.g. photographs and maps, or survey data and satellite images).
Typical
Collect from: photographs, interviews, newspapers, stories, place/street names (historical); surveying peers, satellite images, field photographs, observations of monuments (geographical); identify relevant information from each source.
Wider
  • Compare sources created at different times (e.g. a historical map and a current satellite image) and discuss what each reveals
  • Evaluate the reliability of different source types for a specific inquiry question

Hinge

Recording on timelines and maps requires prior collection of information — without collected material there is nothing to record.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyYear 2 introduces geographical data sources (satellite images, field photographs, surveys) alongside historical ones — build familiarity with each source type through whole-class exploration.
