Develop questions about systems

AC9HS6S01 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop questions to investigate people, events, developments, places and systems

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5S01 (Develop system investigation questions): Students develop purposeful inquiry questions using probing, open-ended and practical question types. Students can frame inquiry questions across HASS stages.

By the end

Students develop questions to investigate people, events, developments, places and systems, including ethical and system-level questions that reveal connections across economic, political and environmental dimensions.

Waypoints

  • 1. Ethical and systemic question typesHingeStudents can develop ethical questions about cultural protocol and sensitivity, and system-mapping questions that reveal connections between economic, political and environmental systems.
  • 2. Questions that guide all inquiry stagesStudents can develop a set of questions that frame and guide all stages of a HASS inquiry — before, during and after investigation — addressing relevant disciplinary concepts.
  • 3. Complete inquiry question set for Year 6 topicsStudents can develop a complete inquiry question set for a Year 6 HASS topic that spans disciplines, addresses cultural sensitivities, and guides investigation from initial framing through to conclusions and action.
