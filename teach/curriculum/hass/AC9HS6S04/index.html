Evaluate sources and perspectives

AC9HS6S04 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate primary and secondary sources to determine origin, purpose and perspectives

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5S04 (Evaluate source purpose and perspective): Students can evaluate sources for origin, purpose and perspective, identify bias and stereotypes, and compare accounts from different sources. Students apply a source analysis framework independently.

By the end

Students evaluate primary and secondary sources to determine origin, purpose and perspectives, including identifying persuasive techniques, distinguishing fact from opinion, and assessing the relevance and context of sources.

Waypoints

  • 1. Publication details and content evaluationHingeStudents can examine a source's publishing details and content to clarify its purpose, distinguish fact from opinion, identify potential bias, assess its relevance, and contextualise its information.
  • 2. Identifying persuasive techniquesStudents can identify persuasive techniques in sources, including modality language and passive voice, and explain why writers use these techniques.
  • 3. Discussing contested perspectives and proposing reasonsStudents can discuss issues explored in sources where there were or are a range of views, and propose reasons for why perspectives differ.
  • 4. Integrated source evaluation across a Year 6 inquiryStudents can evaluate a range of primary and secondary sources for origin, purpose and perspectives, applying persuasive technique analysis and multi-criteria evaluation to assess each source's usefulness.
