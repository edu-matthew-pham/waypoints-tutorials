Roles of government

AC9HS6K07 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the roles and responsibilities of the 3 levels of government in Australia

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS4K08 (Local government and services): Students explain that local government provides specific services to communities. From AC9HS5K06 (Democracy, elections and representatives): Students know that elected representatives exist at different levels of government.

By the end

Students explain the roles and responsibilities of the three levels of government in Australia — local, state/territory and federal — and understand how their responsibilities interact and sometimes overlap.

Waypoints

  • 1. Three levels and their primary responsibilitiesHingeStudents can name the three levels of government and describe the primary roles and responsibilities of each level.
  • 2. Overlapping powers and constitutional hierarchyStudents can identify issues where multiple levels of government have overlapping powers, and explain that federal law overrides state law when they conflict on these issues.
  • 3. How new laws originateStudents can explain how new laws originate in Australia, including from party policy, member or senator proposals, and interest group advocacy.
  • 4. The three-level system in practiceStudents can explain the roles and responsibilities of the three levels of government, identify where responsibilities interact or overlap, and give examples of how each level makes and enforces laws in their community.
