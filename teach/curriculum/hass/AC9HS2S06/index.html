Develop narratives and share observations

AC9HS2S06 · Year 2 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop narratives and share observations, using sources, and subject-specific terms

Prior knowledge

From Y1 (AC9HS1S06): Students developed historical narratives using sources and time sequence vocabulary.

By the end

Students develop narratives and share observations, using sources and subject-specific terms for time and place.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • developing narratives drawing on ideas in sources, such as a comparison of past and present daily life
  • sharing observations using sources such as how access to and use of a place has changed over time
  • sharing with their teacher, other students and members of their family what they know about the past, using terms in speech and writing to denote the passing of time (for example, "in the past", "years ago", "the olden days", "in the future") and to describe direction and location of a place (for example, "north", "south", "opposite", "near", "far")

Waypoints

  • 1. Subject-specific vocabulary for time and placeHingeStudents can use time terms (in the past, years ago, the olden days, in the future) and direction/location terms (north, south, opposite, near, far) accurately in HASS communication.
  • 2. Developing narratives from sourcesStudents can develop narratives drawing on ideas from sources and share observations about how access to and use of a place has changed over time.
