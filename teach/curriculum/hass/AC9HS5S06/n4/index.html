← AC9HS5S06 Assess effects using criteria

Integrated action proposal and effects assessment

AC9HS5S06 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can propose an action or response to a HASS issue or challenge and use a set of criteria to systematically assess its possible effects, drawing on evidence and disciplinary understanding.

Success criteria

  • Students propose a specific action in response to a HASS issue.
  • Students apply at least two criteria to assess the possible effects of their proposed action.
  • Students acknowledge potential unintended effects or limitations of their proposed action.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Forecasting probable and preferred futures.

Width

Minimum
Propose one action for a HASS issue and use at least two criteria to assess its possible effects.
Typical
Propose a coherent action or response to a HASS issue; use a defined set of criteria (economic, environmental, social, health) to systematically assess possible effects; draw on evidence from inquiry to support the assessment; acknowledge that different criteria may lead to different conclusions.
Wider
  • Participate in a democratic decision-making process (e.g. class vote, mock community consultation) about a proposed action, and evaluate the outcome using the criteria developed
  • Evaluate how effectively a past challenge was addressed (e.g. a colonial or environmental challenge) using the same criteria framework, and assess whether the response achieved its goals

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe integrated proposal brings together issue identification, criteria selection, futures thinking and evidence-based assessment. Use a structured proposal format (issue → action → criteria assessment → recommendation) to support synthesis.
