Managing environments and hazards

AC9HS5K05 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the management of Australian environments, including managing severe weather events such as bushfires, floods, droughts or cyclones, and their consequences

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS4K06 (Resource management and sustainability): Students explain why environments are important and how communities manage resources for sustainability. Students understand that environments can be affected by human actions.

By the end

Students explain how Australian environments are managed, including the management of severe weather events such as bushfires, floods, droughts and cyclones, and evaluate the consequences of both events and management responses.

Waypoints

  • 1. How environments are used and managedHingeStudents can explain how Australian environments are managed through practices and laws designed to balance human use with environmental protection.
  • 2. Types of severe weather eventsStudents can describe the types of severe weather events that affect Australian environments — bushfires, floods, droughts and cyclones — and explain their causes and typical locations.
  • 3. Management strategies for hazardsStudents can explain how communities and governments manage the risks of severe weather events through preparation, mitigation and response strategies.
  • 4. Consequences and evaluating responsesStudents can explain the consequences of severe weather events on communities and environments, and evaluate how effectively management approaches reduce those consequences.
