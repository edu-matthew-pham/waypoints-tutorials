How local government is chosen and what it does

AC9HS4K08 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 4 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can describe how local government is chosen and explain the range of services it provides.

Success criteria

  • Describes how local government is chosen
  • Names at least four services provided by local government

Prior knowledge

From Y3 (AC9HS3K07): Students explored why people participate in communities and how they can contribute. From Y3 (AC9HS3K06): Students explored rule-making authority.

Width

Minimum
Describe how local government is chosen and list at least four services it provides.
Typical
Examine how local government is chosen and by whom; explore what local government does — environment and waste management, libraries, health, parks, cultural events, pools, sports facilities, arts and pet management.
Wider
  • Research the specific services provided by the local council in the school's area and compare with another council
  • Investigate how local councillors are elected and what their responsibilities are

Hinge

Students need to know what local government does before they can explain how community members use and contribute to those services.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents need a comprehensive picture of local government services before they can analyse community use and contribution patterns.
