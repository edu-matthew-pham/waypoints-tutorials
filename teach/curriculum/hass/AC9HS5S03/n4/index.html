← AC9HS5S03 Evaluate patterns and trends

Evaluating information to identify patterns and infer relationships

AC9HS5S03 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate information and data from multiple formats to identify and describe patterns and trends, and infer relationships, explaining how the evidence supports their analysis.

Success criteria

  • Students identify at least one pattern or trend from information in multiple formats.
  • Students infer at least one relationship and explain what evidence supports it.
  • Students use appropriate analytical language (e.g. 'pattern', 'trend', 'relationship', 'suggests').

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Inferring relationships from visual and written sources.

Width

Minimum
Identify a pattern or trend and infer one relationship from information in at least two different formats.
Typical
Evaluate information and data from a range of formats (graphs, maps, written sources, photographs) to identify and describe patterns and trends across a HASS topic, and infer relationships between phenomena with reference to supporting evidence.
Wider
  • Evaluate the reliability of an inferred relationship by checking it against additional sources — and revise the inference where evidence is contradictory
  • Analyse data showing patterns in a current HASS issue (e.g. environmental change, migration trends, voting patterns) and explain what the patterns reveal about the underlying relationships

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe integration challenge is moving fluidly across data formats to build a multi-source analytical account — not just reading each source in isolation but drawing on several to construct a coherent analysis.
