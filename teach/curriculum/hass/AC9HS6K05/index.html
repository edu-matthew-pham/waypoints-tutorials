Australia's international interconnections

AC9HS6K05 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Australia's interconnections with other countries and how these change people and places

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5K04 (People shape places): Students understand how people influence place characteristics. From AC9HS6K04 (Asia region diversity and location): Students have studied the Asia region's geography and location.

By the end

Students explain Australia's interconnections with other countries, particularly in the Asia and Pacific regions, and describe how these interconnections change people and places.

Waypoints

  • 1. Types of international interconnectionsHingeStudents can identify and describe the types of interconnections Australia has with other countries, including migration, trade, tourism, aid, education, defence and cultural influences.
  • 2. How interconnections change people and placesStudents can explain how at least one of Australia's international interconnections changes people and places, both in Australia and in connected countries.
  • 3. Geographical divisions and Australia's positionStudents can identify the geographical divisions of the world including the Asia and Pacific regions, and explain Australia's position within these divisions.
  • 4. Evaluating interconnections and their effectsStudents can explain Australia's interconnections with other countries and evaluate how these interconnections change people and places, considering different perspectives on these effects.
