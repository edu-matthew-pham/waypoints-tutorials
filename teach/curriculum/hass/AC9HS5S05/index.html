Develop evidence-based conclusions

AC9HS5S05 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop evidence-based conclusions

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS4S05 (Conclude from analysis): Students draw basic conclusions from their analysis of information. Students can state what information shows but may not yet explicitly ground conclusions in cited evidence.

By the end

Students develop evidence-based conclusions that reflect their inquiry questions, understanding of disciplinary concepts, and evaluation of primary and secondary sources.

Waypoints

  • 1. What makes a conclusion evidence-based?HingeStudents can explain the difference between a conclusion based on evidence and a conclusion based on personal preference, limited sources, or unsupported opinion.
  • 2. Identifying valid evidenceStudents can identify valid evidence from primary and secondary sources that can be used to form a conclusion, explaining why some evidence is more valid than other evidence for a specific inquiry purpose.
  • 3. Forming conclusions from evidenceStudents can draw conclusions from identified evidence that consider differing information, reflect disciplinary concepts, and address their inquiry question.
  • 4. Evaluating the inquiry and conclusionsStudents can consider how the sources they used affected the validity of their conclusions, and identify how additional or different sources would improve the inquiry.
