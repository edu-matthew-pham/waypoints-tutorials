Identifying community changes

AC9HS3K01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 3 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify changes to the local community over time by examining sources such as photographs, newspapers and oral histories.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two changes to the local community
  • Uses at least two source types as evidence

Prior knowledge

From Y2 (AC9HS2K01-K02): Students explained local significance and how technological developments changed daily life. They understand continuity and change and source-based investigation.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two changes to the local community using at least two different source types.
Typical
Explore photographs, newspapers, oral histories, diaries and letters to investigate how aspects of the local community (transport, environment, technology) have changed; compare photographs from past and present of specific locations to identify change and continuity.
Wider
  • Investigate how an aspect of local community life changed from the time of European settlement to the present day
  • Compare change rates across different aspects of the community (natural environment vs built environment vs social life)

Hinge

Students must be able to identify specific changes before analysing their causes and effects — naming the change is prerequisite to explaining it.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.
