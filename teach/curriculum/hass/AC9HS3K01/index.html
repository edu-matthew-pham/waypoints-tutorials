Local community change and diversity

AC9HS3K01 · Year 3 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

causes and effects of changes to the local community, and how people who may be from diverse backgrounds have contributed to these changes

Prior knowledge

From Y2 (AC9HS2K01-K02): Students explained local significance and how technological developments changed daily life. They understand continuity and change and source-based investigation.

By the end

Students explain causes and effects of changes to the local community and describe how people from diverse backgrounds have contributed to those changes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring photographs, newspapers, oral histories, diaries and letters to investigate how an aspect of life in the local community, such as transport, entertainment, the natural and built environment, and technologies, has changed over time; for example, from the time of European settlement to the present day
  • comparing photographs from the past and present of a specific location to identify the change or continuity (similarities and differences over time) associated with people, events or developments, places or ecosystems
  • identifying individuals and groups of diverse backgrounds from the past, who have contributed to the community's economic, social, cultural, civic and environmental development and character
  • investigating reasons for change in the local community, why change was brought about and what happened because of it; for example, the change in use of a building, wasteland turning to wetland

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying community changesHingeStudents can identify changes to the local community over time by examining sources such as photographs, newspapers and oral histories.
  • 2. Causes and effects of changeHingeStudents can explain why a change happened in the local community and what happened as a result.
  • 3. Diverse contributors to community changeStudents can identify individuals and groups from diverse backgrounds who have contributed to the community's development and describe their contribution.
