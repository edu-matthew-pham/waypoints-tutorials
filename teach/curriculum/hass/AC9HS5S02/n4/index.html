← AC9HS5S02 Organise primary and secondary sources

Integrating multiple sources and formats

AC9HS5S02 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can locate, collect and organise information from multiple primary and secondary sources in a range of formats, selecting appropriate organisational tools and conventions for the inquiry purpose.

Success criteria

  • Students collect information from at least one primary and one secondary source.
  • Students organise collected information using an appropriate tool and apply conventions correctly.
  • Students explain why they selected the organisational tools and formats they used.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Constructing maps, timelines, tables and graphs.

Width

Minimum
Collect information from at least two source types (one primary, one secondary) and organise it using appropriate tools for the inquiry purpose.
Typical
Locate, collect and organise information and data from a range of primary and secondary sources in multiple formats (including geospatial tools, surveys, digital and non-digital sources), selecting appropriate organisational tools and applying relevant conventions.
Wider
  • Conduct fieldwork or a survey to gather primary data, then organise and integrate this data with secondary source findings to produce a multi-source investigation
  • Evaluate the completeness of a source collection for an inquiry — identify what types of sources are missing and explain how adding them would improve the investigation

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThe integration challenge is coordinating multiple source types and formats into a coherent organised collection — students need to make deliberate decisions about which tool organises which information and why.
