Organise information and data

AC9HS6S02 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate, collect and organise information and data from primary and secondary sources in a range of formats

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5S02 (Organise primary and secondary sources): Students can locate, collect and organise information from primary and secondary sources using a range of graphic organisers, maps, timelines and graphs with appropriate conventions.

By the end

Students locate, collect and organise information and data from primary and secondary sources in a range of formats, using appropriate methods including digital tools, fieldwork, surveys and spatial technologies.

Waypoints

  • 1. Selecting appropriate methods for collecting informationHingeStudents can determine the most appropriate method for finding information for a specific inquiry purpose, selecting from personal observation, interviews, surveys, internet searches, census data, and primary and secondary sources.
  • 2. Evaluating sources and identifying key wordsStudents can use key words to search for relevant information and evaluate sources, recognising that domain names (com, edu, gov) indicate source provenance.
  • 3. Creating maps and spatial representationsStudents can create maps using spatial technologies and cartographic conventions appropriate to the inquiry purpose, including flow maps and large-scale location maps.
  • 4. Organising multi-source information for a Year 6 inquiryStudents can locate, collect and organise information and data from multiple primary and secondary sources in a range of formats, selecting appropriate tools and applying relevant conventions.
