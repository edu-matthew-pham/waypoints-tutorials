← AC9HS6S07 Communicate findings and viewpoints

Composing informative and persuasive HASS texts

AC9HS6S07 nnull · waypoint null of 2 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students compose informative and/or persuasive descriptions and explanations supported by evidence, using relevant HASS disciplinary terms and conventions, to present conclusions from their inquiry.

Success criteria

  • Text is clearly informative or persuasive in purpose and structure.
  • Uses at least three discipline-appropriate terms accurately.
  • Conclusions drawn are supported by referenced evidence from sources.
  • Conventions of the chosen text type are applied consistently.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint null: Selecting, referencing and integrating source material.

Width

Minimum
Compose an informative text presenting conclusions from a HASS inquiry, using discipline-appropriate terms and at least two referenced sources.
Typical
Compose both informative and persuasive texts that present inquiry conclusions using accurate disciplinary terminology (historical, geographical, civics, economics), appropriate conventions (e.g. argument structure, referencing), and evidence drawn from a range of identified sources including conflicting viewpoints.
Wider
  • Compose a persuasive text that presents a reasoned position on a contested HASS issue, explicitly addressing counter-arguments with evidence.
  • Produce a multimodal presentation (e.g. documentary-style, oral argument with visual evidence) that applies conventions appropriate to the chosen form while maintaining evidentiary rigour.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepWider students can compose texts across multiple inquiry disciplines (historical argument, geographical explanation, civic position paper), each applying the same communication skill in a different disciplinary register.
Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyComposing persuasive text with counter-argument and multimodal presentation builds a communication repertoire that is only demonstrable through the produced work — not separable from the task.
