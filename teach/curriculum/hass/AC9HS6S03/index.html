Identify patterns and relationships

AC9HS6S03 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate information and data in a range of formats to identify and describe patterns and trends, or to infer relationships

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5S03 (Evaluate patterns and trends): Students can identify patterns and trends in information and data across a range of formats, and infer relationships. Students can read and analyse multiple graph types.

By the end

Students evaluate information and data in a range of formats to identify and describe patterns and trends, and infer cause-effect relationships, including across historical, geographic and social domains.

Waypoints

  • 1. Causes, effects and significance in historical dataHingeStudents can examine sources to identify the causes, effects and significance of past events and developments, using evidence to support their analysis.
  • 2. Identifying patterns using graphic organisers and mapsStudents can use graphic organisers, maps and concept maps to identify patterns, trends and cause-effect relationships in HASS data.
  • 3. Evaluating past attitudes and proposing reasons for changeStudents can evaluate attitudes and actions of the past that seem strange or unacceptable by contemporary standards, and propose reasons why values and social norms change over time.
  • 4. Evaluating data to identify patterns and infer relationshipsStudents can evaluate information and data from multiple formats to identify patterns and trends, and infer cause-effect relationships, using evidence to support their analysis.
