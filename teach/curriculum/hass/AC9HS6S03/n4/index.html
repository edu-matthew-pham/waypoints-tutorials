← AC9HS6S03 Identify patterns and relationships

Evaluating data to identify patterns and infer relationships

AC9HS6S03 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate information and data from multiple formats to identify patterns and trends, and infer cause-effect relationships, using evidence to support their analysis.

Success criteria

  • Students identify at least one pattern and infer at least one cause-effect relationship from evidence.
  • Students support their analysis with reference to at least two sources or data formats.
  • Students acknowledge ambiguity or limitations in the evidence where appropriate.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Evaluating past attitudes and proposing reasons for change.

Width

Minimum
Identify a pattern or trend and infer a cause-effect relationship from information in at least two formats.
Typical
Evaluate information and data from a range of formats (maps, charts, written sources, graphic organisers, historical sources) to identify patterns, trends and cause-effect relationships across Year 6 HASS topics; support inferences with evidence; acknowledge where evidence is ambiguous or incomplete.
Wider
  • Evaluate competing explanations for a historical pattern and assess which has stronger evidential support
  • Propose reasons why socially sustainable practices (negotiation, arbitration, reconciliation, cultural mediation) resolve issues peacefully — drawing on patterns identified from historical examples

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierIntegrated pattern analysis across formats and topics requires students to bring together data literacy, historical thinking and cause-effect reasoning. Support with a structured analysis protocol.
