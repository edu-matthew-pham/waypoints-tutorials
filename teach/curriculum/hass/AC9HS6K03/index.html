Migration and Australian society

AC9HS6K03 · Year 6 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the motivation of people migrating to Australia since Federation and throughout the 20th century, their stories and effects on Australian society, including migrants from the Asia region

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS5K03 (Individuals and groups in colonial development): Students understand the contributions and experiences of different groups in colonial Australia, including some migrant groups. Students understand historical significance and multi-perspective analysis.

By the end

Students explain the motivations of people who migrated to Australia since Federation and throughout the 20th century, describe their stories and experiences, and assess the effects of migration on Australian society, including contributions of migrants from the Asia region.

Waypoints

  • 1. Push and pull factors for migrationHingeStudents can explain the push and pull factors that have motivated people to migrate to Australia since Federation, including economic factors, conflict and political refuge.
  • 2. Stories and experiences of migrantsStudents can describe the journeys and experiences of migrants to Australia using primary sources including letters, documents, oral histories and objects, explaining the circumstances of arrival and early settlement.
  • 3. Effects of migration on Australian societyStudents can explain the effects of migration on Australian society, including cultural changes, economic contributions and social impacts, with specific reference to contributions from migrant groups including those from the Asia region.
  • 4. Migration as a shaping force in Australian historyStudents can explain how the motivations, stories and effects of migration since Federation have shaped Australian society, assessing migration's role as a significant force in Australia's social and cultural development.
