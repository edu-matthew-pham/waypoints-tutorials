Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country

AC9HS2K04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 2 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain when Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country are used and why they respectfully recognise Country/Place, Traditional Owners and Custodians.

Success criteria

  • Explains the purpose of Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country
  • Correctly states when each is used (Acknowledgement by anyone; Welcome by Traditional Owner)

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Language, Country/Place and spirituality.

Width

Minimum
Explain the purpose of Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country and when each is used.
Typical
Discuss when to use Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country at ceremonies and events; explain how these practices respectfully recognise the Country/Place and Traditional Owners and Custodians of the land, sea, waterways and sky.
Wider
  • Write or participate in an Acknowledgement of Country for the school that reflects genuine understanding of the local Country/Place and language group
  • Explore how protocols around Acknowledgement of Country differ between communities and discuss why those differences exist

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierAcknowledgement and Welcome to Country practices integrate language, Country/Place and interconnection knowledge into a civic act — the integration of understanding into practice.
