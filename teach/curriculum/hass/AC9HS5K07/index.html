Citizens working for civic goals

AC9HS5K07 · Year 5 HASS (Foundation to Year 6) · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in HASS (Foundation to Year 6): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

how citizens (members of communities) with shared beliefs and values work together to achieve a civic goal

Prior knowledge

From AC9HS4K09 (Community identity and diversity): Students explain how communities are diverse and how people within communities have shared identities and belonging. Students understand that community groups serve different purposes.

By the end

Students explain how citizens with shared beliefs and values work together to achieve a civic goal, giving examples of community groups that pursue shared goals and evaluating the importance of civic participation.

Waypoints

  • 1. What is a civic goal?HingeStudents can explain the concept of a civic goal — a shared aim that benefits a community or society — and distinguish civic goals from personal or commercial goals.
  • 2. How citizens organise and work togetherStudents can explain how citizens with shared beliefs and values organise themselves into groups and work collectively to pursue civic goals, including through volunteering and community organisations.
  • 3. Why citizens participate in civic lifeStudents can explain why citizens choose to participate in civic life and community groups, including the connection between shared beliefs and values and the motivation to act for community benefit.
  • 4. Evaluating civic participationStudents can evaluate the importance of civic participation in a democratic society, explaining how citizens working together for civic goals contributes to community wellbeing and democratic health.
