Evaluating future impact and unintended consequences

AC9TDI10P10 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 9–10 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate a digital solution's possible future impact, including unintended consequences, and explain how design choices affect downstream outcomes.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one unintended consequence of the solution
  • Connects the consequence to a specific design decision or data choice
  • Suggests at least one change that would mitigate the identified consequence

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Evaluating against design criteria and user stories.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one possible unintended consequence of a digital solution and explain how the design decision contributed to it.
Typical
Evaluate a digital solution's future impact — including intended benefits, possible unintended consequences, and how the design could be changed to mitigate negative impacts; consider impacts on diverse user populations.
Wider
  • Examine how AI image generation solutions trained on homogeneous data produce outputs that fail to represent diverse users — explain the training data decision that caused this and what would mitigate it
  • Analyse the complexities of training predictive models on low-probability events (e.g. autonomous vehicle edge cases) and evaluate how solution design should account for these
  • Evaluate a solution's data privacy implications over time — what data is collected, retained, and what could be done with it beyond the original purpose

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepUnintended consequences are abstract without real-world cases. AI bias, autonomous vehicle edge cases, and privacy creep each illuminate a different category of future impact — together they build a more complete framework for impact assessment than any single example.
