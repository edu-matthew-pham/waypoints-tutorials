Evaluating for enterprise and pivot opportunities

AC9TDI10P10 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 9–10 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate how existing products have met the needs of different user groups and identify opportunities for enterprise or solution adaptation.

Success criteria

  • Identifies a specific product adaptation and the user need it addressed
  • Explains what changed in the product to serve the new user group

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Evaluating future impact and unintended consequences.

Width

Minimum
Describe one example of a digital product that has adapted to meet the needs of a different user group; explain what changed and what user need it addressed.
Typical
Evaluate how existing products have pivoted to meet the needs of different users (e.g. how social media applications have expanded the types of media and interactions); identify what made the pivot possible and what new user needs it addressed.
Wider
  • Identify an unmet user need in an existing product category and propose how a solution could be adapted or extended to address it — evaluate the feasibility of the adaptation
  • Examine how platforms that began with one user group have expanded (positively or negatively) to serve others — evaluate the design changes that enabled this
