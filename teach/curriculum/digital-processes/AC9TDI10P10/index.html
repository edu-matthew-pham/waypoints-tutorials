Evaluate solutions against criteria and future impact

AC9TDI10P10 · Years 9–10 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate existing and student solutions against the design criteria, user stories, possible future impact and opportunities for enterprise

Prior knowledge

Students can evaluate existing and student solutions against the design criteria, user stories and sustainability (AC9TDI8P10). They have experience applying criteria-based evaluation. They have not yet evaluated for future impact, unintended consequences of AI systems, or enterprise/pivot opportunities.

By the end

Students evaluate existing and student solutions against design criteria, user stories, possible future impact (including unintended consequences) and opportunities for enterprise.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • evaluating how an existing solution detects violations of site rules and imposes sanctions as described in the Safety by Design Vision for Young People
  • judging the quality of the output of their solution against the design criteria
  • evaluating how existing products have pivoted to meet the needs of a different set of users
  • examining the unintended consequences of an image generation solution implemented using AI; for example, using a model trained on a homogenous population to generate graphics that do not represent the diversity of customers
  • considering the complexities associated with training predictive models to capture events that occur with low probability

Waypoints

  • 1. Evaluating against design criteria and user storiesHingeStudents can evaluate a digital solution by judging its quality against specific design criteria and user stories.
  • 2. Evaluating future impact and unintended consequencesStudents can evaluate a digital solution's possible future impact, including unintended consequences, and explain how design choices affect downstream outcomes.
  • 3. Evaluating for enterprise and pivot opportunitiesStudents can evaluate how existing products have met the needs of different user groups and identify opportunities for enterprise or solution adaptation.
