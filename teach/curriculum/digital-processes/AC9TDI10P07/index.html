Design and prototype user experience

AC9TDI10P07 · Years 9–10 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

design and prototype the user experience of a digital system

Prior knowledge

Students can design the user experience of a digital system, including interface mock-ups and considering user needs (AC9TDI8P07). They have experience with digital design tools. They have not yet prototyped an interactive user experience with clickable flows, or considered all dimensions of UX including emotional, cultural and onboarding experiences.

By the end

Students design and prototype the user experience of a digital system, including interactive prototypes with clickable flows and consideration of aesthetics, functionality, accessibility, and emotional or cultural user responses.

Waypoints

  • 1. UX as more than interface — all dimensions of user experienceHingeStudents can describe what user experience means across its full scope — aesthetics, functionality, onboarding, emotional and cultural responses.
  • 2. Designing for aesthetics, functionality and enjoymentHingeStudents can design a user experience that integrates aesthetic, functional and enjoyment considerations into a coherent interface.
  • 3. Building an interactive prototypeStudents can prototype a user experience with interactive elements that simulate clickable flows between screens or views.
