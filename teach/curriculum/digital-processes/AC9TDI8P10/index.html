Evaluate against future impact

AC9TDI8P10 · Years 7–8 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Evaluate existing and student solutions against the design criteria, user stories and possible future impact

Prior knowledge

Students can evaluate solutions against design criteria, user stories and broader community impact (AC9TDI6P06). They have evaluated whether solutions meet criteria, but have not yet considered possible future impact across economic, environmental, or social dimensions, or evaluated for safety and misinformation risks.

By the end

Students evaluate existing and student solutions against design criteria and user stories, and analyse the possible future impact of solutions across economic, environmental and social dimensions, including safety, misinformation and sustainability considerations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • evaluating how an existing solution ensures users can control their safety and experience online as described in the Safety by Design Vision for Young People, for example ensuring privacy settings are comprehensive, easy to use and set to maximum protection by default
  • reviewing the requirements of a user story to ensure that their solution meets the user's needs, for example making sure that recommendations offered by their music application are of a similar genre to the rest of the user's library
  • judging existing solutions on the basis of their possible impact on the economy, environment or society, for example cloud computing services decrease data loss but require vast amounts of electricity to power the servers
  • discussing the risks and consequences of AI-generated content on social media platforms; for example, the potential for the spread of misinformation due to high volumes of automatically generated and intentionally misleading content being posted

Waypoints

  • 1. Evaluating existing solutions against criteriaHingeStudents can evaluate existing digital solutions against design criteria and user stories to inform their own design decisions.
  • 2. Analysing impact of existing solutionsStudents can analyse the possible future impact of existing digital solutions across economic, environmental and social dimensions to inform their own design.
  • 3. Safety, misinformation and ethical risksStudents can evaluate digital solutions for safety risks, misinformation potential and ethical concerns, and assess whether solutions protect users appropriately
  • 4. Evaluating your solution against criteriaStudents can systematically evaluate their own digital solution against design criteria and user stories, identifying where it meets, partially meets or fails to meet requirements.
  • 5. Analysing impact of your solutionStudents can analyse the possible future impact of their own digital solution across economic, environmental and social dimensions.
