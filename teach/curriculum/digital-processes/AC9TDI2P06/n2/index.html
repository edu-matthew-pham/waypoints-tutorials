Accessing a school account with recorded credentials

AC9TDI2P06 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Years 1–2 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use a recorded username and password to access their school account

Success criteria

  • Successfully logs into the school system using their recorded credentials
  • Enters the username and password in the correct fields

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: What accounts and credentials are.

Width

Minimum
Use a recorded username and password (e.g. from a card given by the teacher) to log into a school digital system
Typical
Access a school account using recorded credentials; locate the login screen; enter the username and password accurately; access their personal account area
Wider
  • Limited width — this is a narrow procedural skill. The genuine extension is understanding why credentials are private and personal (covered in node 1 and in AC9TDI2P07).

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsAccurate credential entry is a procedural skill requiring motor precision — distinguishing username from password fields, entering characters correctly, locating the login screen. Students need practice to build fluency. Consolidation is the appropriate response when errors are procedural (wrong field, typos, forgotten steps) rather than conceptual.
