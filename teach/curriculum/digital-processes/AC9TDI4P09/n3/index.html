Risks associated with personal data online

AC9TDI4P09 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Years 3–4 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can discuss at least two risks associated with personal data stored or shared in online accounts

Success criteria

  • Names at least one specific risk associated with personal data online
  • Explains who could be affected by the risk

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Digital identity from online accounts.

Width

Minimum
Name one risk associated with personal data being stored or visible online and explain who might be affected
Typical
Discuss risks associated with personal data in online accounts — including unwanted access, identity information being visible to unknown people, and data revealing location or habits; explain how these risks affect real users
Wider
  • Discuss how location data shared in online accounts could create safety risks — connect to the scenario of photographs revealing home or school location
  • Investigate how data shared for one purpose can create unexpected risks in another context — for example, a gaming account with location data being visible to other players

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents who understand risk in one context (e.g. a stranger knowing your address) often miss how the same risk emerges from different data types (e.g. a photo timestamp revealing your school). Multiple risk contexts build a more complete and transferable understanding.
