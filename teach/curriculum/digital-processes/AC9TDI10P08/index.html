Generate, modify and critically evaluate alternative designs

AC9TDI10P08 · Years 9–10 Digital Technologies (processes) · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Digital Technologies (processes): treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

generate, modify, communicate and critically evaluate alternative designs

Prior knowledge

Students can generate, modify, communicate and evaluate alternative designs (AC9TDI8P08). They have experience with ideation techniques and design iteration. They have not yet critically evaluated designs against design criteria and user stories, or used generative AI outputs as ideation input.

By the end

Students generate multiple design ideas using a range of ideation techniques, modify and evaluate them critically against design criteria and user stories, and communicate the preferred design with justification.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • eliminating design ideas by evaluating them against the design criteria and user stories, for example in consultation with stakeholders, reviewing the design ideas, making modifications if necessary, and further developing the design of the preferred solution
  • using a range of ideation techniques to create multiple design ideas for a solution, for example using graphic organisers, role-play and mind mapping to develop and then record a range of ideas without evaluating them first
  • combining the output from generative AI models and human capital from recognised experts to meet a specific need; for example, using a range of outputs from an image generator as inspiration for modelling a 3D character in a game

Waypoints

  • 1. Generating multiple design ideasStudents can use ideation techniques to generate multiple design ideas without prematurely evaluating them.
  • 2. Critically evaluating designs against criteria and user storiesHingeStudents can evaluate design ideas critically by comparing them against design criteria and user stories to identify which ideas meet requirements and which do not.
  • 3. Modifying and developing the preferred designStudents can modify a design based on evaluation findings, in consultation with stakeholders, and further develop the preferred design toward implementation.
  • 4. Communicating the designStudents can communicate the preferred design and its rationale clearly to stakeholders.
