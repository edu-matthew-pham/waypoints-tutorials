Nets of prisms and other 3D objects

AC9M7SP01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 7 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students identify, draw and construct nets of prisms and other common 3D objects.

Lessons on this site

  • Nets

Success criteria

  • Correctly identifies valid nets for a cube and rectangular prism.
  • Draws a net that folds correctly to produce a given 3D object.
  • Does not include overlapping faces in a net.

Prior knowledge

Students can compare parallel cross-sections and recognise their relationship to right prisms (from AC9M6SP01). Students have informal experience with drawings of 3D objects.

Width

Minimum
Identify which nets will fold to make a cube or rectangular prism; draw a net for a triangular prism.
Typical
Test multiple possible nets for the same object; identify which are valid; construct 3D objects from nets.
Wider
  • Investigate how many valid nets a cube has (11) — systematically find them all.
  • Design and construct nets for less familiar solids (square pyramid, cylinder).

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents develop spatial reasoning for nets by working with multiple 3D objects. Restricting practice to cubes gives a false sense of mastery that fails with other prisms.
