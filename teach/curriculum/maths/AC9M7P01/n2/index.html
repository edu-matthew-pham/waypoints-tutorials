Assigning probabilities to outcomes

AC9M7P01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 7 Maths · Probability

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students assign theoretical probabilities to outcomes of a single-stage event using P = favourable/total and express these as fractions, decimals or percentages.

Lessons on this site

  • Sample spaces and probability

Success criteria

  • Correctly assigns the probability to each outcome in a sample space.
  • Verifies that all probabilities sum to 1.
  • Expresses probability in the most appropriate form for the context.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Identifying and listing sample spaces.

Width

Minimum
Assign the probability of each outcome for a fair die roll; express as a fraction.
Typical
Assign probabilities for events with unequal outcomes (e.g. spinners with unequal sectors); express in fraction, decimal and percentage forms; verify probabilities sum to 1.
Wider
  • Investigate non-fair devices: if a spinner has sectors of 1/4, 1/4, and 1/2, assign probabilities and verify.
  • Connect probability fractions to relative frequency: a probability of 1/6 means in the long run about 1 in every 6 outcomes.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents often believe probability only applies to fair devices. Encountering non-fair devices (biased coins, unequal spinners) generalises their understanding.
