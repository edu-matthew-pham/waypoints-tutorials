Multiplication facts to 10×10

AC9M4A02 · Year 4 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Recall and demonstrate proficiency with multiplication facts up to 10 x 10 and related division facts; extend and apply facts to develop efficient mental strategies for computation with larger numbers without a calculator

Prior knowledge

Students can recall multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 and apply to develop related division facts (AC9M3A03). They have not yet extended this to all facts up to 10×10, and have not yet applied known facts to derive efficient strategies for multi-digit computation.

By the end

Students recall all multiplication facts up to 10×10 and related division facts, and apply these to develop efficient mental strategies for computation with larger numbers.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using arrays on grid paper to develop, represent and explain patterns in the 10×10 multiplication facts
  • using materials or diagrams to develop and record multiplication strategies such as doubling, halving, commutativity
  • using known multiplication facts for 2, 3, 5 and 10 to establish multiplication facts for 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9
  • using arrays and known multiplication facts for twos and fives to develop the multiplication facts for sevens, applying the distributive property
  • using known multiplication facts and the inverse relationship to establish division facts
  • designing, creating and playing instructive card games involving recall of multiplication facts

Waypoints

  • 1. Patterns in the multiplication tableStudents can identify and explain patterns in the 10×10 multiplication table using arrays.
  • 2. Derivation strategiesStudents can derive unknown multiplication facts from known facts using doubling, halving, and adding/subtracting one group.
  • 3. Recall fluencyHingeStudents can recall multiplication and division facts up to 10×10 with increasing automaticity.
  • 4. Apply to larger numbersStudents can extend and apply multiplication and division facts to compute with larger numbers mentally.
