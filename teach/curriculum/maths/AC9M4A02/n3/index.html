← AC9M4A02 Multiplication facts to 10×10

Recall fluency

AC9M4A02 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Year 4 Maths · Algebra · Hinge

Checkpoint

Students can recall multiplication and division facts up to 10×10 with increasing automaticity.

Success criteria

  • Students can recall multiplication facts up to 10×10 without needing to derive each one
  • Students can quickly state the related division fact for any multiplication fact they know

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Derivation strategies.

Width

Minimum
Recall all facts for two or more fact families (e.g. 4s and 8s) with reasonable speed; identify related division facts.
Typical
Demonstrate increasing automaticity with all multiplication facts up to 10×10 and their related division facts; use varied retrieval practice (games, timed recall, mixed fact families).
Wider
  • Design an instructive card game that embeds multiplication and division fact recall in a game format — drawing on game design principles to make retrieval practice engaging
  • Investigate which fact families students find hardest to recall and use targeted strategies to address those specifically

Hinge

Students who cannot recall facts with automaticity must use working memory for recall during Node 4's extension tasks — applying facts to larger numbers — leaving insufficient capacity to track the place-value reasoning simultaneously. Fluency at this node is a prerequisite for efficient mental computation.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsAutomaticity requires repeated retrieval practice distributed over time. This is a consolidation-heavy node — fluency does not come from understanding alone, and consistent low-stakes practice is the primary intervention for students who are slow but accurate.
