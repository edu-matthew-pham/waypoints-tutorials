Estimation and rounding

AC9M4N07 · Year 4 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Choose and use estimation and rounding to check and explain the reasonableness of calculations including the results of financial transactions

Prior knowledge

Students can estimate quantities in collections and assess reasonableness of calculations (AC9M3N05). They have not yet applied rounding systematically to calculations or used estimation in financial contexts.

By the end

Students use rounding and estimation to check the reasonableness of calculations and financial transactions, explaining whether an estimate is above or below the actual answer and why.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using proficiency with basic facts to estimate the result of a calculation and say what amounts the answer will be between
  • using rounded amounts to complete an estimated budget for a shopping trip or an excursion
  • recognising the effect of rounding in addition and multiplication calculations; rounding both numbers up, both numbers down and one number up and one number down

Waypoints

  • 1. Rounding to convenient numbersStudents can round numbers to the nearest 10 or 100 and explain the rounding rule.
  • 2. Estimation as a checkStudents can use estimation to determine what range a calculation answer should fall within, and identify unreasonable answers.
  • 3. Estimation in financial contextsStudents can apply estimation and rounding to financial transactions and explain their reasoning.
