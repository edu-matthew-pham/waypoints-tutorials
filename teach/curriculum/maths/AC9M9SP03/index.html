Geometric algorithms

AC9M9SP03 · Year 9 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

design, test and refine algorithms involving a sequence of steps and decisions based on geometric constructions and theorems; discuss and evaluate refinements

Prior knowledge

Students can apply the enlargement transformation and identify invariant properties of similar shapes (AC9M9SP02). They have not yet designed and iteratively refined algorithms grounded in geometric constructions and theorems.

By the end

Students design, test and refine algorithms based on geometric constructions and theorems, representing them using pseudocode or flowcharts and evaluating refinements.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating an algorithm using pseudocode or flow charts to apply the triangle inequality, or to generate Pythagorean triples
  • creating and testing algorithms to construct or bisect angles
  • developing an algorithm for an animation of a geometric construction or visual proof; evaluating using test cases

Waypoints

  • 1. Representing a geometric process as an algorithmStudents can represent a known geometric construction or theorem application as a step-by-step algorithm using pseudocode or a flowchart.
  • 2. Testing an algorithm with casesHingeStudents can test an algorithm using specific inputs, identify failure cases, and describe what the algorithm does correctly and incorrectly.
  • 3. Refining and evaluating the algorithmStudents can refine an algorithm based on test results and evaluate the refinement, explaining what changed and why it improves the algorithm.
