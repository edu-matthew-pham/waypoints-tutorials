← AC9M7M01 Area of triangles and parallelograms

Applying area formulas to triangles and parallelograms

AC9M7M01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 7 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students calculate the area of triangles and parallelograms using established formulas, selecting appropriate units.

Lessons on this site

  • Area problems

Success criteria

  • Correctly calculates the area of a triangle using A = ½bh.
  • Correctly calculates the area of a parallelogram.
  • Uses appropriate units and converts if necessary.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Deriving the triangle area formula.

Width

Minimum
Calculate the area of a given triangle and a given parallelogram using their formulas.
Typical
Solve problems involving composite shapes (decompose into triangles and rectangles); select appropriate units; convert between units if needed.
Wider
  • Solve practical problems: floor plans, sail areas, land parcels — requiring decomposition into triangles and parallelograms.
  • Investigate First Nations design elements that use triangular and parallelogram forms — calculate areas in context.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepArea formula application errors are common in non-standard orientations and composite shapes. Varied practical contexts with different orientations are necessary.
