Planning the investigation

AC9M9ST05 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 9 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can pose a statistical question and plan a data collection strategy appropriate to the question, identifying potential sources of bias.

Success criteria

  • Poses a statistical question that can be investigated with data
  • Identifies an appropriate data source (primary or secondary) and justifies the choice
  • Identifies at least one potential source of bias in the planned data collection

Prior knowledge

Students can plan and conduct statistical investigations using samples, make population inferences acknowledging uncertainty (AC9M8ST04). They have not yet applied this across different kinds of data (primary and secondary, quantitative and qualitative), or evaluated the strength of evidence to support conclusions.

Width

Minimum
Pose one statistical question; identify whether primary or secondary data is more appropriate; describe one data collection method and one potential source of bias.
Typical
Pose a clear statistical question; plan data collection including source (primary or secondary), method, sample size and sampling strategy; identify potential sources of bias and how to minimise them; consider ethical and fair data collection practices.
Wider
  • Pose statistical questions in relation to reconciliation and First Nations Australian communities; plan data collection from multiple sources, considering relationships between variables and cultural sensitivity in data collection
  • Plan a multi-variable investigation (e.g. factors affecting location for a tropical fruit plantation) using secondary data from multiple sources; identify what data is available and what its limitations are
  • Investigate how ABS and other government agencies plan large-scale data collection — evaluate the methods they use and the potential sources of error
