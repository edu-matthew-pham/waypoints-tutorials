← AC9M6N07 Fractions, decimals and percentages

Percentage discounts and financial applications

AC9M6N07 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 6 Maths · Number · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can calculate a percentage discount on a price and determine the discounted price.

Success criteria

  • Student correctly calculates a percentage discount and determines the discounted price.
  • Student explains the two steps: finding the discount amount and subtracting from the original price.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Finding a percentage of a quantity.

Width

Minimum
Calculate a common percentage discount (10%, 25%, 50%) on a price; find the discounted price by subtracting the discount from the original.
Typical
Calculate percentage discounts including 5%, 15%, 20%; find the discounted price; compare prices to determine better value; apply to a range of financial contexts including GST, savings, and price comparison.
Wider
  • Investigate multi-step discount problems (e.g. 20% off, then further 10% off — is this the same as 30% off?)
  • Explore how GST (10%) affects prices — calculate prices with and without GST; connect to business and consumer mathematics contexts

Hinge

Conflating discount amount with discounted price is a persistent conceptual error — students who do not distinguish the two steps will produce systematically wrong answers in AC9M6N09 financial modelling contexts where multi-step percentage calculations are embedded.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents often confuse the discount amount with the discounted price. The two-step reasoning (calculate the discount, then subtract from original) needs explicit scaffolding, particularly for students who conflate percentage with the final result.
Integration: a combining task at the wider tierPercentage discount problems integrate finding a fraction/decimal/percentage of a quantity (Nodes 1-2) with multi-step calculation and financial reasoning. Coordinating these components in a real-world context is the endpoint of this standard.
