Number names and numerals to 10

AC9MFN01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Foundation Maths · Number · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can name numbers to 10 and match number names to numerals and to quantities.

Success criteria

  • Student reads and names numerals 0 to 10 in and out of order
  • Student matches each numeral 0 to 10 to a collection of the correct number of objects

Prior knowledge

Students have informal number experience from pre-school and home contexts — counting objects in play, hearing number words in songs and stories. They have not yet formally connected number names, numerals and quantities in a consistent ordered sequence to 20.

Width

Minimum
Match spoken number names to the correct numeral and to a collection of objects for numbers 1 to 10.
Typical
Name, read and write numerals 0 to 10; match each numeral to a quantity; recognise numerals in familiar contexts (books, labels, number tracks); connect number names to quantities in counting songs and stories.
Wider
  • Explore numbers 0 to 10 across multiple representations (numeral, dot pattern, fingers, ten-frame) and connect between representations
  • Connect quantities to number names and numerals through First Nations Australians' counting contexts — e.g. Segur etug from Mer Island in the Torres Strait region

Hinge

Name–numeral–quantity correspondence to 10 is the foundation for extending to 20 (node 2) and ordering (node 3). Students with incomplete correspondence at 0–10 cannot reliably read teen numerals or place numbers on a number track — gaps here produce systematic errors across all subsequent number work.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsNumber–numeral correspondence requires repeated exposure and practice. Students commonly know some number names from songs without connecting them reliably to numerals or quantities. Repeated practice connecting the three forms (name, numeral, quantity) is the primary support need here — breadth alone does not resolve incomplete correspondence.
