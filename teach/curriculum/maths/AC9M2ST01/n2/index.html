← AC9M2ST01 Acquire and sort categorical data

Collecting and sorting into categories

AC9M2ST01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 2 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can collect data and sort it into relevant, mutually exclusive categories.

Success criteria

  • Collects data using a survey, observation or digital tool
  • Sorts all collected data into defined categories with no item left unsorted

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Posing a question and choosing a method.

Width

Minimum
Collect data using one method; sort responses into at least three categories; confirm that each response belongs to exactly one category.
Typical
Collect data through surveys, observation or digital tools; define categories before collecting; sort responses; use counting strategies to determine totals per category.
Wider
  • Use familiar software or AI tools to construct a survey; collect class data; sort and interpret responses; consider whether questions need modifying for reuse
  • Investigate 'How much rubbish is really rubbish?' by gathering and categorising objects: throw away, recycle, reuse; decide whether the data answers the question
