Factors and multiples

AC9M5N02 · Year 5 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Express natural numbers as products of their factors, recognise multiples and determine if one number is divisible by another

Prior knowledge

Students can identify odd and even numbers and recognise some basic number properties (AC9M4N02). They have not yet systematically expressed numbers as products of factors or applied divisibility reasoning.

By the end

Students express natural numbers as products of their factors, recognise multiples, and determine divisibility.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using a certain number of blocks to form different rectangles and using these to list all possible factors for that number; for example, 12 12 blocks can form the following rectangles: 1 × 12 , 2 × 6 1×12,2×6, and 3 × 4 3×4
  • researching divisibility tests and explaining each rule using materials; for example, using base- 10 10 blocks to test if numbers are divisible by 2 , 5 2,5 and 10 10
  • using divisibility tests to determine if larger numbers are multiples of one-digit numbers; for example, testing if 89 89 472 472 is divisible by 3 3 using 8 + 9 + 4 + 7 + 2 = 30 8+9+4+7+2=30 as 30 30 is divisible by 3 3 then 89 89 472 472 is a multiple of 3 3
  • demonstrating and reasoning that all multiples can be formed by combining or regrouping; for example, multiples of 7 7 can be formed by combining a multiple of 2 2 with the corresponding multiple of 5 5; 3 × 7 = 3 × 2 + 3 × 5 3×7=3×2+3×5, and 4 × 7 = 4 × 2 + 4 × 5 4×7=4×2+4×5

Waypoints

  • 1. MultiplesHingeStudents can list multiples of a given number and recognise whether a number is a multiple of another.
  • 2. Factors and factor pairsHingeStudents can find all factor pairs of a natural number and list all its factors.
  • 3. Products of factorsStudents can express a natural number as a product of two or more factors in multiple ways.
  • 4. DivisibilityStudents can determine whether one natural number is divisible by another and explain their reasoning.
