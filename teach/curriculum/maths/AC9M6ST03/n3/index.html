← AC9M6ST03 Plan and refine investigations

Analysing, interpreting and communicating findings

AC9M6ST03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 6 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse collected data using appropriate measures and displays, interpret the findings in context, and communicate conclusions that address the original investigation question.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student produces an appropriate display and states findings using statistical measures.
  • Student communicates conclusions that specifically address the original investigation question.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Collecting and organising relevant data.

Width

Minimum
Calculate mode and range from collected data; create an appropriate display; state at least two findings that address the investigation question in context.
Typical
Analyse data using mode, range, shape, and appropriate displays; interpret findings in context; connect findings back to the original question; acknowledge limitations of the investigation (sample size, bias, data quality); communicate as a written or presented report.
Wider
  • Refine the investigation based on findings — pose a follow-up question that the initial investigation raised and plan how it could be addressed
  • Communicate findings to a real or simulated audience (class, school community, partner class) and respond to questions about methods and conclusions

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierCommunicating findings integrates data analysis, contextual interpretation, and critical reflection on the investigation's limitations. The coordination of these — producing a coherent response to the original question — is the defining capability goal of this descriptor.
