Applying rates to practical problems

AC9M8M05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 8 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students apply rates to solve practical problems including pay rates, run rates, dilution, taxation, and unit conversions.

Lessons on this site

  • Using rates

Success criteria

  • Correctly applies a given rate to solve a practical problem.
  • Shows the rate calculation clearly with appropriate units.
  • Interprets the result in context.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Rates as comparisons of different units.

Width

Minimum
Solve a practical problem using a given rate (e.g. calculate cost at a given rate per kg, or distance at a given speed).
Typical
Apply rates to varied contexts including required run rates in sport, chemical dilution, income tax using rate tables, and currency conversion.
Wider
  • Use taxation rate tables to calculate annual income tax for a given salary — apply marginal rate structure and compute effective rate.
  • Investigate currency conversion as a rate problem: plan a budget for overseas travel using current exchange rates.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepRate application problems span a very wide range of real-world contexts and problem structures. Breadth of context is the primary driver of transferable rate reasoning.
