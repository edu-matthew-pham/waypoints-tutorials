Divide with remainders

AC9M5N07 · Year 5 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Solve problems involving division, choosing efficient strategies and using digital tools where appropriate; interpret any remainder according to the context and express results as a whole number, decimal or fraction

Prior knowledge

Students can apply estimation and rounding strategies (AC9M4N07). They have not yet interpreted remainders in context or expressed division results as decimals or fractions.

By the end

Students solve problems involving division using efficient strategies and interpret any remainder as a whole number, decimal or fraction according to context.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • interpreting and solving everyday division problems such as, “How many buses are needed if there are 436 436 passengers, and each bus carries 50 50 people?”, deciding whether to round up or down in order to accommodate the remainder and justifying choices
  • solving division problems mentally like 72 72 divided by 9 , 72 ÷ 9 9,72÷9, by thinking, “how many 9 9s make 72 72”, ? × 9 = 72 ×9=72 or “share 72 72 equally 9 9 ways”
  • using the fact that equivalent division calculations result if both numbers are divided by the same factor

Waypoints

  • 1. Division strategiesStudents can apply and explain at least two division strategies for dividing multi-digit numbers.
  • 2. Interpreting remaindersHingeStudents can interpret a remainder in three ways (whole number remainder, decimal, fraction) according to context.
  • 3. Choosing strategies and digital toolsStudents can choose an efficient division strategy for a given problem and use digital tools appropriately.
