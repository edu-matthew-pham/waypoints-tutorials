Faces and surfaces of 3D objects

AC9M5SP01 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 5 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify and describe the faces of common 3D objects and explain how they relate to the object's surface.

Success criteria

  • Student correctly identifies and names all faces of a given 3D object
  • Student describes each face shape using geometric vocabulary

Prior knowledge

Students can create and classify composite shapes using geometric properties (AC9M4SP01). They have not yet connected 3D objects to their 2D nets or reasoned about which 2D arrangements fold into which 3D objects.

Width

Minimum
Identify the number and type of faces on a given 3D object (cube, rectangular prism, triangular prism, pyramid); describe each face shape.
Typical
Describe face properties of a range of prisms and pyramids; distinguish flat faces from curved surfaces; connect face count and shape to object name.
Wider
  • Investigate Platonic solids and their face properties
  • Connect face descriptions to packaging and engineering design
