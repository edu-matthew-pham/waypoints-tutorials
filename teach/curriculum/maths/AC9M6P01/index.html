Probability scale 0 to 1

AC9M6P01 · Year 6 Maths · Probability

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Recognise that probabilities lie on numerical scales of 0 – 1 or 0% – 100% and use estimation to assign probabilities that events occur in a given context, using common fractions, percentages and decimals

Prior knowledge

Students can list outcomes of chance experiments and compare equally and unequally likely outcomes (AC9M5P01). They can estimate likelihoods from frequency (AC9M5P02). They have not yet connected likelihood to the 0–1 numerical scale or used fractions, decimals, and percentages as probability representations.

By the end

Students can recognise that probabilities lie on numerical scales of 0–1 or 0%–100% and use estimation to assign probabilities using fractions, percentages and decimals.

Waypoints

  • 1. The probability scaleHingeStudents can place events on a 0–1 probability scale and connect impossible (0), certain (1), and unlikely/likely/even chance positions to their numerical values.
  • 2. Representing probability as fractions, decimals and percentagesStudents can express the same probability value as a fraction, decimal, and percentage, and convert between these representations.
  • 3. Estimating probabilities in contextStudents can use estimation to assign reasonable probabilities to events in a given context, justifying their estimates using the 0–1 scale.
