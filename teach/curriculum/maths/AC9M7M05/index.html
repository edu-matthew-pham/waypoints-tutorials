Angle sum of triangles

AC9M7M05 · Year 7 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

demonstrate that the interior angle sum of a triangle in the plane is 180° and apply this to determine the interior angle sum of other shapes and the size of unknown angles

Prior knowledge

Students can identify angle relationships on straight lines and at points (from AC9M6M04). Students understand that angles on a straight line sum to 180°.

By the end

Students demonstrate that the interior angle sum of a triangle is 180° and apply this and the decomposition principle to find interior angle sums of polygons and unknown angles.

Waypoints

  • 1. Demonstrating the triangle angle sumHingeStudents demonstrate, using concrete or dynamic methods, that the interior angles of any triangle sum to 180°.
  • 2. Interior angle sum of polygons by decompositionStudents use decomposition of polygons into triangles to determine the interior angle sum formula for n-sided polygons.
  • 3. Finding unknown angles using angle sumsStudents use triangle and polygon angle sum properties to find unknown angles, giving reasons.
