Days between events

AC9M2M03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 2 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can determine the number of days between two events using a calendar, including events in the same month and across months.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Counts the number of days between two events in the same month
  • Determines the date 2 weeks after a given date using a calendar

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Reading a calendar.

Width

Minimum
Count the days between two events in the same month using a calendar.
Typical
Determine the number of days between events within and across months; use addition to total days across months; locate a date 2 weeks prior to or after a given date.
Wider
  • Model 'How many days are left in this year?' by identifying days remaining in the current month and adding remaining full months; use addition to solve
  • Investigate how many days are in each month; discuss why some months have different numbers of days

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkCounting days across a month boundary requires a multi-step reasoning process (days remaining in first month + days in intervening months + days in final month). The reasoning scaffold needs to be made visible and practised before students can apply it independently.
