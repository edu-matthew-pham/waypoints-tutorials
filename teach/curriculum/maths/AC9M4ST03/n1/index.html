Pose and plan

Checkpoint

Students can pose a statistical question and plan how to collect relevant data.

Success criteria

  • Students can pose a clear statistical question and identify what data is needed to answer it
  • Students can create a survey question that will produce data relevant to their question

Prior knowledge

Students can conduct guided statistical investigations involving collection, representation and interpretation of data (AC9M3ST03). They have not yet independently designed survey instruments or accessed data from online sources.

Width

Minimum
Pose one statistical question and identify what data needs to be collected to answer it; describe how the data will be collected.
Typical
Pose and refine statistical questions for different contexts; identify the most appropriate data collection method (survey, observation, secondary data); create survey questions that will produce useful data; plan the investigation before collecting.
Wider
  • Investigate a consumer context (e.g. product placement in supermarkets) by identifying exactly what questions need to be answered and what data is needed — distinguishing relevant from irrelevant information
  • Compare what different types of data sources (own survey vs secondary data) can and cannot tell you about the same question

Hinge

Students who collect data without verifying it can answer their question arrive at Node 3 with data that cannot support a meaningful interpretation. The investigation cycle breaks at the communication phase when the collected data and the original question are misaligned.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkStudents often jump to data collection without asking whether the planned data can actually answer their question. The planning scaffold — 'what question am I asking? what data answers it? how will I collect that data?' — is the key reasoning move that separates purposeful investigation from accidental data gathering.
