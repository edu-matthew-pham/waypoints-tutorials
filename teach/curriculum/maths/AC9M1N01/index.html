Numbers to 120

AC9M1N01 · Year 1 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 120 using physical and virtual materials, numerals, number lines and charts

Prior knowledge

Students can name, represent and order numbers to at least 20 (AC9MFN01). They can quantify and compare collections to at least 20 using counting (AC9MFN03). They have not yet read, written or ordered numbers in the two-digit range up to 120, or used patterns in the number system (tens/ones) to navigate across decades.

By the end

Students recognise, represent and order numbers to at least 120 using physical and virtual materials, numerals, number lines and charts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Two-digit numeral recognitionHingeStudents can read and write two-digit numerals to at least 99, including numerals that look or sound similar (e.g. 16 and 61).
  • 2. Tens and ones structureStudents can identify the tens and ones in a two-digit number and use the hundreds chart to navigate the number system.
  • 3. Ordering to 120 on a number lineStudents can order a set of numbers to at least 120 and position them on a number line, using patterns in the number system.
