← AC9M7N04 Rational numbers on a number line

Converting between fraction, decimal and percentage

AC9M7N04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 7 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students convert fluently between fraction, decimal and percentage representations of rational numbers.

Lessons on this site

  • Fractions, decimals and percentages

Success criteria

  • Correctly converts a given rational number between fraction, decimal and percentage forms.
  • Identifies when representations are equivalent without performing full conversion.
  • Does not confuse 0.5 with 5% (confusing decimal and percentage forms).

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Equivalence and simplification of fractions.

Width

Minimum
Convert a given rational number between all three representations (fraction, decimal, percentage) for common values.
Typical
Explain the conversion process for each direction; identify equivalent representations for less familiar fractions (e.g. 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%).
Wider
  • Investigate repeating decimals (e.g. 1/3 = 0.333...) and understand these as rational numbers.
  • Explore how percentage, decimal and fraction choices affect ease of computation in different contexts.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents often have strong conversion skills in one direction (fraction to decimal) but poor skills in others. Encountering the same rational number across varied contexts and conversion directions resolves partial understanding.
