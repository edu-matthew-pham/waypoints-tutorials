Rational numbers on the number line

AC9M7N04 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 7 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students place positive and negative fractions, decimals and mixed numerals accurately on a number line, including intervals other than 0–1.

Lessons on this site

  • Fractions and decimals on the line

Success criteria

  • Correctly places a given set of rational numbers (including negatives and mixed numerals) on a number line.
  • Selects and labels appropriate scale for the interval.
  • Correctly orders the set from least to greatest.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Converting between fraction, decimal and percentage.

Width

Minimum
Plot a given set of positive and negative fractions and mixed numerals on a number line with appropriate scale.
Typical
Construct a number line for a given interval, select appropriate scale, and place a mixed set of rational numbers including fractions, decimals and percentages on it.
Wider
  • Given a number line with some rational numbers marked, identify and justify the positions of unlabelled points.
  • Explore density of rational numbers: between any two rational numbers, find another — repeat and discuss what this implies.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkPlacing negative fractions requires a reasoning scaffold: students must convert to a comparable form and reason about which direction is more negative. Without explicit steps, students often mirror positive fractions incorrectly.
