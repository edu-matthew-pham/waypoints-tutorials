Give and follow directions

AC9M1SP02 · Year 1 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Give and follow directions to move people and objects to different locations within a space

Prior knowledge

Students can describe position and location of themselves and objects within a familiar space (AC9MFSP02). They have not yet given or followed multi-step directional instructions, created algorithms for movement, or used ordinal and positional language to direct others.

By the end

Students give and follow directions to move people and objects to different locations within a space.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • interpreting and following directions around familiar locations, using words like 'forwards' and 'backwards', 'straight ahead', 'left or right' to describe movement
  • creating and following an algorithm consisting of a set of instructions to move an object to a different location; for example, role-playing being a robot and following step-by-step instructions
  • following directions to move people into different positions within a line using both ordinal and positional language
  • describing a familiar journey across Country/Place using directional language

Waypoints

  • 1. Interpret and follow directionsHingeStudents can interpret and follow a sequence of directional instructions using forwards, backwards, left, right and straight ahead to reach a stated location.
  • 2. Create and give directionsStudents can create a sequence of directional instructions to move an object or person from one location to another, testing whether the instructions work.
  • 3. Algorithms for movementStudents can create a step-by-step algorithm of instructions to move an object or person through a space, recognising that each instruction must be precise and complete.
