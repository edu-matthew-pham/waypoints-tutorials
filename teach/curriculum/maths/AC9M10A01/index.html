Expand, factorise and simplify

AC9M10A01 · Year 10 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

expand, factorise and simplify expressions and solve equations algebraically, applying exponent laws involving products, quotients and powers of variables, and the distributive property

Prior knowledge

Students can expand binomial products and factorise monic quadratic expressions (AC9M9A02). They have not yet applied exponent laws to algebraic terms with positive and negative integral exponents, nor factorised non-monic quadratics or used the completed square form.

By the end

Students expand, factorise and simplify algebraic expressions and solve equations algebraically, applying exponent laws for products, quotients and powers of variables with positive and negative integral exponents, and the distributive property including completed square form.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • explaining the relationship between factorisation and expansion, including the completed square form for quadratic expressions
  • applying knowledge of exponent laws to algebraic terms and using both positive and negative integral exponents to simplifying algebraic expressions and solve equations algebraically

Waypoints

  • 1. Exponent laws for algebraic termsHingeStudents can apply exponent laws (product, quotient, power of a power) to simplify algebraic expressions involving positive integral exponents.
  • 2. Negative integral exponentsStudents can apply exponent laws to expressions involving negative integral exponents and express results with positive exponents.
  • 3. Expansion using the distributive propertyHingeStudents can expand products of binomials and trinomials using the distributive property, including special products (difference of two squares, perfect square trinomials).
  • 4. Factorisation of quadratic expressionsStudents can factorise monic and non-monic quadratic expressions, including using the completed square form.
  • 5. Solving equations algebraicallyStudents can solve equations algebraically by applying exponent laws and factorisation, including quadratic equations.
