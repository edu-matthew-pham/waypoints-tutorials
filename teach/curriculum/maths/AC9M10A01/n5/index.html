← AC9M10A01 Expand, factorise and simplify

Solving equations algebraically

AC9M10A01 n5 · waypoint 5 of 5 · Year 10 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can solve equations algebraically by applying exponent laws and factorisation, including quadratic equations.

Success criteria

  • Student solves a quadratic equation by factorisation with correct solutions including sign handling
  • Student verifies solutions by substitution into the original equation

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 4: Factorisation of quadratic expressions.

Width

Minimum
Solve a quadratic equation by factorisation; solve a simple equation involving an algebraic exponent law manipulation.
Typical
Solve quadratic equations using factorisation, completing the square, or the quadratic formula where appropriate; apply exponent laws to solve equations involving variable exponents; verify solutions by substitution.
Wider
  • Investigate how different solution methods for quadratic equations (factorisation, completing the square, quadratic formula) are related and when each is most efficient
  • Explore equations that reduce to quadratic form through substitution (e.g. u = x²) and solve them using factorisation techniques

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents need a range of equation-solving approaches (factorisation, completing the square) and the judgement to select the most appropriate one for a given equation. Repertoire, not just one method, is the goal.
