Cartesian plane — 4 quadrants

AC9M6SP02 · Year 6 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Locate points in the 4 quadrants of a Cartesian plane; describe changes to the coordinates when a point is moved to a different position in the plane

Prior knowledge

Students can construct and use grid coordinate systems to describe position and movement in the first quadrant (AC9M5SP02). Students can locate integers on a number line (AC9M6N01). They have not yet extended the coordinate system to all four quadrants using negative integers.

By the end

Students can locate points in all 4 quadrants of a Cartesian plane and describe how coordinates change when a point is moved.

Waypoints

  • 1. Extending coordinates to all four quadrantsHingeStudents can identify and name the four quadrants of the Cartesian plane and locate points with integer coordinates in all four quadrants.
  • 2. Describing coordinate changes when moving a pointStudents can describe how the coordinates of a point change when it is translated horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in the Cartesian plane.
