← AC9M7ST03 Statistical investigations

Conducting, analysing and reporting

AC9M7ST03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 7 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students collect data, display it appropriately, analyse the distribution using summary statistics, and report findings in terms of the statistical question.

Lessons on this site

  • Statistical investigations

Success criteria

  • Collects, displays and analyses data appropriate to the variable type.
  • Reports findings that address the original statistical question.
  • Uses distribution language (shape, centre, spread) in the report.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Planning a statistical investigation.

Width

Minimum
Collect data, create an appropriate display, calculate summary statistics, and write a brief report answering the statistical question.
Typical
Conduct a full investigation cycle; choose appropriate displays and statistics for the variable type; report findings including shape, centre, spread and context.
Wider
  • Use secondary data from a public source (e.g. Reconciliation Barometer, ABS) to conduct a statistical investigation on a social or cultural question.
  • Investigate: does the investigation support or refute a stated claim? Frame the report around evidence for and against.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierConducting and reporting a full investigation integrates all statistics strand skills. The integration and contextual communication are the learning at this node.
Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStatistical investigation only develops as a transferable skill through conducting multiple investigations in varied contexts. A single investigation produces performance on that topic, not transferable investigation skill.
