← AC9M8N05 Mathematical modelling with percentages

Modelling financial and real-world contexts

AC9M8N05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 8 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students formulate and solve models for financial problems including personal income tax, and interpret and communicate solutions including review of model appropriateness.

Lessons on this site

  • Money models

Success criteria

  • Formulates a model correctly from a financial or real-world context.
  • Interprets the solution with appropriate units and contextual meaning.
  • Reviews whether the model captures the situation accurately.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Modelling percentage increase and decrease.

Width

Minimum
Model a simple financial problem (e.g. tax bracket, pay rate) and interpret the solution in context.
Typical
Apply modelling to income tax, comparing taxation brackets; model weather/environmental contexts with positive and negative quantities; review whether the model is appropriate for the situation.
Wider
  • Model personal income tax across multiple tax brackets — calculate effective tax rate and compare to marginal rate.
  • Investigate modelling of sea depth or temperature changes using negative quantities — apply to real environmental data.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierFinancial modelling requires simultaneous application of percentage operations, negative number handling, and communication of results in context — the integration of all prior number strand work at Year 8.
