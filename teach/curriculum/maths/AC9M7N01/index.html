Perfect squares and square roots

AC9M7N01 · Year 7 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe the relationship between perfect square numbers and square roots, and use squares of numbers and square roots of perfect square numbers to solve problems

Prior knowledge

Students can multiply and divide whole numbers fluently, use arrays to represent multiplication, and understand that area of a square = side × side (from Y6 measurement and number work).

By the end

Students describe the relationship between perfect square numbers and their square roots, and apply this relationship to solve problems including finding the perimeter of squares given area.

Waypoints

  • 1. Squares as area modelsHingeStudents can connect squaring a number to the area of a square with that side length, and list perfect square numbers from 1 to 144.
  • 2. Square root as inverse operationHingeStudents understand that square root is the inverse of squaring and can find the exact square root of any perfect square up to 144.
  • 3. Locating non-perfect square rootsStudents can identify which two consecutive integers a square root falls between for numbers that are not perfect squares.
  • 4. Applying square roots to problemsStudents use the relationship between perfect square numbers and square roots to solve practical problems, including finding the side length of a square given its area.
