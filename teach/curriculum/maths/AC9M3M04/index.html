Analog and digital clocks to the minute

AC9M3M04 · Year 3 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe the relationship between the hours and minutes on analog and digital clocks, and read the time to the nearest minute

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise and use the relationship between formal units of time (AC9M3M03) and can read analog time to the quarter-hour from Y2. They have not yet read analog or digital clocks to the nearest minute, or described the relationship between the hour and minute hands at all positions.

By the end

Students describe the hour/minute relationship on both analog and digital clocks and read time to the nearest minute.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • describing the relationship between the hour and minute hands on an analog clock
  • reading and recording times to the nearest minute on both analog and digital clocks
  • setting the time on a digital device or clock
  • using a clock to time activities and record durations to the nearest minute

Waypoints

  • 1. Minute hand and its relationship to hoursHingeStudents can explain how the minute hand relates to hours on an analog clock — one full revolution = one hour = 60 minutes.
  • 2. Reading time to the nearest minuteStudents can read and record time to the nearest minute on both analog and digital clocks.
