Compare and order fractions

AC9M6N03 · Year 6 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Apply knowledge of equivalence to compare, order and represent common fractions including halves, thirds and quarters on the same number line and justify their order

Prior knowledge

Students can compare and order fractions with the same and related denominators including mixed numerals using knowledge of factors and multiples, and represent these on a number line (AC9M5N03). They have not yet compared fractions with unrelated denominators or systematically justified ordering using equivalence across a range of common fractions on the same number line.

By the end

Students can apply equivalence to compare, order and represent common fractions including halves, thirds and quarters on the same number line, and justify their order.

Waypoints

  • 1. Equivalence as the comparison toolHingeStudents can explain why equivalent fractions are needed to compare fractions with different denominators, and generate equivalent fractions for common fractions by multiplying or dividing numerator and denominator by the same factor.
  • 2. Common denominator for comparisonHingeStudents can find a common denominator for two or more fractions with different denominators and use it to determine which is greater.
  • 3. Order fractions on a number lineStudents can order three or more common fractions including halves, thirds and quarters on the same number line and justify the ordering using equivalence.
