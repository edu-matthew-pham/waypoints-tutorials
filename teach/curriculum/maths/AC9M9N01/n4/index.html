← AC9M9N01 Real number system

Solving problems with real numbers

AC9M9N01 n4 · waypoint 4 of 4 · Year 9 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can solve problems by substituting real numbers (including irrationals) into formulas, expressing solutions in both exact and approximate decimal form as appropriate.

Success criteria

  • Correctly substitutes real number values into a formula and simplifies
  • Expresses the answer in exact form where appropriate (e.g. 9π cm²) and as a decimal approximation correct to 2 decimal places
  • Uses correct units and explains the choice between exact and approximate form

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Real number line and inequalities.

Width

Minimum
Substitute given real number values into a formula (e.g. A=πr²) to calculate a result; express the answer in exact form (e.g. 25π) and as a decimal approximation; use positive and negative rational numbers to solve one practical problem.
Typical
Solve a range of problems requiring substitution of real numbers into formulas; choose between exact and approximate forms based on context; use positive and negative rational numbers (including financial contexts); apply digital tools to verify or extend manual calculations.
Wider
  • Investigate problems where keeping exact form throughout a multi-step calculation prevents cumulative rounding error — compare the final result of exact vs approximated intermediate steps
  • Explore financial planning contexts using positive and negative rational numbers (e.g. budgeting with income and expenses) — model a simple scenario algebraically
  • Investigate the use of exact real number expressions in engineering or architecture contexts where precision matters

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates the earlier concepts — number classification, exact vs approximate representation, and real number line reasoning — into applied problem-solving. Students need to coordinate: identifying which type of real number is involved, deciding whether to leave answers in exact form, and substituting correctly into formulas.
