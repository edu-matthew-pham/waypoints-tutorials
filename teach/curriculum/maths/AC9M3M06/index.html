Money values and representation

AC9M3M06 · Year 3 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise the relationships between dollars and cents and represent money values in different ways

Prior knowledge

No direct predecessor identified in the progression map at Y3. Students have used money in additive modelling contexts at Y2 (AC9M2N06) but have not yet formally studied the dollar-cent relationship or represented money values in multiple ways.

By the end

Students recognise the relationship between dollars and cents and represent money amounts in different equivalent forms.

Waypoints

  • 1. Dollar and cent relationshipHingeStudents can state that 100 cents = $1 and use this to convert between dollar and cent representations.
  • 2. Representing money values in different waysStudents can represent a given money amount using different combinations of coins and notes, and identify equivalent representations.
