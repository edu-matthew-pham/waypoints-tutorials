Rearranging linear expressions

AC9M8A01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 8 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students rearrange linear expressions and formulas using inverse operations and algebraic properties, maintaining equivalence.

Lessons on this site

  • Changing the subject

Success criteria

  • Correctly rearranges a linear formula to make a specified variable the subject.
  • Applies inverse operations in the correct order.
  • Verifies the rearranged formula by substitution.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Factorising linear expressions.

Width

Minimum
Rearrange a two-term linear expression to isolate a specified variable.
Typical
Rearrange multi-term linear formulas to make any variable the subject; apply commutative and inverse properties explicitly; verify by substitution.
Wider
  • Apply rearrangement to real formulas from science and everyday contexts: rearrange distance = speed × time for speed; rearrange area formulas for dimensions.
  • Investigate how rearrangement connects to solving equations — express the connection between 'rearranging a formula' and 'solving for a variable' explicitly.
