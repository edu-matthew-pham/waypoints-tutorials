Terminating and recurring decimals

AC9M8N03 · Year 8 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise terminating and recurring decimals, using digital tools as appropriate

Prior knowledge

Students can convert fractions to decimals and understand that some fractions produce repeating decimals (from AC9M7N04). Students understand prime factorisation (from AC9M7N02).

By the end

Students recognise terminating and recurring decimals and identify from the prime factorisation of the denominator whether a fraction produces a terminating decimal.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying when a fraction has a terminating decimal expansion from the prime factorisation of its denominator
  • identifying terminating, recurring and non-terminating decimals and choosing their appropriate representations

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying terminating and recurring decimalsHingeStudents distinguish terminating from recurring decimals and use correct notation for recurring decimals.
  • 2. Predicting termination from prime factorisation of denominatorStudents identify whether a fraction terminates by examining whether the prime factorisation of the denominator contains only factors of 2 and/or 5.
