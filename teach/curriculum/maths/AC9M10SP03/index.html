Algorithms for spatial problems

AC9M10SP03 · Year 10 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

design, test and refine solutions to spatial problems using algorithms and digital tools; communicate and justify solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can design, test and refine algorithms based on geometric constructions and theorems (AC9M9SP03). They have not yet applied this capability to the full range of spatial problem types at Y10 — scale models, floor plan optimisation, network routing, and self-similar patterns — or communicated and justified solutions at this level.

By the end

Students design, test and refine algorithmic solutions to spatial problems using digital tools, and communicate and justify their solutions.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • designing and making scale models of 3D objects using digital tools (e.g. 3D printing, tessellation-based puzzles)
  • applying computational thinking to network problems (connectedness, coverage, weighted measures)
  • defining and decomposing spatial problems; creating and applying algorithms; evaluating solutions
  • designing, creating and testing algorithms using pseudocode or flowcharts for self-similar patterns; validating using a range of test cases
  • exploring geospatial technologies used by First Nations Australians' communities

Waypoints

  • 1. Defining and decomposing spatial problemsHingeStudents can precisely define a spatial problem and decompose it into sub-problems suitable for algorithmic solution.
  • 2. Designing algorithms for spatial problemsStudents can design an algorithm (using pseudocode or flowchart) that addresses a defined spatial problem.
  • 3. Testing, refining and justifyingStudents can test an algorithm against multiple cases, identify and fix failures, and communicate why the refined solution addresses the problem.
