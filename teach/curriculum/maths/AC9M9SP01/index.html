Trigonometric ratios

AC9M9SP01 · Year 9 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise the constancy of the sine, cosine and tangent ratios for a given angle in right-angled triangles using properties of similarity

Prior knowledge

Students can identify conditions for congruence and similarity of triangles (AC9M8SP01). They have not yet used similarity to establish that side-length ratios in right-angled triangles are constant for a given angle, or named and used sine, cosine and tangent.

By the end

Students recognise that sine, cosine and tangent ratios are constant for a given angle in right-angled triangles and explain this using properties of similarity.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • understanding the terms base, altitude, hypotenuse, adjacent and opposite in a right-angled triangle
  • investigating patterns in nested similar triangles to identify constancy of ratios of corresponding sides for a given angle
  • establishing that sine is the altitude and cosine is the base of a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse of length one unit
  • relating tangent to altitude and base of nested similar triangles; connecting to gradient of a straight line

Waypoints

  • 1. Sides of a right-angled triangle relative to an angleHingeStudents can correctly identify the hypotenuse, opposite and adjacent sides for a specified angle in a right-angled triangle.
  • 2. Constancy of ratios — similarity argumentStudents can explain using properties of similarity why the ratio of two specified sides is constant for a given angle, regardless of triangle size.
  • 3. Naming the ratios: sine, cosine, tangentStudents can name the sine, cosine and tangent ratios and connect each to the appropriate pair of sides in a right-angled triangle.
