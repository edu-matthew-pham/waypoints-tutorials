Naming the ratios: sine, cosine, tangent

AC9M9SP01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 9 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can name the sine, cosine and tangent ratios and connect each to the appropriate pair of sides in a right-angled triangle.

Lessons on this site

  • Sine, cosine and tangent

Success criteria

  • States the correct ratio definition for each of sin, cos and tan
  • Correctly evaluates each ratio for a right-angled triangle with given side lengths
  • Correctly connects the tangent ratio to the gradient of a line

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Constancy of ratios — similarity argument.

Width

Minimum
State the definitions of sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, tan θ = opp/adj; evaluate each ratio for a given triangle using measured or given side lengths.
Typical
Define and apply all three trigonometric ratios; connect tangent to the ratio of opposite to adjacent; relate the tangent of the angle a line makes with the x-axis to the gradient of the line; evaluate ratios for several angles using calculators and tables.
Wider
  • Connect the tangent ratio to gradient — investigate that the angle a line makes with the positive x-axis has tan θ = gradient of the line
  • Investigate how the values of sine, cosine and tangent change as the angle increases from 0° to 90°, identifying boundary cases (sin 0°=0, cos 0°=1, tan 90° undefined)
  • Explore historical and cultural contexts for trigonometry — how did ancient astronomers and surveyors use these relationships before the formal ratio notation existed?
