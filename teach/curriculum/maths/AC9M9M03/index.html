Spatial problems — pythagoras, trig and similarity

AC9M9M03 · Year 9 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

solve spatial problems, applying angle properties, scale, similarity, Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry in right-angled triangles

Prior knowledge

Students can use Pythagoras' theorem to find unknown side lengths in right-angled triangles (AC9M8M06). They have not yet applied trigonometric ratios in right-angled triangles, or combined similarity, scale, and Pythagoras in multi-concept spatial problems.

By the end

Students solve spatial problems applying angle properties, scale, similarity, Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry in right-angled triangles.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating applications of Pythagoras' theorem in authentic problems, including surveying and design
  • applying the distance formula on the Cartesian plane
  • understanding the relationship between corresponding sides of similar right-angled triangles and the scale factor for areas
  • using photographic images to estimate actual measurements using scale
  • investigating the triangle inequality and generalising links between Pythagoras and inequalities for acute/obtuse triangles
  • designing and using a Biltmore stick using similar triangles, Pythagoras, rates and algebra
  • investigating how autonomous vehicles solve spatial problems using algorithms based on geometric properties

Waypoints

  • 1. Trigonometric ratios — finding unknown sidesHingeStudents can apply sine, cosine and tangent ratios to find unknown sides in right-angled triangles.
  • 2. Trigonometric ratios — finding unknown anglesStudents can apply inverse trigonometric ratios to find unknown angles in right-angled triangles.
  • 3. Similarity, scale and proportion in trianglesHingeStudents can apply similarity and scale factor to solve problems involving corresponding sides and areas of similar triangles.
  • 4. Solving multi-concept spatial problemsStudents can solve spatial problems requiring a combination of Pythagoras' theorem, trigonometry, similarity and/or scale.
