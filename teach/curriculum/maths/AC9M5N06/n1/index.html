← AC9M5N06 Multiply larger numbers

Mental and informal multiplication strategies

AC9M5N06 n1 · waypoint 1 of 4 · Year 5 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply and explain mental strategies for multiplication including doubling, halving and place value partitioning.

Success criteria

  • Student applies at least two distinct mental strategies correctly
  • Student can explain why a chosen strategy is efficient for a given calculation

Prior knowledge

Students can multiply and divide by powers of 10 and apply multiplication to practical problems (AC9M4N05). They have not yet used efficient written strategies for multiplying larger numbers by two-digit numbers.

Width

Minimum
Apply at least two mental strategies to multiply a two- or three-digit number by a single digit; explain the strategy used.
Typical
Select and justify mental strategies (doubling/halving, near doubles, place value partitioning); apply to a range of two-digit by one-digit calculations.
Wider
  • Compare strategy efficiency across different number types
  • Use mental strategies to estimate before calculating

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyStudents who have only one multiplication strategy cannot adapt to different number types. Building a repertoire of mental strategies gives flexibility and supports estimation.
