Estimation strategies

AC9M5N08 · Year 5 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Check and explain the reasonableness of solutions to problems including financial contexts using estimation strategies appropriate to the context

Prior knowledge

Students can use equal sharing and grouping strategies (AC9M1N06). They have not yet applied formal estimation strategies to check reasonableness in financial or multi-step contexts.

By the end

Students check and explain the reasonableness of solutions using appropriate estimation strategies including in financial contexts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • interpreting a series of contextual problems to decide whether an exact answer or an approximate calculation is appropriate; explaining their reasoning in relation to the context and the numbers involved
  • recognising the effect of rounding addition, subtraction, multiplication and division calculations, rounding both numbers up, both numbers down, and one number up and one number down; explaining which estimation is the best approximation and why
  • considering the type of rounding that is appropriate when estimating the amount of money required; for example, rounding up or rounding down when buying one item from a store using cash, compared to rounding up the cost of every item when buying groceries to estimate the total cost and not rounding when the financial transactions are digital

Waypoints

  • 1. Estimation strategiesHingeStudents can apply rounding and front-end estimation to approximate a calculation result before or after computing.
  • 2. Reasonableness judgementStudents can evaluate whether a given answer to a problem is reasonable using estimation, and explain their judgement.
  • 3. Estimation in financial contextsStudents can apply estimation strategies to check the reasonableness of solutions in financial contexts.
