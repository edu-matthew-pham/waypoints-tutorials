Relative frequencies and compound probability

AC9M9P02 · Year 9 Maths · Probability

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

calculate relative frequencies from given or collected data to estimate probabilities of events involving "and", inclusive "or" and exclusive "or"

Prior knowledge

Students can list all outcomes for compound events with and without replacement and assign probabilities (AC9M9P01). They have not yet used relative frequencies from data to estimate probabilities involving compound logical connectives ('and', inclusive 'or', exclusive 'or'), or used Venn diagrams and two-way tables for this purpose.

By the end

Students calculate relative frequencies to estimate probabilities of compound events involving 'and', inclusive 'or' and exclusive 'or', using Venn diagrams and two-way tables.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • understanding that relative frequencies from large data sets or long-run experiments provide reliable probability estimates
  • using Venn diagrams or two-way tables to estimate frequencies of events involving 'and' and 'or'
  • designing, testing and refining an algorithm to determine relative frequencies from a generated data set to estimate different probabilities

Waypoints

  • 1. Relative frequency as a probability estimateStudents can calculate relative frequencies from data and explain how they estimate theoretical probability for compound events.
  • 2. Compound events: 'and', inclusive 'or', exclusive 'or'HingeStudents can distinguish between 'and', inclusive 'or' and exclusive 'or' events and represent them using Venn diagrams or two-way tables.
  • 3. Estimating compound event probabilities from dataStudents can use relative frequencies from data to estimate probabilities of compound events ('and', 'or') and use these to make predictions.
