← AC9M5A01 Multiplication and division as inverses

Families of number facts

AC9M5A01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 5 Maths · Algebra · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can generate a family of four related multiplication and division facts from a single multiplication situation.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Student generates all four facts in a family from a given multiplication
  • Student applies a fact family to find an unknown value

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Inverse relationship.

Width

Minimum
Given a multiplication fact, write all four related facts in the family (a×b=c, b×a=c, c÷a=b, c÷b=a).
Typical
Generate fact families for a range of multiplication situations; use fact families to identify unknown values; apply to multi-digit facts using digital tools.
Wider
  • Extend fact families to larger numbers using the inverse as a calculation strategy (240÷20 via 20×?=240)
  • Investigate how fact families support mental calculation strategies

Hinge

Fluent fact family generation is required by Node 3 — students who cannot reliably produce all four related facts cannot apply inverse reasoning efficiently to multi-digit division problems.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFluent fact family generation is a prerequisite for all inverse-based calculation. Students need practice generating the four-fact structure before applying it to solve unknowns.
