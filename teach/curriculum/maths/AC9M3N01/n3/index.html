← AC9M3N01 Numbers beyond 10 000

Representing large numbers on number lines

AC9M3N01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Year 3 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can place and read numbers beyond 10 000 on a number line, making reasonable estimates of unmarked positions.

Success criteria

  • Students can place given five-digit numbers at approximately correct positions on a number line.
  • Students can estimate the value of a position on a number line and justify their estimate using a benchmark.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Naming conventions for large numerals.

Width

Minimum
Place three given five-digit numbers on a number line with marked endpoints; read one number from an indicated position.
Typical
Place and estimate positions of five-digit numbers on number lines with varying scales; explain reasoning for estimates using benchmark numbers (e.g. 50 000 as midpoint).
Wider
  • Use interactive digital number lines to explore scale across orders of magnitude — from thousands to millions
  • Investigate how population data for Australian towns and cities can be ordered on a number line, connecting to real-world context
