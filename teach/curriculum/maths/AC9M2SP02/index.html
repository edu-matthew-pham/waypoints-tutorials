Locate positions in 2D space

AC9M2SP02 · Year 2 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate positions in two-dimensional representations of a familiar space; move positions by following directions and pathways

Prior knowledge

Students give and follow directions in familiar spaces using positional language (AC9M1SP02). They have not yet located positions in 2D map representations or moved through represented spaces by following systematic directional pathways.

By the end

Students locate positions in two-dimensional representations of familiar spaces and move positions by following and giving directional pathways, including on grids and maps.

Waypoints

  • 1. Interpreting 2D representationsHingeStudents can interpret a simple map or diagram of a familiar space and locate key features or positions.
  • 2. Following directional pathwaysStudents can follow a set of directional instructions (including forward, backward, left, right, and fractional turns) to move through a grid or map.
  • 3. Giving directional pathwaysStudents can give clear directional instructions to move from one position to another in a 2D representation, considering order, direction and distance.
