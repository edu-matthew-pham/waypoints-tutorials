Familiar shapes

AC9MFSP01 · Foundation Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

sort, name and create familiar shapes; recognise and describe familiar shapes within objects in the environment, giving reasons

Prior knowledge

Students have informal experience with shapes in play and everyday objects. They may be able to identify some shapes by name (circle, square) in canonical orientations. They have not yet systematically sorted shapes by attributes or described shapes within objects in the environment.

By the end

Students sort, name and create familiar shapes; recognise and describe familiar shapes within objects in the environment, giving reasons for their identification.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • sorting a collection of shapes into groups based on different features such as the number of sides, colour or size, and describing how they have been sorted
  • creating a picture using a variety of shapes and a range of materials, including objects to trace around, describing the shapes they have created or used
  • creating familiar shapes using groups of people; for example, holding hands and creating a circle
  • recognising and naming shapes that are (close to) rectangles, squares, triangles and circles in component parts of everyday items
  • describing and naming shapes within objects that can be observed on Country/Place, recreating and sorting into groups based on their shape

Waypoints

  • 1. Naming and recognising familiar shapesHingeStudents can name familiar shapes (circle, triangle, rectangle, square) and recognise them in different sizes and orientations.
  • 2. Sorting shapes by attributesStudents can sort a collection of shapes by a chosen attribute and describe how they sorted.
  • 3. Creating shapes and describing shapes in the environmentStudents can create familiar shapes and recognise and describe familiar shapes within objects in the environment, giving reasons for their identification.
