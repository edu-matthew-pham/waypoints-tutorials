Surface area and volume of composite objects

AC9M10M01 · Year 10 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

solve problems involving the surface area and volume of composite objects using appropriate units

Prior knowledge

Students can solve surface area and volume problems for right prisms and cylinders (AC9M9M01). They have not yet worked with composite objects — solids formed by combining or subtracting multiple familiar 3D shapes.

By the end

Students solve problems involving the surface area and volume of composite objects by decomposing them into familiar component solids and applying appropriate units.

Waypoints

  • 1. Decomposing composite objectsHingeStudents can identify the component solids in a composite object and describe how they combine (addition or subtraction of volumes/surfaces).
  • 2. Surface area of composite objectsHingeStudents can calculate the surface area of a composite object by summing exposed faces of component solids.
  • 3. Volume of composite objectsStudents can calculate the volume of a composite object by summing or subtracting volumes of component solids.
  • 4. Modelling with composite objectsStudents can use surface area and volume calculations for composite objects to solve a practical problem, stating assumptions and interpreting results in context.
