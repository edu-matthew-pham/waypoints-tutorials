Add and subtract two-digit numbers

AC9M2N04 · Year 2 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

add and subtract one- and two-digit numbers, representing problems using number sentences, and solve using part-part-whole reasoning and a variety of calculation strategies

Prior knowledge

Students add and subtract numbers within 20 using part-part-whole knowledge to 10 and a variety of calculation strategies (AC9M1N04). They have not yet extended these strategies to two-digit numbers or represented problems as number sentences.

By the end

Students add and subtract one- and two-digit numbers by representing problems as number sentences and solving using part-part-whole reasoning and a range of strategies including bridging tens, near doubles, partitioning and number lines.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using the associative property of addition to assist with mental calculation by partitioning, rearranging and regrouping
  • using strategies such as doubles, near doubles, part-part-whole to 10, bridging tens and partitioning for two-digit numbers
  • representing addition and subtraction problems using a bar model and writing a number sentence
  • using mental strategies and informal jottings for two-digit problems; recognising zero added leaves a number unchanged
  • using a number line or hundreds chart to solve addition or subtraction problems
  • using First Nations Australians' stories and dances to understand the balance between addition and subtraction

Waypoints

  • 1. Number sentences for problemsHingeStudents can represent an addition or subtraction situation as a number sentence and explain how each number in the sentence connects to the problem.
  • 2. Strategies for two-digit additionStudents can add two-digit numbers using at least two strategies (e.g. bridging tens, partitioning, near doubles) and explain their reasoning.
  • 3. Strategies for two-digit subtractionStudents can subtract one- and two-digit numbers using part-part-whole reasoning and at least one mental strategy, and represent the problem as a number sentence.
