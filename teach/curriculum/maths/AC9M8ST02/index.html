Sampling methods and distribution analysis

AC9M8ST02 · Year 8 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse and report on the distribution of data from primary and secondary sources using random and non-random sampling techniques to select and study samples

Prior knowledge

Students can describe and compare distributions using displays and summary statistics (from AC9M7ST01, AC9M7ST02). Students understand random sampling informally.

By the end

Students compare random and non-random sampling methods, collect samples, analyse and report on distributions from primary and secondary sources.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating different methods of sampling used to collect data, considering the source and size of samples
  • comparing the sampling methods of simple random, systematic, stratified, quota, clustered or convenience, or judgement, and discussing the reliability of each
  • defining and distinguishing between probabilistic terms such as random, sample space, sample and sample distribution
  • investigating primary and secondary data sources relating to reconciliation between First Nations Australians and non-Indigenous Australians

Waypoints

  • 1. Comparing sampling methodsHingeStudents compare simple random, systematic, stratified and convenience sampling methods and evaluate the reliability of each.
  • 2. Analysing and reporting on sample distributionsStudents collect or obtain a sample, analyse the distribution, and report findings from primary or secondary sources.
