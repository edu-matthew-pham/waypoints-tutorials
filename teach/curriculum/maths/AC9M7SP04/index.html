Algorithms for classifying shapes

AC9M7SP04 · Year 7 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

design and create algorithms involving a sequence of steps and decisions that will sort and classify sets of shapes according to their attributes, and describe how the algorithms work

Prior knowledge

Students can classify triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons by properties (from AC9M7SP02). Students have experience writing step-by-step instructions and using decision trees informally.

By the end

Students design and describe algorithms (using flowcharts or pseudocode) that sort and classify shapes by their attributes.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • creating a classification scheme for triangles based on sides and angles, using a flow chart using sequences and decisions
  • creating a flow chart or hierarchy for quadrilaterals that shows the relationships between trapeziums, parallelograms, rhombuses, rectangles, squares and kites
  • creating a classification scheme for regular, irregular, concave or convex polygons that are sorted according to the number of sides

Waypoints

  • 1. Designing a classification algorithmHingeStudents design a step-by-step algorithm (as a flowchart or decision tree) that classifies shapes according to their attributes.
  • 2. Describing and evaluating algorithmsStudents describe in words how an algorithm works and evaluate its correctness by tracing through it with test cases.
