Tessellations and transformations

AC9M6SP03 · Year 6 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Recognise and use combinations of transformations to create tessellations and other geometric patterns, using dynamic geometric software where appropriate

Prior knowledge

Students can describe and perform translations, reflections and rotations of shapes and identify symmetries (AC9M5SP03). They understand each transformation individually. They have not yet combined transformations or used combinations to create tessellations.

By the end

Students can recognise and use combinations of transformations to create tessellations and other geometric patterns.

Waypoints

  • 1. Combining transformationsHingeStudents can apply a sequence of two or more transformations to a shape and describe the combined effect.
  • 2. What makes a tessellationStudents can identify what properties of a shape and transformation allow it to tessellate (tile a plane without gaps or overlaps).
  • 3. Creating tessellations and geometric patternsStudents can create a tessellation or geometric pattern using a specified combination of transformations and describe the rule generating the pattern.
