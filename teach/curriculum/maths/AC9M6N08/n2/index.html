← AC9M6N08 Approximate with rational numbers

Benchmark and mental estimation strategies

AC9M6N08 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 6 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can apply benchmark estimation strategies (using 0, ½, 1 as reference points; using 10% as a base for percentage estimates) to approximate rational number solutions.

Success criteria

  • Student applies at least two different estimation strategies to rational number problems and explains when each is useful.
  • Student uses estimation to check whether a calculated answer to a percentage or fraction problem is plausible.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Rounding rational numbers for estimation.

Width

Minimum
Use benchmark fractions (0, ½, 1) to estimate whether a fraction calculation result is less than, around, or greater than one; use 10% as a base to estimate other percentages.
Typical
Apply multiple estimation strategies to rational number problems: benchmark fractions, 10%-building for percentages, compatible numbers for decimals; select the most efficient strategy for a given problem; estimate financial calculations (e.g. approximate total after percentage discount).
Wider
  • Compare front-end, rounding, and benchmark strategies on the same set of problems and evaluate which produces closer estimates
  • Investigate contexts where estimation is the expected output (e.g. reading graphs, navigating, budgeting) and distinguish from contexts requiring exact answers

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyEstimation fluency comes from having multiple strategies available and recognising which applies. Students need to accumulate a repertoire — not just know one estimation method — to handle the diversity of rational number problems they will encounter.
