Exponential functions

AC9M10A03 · Year 10 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise the connection between algebraic and graphical representations of exponential relations and solve related exponential equations, using digital tools where appropriate

Prior knowledge

Students can experiment with parameter variation on graphs and generalise emerging patterns using digital tools (AC9M9A06). They have not yet worked specifically with exponential relations — identifying them from tables, connecting their algebraic and graphical representations, or solving exponential equations.

By the end

Students recognise exponential relations from tables, equations and graphs; connect algebraic and graphical representations of exponential functions; and solve related exponential equations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • recognising that in a table of values, if the ratio between consecutive values of the dependent variable is constant, then it is an exponential relation
  • investigating the links between algebraic and graphical representations of exponential functions using graphing software
  • using digital tools with symbolic manipulation functionality to systematically explore exponential relations
  • investigating First Nations Australian Ranger groups' and other groups' programs that attempt to eradicate feral animals for survival of native animals on Country/Place, exploring the competition between feral and native animals and their impact on natural resources by formulating exponential equations for population growth

Waypoints

  • 1. Recognising exponential relationsHingeStudents can identify an exponential relation from a table of values by recognising a constant multiplicative ratio between consecutive dependent variable values.
  • 2. Graphical features of exponential functionsHingeStudents can sketch or interpret the graph of an exponential function, identifying key features including the y-intercept, asymptote, and whether it represents growth or decay.
  • 3. Connecting algebraic and graphical representationsStudents can move fluently between the algebraic equation and graphical representation of an exponential relation, reading key information from each form.
  • 4. Solving exponential equationsStudents can solve simple exponential equations by writing both sides in terms of a common base and equating exponents.
