Integers and Cartesian plane

AC9M6N01 · Year 6 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Recognise situations, including financial contexts, that use integers; locate and represent integers on a number line and as coordinates on the Cartesian plane

Prior knowledge

Students can express natural numbers as products of factors, recognise multiples and determine divisibility (AC9M5N02). They can use mathematical modelling to solve additive and multiplicative problems including financial contexts (AC9M5N09). They have not yet encountered negative integers or coordinate systems extending beyond positive values.

By the end

Students can recognise real-world situations that require integers, locate positive and negative integers on a number line, and represent integer coordinates on all regions of the Cartesian plane.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • extending the number line in the negative direction to locate and represent integers, recognising the difference in location between negative and positive integers
  • using integers to represent quantities in financial contexts, including the concept of profit and loss for a planned event
  • using horizontal and vertical number lines to represent and find solutions to everyday problems involving locating and ordering integers around zero
  • recognising that the sign (positive or negative) indicates a direction in relation to zero
  • representing the temperatures of the different planets in the solar system, using a diagram of a thermometer that models a vertical number line

Waypoints

  • 1. Meaning of integersStudents can explain what integers are, distinguish positive and negative integers from whole numbers, and give real-world examples of situations where negative integers are necessary.
  • 2. Integers on a number lineHingeStudents can locate and represent positive and negative integers on a number line, order integers including negative values, and describe the distance between integers.
  • 3. Integer coordinates — first quadrant extensionHingeStudents can extend their understanding of grid coordinates to include negative values on both axes and locate points with integer coordinates in all four quadrants of the Cartesian plane.
  • 4. Integers in financial and real-world modellingStudents can recognise and use integers in financial contexts, represent financial situations on number lines, and interpret positive and negative integer values in terms of the situation.
