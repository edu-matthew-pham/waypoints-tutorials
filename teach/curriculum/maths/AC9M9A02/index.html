Binomial products and factorisation

AC9M9A02 · Year 9 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

simplify algebraic expressions, expand binomial products and factorise monic quadratic expressions

Prior knowledge

Students can apply exponent laws to numerical and algebraic expressions (AC9M9A01) and manipulate linear expressions using algebraic properties (AC9M8A01). They have not yet expanded products of two binomials or factorised monic quadratic expressions.

By the end

Students expand binomial products and factorise monic quadratic expressions with integer coefficients, recognising expansion and factorisation as inverse operations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • expanding combinations of binomials such as (x+7)(x+8), (x+7)(x−8), (x−7)(x+8), (x−7)(x−8) to identify expansion and factorisation patterns related to (x+a)(x+b) = x²+(a+b)x+ab, where a and b are integers
  • using manipulatives such as algebra tiles or area models to expand or factorise; e.g. (x+1)(x+3) = x²+4x+3, (x−5)² = x²−10x+25
  • recognising the relationship between expansion and factorisation, using digital tools to explore factorisation of x²+mx+n where m and n are integers

Waypoints

  • 1. Expanding binomial productsHingeStudents can expand a product of two linear binomials using the distributive property and simplify the result.
  • 2. Generalising the expansion patternStudents can identify the pattern (x+a)(x+b) = x²+(a+b)x+ab and use it to expand binomial products and predict features of the result.
  • 3. Factorising monic quadraticsHingeStudents can factorise monic quadratic expressions x²+bx+c with integer roots by finding two integers whose product is c and sum is b.
  • 4. Expansion and factorisation as inverse operationsStudents can move fluently between expanded and factorised forms of monic quadratics and explain the relationship between the two operations.
