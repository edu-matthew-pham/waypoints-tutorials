Efficient addition and multiplication strategies

AC9M4N06 · Year 4 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Develop efficient strategies and use appropriate digital tools for solving problems involving addition and subtraction, and multiplication and division where there is no remainder

Prior knowledge

Students can add and subtract two- and three-digit numbers using place value (AC9M3N03) and multiply and divide one- and two-digit numbers (AC9M3N04). They have not yet developed efficient strategies for multi-digit multiplication or applied digital tools strategically.

By the end

Students develop and apply efficient strategies for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division problems with larger numbers, selecting strategies appropriately and using digital tools where helpful.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using an array to develop and explain the area model for multiplication of two- and three-digit numbers by one-digit numbers
  • using basic facts and knowledge of place value to develop and explain efficient mental computation strategies
  • using digital tools such as calculators or spreadsheets to check and extend solutions

Waypoints

  • 1. Efficient addition and subtraction strategiesStudents can apply and explain at least two efficient strategies for multi-digit addition or subtraction.
  • 2. Multiplication strategies for larger numbersStudents can apply area model or partitioning strategies to multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number.
  • 3. Division without remaindersStudents can solve division problems without remainders using known multiplication facts and sharing/grouping strategies.
