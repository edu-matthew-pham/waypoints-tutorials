← AC9M8A04 Experimenting with linear functions

Effect of intercept and generalising patterns

AC9M8A04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 8 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students investigate the effect of varying the y-intercept b, and generalise patterns including the behaviour of parallel lines.

Lessons on this site

  • What c does to a line

Success criteria

  • Correctly states the effect of varying b on the graph.
  • Identifies that lines with equal gradient are parallel.
  • States a generalisation supported by evidence from their investigation.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Effect of gradient parameter on linear graphs.

Width

Minimum
Vary b in y = mx + b; describe the effect on the graph and state what b controls.
Typical
Vary both m and b systematically; generalise that lines with the same m are parallel; investigate integer solutions to two-variable equations using digital tools.
Wider
  • Investigate how linear functions are used in linear regression models in machine learning — describe what the gradient and intercept represent in this context.
  • Explore integer solutions to 2x + 3y = 48 — use digital tools to find all solutions and represent them on the Cartesian plane.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepGeneralising about linear functions requires encountering the pattern across multiple parameter combinations. Students who only investigate one direction miss the interaction between m and b.
