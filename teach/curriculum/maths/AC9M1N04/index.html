Add and subtract within 20

AC9M1N04 · Year 1 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Add and subtract numbers within 20, using physical and virtual materials, part-part-whole knowledge to 10 and a variety of calculation strategies

Prior knowledge

Students can represent practical addition and subtraction situations using materials and counting strategies (AC9MFN05). They can partition and combine collections to 10 using part-part-whole relationships (AC9MFN04). They have not yet applied systematic calculation strategies (counting on, bridging through 10, partitioning) to add and subtract within 20.

By the end

Students add and subtract numbers within 20 using physical and virtual materials, part-part-whole knowledge to 10 and a variety of calculation strategies.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using drawings, physical and virtual materials and number combinations within 10 to add and subtract collections to 20
  • adding and subtracting numbers within 20, using a variety of representations and strategies such as counting on, counting back, partitioning and part-part-whole knowledge of numbers to 10; for example, using partitioning and combining 7+5=7+3+2=10+2=12
  • developing and using strategies for one-digit addition and subtraction based on part-part-whole relationships for each of the numbers to 10 and subitising with physical and virtual materials
  • representing story problems involving addition and subtraction of numbers within 20 using a Think Board, recognising and using + and – symbols and the equal sign
  • creating and performing addition and subtraction stories told through First Nations Australians' dances

Waypoints

  • 1. Counting-on and counting-back strategiesHingeStudents can add and subtract within 20 using counting-on from the larger number and counting-back strategies, recording with + and − symbols and the equal sign.
  • 2. Bridging through 10HingeStudents can add numbers within 20 by partitioning one addend to bridge through 10, using part-part-whole knowledge.
  • 3. Subtraction strategies within 20Students can subtract within 20 using a range of strategies including counting-back, using related addition facts, and partitioning, choosing strategies based on the numbers involved.
