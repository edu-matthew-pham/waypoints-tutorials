← AC9M8M07 Modelling with ratios and rates

Formulating and solving ratio/rate models

AC9M8M07 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Year 8 Maths · Measurement · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students identify whether a problem requires a ratio or rate model, formulate it, and solve in context.

Lessons on this site

  • Ratio and rate models

Success criteria

  • Correctly identifies whether the problem requires a ratio or rate model.
  • Formulates and solves the model correctly.
  • Justifies the model choice and interprets the solution in context.

Prior knowledge

Students can solve ratio and rate problems (from AC9M7M06, AC9M8M05). Students have modelling experience from prior years.

Width

Minimum
Formulate and solve a practical problem using either a ratio or rate model; state which is appropriate and why.
Typical
Apply modelling to scales and plans, chemical mixing, currency conversion, and income tax; justify model choice; select efficient calculation strategy.
Wider
  • Investigate radiocarbon dating as a ratio application: model the ratio of carbon-14 to carbon-12 isotopes in organic material to estimate age.
  • Model the ratio applications in First Nations Australians' string and cordage making — investigate the ratio of fibre lengths and twisting rates involved.

Hinge

Node 2 requires evaluating model appropriateness — students who have only formulated one model type cannot identify when the chosen model oversimplifies or when a different structure would be more appropriate.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepRatio and rate modelling problems span an unusually wide range of contexts at Year 8. Multiple varied contexts are needed to build the judgment to select the right model structure.
