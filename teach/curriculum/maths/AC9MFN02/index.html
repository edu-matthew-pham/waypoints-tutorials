Subitising to 5

AC9MFN02 · Foundation Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recognise and name the number of objects within a collection up to 5 using subitising

Prior knowledge

Students can count small collections of objects using one-to-one correspondence. They have not yet recognised quantities instantly without counting — subitising as a deliberate strategy is new.

By the end

Students recognise and name the number of objects within a collection up to 5 using subitising, without counting.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • recognising how many objects are in a collection or in images on a card with a quick look and saying the associated number without counting
  • playing instructive card games that rely on the recognition of numbers represented in different ways; for example, playing memory games, matching pairs of quantities on dot cards or similar where the arrangement on each is different; using subitising to compare and order collections

Waypoints

  • 1. Instant recognition to 3HingeStudents can recognise and name collections of 1, 2 and 3 objects instantly, without counting.
  • 2. Extending subitising to 5Students can recognise and name collections of 4 and 5 objects using subitising.
