← AC9M3A03 Multiplication facts 3, 4, 5 and 10

Fluency and division fact extension

AC9M3A03 n3 · waypoint 3 of 3 · Year 3 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can recall multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 with proficiency and immediately state the related division facts.

Success criteria

  • Students can state any multiplication fact for 3, 4, 5 or 10 without hesitation.
  • Students can immediately state the two related division facts for any given multiplication fact.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Building the 4 times table and deriving related facts.

Width

Minimum
Recall any multiplication fact for 3, 4, 5 or 10 and immediately state both related division facts.
Typical
Demonstrate fluent recall across all 3, 4, 5 and 10 times tables; derive and use related division facts in calculation contexts.
Wider
  • Systematically explore algorithms using the multiplication facts — e.g. finding all numbers up to 100 that are divisible by 3, 4, 5 or 10
  • Investigate commutativity across tables: use 4×7=28 to derive 7×4, connecting to less-familiar combinations

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsFact fluency is the explicit goal — 'recall and demonstrate proficiency' requires automaticity, which is built through practice after understanding. Students who understand patterns but hesitate under time pressure need consolidation not more breadth.
