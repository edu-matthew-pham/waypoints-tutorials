Multiplication facts 3, 4, 5 and 10

AC9M3A03 · Year 3 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

recall and demonstrate proficiency with multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10; extend and apply facts to develop the related division facts

Prior knowledge

Students can recall and demonstrate proficiency with multiplication facts for twos; extend and apply facts to develop related division facts (AC9M2A03). They have not yet developed automatic recall of 3, 4, 5 and 10 times tables.

By the end

Students recall multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 with proficiency and derive the related division facts.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using concrete or virtual manipulatives, groups and repeated addition to recognise patterns and establish the 3, 4, 5 and 10 multiplication facts
  • recognising that when they multiply a number by 5, the resulting number will either end in 0 or 5
  • practising calculating and deriving multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10, explaining and recalling the patterns
  • systematically exploring algorithms used for repeated addition and using them to establish multiplication facts

Waypoints

  • 1. Building the 3, 5 and 10 times tablesHingeStudents can demonstrate and explain the pattern in the 3, 5 and 10 multiplication facts using skip-counting, grouping or repeated addition.
  • 2. Building the 4 times table and deriving related factsHingeStudents can recall the 4 times table and explain its relationship to the 2 times table (doubling).
  • 3. Fluency and division fact extensionStudents can recall multiplication facts for 3, 4, 5 and 10 with proficiency and immediately state the related division facts.
