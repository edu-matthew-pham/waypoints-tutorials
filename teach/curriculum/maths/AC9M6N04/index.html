Add and subtract decimals

AC9M6N04 · Year 6 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Apply knowledge of place value to add and subtract decimals, using digital tools where appropriate; use estimation and rounding to check the reasonableness of answers

Prior knowledge

Students can interpret, compare and order numbers with more than 2 decimal places using place value understanding (AC9M5N01). They can solve problems involving multiplication of larger numbers and use estimation to check reasonableness (AC9M5N06). They have not yet systematically applied place value to addition and subtraction of decimals with different numbers of decimal places.

By the end

Students can apply place value knowledge to add and subtract decimals, use digital tools where appropriate, and use estimation and rounding to check the reasonableness of answers.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • applying estimation strategies to addition and subtraction of decimals to at least thousandths before calculating
  • applying whole-number strategies including basic facts, place value, partitioning and inverse relationships
  • working additively with linear measurements expressed as decimals up to 2 and 3 decimal places
  • deciding to use a calculator as a calculation strategy for solving additive problems involving decimals that vary in their number of decimal places

Waypoints

  • 1. Place value alignment for decimal operationsHingeStudents can align decimal numbers by place value to set up addition and subtraction calculations, including numbers with different numbers of decimal places.
  • 2. Adding and subtracting decimalsHingeStudents can accurately add and subtract decimal numbers with up to three decimal places using written and mental strategies.
  • 3. Estimation and rounding as a reasonableness checkStudents can round decimal numbers to check the reasonableness of addition and subtraction results, and identify when a calculated answer is unreasonable.
