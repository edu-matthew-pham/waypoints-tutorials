← AC9M1SP01 Compare and classify shapes

Tactile and environmental recognition

AC9M1SP01 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 1 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify familiar shapes by touch alone and recognise familiar shapes in objects and structures in the environment.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Correctly names a shape identified by touch, using a property to justify the identification.
  • Identifies at least two instances of a named shape in the environment.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Classify by shared properties.

Width

Minimum
Identify a shape from a bag by feel and name it; find at least two instances of a named shape in the classroom environment.
Typical
Identify a range of shapes by feel (circle, triangle, square, rectangle, oval) and explain the properties used to identify each; recognise familiar shapes in a range of environmental objects; compare 3D objects and identify which 2D shapes are visible on their faces.
Wider
  • Conduct a shape walk in a broader environment (school grounds, local area), photographing or sketching instances of each shape type and creating a class display of shapes found in the environment.
  • Explore shapes in First Nations Australian art and craft, identifying and classifying the shapes used and connecting them to the shapes found on Country/Place.
