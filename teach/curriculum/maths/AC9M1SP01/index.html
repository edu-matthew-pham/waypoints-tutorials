Compare and classify shapes

AC9M1SP01 · Year 1 Maths · Space

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Make, compare and classify familiar shapes; recognise familiar shapes and objects in the environment, identifying the similarities and differences between them

Prior knowledge

Students can sort, name and create familiar shapes and recognise them in the environment (AC9MFSP01). They have not yet classified shapes by comparing multiple properties simultaneously, nor distinguished between regular and irregular instances of the same shape type.

By the end

Students make, compare and classify familiar shapes; recognise familiar shapes and objects in the environment, identifying the similarities and differences between them.

Waypoints

  • 1. Classify by shared propertiesStudents can sort a collection of shapes into groups based on shared properties and explain what is the same within a group and different between groups.
  • 2. Tactile and environmental recognitionStudents can identify familiar shapes by touch alone and recognise familiar shapes in objects and structures in the environment.
  • 3. Make and compare shapesStudents can make familiar shapes using materials and compare shapes built from the same materials, explaining the similarities and differences.
