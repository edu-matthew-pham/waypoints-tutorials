← AC9M1N03 Quantify sets by skip counting

Skip count the groups

AC9M1N03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 1 Maths · Number · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can skip count a grouped collection to find the total and connect the count to the digit representation of the total.

Lessons on this site

None yet.

Success criteria

  • Skip counts correctly across a grouped collection to state the total.
  • Includes any remaining ungrouped objects in the final count.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Group into equal sets.

Width

Minimum
Skip count by 5s or 10s to find the total of a grouped collection of at least 50 objects; state the total and connect it to the count of groups.
Typical
Skip count by 2s, 5s or 10s over grouped collections to find totals to at least 120; include any ungrouped remainder in the count; record the total; connect the number of groups and the group size to the resulting total.
Wider
  • Apply skip counting to count collections of Australian coins by denomination (5c, 10c, $1, $2), connecting skip counting to practical money contexts.
  • Count the same large collection using different group sizes, comparing the number of groups produced and the skip counting sequence used, and discussing which is most efficient.

Hinge

Skip counting fluency is required for Node 3 — students who lose track of the count mid-sequence cannot reliably produce the total needed to connect groups to digit notation. Errors here prevent the place-value connection in Node 3 from working.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsSkip counting fluency for groups of 5 and 10 requires practice — the sequence must be automatic enough to apply reliably to counting tasks. Students who lose track mid-sequence need more practice with the sequence itself before applying it to collections.
