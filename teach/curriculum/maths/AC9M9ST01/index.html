Analysing survey reports

AC9M9ST01 · Year 9 Maths · Statistics

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse reports of surveys in digital media and elsewhere for information on how data was obtained to estimate population means and medians

Prior knowledge

Students can compare distributions from random samples and recognise the effect of sample size on variation (AC9M8ST03). They have not yet critically analysed survey reports for methodology, or evaluated whether reported means and medians are reliable estimates of population parameters.

By the end

Students analyse survey reports to evaluate how data was obtained and assess the reliability of claims about population means and medians.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • investigating and evaluating statistical reports in the media by linking claims to displays, statistics and representative data
  • investigating the use of statistics in reports about Australia's trade with Asia region countries
  • exploring how NLP tools can be used to analyse large volumes of survey reports, discussing benefits and limitations
  • investigating a range of data and its sources; e.g. age of residents in Australia, Cambodia and Tonga; subjects studied by 14-year-olds across multiple countries
  • analysing reports of public opinion surveys on environmental issues; discussing data collection methods and the reasonableness of inferences

Waypoints

  • 1. How data is obtained — sampling methods in survey reportsHingeStudents can identify from a survey report how data was obtained and explain how the sampling method could affect the representativeness of the sample.
  • 2. Evaluating statistical claims — means and mediansStudents can evaluate a claim about a population mean or median by linking it back to how the data was collected and what the data displays show.
