← AC9M3M02 Measuring with instruments

Measuring and comparing across attributes

AC9M3M02 n2 · waypoint 2 of 2 · Year 3 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can measure objects for length, mass and capacity using appropriate instruments and compare results using metric language.

Success criteria

  • Students can use an appropriate instrument to measure an object for length, mass or capacity.
  • Students can compare two measurements using correct metric units and comparison language.

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Reading labelled markings on instruments.

Width

Minimum
Measure and record two objects for each of length, mass and capacity; make at least one comparison statement for each attribute.
Typical
Measure a range of objects across all three attributes; compare using metric units and comparative language; connect measurements to real-world context.
Wider
  • Conduct a measurement investigation comparing objects across contexts (e.g. science measurement tasks) and discuss why precision matters in the context
  • Investigate what happens when you measure the same object with different instruments — exploring measurement variation and its sources

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierCross-attribute measurement integrates reading skill (node 1) with instrument selection (from AC9M3M01) and the comparison of results. Students who struggle here often have a gap in instrument reading or unit identification.
