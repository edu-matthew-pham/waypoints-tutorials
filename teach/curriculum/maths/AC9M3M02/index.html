Measuring with instruments

AC9M3M02 · Year 3 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

measure and compare objects using familiar metric units of length, mass and capacity, and instruments with labelled markings

Prior knowledge

Students can identify which metric units are used to measure everyday items and use benchmarks to make estimates (AC9M3M01). They have not yet used measuring instruments with labelled metric markings to produce precise measurements.

By the end

Students measure and compare objects using metric units of length, mass and capacity with appropriately marked measuring instruments.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • making a measuring tape using metric units and using it to measure and compare things
  • using centimetre grid paper to measure length and connecting with centimetre units on a ruler
  • measuring and comparing the mass of objects and capacity of containers using kitchen scales and measuring jugs
  • interpreting and explaining what the lines on a measuring jug or scales mean
  • comparing the capacity of different beakers used in science lessons

Waypoints

  • 1. Reading labelled markings on instrumentsHingeStudents can read a measurement from a ruler, measuring jug or scales by identifying the value at a marked graduation.
  • 2. Measuring and comparing across attributesStudents can measure objects for length, mass and capacity using appropriate instruments and compare results using metric language.
