Quarters through repeated halving

AC9M2N03 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 2 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can create quarters by halving a half, and name each part as one-quarter.

Success criteria

  • Creates quarters by halving a half using a material or shape
  • Names each of the four equal parts as one-quarter and confirms they are equal in size

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: One-half as equal parts.

Width

Minimum
Fold or divide a shape or object in half, then fold the half in half again; name each of the four equal parts as one-quarter.
Typical
Create quarters of shapes, objects and collections using repeated halving; demonstrate that a quarter is a half of a half; name parts and compare sizes.
Wider
  • Investigate how food items (sandwiches, pizzas, slices) can be cut into quarters by halving twice; confirm all parts are equal
  • Explore halves and quarters in real measurement contexts: half a cup, quarter of a metre length, half an hour
