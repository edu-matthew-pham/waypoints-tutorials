Gradient, midpoint and distance

AC9M9A03 · Year 9 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

find the gradient of a line segment, the midpoint of the line interval and the distance between 2 distinct points on the Cartesian plane

Prior knowledge

Students can graph linear relations on the Cartesian plane and solve linear equations (AC9M8A02). They have not yet formally derived gradient from two coordinate pairs, calculated midpoints algebraically, or applied Pythagoras' theorem to find distance between points.

By the end

Students calculate the gradient, midpoint and distance for any two distinct points on the Cartesian plane and apply these in context.

Waypoints

  • 1. Gradient from two pointsHingeStudents can calculate the gradient of a line segment between two given points and explain why gradient is independent of which two points on the line are chosen.
  • 2. Parallel and perpendicular linesHingeStudents can use gradient to determine whether two lines are parallel or perpendicular.
  • 3. Distance between two pointsStudents can calculate the distance between two points on the Cartesian plane using Pythagoras' theorem.
  • 4. Midpoint of a line intervalStudents can calculate the midpoint of a line interval between two given points.
  • 5. Applying coordinate geometry in contextStudents can apply gradient, midpoint and distance in combination to solve problems in context, including interpreting gradient as rate of change.
