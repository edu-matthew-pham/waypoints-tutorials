Equivalent fractions and decimals

AC9M4N03 · Year 4 Maths · Number

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Find equivalent representations of fractions using related denominators and make connections between fractions and decimal notation

Prior knowledge

Students can recognise unit fractions (1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/10) and their multiples (AC9M3N02) and can recognise halves, quarters and eighths through repeated halving (AC9M2N03). They have not yet found equivalent fractions using related denominators or connected fractions to decimal notation.

By the end

Students find equivalent fractions using related denominators and connect fraction notation to decimal notation using models and measurement.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • extending fraction families within collections of materials; for example, seeing 3/4 as 3 in each 4, showing this within related fractions like 6/8
  • creating models of equivalent fractions by subdividing capacity measures into smaller fractions
  • folding paper to show equivalence between different fractions
  • identifying and using the connection between fractions of metres and decimals
  • using array diagrams to show the relationship between fractions and division and multiplication of natural numbers

Waypoints

  • 1. Equivalent fractions — same quantity, different namesHingeStudents can find at least two equivalent fractions for a given fraction using related denominators, and explain what makes them equivalent.
  • 2. Fraction–decimal connectionsStudents can connect familiar fractions to their decimal equivalents using measurement models.
