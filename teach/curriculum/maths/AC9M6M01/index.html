Convert metric units

AC9M6M01 · Year 6 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Convert between common metric units of length, mass and capacity; choose and use decimal representations of metric measurements relevant to the context of a problem

Prior knowledge

Students can choose appropriate metric units when measuring length, mass and capacity (AC9M5M01). They can multiply and divide by powers of 10 (AC9M6N06). They have not yet systematically applied decimal representations in metric conversions or selected appropriate units for problem contexts.

By the end

Students can convert between common metric units of length, mass and capacity and choose and use decimal representations of metric measurements relevant to a problem context.

Waypoints

  • 1. Metric conversion as powers-of-10 operationHingeStudents can explain that metric conversions involve multiplying or dividing by powers of 10 and apply this to convert between adjacent metric units.
  • 2. Decimal representations of metric measurementsStudents can express metric measurements using decimal notation and choose the representation most relevant to the context.
  • 3. Metric conversions in problem solvingStudents can identify when unit conversion is necessary to solve a problem, perform the conversion, and use the result appropriately.
