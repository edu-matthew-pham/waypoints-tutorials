Modelling with linear functions

AC9M8A03 · Year 8 Maths · Algebra

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

use mathematical modelling to solve applied problems involving linear relations, including financial contexts; formulate problems with linear functions, choosing a representation; interpret and communicate solutions in terms of the situation, reviewing the appropriateness of the model

Prior knowledge

Students can graph linear relations and solve linear equations (from AC9M8A02). Students have experience with mathematical modelling (from AC9M7N09).

By the end

Students formulate linear function models for applied problems, choose appropriate representations, solve and interpret solutions, and review model appropriateness.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • modelling situations involving linear functions, including practical contexts such as taxi fares involving flag fall fees, motion in a straight line
  • modelling problems in practical situations and interpreting solutions within the context of the problem, including giving attention to all units of measurement
  • modelling financial problems involving pay rates, using a table of values to represent the pay amounts and hours worked using an hourly rate of pay
  • modelling patterns on Country/Place and exploring their connections and meaning to linear equations, using the model as a predictive tool

Waypoints

  • 1. Formulating linear modelsHingeStudents identify a linear relationship in a practical context, formulate it as a function y = mx + b, and identify the meaning of gradient and intercept in context.
  • 2. Solving, interpreting and reviewing the modelStudents solve a linear model for a given value, interpret the solution in context, and evaluate whether the model is appropriate for the situation.
