Full and half turns

AC9M2M05 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Year 2 Maths · Measurement

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Maths: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify, describe and demonstrate a full turn and a half turn in everyday contexts.

Success criteria

  • Demonstrates a full turn and a half turn correctly with body or object
  • Describes both turns in words (e.g. 'a half turn faces you the opposite way')

Prior knowledge

Students give and follow directions using positional and movement language (AC9M1SP02). They have not yet identified fractional turns as measures of rotation or connected turns to the concept of angle.

Width

Minimum
Demonstrate a full turn and a half turn with their body or an object; describe each in words.
Typical
Identify, describe and demonstrate full and half turns in everyday contexts: spinning, door handles, tap handles, equipment dials; draw labelled diagrams showing direction and amount of turn.
Wider
  • Identify things that turn in the school environment (handles, dials, switches); demonstrate half and full turns for each; label with arrows showing direction
  • Connect half and full turns to half-past and o'clock on a clock face
