Food selection and healthy eating

AC9TDE4K04 · Years 3–4 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

describe the ways food can be selected and prepared for healthy eating

Prior knowledge

Students can explore how food can be selected and prepared for healthy eating (AC9TDE2K04). They have explored food selection at a general level. They have not yet described specific selection criteria, examined food technologies for health and safety, or compared fresh and processed foods by nutritional content.

By the end

Students describe the ways food can be selected and prepared for healthy eating, including how food technologies support food safety and nutrient retention and how sensory properties inform selection.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring how First Nations Australians consider the nutrient content of seasonal foods as a means of maintaining a balanced diet
  • recognising the benefits food technologies provide for health and food safety and to ensure that a wide variety of food is available and can be prepared for healthy eating, for example pasteurisation of milk for food safety and freezing of vegetables to retain nutrients and reduce food waste
  • exploring the differences between fresh food and processed food by researching nutrient content of fresh food using a nutrition calculator app and examining nutrition information labels on processed foods for nutritional values
  • considering creative ways foods can be prepared for maximum taste and appeal, for example locating and discussing images online that show colourful or fun ways to present food that might encourage healthy eating
  • describing foods using the senses, for example describing the colour, aroma, sound, texture and taste of the ingredients in a salad or stir-fry and how our senses influence what we select to eat

Waypoints

  • 1. How food is selected for healthy eatingHingeStudents can describe factors that influence food selection for healthy eating, including nutrient content and sensory properties
  • 2. Food technologies and their role in health and safetyStudents can describe what food technologies do and explain how they support food safety, nutrient retention or food availability
  • 3. Fresh vs processed food and preparation choicesStudents can describe differences between fresh and processed foods in terms of nutritional value and explain how preparation choices affect healthy eating
