Analyse needs, develop design brief, select materials

AC9TDE10P01 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse needs or opportunities for designing; develop design briefs; and investigate, analyse and select materials, systems, components, tools and equipment to create designed solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can analyse needs or opportunities for designing, and investigate and select materials, systems, components, tools and equipment (AC9TDE8P01). They can develop basic design briefs. They have not yet developed design briefs independently that incorporate universal design or Safety by Design principles, nor analysed intellectual property rights in design contexts, nor interviewed community members to identify design needs.

By the end

Students independently analyse needs or opportunities for designing; develop design briefs that incorporate user needs and ethical considerations; and investigate, analyse and select materials, systems, components, tools and equipment for designed solutions.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • analysing First Nations Australians' traditional grains for their potential for providing nutritional and commercial solutions
  • analysing the design of new products to identify how well design ideas respond to sustainability issues
  • developing design briefs that take into consideration the needs of users, for example universal design principles or Safety by Design principles
  • analysing a range of design and technologies ideas including those that draw on the intellectual property of others, including Indigenous cultural and intellectual property rights
  • considering the needs of community groups to identify rich design tasks, for example interviewing community members about accessibility requirements
  • examining tools, techniques, equipment and relationships of properties for complementary materials

Waypoints

  • 1. Analysing needs and design opportunitiesHingeStudents can analyse a need or opportunity for designing, including considering the needs of diverse users and communities.
  • 2. Developing a design briefHingeStudents can independently develop a design brief that defines the problem, identifies user needs, and establishes the scope and constraints of a designed solution.
  • 3. Investigating, analysing and selecting materials, systems, components, tools and equipmentStudents can investigate, analyse and select appropriate materials, systems, components, tools and equipment for a design project with justification.
