Test and refine design ideas

AC9TDE8P02 · Years 7–8 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas, processes and solutions using technical terms and graphical representation techniques, including using digital tools

Prior knowledge

Students can generate, iterate and communicate design ideas, decisions and processes using technical terms and graphical representation techniques, including using digital tools (AC9TDE6P02). They have generated and communicated ideas but have not yet systematically tested ideas through prototyping, used multiple generation strategies, or produced formal production drawings with scale and symbols.

By the end

Students generate design ideas using multiple strategies, test ideas through models and prototypes, iterate based on testing, and communicate ideas and processes using annotated concept sketches, production drawings with technical terms, scale and symbols, and digital tools.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using a variety of strategies such as brainstorming, sketching, 3D modelling and experimenting to generate innovative design ideas to present to others
  • considering which ideas to further explore and investigating the benefits and drawbacks of ideas including identifying factors that may hinder or enhance project development, for example using digital polling to capture the views of different groups in the community to inform the production of a solution designed with intercultural understanding
  • developing models, prototypes or samples using a range of materials, tools and equipment to test the functionality of ideas
  • producing annotated concept sketches and drawings, using technical terms, scale, symbols, pictorial and aerial views to draw environments; production drawings, perspective drawings, orthogonal drawings; patterns and templates to explain product design ideas
  • documenting and communicating the generation and development and selection of design ideas for an intended audience, for example developing a digital portfolio with images and text which clearly communicate each step of a design process

Waypoints

  • 1. Generating ideas with multiple strategiesHingeStudents can use a variety of strategies to generate design ideas rather than committing to a first idea
  • 2. Testing through models and prototypesStudents can develop models, prototypes or samples to test the functionality of design ideas before committing to production
  • 3. Communicating with technical drawingsStudents can communicate design ideas and processes using annotated concept sketches and production drawings with technical terms, scale and symbols
