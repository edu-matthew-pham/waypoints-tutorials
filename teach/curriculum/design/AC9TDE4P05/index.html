Sequence collaborative production

AC9TDE4P05 · Years 3–4 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

sequence steps to individually and collaboratively make designed solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can sequence steps for making designed solutions cooperatively (AC9TDE2P04). They have sequenced steps in a cooperative context. They have not yet worked with explicit planning tools (timelines, spreadsheets, flowcharts), managed time and resource allocation, or understood mass production processes.

By the end

Students sequence steps to individually and collaboratively make designed solutions, using planning tools to record steps and manage time and resource allocation.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • determining planning processes as a class, for example recording when parts of a project need to be completed on a timeline, in a spreadsheet, calendar or list
  • discussing the importance of managing time and resource allocation throughout production, for example discussing the roles different people might take in a team and identifying the tasks they will complete and the resources they will each need
  • identifying the steps in a mass production process, for example drawing a flowchart or making a video recording of a procedure for packing identical boxes of food for community members in need, where each student in a group has a separate task as part of the production process

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning and recording production stepsHingeStudents can plan a production sequence and record it using a planning tool such as a timeline, list or flowchart
  • 2. Managing time and resource allocationHingeStudents can identify roles and resources needed in a collaborative production task and explain how these are managed to complete the task
  • 3. Individual and collaborative production sequencesStudents can sequence steps for both individual and collaborative production tasks and explain how collaborative sequences differ from individual ones
