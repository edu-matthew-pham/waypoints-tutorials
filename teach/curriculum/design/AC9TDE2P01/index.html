Describe, draw and model ideas

AC9TDE2P01 · Years 1–2 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

generate and communicate design ideas through describing, drawing or modelling, including using digital tools

Prior knowledge

Students can generate and communicate design ideas through drawing or modelling (AC9TDEFP01). They have communicated ideas for a purpose. They have not yet labelled drawings, shown multiple views, used digital tools to communicate design ideas, or compared existing products to generate new ideas.

By the end

Students generate and communicate design ideas through describing, drawing or modelling — including labelled drawings with multiple views and using digital tools.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • comparing and contrasting features of existing products to develop new ideas, for example designing and making a puppet with a movable part after experimenting with other toys with several movable parts
  • communicating design ideas by modelling or producing and labelling 2-dimensional drawings using a range of technologies, for example designing a new environment such as a cubbyhouse or animal shelter and showing different views (top view and side view) with descriptions of materials and features
  • communicating an opinion about their design ideas, for example expressing own likes and dislikes about a design idea for felt finger puppets including how they have made changes to their design ideas
  • describing the results from exploring design ideas, for example recording the results from people taste-testing a food product

Waypoints

  • 1. Generating ideas from existing productsHingeStudents can compare and contrast features of existing products to generate new design ideas
  • 2. Communicating design ideas through labelled drawings and modellingHingeStudents can communicate a design idea through a labelled 2D drawing or model, including showing different views and describing materials and features
  • 3. Using digital tools to communicate design ideasStudents can use a digital tool to communicate or record a design idea
