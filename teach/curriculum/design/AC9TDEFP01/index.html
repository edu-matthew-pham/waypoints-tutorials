Design and make solutions

AC9TDEFP01 · Foundation Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

generate, communicate and evaluate design ideas, and use materials, equipment and steps to safely make a solution for a purpose

Prior knowledge

This is the first Design and Technologies process descriptor. Students have no prior design process experience assumed. They bring general play and construction experience. They have not yet framed design as purposeful — generating ideas for a specific purpose, communicating those ideas in a design form, making a solution, or evaluating against a purpose.

By the end

Students generate and communicate at least one design idea for a purpose, use materials and equipment safely to make a solution, and evaluate what they made using personal preferences.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying a purpose for designing and making a solution, for example the sand keeps blowing out of the sandpit, the birds keep flying into the waste bin and taking food scraps or people with disability need to know where they can park at school
  • exploring ideas by drawing or modelling and choosing the most suitable idea, for example drawing or modelling designs for bee hotels to attract native bees to the school garden and choosing one to make, and changing perspectives from front view to plan view
  • evaluating what they have made using personal preferences, for example using a smiley face Likert scale
  • exploring how available materials can be used or re-used in construction play, for example using blocks and rain gutters or cardboard to make a ramp to roll a ball or toy car down
  • practising a range of technical skills safely using equipment, for example joining techniques when making a product from materials, such as a greenhouse to keep a seedling warm or a trellis for holding up tomato plants
  • assembling components of systems and checking they function as planned, for example making and testing a bowling, stacking or obstacle game with discarded food containers or packaging

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying a purpose and generating ideasHingeStudents can identify a purpose for designing and making, and generate at least one idea for a solution
  • 2. Communicating design ideasHingeStudents can communicate a design idea through drawing or modelling
  • 3. Making a solution safelyStudents can use materials, equipment and steps to safely make a designed solution
  • 4. Evaluating the solutionStudents can evaluate what they made using personal preferences
