Sensory and functional food properties for healthy eating

AC9TDE10K05 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse and make judgements on how the sensory and functional properties of food influence the design and preparation of sustainable food solutions for healthy eating

Prior knowledge

Students can analyse how the properties of foods determine their preparation and presentation techniques, and how health, food safety and sustainability affect food choices (AC9TDE8K05). They can identify sensory and nutritional properties of food. They have not yet made judgements on how these properties interact in designing sustainable food solutions, nor examined food preservation, trend-driven food innovation, or emerging food technologies.

By the end

Students analyse and make judgements on how sensory and functional food properties influence the design and preparation of sustainable food solutions for healthy eating — including food preservation, trends, and emerging technologies.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • analysing how First Nations Australians have long understood techniques to turn plant products into food sources with high nutritional value
  • experimenting with food preservation methods such as freezing and dehydrating to determine changes to food structure
  • conducting sensory and nutritional assessment testing of a range of foods to determine how these characteristics might be used to enhance food solutions
  • determining how the causes of food spoilage can be addressed when preparing, cooking, presenting and storing food items
  • reflecting on food trends and how they may influence choosing food or designing food solutions for healthy eating
  • investigating ways innovations may influence human health and sustainability, for example 3D printing of foods, Internet of Things (IoT) in the food supply chain
  • considering factors that influence the preparation and presentation of foods using a range of techniques to ensure optimum nutrient content, flavour, texture and visual appeal

Waypoints

  • 1. Sensory and nutritional assessment of foodHingeStudents can conduct or interpret sensory and nutritional assessments of foods and explain how these characteristics inform food solution design.
  • 2. Food preservation and spoilageHingeStudents can explain how food spoilage occurs and how preservation methods affect food structure, sensory properties and nutritional value.
  • 3. Food trends and sustainable healthy eatingStudents can analyse food trends and evaluate how they influence food solution design for healthy eating and sustainability.
