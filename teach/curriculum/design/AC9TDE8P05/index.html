Manage time, cost and production

AC9TDE8P05 · Years 7–8 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop project plans to individually and collaboratively manage time, cost and production of designed solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can develop project plans that include consideration of resources to individually and collaboratively make designed solutions (AC9TDE6P05). They have planned with resources in mind but have not yet managed time, cost and production as explicit planning variables, or used planning tools to track and evaluate progress.

By the end

Students develop project plans that explicitly manage time, cost and production of designed solutions, including interpreting drawings to identify resources, estimating time for production stages, identifying and minimising risks, and evaluating decisions throughout production.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • interpreting drawings to plan resources and production steps needed to produce products, services or environments for specific purposes, for example identifying resource requirements from specifications on a labelled drawing and collaboratively developing a detailed procedure
  • identifying risks and how to minimise them, organising time, evaluating decisions and managing resources to ensure successful project completion, for example using digital tools to keep track of tasks, resources, expenses and deadlines
  • investigating the time needed for each step of production, for example estimating time allocations on a planning template for the different stages of the design process needed to produce a clock, acoustic speaker or desk lamp using prior knowledge, research and testing

Waypoints

  • 1. Planning resources and steps from drawingsHingeStudents can interpret design drawings to identify the resources and production steps needed to produce a designed solution
  • 2. Managing time and costHingeStudents can estimate time allocations for production stages, track costs, and organise schedules using planning templates or digital tools
  • 3. Identifying project risks and contingenciesHingeStudents can identify project-level risks — time overruns, resource constraints, scope changes — and plan contingency strategies before production begins.
  • 4. Managing risks during productionStudents can apply planned risk mitigation strategies during production and respond to unexpected hazards as they arise.
  • 5. Evaluating decisions during productionStudents can evaluate production decisions at key points during making and adjust the plan when circumstances change.
  • 6. Reflecting on production after completionStudents can reflect on how production decisions held up against the plan and identify what they would do differently in a future project.
