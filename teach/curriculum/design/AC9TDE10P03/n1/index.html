Independent and safe production practice

AC9TDE10P03 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can set up and use tools, equipment and techniques independently and safely, applying appropriate personal protective equipment and safe working practices for the specific design project.

Success criteria

  • Sets up and operates at least one tool or piece of equipment independently
  • Applies appropriate safe working practices without being prompted

Prior knowledge

Students can select, justify and use suitable materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques safely (AC9TDE8P03). They have experience with safe production practices. They have not yet worked with full production independence, adapted processes to unforeseen challenges, or experimented with innovative material combinations at this level.

Width

Minimum
Independently set up and use at least one tool or piece of equipment relevant to the design project; demonstrate appropriate safe working practices without supervision prompts.
Typical
Demonstrate independent safe working practice across the production process — including selecting and setting up equipment appropriately, wearing appropriate PPE, identifying hazards in the production process, and applying risk-reduction strategies without prompting.
Wider
  • Independently set up a specific machine (e.g. lathe) to produce a part to specified dimensions — explain each setup step and the safety rationale for each
  • Review production safety for a group project and identify risks for each team member's role — develop safety protocols for the shared production environment

Hinge

Independent safe production is prerequisite for N2 — students who cannot operate tools and equipment safely and independently cannot conduct the experimental material combinations that N2 requires without constant supervision, which prevents genuine experimentation.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsSafe production practice is procedural and requires sufficient hands-on repetition to become genuinely independent. Students who have only observed or worked with close supervision are not yet operating independently. Practice in context-specific setups (the actual tools and materials of the project) is the path to independence.
