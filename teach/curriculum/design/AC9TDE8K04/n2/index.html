Ethical and sustainable techniques

AC9TDE8K04 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 7–8 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can analyse the ethical and sustainable techniques used in food and fibre production and evaluate their effectiveness

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least one ethical and one sustainable technique
  • Does not conflate ethical with sustainable — explains what each specifically achieves

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: How managed environments work.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least one ethical and one sustainable technique used in food or fibre production; explain what each achieves
Typical
Compare ethical and sustainable techniques across production systems — animal welfare practices, organic vs conventional methods, water management strategies; analyse trade-offs between sustainability and productivity
Wider
  • Investigate the relationship between animal nutrition strategies and product quality across different production systems
  • Research how food and fibre production contributes to Australia's food security and economy, including trade with Asian countries

Hinge

Students who conflate ethical and sustainable techniques, or treat sustainability as a single checkbox, will produce superficial evaluations at Node 3 — proposing improvements requires accurately distinguishing what each technique achieves and why.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents who cannot distinguish ethical from sustainable techniques (or who treat sustainability as a single checkbox) will produce superficial analyses. Seeing multiple ethical and sustainable techniques across different production contexts — animal welfare, organic farming, water management, cultural practices — builds the discrimination needed for genuine analysis.
