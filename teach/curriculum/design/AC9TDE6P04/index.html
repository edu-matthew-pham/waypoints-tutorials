Negotiate criteria and evaluate

AC9TDE6P04 · Years 5–6 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

Negotiate design criteria including sustainability to evaluate design ideas, processes and solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can use given or co-developed design criteria including sustainability to evaluate design ideas and solutions (AC9TDE4P04). They have applied provided or jointly created criteria. They have not yet negotiated criteria — the new demand is taking ownership of developing the criteria collaboratively, including sustainability as a non-optional factor.

By the end

Students negotiate design criteria — including sustainability — collaboratively, and use those criteria to evaluate design ideas, processes and solutions throughout a project.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • deciding on design criteria collaboratively for a designed solution, for example including an environmental sustainability criterion such as product should be recyclable
  • developing design criteria with others to evaluate the suitability of materials, tools and equipment for specific purposes, for example considering the most suitable fabric, tools and equipment needed to make beeswax wraps
  • iterating and modifying design ideas based on evaluation to improve solutions, for example modifying the sensitivity of sensors in the design of an automated light
  • evaluating their designed solutions including considering the benefits and costs of production processes and the environmental impact, for example for the production of an animal shelter
  • reflecting on designed solutions to evaluate and assess suitability and sustainability and determine how well they meet design criteria, for example gathering relevant data to make judgements about a school or community fundraising event in relation to waste reduction, attendance and funds raised, and considering how these aspects could be handled in future events

Waypoints

  • 1. Negotiating design criteria collaborativelyHingeStudents can collaborate to develop and agree on design criteria that will be used to evaluate a designed solution — including at least one sustainability criterion
  • 2. Evaluating solutions against criteria — including processes and sustainabilityStudents can evaluate design ideas, production processes and finished solutions against negotiated criteria — including sustainability — and identify where solutions meet or fall short
