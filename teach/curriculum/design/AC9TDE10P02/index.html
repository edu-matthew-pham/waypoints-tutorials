Generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas

AC9TDE10P02 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

apply innovation and enterprise skills to generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas, processes and solutions, including using digital tools

Prior knowledge

Students can generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas, processes and solutions (AC9TDE8P02). They have experience with ideation techniques, modelling and design documentation. They have not yet applied innovation and enterprise skills, reimagined designs to feature emerging technologies, or used the full range of technical drawing and 3D modelling/prototyping tools at this level.

By the end

Students apply innovation and enterprise skills to generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas and solutions — using analysis of benefits and constraints, a range of modelling and prototyping techniques, and appropriate technical documentation for an intended audience.

Waypoints

  • 1. Generating ideas with innovation and enterprise thinkingStudents can use innovation and enterprise thinking to generate design concepts, including reimagining existing designs and incorporating emerging technologies.
  • 2. Functional, structural and aesthetic analysisHingeStudents can undertake functional, structural and aesthetic analysis of design ideas, identifying benefits and constraints for different communities and environments.
  • 3. Producing drawings, models and prototypesStudents can produce drawings, models and prototypes using appropriate techniques and digital tools to explore and develop design ideas.
  • 4. Communicating design ideas and processesStudents can communicate design ideas, processes and solutions using appropriate technical language and documentation for an intended audience.
