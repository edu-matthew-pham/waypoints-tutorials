← AC9TDE10P02 Generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas

Generating ideas with innovation and enterprise thinking

AC9TDE10P02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 4 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can use innovation and enterprise thinking to generate design concepts, including reimagining existing designs and incorporating emerging technologies.

Success criteria

  • At least two distinct design concepts are generated — not variations of the same approach
  • At least one concept reflects innovation or enterprise thinking beyond conventional solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can generate, test, iterate and communicate design ideas, processes and solutions (AC9TDE8P02). They have experience with ideation techniques, modelling and design documentation. They have not yet applied innovation and enterprise skills, reimagined designs to feature emerging technologies, or used the full range of technical drawing and 3D modelling/prototyping tools at this level.

Width

Minimum
Generate at least two distinct design concepts using at least one innovation or enterprise technique; concepts should be different approaches, not variants of the same solution.
Typical
Apply innovation and enterprise skills — brainstorming novel ideas inspired by nature, transforming a solution for a target market, reimagining designs with emerging technologies — to generate a range of design concepts; consider competing variables (weight, strength, price, sustainability, user needs, community consultation) that may shape or constrain the concepts.
Wider
  • Use nature-inspired design (biomimicry) as an ideation framework — generate concepts that translate a natural mechanism or structure into a designed solution
  • Generate concepts that respond to an identified enterprise opportunity — explain what market gap or user need each concept addresses and how it would be communicated to a target market
  • Design wearable technology concepts that incorporate emerging technologies (e.g. biometric sensing) to provide independence for elderly or health-focused users
