← AC9TDE4P02 Generate and communicate design ideas

Generating design ideas through sketching and modelling

AC9TDE4P02 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 3–4 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can produce multiple design ideas for a problem using thumbnail sketches or models

Success criteria

  • Produces at least two visibly different design ideas
  • Each sketch or model shows enough detail to understand what it proposes

Prior knowledge

Students can generate and communicate design ideas through describing, drawing or modelling, including using digital tools (AC9TDE2P01). They have communicated design ideas informally. They have not yet used technical terms, annotated diagrams, attributions, or more formal graphical representation techniques.

Width

Minimum
Produce at least two thumbnail sketches or models for a design problem, with enough detail to distinguish the ideas
Typical
Produce multiple thumbnail sketches, labelled drawings and/or models for design problems; include features and modifications in each idea; explore ideas for different design problems
Wider
  • Generate design ideas inspired by First Nations Australians' watercraft or other traditional designs — describe how prior design knowledge can inform new ideas
  • Generate design ideas for solutions using Safety by Design principles — produce sketches that show how the design ensures accessibility for all users (e.g. all parents and carers can access communication)

Hinge

Students who produce only one design idea arrive at N2 and N3 with nothing to annotate or communicate distinctively — the communication tasks assume multiple ideas have been generated.
