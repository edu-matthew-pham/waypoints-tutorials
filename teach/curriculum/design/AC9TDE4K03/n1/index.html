Methods of food production

AC9TDE4K03 n1 · waypoint 1 of 3 · Years 3–4 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding

Checkpoint

Students can describe at least two methods used to produce food and explain what each involves

Success criteria

  • Names at least two food production methods
  • Describes what is involved in at least one method (tools, procedures, environment)

Prior knowledge

Students can explore how plants and animals are grown for food, clothing and shelter (AC9TDE2K03). They have encountered food and fibre production at an exploratory level. They have not yet described production methods, compared different production systems, or examined tools and technologies used in food and fibre production.

Width

Minimum
Describe at least two food production methods (e.g. growing vegetables, raising livestock, fishing) and explain what each involves
Typical
Describe a range of food production methods in Australia — plant-based (crops, vegetables, orchards) and animal-based (livestock, dairy, seafood); explain what tools, equipment and procedures are involved in each
Wider
  • Research food and fibre production techniques developed by First Nations Australians — including burning, tilling, planting, transplanting, watering, weeding, thinning, cropping, storing and trading — and describe how these methods support sustainable production
  • Compare farming methods for food in Australia and a country in Asia — describe how different plants and animals are used and how diverse technologies support their production
