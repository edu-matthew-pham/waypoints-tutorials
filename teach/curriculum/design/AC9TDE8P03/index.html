Justify production choices

AC9TDE8P03 · Years 7–8 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select, justify and use suitable materials, components, tools, equipment, skills and processes to safely make designed solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can select and use suitable materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques to safely make designed solutions (AC9TDE6P03). They can select and use materials but have not yet justified selections with evidence, compared traditional and emerging technologies, or independently managed safety risks.

By the end

Students select, justify and use suitable materials, components, tools, equipment, skills and processes to safely make designed solutions, including comparing traditional and contemporary technologies and managing risks through safe, inclusive working practices.

Waypoints

  • 1. Selecting materials and processesHingeStudents can select suitable materials, components, tools, equipment and processes for a design task based on their properties and the design requirements
  • 2. Justifying selectionsStudents can justify their material, tool and process selections by explaining why each choice is the most appropriate option for the design requirements
  • 3. Identifying production hazards and risksHingeStudents can identify material, equipment and process hazards relevant to their production task and plan safe working practices before production begins.
  • 4. Safe and inclusive productionStudents can apply safe and inclusive working practices throughout production, managing hazards as they arise.
  • 5. Reflecting on safe practice after productionStudents can reflect on how safe working practices held up during production and identify improvements for future projects.
