← AC9TDE10P05 Develop project plans for time, cost, risk and production

Collaborative planning with equitable role distribution

AC9TDE10P05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can collaboratively develop a production plan with equitable distribution of roles, tasks and deadlines, including flexibility and contingencies.

Success criteria

  • All team members have assigned roles and tasks in the plan
  • At least one contingency is documented for an identified risk

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Planning and interpreting production timelines.

Width

Minimum
In a collaborative project, assign roles and tasks to all team members with deadlines; identify at least one contingency if a task is delayed or a person is unavailable.
Typical
Collaboratively develop a production plan that distributes work equitably — assign roles and tasks based on skills and availability; discuss and agree on deadlines and flexibility; build contingencies for foreseeable risks; monitor and adjust the plan as the project progresses.
Wider
  • Reflect on the equity of role distribution in a completed collaborative project — evaluate whether the plan achieved equitable contribution and what changes would improve future plans
  • Develop a contingency plan that addresses at least three distinct risks (material, personnel, timeline) — test the contingency plan by simulating a specific risk event
  • Investigate how professional design and manufacturing teams structure equitable collaboration — identify what practices could be applied to student projects
