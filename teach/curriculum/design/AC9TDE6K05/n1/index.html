Material properties determine selection

AC9TDE6K05 n1 · waypoint 1 of 2 · Years 5–6 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can identify properties of a material and explain how those properties determine whether it is suitable for a specific designed solution

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two properties of a material
  • Explains how each property makes the material suitable or unsuitable for a specific purpose

Prior knowledge

Students can describe how forces and the properties of materials affect function in a product or system (AC9TDE4K02). They can describe material properties in relation to how products work. They have not yet explained how properties determine selection across a range of materials, systems, components, tools and equipment — or compared production approaches across different scales or cultural contexts.

Width

Minimum
Identify at least two properties of a material and explain how each affects whether the material is appropriate for a specific purpose
Typical
Describe the properties of multiple materials and explain how those properties determine their use in a designed solution — including household sustainability products, textiles, or structural applications; test or compare materials to determine which best matches the design requirements
Wider
  • Investigate how First Nations Australians used material science knowledge to select and prepare fibres for different environmental conditions — for example, twining techniques for string and rope suited to wet, dry, freshwater and saltwater applications
  • Investigate the properties of fibres and design an experiment to test which fabrics are warmest — explaining how measured properties map to real-world use decisions
  • Compare materials used in household sustainability products (water storage, insulation, solar panels) — explaining which properties make each material appropriate for its role

Hinge

Material property reasoning in N1 is prerequisite for P01 (determining appropriate materials) and P03 (selecting and justifying material choices) — students who cannot reason from property to suitability will make unjustified material selections throughout production.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepStudents typically identify properties by name without connecting them to selection decisions. Multiple design contexts — each requiring a different set of properties to be matched — build the habit of reasoning from property to suitability rather than describing properties in isolation.
