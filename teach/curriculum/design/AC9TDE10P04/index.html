Develop design criteria and evaluate independently

AC9TDE10P04 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop design criteria independently including sustainability to evaluate design ideas, processes and solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can develop design criteria collaboratively including sustainability to evaluate design ideas, processes and solutions (AC9TDE8P04). They have experience using criteria to guide evaluation. They have not yet developed criteria independently, reflected substantively on process choices, or iterated based on evaluation feedback.

By the end

Students independently develop design criteria including sustainability and use them to evaluate and iterate design ideas, processes and solutions — including reflecting on process choices and responding creatively to evaluation feedback.

Waypoints

  • 1. Independently developing design criteriaHingeStudents can independently develop specific, assessable design criteria that include sustainability considerations.
  • 2. Evaluating and justifying technology use against criteriaHingeStudents can evaluate and justify the use of traditional, contemporary and emerging technologies during a design project against design criteria including sustainability.
  • 3. Reflecting on process and iteratingStudents can reflect on process choices made at various stages of a design process and iterate design ideas to improve outcomes, including sustainability.
