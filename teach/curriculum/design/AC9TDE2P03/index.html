Evaluate success and sustainability

AC9TDE2P03 · Years 1–2 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

evaluate the success of design ideas and solutions based on personal preferences and including sustainability

Prior knowledge

Students can evaluate what they made using personal preferences (AC9TDEFP01). They have shared opinions about their design. They have not yet evaluated against sustainability considerations, reflected on environmental impacts, or compared design ideas for strengths and weaknesses in a structured way.

By the end

Students evaluate the success of design ideas and solutions based on personal preferences and sustainability considerations.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • reflecting on and recording a judgement about design ideas, for example describing how design ideas meet the needs of those who will use the solution using audio-recording or video-recording software
  • sharing design strengths and weaknesses, for example explaining how the equipment in a playground might be unsuitable for some children to use and suggesting areas for design improvement
  • reflecting on the environmental impacts of the production of a solution and considering alternative approaches that would minimise future negative impacts, for example identifying the negative environmental impacts of different food packaging and how these could be minimised
  • reflecting on the challenges of designing and producing a solution and recording these reflections, for example when growing a food product, designing a structure to take a load or making a nutritious snack

Waypoints

  • 1. Evaluating against personal preferencesHingeStudents can reflect on and record a judgement about a design idea or solution based on personal preferences, identifying strengths and areas for improvement
  • 2. Evaluating sustainability impactsStudents can reflect on the environmental impacts of a solution and consider alternative approaches that minimise negative impacts
