Select and use materials safely

AC9TDE4P03 · Years 3–4 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

select and use materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques to safely make designed solutions

Prior knowledge

Students can use materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques to safely make designed solutions (AC9TDE2P02). They have used materials and tools in a supervised context. They have not yet selected materials with justified reasons, considered environmental impact across the production process, or used appropriate technologies terminology to describe procedures.

By the end

Students select and use materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques to safely make designed solutions, with consideration of environmental impact and using appropriate terminology to describe procedures.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • exploring ways of joining, connecting and assembling components that ensure success including the impact digital tools have on these processes, for example using virtual reality or simulations to experience assembling materials or using tools
  • using tools and equipment accurately when measuring, marking and cutting, for example when creating a template or pattern, measuring ingredients in a recipe or preparing a garden bed for sowing seeds
  • explaining the importance of safe, responsible, inclusive and cooperative work practices when designing and making, for example when handling sharp equipment such as knives and scissors
  • selecting and using materials, components, tools, equipment and processes with consideration of the environmental impact at each stage of the production process, for example considering how packaging and offcuts could be recycled or used for other purposes before choosing materials for a project
  • using appropriate technologies terms to describe and share with other students the procedures and techniques for making, for example how to safely make an engineered solution such as a robotic device

Waypoints

  • 1. Selecting materials with reasonsHingeStudents can select materials and components for a design purpose and give reasons for their choices that reference material properties or sustainability
  • 2. Safe, responsible and cooperative making practicesHingeStudents can demonstrate safe, responsible and inclusive work practices when using tools and equipment
  • 3. Using technologies terminology to describe proceduresStudents can describe the procedures and techniques used in making, using appropriate technologies terms
