Sequence making steps cooperatively

AC9TDE2P04 · Years 1–2 Design and Technologies · Processes and production skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

sequence steps for making designed solutions cooperatively

Prior knowledge

Students can use materials and equipment to make solutions (AC9TDEFP01, AC9TDE2P02). They have followed steps to make things. They have not yet formally sequenced steps for making as a planning activity, recorded procedures, or identified roles for cooperative group making.

By the end

Students sequence the steps for making a designed solution and work cooperatively with others, identifying roles within a group.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • using lists or storyboarding when planning and making, for example when creating an electronic planting calendar
  • recording the procedure for making a product, for example the ordered steps for making a salad, instructions for making a container or bag
  • identifying roles for each member of a group when working cooperatively, for example when making a number of items for a school fete

Waypoints

  • 1. Sequencing making stepsHingeStudents can sequence the steps needed to make a designed solution in a logical order
  • 2. Working cooperatively with identified rolesStudents can identify roles for group members and work cooperatively toward a shared making outcome
