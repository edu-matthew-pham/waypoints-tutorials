Ethics, security and sustainability in DT occupations

AC9TDE10K01 · Years 9–10 Design and Technologies · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Design and Technologies: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

analyse how people in design and technologies occupations consider ethical, security and sustainable factors to innovate and improve products, services and environments

Prior knowledge

Students can analyse how people in design and technologies occupations consider ethical and sustainability factors when designing solutions (AC9TDE8K01). They can identify how designers respond to community needs and sustainability constraints. They have not yet analysed how ethical, security and sustainability factors combine to drive innovation, nor examined product life cycle thinking, mass production systems, or the role of global professional collaboration.

By the end

Students analyse how people in design and technologies occupations integrate ethical, security and sustainability considerations — including product life cycle thinking, mass production ethics, and professional collaboration — to innovate and improve products, services and environments.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • examining sustainability factors influencing the design and production of a solution developed by First Nations Australians, such as the sustainable production of culturally significant pigments
  • analysing design and technologies professions and their contributions to society, for example engineers working in disaster recovery or empowering communities
  • recognising the impact of past designed solutions when creating solutions for preferred futures
  • considering the factors that influence design and manufacture and the work of professional designers including time, access to skills, knowledge, finance, expertise in global engineering
  • explaining how product life cycle thinking can influence decision-making related to design and technologies
  • examining mass production systems taking into account ethics and sustainability considerations
  • explaining the consequences of ethical and sustainability decisions for products, services and environments

Waypoints

  • 1. Product life cycle thinkingHingeStudents can explain what product life cycle thinking is and how it influences design decisions.
  • 2. Ethics and sustainability in mass productionStudents can analyse ethics and sustainability considerations in mass production systems, including why manufacturers make different versions of the same product.
  • 3. Professional roles and global collaborationStudents can analyse how design and technologies professionals contribute to society and collaborate across global contexts.
  • 4. Consequences of ethical and sustainability decisionsStudents can analyse the consequences of ethical and sustainability decisions for products, services and environments, including the accessibility of public environments and infrastructure.
