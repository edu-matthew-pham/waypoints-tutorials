Superannuation system

AC9HE10K04 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the importance of Australia's superannuation system and how this system affects consumer and financial decision-making

Prior knowledge

Students understand factors that influence major consumer and financial decisions and their short- and long-term consequences (AC9HE10K03). They can analyse how financial commitments accumulate over time and consider the role of savings. Students have also explored how governments redistribute income through transfers and services (AC9HE10K02).

By the end

Students explain the importance of Australia's superannuation system for individual retirement wellbeing and government fiscal sustainability, and explain how the system shapes consumer and financial decision-making.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • explaining the importance of Australia's superannuation system in relation to human and financial wellbeing in retirement and the government's ability to maintain a welfare payment system for the common good
  • identifying why individuals make decisions about superannuation investment options and how their circumstances, such as age, employment status, dependents and anticipated retirement age, affect these decisions
  • explaining how individuals manage a personal superannuation fund; for example, who contributes, taxation, consolidating superannuation funds, finding lost superannuation and tracking the performance of a superannuation account

Waypoints

  • 1. What superannuation is and why it existsHingeStudents can explain what superannuation is, why Australia introduced a compulsory super system, and the dual purpose it serves for individuals and government.
  • 2. How a super fund worksStudents can explain how an individual superannuation fund operates — who contributes, how it is taxed, and how the fund grows over time.
  • 3. Super investment decisionsStudents can explain why individuals make decisions about superannuation investment options and how personal circumstances affect these decisions.
  • 4. Superannuation and broader financial decision-makingStudents can explain how the existence of superannuation affects other consumer and financial decisions — including career choices, savings behaviour, insurance, and planning for retirement.
