Government economic intervention

AC9HE10K02 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the ways that government intervenes in the economy to improve economic performance and living standards within Australian society

Prior knowledge

Students understand how economic decision-making involves the interdependence of consumers, businesses, the financial sector and government (AC9HE9K02). They have explored how Australia trades with other nations and its patterns with Asia (AC9HE9K03). They can explain how different actors in the economy interact and how decisions ripple across the system. Students have also begun working with AC9HE10K01 — how economic indicators signal the need for government response.

By the end

Students explain the ways that the Australian Government intervenes in the economy through fiscal policy, income redistribution and regulation to improve economic performance and living standards.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • explaining how the Australian Government uses fiscal policy to help improve the standard of living; for example, the federal Budget, taxation, expenditure on skills training and workforce development
  • explaining the forces that shape and affect variations in living standards within Australian society, such as unemployment and poverty, and identifying why the government may redistribute income and wealth
  • explaining how the government may redistribute income to achieve a more equal distribution of income and wealth; for example, income transfers such as pensions, youth allowance and job search, or the provision of services for all such as healthcare and education
  • investigating the role and function of the Reserve Bank of Australia within the Australian economy

Waypoints

  • 1. What government intervention meansHingeStudents can explain why markets alone do not always produce desirable outcomes and why governments intervene in the economy.
  • 2. Fiscal policy and the federal BudgetHingeStudents can explain how the Australian Government uses fiscal policy — taxation and expenditure — to influence economic performance and living standards.
  • 3. Income redistribution and living standardsStudents can explain how and why the government redistributes income, and identify the mechanisms through which redistribution affects living standards and inequality.
  • 4. The Reserve Bank and monetary policyStudents can describe the role and function of the Reserve Bank of Australia and explain how monetary policy tools (particularly the cash rate) are used to manage economic performance.
  • 5. Government intervention — integrated evaluationStudents can explain multiple ways the Australian Government intervenes in the economy and evaluate how these tools together aim to improve economic performance and living standards.
