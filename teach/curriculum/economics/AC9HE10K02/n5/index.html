Government intervention — integrated evaluation

AC9HE10K02 n5 · waypoint 5 of 5 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain multiple ways the Australian Government intervenes in the economy and evaluate how these tools together aim to improve economic performance and living standards.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two distinct types of government intervention and explains what each aims to achieve
  • Explains how both tools relate to improving economic performance or living standards

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 4: The Reserve Bank and monetary policy.

Width

Minimum
Explain two distinct ways the government intervenes (e.g. fiscal policy and income redistribution) and describe what each is trying to achieve.
Typical
Explain how fiscal policy, income redistribution and monetary policy work together as a suite of government interventions aimed at improving economic performance and living standards; discuss variation in living standards and how government tools address it.
Wider
  • Evaluate a current or recent government economic policy package, identifying which intervention types are present and assessing whether the mix is coherent
  • Investigate how the government's economic intervention approach has shifted under different political administrations and what this reveals about assumptions behind the tools
  • Examine how intervention affects different groups unequally — who benefits most from fiscal expansion, who bears the burden of monetary tightening

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node draws fiscal policy, redistribution and monetary policy together into a coherent picture of government economic management. The integration demand is the descriptor's full scope — explaining the *ways* (plural) of intervention.
