Locate and organise information

AC9HE8S02 · Year 8 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

locate, select and organise information and data from a range of sources

Prior knowledge

Students can locate, select and organise information and data from a range of sources (AC9HE7S02). At Year 8, the expectation shifts toward more advanced search strategies, explicit source evaluation, and using specialised digital tools to organise data.

By the end

Students locate, select and organise information and data from a range of sources, including using advanced search functions and digital tools.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • locating online sources; for example, exploring advanced search functions such as 'allintitle: taxation in Australia' or 'define: not-for-profit organisation'
  • selecting relevant information and data from sources about websites of government departments or non-government organisations by asking questions such as 'How will the data or information help answer the question?'
  • organising data into appropriate formats using specialised digital tools; for example, constructing a diagram modelling the relationship between consumers, producers and workers in a market or a table showing the features of different ways businesses adapt to opportunities in the market

Waypoints

  • 1. Locating and evaluating sourcesHingeStudents can locate information from a range of sources using advanced search strategies and evaluate the relevance of sources by asking whether the data or information helps answer the inquiry question.
  • 2. Selecting and organising dataStudents can select relevant information and data from located sources and organise it into appropriate formats — including diagrams or tables — using specialised digital tools.
