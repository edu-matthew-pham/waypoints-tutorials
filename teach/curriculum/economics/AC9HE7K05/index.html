Consumer and financial rights and responsibilities

AC9HE7K05 · Year 7 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the rights and responsibilities of individuals and businesses in relation to consumer and financial products and services

Prior knowledge

Students have explored consumer choices and strategies for making informed financial decisions (AC9HS6K08) and are developing understanding of work and income (AC9HE7K04). They are familiar with making purchases but have not formally examined the legal and ethical framework of rights and responsibilities that governs consumer and financial transactions.

By the end

Students explain the rights and responsibilities of individuals and businesses in relation to consumer and financial products and services.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • distinguishing the difference between rights and responsibilities, and creating a list of the rights and responsibilities of individuals and businesses
  • explaining the rights of individuals and businesses in relation to consumer and financial products and services; for example, warranties, cooling-off periods and processes for returning goods
  • discussing the responsibilities of businesses in relation to consumer and financial products and services, such as the role of mandatory and voluntary standards, and product safety recalls
  • explaining the importance of developing personal or business budgets or savings plans before making decisions in relation to consumer and financial products and services

Waypoints

  • 1. Rights versus responsibilitiesHingeStudents can distinguish between rights and responsibilities, and apply this distinction to consumer and financial contexts.
  • 2. Individual rights in consumer and financial contextsStudents can explain the rights of individuals in relation to consumer and financial products and services, including warranties, cooling-off periods and return processes.
  • 3. Business responsibilities and product safetyStudents can explain the responsibilities of businesses in relation to consumer and financial products and services, including mandatory and voluntary standards and product safety recalls.
  • 4. Budgeting, decision-making and integrationStudents can explain how developing personal or business budgets and savings plans supports better decision-making about consumer and financial products and services, integrating rights and responsibilities into that framework.
