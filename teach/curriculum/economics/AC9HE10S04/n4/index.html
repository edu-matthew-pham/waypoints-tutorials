Evaluating using criteria

AC9HE10S04 n4 · waypoint 4 of 5 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can evaluate a response to an economic issue using explicit criteria (e.g. efficiency, profitability, equity) and explain the reasoning behind a recommended course of action.

Success criteria

  • Applies at least one named evaluation criterion and explains what it reveals about the response being evaluated
  • Uses the criterion to justify a recommended course of action, not just to describe the options

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 3: Evaluating short- and long-term financial implications.

Width

Minimum
Apply at least one named criterion (e.g. efficiency, equity) to evaluate a response to an economic issue and explain what the criterion reveals about the response.
Typical
Evaluate a response using criteria such as efficiency, profitability or equity to explain reasons for a course of action — for example, using economic performance indicators to identify variations within the Australian economy and evaluate government intervention responses.
Wider
  • Apply multiple criteria to the same response and evaluate whether the criteria point to the same recommendation or require a trade-off decision
  • Investigate how equity and efficiency criteria can conflict in economic policy (e.g. progressive taxation) and evaluate how governments typically navigate the trade-off
  • Compare how different stakeholders (business, government, community) would weight the same set of evaluation criteria differently for the same decision

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Context breadth: more contexts in the vary or modify stepApply criteria-based evaluation to different economic contexts — policy evaluation, business decision, consumer choice — so students can use criteria flexibly and not just in one context type.
