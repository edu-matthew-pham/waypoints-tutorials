Create descriptions, explanations and arguments

AC9HE9S05 · Year 9 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

create descriptions, explanations and arguments, using economic and business knowledge, concepts and terms that incorporate and acknowledge research findings

Prior knowledge

From Y8 AC9HE8S05 (direct predecessor): create descriptions and explanations using economic and business knowledge, concepts and terms, referencing information and data from sources. Students can create descriptions and explanations with source references. Y9 adds two moves: (1) arguments — a new text type requiring a reasoned position with evidence; and (2) acknowledge research findings — moving from referencing to formal acknowledgement of sources. Y9 also adds audience orientation and appropriate tone.

By the end

Students create descriptions, explanations and arguments, using economic and business knowledge, concepts and terms that incorporate and acknowledge research findings.

Waypoints

  • 1. Orientating the audience using concepts and termsHingeStudents can develop responses that orient the audience to an economic or business issue using relevant concepts and terms.
  • 2. Explaining and supporting decisions with data representationsStudents can explain decisions and conclusions related to an economic or business issue, supported by visual displays or data representations, and acknowledge research sources.
  • 3. Constructing reasoned arguments with appropriate toneStudents can present a reasoned argument in relation to an economic or business issue, applying tone appropriate to the purpose and audience.
