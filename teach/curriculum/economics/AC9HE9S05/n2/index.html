← AC9HE9S05 Create descriptions, explanations and arguments

Explaining and supporting decisions with data representations

AC9HE9S05 n2 · waypoint 2 of 3 · Year 9 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain decisions and conclusions related to an economic or business issue, supported by visual displays or data representations, and acknowledge research sources.

Success criteria

  • Supports at least one conclusion with a data representation (table, graph, or graphic organiser)
  • Explains what the representation shows and how it supports the conclusion
  • Acknowledges at least one research source

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 1: Orientating the audience using concepts and terms.

Width

Minimum
Write an explanation of one conclusion from an economic investigation, supported by one data representation (table, graph or graphic organiser), and acknowledge the source.
Typical
Explain decisions and conclusions related to an economic or business issue supported by representations of relevant data — applying the elaboration examples (visual displays, graphic organisers, tables and graphs, explanations, procedures or reports). Acknowledge research findings formally. The explanation should: state the conclusion, show how the data representation supports it, explain the reasoning connecting data to conclusion, and cite the source.
Wider
  • Construct a multi-representation explanation — using a combination of text, table, and graph to build an explanation that uses each representation for what it does best
  • Evaluate the data representations used in a published economic report and explain which are well-chosen for the conclusions they support and which are misleading or poorly matched
  • Apply formal acknowledgement conventions to a range of source types (government statistics, news articles, expert reports) and explain why proper acknowledgement matters for the credibility of economic arguments

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Consolidation: more practise steps, short feedback loopsData-supported explanation requires repeated practice to build the habit of connecting the representation to the conclusion explicitly rather than inserting it as decoration. Students commonly include graphs without explaining what they show or why they support the claim.
