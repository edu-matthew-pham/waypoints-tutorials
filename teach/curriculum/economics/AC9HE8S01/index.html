Develop inquiry questions

AC9HE8S01 · Year 8 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

develop questions to investigate a contemporary economic and business issue

Prior knowledge

Students can develop questions to investigate a contemporary economic and business issue (AC9HE7S01). At Year 8, the expectation shifts toward questions that are more precisely targeted using economics and business concepts and terms, and toward a wider range of issue types.

By the end

Students develop questions — including targeted questions using economics and business concepts and terms — to investigate a contemporary economic and business issue.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • developing a range of questions to form the basis of an investigation; for example, 'Why does an individual or group establish a business?' and 'How are consumers' rights protected?'
  • developing targeted or key questions using economics and business concepts and terms, such as, 'How is the price of goods and services determined?' and 'Why do changes in market price act as a signal about how scarce resources should be allocated?'

Waypoints

  • 1. Broad and targeted question typesHingeStudents can distinguish between broad inquiry questions and targeted questions that use economics and business concepts and terms, and explain why targeted questions produce more useful investigations.
  • 2. Developing a question set for investigationStudents can develop a range of questions — forming the basis of an investigation — that together enable systematic inquiry into a contemporary economic and business issue.
