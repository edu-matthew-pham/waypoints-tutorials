The role of entrepreneurs

AC9HE10K05 n3 · waypoint 3 of 4 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how entrepreneurs lead responses to economic and technological change, including in the context of Australian First Nations businesses and support organisations.

Success criteria

  • Explains what distinguishes entrepreneurial activity from standard business management
  • Gives a specific example of an entrepreneur responding to an economic or technological change

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Productivity improvement strategies.

Width

Minimum
Explain what an entrepreneur does and give one example of how entrepreneurial activity responds to economic change.
Typical
Explain how entrepreneurs lead responses to changes in global and domestic economic conditions and use technologies to drive innovation — for example, influencing approaches to waste management or sustainable energy; investigate support structures for start-up businesses including those for First Nations Australian entrepreneurs.
Wider
  • Investigate a specific Australian entrepreneur whose work responds to an economic or environmental challenge and evaluate the impact of their innovation
  • Examine First Nations Australian businesses supported by organisations such as the Registrar of Indigenous Corporations or First Nations Chambers of Commerce and evaluate how these structures enable entrepreneurship
  • Compare the support ecosystems for entrepreneurs across different countries and evaluate which features of the Australian system are distinctive or effective

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Repertoire building: more cases to sort or classifyBuild a range of entrepreneurial examples across industries and contexts — including First Nations entrepreneurs — so students have a diverse reference set rather than a single prototype case.
