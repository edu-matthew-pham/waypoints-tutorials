Interpret and predict economic impacts

AC9HE10S03 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Skills

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

interpret information and data, explaining economic and business issues, trends and economic cause-and-effect relationships, and make predictions about consumer and financial impacts

Prior knowledge

Students can interpret information and data, explain economic and business issues, trends and cause-and-effect relationships, and make predictions at Year 9 level (AC9HE9S03). They have practised interpreting single-variable economic data, identifying trends and articulating cause-and-effect logic. Y10 builds on this with multi-variable data, more complex trend interpretation and more explicit prediction reasoning.

By the end

Students interpret information and data to explain economic and business issues, trends and cause-and-effect relationships, and make reasoned predictions about consumer and financial impacts.

Waypoints

  • 1. Explaining economic issues from dataHingeStudents can interpret information to explain the nature, extent and importance of an economic or business issue.
  • 2. Interpreting multi-variable trendsStudents can interpret multi-variable data to analyse trends over time, explaining reasons for changes and what the trend reveals about economic conditions.
  • 3. Cause-and-effect analysisHingeStudents can analyse causes and effects of an economic issue — explaining why the issue arises and what its consequences are for individuals, businesses or the broader economy.
  • 4. Making predictions about consumer and financial impactsStudents can analyse trends and cause-and-effect relationships to make reasoned predictions about who will be affected and how, with reference to economic evidence.
