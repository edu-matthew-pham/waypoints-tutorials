Competitive advantage

AC9HE9K04 · Year 9 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

processes that businesses use to create and maintain competitive advantage, including the role of entrepreneurs

Prior knowledge

From Y8: how businesses adapt to market opportunities and the changing nature of work (AC9HE8K02); how First Nations Australian businesses and entrepreneurs develop market opportunities (AC9HE8K03). From Y7: entrepreneur characteristics and how these influence business success (AC9HE7K03). Students understand why businesses exist and how entrepreneurs operate but have not yet studied the specific processes used to create and maintain competitive advantage in global markets.

By the end

Students explain the processes businesses use to create and maintain competitive advantage, including the role of entrepreneurs.

Waypoints

  • 1. First Nations businesses and competitive advantageHingeStudents can identify and explain processes used by First Nations Australian businesses to outperform competitors and innovate in global markets, including managing cultural and intellectual property.
  • 2. Why businesses seek competitive advantageStudents can explain why businesses seek to build competitive advantage, including meeting changing market demands and improving profit margins.
  • 3. Building connections as a competitive processStudents can explain processes businesses use to build connections that support competitive advantage, including industry collaboration, government partnerships and developing professional talent.
  • 4. Cost reduction and innovation processesStudents can explain the processes businesses use to produce goods and services at lower cost and to innovate and differentiate products and services from competitors.
