Business adaptation

AC9HE8K02 · Year 8 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

different ways that businesses adapt to opportunities in the market and respond to the changing nature of work

Prior knowledge

Students understand the reasons businesses exist and how different types of businesses provide goods and services (AC9HE7K02). They understand entrepreneur characteristics and how these influence business success (AC9HE7K03). They understand how markets allocate resources through price signals (AC9HE8K01).

By the end

Students discuss different ways that businesses adapt to opportunities in the market and respond to the changing nature of work.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • discussing how businesses identify needs, niches and gaps in established markets to guide the development of specific services or products to address these opportunities; for example, electric cars and solar power
  • identifying where businesses have used technologies to streamline or gain efficiencies in existing business models; for example, ride share, food delivery applications and online retail
  • explaining current influences on the ways people work; for example, technological change, outsourced labour in the global economy, rapid communication changes, casualisation of the workforce
  • identifying and explaining changes to the workforce over time; for example, the jobs available, the way individuals or businesses value particular work, career length and human resource development, changing demography, corporate social responsibility, sustainability practices, changes to workplace laws

Waypoints

  • 1. Identifying market opportunitiesHingeStudents can explain how businesses identify needs, niches and gaps in established markets and how these guide the development of new products or services.
  • 2. Technology-driven business adaptationStudents can explain how businesses use technologies to streamline operations or gain efficiencies, using specific examples such as ride share, food delivery and online retail.
  • 3. Changing nature of workStudents can explain current and historical influences on how people work, including technological change, globalisation, communication changes and workforce casualisation.
  • 4. Business adaptation — integratedStudents can discuss how businesses adapt to market opportunities and the changing nature of work, connecting opportunity identification, technology use, and workforce change into a coherent account of business adaptation.
