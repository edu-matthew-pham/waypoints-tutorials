Major financial decisions

AC9HE10K03 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

factors that influence major consumer and financial decisions, and the short- and long-term consequences of these decisions

Prior knowledge

Students understand how individuals and businesses manage consumer and financial risks and rewards (AC9HE9K05). They can identify financial risks, explain strategies for managing them, and consider trade-offs in financial decision-making. They have also explored how consumer decisions are influenced by market forces.

By the end

Students analyse factors that influence major consumer and financial decisions and explain both the short- and long-term consequences of these decisions.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying examples of minor and major consumer and financial decisions, and explaining the processes that guide decision-making
  • analysing factors that influence major consumer and financial decisions, such as price, availability and cost of finance, marketing of products, age and gender of consumer, convenience, and ethical and environmental considerations
  • identifying the costs of consumer and financial transactions over time, such as the ability to make loan repayments, available savings, depreciation, and maintenance costs and insurance, as well as the benefits such as independence and convenience when purchasing a car or home

Waypoints

  • 1. Minor vs major financial decisionsHingeStudents can distinguish between minor and major consumer and financial decisions, and explain what makes a decision 'major' in terms of scale, commitment and consequence.
  • 2. Factors influencing major decisionsHingeStudents can analyse multiple factors that influence major consumer and financial decisions, including economic, personal and ethical factors.
  • 3. Short- and long-term consequencesStudents can identify and explain both the short- and long-term consequences of major financial decisions, distinguishing costs from benefits across different time horizons.
  • 4. Factors and consequences — integrated analysisStudents can analyse factors influencing a major financial decision and connect these to predicted short- and long-term consequences, demonstrating integrated understanding of the descriptor.
