Economic indicators

AC9HE10K01 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

how and why the economic indicators influence economic decision-making

Prior knowledge

Students understand the role of Australia's financial sector and its effect on economic decision-making by individuals, businesses and global markets (AC9HE9K01). They understand how economic decision-making involves the interdependence of consumers, businesses, the financial sector and government (AC9HE9K02). They can explain how economic decisions by one actor affect others across the system.

By the end

Students explain how and why economic indicators influence economic decision-making by governments, businesses and individuals in Australia.

Waypoints

  • 1. What economic indicators areHingeStudents can identify key economic indicators and describe what each measures about the economy.
  • 2. Interpreting indicator dataHingeStudents can read and interpret indicator data — identifying trends, describing what a change in direction signals, and using appropriate economic vocabulary.
  • 3. Government economic objectivesHingeStudents can name and explain the Australian Government's key economic objectives and connect each to one or more indicators that track progress toward it.
  • 4. How indicators prompt government responseStudents can explain how changes in specific indicators trigger or justify government economic decisions, using examples of actual or hypothetical government responses.
  • 5. Indicators and individual/business decision-makingStudents can explain how economic indicators influence decision-making beyond government — by businesses and individuals — and describe the logic of these responses.
