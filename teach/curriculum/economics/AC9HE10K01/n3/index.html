Government economic objectives

AC9HE10K01 n3 · waypoint 3 of 5 · Year 10 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding · Hinge

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can name and explain the Australian Government's key economic objectives and connect each to one or more indicators that track progress toward it.

Success criteria

  • Names at least two government economic objectives and explains why each matters for Australians
  • Correctly matches each objective to the indicator(s) used to track it

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 2: Interpreting indicator data.

Width

Minimum
Name at least two government economic objectives (e.g. economic growth, full employment, price stability) and identify which indicator tracks each.
Typical
Explain the Australian Government's main economic objectives — economic growth, full employment and price stability — and explain why each matters for living standards; connect each objective to its corresponding indicator(s).
Wider
  • Investigate how environmental sustainability or wellbeing has been framed as a policy objective and which indicators governments are beginning to use to track it
  • Compare Australia's stated economic objectives with those of a different country and examine whether the same indicators are used
  • Examine how objectives can conflict (e.g. low unemployment can drive inflation) and how governments balance competing indicators

Hinge

Without understanding that indicators are proxies for objectives — that GDP is tracked because growth is a goal — students cannot explain *why* indicators matter to decision-making. They will describe reaction without understanding rationale.

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Structured reasoning: a worked chain of reasons, then a write-and-compare (reasoning) checkHelp students build the explicit connection: objective → what it means → why it matters → which indicator tracks it → what direction signals concern. This logic chain is the scaffold for the integration node.
