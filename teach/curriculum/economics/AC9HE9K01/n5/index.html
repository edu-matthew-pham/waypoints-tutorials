The financial sector in global markets

AC9HE9K01 n5 · waypoint 5 of 5 · Year 9 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Checkpoint

Students can explain how Australia's financial sector supports businesses in global markets, including payment systems, currency access, insurance and capital.

Success criteria

  • Identifies at least two services the financial sector provides to businesses operating in global markets
  • Explains how each named service enables international trade or investment
  • Connects financial sector services to economic decision-making by businesses

Prior knowledge

Waypoint 4: Forces shaping Australia's financial landscape.

Width

Minimum
Identify one way Australia's financial sector supports businesses operating in global markets (e.g. currency exchange, payment clearing) and explain why this matters for international trade.
Typical
Explain how Australia's financial sector supports businesses in global markets across the range of elaboration examples: global financial services (trade finance, letters of credit), payment systems (clearing and settlement of international transactions), access to foreign currencies, international insurance, and access to capital for investment in global operations. Explain how each service enables Australian businesses to participate in global trade.
Wider
  • Examine how correspondent banking relationships connect Australian banks to international payment networks and explain the risks when these relationships are withdrawn (derisking)
  • Investigate how Australian superannuation funds invest in global markets and explain the effect of this on Australia's financial integration with the global economy
  • Analyse a case where disruption to global financial systems (e.g. sanctions, correspondent banking withdrawal) affected Australian businesses or individuals, explaining the channels of effect

Response modes

Where extra effort goes at this waypoint (width), not what kind of lesson it is.

Integration: a combining task at the wider tierThis node integrates the sector structure (Node 1), financial products (Node 2), and forces shaping the sector (Node 4) into an explanation of how the sector enables global economic participation. Students should connect prior nodes explicitly — the sector's structure enables the services that support global trade.
