Australia's financial sector

AC9HE9K01 · Year 9 Economics and Business · Knowledge and understanding

Draft, from Waypoints Kit v0.2. Generated from Kit's node data and not yet reviewed by a specialist in Economics and Business: treat it as a starting suggestion and check it before use.

Descriptor

the role of Australia's financial sector and its effect on economic decision-making by individuals, businesses and global markets

Prior knowledge

From Y8: how markets influence resource allocation decisions and the effect of prices (AC9HE8K01); how businesses adapt to market opportunities and changing work (AC9HE8K02); First Nations Australian businesses and entrepreneurs in markets (AC9HE8K03). Students understand basic market mechanisms and business types but have not yet studied the financial sector's specific role as an intermediary.

By the end

Students explain the role of Australia's financial sector and its effect on economic decision-making by individuals, businesses and global markets.

ACARA elaborations

Examples from ACARA that illustrate this standard. They are examples, not a checklist, and belong to the standard as a whole.

  • identifying different organisations within Australia's financial sector, such as banks, credit unions or building societies, and explaining their functions; for example, collecting deposits, pooling savings, and lending funds to individuals and businesses
  • explaining the nature of investment or the financial products and services that enable people to receive income in the future; for example, shares, earning interest on term deposits, managed funds or superannuation
  • explaining the difference between good and bad debt, how to manage debt, the risks of over-indebtedness and the importance of having a savings buffer
  • explaining the forces that shape and affect Australia's financial landscape, such as financial deregulations, technological changes, economic and business activities, and consumer and business sentiment about the future
  • identifying examples and explaining how Australia's financial sector supports businesses in global markets; for example, the nature of global financial services, payment systems – clearing and settlement, access to currencies, insurance and capital

Waypoints

  • 1. Structure of the financial sectorHingeStudents can identify the main types of organisations within Australia's financial sector and describe their core functions.
  • 2. Financial products and investmentStudents can explain what investment means and describe how financial products enable individuals to receive future income.
  • 3. Debt, savings and financial managementHingeStudents can explain the difference between good and bad debt, describe how to manage debt, and explain why a savings buffer matters.
  • 4. Forces shaping Australia's financial landscapeStudents can explain the forces that shape and affect Australia's financial landscape, including deregulation, technology, economic conditions and consumer sentiment.
  • 5. The financial sector in global marketsStudents can explain how Australia's financial sector supports businesses in global markets, including payment systems, currency access, insurance and capital.
