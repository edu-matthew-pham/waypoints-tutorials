# Modelling

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**percentage change multiplier**: One change after another: multiply the multipliers. Up 20% then down 20% is × 0.96.

$$\text{up } p\%: \times\left(1 + \tfrac{p}{100}\right) \qquad \text{down } p\%: \times\left(1 - \tfrac{p}{100}\right)$$

_$100 up 20%, then down 20%: $96_

:::

:::keep-together

**tax brackets**: The marginal rate is the rate on the last dollar; the effective rate is total tax ÷ income.

$$\text{each part of the income} \times \text{its bracket's rate}$$

:::

:::keep-together

**percentages in two steps**: Each percentage is of a different amount, so they cannot be added: 20% off then 10% off is not 30% off.

$$200 \times 0.8 \times 0.9 = 144$$

:::
