# Reflections

Angles and shapes · Transformations

<!-- Speaker note: Students reflect shapes in the x-axis and y-axis and state which coordinate changes sign (AC9M7SP03, node 2). About 35 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 8 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 9 min). -->

---

## Today

**We are learning to** a reflection flips a shape over a mirror line. In the x-axis, (x, y) becomes (x, −y); in the y-axis, (x, y) becomes (−x, y). Each point lands as far on the other side as it started.

- I can reflect a shape in the x-axis or the y-axis.
- I can say which coordinate changes sign for each axis.
- I can check a reflection by its distance from the mirror.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Do the Run board yourself first. Students need translations (previous lesson). The one idea: a point and its image are the same distance from the mirror on opposite sides. In the x-axis the point crosses up or down, so y changes sign; in the y-axis it crosses left or right, so x changes sign. The common mistake is the swap (Mia's mistake). Reflecting in other lines (such as y = x) is wider and is left for the stretch. Seeing both reflections close together is deliberate: the Run board lets students flip between them. -->

---

## Why it matters

> A face in a mirror, a butterfly's wings, a lake showing a mountain. The mirror line sits exactly halfway between each point and its reflection.

<!-- Speaker note: A **reflection** flips a shape over a mirror line. Each point and its image are the same distance from the mirror, on opposite sides, and the mirror cuts the line joining them at right angles. - Reflect in the **x-axis**: the point moves up or down, so $y$ changes sign: $(x, y) \to (x, -y)$. - Reflect in the **y-axis**: the point moves left or right, so $x$ changes sign: $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$. -->

## Find the mistake

**Mia reflects P (4, 3) in the x-axis. Click the line where her reasoning goes wrong.**

```
 1  The point P is (4, 3). Mia reflects it in the x-axis.
 2  The x-axis is horizontal, so she changes the x-coordinate.
 3  She changes 4 to −4.
 4  The image of P is (−4, 3).
```

* Answer: line 2

## On the mirror

**A point is on the mirror line, for example (0, 3) reflected in the y-axis. Where is its image?**

- At (3, 0)
- In the same place: (0, 3)
- At (0, −3)
- At (−3, 0)

* Answer: In the same place: (0, 3)

<!-- Speaker note: It is zero distance from the mirror, so it does not move: −0 is still 0. -->

## The rule

**Which rule describes a reflection in the y-axis?**

- (x, y) → (y, x)
- (x, y) → (−x, y)
- (x, y) → (−x, −y)
- (x, y) → (x, −y)

* Answer: (x, y) → (−x, y)

<!-- Speaker note: The y-axis is the vertical mirror: x changes sign and y stays. -->
