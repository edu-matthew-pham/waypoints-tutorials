## Write the proof


:::keep-together

### The proof

Here is a proof that a parallelogram's diagonals cut each other in half. Every proof in this lesson has the same plan.

1. $\text{Parallelogram } ABCD,\ \text{diagonals cross at } M$ — Look at triangles ABM and CDM.
2. $AB = CD$ — Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.
3. $\angle BAM = \angle DCM,\ \angle ABM = \angle CDM$ — Alternate angles: AB is parallel to DC.
4. $\triangle ABM \equiv \triangle CDM\ (\text{ASA})$ — Name the test.
5. $AM = CM,\ BM = DM$ — Matching sides are equal: the diagonals cut each other in half.

**Put the proof that a rectangle's diagonals are equal in order.**

A. `Two sides and the angle between them match, so △ABC ≡ △BAD (SAS).`

B. `Side AB is in both, BC = AD (opposite sides of a rectangle), and angle B = angle A = 90°.`

C. `The diagonals look the same length, so they are equal.`

D. `Matching sides of congruent triangles are equal, so AC = BD: the diagonals are equal.`

E. `ABCD is a rectangle. Look at triangles ABC and BAD, made by the diagonals AC and BD.`

Correct order (letters): {{field-24}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** see the program below

Find two triangles, give the equal parts with reasons, name the test, then use matching sides.

```maths
ABCD is a rectangle. Look at triangles ABC and BAD, made by the diagonals AC and BD.
Side AB is in both, BC = AD (opposite sides of a rectangle), and angle B = angle A = 90°.
Two sides and the angle between them match, so △ABC ≡ △BAD (SAS).
Matching sides of congruent triangles are equal, so AC = BD: the diagonals are equal.
```

:::

### A kite

**Kite ABCD has AB = AD and CB = CD. The diagonal AC splits it into triangles ABC and ADC. Which test shows they are congruent?**

☐ They are not congruent

☐ SAS

☐ SSS: AB = AD, CB = CD and AC is shared

☐ RHS

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** SSS: AB = AD, CB = CD and AC is shared

AC is in both triangles, so all three sides match: SSS. Then angle BAC = angle DAC, so AC cuts angle A in half.

## Reasons


:::keep-together

### Parallelogram

**In parallelogram ABCD, the diagonal AC makes triangles ABC and CDA. AB = CD and BC = DA (opposite sides). Which test shows they are congruent?**

☐ SSA

☐ They are not congruent

☐ RHS

☐ SSS: AC is shared

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** SSS: AC is shared

AB = CD, BC = DA and AC is in both: SSS. So angle B = angle D: opposite angles are equal.

:::


:::keep-together

### Rhombus

**Which is a proof that a rhombus's diagonals cross at 90°?**

☐ I measured one and got 90°

☐ The four small triangles are congruent by SSS, so the four angles at the centre are equal and add to 360°

☐ All rhombuses are squares

☐ It looks like a plus sign

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The four small triangles are congruent by SSS, so the four angles at the centre are equal and add to 360°

Four equal angles that make 360° are 90° each.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which shape

**Which quadrilateral's diagonals always cut each other in half, but are not always equal?**

☐ A rectangle

☐ A square

☐ A kite

☐ A parallelogram

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A parallelogram

Every parallelogram's diagonals bisect each other; they are equal only when it is a rectangle.
