# Parallel lines

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**transversal**: Parallel lines go the same way and never meet; arrows (>>) mark them. A transversal crosses both, making eight angles.

$$\text{parallel lines} \gg \text{ crossed by a transversal}$$

:::

:::keep-together

**corresponding angles**: The same place at each crossing (an F shape). With parallel lines they are equal.

$$a = e$$

_a and e: the same place at each crossing, both 120°_

:::

:::keep-together

**alternate angles**: Between the parallel lines, on opposite sides of the transversal (a Z shape). With parallel lines they are equal.

$$c = f$$

_c and f: between the lines, opposite sides, both 60°_

:::

:::keep-together

**co-interior angles**: Between the parallel lines, on the same side (a C shape). With parallel lines they add to $180^\circ$: they are not equal unless both are $90^\circ$.

$$c + e = 180^\circ$$

_c and e: between the lines, same side, 60° + 120° = 180°_

:::

:::keep-together

**vertically opposite angles**: Opposite each other at one crossing: always equal, parallel lines or not. Not one of the parallel-line pairs.

$$b = c$$

:::

:::keep-together

**give a reason**: For each angle you find, name the rule: corresponding or alternate angles are equal; co-interior angles add to $180^\circ$; angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$.

$$f = 70^\circ \;\text{(corresponding to } b\text{)}$$

:::
