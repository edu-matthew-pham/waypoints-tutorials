# What enlargement keeps

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A video made for a TV screen is shown on a phone. Why does it sometimes have black bars?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students say what an enlargement keeps (angles, shape) and what it changes (lengths, perimeter, area) (AC9M9SP02, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what stays the same in an enlargement. I can use a scale factor to find a length. I can tell an enlargement from a stretch.

## Predict


:::keep-together

**A triangle has angles 30°, 60° and 90°. It is enlarged with a scale factor of 2. What are the angles of the enlarged triangle?**

☐ 15°, 30° and 45°

☐ 30°, 60° and 90°

☐ 60°, 120° and 180°

☐ 30°, 60° and 120°

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** 30°, 60° and 90°. An enlargement keeps the angles; only the lengths change. Angles in a triangle must still add to 180°, which the doubled angles (360°) would not.

## Kept and changed


:::keep-together

### Kept and changed

1. $\text{angles: } 40°, 60°, 80° \to 40°, 60°, 80°$ — Kept: the shape is the same.
2. $\text{sides: } 3, 4, 5 \to 6, 8, 10$ — Changed: every side is multiplied by 2.
3. $\text{perimeter: } 12 \to 24$ — Also multiplied by 2.
4. $\text{area: } 6 \to 24$ — Changed by more: 2 × 2 = 4 times bigger.

**A rectangle is 4 cm by 3 cm. It is enlarged by a scale factor of 3. How long is the longer side now? (cm)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12

4 × 3 = 12 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Keep and change

**Which of these does an enlargement keep?**

☐ Side lengths

☐ Perimeter

☐ Angles and the shape

☐ Area

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Angles and the shape

The angles and the shape stay the same: only the size changes.

### The perimeter

**A rectangle is 5 cm by 2 cm. Enlarge it by a scale factor of 3. What is its perimeter? (cm)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 42

The new sides are 15 and 6, so the perimeter is 2 × (15 + 6) = 42.
