## Lengths, areas, volumes

$$\text{length} \times k$$

1) $\text{area} \times k^2$ — Two directions, so k × k.
2) $\text{volume} \times k^3$ — Three directions, so k × k × k.
3) $k = 10 \Rightarrow \times 10, \times 100, \times 1000$ — The monster.

## Find the mistake

**Ray enlarges a box by a scale factor of 3. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A box has surface area 40 cm². It is enlarged with a scale factor of 3.
 2  Lengths are multiplied by 3, so the area is too.
 3  New area = 40 × 3 = 120 cm².
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: area is multiplied by k², which is 9, not 3. Only lengths are multiplied by 3. -->
