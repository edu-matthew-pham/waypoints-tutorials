# Fractions, decimals and percentages

Number Foundations · Fractions and decimals

## Today

**We are learning to** one amount, three ways to write it. A percentage is out of 100, so 3/4 = 75/100 = 75% = 0.75. Divide the top by the bottom for a decimal; put a percentage over 100 and simplify for a fraction.

- I can convert between fractions, decimals and percentages.
- I can explain why 0.5 = 50%, not 5%.
- I can compare amounts written in different forms.

## Why it matters

> A 25% discount, a quarter off, 0.25 of the price: the same saving, written three ways. Which is easiest depends on the job.

<!-- Speaker note: In Equivalent fractions you wrote one amount as different fractions. **Percent** means "out of 100", so a percentage is a fraction with 100 parts: $75\% = \tfrac{75}{100}$. A **decimal** shows tenths, hundredths and thousandths: $0.75$ is 75 hundredths. In this lesson you move between all three, using a hundred grid to see why they match. -->

## Three ways

Here is how to move between the three.

Try one: write $\tfrac{3}{8}$ as a decimal.

$$\tfrac{3}{4} = \tfrac{75}{100} = 75\%$$

1) $\tfrac{3}{8} = 3 \div 8 = 0.375$ — Fraction to decimal: divide the top by the bottom.
2) $0.375 \times 100 = 37.5\%$ — Decimal to percentage: multiply by 100.
3) $16\% = \tfrac{16}{100} = \tfrac{4}{25}$ — Percentage to fraction: put it over 100, then simplify.
4) $45\% = 0.45$ — Percentage to decimal: divide by 100.
