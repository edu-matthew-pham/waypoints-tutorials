## Today

**We are learning to** fractions are equal when they shade the same amount. Multiply or divide the top and the bottom by the same number: 2/3 = 4/6 = 8/12. Dividing by the highest common factor gives the simplest form.

- I can find equivalent fractions by multiplying or dividing the top and the bottom by the same number.
- I can write a fraction in its simplest form using the highest common factor.
- I can explain with a picture why two fractions are equal.

## Why it matters

> Half a pizza cut into 2 slices or into 8: the same amount to eat. The numbers change; the amount does not.

<!-- Speaker note: A fraction names an amount: $\tfrac{2}{3}$ is 2 of 3 equal parts. The top number is the **numerator** (how many parts) and the bottom number the **denominator** (how many equal parts in the whole); this lesson calls them the top and the bottom. The same amount can be cut into more parts, or fewer, and named by a different fraction. Fractions that name the same amount are **equivalent**. In this lesson you find equivalent fractions on fraction bars, then write a fraction in its **simplest form**. -->

## Simplest form

Here is how to find equivalent fractions, and the simplest one.

A fraction is in **simplest form** (lowest terms) when the top and the bottom have no common factor but 1. Try one: simplify $\tfrac{12}{18}$.

$$\tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \tfrac{8}{12}$$

1) $\tfrac{12}{18} = \tfrac{12 \div 2}{18 \div 2} = \tfrac{6}{9}$ — Join parts: divide the top and the bottom by a common factor.
2) $\tfrac{6}{9} = \tfrac{6 \div 3}{9 \div 3} = \tfrac{2}{3}$ — 6 and 9 still share 3, so divide again.
3) $\tfrac{12}{18} = \tfrac{12 \div 6}{18 \div 6} = \tfrac{2}{3}$ — Or in one step: divide by the highest common factor, 6.

<!-- Speaker note: Model this live on the board before revealing anything: write each line and say why, pausing to ask the class what comes next. Then click to reveal the lines and compare. Script. ## Why they are equal

**Why is $\tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{8}{12}$?**

- 8 − 2 = 6 and 12 − 3 = 9
- 8 and 12 are both even
- Each third is split into 4 twelfths, so 2 thirds are 8 twelfths

* Answer: Each third is split into 4 twelfths, so 2 thirds are 8 twelfths

<!-- Speaker note: × 4 on the top and the bottom: the same amount, cut into 4 times as many parts. -->
