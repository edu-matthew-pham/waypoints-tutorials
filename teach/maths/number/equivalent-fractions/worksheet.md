## Multiply or divide both


:::keep-together

### Simplest form

Here is how to find equivalent fractions, and the simplest one.

1. $\tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \tfrac{8}{12}$ — Split each part into 4: multiply the top and the bottom by 4.
2. $\tfrac{12}{18} = \tfrac{12 \div 2}{18 \div 2} = \tfrac{6}{9}$ — Join parts: divide the top and the bottom by a common factor.
3. $\tfrac{6}{9} = \tfrac{6 \div 3}{9 \div 3} = \tfrac{2}{3}$ — 6 and 9 still share 3, so divide again.
4. $\tfrac{12}{18} = \tfrac{12 \div 6}{18 \div 6} = \tfrac{2}{3}$ — Or in one step: divide by the highest common factor, 6.

A fraction is in **simplest form** (lowest terms) when the top and the bottom have no common factor but 1. Try one: simplify $\tfrac{12}{18}$.

**Write $\tfrac{12}{18}$ in its simplest form.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2/3

The highest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3, so 2/3.

:::


:::keep-together
