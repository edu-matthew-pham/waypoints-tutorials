## Sign, size, strategy


:::keep-together

### Sign, then size, then strategy

Here is how to work with negative fractions and decimals, and two shortcuts.

1. $-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5}$ — Sign first: different signs, so negative.
2. $= -\tfrac{6}{20} = -\tfrac{3}{10}$ — Then size: multiply tops and bottoms, then simplify.
3. $25 \times (-3.7) \times 4 = 100 \times (-3.7)$ — Any order: pair 25 and 4 first (commutative and associative).
4. $= -370$ — × 100 is easy.
5. $6 \times (-2.5) = 6 \times (-2) + 6 \times (-0.5)$ — Split a hard number (distributive): $-12 + (-3)$.
6. $= -15$ — Then add.

Try one: $-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5}$.

**Work out $-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5}$. Simplify.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** -3/10

Different signs: negative. 3 × 2 = 6 and 4 × 5 = 20: −6/20 = −3/10.

:::


:::keep-together

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $25 \times 4 = 100$ (multiplying can be done in any order, so pair the friendly numbers first); $100 \times (-3.7) = -370$ (different signs give a negative; × 100 moves the digits two places)
