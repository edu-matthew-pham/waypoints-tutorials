## Today

**We are learning to** the sign rules for integers work for every fraction and decimal: work out the sign, then the size. Choose an efficient order: multiplying can be done in any order (25 × 4 first), and a hard product can be split (6 × 2.5 = 6 × 2 + 6 × 0.5).

- I can add, subtract, multiply and divide negative fractions and decimals.
- I can work out the sign first, then the size.
- I can choose an efficient order or split a number to make a calculation easier, and say why it works.

## Why it matters

> A temperature falls half a degree an hour for three hours. A share loses 2.4 dollars over 6 days. Negative fractions and decimals are everywhere money and weather are measured.

<!-- Speaker note: You know the sign rules for integers: the same signs give a positive, different signs a negative, for multiplying and dividing. They work just the same for fractions and decimals: $-0.5 \times (-4) = 2$, and $-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5} = -\tfrac{3}{10}$. This lesson also looks for **efficient** ways to calculate: choosing the order, or splitting a number, so the arithmetic is easy. -->

## Sign, then size, then strategy

Here is how to work with negative fractions and decimals, and two shortcuts.

Try one: $-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5}$.

$$-\tfrac{3}{4} \times \tfrac{2}{5}$$

1) $= -\tfrac{6}{20} = -\tfrac{3}{10}$ — Then size: multiply tops and bottoms, then simplify.
2) $25 \times (-3.7) \times 4 = 100 \times (-3.7)$ — Any order: pair 25 and 4 first (commutative and associative).
3) $= -370$ — × 100 is easy.
4) $6 \times (-2.5) = 6 \times (-2) + 6 \times (-0.5)$ — Split a hard number (distributive): $-12 + (-3)$.
5) $= -15$ — Then add.

## Find the mistake

**Leo works out $-0.4 \times (-0.5)$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Work out −0.4 × (−0.5).
 2  4 × 5 = 20, so 0.4 × 0.5 = 0.20.
 3  Both numbers are negative, so the answer is negative.
 4  −0.4 × (−0.5) = −0.2.
```

* Answer: line 3
