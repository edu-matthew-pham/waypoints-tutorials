Number Foundations · Fractions and decimals

## Today

**We are learning to** split each unit into equal parts and count from zero. A negative fraction or decimal counts to the left, so −1.5 sits halfway between −2 and −1.

- I can place positive and negative fractions, decimals and mixed numerals on a number line.
- I can explain why −1.5 is between −2 and −1, not between −1 and 0.
- I can put a mixed set of fractions and decimals in order.

## Why it matters

> A tide of −0.6 m, a share price down 1¼ dollars, a recipe with ¾ of a cup: numbers between the whole numbers, above and below zero.

<!-- Speaker note: Between any two whole numbers on a number line there are fractions and decimals: $\tfrac{1}{2}$, $0.3$, $1\tfrac{3}{4}$. Below zero there are negative ones too: $-\tfrac{1}{2}$, $-0.3$, $-1\tfrac{3}{4}$. In this lesson you find out how to place them: positive and negative, as fractions, decimals and mixed numerals. -->

## Placing −3/4 and −1 1/4

Here is how to place a negative fraction and a negative mixed numeral.

Then: which is less, $-\tfrac{3}{4}$ or $-\tfrac{1}{4}$?

$$-\tfrac{3}{4}$$

1) $\text{count 3 quarters left from } 0$ — Negative means left of zero.
2) $-\tfrac{3}{4} \text{ is between } -1 \text{ and } 0$ — It is 3 of the 4 quarters from 0 to $-1$.
3) $-1\tfrac{1}{4} = -1 - \tfrac{1}{4}$ — A negative mixed numeral is a whole number and a part, both below zero.
4) $\text{count 1 unit left, then 1 quarter further left}$ — The quarter is further from zero, not back towards it.
5) $-1\tfrac{1}{4} \text{ is between } -2 \text{ and } -1$ — Just past $-1$, a quarter of the way to $-2$.

## Find the mistake

**Jo places $-1.5$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Place −1.5 on the number line.
 2  −1.5 is one and a half below zero.
 3  Start at −1.
 4  Move half a unit to the right, because 0.5 is positive.
 5  So −1.5 is halfway between −1 and 0.
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: −1.5 is 1.5 below zero, so the 0.5 is further below too. From −1, move half a unit left, to halfway between −2 and −1. -->
