## Shared and all


:::keep-together

### Two factorisations side by side

Here is the method for 12 and 18.

1. $12 = 2^2 \times 3, \quad 18 = 2 \times 3^2$ — Write both prime factorisations.
2. $\text{HCF} = 2 \times 3 = 6$ — Shared primes: each prime in both, to its lower power.
3. $\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 36$ — All the primes, each to its higher power (shared ones once).
4. $6 \times 36 = 216 = 12 \times 18$ — A check: HCF × LCM = the two numbers multiplied.

Now try one: the HCF of 24 and 36.

**Find the HCF of 24 and 36.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12

24 = 2³ × 3 and 36 = 2² × 3². Shared: 2 × 2 × 3 = 12.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Zoe finds the LCM of 6 and 8. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Find the lowest common multiple of 6 and 8.
 2  6 = 2 × 3 and 8 = 2³
 3  Multiply the two numbers: 6 × 8 = 48
 4  So the LCM of 6 and 8 is 48.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: 6 × 8 counts the shared 2 twice. All the primes, the shared one once: 2³ × 3 = 24. 24 is a multiple of both, and lower than 48.

:::


:::keep-together

### HCF or LCM?

**Bus A leaves every 12 minutes and bus B every 18 minutes. Both leave at 8:00. After how many minutes do they next leave together? Write each step with its reason.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $12 = 2^2 \times 3$ and $18 = 2 \times 3^2$ (prime factorisations, from factor trees); They leave together at common multiples of 12 and 18 (each bus leaves at multiples of its own time); LCM $= 2^2 \times 3^2 = 36$ (the lowest common multiple: all the primes, shared ones once)

36 minutes, at 8:36: bus A has made 3 trips and bus B 2.

:::

## HCF and LCM


:::keep-together

### LCM

**Find the LCM of 4 and 10.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 20

4 = 2² and 10 = 2 × 5: LCM = 2² × 5 = 20.

:::


:::keep-together

### HCF

**Find the HCF of 18 and 30.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 6

18 = 2 × 3² and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5. Shared: 2 × 3 = 6.

:::


:::keep-together

### A common denominator

**To add $\tfrac{1}{6} + \tfrac{1}{8}$, both fractions need the same denominator. What is the smallest one that works?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 24

The LCM of 6 and 8 is 24 (the next lessons use this).
