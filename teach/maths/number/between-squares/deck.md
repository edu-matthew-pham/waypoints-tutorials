## Today

**We are learning to** 43 is not a perfect square, but it sits between 36 and 49, so √43 is between 6 and 7. Closer to 49 or to 36? Square a decimal to find out: 6.6² ≈ 43.6.

- I can say which two whole numbers a square root is between, with a reason.
- I can estimate a square root to one decimal place by squaring decimals.
- I can solve problems with the side, area and perimeter of a square.

## Bound it, then get closer

Here is the method for $\sqrt{43}$.

Now try one: between which whole numbers is $\sqrt{70}$?

$$36 < 43 < 49$$

1) $6 < \sqrt{43} < 7$ — Take their roots: √43 is between 6 and 7.
2) $6.5^2 = 42.25 \quad 6.6^2 = 43.56$ — Square some tenths. 6.5 is under and 6.6 is over.
3) $\sqrt{43} \approx 6.6$ — 43.56 is closer to 43 (0.56 over) than 42.25 (0.75 under).

## With reasons

**A square garden bed has area 90 m². Between which two whole numbers of metres is its side? Write each step with its reason.**

* Answer: $9^2 = 81$ and $10^2 = 100$ (these are the perfect squares either side of 90); $81 &lt; 90 &lt; 100$, so $9 &lt; \sqrt{90} &lt; 10$ (a bigger square has a bigger side)

<!-- Speaker note: 81 < 90 < 100, so the side is between 9 m and 10 m (about 9.5 m: 9.5² = 90.25). -->
