Number Foundations · Fractions and decimals

## Today

**We are learning to** the same calculation can be quick or slow. Change the order (4 × 2.7 × 25 = 100 × 2.7), change the form (0.75 × 36 = 3/4 of 36), or split a number (12 × 2.5 = 24 + 6). Then check the answer is about right.

- I can choose a quick strategy for a calculation and explain why it is quick.
- I can use any order for multiplying, a friendly fraction, or splitting a number.
- I can estimate to check that my answer is about right.

## Why it matters

> Two students work out 25% of 48. One writes a long multiplication; the other halves twice in their head. Both get 12. Which would you rather do in a shop?

<!-- Speaker note: Every calculation with fractions, decimals and percentages can be done the long way. A good strategy makes it quick, and it usually comes from one of three moves: - **Change the order.** Multiplying can be done in any order: $4 \times 2.7 \times 25 = (4 \times 25) \times 2.7 = 100 \times 2.7$. - **Change the form.** $0.75 = \tfrac{3}{4} = 75\%$: use whichever suits the numbers. - **Split a number.** $12 \times 2.5 = 12 \times 2 + 12 \times 0.5 = 24 + 6$. Then **check**: is the answer about the right size? -->

## Three moves

Here are the three moves, each with a check.

Your working can be short notes. Then choose the reason for the key step.

$$4 \times 2.7 \times 25 = (4 \times 25) \times 2.7 = 270$$

1) $0.75 \times 36 = \tfrac{3}{4} \times 36 = 27$ — Change the form: 36 ÷ 4 = 9, × 3 = 27. Check: a bit less than 36.
2) $12 \times 2.5 = 12 \times 2 + 12 \times 0.5 = 24 + 6 = 30$ — Split a number: 2.5 = 2 + 0.5. Check: between 12 × 2 and 12 × 3.

## Three moves

**Work out $4 \times 2.7 \times 25$ without a calculator. Write your steps.**

* Answer: $4 \times 25 = 100$ (multiplying can be done in any order, so pair the 4 and the 25); $100 \times 2.7 = 270$ (multiplying by 100 makes each digit 100 times bigger)

<!-- Speaker note: 4 × 25 = 100, and 100 × 2.7 = 270. -->

## Change the form

**Work out $0.75 \times 36$ without a calculator. Write your steps.**

* Answer: $0.75 = \tfrac{3}{4}$ (a decimal can be written as a fraction); $\tfrac{1}{4}$ of 36 is 9, so $\tfrac{3}{4}$ of 36 is 27 (36 divides by 4, so the fraction is quick)

<!-- Speaker note: 0.75 = 3/4. 36 ÷ 4 = 9, × 3 = 27. -->

## Split

**Which way works out $25 \times 3.2$ by splitting?**

- $25 \times 3 + 25 \times 0.2 = 75 + 5 = 80$
- $25 \times 3 + 0.2 = 75.2$
- $25 \times 3 \times 0.2 = 15$
- $20 \times 3.2 + 5 \times 3 = 79$

* Answer: $25 \times 3 + 25 \times 0.2 = 75 + 5 = 80$

<!-- Speaker note: 3.2 = 3 + 0.2, so 25 × 3.2 = 25 × 3 + 25 × 0.2 = 75 + 5 = 80 (the distributive law). -->

## Exit ticket

**Work out 5 × 3.6 × 2 and 40% of 15, each in a quick way. Say which move you used.**
