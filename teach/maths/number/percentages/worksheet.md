**In one sentence:** Students use a percentage bar to find a percentage of an amount, apply increases and decreases (in two steps and with one multiplier), and express one amount as a percentage of another (AC9M7N06, node 3).

## Of, off and out of


:::keep-together

### Find it, then use it

Here is each kind with $80.

1. $10\% \text{ of } 80 = 8, \quad 5\% \text{ of } 80 = 4$ — Mental: 10% is a tenth, 5% is half of that.
2. $15\% \text{ of } 80 = 0.15 \times 80 = 12$ — Or as a decimal: 15% = 0.15.
3. $80 - 12 = 68 \quad \text{or} \quad 80 \times 0.85 = 68$ — A decrease: take it off, or keep 85% in one step.
4. $80 \times 1.15 = 92$ — An increase: 100% + 15% = 115%, so × 1.15.
5. $18 \div 24 = 0.75 = 75\%$ — What percentage: divide, then × 100.

Now try one: 35% of 60.

**Find 35% of 60.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 21

10% of 60 is 6, so 30% is 18, and 5% is 3: 21. Or 0.35 × 60 = 21.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Grace finds the sale price of a $50 game that is 20% off. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A $50 game is 20% off. Find the sale price.
 2  20% = 0.2
 3  Sale price = 50 × 0.2
 4  = $10
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: 50 × 0.2 is the discount ($10), not the price. What is left is 80%: 50 × 0.8 = $40.

:::

## Percentages

### A friendly percentage

**Find 12.5% of 64. (12.5% is $\tfrac{1}{8}$.)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

12.5% = 1/8, and 64 ÷ 8 = 8.

:::


:::keep-together

### A multiplier

**What do you multiply by to decrease an amount by 30%?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.7

100% − 30% = 70% = 0.7.

:::


:::keep-together

### Growth

**A plant 120 mm tall grows 5% taller. How tall is it now, in mm?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 126

5% of 120 = 6, so 126 mm. Or 120 × 1.05 = 126.

:::
