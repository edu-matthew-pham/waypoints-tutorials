# Percentages of amounts

Number Foundations · Fractions and decimals

## Today

**We are learning to** 15% of $80 is 0.15 × 80 = $12. Take it off for a discount ($68, or 80 × 0.85 in one step), add it on for an increase ($92, or 80 × 1.15). And 18 out of 24 is 18 ÷ 24 = 75%.

- I can find a percentage of an amount, mentally and with a decimal.
- I can increase or decrease an amount by a percentage, including with one multiplier.
- I can write one amount as a percentage of another.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Do the Run board yourself first. ## Why it matters

> A jacket is $80, and the sign says 15% off. Before you reach the counter, can you work out what you will pay?

<!-- Speaker note: A percentage is a number of hundredths: $15\% = \tfrac{15}{100} = 0.15$. So 15% **of** an amount is $0.15 \times$ the amount, the same "of means multiply" as for fractions. Three questions come up again and again: - **A percentage of an amount:** 15% of $80 is 0.15 × 80 = $12. - **An increase or decrease:** add it on or take it off, or multiply by what is left ($100\% - 15\% = 85\%$, so $\times 0.85$). - **What percentage one amount is of another:** divide, then $\times 100$. -->

## Find it, then use it

Here is each kind with $80.

Now try one: 35% of 60.

$$10\% \text{ of } 80 = 8, \quad 5\% \text{ of } 80 = 4$$

1) $15\% \text{ of } 80 = 0.15 \times 80 = 12$ — Or as a decimal: 15% = 0.15.
2) $80 - 12 = 68 \quad \text{or} \quad 80 \times 0.85 = 68$ — A decrease: take it off, or keep 85% in one step.
3) $80 \times 1.15 = 92$ — An increase: 100% + 15% = 115%, so × 1.15.
4) $18 \div 24 = 0.75 = 75\%$ — What percentage: divide, then × 100.
