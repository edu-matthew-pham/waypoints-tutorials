# Squares and square roots

Number Foundations · Squares, powers and primes

## Today

**We are learning to** a square number is the area of a square: 6² = 6 × 6 = 36. The square root undoes it: √36 = 6, the side of a square with area 36.

- I can work out the square of a number and list the perfect squares to 144.
- I can find the square root of a perfect square and explain what it means.
- I can find the side and the perimeter of a square from its area.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Do the Run board yourself first. Students need times tables and area as side × side (Year 6). The one idea: squaring is a number times itself (the area of a square), and the square root goes back (the side). The common mistakes are squaring as doubling (5² = 10) and rooting as halving (√36 = 18); both are planted. Recall of the perfect squares to 144 is the hinge for the next lesson (roots between whole numbers). -->

---

## Why it matters

> A square patio uses 49 tiles. How long is each side? Squares and square roots go back and forth between a side and an area.

<!-- Speaker note: Multiply a number by itself and you get its **square**: $6 \times 6 = 36$, written $6^2$ ("6 squared"). The name comes from the shape: a square with side 6 covers 36 unit squares. Numbers like 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 and 36 are **perfect squares**. The **square root** goes the other way: $\sqrt{36} = 6$, because $6 \times 6 = 36$. It is the side of a square with that area. -->

## Undo the square

The square root asks: what number, times itself, makes this?

Now try one: $\sqrt{81}$.

$$6^2 = 6 \times 6 = 36$$

1) $\sqrt{36} = 6$ — The square root goes back: 6 × 6 = 36, so the side is 6.
2) $1,\ 4,\ 9,\ 16,\ 25,\ 36,\ 49,\ 64,\ 81,\ 100,\ 121,\ 144$ — The perfect squares from 1² to 12². Learn them.

## Why it works

**Why is $\sqrt{64} = 8$?**

- Because $8 \times 8 = 64$
- Because 64 is even
- Because $64 \div 8 = 8$ and $64 \div 2 = 32$

* Answer: Because $8 \times 8 = 64$

<!-- Speaker note: The square root of 64 is the number that times itself makes 64: 8 × 8 = 64. -->
