Linear relations · Coordinate geometry

## Today

- I can find the across and up differences between two points.

## Why it matters

> A rescue helicopter doesn't follow the roads. How far does it fly, straight from base to the farm?

## What happened?

* 13 km. The flight is the long side of a right-angled triangle with sides 12 and 5: 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169, and √169 = 13. Going 12 east and then 5 north would be 17 km, but the helicopter takes the shortcut.

## Across and up

Here's finding the distance between two points.

Now try one on paper: $(1, 3)$ and $(9, 18)$. Find the across, the up and the distance, then type the distance.

$$A(2, 1),\; B(8, 9)$$

1) $\text{across} = 8 - 2 = 6,\; \text{up} = 9 - 1 = 8$ — The short sides.
2) $d^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$ — Pythagoras: square and add.
3) $d = \sqrt{100} = 10$ — The long side.

## Left and right

**A point is 4 to the left of another and 3 up. The across difference is $-4$. What is $(-4)^2$?**

- −16
- −8
- 8
- 16

* Answer: 16

<!-- Speaker note: (−4) × (−4) = 16. The square is never negative, so left or right makes no difference to the distance. -->

## Find the mistake

**Lin finds the distance between $(1, 5)$ and $(4, 1)$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Find the distance between (1, 5) and (4, 1).
 2  Across: 4 - 1 = 3
 3  Up: 5 - 1 = 4
 4  Distance: 3 + 4 = 7
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: she added the sides, 3 + 4 = 7. The distance is the longest side of the triangle: √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5. -->
