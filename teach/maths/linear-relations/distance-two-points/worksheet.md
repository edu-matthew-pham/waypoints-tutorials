> **Key question:** A rescue helicopter doesn't follow the roads. How far does it fly, straight from base to the farm?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find the straight-line distance between two points on a grid using Pythagoras on the across and up differences (AC9M9A03, node 3).

**Students should be able to say:** I can find the across and up differences between two points. I can use Pythagoras to find the distance between them. I can say why the distance is shorter than going across and then up.

**A rescue helicopter flies straight from its base to a farm. On the map grid the farm is 12 km east and 5 km north of the base. About how far is the flight?**

☐ 7 km

☐ 13 km

☐ 17 km

☐ 60 km

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**What happens:** 13 km. The flight is the long side of a right-angled triangle with sides 12 and 5: 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169, and √169 = 13. Going 12 east and then 5 north would be 17 km, but the helicopter takes the shortcut.

## Pythagoras on the grid


:::keep-together

### Across and up

Here's finding the distance between two points.

1. $A(2, 1),\; B(8, 9)$ — The two points.
2. $\text{across} = 8 - 2 = 6,\; \text{up} = 9 - 1 = 8$ — The short sides.
3. $d^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$ — Pythagoras: square and add.
4. $d = \sqrt{100} = 10$ — The long side.

Now try one on paper: $(1, 3)$ and $(9, 18)$. Find the across, the up and the distance, then type the distance.

**Find the distance between $(1, 2)$ and $(7, 10)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 10

Across 7 − 1 = 6, up 10 − 2 = 8. 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100. √100 = 10.

:::


:::keep-together

### Left and right

**A point is 4 to the left of another and 3 up. The across difference is $-4$. What is $(-4)^2$?**

☐ −16

☐ −8

☐ 8

☐ 16

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 16

(−4) × (−4) = 16. The square is never negative, so left or right makes no difference to the distance.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Lin finds the distance between $(1, 5)$ and $(4, 1)$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Find the distance between (1, 5) and (4, 1).
 2  Across: 4 - 1 = 3
 3  Up: 5 - 1 = 4
 4  Distance: 3 + 4 = 7
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: she added the sides, 3 + 4 = 7. The distance is the longest side of the triangle: √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5.

:::


:::keep-together

### Not a whole number

**Find the distance between $(0, 0)$ and $(3, 5)$ to 2 decimal places.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5.83

3² + 5² = 9 + 25 = 34. √34 ≈ 5.83.

## Find the distance


:::keep-together

### Whole number

**Find the distance between $(0, 0)$ and $(5, 12)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 13

5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169. √169 = 13.

:::


:::keep-together

### Negative coordinates

**Find the distance between $(-2, 1)$ and $(4, 9)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 10

Across 4 − (−2) = 6, up 9 − 1 = 8. 36 + 64 = 100. √100 = 10.

:::


:::keep-together

### A drone

**A drone flies from $(1, 1)$ to $(4, 6)$ on a map grid in km. How far is that, to 1 decimal place?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5.8

Across 3, up 5: 9 + 25 = 34. √34 ≈ 5.8 km.

:::
