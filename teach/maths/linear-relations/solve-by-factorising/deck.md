## The null factor law

Here's solving the garden equation.

Now try one on paper: $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$. Factorise, find both solutions, then type the positive one.

$$x(x + 3) = 40$$

1) $x^2 + 3x - 40 = 0$ — Expand and make one side 0.
2) $(x + 8)(x - 5) = 0$ — Factorise: 8 × −5 = −40 and 8 + (−5) = 3.
3) $x + 8 = 0 \text{ or } x - 5 = 0$ — If a product is 0, a factor is 0.
4) $x = -8 \text{ or } x = 5$ — Both solve the equation.
5) $x = 5 \text{ (a width can't be negative)}$ — The situation picks one.

## The null factor law

**$(x + 8)(x - 5) = 0$. What are the solutions?**

- $x = 8$ or $x = 5$
- $x = 8$ or $x = -5$
- $x = -3$
- $x = -8$ or $x = 5$

* Answer: $x = -8$ or $x = 5$

<!-- Speaker note: x + 8 = 0 gives x = −8, and x − 5 = 0 gives x = 5. -->

## Which is the width?

**A rectangle is $x$ m wide and $(x + 4)$ m long with area 32 m². The equation gives $x = 4$ or $x = -8$. Which is the width?**

- Both: they both solve the equation
- 4 m: a width can't be negative
- −8 m: it's the smaller solution
- Neither

* Answer: 4 m: a width can't be negative

<!-- Speaker note: Both numbers solve x² + 4x − 32 = 0, but only x = 4 can be a width. -->
