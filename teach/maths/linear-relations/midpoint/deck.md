## Why it matters

> Two friends live at opposite ends of town and want to meet halfway. Where on the map is halfway?

## Halfway between numbers

Here's finding a midpoint.

Now try one on paper: the points $(-4, 1)$ and $(6, 9)$. Find the midpoint, then type its $x$-coordinate.

$$A(2, 3),\; B(10, 9)$$

1) $x = \frac{2 + 10}{2} = 6$ — Average the x-coordinates.
2) $y = \frac{3 + 9}{2} = 6$ — Average the y-coordinates.
3) $M(6, 6)$ — The midpoint.

## Find the mistake

**Tam finds the midpoint of $(2, 3)$ and $(10, 9)$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Find the midpoint of (2, 3) and (10, 9).
 2  Add the x-coordinates: 2 + 10 = 12
 3  Add the y-coordinates: 3 + 9 = 12
 4  The midpoint is (12, 12)
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: he stopped after adding. (2 + 10) ÷ 2 = 6 and (3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 6, so the midpoint is (6, 6). -->
