## Why it matters

> A ball is thrown in the air and comes down again. How high does it go, and when does it land?

<!-- Speaker note: A quadratic makes a curve called a parabola. Its highest or lowest point is the turning point, and the curve is a mirror image either side of it. -->

## Halfway

Here's finding the turning point from the intercepts.

Now try one on paper: $y = x^2 - 6x + 5$ crosses the $x$-axis at $1$ and $5$. Find the turning point, then type its $y$-coordinate.

$$y = x^2 - 4x + 3,\; \text{crosses the x-axis at } 1 \text{ and } 3$$

1) $x = \frac{1 + 3}{2} = 2$ — The axis of symmetry: halfway between the intercepts.
2) $y = 2^2 - 4 \times 2 + 3 = -1$ — Put x = 2 into the rule.
3) $\text{turning point } (2, -1)$ — The lowest point of this curve.

* Answer: line 3
