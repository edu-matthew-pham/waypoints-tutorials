## Reading the parabola


:::keep-together

### Halfway

Here's finding the turning point from the intercepts.

1. $y = x^2 - 4x + 3,\; \text{crosses the x-axis at } 1 \text{ and } 3$ — The graph and its x-intercepts.
2. $x = \frac{1 + 3}{2} = 2$ — The axis of symmetry: halfway between the intercepts.
3. $y = 2^2 - 4 \times 2 + 3 = -1$ — Put x = 2 into the rule.
4. $\text{turning point } (2, -1)$ — The lowest point of this curve.

Now try one on paper: $y = x^2 - 6x + 5$ crosses the $x$-axis at $1$ and $5$. Find the turning point, then type its $y$-coordinate.

**A ball's height is 0 m at 0 s and again at 8 s. At what time is it highest?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4

Halfway between 0 and 8: (0 + 8) ÷ 2 = 4 s.

:::


:::keep-together

### The height

**The height is $y = 6x - x^2$. What is $y$ when $x = 3$?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 9

6 × 3 − 3² = 18 − 9 = 9.

:::


:::keep-together

### The axis

**A parabola crosses the $x$-axis at $x = 1$ and $x = 5$. What is its axis of symmetry?**

☐ $x = 3$

☐ $x = 1$

☐ $x = 6$

☐ $x = 5$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $x = 3$

(1 + 5) ÷ 2 = 3.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Jo reads the graph of the ball's height. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The ball's height is y = 6x - x^2, with x in seconds.
 2  The turning point is at (3, 9).
 3  So the highest the ball goes is 3 metres.
 4  It's highest after 3 seconds.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the height is the y-coordinate, 9. The 3 is the time (the x-coordinate): the ball is highest after 3 s.

## Read the curve


:::keep-together

### The axis

**A parabola crosses the $x$-axis at $x = 2$ and $x = 8$. What is its axis of symmetry, $x = $ ?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5

(2 + 8) ÷ 2 = 5.

:::


:::keep-together

### Highest height

**A ball follows $y = 10x - x^2$. What is its highest height?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 25

The ball lands at x = 10, so the top is at x = 5. y = 50 − 25 = 25.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which point?

**The graph of $y = x^2 - 4$ opens upward. What is its turning point?**

☐ It has no turning point

☐ The lowest point, at $(-4, 0)$

☐ The highest point, at $(0, -4)$

☐ The lowest point, at $(0, -4)$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The lowest point, at $(0, -4)$

At x = 0, y = −4, and the curve rises either side: the lowest point is (0, −4).

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: the second differences of a quadratic table are 4. If $y = ax^2$, what is $a$?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

The second difference is 2a, so 2a = 4 and a = 2.
