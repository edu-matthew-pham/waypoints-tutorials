# Describing a relationship

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A graph tells a story about how two things change together. Telling that story in words, section by section, is how scientists and journalists use graphs.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students describe how one measurement changes as the other increases, section by section, and tell the story a graph shows without reading it as a picture (AC9M7A04, node 2).

**Students should be able to say:** I can describe a section of a graph as increasing, decreasing or constant. I can tell the story of a graph in order, with the measurements named. I can draw a graph that matches a story.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Describing sections


:::keep-together

### A rest

Here is how to describe a graph, one section at a time.

1. $\text{0 to 2 h: } 0 \to 60 \text{ km}$ — Take one section at a time and compare its start and end. The distance increases.
2. $\text{2 to 3 h: } 60 \to 60 \text{ km}$ — The distance does not change. It is constant.
3. $\text{3 to 5 h: } 60 \to 0 \text{ km}$ — The distance decreases.
4. $\text{increases} \to \text{constant} \to \text{decreases}$ — Put the sections in order. Say it: "As time increases, the distance from home increases, then stays constant, then decreases."

**Between 2 and 3 hours, which sentence is true?**

☐ As time increases, the distance from home stays constant

☐ As time increases, the distance from home increases

☐ As time increases, the distance from home decreases

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** As time increases, the distance from home stays constant

The line is flat from 2 to 3 hours: the distance is 60 km at both times, so it does not change. The cyclist has stopped.

:::


:::keep-together

### Riding home

**What is the cyclist doing between 3 and 5 hours?**

☐ Riding back towards home

☐ Riding away from home

☐ Riding down a hill

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Riding back towards home

The distance from home decreases from 60 km to 0 km: the cyclist is riding back towards home.

:::


:::keep-together

## Describe and match


:::keep-together

### The ride

**Which sentence describes the ride from 0 to 2 hours?**

☐ As time increases, the distance from home stays constant

☐ The cyclist rides up a hill

☐ As time increases, the distance from home increases

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** As time increases, the distance from home increases

The line goes up from 0 to 60 km: the distance from home increases.

:::


:::keep-together

### A tank

**Which story fits the graph?**

☐ The tank fills, then drains, then fills again

☐ The tank fills at the same rate the whole time

☐ The tank fills slowly, the water is left, then it fills faster

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The tank fills slowly, the water is left, then it fills faster

From 0 to 3 min it rises (30 L). From 3 to 6 min it is flat (nothing added). From 6 to 9 min it rises 60 L, faster than before.

:::
