Data and chance · Year 9: chance

## Count the days

$$\text{late and rainy} = 10$$

1) $\text{late or rainy} = 14 + 14 - 10 = 18$ — Add, then take off the overlap once.
2) $\tfrac{18}{40} = 0.45$ — Relative frequency of "late or rainy".

## Find the mistake

**Kim estimates the chance a day is late or rainy. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  Late on 14 of the 40 days, and rainy on 14 of the 40 days.
 2  So late or rainy is 14 + 14 = 28 days out of 40.
 3  That is 28/40 = 0.7.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: the 10 days that were both are in the late total and in the rainy total. Adding 14 + 14 counts them twice. Late or rainy = 14 + 14 − 10 = 18 days. -->
