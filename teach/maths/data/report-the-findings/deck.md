# Report the findings and how sure you are

Data and chance · Year 9: statistics

<!-- Speaker note: A report says what was found, how strong the evidence is, and what to do. A small difference between two weeks can be chance; say so. About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

- I can report findings with a number.

## Why it matters

> Week 2's mean is a point higher. Is the recycling improving?

## Predict

**Week 2's mean is 1 point higher than week 1's, but bins in each week vary by about 20 points. How strong is the evidence that recycling improved?**

- Weak: the difference is small compared with how much bins vary
- Strong: the mean went up
- Proof: week 2 is higher

<!-- Speaker note: What happens: Weak. A 1-point rise is small compared with a spread of 20, so it could easily be chance. More weeks of data would tell. Take two or three predictions before anyone runs it. -->

## Honest summary

$$68.1 - 67.1 = 1.0$$

1) $\text{range} \approx 20$ — How much bins vary.
2) $1 \ll 20 \Rightarrow \text{weak evidence}$ — A small difference next to a big spread.

## Strong or weak

**Both groups vary by about 30 points. Is a 3-point difference strong evidence?**

- Yes: B is higher
- Yes: 3 is a lot
- No: it is small next to the spread
- Cannot tell: no spread given

* Answer: No: it is small next to the spread
