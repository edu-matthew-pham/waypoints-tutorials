## How strong is the evidence


:::keep-together

### Honest summary

1. $68.1 - 67.1 = 1.0$ — The difference in means.
2. $\text{range} \approx 20$ — How much bins vary.
3. $1 \ll 20 \Rightarrow \text{weak evidence}$ — A small difference next to a big spread.

**Which sentence is the most honest summary?**

☐ Recycling is perfect

☐ Recycling has got worse

☐ Recycling has improved

☐ Week 2's mean was 1 point higher, but the bins vary so much that this may be chance

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Week 2's mean was 1 point higher, but the bins vary so much that this may be chance

It reports the finding and its limit.

:::


:::keep-together

## Differences and spreads


:::keep-together

### Difference

**Group A mean 52, group B mean 55. How many points higher is B's mean?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3

55 − 52 = 3.

:::


:::keep-together

### Strong or weak

**Both groups vary by about 30 points. Is a 3-point difference strong evidence?**

☐ Yes: B is higher

☐ Yes: 3 is a lot

☐ No: it is small next to the spread

☐ Cannot tell: no spread given

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** No: it is small next to the spread

3 out of a spread of 30 is well within variation.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: set 4, 8, 6, 10, 2. What is the range?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

10 − 2 = 8.
