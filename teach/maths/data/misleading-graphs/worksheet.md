# Misleading graphs

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Your club's newsletter says membership is soaring, and its graph agrees.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find the feature that makes a graph misleading (a cut axis, a chosen window, a picture scaled in two directions), say how it acts on the reader, and redraw the numbers honestly (AC9M10ST01, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can name the feature that makes a graph misleading. I can say how that feature works on the reader. I can work out the honest change from the labelled numbers.

## Find the feature and redraw


:::keep-together

### Redraw it honestly

1. $\text{Axis starts at } 205$ — The heights are 7 and 19 above the axis, so the line looks 2.7 times as high.
2. $212 \text{ and } 224$ — The real values are the labels.
3. $224 - 212 = 12$ — The real change in members.
4. $12 \div 212 \approx 0.057$ — As a fraction of the starting members.
5. $\approx 5.7\%$ — The honest growth is under 6%.

Now try one on paper: the canteen chart, with sales of $480 in week 1 and $520 in week 8 and the axis starting at $470. Work out how much sales really grew, as a percentage.

**A canteen chart shows weekly sales of $480 in week 1 and $520 in week 8 on bars whose axis starts at $470. By what percentage did sales really grow? (1 decimal place) (Made up for the task.)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $520 - 480 = 40$ (the real difference in dollars); $40 \div 480 \times 100 = 8.3$ (divide by the starting value, then make it a percentage)

40 ÷ 480 = 0.0833, so sales grew about 8.3%. The axis starts at $470, so the bars look 10 and 50 tall: five times as tall for a real growth of under a tenth.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which feature

**Which feature of the club's graph makes the growth look bigger than it is?**

☐ The line is orange

☐ The vertical axis starts at 205, not at 0

☐ There are only five points

☐ The years are along the bottom

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The vertical axis starts at 205, not at 0

With the axis cut at 205 the heights show only the part above 205, so a rise of 12 members fills most of the graph.

:::


:::keep-together

### Honest height

**If the vertical axis started at 0, how many times as high as the 2022 point would the 2026 point be? (2 decimal places)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 1.06

224 ÷ 212 is about 1.06: the two points would be almost the same height.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Kai reads the club's graph. Click the line where the working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The 2022 point is 7 units above the bottom of the axis, and the 2026 point is 19 units above it.
 2  19 ÷ 7 is about 2.7.
 3  So 2026 has about 2.7 times as many members as 2022.
 4  The newsletter's "soaring" is fair.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the heights are measured from 205, not from 0, so 19 ÷ 7 compares how far above 205 the points are. The members are 224 and 212, a ratio of about 1.06.

:::


:::keep-together

### Icons that grow

**A pictograph shows twice as many cars sold this year by drawing the car icon twice as wide and twice as tall. How many times as big, by area, does the icon look?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4

Twice as wide and twice as tall is 2 × 2 = 4 times the area. The eye reads the whole picture, so the icon looks four times as big for twice the cars.

## Bars on a cut axis


:::keep-together

### Bars that look 3 times as tall

**Two bars are drawn on an axis that starts at 60. Their values are 90 and 70. How many times as tall does the first bar look as the second? (1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3

(90 − 60) ÷ (70 − 60) = 30 ÷ 10 = 3.

:::


:::keep-together

### The real difference

**The values really are 90 and 70. How many percent bigger is 90 than 70? (1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 28.6

(90 − 70) ÷ 70 = 0.2857, about 28.6%.

:::


:::keep-together
