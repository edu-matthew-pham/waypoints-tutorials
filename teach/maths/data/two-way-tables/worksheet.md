## Build them from a story


:::keep-together

### Neither

Here is the routine. Start in the middle.

1. $25 \text{ students: } 15 \text{ dog},\ 10 \text{ cat},\ 4 \text{ both}$ — Read the story.
2. $\text{both} = 4$ — Write the overlap first.
3. $\text{dog only} = 15 - 4 = 11,\ \text{cat only} = 10 - 4 = 6$ — Take the overlap from each total.
4. $\text{neither} = 25 - (11 + 4 + 6) = 4$ — Everyone else.

**40 people: 22 like tea, 18 like coffee and 7 like both. How many like neither?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7

Tea only 22 − 7 = 15, coffee only 18 − 7 = 11. 15 + 7 + 11 = 33, so 40 − 33 = 7 like neither.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Lia draws the Venn diagram for the same 40 people. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  In a group of 40 people, 22 like tea, 18 like coffee and 7 like both.
 2  Lia writes 7 in the overlap of the Venn diagram.
 3  She writes 22 in the tea-only part and 18 in the coffee-only part.
 4  Neither: 40 − 22 − 18 − 7 = −7.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: 22 like tea, but 7 of them are already in the overlap. Tea only is 15 and coffee only is 11. A negative count shows something went wrong.

:::


:::keep-together

The table has total rows and columns; in a Venn diagram you add the parts yourself.

## Tables and diagrams


:::keep-together

### Row total

**A two-way table: 18 students walk and own a bike, 7 walk and don't own a bike. How many students walk in all?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 25

The Walk row: 18 + 7 = 25.

:::


:::keep-together

### Case only

**60 people: 35 have a phone case, 20 have a screen protector, 12 have both. How many have a case but no screen protector?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 23

35 − 12 = 23.

:::


:::keep-together

### Neither

**In that group of 60, how many have neither?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 17

Case only 23, both 12, protector only 8: 43. So 60 − 43 = 17 have neither.

:::
