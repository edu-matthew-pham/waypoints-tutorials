## Planning and bias


:::keep-together

### Variable type

**Hours of sleep is measured on a clock. Is it discrete or continuous?**

☐ Discrete: it is counted

☐ Continuous: any value in a range can occur

☐ Neither

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Continuous: any value in a range can occur

Measured quantities such as time are continuous. Counted ones, such as the number of pets, are discrete.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Kai wants to know how long Year 7 students sleep. Click the line where he draws a conclusion he cannot support.**

```
 1  Kai's question: how many hours do Year 7 students sleep on school nights?
 2  He asks the 8 students in his swimming squad.
 3  Their median is 8 hours, so Year 7 students sleep 8 hours.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: eight students from one squad are a small group and not a fair sample of Year 7. Swimmers may sleep more or less than others. The result describes his squad.

:::


:::keep-together

### A fair sample

**Which is the fairest way to choose 20 students from Year 7?**

☐ Ask those who put up their hands

☐ Ask your friends

☐ Pick 20 names at random from the class lists

☐ Ask the first 20 students at the canteen

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Pick 20 names at random from the class lists

Random selection gives every student the same chance and avoids bias.

## Investigations


:::keep-together

### The mean

**Hours of sleep: 7, 8, 9. What is the mean?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

(7 + 8 + 9) ÷ 3 = 8 hours.

:::


:::keep-together

### Discrete

**Which variable is discrete?**

☐ A student's height

☐ The time to run 100 m

☐ The mass of a school bag

☐ The number of siblings a student has

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The number of siblings a student has

Counted quantities are discrete. The others are measured.

:::


:::keep-together

### A biased survey

**A survey asks only students who walk to school about how long they sleep. What is the problem?**

☐ The sample is too big

☐ Nothing

☐ Sleep cannot be measured

☐ The sample is biased: walkers may not be like everyone else

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The sample is biased: walkers may not be like everyone else

A sample must represent the group the question is about.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review: median

**Review: what is the median of 4, 7, 9, 12, 13?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 9

The middle value of the five is 9.
