# Two data sets, one scale

Data and chance · Year 9: statistics

<!-- Speaker note: To compare two data sets fairly, draw them on the same scale, one above the other. Then the centre, the spread and the shape can be read side by side. About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

## Today

- I can draw or read two sets on a shared scale.
- I can find and compare their ranges.

## Why share a scale

$$A:\ 8\text{ to }12,\ \text{range }4$$

1) $B:\ 5\text{ to }15,\ \text{range }10$ — Same scale, so it is clearly wider.
2) $\text{both medians} = 10$ — The centres agree, the spreads do not.

## Why share a scale

**Why must two plots being compared use the same scale?**

- So both have the same number of dots
- So the same distance means the same number on both
- So they are the same colour
- So they look neat

* Answer: So the same distance means the same number on both

<!-- Speaker note: With the same scale, a gap or a spread of the same length means the same amount on both. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Range of B

**Brand B's results run from 5 to 15 hours. What is its range?**

* Answer: 10

<!-- Speaker note: 15 − 5 = 10. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Find the mistake

**Nina compares the brands. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  Brand A is drawn on a plot from 0 to 20 and brand B on a plot from 0 to 100.
 2  B looks narrower, so B's results are more consistent.
 3  So B is the better battery.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: the two plots use different scales, so the same width shows different spreads. Redraw both on one scale before comparing. -->

## Range difference

**Set A: 12, 14, 15, 17, 18. Set B: 9, 13, 15, 18, 24. What is B's range minus A's range?**

* Answer: 9

<!-- Speaker note: B: 24 − 9 = 15. A: 18 − 12 = 6. 15 − 6 = 9. -->

**What is the median of set B (9, 13, 15, 18, 24)?**

## Review

**Review: the mean is 30 and the median 24. What shape is it likely?**

- Symmetric
- Skewed left
- Skewed right
- Uniform

* Answer: Skewed right
