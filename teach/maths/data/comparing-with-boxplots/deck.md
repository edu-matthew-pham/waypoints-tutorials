# Comparing with boxplots

Data and chance · Year 10: boxplots

<!-- Speaker note: Students compare two data sets drawn as boxplots on one scale by centre, spread, shape and outliers, and learn that a lower median doesn't make a route more reliable (AC9M10ST02, node 3). ## Today

- I can compare two boxplots by centre and spread.
- I can say what an outlier does and doesn't change.

## Comparing two journeys

Now try one on paper: two ways to get to school with the five numbers walk 14, 16, 18, 21, 25 and bus 10, 15, 19, 24, 38 minutes. Find how many minutes wider the bus's middle half is than the walk's.

$$\text{medians: } 4 \text{ and } 5$$

1) $\text{IQR: } 6 - 2 = 4 \text{ and } 6 - 4 = 2$ — Route 1's middle half is twice as spread out. This compares the spreads.
2) $\text{whiskers: } 0 \text{ to } 8 \text{ and } 3 \text{ to } 7$ — Route 1's days range further.
3) $\text{outlier: } 15$ — Route 1 has one very late day, and Route 2 has none.
4) $\text{So Route 2 is steadier.}$ — Say what it means: you can plan around Route 2 more easily.

## Comparing two journeys

**Two ways to get to school have these five numbers for the journey in minutes (made up). Walk: 14, 16, 18, 21, 25. Bus: 10, 15, 19, 24, 38. How many minutes wider is the bus's middle half than the walk's?**

* Answer: walk: $21 - 16 = 5$ (the interquartile range of the walk); bus: $24 - 15 = 9$ (the interquartile range of the bus); $9 - 5 = 4$ (how much wider the bus's middle half is)

<!-- Speaker note: Walk IQR 21 − 16 = 5; bus IQR 24 − 15 = 9; the bus's middle half is 9 − 5 = 4 minutes wider. The walk is the steadier trip, though the bus's median (19) is only 1 minute above the walk's (18). -->

## Both medians and spreads

**Route 1 has a median of 4 minutes and an IQR of 4; Route 2 has a median of 5 and an IQR of 2. Which statement does the boxplot support best?**

- Route 1 is usually a bit earlier, but its lateness varies more, so Route 2 is steadier
- Route 2 is better in every way, because its median is higher
- Route 1 is better in every way, because its median is lower
- The routes are the same, because the medians are close

* Answer: Route 1 is usually a bit earlier, but its lateness varies more, so Route 2 is steadier

## Find the mistake

**Sam compares the two routes. Click the line where the reasoning goes wrong.**

```
 1  Route 1's median is 4 minutes late and Route 2's median is 5.
 2  Route 1's box is from 2 to 6 and Route 2's box is from 4 to 6.
 3  Route 1 has the lower median, so it is the more reliable route.
 4  So Sam takes Route 1.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: the medians compare the typical day on each route, not how much each route varies from day to day. The boxes and whiskers show the spread, and Route 1's is twice as long. -->

## An outlier's effect

**Route 1 has one very late day, 15 minutes, drawn as a cross. Which part of Route 1's boxplot does that day change least?**

- The highest value
- The upper whisker
- The box and the median bar
- The range

* Answer: The box and the median bar

<!-- Speaker note: The box and the median bar depend on the middle values. One very late day changes the highest value and the range, and shows up as a cross, but the quartiles and median stay the same. -->

## Exit ticket

**Class A has a median of 62 and an interquartile range of 8. Class B has a median of 65 and an interquartile range of 20. Which class has marks that are more alike, and which has the higher typical mark?**

* Class A's marks are more alike (IQR 8); Class B has the higher typical mark (median 65).
