> **Key question:** A form asks: "Do you play sport or music?" What if you do both?


:::teacher-note

## Three meanings


:::keep-together

### Exactly one

1. $\text{sport} = 20,\ \text{music} = 13,\ \text{both} = 8$ — Totals include the overlap.
2. $20 + 13 - 8 = 25$ — Inclusive or: add, then take the overlap off once.
3. $25 - 8 = 17$ — Exclusive or: either circle but not the overlap.

**How many play sport or music, but NOT both (exclusive or)?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 17

Sport only 12 + music only 5 = 17.

:::


:::keep-together

### And

**A form says: "Tick if you play sport and music." Which students tick?**

☐ Only the 8 who play both

☐ The 12 who play sport only

☐ The 17 who play exactly one

☐ The 25 who play either

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Only the 8 who play both

"And" means in both circles: the overlap.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Zoe counts the students who play sport or music. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  20 students play sport and 13 play music, out of 30.
 2  To find how many play sport or music I add: 20 + 13 = 33.
 3  So 33 students play sport or music.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: the 8 students who play both are in the sport total and in the music total. Adding 20 + 13 counts them twice. Take them away once: 20 + 13 − 8 = 25.

## And, or and neither


:::keep-together

### Chess or draughts

**20 students: 7 play chess only, 3 play both chess and draughts, 6 play draughts only, the rest play neither. How many play chess or draughts?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 16

7 + 3 + 6 = 16.

:::


:::keep-together

### Exactly one

**In the same group, how many play exactly one of chess and draughts?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 13

7 + 6 = 13.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: a bus was late on 9 of 30 days. What is the relative frequency (a decimal)?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.3

9 ÷ 30 = 0.3.
