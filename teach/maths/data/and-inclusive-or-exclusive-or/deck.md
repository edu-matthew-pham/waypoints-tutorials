## Exactly one

$$\text{sport} = 20,\ \text{music} = 13,\ \text{both} = 8$$

1) $20 + 13 - 8 = 25$ — Inclusive or: add, then take the overlap off once.
2) $25 - 8 = 17$ — Exclusive or: either circle but not the overlap.

## And

**A form says: "Tick if you play sport and music." Which students tick?**

- Only the 8 who play both
- The 12 who play sport only
- The 17 who play exactly one
- The 25 who play either

* Answer: Only the 8 who play both

<!-- Speaker note: "And" means in both circles: the overlap. -->

## Find the mistake

**Zoe counts the students who play sport or music. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  20 students play sport and 13 play music, out of 30.
 2  To find how many play sport or music I add: 20 + 13 = 33.
 3  So 33 students play sport or music.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: the 8 students who play both are in the sport total and in the music total. Adding 20 + 13 counts them twice. Take them away once: 20 + 13 − 8 = 25. -->
