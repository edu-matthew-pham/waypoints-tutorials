# Which is better? Centre, spread, shape and outliers

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Both brands have a median of 10 hours. Are the batteries the same?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Compare two distributions on centre, spread, shape and outliers, and say which is better for the job. Two sets can share a median and still be very different.

**Students should be able to say:** I can compare two sets on centre, spread, shape and outliers. I can say which is better for a purpose. I can say why a median alone is not enough.

### Mean of B

1. $\text{centre: both median } 10$ — Same.
2. $\text{spread: } 4 \text{ against } 10$ — A is steadier.
3. $\text{shape: both roughly symmetric; outliers: none}$ — Nothing odd.

**What is brand B's mean to 1 decimal place? (5, 7, 9, 10, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15)**

## Compare and decide


:::keep-together

### Difference of means

**Set A has mean 50 and range 10. Set B has mean 52 and range 40. By how much does B's mean exceed A's?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

52 − 50 = 2.

:::


:::keep-together

### Reliable route

**A bus company wants the most reliable route. Which statistic matters most?**

☐ The mean lateness only

☐ The number of buses

☐ The spread of the lateness

☐ The highest lateness only

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The spread of the lateness

A small spread means the bus is steady.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: set A runs from 12 to 20 and set B from 8 to 24. How much wider is B's range?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

B: 16, A: 8, so 16 − 8 = 8.
