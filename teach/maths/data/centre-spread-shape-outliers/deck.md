# Which is better? Centre, spread, shape and outliers

Data and chance · Year 9: statistics

<!-- Speaker note: Compare two distributions on centre, spread, shape and outliers, and say which is better for the job. Two sets can share a median and still be very different. About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

## Why it matters

> Both brands have a median of 10 hours. Are the batteries the same?

<!-- Speaker note: The battery data again: brand A (8 to 12 hours) and brand B (5 to 15 hours), nine batteries each (made up for the task). The medians are both 10. ## Predict

**Both brands have median 10 hours. Brand A ranges from 8 to 12 and brand B from 5 to 15. For a torch you need to rely on, which do you choose?**

- Either: the medians are equal
- Brand B: its best battery lasts longest
- Brand A: its lives are closer together

## Difference of means

**Set A has mean 50 and range 10. Set B has mean 52 and range 40. By how much does B's mean exceed A's?**

* Answer: 2

<!-- Speaker note: 52 − 50 = 2. -->

## Reliable route

**A bus company wants the most reliable route. Which statistic matters most?**

- The mean lateness only
- The number of buses
- The spread of the lateness
- The highest lateness only

* Answer: The spread of the lateness

<!-- Speaker note: A small spread means the bus is steady. -->
