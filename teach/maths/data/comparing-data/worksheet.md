## Describe, then compare


:::keep-together

### Shape, centre, spread

Here is how to describe and compare.

1. $\text{Shape}$ — Symmetrical, or skewed with a tail? Clusters, gaps, outliers?
2. $\text{Centre: medians } 15 \text{ and } 16$ — Close: a typical score is about the same.
3. $\text{Spread: ranges } 6 \text{ and } 16$ — Very different: Class B's scores vary much more.
4. $\text{Compare both}$ — "Similar centres, but A is more consistent (range 6, not 16)."

Now describe the shape of the books data.

**Which describes the shape of this data?**

☐ Every value is equally common

☐ Skewed: most values are low, with a tail of a few high values

☐ Symmetrical about the middle

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Skewed: most values are low, with a tail of a few high values

Most students read 1 to 3 books, a few read many: the data is skewed, with a tail to the right.

:::


:::keep-together

```
 2  The best score in Class B is 22; in Class A it is 18.

```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: one student's score does not describe a class. Compare centres (medians 15 and 16: close) and spreads (ranges 6 and 16: A is much more consistent).

:::

### A good comparison

**Which is the best comparison of Class A and Class B?**

☐ Class B is better because it has a 22.

☐ Class A is better because 15 + 14 is more than 6 + 10.

☐ The classes have similar medians (15 and 16), but Class A's scores are much less spread (range 6 against 16), so A was more consistent.

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The classes have similar medians (15 and 16), but Class A's scores are much less spread (range 6 against 16), so A was more consistent.

A good comparison names the centre and the spread of both, with numbers, and says what they mean.

## Compare in context


:::keep-together

### Centre and spread

**Two bakeries' delivery times (min). Bakery X: median 25, range 10. Bakery Y: median 22, range 30. Which statement is right?**

☐ Y is slower and more reliable

☐ Y is usually a bit faster, but X is more reliable

☐ X is faster and more reliable

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Y is usually a bit faster, but X is more reliable

Lower median: Y is faster typically. Smaller range: X is more reliable.

:::


:::keep-together

### A fair comparison

**Which is NOT a fair comparison of two netball teams' goals?**

☐ Team 1 scored the single highest game, so Team 1 is better

☐ Team 1's median was 3 goals higher

☐ Team 2's goals varied less (smaller range)

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Team 1 scored the single highest game, so Team 1 is better

One game does not describe a team.

:::


:::keep-together

### Skewed

**Data bunched at the high end with a few low values. What shape is it?**

☐ Skewed, with a tail to the right

☐ Skewed, with a tail to the left

☐ Symmetrical

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Skewed, with a tail to the left

The tail points towards the few low values: to the left.

:::
