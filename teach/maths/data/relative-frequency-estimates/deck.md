# Records as probabilities

Data and chance · Year 9: chance

## Why it matters

> The 8:05 bus has been late on some days. How likely is it to be late tomorrow?

## Work it out

$$\text{late on 8 of 40 days}$$

1) $\tfrac{8}{40} = 0.2$ — Relative frequency = times it happened ÷ number of trials.
2) $0.2 \times 50 = 10$ — In 50 more days, expect about 10 late.

## Trust the estimate

**Which estimate of the chance of a late bus can you trust most?**

- Late on 2 of 5 days
- They are equally good
- Late on 80 of 400 days
- Late on 1 of 2 days

* Answer: Late on 80 of 400 days

<!-- Speaker note: The longer record. Relative frequencies from lots of trials are steadier. -->

## Find the mistake

**Tess uses her first 5 days of records. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  The bus was late on 2 of her first 5 school days.
 2  So the probability of a late bus is exactly 2/5 = 0.4.
 3  In the next 100 days it will be late on exactly 40.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: a relative frequency from 5 days is only an estimate. The real chance could be quite different, and in 100 days the number of late days will only be about 40, not exactly 40. -->

## Make: tomorrow's bus

Predict: in the next 30 school days, about how many late days do you expect?

**Also used in**

- **Weather forecasts.** "30% chance of rain" comes from how often it rained on past days that looked like this one.
- **Sport.** A goalkeeper's save rate is a relative frequency from past shots.
