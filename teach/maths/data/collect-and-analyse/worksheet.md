## The numbers


:::keep-together

### Mean

1. $\text{mean} = \tfrac{671}{10} = 67.1$ — Add the percentages and divide by 10.
2. $\text{range} = 81 - 55 = 26$ — Highest minus lowest.
3. $\text{median} = 67$ — Middle of the ordered values.

**What is the mean percentage recyclable of the 10 bins (1 decimal place)?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 67.1

671 ÷ 10 = 67.1.

:::


:::keep-together

### Range

**What is the range of the 10 bins?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 26

81 − 55 = 26.

:::


:::keep-together
