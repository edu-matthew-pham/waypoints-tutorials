# Who was asked, and how

Data and chance · Year 9: statistics

<!-- Speaker note: A survey report says how the data was obtained. Find that, say who was left out, and say how it could change the result. About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

## Today

- I can find how data was obtained in a report.
- I can say who was left out.
- I can say how that might change the result.

## Predict

**1200 people voted in the club website poll and 92% said yes. Does that show 92% of the city wants a bigger stadium?**

- Probably not: the voters were mostly fans who chose to vote
- Yes: 92% is a clear result
- Yes: 1200 is a big sample

## Who chose

**In the club poll, who chose whether to be in the sample?**

- The club chose them
- The people who voted chose themselves
- The council chose them
- They were picked at random

* Answer: The people who voted chose themselves

<!-- Speaker note: Self-selected: whoever visited and felt like voting. That tends to over-represent people with strong views, here fans. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Who is missing

**Which group is most likely missing from the poll?**

- Members of the club
- Residents who never visit the club site, such as those near the stadium who dislike noise
- People who voted twice
- Fans who visit every day

* Answer: Residents who never visit the club site, such as those near the stadium who dislike noise

<!-- Speaker note: People who never visit the site, and may feel quite differently, had no chance to answer. -->

## Find the mistake

**Dan reads the report. Click the line where he goes wrong.**

```
 1  The club poll had 1200 voters and 92% said yes.
 2  1200 is a big sample, so the result is reliable.
 3  So 92% of the city wants a bigger stadium.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: a large sample reduces chance error but not bias. 1200 self-selected fans are still fans. Ask how they were chosen, not just how many. -->

## Reviews

**"Customers who left a review online rated the café 4.8." What is the main issue?**

- Nothing
- The rating is too high
- Only customers who chose to review are counted
- There are too many customers

* Answer: Only customers who chose to review are counted

<!-- Speaker note: Reviewers choose themselves, and people with strong feelings review most. -->

## Library

**A university surveys every 20th student leaving the library. What is the weakness?**

- It is a census
- The sample is too regular
- It is random
- Library users aren't all students

* Answer: Library users aren't all students

<!-- Speaker note: Only people leaving the library are reachable, so students who rarely go are left out. -->

## Review

**Review: of 40 days, it rained on 14, the bus was late on 14, and both on 10. How many days had neither?**

* Answer: 22

<!-- Speaker note: 14 + 14 − 10 = 18 days had rain or late, so 40 − 18 = 22. -->
