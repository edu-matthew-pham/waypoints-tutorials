## What the method does to the result


:::keep-together

### Who chose

1. $\text{Result: } 92\% \text{ yes}$ — What the report says.
2. $\text{Method: open poll, self-selected}$ — How the data was obtained.
3. $\text{Missing: people who never visit the club site}$ — Who could not answer, so the result may differ.

**In the club poll, who chose whether to be in the sample?**

☐ The club chose them

☐ The people who voted chose themselves

☐ The council chose them

☐ They were picked at random

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The people who voted chose themselves

Self-selected: whoever visited and felt like voting. That tends to over-represent people with strong views, here fans.

:::


:::keep-together

### Who is missing

**Which group is most likely missing from the poll?**

☐ Members of the club

☐ Residents who never visit the club site, such as those near the stadium who dislike noise

☐ People who voted twice

☐ Fans who visit every day

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Residents who never visit the club site, such as those near the stadium who dislike noise

People who never visit the site, and may feel quite differently, had no chance to answer.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Dan reads the report. Click the line where he goes wrong.**

```
 1  The club poll had 1200 voters and 92% said yes.
 2  1200 is a big sample, so the result is reliable.
 3  So 92% of the city wants a bigger stadium.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: a large sample reduces chance error but not bias. 1200 self-selected fans are still fans. Ask how they were chosen, not just how many.

## Spot the method


:::keep-together

### Reviews

**"Customers who left a review online rated the café 4.8." What is the main issue?**

☐ Nothing

☐ The rating is too high

☐ Only customers who chose to review are counted

☐ There are too many customers

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Only customers who chose to review are counted

Reviewers choose themselves, and people with strong feelings review most.

:::


:::keep-together

### Library

**A university surveys every 20th student leaving the library. What is the weakness?**

☐ It is a census

☐ The sample is too regular

☐ It is random

☐ Library users aren't all students

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Library users aren't all students

Only people leaving the library are reachable, so students who rarely go are left out.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: of 40 days, it rained on 14, the bus was late on 14, and both on 10. How many days had neither?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 22

14 + 14 − 10 = 18 days had rain or late, so 40 − 18 = 22.

:::
