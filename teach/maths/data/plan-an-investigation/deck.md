## How many bins

$$\text{question: what \% of recycling bin contents is recyclable?}$$

1) $5 \text{ bins} \times 5 \text{ days} = 25$ — A sampling plan.
2) $\text{ordinary days, different places}$ — Avoid skew.

## How many bins

**You will sort 5 bins from different places each day for 5 days. How many bins is that in all?**

* Answer: 25

## Which days

**Which choice of days gives the fairest picture?**

- Only the day with the best weather
- Only Fridays
- Five ordinary school days across a week
- Only the day after the school fair

* Answer: Five ordinary school days across a week

<!-- Speaker note: Ordinary days spread across the week. Special days skew the data. -->

## Find the mistake

**Zed plans the sampling. Click the line where he goes wrong.**

```
 1  We want the percentage of recycling that is recyclable.
 2  I will sort the bins on the day after the school fair because there will be a lot of rubbish.
 3  That will give a good picture of the whole year.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: one unusual day (with an event) is not typical. Sample over several ordinary days and different places. -->
