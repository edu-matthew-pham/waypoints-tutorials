## Sample and skew


:::keep-together

### How many bins

1. $\text{question: what \% of recycling bin contents is recyclable?}$ — A question data can answer.
2. $5 \text{ bins} \times 5 \text{ days} = 25$ — A sampling plan.
3. $\text{ordinary days, different places}$ — Avoid skew.

**You will sort 5 bins from different places each day for 5 days. How many bins is that in all?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 25

5 × 5 = 25 bins.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which days

**Which choice of days gives the fairest picture?**

☐ Only the day with the best weather

☐ Only Fridays

☐ Five ordinary school days across a week

☐ Only the day after the school fair

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Five ordinary school days across a week

Ordinary days spread across the week. Special days skew the data.

:::


:::keep-together

Why is it wrong?

Line 2: one unusual day (with an event) is not typical. Sample over several ordinary days and different places.

## Plan numbers


:::keep-together

### Bins in all

**6 bins a day for 4 days. How many bins in all?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 24

6 × 4 = 24.

:::keep-together

### Sorting time

**Sorting each bin takes 5 minutes. How many minutes for 24 bins?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 120

24 × 5 = 120.
