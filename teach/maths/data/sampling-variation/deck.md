Data and chance · Samples

## Today

**We are learning to** random samples from the same population give different results. That is sampling variation, not a mistake. Bigger samples vary less, so their results are more reliable.

- I can explain why two fair samples give different results.
- I can describe how the spread of sample results changes with sample size.
- I can choose a sample size that is big enough for a job.

## Least variation

**Which samples will give results that vary the least?**

- Samples of 20
- They all vary the same
- Samples of 10
- Samples of 100

* Answer: Samples of 100

<!-- Speaker note: Bigger samples average over more people, so one unusual person changes the result less. -->

## Small samples

**Two classes each take a random sample of 10 students from the same school. One finds 0.3 own a dog; the other finds 0.6. What is the best explanation?**

- Sampling variation: samples of 10 vary a lot
- The second class made a mistake
- The first class made a mistake
- Dogs changed

* Answer: Sampling variation: samples of 10 vary a lot

<!-- Speaker note: Small samples vary a lot. Larger samples would agree more closely. -->
