# Shape: symmetric or skewed

Data and chance · Year 9: statistics

<!-- Speaker note: A distribution's shape says where the values pile up and where the long tail runs. A tail to the right is skewed right and pulls the mean above the median. About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

---

## Today

- I can describe a distribution as symmetric or skewed.
- I can name the direction by the tail.
- I can link the skew to the mean and the median.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Students need mean and median, dot plots. The one idea: the tail names the skew, and it pulls the mean towards it. Waiting times are made up. -->

---

## Why it matters

> Waiting times at a clinic: lots of short waits, a few very long ones. What shape is that?

<!-- Speaker note: Fourteen patients' waiting times at a clinic, in minutes, are plotted below (made up for the task). Most waits are short, but a few are very long. The long stretch of dots on one side is the tail, and it names the shape: the tail points the way the data is skewed. Have a guess which way the tail points, and we'll come back to it at the end. -->

## Predict

**Most waits are 5 to 10 minutes. A few are 22 and 30 minutes. Which way does the tail point, and what is the skew called?**

- Right: skewed right
- Left: skewed left
- No tail: symmetric

## What happened?

* The tail points right (towards the large values), so it is skewed right (or positively skewed). The bulk of the data is on the left.

<!-- Speaker note: Reveal after running. Ask who predicted it, and what made them think so. -->

<span style="background:#dbeafe; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #3b82f6; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">💬 Discussion</span>

## Skewed left

$$\text{most waits } 5\text{--}10,\ \text{a few } 22, 30$$

1) $\text{skewed right: mean} > \text{median}$ — The tail pulls the mean up.
2) $\text{symmetric: mean} \approx \text{median}$ — Balanced both sides.

<!-- Speaker note: Model this live on the board before revealing anything: write each line and say why, pausing to ask the class what comes next. Then click to reveal the lines and compare. Script. Line 2: write symmetric: mean median and say "Balanced both sides." -->

## Skewed left

**The tail of a distribution is on the left, towards the small values. What is it called?**

- Symmetric
- Skewed right
- Skewed left
- Uniform

* Answer: Skewed left

<!-- Speaker note: The long tail is on the left, so it is skewed left. -->

## Mean and median

**A distribution is skewed left. How do the mean and median compare?**

- They are equal
- The mean is below the median
- The mean is above the median
- They can't be compared

* Answer: The mean is below the median

<!-- Speaker note: The low tail pulls the mean down below the median. -->

## Find the mistake

**Omar describes the clinic waits. Click the line where he goes wrong.**

```
 1  The clinic waits are mostly 5 to 10 minutes with a few long ones.
 2  Most of the data is on the left, so it is skewed left.
 3  So the mean is below the median.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: skew is named for the tail, not for where most data sits. The tail runs to the right (long waits), so it is skewed right. -->

## Mean above median

**A set has mean 52 and median 48. What shape is most likely?**

- Symmetric
- Skewed right
- Skewed left
- Uniform

* Answer: Skewed right

<!-- Speaker note: Mean above median means a tail to the right. -->
