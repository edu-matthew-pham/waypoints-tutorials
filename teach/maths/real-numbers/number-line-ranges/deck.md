## Why it matters

> A hole has to be 20 mm, give or take a tiny bit. Where's the line between a part that fits and one that doesn't?

<!-- Speaker note: Real numbers fill the whole number line, so a range is a stretch of it with two ends. The ends need care. -->

## Predict

**A bolt hole must be 20 mm, give or take 0.05 mm. A hole is measured at 20.04 mm. Does it pass?**

- Yes: it's inside the range
- No: it's more than 20 mm
- No: it isn't 20 mm
- You can't tell without a bigger ruler

## Both limits

Here's turning a tolerance into a range on the line.

Now try one on paper: a shaft of 12 mm ± 0.02 mm. Write the range, then type the upper limit.

$$20 \pm 0.05$$

1) $20 - 0.05 = 19.95$ — The lower limit.
2) $20 + 0.05 = 20.05$ — The upper limit.
3) $19.95 \le x \le 20.05$ — Both ends count: closed circles.
4) $20.04 \text{ is between } 19.95 \text{ and } 20.05$ — So it passes.

## Both limits

**Which inequality describes every hole that passes, from 19.95 mm to 20.05 mm, including both limits?**

- $19.95 &lt; x &lt; 20.05$
- $19.95 \le x \le 20.05$
- $x \le 20.05$
- $x = 20$

* Answer: $19.95 \le x \le 20.05$

<!-- Speaker note: ≤ keeps the end points: a closed circle at each limit. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Find the mistake

**Ben writes the passing range. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A hole passes if it is from 19.95 mm up to 20.05 mm, including both limits.
 2  So 19.95 < x < 20.05.
 3  A hole of exactly 20.05 mm passes.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: < leaves the ends out, but a hole of exactly 19.95 or 20.05 passes. It should be 19.95 ≤ x ≤ 20.05. -->

## Which signs

**Which means 'at least 5, but less than 9'?**

- $5 &lt; x &lt; 9$
- $5 \le x &lt; 9$
- $5 \le x \le 9$
- $5 &lt; x \le 9$

* Answer: $5 \le x &lt; 9$

<!-- Speaker note: At least 5 includes 5 (≤). Less than 9 leaves out 9 (<). -->

## How many pass
