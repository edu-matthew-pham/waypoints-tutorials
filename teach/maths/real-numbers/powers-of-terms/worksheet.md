## A factor with a letter in it


:::keep-together

### A scale factor of 3k

A power of a bracket applies to everything inside it. Here's the routine for a factor of $2k$.

1. $(2k)^3$ — The weight factor: cube the whole bracket.
2. $= 2^3 \times k^3$ — The 2 is cubed as well as the k.
3. $= 8k^3$ — 2³ = 8.
4. $(2k)^2 = 4k^2$ — The leg factor, the same way.
5. $8k^3 \div 4k^2$ — Weight factor divided by leg factor.
6. $= (8 \div 4) \times k^{3-2}$ — Numbers first, then subtract the indices.
7. $= 2k$ — k¹ is just k.

Now try one on paper: a factor of $3k$, so work out $(3k)^3 \div (3k)^2$ the same way. Then type your answer.

**The scale factor is $3k$. Weight goes up by $(3k)^3$ and leg strength by $(3k)^2$. Simplify $(3k)^3 \div (3k)^2$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3k

(3k)³ = 27k³ and (3k)² = 9k². 27 ÷ 9 = 3 and k³ ÷ k² = k, so 3k.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Dev simplifies $6a^3 \div 3a^5$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 6a³ ÷ 3a⁵ and write the answer with a positive index.
 2  Numbers first: 6 ÷ 3 = 2.
 3  Letters: a³ ÷ a⁵ = a³⁻⁵ = a⁻².
 4  So the answer is −2a².
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: a⁻² is a reciprocal, not a negative number. 2a⁻² = 2/a², which is positive.

:::

### A power of a product

**Which is the same as $(3a^2b)^2$?**

☐ $9a^4b$

☐ $3a^4b^2$

☐ $9a^4b^2$

☐ $6a^4b^2$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $9a^4b^2$

Square each part: 3² = 9, (a²)² = a⁴ and b² = b². So 9a⁴b².

## Terms and powers


:::keep-together

### Multiply

**Simplify $6x^4y^2 \times 2x^3y$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12x^7y^3

6 × 2 = 12, x⁴ × x³ = x⁷ and y² × y = y³.

:::


:::keep-together

### A power of a product

**Simplify $(2a^3b)^4$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 16a^12b^4

2⁴ = 16, (a³)⁴ = a¹² and b⁴ = b⁴.

:::


:::keep-together

### Divide and flip

**Simplify $5a^2 \div a^5$ and write the answer with a positive index.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5/a^3

a² ÷ a⁵ = a⁻³ = 1/a³, so 5a² ÷ a⁵ = 5/a³.

:::


:::keep-together

Answer: {{field-15}}

**Answer:** 1/16

2⁻⁴ = 1/2⁴ = 1/16.
