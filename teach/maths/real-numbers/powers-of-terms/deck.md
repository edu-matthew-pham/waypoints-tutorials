## A scale factor of 3k

A power of a bracket applies to everything inside it. Here's the routine for a factor of $2k$.

Now try one on paper: a factor of $3k$, so work out $(3k)^3 \div (3k)^2$ the same way. Then type your answer.

$$(2k)^3$$

1) $= 2^3 \times k^3$ — The 2 is cubed as well as the k.
2) $= 8k^3$ — 2³ = 8.
3) $(2k)^2 = 4k^2$ — The leg factor, the same way.
4) $8k^3 \div 4k^2$ — Weight factor divided by leg factor.
5) $= (8 \div 4) \times k^{3-2}$ — Numbers first, then subtract the indices.
6) $= 2k$ — k¹ is just k.

## Find the mistake

**Dev simplifies $6a^3 \div 3a^5$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 6a³ ÷ 3a⁵ and write the answer with a positive index.
 2  Numbers first: 6 ÷ 3 = 2.
 3  Letters: a³ ÷ a⁵ = a³⁻⁵ = a⁻².
 4  So the answer is −2a².
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: a⁻² is a reciprocal, not a negative number. 2a⁻² = 2/a², which is positive. -->
