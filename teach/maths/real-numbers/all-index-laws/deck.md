## Add the indices

Here's the routine with negatives in it.

Now try one on paper: $5^{-3} \times 5^7 \div 5^2$. Then type the value.

$$(2^{-2})^3 \times 2^9 \div 2^{-1}$$

1) $= 2^{-6} \times 2^9 \div 2^{-1}$ — −2 × 3 = −6.
2) $= 2^{3} \div 2^{-1}$ — Multiply: −6 + 9 = 3.
3) $= 2^{3 - (-1)}$ — Divide: subtract the index.
4) $= 2^4 = 16$ — 3 − (−1) = 4.

## Find the mistake

**Jo simplifies $5^6 \div 5^{-2}$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 5⁶ ÷ 5⁻².
 2  Same base, so subtract the indices.
 3  6 − 2 = 4.
 4  So 5⁶ ÷ 5⁻² = 5⁴.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: 6 − (−2) = 6 + 2 = 8. Subtracting a negative adds, so it's 5⁸. -->
