## All the laws


:::keep-together

### Add the indices

Here's the routine with negatives in it.

1. $(2^{-2})^3 \times 2^9 \div 2^{-1}$ — Powers of powers first.
2. $= 2^{-6} \times 2^9 \div 2^{-1}$ — −2 × 3 = −6.
3. $= 2^{3} \div 2^{-1}$ — Multiply: −6 + 9 = 3.
4. $= 2^{3 - (-1)}$ — Divide: subtract the index.
5. $= 2^4 = 16$ — 3 − (−1) = 4.

Now try one on paper: $5^{-3} \times 5^7 \div 5^2$. Then type the value.

**Simplify $2^{-3} \times 2^{5}$. It's 2 to what power?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

Multiplying adds the indices: −3 + 5 = 2, so 2².

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Jo simplifies $5^6 \div 5^{-2}$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 5⁶ ÷ 5⁻².
 2  Same base, so subtract the indices.
 3  6 − 2 = 4.
 4  So 5⁶ ÷ 5⁻² = 5⁴.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: 6 − (−2) = 6 + 2 = 8. Subtracting a negative adds, so it's 5⁸.

:::


:::keep-together

## Mixed laws


:::keep-together

### Multiply

**Simplify $3^{-4} \times 3^{6}$. It's 3 to what power?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

−4 + 6 = 2.

:::


:::keep-together

### Divide

**Simplify $7^{2} \div 7^{-3}$. It's 7 to what power?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5

2 − (−3) = 5.

:::


:::keep-together

### Power of a power

**Simplify $(5^{-2})^{3}$. It's 5 to what power?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** -6

−2 × 3 = −6.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: work out $10^3 \times 10^{-5}$ as a decimal.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.01

10³ × 10⁻⁵ = 10⁻² = 0.01.
