## What happened?

* 10⁻⁶. A negative power is not a negative number. It's a reciprocal: 10⁻⁶ = 1/10⁶ = 0.000001, a millionth. 10⁶ is a million; −10⁶ is minus a million.

## Reciprocal

Here's the pattern that makes negative powers make sense.

Now try one on paper: $10^{-4}$ as a fraction and as a decimal. Then type the decimal.

$$10^3 = 1000$$

1) $10^2 = 100$ — Divide by 10 each time.
2) $10^1 = 10$ — Still dividing by 10.
3) $10^0 = 1$ — The pattern says one.
4) $10^{-1} = 1 \div 10 = \tfrac{1}{10}$ — Keep going.
5) $10^{-2} = \tfrac{1}{100}$ — A hundredth.

## Which is the same?

**Which is the same as $5^{-1}$?**

- $-\tfrac{1}{5}$
- $5^{-1}$ can't be worked out
- $\tfrac{1}{5}$
- $-5$

* Answer: $\tfrac{1}{5}$
