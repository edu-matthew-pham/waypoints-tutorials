## Why it matters

> Make an animal 10 times longer, and how much heavier does it get? Letters and index laws can tell you.

## Add the indices

Here's the routine with numbers and two letters.

Now try one on paper: $5a^3b^2 \times 2ab^4$. Then type your answer.

$$3x^2y \times 4xy^3 \div 6x^2y$$

1) $= 12x^3y^4 \div 6x^2y$ — Multiply: 3 × 4 = 12, x² × x = x³, y × y³ = y⁴.
2) $= 2 x^{3-2} y^{4-1}$ — Divide: 12 ÷ 6 = 2, subtract the indices.
3) $= 2xy^3$ — x¹ is just x.

## Find the mistake

**Kim simplifies $3a^2 \times 4a^3$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 3a² × 4a³.
 2  Multiply the numbers: 3 × 4 = 12.
 3  Multiply the powers of a: 2 × 3 = 6.
 4  So 3a² × 4a³ = 12a⁶.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: multiplying powers adds the indices: 2 + 3 = 5. So 3a² × 4a³ = 12a⁵. -->
