# Index laws with letters

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Make an animal 10 times longer, and how much heavier does it get? Letters and index laws can tell you.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students apply the index laws to letters: coefficients multiply, indices add, subtract or multiply, and a scaled-up lizard shows weight and leg strength growing at different rates (AC9M9A01, node 3).

**Students should be able to say:** I can simplify products and quotients of powers of letters. I can deal with the number in front separately from the letters. I can explain why volume grows faster than area.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

:::teacher-note

**What happens:** 1000 times. Weight follows volume: length × width × height. 10 × 10 × 10 = 10³ = 1000. Its leg bones only get 10 × 10 = 100 times the cross-section, so each part of a leg carries 10 times as much as before.

## Numbers and letters


:::keep-together

### Add the indices

Here's the routine with numbers and two letters.

1. $3x^2y \times 4xy^3 \div 6x^2y$ — Numbers, then each letter.
2. $= 12x^3y^4 \div 6x^2y$ — Multiply: 3 × 4 = 12, x² × x = x³, y × y³ = y⁴.
3. $= 2 x^{3-2} y^{4-1}$ — Divide: 12 ÷ 6 = 2, subtract the indices.
4. $= 2xy^3$ — x¹ is just x.

Now try one on paper: $5a^3b^2 \times 2ab^4$. Then type your answer.

**Simplify $a^3 \times a^5$. Type $a^8$ as a^8.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** a^8

a³ × a⁵: three a's and five a's make eight: a⁸.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Kim simplifies $3a^2 \times 4a^3$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Simplify 3a² × 4a³.
 2  Multiply the numbers: 3 × 4 = 12.
 3  Multiply the powers of a: 2 × 3 = 6.
 4  So 3a² × 4a³ = 12a⁶.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: multiplying powers adds the indices: 2 + 3 = 5. So 3a² × 4a³ = 12a⁵.

:::


:::keep-together

## Mixed laws with letters


:::keep-together

### Multiply

**Simplify $6x^4 \times 2x^3$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12x^7

6 × 2 = 12 and x⁴ × x³ = x⁷.

:::


:::keep-together

### Divide

**Simplify $10a^7b^2 \div 5a^3b$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2a^4b

10 ÷ 5 = 2, a⁷ ÷ a³ = a⁴, b² ÷ b = b.

:::


:::keep-together

### A power of a product

**Simplify $(3a^2)^3$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 27a^6

3³ = 27 and (a²)³ = a⁶.

:::

4⁻² = 1/4² = 1/16.
