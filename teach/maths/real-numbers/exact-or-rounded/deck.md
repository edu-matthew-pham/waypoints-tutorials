## Why it matters

> A calculator shows √50 as 7.0710678… and a builder cuts to 7.1 m. How wrong is that?

## Exact first

Here's the difference rounding early makes.

Now try one on paper: use $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.4$ to work out $(\sqrt{2})^4$ by multiplying four 1.4s. Then type the value you get.

$$\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}$$

1) $1.4 \times 1.4 \times 1.4 \times 1.4$ — Rounded to 1 decimal place first.
2) $= 3.8416$ — Not 4.
3) $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.41421356 \text{, so } 1.41421356^4 \approx 4.0000$ — Keep more digits and it's closer.

## Find the mistake

**Lee works out the cable along 2 diagonals of a 1 m square. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A 1 m square has a diagonal of √2 m. Find the length of a cable that goes along 2 diagonals.
 2  √2 is about 1.4.
 3  2 diagonals: 2 × 1.4 = 2.8.
 4  So the cable is exactly 2.8 m.
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: 2 × 1.4 = 2.8 is an approximation, because √2 was rounded. The exact length is 2√2 m, which is about 2.83 m. -->
