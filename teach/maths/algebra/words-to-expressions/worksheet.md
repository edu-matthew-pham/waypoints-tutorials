# Words into expressions

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** "Twice as many", "5 less than", "shared between 4": every maths sentence about a number can be written with a letter.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students turn words such as more than, less than, twice and divided by into expressions, writing the operations in the order the words say them and checking with a number (AC9M7A02, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what operation each word in a sentence about a number means. I can write an expression from words, with the operations in the right order. I can check my expression by trying a number I can work out in words.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## From words to an expression


:::keep-together

### Three more than twice n

Here is how to turn words into an expression, one step at a time.

1. $\text{3 more than twice } n$ — Break the words into the parts they name.
2. $\text{twice } n$ — Start with the part said first: twice a number is $2 \times n$.
3. $2n$ — A number next to a letter means multiply, so write $2n$.
4. $\text{3 more than } 2n$ — "More than" means add 3 to that.
5. $2n + 3$ — The expression.
6. $n = 4: \; 2 \times 4 + 3 = 11$ — Check with a number: twice 4 is 8, and 3 more is 11.

**Write an expression for "3 more than $x$".**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x + 3

x + 3. Check with x = 10: 3 more than 10 is 13, and 10 + 3 = 13.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**A number $n$ is divided by 4. Which expression is that?**

☐ $\frac{n}{4}$

☐ $\frac{4}{n}$

☐ $4n$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $\frac{n}{4}$

n ÷ 4 puts n on top: n/4. Check with n = 20: 20 ÷ 4 = 5. 4/n would be 4 ÷ 20.

:::


:::keep-together

## Say it, then write it


:::keep-together

### Older

**Ben is $n$ years old. His sister is 6 years older. Write an expression for his sister's age.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** n + 6

n + 6. If Ben is 10, his sister is 16.

:::


:::keep-together

### Eggs

**Eggs come in boxes of 12. Write an expression for the number of eggs in $b$ boxes.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12b

12b: 12 eggs in each of b boxes. Check with b = 2: 24 eggs.

:::


:::keep-together

### Pizza

**A pizza has 8 slices. $n$ slices are eaten. Write an expression for the slices left.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8 - n

8 − n: you start with 8 and take n away. Here 8 comes first, unlike "8 less than n".

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: find $3n + 5$ when $n = 4$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 17

3 × 4 + 5 = 12 + 5 = 17.
