> **Key question:** Three friends each buy a drink and a $4 snack. Three drinks and three snacks: the 3 goes to both. That is all expanding a bracket is.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students build k(ax + b) as rows of algebra tiles, find that the number outside multiplies every term inside (signs included), expand, then collect like terms, writing their working one step per line (AC9M8A01, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can expand a bracket by multiplying every term inside by the number outside. I can expand when there are negative numbers inside or outside the bracket. I can expand, then collect like terms, and show each step.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

## Multiply every term


:::keep-together

### Expand, then collect

Here is how to expand a bracket and then collect like terms.

1. $3(2x + 4) - x$ — Expand the bracket first.
2. $3 \times 2x = 6x$ — The 3 multiplies the first term.
3. $3 \times 4 = 12$ — And the second term: every term in the bracket.
4. $6x + 12 - x$ — The bracket is gone. The $-x$ was outside it, so it stays as it is.
5. $5x + 12$ — Collect like terms: $6x - x = 5x$. The 12 has no like term.

Try one: expand $2(5x - 3)$.

**Expand $2(5x - 3)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 10x - 6

2 × 5x = 10x and 2 × (−3) = −6, so 10x − 6.

:::


:::keep-together

```
 1  Expand 4(x + 3).
 2  The 4 multiplies the x: 4 × x = 4x.
 3  The 3 stays as it is.
 4  So 4(x + 3) = 4x + 3.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the 4 multiplies every term in the bracket, so the 3 becomes 4 × 3 = 12. 4(x + 3) = 4x + 12.

### Negative outside

**Expand $-3(2x - 4)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** -6x + 12

−3 × 2x = −6x and −3 × (−4) = +12.

**Expand and simplify $4(2x - 3) + 5$. Write one step per line.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4(2x - 3) + 5 → 8x - 12 + 5 → 8x - 7

8x − 12 + 5 = 8x − 7.
