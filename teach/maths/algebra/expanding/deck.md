# Expanding brackets

Algebra Foundations · Brackets

## Today

**We are learning to** the number outside a bracket multiplies every term inside it. 3(2x + 4) is 3 rows of 2x + 4, so 6x + 12. Then collect any like terms.

- I can expand a bracket by multiplying every term inside by the number outside.
- I can expand when there are negative numbers inside or outside the bracket.
- I can expand, then collect like terms, and show each step.

## Expand, then collect

Here is how to expand a bracket and then collect like terms.

Try one: expand $2(5x - 3)$.

$$3(2x + 4) - x$$

1) $3 \times 2x = 6x$ — The 3 multiplies the first term.
2) $3 \times 4 = 12$ — And the second term: every term in the bracket.
3) $6x + 12 - x$ — The bracket is gone. The $-x$ was outside it, so it stays as it is.
4) $5x + 12$ — Collect like terms: $6x - x = 5x$. The 12 has no like term.
