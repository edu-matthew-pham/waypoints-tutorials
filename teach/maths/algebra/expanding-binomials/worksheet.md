# Two brackets

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Make a garden bed longer and wider at the same time, and the new area comes in pieces. How many pieces, and how big?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students build (x + p)(x + q) as a rectangle on the board, see four pieces (x², two strips, a corner), expand every sign combination, and come back to a garden bed made bigger on two sides (AC9M9A02, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can expand two brackets by finding all four pieces. I can expand two brackets with negative numbers in them. I can use an expansion to find an area.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Four pieces


:::keep-together

### Four pieces

Here's the garden bed worked out, piece by piece.

1. $(x + 4)(x + 3)$ — The rectangle: x + 4 across, x + 3 down.
2. $x \times x = x^2$ — The big square.
3. $x \times 3 = 3x$ — One strip.
4. $4 \times x = 4x$ — The other strip.
5. $4 \times 3 = 12$ — The corner: the old bed.
6. $x^2 + 3x + 4x + 12 = x^2 + 7x + 12$ — Collect the two strips.

Now try one on paper, piece by piece like the example: $(x + 5)(x + 2)$. Then type your answer.

**Expand $(x + 5)(x + 2)$. Type $x^2$ as x^2.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x^2 + 7x + 10

x × x = x², x × 2 = 2x, 5 × x = 5x, 5 × 2 = 10. Collect: x² + 7x + 10.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Ali expands $(x + 3)(x + 2)$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Expand (x + 3)(x + 2).
 2  x times x is x².
 3  3 times 2 is 6.
 4  So (x + 3)(x + 2) = x² + 6.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: Ali left out the two strips, 2x and 3x. Every term in one bracket multiplies every term in the other: x² + 2x + 3x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6.

:::


:::keep-together

**Expand $(x + 4)(x - 3)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

x² − 3x + 4x − 12 = x² + x − 12.
