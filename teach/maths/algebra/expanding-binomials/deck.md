## Four pieces

Here's the garden bed worked out, piece by piece.

Now try one on paper, piece by piece like the example: $(x + 5)(x + 2)$. Then type your answer.

$$(x + 4)(x + 3)$$

1) $x \times x = x^2$ — The big square.
2) $x \times 3 = 3x$ — One strip.
3) $4 \times x = 4x$ — The other strip.
4) $4 \times 3 = 12$ — The corner: the old bed.
5) $x^2 + 3x + 4x + 12 = x^2 + 7x + 12$ — Collect the two strips.
