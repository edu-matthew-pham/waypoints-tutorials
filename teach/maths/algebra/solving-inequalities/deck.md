## Find the mistake

**Lena solves $40 - 4.5r \ge 12$, the money left after the rides. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Solve 40 − 4.5r ≥ 12.
 2  Take 40 from both sides: −4.5r ≥ −28.
 3  Divide both sides by −4.5: r ≥ 6.2.
 4  Check r = 0: 40 − 0 = 40 ≥ 12 is true, but 0 ≥ 6.2 is false.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: dividing by −4.5 reverses the sign, so r ≤ 6.2. Her check on line 4 is the clue: r = 0 is allowed, and 0 ≥ 6.2 is false. -->

## Read the answer

**$12 + 4.5r \le 40$ gives $r \le 6.2$. How many rides can you go on?**

- 6
- 6.2
- 7

* Answer: 6
