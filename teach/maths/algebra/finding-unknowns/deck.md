# Finding an unknown

Algebra Foundations · Expressions

<!-- Speaker note: Students substitute the known values into a formula, solve the equation that is left for the unknown, and check by substituting back (AC9M7A01, node 3). About 45 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 15 min · Modify 12 min · Practise and Make 10 min). -->

## Today

**We are learning to** when a formula's answer is known but one letter is not, substitute what you know. That leaves an equation; solve it by undoing, then check.

- I can substitute the values I know into a formula to make an equation.
- I can solve the equation by undoing each operation, last first.
- I can check my answer by substituting it back into the formula.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: Do the Run and Modify boards yourself first. ## Why it matters

> You know how much fence you have and how long the garden bed must be. How wide can it be? A formula answers that too, worked backwards.

## Area

Here is how to find an unknown in a formula.

$$P = 2l + 2w$$

1) $P = 20, \; l = 6$ — Write what you know. The unknown is $w$.
2) $20 = 2 \times 6 + 2w$ — Substitute. Now it is an equation with one unknown.
3) $20 = 12 + 2w$ — Work out what you can.
4) $8 = 2w$ — Undo the + 12: take 12 from both sides.
5) $w = 4$ — Undo the × 2: divide both sides by 2.
6) $2 \times 6 + 2 \times 4 = 20 \;\checkmark$ — Check by substituting back into the formula.

**Mia finds the width of a rectangle. Click the line where she goes wrong.**

```
 1  P = 2l + 2w, with P = 20 and l = 6.

 3  20 = 12 + 2w

 5  w = 16
```

* Answer: line 5

<!-- Speaker note: Line 5: 2w = 8 means 2 × w = 8. To undo × 2, divide by 2: w = 4. Mia multiplied again. Check: 2 × 6 + 2 × 16 = 44, not 20. -->
