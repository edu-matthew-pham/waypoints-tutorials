> **Key question:** Recipes, phone bills and the perimeter of a garden bed all follow a formula. The letters are what changes; the numbers are what stays.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students tell the variables in a formula from the constants, and see that in an equation a letter can be one unknown number (AC9M7A01, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can say which letters in a formula are variables and which numbers are constants. I can say what a constant means in a real formula, such as a call-out fee. I can tell a variable (can take many values) from an unknown (one value makes the equation true).

## Variables, constants and unknowns


:::keep-together

### Reading a formula

Here is how to read the parts of a formula.

1. $P = 2l + 2w$ — The perimeter of a rectangle: twice the length plus twice the width.
2. $P, \; l, \; w$ — These letters are **variables**: quantities that change from one rectangle to the next.
3. $2$ — The 2s are **constants**: they are the same for every rectangle.
4. $l = 5, \; w = 3$ — For one particular rectangle, each variable has a value.
5. $3x + 2 = 14$ — In an equation, a letter can be an **unknown**: one particular number.
6. $x = 4$ — Only one value makes this equation true.

**A plumber charges $C = 5h + 20$ dollars for a job of $h$ hours. Which are the variables?**

☐ 5 and 20

☐ $C$ and $h$

☐ Only $h$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $C$ and $h$

C and h change from job to job. 5 (dollars an hour) and 20 (the call-out fee) are constants: the same for every job.

:::


:::keep-together

### Unknown or variable?

**In the equation $3x + 2 = 14$, what does $x$ stand for?**

☐ An unknown: one number makes it true

☐ The letter x, not a number

☐ A variable that can be any number

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** An unknown: one number makes it true

In an equation, the letter is an unknown: only x = 4 makes 3x + 2 = 14 true. In a formula, a letter is a variable that can take many values.

## Read the formula


:::keep-together

### What the constant means

**Water in a tank: $V = 3t + 10$ litres after $t$ minutes. What does the 10 mean?**

☐ The tank fills in 10 minutes

☐ 10 litres in the tank at the start

☐ 10 litres added every minute

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 10 litres in the tank at the start

When t = 0, V = 10: the 10 litres were there at the start. 3 litres are added each minute.

:::


:::keep-together

### Variables

**Which letters are variables in $T = 12n + 6$ (the cost of $n$ concert tickets)?**

☐ $T$ and $n$

☐ 12 and 6

☐ Only $T$

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $T$ and $n$

T and n change with the order. 12 (dollars a ticket) and 6 (the booking fee) stay the same.

:::


:::keep-together

### Unknown

**In $2y + 1 = 9$, what is $y$?**

☐ An unknown: $y = 4$ makes it true

☐ A variable: any value works

☐ A constant

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** An unknown: $y = 4$ makes it true

It is an equation, so y is an unknown: 2 × 4 + 1 = 9, and no other value works.

:::
