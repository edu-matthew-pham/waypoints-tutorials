# The pattern

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Someone says 47 × 53 is 2491 before you can find your phone. There's a pattern in two brackets that does it.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students see that (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab from the rectangle, use it without drawing, and come back to 47 × 53 as (50 − 3)(50 + 3) (AC9M9A02, node 2).

**Students should be able to say:** I can expand two brackets by adding the numbers and multiplying them. I can spot when the middle term disappears. I can use the pattern to multiply in my head.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Add and multiply


:::keep-together

### Add and multiply

Here's the pattern, worked out.

1. $(x + 5)(x + 2)$ — Numbers: 5 and 2.
2. $5 + 2 = 7$ — The middle: the two strips together.
3. $5 \times 2 = 10$ — The end: the corner.
4. $x^2 + 7x + 10$ — Put it together.

Now try one on paper: $(x + 3)(x + 8)$. Then type your answer.

**Use the pattern: expand $(x + 6)(x + 4)$. Type $x^2$ as x^2.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x^2 + 10x + 24

6 + 4 = 10 in the middle and 6 × 4 = 24 at the end: x² + 10x + 24.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Sam uses the pattern on $(x + 3)(x + 5)$. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Expand (x + 3)(x + 5).
 2  The two numbers are 3 and 5.
 3  The middle number is 3 times 5, which is 15.
 4  The last number is 3 plus 5, which is 8.
 5  So (x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 15x + 8.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the middle number is the sum, 3 + 5 = 8, and the last is the product, 3 × 5 = 15: x² + 8x + 15.

:::


:::keep-together

### With a negative

**Use the pattern: expand $(x + 7)(x - 2)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x^2 + 5x - 14

7 + (−2) = 5 in the middle, and 7 × (−2) = −14 at the end: x² + 5x − 14.

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::

**Answer:** x^2 + 11x + 18

9 + 2 = 11 and 9 × 2 = 18: x² + 11x + 18.

### One negative

**Expand $(x - 8)(x + 3)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x^2 - 5x - 24

−8 + 3 = −5 and −8 × 3 = −24.

:::

### Two negative

**Expand $(x - 7)(x - 5)$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** x^2 - 12x + 35

−7 + (−5) = −12 and (−7) × (−5) = 35.
