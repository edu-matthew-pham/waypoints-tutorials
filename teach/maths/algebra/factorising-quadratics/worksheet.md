# Factorising quadratics

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** You know the area of a garden bed and one side. How do you find the other side from x² + 7x + 12?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students run the pattern backwards: pack x² + bx + c into a rectangle by finding two numbers that add to b and multiply to c, and come back to a garden bed's missing side (AC9M9A02, node 3).

**Students should be able to say:** I can factorise x² + bx + c by finding two numbers. I can use the signs of b and c to find the signs of the numbers. I can check a factorisation by expanding.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Add and multiply


:::keep-together

### Two numbers

Here's the method, worked out.

1. $x^2 + 7x + 12$ — Two numbers: add to 7, multiply to 12.
2. $1 \times 12 = 12, \; 1 + 12 = 13$ — Too big.
3. $2 \times 6 = 12, \; 2 + 6 = 8$ — Close.
4. $3 \times 4 = 12, \; 3 + 4 = 7$ — That's it.
5. $(x + 3)(x + 4)$ — The brackets.

Now try one on paper: $x^2 + 9x + 20$. Then type your answer.

**Factorise $x^2 + 8x + 15$. Type $x^2$ as x^2.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** (x + 3)(x + 5)

Pairs that multiply to 15: 1 and 15 (sum 16), 3 and 5 (sum 8). So (x + 3)(x + 5).

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Mia factorises $x^2 - x - 6$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Factorise x² − x − 6.
 2  Two numbers that multiply to 6: 2 and 3.
 3  So x² − x − 6 = (x − 2)(x + 3).
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: −2 + 3 = 1, but the middle is −1. The numbers multiply to −6 and add to −1: 2 and −3. So (x + 2)(x − 3).

:::


:::keep-together

### Check by expanding

**How can you check that $(x + 2)(x - 3)$ is the factorisation of $x^2 - x - 6$?**

☐ Check that it has two brackets

☐ Expand it and compare

☐ Check that 2 and 3 multiply to 6

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Expand it and compare

Expanding is the check: x² − 3x + 2x − 6 = x² − x − 6.

Answer: {{field-15}}

**Answer:** (x + 2)(x + 7)

### One negative

**Factorise $x^2 - 3x - 10$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** (x - 5)(x + 2)

−5 and 2 multiply to −10 and add to −3.

**Factorise $x^2 - 11x + 24$.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** (x - 3)(x - 8)

−3 and −8 multiply to 24 and add to −11.
