## Two numbers

Here's the method, worked out.

Now try one on paper: $x^2 + 9x + 20$. Then type your answer.

$$x^2 + 7x + 12$$

1) $1 \times 12 = 12, \; 1 + 12 = 13$ — Too big.
2) $2 \times 6 = 12, \; 2 + 6 = 8$ — Close.
3) $3 \times 4 = 12, \; 3 + 4 = 7$ — That's it.
4) $(x + 3)(x + 4)$ — The brackets.

## Find the mistake

**Mia factorises $x^2 - x - 6$. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Factorise x² − x − 6.
 2  Two numbers that multiply to 6: 2 and 3.
 3  So x² − x − 6 = (x − 2)(x + 3).
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: −2 + 3 = 1, but the middle is −1. The numbers multiply to −6 and add to −1: 2 and −3. So (x + 2)(x − 3). -->

## Check by expanding

**How can you check that $(x + 2)(x - 3)$ is the factorisation of $x^2 - x - 6$?**

- Check that it has two brackets
- Expand it and compare
- Check that 2 and 3 multiply to 6

* Answer: Expand it and compare

<!-- Speaker note: Expanding is the check: x² − 3x + 2x − 6 = x² − x − 6. -->
