# Multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1000

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Move the decimal point or add a zero? One of these rules is wrong.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students see multiplying and dividing by powers of ten as every digit moving places, and drop the "add a zero" rule.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what happens to each digit when I multiply by 10. I can multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1000. I can say why "add a zero" does not work for decimals.

## The idea

Each place is worth 10 times the place on its right. **× 10** moves every digit one place to the left. **÷ 10** moves every digit one place to the right. For 100 move 2 places and for 1000 move 3 places.

:::keep-together

### The zero rule

**Why is "add a zero" a bad rule for 3.5 × 10?**

☐ The rule works for decimals but not whole numbers

**Answer:** The answer is 35, not 3.50: the digits move, nothing is added
