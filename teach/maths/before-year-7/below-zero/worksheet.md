# Below zero

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Which is colder, -8 degrees or -2 degrees? The 8 looks bigger.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students place negative numbers on a number line and see that further left is less, and drop the idea that -8 is bigger than -2.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what a negative number is. I can say which of two negative numbers is greater. I can say what happens when a temperature rises or falls.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea

On a number line, **negative numbers** are to the left of zero. The further left, the smaller the number, so $-8 < -2$. Going up the line is adding, and going down is taking away.

:::keep-together

### Greater or less

**Which is greater, -3 or -7?**

☐ -3

☐ They are the same

☐ -7

☐ You cannot tell

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** -3

-3 is further right on the number line, so it is greater.

:::


:::keep-together

### Warming up

**The temperature is -4 °C. It rises by 6 degrees. What is the new temperature?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

From -4 go up 4 to reach 0, then 2 more to reach 2.

:::


:::keep-together

### Above or below

**Is it above zero or below zero?**

Groups: Above zero, Below zero

- 5 °C: {{field-15}}
- -5 °C: {{field-15}}
- A lift going down to -2: {{field-15}}
- 3 floors up: {{field-15}}
- Owing $10: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5 °C → Above zero; -5 °C → Below zero; A lift going down to -2 → Below zero; 3 floors up → Above zero; Owing $10 → Below zero

:::

### Falling

**The temperature is -5 °C and falls by 3 degrees. What is it now?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** -8

Falling moves left: -5 and then 3 more below is -8.

:::
