# Below zero

Before Year 7 maths · Number tools

<!-- Speaker note: Students place negative numbers on a number line and see that further left is less, and drop the idea that -8 is bigger than -2. About 17 minutes (Think first 3 min · Try 4 min · The idea 5 min · Check 3 min · Apply 2 min). -->

---

## Today

- I can say what a negative number is.
- I can say which of two negative numbers is greater.
- I can say what happens when a temperature rises or falls.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: The one idea: on the line, left is less. The common mistake is that -8 is bigger than -2 because 8 is bigger than 2. The Think first question meets it. The board is the number line from the Year 7 lessons *Comparing integers* and *Adding integers*. Here it is set up as a thermometer. A real thermometer or a lift with basement floors makes it concrete. Not yet tried with a class. Teacher: Think first: ask which is colder, and why. Teacher: Try: ask students to say what happens to the number as P moves left. Teacher: Apply: look for "further left" or "further below zero". -->

---

## Why it matters

> Which is colder, -8 degrees or -2 degrees? The 8 looks bigger.

<!-- Speaker note: Numbers do not stop at zero. Below zero we use negative numbers, like $-3$ degrees on a cold morning. Whole numbers above and below zero are called **integers**. -->

## Think first

**Which is colder, -8 °C or -2 °C?**

- They are the same
- -2 °C
- -8 °C
- You cannot tell

## What happened?

* -8 °C. It is 8 degrees below zero, which is further below than 2 degrees below. The further left on the line, the smaller.

## Greater or less

**Which is greater, -3 or -7?**

- -3
- They are the same
- -7
- You cannot tell

* Answer: -3

<!-- Speaker note: -3 is further right on the number line, so it is greater. -->

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Warming up

**The temperature is -4 °C. It rises by 6 degrees. What is the new temperature?**

* Answer: 2

<!-- Speaker note: From -4 go up 4 to reach 0, then 2 more to reach 2. -->

---

## Above or below

**Is it above zero or below zero?**

Groups: Above zero · Below zero

5 °C · -5 °C · A lift going down to -2 · 3 floors up · Owing $10

* Answer: 5 °C → Above zero; -5 °C → Below zero; A lift going down to -2 → Below zero; 3 floors up → Above zero; Owing $10 → Below zero

## In order

**Which list goes from smallest to biggest?**

- -4, -9, 0, 3
- 3, 0, -4, -9
- 0, -4, -9, 3
- -9, -4, 0, 3

* Answer: -9, -4, 0, 3

<!-- Speaker note: Read the line from left to right: -9, -4, 0, 3. -->

## Falling

**The temperature is -5 °C and falls by 3 degrees. What is it now?**

* Answer: -8

<!-- Speaker note: Falling moves left: -5 and then 3 more below is -8. -->

## Exit ticket

**Which is less, -6 or -9, and how do you know?**

* -9, because it is further left.
