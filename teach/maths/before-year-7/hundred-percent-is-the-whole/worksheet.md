# 100% is the whole

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A phone is at 100% charge. A tank is 50% full. What do the numbers mean?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students see 100% as the whole and use 50% and 25% as a half and a quarter, and drop the idea that a percentage is the amount itself.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say what percent means. I can say 100% is the whole amount. I can find 50% and 25% of an amount.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea

**Percent** means "out of 100". 100% is the whole, 50% is half and 25% is a quarter. To find 50% of an amount, halve it. For 25%, halve it twice.

:::keep-together

### The whole

**What is 100% of $20?**

☐ $120

☐ $20

☐ $2

☐ $100

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $20

100% of an amount is the whole amount.

:::


:::keep-together

### Half

**What is 50% of 80?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 40

50% is half, and half of 80 is 40.

:::

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 1/4

25 out of 100 is one quarter.
