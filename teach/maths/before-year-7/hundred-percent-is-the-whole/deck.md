# 100% is the whole

Before Year 7 maths · Parts of a whole

<!-- Speaker note: Students see 100% as the whole and use 50% and 25% as a half and a quarter, and drop the idea that a percentage is the amount itself. About 17 minutes (Think first 3 min · Try 4 min · The idea 5 min · Check 3 min · Apply 2 min). -->

---

## Today

- I can say what percent means.
- I can say 100% is the whole amount.
- I can find 50% and 25% of an amount.

<!-- Speaker note: Before class: The one idea: percent means out of 100, so 100% is the whole. The common mistake is that 50% of 60 is 50. The Think first question meets it. The board is the percentage bar from the Year 7 lesson *Percentages*. Only 50%, 25% and 100% matter here. A hundred grid or a strip folded into quarters helps. Not yet tried with a class. Teacher: Think first: ask what 100% of their class is. Teacher: Try: ask students to say which percent makes half the bar. Teacher: Apply: look for "out of 100". -->

---

## Why it matters

> A phone is at 100% charge. A tank is 50% full. What do the numbers mean?

<!-- Speaker note: The word **percent** means "out of 100". So 100% is all of something, 50% is half of it (50 out of 100) and 25% is a quarter of it. -->

## The whole

**What is 100% of $20?**

- $120
- $20
- $2
- $100

* Answer: $20

<!-- Speaker note: 100% of an amount is the whole amount. -->

---

## Half

**What is 50% of 80?**

* Answer: 40

<!-- Speaker note: 50% is half, and half of 80 is 40. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## A quarter

**Which fraction is the same as 25%?**

- 1/25
- 1/4
- 2/5
- 1/2

* Answer: 1/4

<!-- Speaker note: 25 out of 100 is one quarter. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## A quarter

**What is 25% of 40?**

* Answer: 10

<!-- Speaker note: 25% is a quarter. 40 ÷ 4 = 10. -->

---

## Three quarters

**What is 75% of 8?**

* Answer: 6
