# Angles on a line and at a point

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Two angles sit side by side on a straight line. One is 70 degrees. How big is the other?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students use 180 for a straight line and 360 round a point to find missing angles.

**Students should be able to say:** I can say how many degrees are on a straight line. I can say how many degrees are round a point. I can find a missing angle.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## The idea

**Angles on a straight line** add to $180°$. **Angles round a point** add to $360°$. To find a missing angle, add up the ones you know and take the total from 180 or 360.

:::keep-together

### On a line

**Two angles on a straight line are 125° and ?°. What is the missing angle?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 55

180 - 125 = 55.

:::


:::keep-together

### Round a point

**Angles around a point are 90°, 120°, 100° and ?°. What is the missing angle?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 50

90 + 120 + 100 = 310, and 360 - 310 = 50.

:::


:::keep-together

### A full turn

**How big is a full turn?**

☐ 90°

☐ 100°

☐ 180°

☐ 360°

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 360°

A full turn is 360°. Half a turn is 180° and a quarter turn is 90°.

:::

### Round a point

**Angles round a point are 150°, 150° and ?°. What is the missing angle?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 60

150 + 150 = 300, and 360 - 300 = 60.

:::
