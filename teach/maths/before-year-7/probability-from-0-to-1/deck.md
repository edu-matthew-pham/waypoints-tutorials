# Probability from 0 to 1

Before Year 7 maths · Unknowns

<!-- Speaker note: Students place chances on a scale from 0 to 1 and find a simple probability as a fraction, and drop the idea that every event is a 50-50 chance. About 17 minutes (Think first 3 min · Try 4 min · The idea 5 min · Check 3 min · Apply 2 min). -->

---

## Today

- I can say what 0 and 1 mean for a chance.
- I can work out a simple chance as a fraction or a decimal.
- I can say which events are likely and unlikely.

## Why it matters

> Pick a counter from a bag with 3 red and 1 blue. Is red likely?

<!-- Speaker note: **Probability** is a number that says how likely an event is. It goes from $0$ (impossible) through $\tfrac{1}{2}$ (even chance) to $1$ (certain). -->

## Think first

**A bag has 3 red counters and 1 blue counter. You pick one without looking. How likely is red?**

- Even, a 50-50 chance
- Certain
- Unlikely
- Likely, more than half the time

<!-- Speaker note: What happens: Likely, more than half the time. 3 of the 4 counters are red, so the chance of red is 3/4. Take two or three predictions before anyone runs it. -->

## How likely?

**How likely is it?**

Groups: Impossible (0) · Unlikely · Even chance · Likely · Certain (1)

A coin lands heads or tails · A coin lands on heads · It snows in Canberra in January · A die shows a 7 · A die shows a number less than 6

* Answer: A coin lands heads or tails → Certain (1); A coin lands on heads → Even chance; It snows in Canberra in January → Unlikely; A die shows a 7 → Impossible (0); A die shows a number less than 6 → Likely

## Counters in a bag

**A bag has 10 counters and 3 are red. What is the chance of red, as a decimal?**

* Answer: 0.3

<!-- Speaker note: 3 out of 10 is 3/10, which is 0.3. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Not a probability

**Which of these cannot be a probability?**

- 0
- 0.5
- 1
- 1.5

* Answer: 1.5

<!-- Speaker note: A probability is never more than 1, because certain is 1. -->

---

<span style="background:#dcfce7; color:#1a1a1a; border:1.5px solid #22c55e; padding:6px 18px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1em; font-weight:500;">✅ Check</span>

## Nearly certain

**Which probability is closest to certain?**

- 0.05
- 0.25
- 0.5
- 0.95

* Answer: 0.95

<!-- Speaker note: 0.95 is close to 1, so it is nearly certain. -->

## A die

**A die is rolled. What is the chance it shows a number less than 7?**

* Answer: 1

<!-- Speaker note: All 6 numbers are less than 7, so it is certain: 6 out of 6 is 1. -->

**A bag has 2 red and 8 blue counters. How likely is red, and what number is that?**

* Unlikely, 2/10 or 0.2.
