# Scale factors

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A plan says 1 : 100. What does that actually promise?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find the scale factor between a model or plan and the real thing and convert both ways: multiply to go from the plan to the real thing, divide to go back, and change units last. They check that areas use the scale factor squared (AC9M10M05, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can find the scale factor from two matching lengths. I can go from a plan to the real thing and back. I can change units after converting.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Both directions


:::keep-together

### A wall at 1 : 50

1. $1 : 100 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{real} = \text{plan} \times 100$ — Plan to real: multiply by the scale factor.
2. $2.5 \times 100 = 250 \text{ cm}$ — The answer is in the same unit as the plan.
3. $250 \text{ cm} = 2.5 \text{ m}$ — Change to a sensible unit last.
4. $\text{real } 3.6 \text{ m} = 360 \text{ cm} \to 360 \div 100 = 3.6 \text{ cm}$ — Real to plan: divide.

**A plan is drawn at 1 : 50. A wall is 5 cm long on the plan. How long is the real wall? (m)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $5 \times 50$ (plan to real: multiply by the scale factor); $= 250$ cm (the answer is in the plan's unit, centimetres); $250 \text{ cm} = 2.5$ m (change to metres last)

5 × 50 = 250 cm = 2.5 m.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Ivy finds the real length of a wall drawn 5 cm long at 1 : 50. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Ivy's plan is drawn at a scale of 1 : 50.
 2  A wall measures 5 cm on the plan.
 3  Real length = 5 ÷ 50 = 0.1 cm.
 4  So the real wall is 0.1 cm long.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the real wall is bigger than the plan, so multiply by 50. 5 × 50 = 250 cm, which is 2.5 m.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which way round

**What does a scale of 1 : 100 mean?**

☐ 100 cm on the plan is 1 cm in real life

☐ 1 cm on the plan is 100 cm in real life

☐ The plan is 100 times bigger than the real thing

☐ 1 cm in real life is 100 cm on the plan

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 1 cm on the plan is 100 cm in real life

The first number is the plan, the second is real life: 1 cm on the plan stands for 100 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### On the plan

**The real wall is $3.6$ m. At 1 : 100, how long is it on the plan? (cm)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3.6

3.6 m = 360 cm, and 360 ÷ 100 = 3.6 cm.

## Scales


:::keep-together

### A wall

**At 1 : 100, a wall is $7.2$ cm on the plan. How long is it in real life? (m)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7.2

7.2 × 100 = 720 cm = 7.2 m.

:::


:::keep-together

### A real wall

**At 1 : 50, a real wall is $4$ m long. How long is it on the plan? (cm)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

4 m = 400 cm, and 400 ÷ 50 = 8 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### A model car

**A model car is $12$ cm long. The real car is $4.8$ m long. What is the scale factor from model to real?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 40

4.8 m = 480 cm and 480 ÷ 12 = 40, so the scale is 1 : 40.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: a shape is enlarged with every length multiplied by $3$. By what number is its area multiplied?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 9

Area scales with the square of the scale factor: 3 × 3 = 9.
