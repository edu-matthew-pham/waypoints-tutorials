**A right-angled triangle has opposite 3, adjacent 4 and hypotenuse 5 from the marked angle. Which ratio equals $0.75$?**

- opposite ÷ hypotenuse ($3 \div 5$)
- hypotenuse ÷ adjacent
- opposite ÷ adjacent ($3 \div 4$)
- adjacent ÷ hypotenuse ($4 \div 5$)

## Which sides

$$\sin = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

1) $\cos = \dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$ — Cosine: adjacent over hypotenuse.
2) $\tan = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$ — Tangent: opposite over adjacent.
3) $\text{3, 4, 5 triangle: } \sin = 0.6, \cos = 0.8, \tan = 0.75$ — The same sides give three ratios.

## Which ratio

**Which ratio is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse?**

- Sine
- Tangent
- Cosine
- None of them

* Answer: Cosine

## Find the mistake

**Lee finds the sine of the angle. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The triangle has opposite 3, adjacent 4 and hypotenuse 5.
 2  I want the sine of the marked angle.
 3  Sine is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = 4 ÷ 5 = 0.8.
 4  So sin = 0.8.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: sine uses the opposite side, not the adjacent. Adjacent ÷ hypotenuse is the cosine. -->
