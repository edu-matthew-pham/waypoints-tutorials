## Which ratio is which


:::keep-together

### Which sides

1. $\sin = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$ — Sine: opposite over hypotenuse.
2. $\cos = \dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$ — Cosine: adjacent over hypotenuse.
3. $\tan = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$ — Tangent: opposite over adjacent.
4. $\text{3, 4, 5 triangle: } \sin = 0.6, \cos = 0.8, \tan = 0.75$ — The same sides give three ratios.

**A triangle has opposite 6 and hypotenuse 10. What is the sine of the angle?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.6

sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse = 6 ÷ 10 = 0.6.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which ratio

**Which ratio is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse?**

☐ Sine

☐ Tangent

☐ Cosine

☐ None of them

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Cosine

Cosine is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Lee finds the sine of the angle. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The triangle has opposite 3, adjacent 4 and hypotenuse 5.
 2  I want the sine of the marked angle.
 3  Sine is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = 4 ÷ 5 = 0.8.
 4  So sin = 0.8.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: sine uses the opposite side, not the adjacent. Adjacent ÷ hypotenuse is the cosine.
