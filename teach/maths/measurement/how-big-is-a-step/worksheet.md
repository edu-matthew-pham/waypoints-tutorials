# How big is a step?

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Magnitude 5 and magnitude 6 look like neighbours on the news. Are they?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students see that one step up the earthquake magnitude scale multiplies the energy by about 32, so several steps multiply it over and over (32 × 32 for two steps) instead of adding. This is what makes the scale logarithmic (AC9M10M02, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can say that one step on a log scale multiplies, not adds. I can work out how many times as much energy two quakes differ by when they are several steps apart. I can explain why "only two points bigger" hides a multiplication of about a thousand.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## More than one step


:::keep-together

### Steps multiply

Here is one with three steps.

1. $3 \to 6$ — Magnitude 3 up to magnitude 6 is 3 steps.
2. $32 \times 32 \times 32$ — Each step multiplies the energy by about 32, so multiply three 32s.
3. $32 \times 32 = 1024$ — Two steps so far.
4. $1024 \times 32 = 32\,768$ — Three steps: about 33 000 times the energy.

Now try one on paper: a magnitude 4 against a magnitude 2. Work out the steps first, then type how many times as much energy.

**How many times as much energy does a magnitude 4 release than a magnitude 2? Use about 32 times for each step.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** From 2 to 4 is 2 steps. (count the steps first); $32 \times 32$ (each step multiplies by 32, so multiply 32 by itself once for two steps); $32 \times 32 = 1024$ (work it out)

Two steps: 32 × 32 = 1024, so about a thousand times as much.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Maya works out how much more energy a magnitude 7 releases than a magnitude 4. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Maya works out how many times more energy a magnitude 7 earthquake releases than a magnitude 4.
 2  Each step up the scale multiplies the energy by about 32.
 3  From 4 to 7 is 3 steps.
 4  So the energy goes up by 3 × 32 = 96 times.
 5  That is about 96 times as much energy.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: three steps multiply by 32 three times, 32 × 32 × 32 = 32 768, not 3 × 32 = 96.

:::


:::keep-together

### The same step

**The step from magnitude 4 to 5 and the step from magnitude 7 to 8 are both one step. What's the same about them?**

☐ Nothing: the bigger quake is a bigger step

☐ Each one adds about the same amount of energy

☐ Each one multiplies the energy by about the same number

☐ Each one releases about the same energy

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Each one multiplies the energy by about the same number

Equal steps on the scale mean the same multiplier. That's what makes it a logarithmic scale.
