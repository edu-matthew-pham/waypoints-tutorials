> **Key question:** A bike wheel 70 cm across rolls forward one circumference with every turn. How many turns to school? A trundle wheel measures a sports field the same way.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students use C = πd and C = 2πr to find circumferences, check against circles rolled out on the board, go backwards with d = C ÷ π, and design a trundle wheel (AC9M7M03, node 3).

## Forwards and backwards


:::keep-together

### Use the diameter

Here is the formula forwards and backwards.

1. $C = \pi d = 3.14 \times 8 = 25.12 \text{ cm}$ — From the diameter: multiply by π.
2. $C = 2\pi r = 2 \times 3.14 \times 30 = 188.4 \text{ cm}$ — From the radius: double it first (2r is the diameter).
3. $d = C \div \pi = 18.84 \div 3.14 = 6 \text{ m}$ — Backwards: divide by π.
4. $C = 10\pi \text{ cm}$ — Exact: leave π in the answer (for d = 10). 10π means 10 × π, about 31.4.

Now try one: diameter 8 cm.

**A circle has diameter 8 cm. Using π ≈ 3.14, what is its circumference, in cm?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 25.12

3.14 × 8 = 25.12 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Mei finds how far a wheel rolls in one turn. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A wheel has radius 30 cm. How far does it roll in one turn?
 2  C = π × d
 3  = 3.14 × 30
 4  = 94.2 cm
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: 30 cm is the radius, but C = π × d needs the diameter, 60 cm. 3.14 × 60 = 188.4 cm (or C = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 30).

:::


:::keep-together

### Backwards

**A circular garden bed has circumference 18.84 m. Using π ≈ 3.14, find its diameter. Write your steps.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $C = \pi d$, so $18.84 = 3.14 \times d$ (the circumference is π times the diameter); $d = 18.84 \div 3.14 = 6$ m (divide by π to undo multiplying by π)

18.84 ÷ 3.14 = 6 m.

:::

:::teacher-note

### From the radius

**Radius 3 cm. Circumference, in cm (π ≈ 3.14)?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 18.84

2 × 3.14 × 3 = 18.84 cm.

### A bike ride

**A bike wheel has diameter 70 cm. How far does the bike go in 100 turns, in metres (π ≈ 3.14)?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 219.8

One turn: 3.14 × 70 = 219.8 cm. 100 turns: 21 980 cm = 219.8 m.
