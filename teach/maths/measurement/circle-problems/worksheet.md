> **Key question:** A running track, a round garden bed, a pizza deal: is a 30 cm pizza more than twice a 20 cm one? Circle problems need the right formula, sometimes run backwards.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students choose between circumference and area, find a radius from an area, and solve problems with semicircles, quarter circles, rings and tracks (AC9M8M03, node 2).

**Students should be able to say:** I can choose the circumference or the area formula for a problem. I can find the radius from an area. I can solve problems with parts of circles.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

## Backwards and in parts


:::keep-together

### Backwards

Here is the routine.

1. $78.5 \div \pi \approx 25$ — Undo the π: this is r².
2. $r = \sqrt{25} = 5\ \text{cm}$ — Undo the square with a square root.
3. $\pi \times 5^2 \approx 78.5$ — Check by going forwards.

**A circle has an area of 113.1 cm². Find its radius in cm.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 6

113.1 ÷ π ≈ 36, and √36 = 6 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Leo finds the radius of a circle with area 50 cm². Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  A circle has an area of 50 cm².
 2  A = πr², so r = 50 ÷ 3.14.
 3  r = 15.9 cm.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: 50 ÷ 3.14 is r², not r. Take the square root: r = √15.9 ≈ 4.0 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Semicircles

**How do you find the area of a semicircle of radius 4 cm?**

☐ Half of π × 4²

☐ π × 4² × 2

☐ Half of 2 × π × 4

☐ π × 2²

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Half of π × 4²

A semicircle is half the circle: ½ × π × 16 ≈ 25.1 cm².

### Back to the radius

**A circular pool cover has an area of 154 m². Find its radius in m.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7

154 ÷ π ≈ 49, and √49 = 7 m.

### A quarter circle

**Find the area of a quarter circle of radius 10 cm, to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 78.5

¼ × π × 100 ≈ 78.5 cm².

:::


:::keep-together

### A ring

**A ring is between circles of radius 5 cm and 3 cm. Find its area in cm², to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 50.3

π × 25 − π × 9 = π × 16 ≈ 50.3 cm².

:::


:::keep-together

### A semicircle's perimeter

**Find the perimeter of a semicircle of radius 5 cm (the curve and the diameter), to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 25.7

Half the circumference, π × 5 ≈ 15.7 cm, plus the diameter 10 cm: 25.7 cm.
