# Composite shapes

Area, volume, circles and Pythagoras · Area

<!-- Speaker note: Students cut composite shapes into rectangles and triangles, or take pieces away from a rectangle, and find the area and the perimeter (AC9M8M01, node 1). About 40 minutes (Predict and Run 8 min · Investigate 10 min · Modify 10 min · Practise and Make 12 min). -->

## Today

**We are learning to** a composite shape is made of shapes you know. Cut it into pieces and add their areas, or see it as a big rectangle with pieces taken away; the area is the same either way. The perimeter is the distance round the outside.

- I can cut a composite shape into pieces I know and add their areas.
- I can find an area by taking pieces away from a big rectangle.
- I can work out a missing side and the perimeter.

## Why it matters

> An L-shaped bedroom, a house front with a pointed roof, a photo frame: none is a single rectangle, but each is easy once you cut it up.

<!-- Speaker note: A **composite shape** is made of simple shapes joined together. To find its area: 1. **Cut** it into rectangles, triangles or other shapes you know, find each area and **add** them; or 2. see it as a **big rectangle** and **take away** the pieces that are missing. Read every length you need from the diagram: a missing side is often the difference of two others. The **perimeter** is the total length round the outside, in units of length (m), not area (m²). -->

## Missing lengths

Here is the routine.

Now find the step.

$$\text{an L } 10 \text{ m by } 7 \text{ m, a } 6 \times 4 \text{ corner missing}$$

1) $\text{pieces: } 4 \times 7 \text{ and } 6 \times 3$ — Cut into shapes you know, with each piece's own lengths.
2) $28 + 18 = 46\ \text{m}^2$ — Add the pieces.
3) $10 \times 7 - 6 \times 4 = 70 - 24 = 46\ \text{m}^2$ — Or: the big rectangle, take away the missing corner.
4) $10 + 3 + 6 + 4 + 4 + 7 = 34\ \text{m}$ — The perimeter adds every side, in metres.

## Find the mistake

**Ella finds the area of the L-shape. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The L-shape is 8 m wide at the bottom and 6 m tall on the left.
 2  Ella cuts it up and down into a piece 3 m wide and a piece 5 m wide.
 3  Left piece: 3 × 6 = 18 m².
 4  Right piece: 5 × 6 = 30 m².
 5  Total: 18 + 30 = 48 m².
```

* Answer: line 4

<!-- Speaker note: Line 4: the right piece is only 3 m tall, not 6 m. 5 × 3 = 15, so the total is 18 + 15 = 33 m². -->
