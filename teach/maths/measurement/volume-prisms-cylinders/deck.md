## Base times height

$$V = A \times h$$

1) $A = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 1.1^2 = 3.8$ — The base is a circle.
2) $V = 3.8 \times 2 = 7.6 \text{ m}^3$ — The cylinder's volume.
3) $7.6 \times 1000 = 7600 \text{ L}$ — 1 m³ = 1000 litres.
