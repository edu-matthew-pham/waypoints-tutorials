## Base times height


:::keep-together

### Base times height

1. $V = A \times h$ — Volume: base area times height.
2. $A = \pi r^2 = 3.14 \times 1.1^2 = 3.8$ — The base is a circle.
3. $V = 3.8 \times 2 = 7.6 \text{ m}^3$ — The cylinder's volume.
4. $7.6 \times 1000 = 7600 \text{ L}$ — 1 m³ = 1000 litres.

**The base area is $3.8$ m² and the tank is $2$ m tall. What is its volume? (m³, 1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7.6

3.8 × 2 = 7.6 m³.

:::


:::keep-together

### Base area

**$1$ m³ is $1000$ litres. How many litres does the tank hold? (to the nearest 100)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7600

7.6 × 1000 = 7600 L.

:::


:::keep-together

## Volume


:::keep-together

### Box

**A box has base 4 cm by 5 cm and height 6 cm. What is its volume? (cm³)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 120

4 × 5 × 6 = 120.

:::


:::keep-together

### Cylinder

**A cylinder has radius 2 cm and height 5 cm. What is its volume? (cm³, 1 decimal place, $\pi \approx 3.14$)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 62.8

3.14 × 4 × 5 = 62.8.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: a can has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. What is its curved surface area? (cm², nearest whole number)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 188

2 × 3.14 × 3 × 10 = 188.4.
