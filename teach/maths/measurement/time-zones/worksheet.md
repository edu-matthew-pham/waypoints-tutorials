> **Key question:** You call your cousin in London after school and wake her up at 5 am. A flight from Sydney lands in Los Angeles before it took off. Time zones explain both.

**In one sentence:** Students move one city's time and watch the others follow, convert between time zones using UTC offsets, and plan flights and a call across zones (AC9M8M04, nodes 1 and 2).

**Students should be able to say:** I can change a time from one time zone to another using UTC offsets. I can tell when the day changes. I can work out when a flight lands, in local time.

## Ahead and behind


:::keep-together

### Perth

Here is the routine.

1. $+11 - (+1) = 10$ — The difference between the offsets: 10 hours.
2. $\text{London's offset is smaller}$ — So London is behind Sydney.
3. $09{:}00 - 10\ \text{h} = 23{:}00\ \text{the day before}$ — Take it away, crossing midnight back to Sunday.

Now find Perth's time.

**It is 14:00 in Sydney (UTC+11). What hour is it in Perth (UTC+8), in 24-hour time?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 11

Perth is 11 − 8 = 3 hours behind: 14:00 − 3 = 11:00.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Kim finds the time in London. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  It is 9:00 am Monday in Sydney (UTC+11). Kim wants the time in London (UTC+1).
 2  The zones are 10 hours apart, so she adds 10 hours.
 3  London time is 7:00 pm Monday.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 2

Line 2: London's offset (+1) is smaller than Sydney's (+11), so London is behind: take 10 hours away. 9:00 am Monday becomes 11:00 pm Sunday.

## Time zones


:::keep-together

### Sydney to Perth

**It is 10:00 in Sydney (UTC+11). What hour is it in Perth (UTC+8), in 24-hour time?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7

Perth is 3 hours behind: 10:00 − 3 = 07:00.

:::


:::keep-together

### A trip's length

**A train leaves at 08:45 and arrives at 17:20 the same day (one time zone). How many minutes is the trip?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 515

08:45 to 17:00 is 8 h 15 min; plus 20 min is 8 h 35 min = 515 minutes.

:::


:::keep-together

### 12-hour time

**What is 21:45 in 12-hour time?**

☐ 11:45 pm

☐ 10:45 pm

☐ 9:45 pm

☐ 9:45 am

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 9:45 pm

21 − 12 = 9: 9:45 pm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Past midnight

**It is 23:00 on Sunday in Perth (UTC+8). What hour is it in Sydney (UTC+11), in 24-hour time? (It is Monday there.)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

23:00 + 3 hours = 02:00 on Monday.

:::


:::keep-together

### A flight's length

**A flight leaves Perth at 06:00 Perth time and lands in Sydney at 13:00 Sydney time. How many hours is the flight?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4

06:00 in Perth is 09:00 in Sydney; 09:00 to 13:00 is 4 hours.

## Make: the final, on paper

Do this one in your book, not on the screen. The final is made up for the task, and the answer uses the offsets for 4 to 24 October 2026, so it holds only on a date in that window. Write the offsets, the sum and the day, then type the hour.

**Also used in**

- **Watching sport live.** A match overseas kicks off at a strange hour here. Daylight saving moves different places' clocks on different dates, so the gap can change by an hour.
- **Calls to family abroad.** Work out whose clock is later before you ring, or you wake someone up.
- **Flights.** A flight's arrival time is local time at the landing city, so it can look earlier than the departure.
