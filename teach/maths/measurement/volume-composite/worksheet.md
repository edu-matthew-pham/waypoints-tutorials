### Two ways to see a pool

A pool is 10 m long and 5 m wide. The first 6 m is 1 m deep and the last 4 m is 2 m deep (made up for the task).

1. $V = 6 \times 5 \times 1 + 4 \times 5 \times 2$ — Add: the shallow part is one box and the deep part is another.
2. $V = 30 + 40 = 70 \text{ m}^3$ — The total.
3. $V = 10 \times 5 \times 2 - 6 \times 5 \times 1$ — Take away: a big box as deep as the deep end, minus the box of missing water.
4. $V = 100 - 30 = 70 \text{ m}^3$ — The same answer both ways.

Now try a bigger pool on paper: work it both ways, one step per line, then type the volume.

**A pool is 12 m long and 6 m wide. The first 8 m is 1 m deep and the last 4 m is 2.5 m deep (made up for the task). What is its volume? (m³) Find it by adding two boxes, then check it by taking one box away from a bigger one.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** $V = 8 \times 6 \times 1 + 4 \times 6 \times 2.5$ (add: a shallow box and a deep box); $V = 48 + 60 = 108$ (the total); $12 \times 6 \times 2.5 - 8 \times 6 \times 1.5 = 180 - 72 = 108$ (check: a big box as deep as the deep end, minus the missing water)

Adding: 48 + 60 = 108 m³. Taking away: 180 − 72 = 108 m³. The same, so I can trust it.

:::

## Composite volumes


:::keep-together

### Steps

**A step: a block $3$ m long, $2$ m wide and $1$ m tall sits on a block $5$ m long, $2$ m wide and $0.5$ m tall. What is the total volume? (m³)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 11

3 × 2 × 1 = 6 and 5 × 2 × 0.5 = 5. Total 11 m³.

:::


:::keep-together

### The pipe

**A concrete pipe is $4$ m long, with an outer radius of $0.3$ m and a hole radius of $0.25$ m. What is the volume of concrete? (m³, 2 decimal places, $\pi \approx 3.14$)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.35

3.14 × 0.3² × 4 = 1.1304 and 3.14 × 0.25² × 4 = 0.785. The difference is 0.3454, about 0.35 m³.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: a student works out the tank's volume as $8.4$ m³ when the true value is $8.36$ m³. What is the percentage error? (%, 1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.5

The absolute error is 0.04 m³. 0.04 ÷ 8.36 × 100 = 0.48%, about 0.5%.

:::
