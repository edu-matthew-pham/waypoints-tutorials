## Ratio to angle

$$\tan\theta = \dfrac{1}{14}$$

1) $\tan\theta = 0.0714$ — Divide.
2) $\theta = \tan^{-1}(0.0714)$ — Use the inverse key.
3) $\theta \approx 4.1°$ — The ramp is about 4°.

## Ratio to angle

**The ramp has opposite $= 1$ and adjacent $= 14$. What is the ratio $1 \div 14$ to 3 decimal places?**

* Answer: 0.071

## The inverse key

**Use $\tan^{-1}$ on $0.5$ (opposite $=$ 1, adjacent $= 2$). What is the angle, to the nearest degree?**

* Answer: 27

## Find the mistake

**Sam finds the angle of the ramp. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The ramp rises 1 m over a run of 14 m. I want the angle.
 2  tan(angle) = 1 ÷ 14 = 0.071.
 3  So the angle = tan 0.071 = 0.07°.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: tan 0.071 would be an angle's tangent, not an angle. Use tan⁻¹ 0.071 to turn the ratio into an angle. -->
