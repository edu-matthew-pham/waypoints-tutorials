# Area of a circle

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** How much pizza is in a pizza? Cut it into slices, lay them out top to tail, and a rectangle appears.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students cut a circle into slices and rearrange them into a near-rectangle πr by r, compare it with squares of r², and use A = πr² (AC9M8M03, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain why the area of a circle is πr². I can say why a circle's area is between 2r² and 4r². I can find the area from the radius or the diameter.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Why it works


:::keep-together

### The long side

**Why is the rearranged shape πr long?**

☐ Because there are n slices

☐ Because π is about 3

☐ Half the slices' curved edges are along the bottom: half of the circumference 2πr

☐ Because the circle has radius r

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Half the slices' curved edges are along the bottom: half of the circumference 2πr

The curved edges of the slices make the circumference; half is on top, half on the bottom: πr each.

:::


:::keep-together

### A check with squares

**A circle has radius 5 cm. Why must its area be between 50 and 100 cm²?**

☐ It is more than the inside square (2r² = 50) and less than the outside square (4r² = 100)

☐ Because 5 × 10 = 50

☐ Because π is between 2 and 4 exactly

☐ Because the diameter is 10

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** It is more than the inside square (2r² = 50) and less than the outside square (4r² = 100)

The outside square has side 2r (area 4r² = 100); the square on the diagonals has area 2r² = 50.

:::

## Circle areas


:::keep-together

### Radius 7

**Find the area of a circle of radius 7 cm, in cm², to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 153.9

π × 49 ≈ 153.9 cm² (with 3.14: 153.86).

:::


:::keep-together

### From the diameter

**A circle has diameter 10 m. Find its area in m², to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 78.5

r = 5 m, so A = π × 25 ≈ 78.5 m².

:::


:::keep-together

### The formula

**Which formula gives the area of a circle?**

☐ A = πr²

☐ A = 2r²

☐ A = πd

☐ A = 2πr

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A = πr²

A = πr²; the circumference is C = 2πr = πd.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review: circumference

**Review: find the circumference of a circle of radius 5 cm, in cm, to 1 decimal place.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 31.4
