## Matching sides


:::keep-together

### Scale factor

1. $3, 4, 5 \to ?, ?, 20$ — The longest side goes from 5 to 20.
2. $20 \div 5 = 4$ — The scale factor.
3. $3 \times 4 = 12, \quad 4 \times 4 = 16$ — Multiply every side by 4.
4. $\text{area} \times 4^2 = \times 16$ — Area is multiplied by the factor squared.

**Triangle A has sides 3, 4, 5. Triangle B is similar with its longest side 20. What is the scale factor from A to B?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4

20 ÷ 5 = 4.

:::


:::keep-together

### The factor

**Using that scale factor, how long is the shortest side of triangle B?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 12

3 × 4 = 12.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Jo enlarges the 3, 4, 5 triangle so its longest side is 20. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Sides 3, 4, 5. The longest side must become 20.
 2  20 − 5 = 15, so I add 15 to every side.
 3  New triangle: 18, 19, 20.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: she added 15 to every side. A similar triangle needs every side multiplied by the same factor (4), not the same amount added.

## Scale factors


:::keep-together

### The factor

**Triangle P has sides 2, 3, 4. Triangle Q is similar with its shortest side 10. What is the scale factor?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 5

10 ÷ 2 = 5.

:::


:::keep-together

### The longest side

**How long is the longest side of triangle Q?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 20
