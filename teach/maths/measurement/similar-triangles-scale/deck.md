## Scale factor

$$3, 4, 5 \to ?, ?, 20$$

1) $20 \div 5 = 4$ — The scale factor.
2) $3 \times 4 = 12, \quad 4 \times 4 = 16$ — Multiply every side by 4.
3) $\text{area} \times 4^2 = \times 16$ — Area is multiplied by the factor squared.

## Scale factor

**Triangle A has sides 3, 4, 5. Triangle B is similar with its longest side 20. What is the scale factor from A to B?**

* Answer: 4

## Find the mistake

**Jo enlarges the 3, 4, 5 triangle so its longest side is 20. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Sides 3, 4, 5. The longest side must become 20.
 2  20 − 5 = 15, so I add 15 to every side.
 3  New triangle: 18, 19, 20.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: she added 15 to every side. A similar triangle needs every side multiplied by the same factor (4), not the same amount added. -->
