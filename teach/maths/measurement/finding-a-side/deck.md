## Today

**We are learning to** pythagoras finds any side. For the hypotenuse, add the squares: c² = a² + b². For a shorter side, subtract from the hypotenuse's square: b² = c² − a². Then take the square root, and check the hypotenuse is still the longest.

- I can find a shorter side of a right-angled triangle.
- I can decide whether to add or subtract the squares.
- I can solve problems with ladders, screens and ramps, and check my answer makes sense.

## Add for c, subtract for a side

Here are both kinds.

Now try one: hypotenuse 13 cm, one side 5 cm.

$$c^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 100,\ c = 10$$

1) $b^2 = 10^2 - 6^2 = 100 - 36 = 64,\ b = 8$ — Missing a shorter side: subtract from the hypotenuse's square.
2) $b^2 = 7^2 - 4^2 = 33,\ b = \sqrt{33} \approx 5.74$ — Not a perfect square: between 5 and 6 (25 and 36), about 5.74.
3) $5.74 < 7$ — Check: the hypotenuse must still be the longest.

## A screen

**A rectangular screen is 48 cm wide and 36 cm high. How long is its diagonal? Write your steps.**

* Answer: The diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with sides 48 and 36 (the corners of a rectangle are right angles); $48^2 + 36^2 = 2304 + 1296 = 3600$ (for the hypotenuse, add the squares); $\sqrt{3600} = 60$ cm (take the square root)
