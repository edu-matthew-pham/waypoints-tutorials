## Absolute and relative

$$\text{absolute error} = |\text{measured} - \text{true}|$$

1) $|20.04 - 20| = 0.04 \text{ mm}$ — The hole.
2) $\text{relative error} = \dfrac{\text{absolute error}}{\text{true value}}$ — How big compared with the thing.
3) $0.04 \div 20 = 0.002$ — The hole's relative error.
4) $1 \div 2000 = 0.0005$ — The beam's relative error.
