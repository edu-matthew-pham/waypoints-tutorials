## Find the opposite

$$\tan 40° = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

1) $\text{opposite} = 20 \times \tan 40°$ — Multiply both sides by 20.
2) $\text{opposite} = 20 \times 0.839 = 16.8$ — Use the calculator in degree mode.
3) $\text{opposite} \approx 16.8 \text{ m}$ — Above eye level.

## Find the mistake

**Pat finds the height of the tree. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Adjacent = 20 m, angle = 40°. I want the opposite side.
 2  tan 40° = opposite ÷ adjacent.
 3  So opposite = 20 ÷ tan 40° = 20 ÷ 0.839 = 23.8 m.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: tan = opposite ÷ adjacent, so opposite = adjacent × tan. Pat divided, which gives a height far too big. -->
