# Find a side with trigonometry

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** You can't climb the tallest tree in the grounds. You can stand back, measure an angle and work out its height.


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students choose sine, cosine or tangent and use it to find an unknown side, checking the answer makes sense (AC9M9M03, node 1).

**Students should be able to say:** I can pick the right ratio for the sides I know and want. I can multiply or divide to find the side. I can say if an answer is possible.

## Choose and calculate


:::keep-together

### Find the opposite

1. $\tan 40° = \dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$ — The ratio that links the two sides.
2. $\text{opposite} = 20 \times \tan 40°$ — Multiply both sides by 20.
3. $\text{opposite} = 20 \times 0.839 = 16.8$ — Use the calculator in degree mode.
4. $\text{opposite} \approx 16.8 \text{ m}$ — Above eye level.

**For the tree, opposite $=$ adjacent $\times \tan 40°$ with adjacent $= 20$ m and $\tan 40° = 0.839$. How high above your eye is the top? (1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 16.8

20 × 0.839 = 16.78, about 16.8 m.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which ratio

**You know the adjacent side and want the opposite side. Which ratio do you use?**

☐ Any of them

☐ Sine

☐ Tangent

☐ Cosine

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Tangent

Opposite and adjacent: tangent.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Pat finds the height of the tree. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Adjacent = 20 m, angle = 40°. I want the opposite side.
 2  tan 40° = opposite ÷ adjacent.
 3  So opposite = 20 ÷ tan 40° = 20 ÷ 0.839 = 23.8 m.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: tan = opposite ÷ adjacent, so opposite = adjacent × tan. Pat divided, which gives a height far too big.

:::

## Find the side


:::keep-together

### Opposite

**A triangle has hypotenuse 12 and a 30° angle. The opposite side is hypotenuse × $\sin 30°$ (0.5). How long is it?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 6

12 × 0.5 = 6.

:::


:::keep-together

### Adjacent

**A cliff is $50$ m high. From the boat the angle up to its top is $35°$. How far is the boat from the foot of the cliff? ($\tan 35° = 0.700$; nearest metre. Made up for the task.)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 71

50 ÷ 0.700 = 71.4, about 71 m.

:::


:::keep-together
