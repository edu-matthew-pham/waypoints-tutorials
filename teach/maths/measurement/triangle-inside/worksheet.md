# The triangle inside

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** The umbrella is longer than any side of the suitcase. Will it go in?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find the right-angled triangle inside a box, floor first and then the one standing on the floor diagonal, and use Pythagoras twice, or trigonometry for the slope of the line (AC9M10M03, node 3).

**Students should be able to say:** I can find the right-angled triangle on the floor of a box and the one standing up inside it. I can find the longest line inside a box with Pythagoras twice. I can find the angle that line makes with the floor.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Two triangles


:::keep-together

### Two steps

Here is one with a room.

1. $\text{floor} = \sqrt{6^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{52}$ — The first triangle lies flat on the floor. Its shorter sides are the length and the width.
2. $\text{pole} = \sqrt{52 + 3^2} = \sqrt{61}$ — The second triangle stands up. Its shorter sides are the floor diagonal and the height.
3. $\sqrt{61} = 7.8$ — The longest pole that fits is about 7.8 m.

A storeroom is 6 m long, 4 m wide and 3 m high. The longest straight pole that fits from a floor corner to the opposite ceiling corner is about 7.8 m.

Now try one on paper: a fish tank, 90 cm by 40 cm by 50 cm. Draw the floor triangle first.

**A fish tank is 90 cm long, 40 cm wide and 50 cm high (made up for the task). What is the longest straight rod that fits inside? (1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Floor first: $\sqrt{90^2 + 40^2} = \sqrt{9700}$ (draw the triangle on the floor and find its hypotenuse); Then up: $\sqrt{9700 + 50^2} = \sqrt{12\,200}$ (the floor diagonal and the height are the shorter sides of the second triangle); $\sqrt{12\,200} = 110.5$ cm (work it out)

Floor diagonal √(90² + 40²) = √9700. Then √(9700 + 50²) = √12 200 = 110.5 cm.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

**Sam checks whether the umbrella fits. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The floor of the suitcase is 80 cm by 50 cm, so its diagonal is √(80² + 50²) = 94.3 cm.
 2  The second triangle has the floor diagonal and the 30 cm height as its shorter sides.
 3  So the longest line is 94.3 + 30 = 124.3 cm.
 4  124.3 is longer than 120, so the umbrella fits.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3: the floor diagonal and the height are the two shorter sides of a right-angled triangle, so Pythagoras again: √(94.3² + 30²) = 99.0 cm. Adding gives 124.3 cm, which is longer than the real diagonal, and makes the umbrella look as if it fits.

:::

The standing triangle has the floor diagonal along the bottom and the height up the side. The space diagonal is its hypotenuse.

## Lines inside boxes


:::keep-together

### The room

**A room is $5$ m long, $4$ m wide and $3$ m high. How far is it from a floor corner to the opposite ceiling corner? (1 decimal place)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 7.1

5² + 4² + 3² = 50, and √50 = 7.07, about 7.1 m.

:::


:::keep-together

### The box

**A box is $12$ cm by $9$ cm by $8$ cm. How long is its longest straight line, in cm?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 17

12² + 9² = 225, so the floor diagonal is 15. Then 15² + 8² = 289, and √289 = 17 cm.

:::
