## As a percentage


:::keep-together

### Percentage error

1. $\text{percentage error} = \dfrac{\text{absolute error}}{\text{true value}} \times 100\%$ — Relative error as a percentage.
2. $0.04 \div 20 = 0.002$ — The relative error.
3. $0.002 \times 100 = 0.2\%$ — The percentage error.
4. $\text{gap} > \text{combined error} \Rightarrow \text{result is real}$ — Compare the difference with what the measurements could be out by.

**A timer is out by $0.001$ s on a $52.34$ s race. What is the percentage error? (%, 4 decimal places)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.0019

0.001 ÷ 52.34 = 0.0000191, × 100 = 0.0019%.

:::


:::keep-together

### Times 100

**The gap between the swimmers is $0.010$ s. What percentage of $52.34$ s is that? (%, 3 decimal places)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.019

0.010 ÷ 52.34 = 0.000191, × 100 = 0.019%.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find the mistake

Why is it wrong?

**Answer:** line 3

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2
