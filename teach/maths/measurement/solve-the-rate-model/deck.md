## Up and back

$$\text{up} = 8 \div 5 + 450 \div 600 = 2.35$$

1) $\text{back} = 8 \div 5 = 1.6$ — Hours back: no climb.
2) $\text{up and back} = 2.35 + 1.6 = 3.95$ — Walking only.
3) $3.95 + 0.5 = 4.45$ — Add a 30 minute stop.
4) $\text{start} = 4{:}30 - 4{:}27 = 12{:}03$ — Work back from the finish.
