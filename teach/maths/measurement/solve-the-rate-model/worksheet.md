**Going up the lookout track (8 km, 450 m climb) takes 2.35 hours by the rule. About how long does walking back down take, with no climb to count?**

☐ 0.75 hours

☐ 1.6 hours

☐ 4.7 hours

☐ 2.35 hours

My prediction, and why:

{{lines-3}}

:::teacher-note

**What happens:** 1.6 hours. The way back is 8 km on a rule of 5 km/h, with no climb to add: 8 ÷ 5 = 1.6 hours. Doubling the whole walk would give 4.7 hours, counting the climb twice.

## Put the times together


:::keep-together

### Up and back

1. $\text{up} = 8 \div 5 + 450 \div 600 = 2.35$ — Hours up.
2. $\text{back} = 8 \div 5 = 1.6$ — Hours back: no climb.
3. $\text{up and back} = 2.35 + 1.6 = 3.95$ — Walking only.
4. $3.95 + 0.5 = 4.45$ — Add a 30 minute stop.
5. $\text{start} = 4{:}30 - 4{:}27 = 12{:}03$ — Work back from the finish.

**Up takes $2.35$ hours and back takes $1.6$ hours. How long is the walk up and back? (hours)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3.95

2.35 + 1.6 = 3.95 hours.

:::


:::keep-together

### Both ways

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4.45

3.95 + 0.5 = 4.45 hours.

:::

## Use the model


:::keep-together

### Up

**A track is 10 km with 300 m of climb. How many hours up by the rule?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2.5

10 ÷ 5 + 300 ÷ 600 = 2 + 0.5 = 2.5.

:::


:::keep-together

### Back

**How many hours is the way back, with no climb? (hours)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

10 ÷ 5 = 2.

:::


:::keep-together

### Review

**Review: a walk is 5 km with 600 m of climb. How many hours up?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2

5 ÷ 5 + 600 ÷ 600 = 2.

:::
