## The net

$$\text{curved side} = 2\pi r \times h$$

1) $2 \times 3.14 \times 3 \times 10 = 188.4$ — The label.
2) $\text{two ends} = 2 \times \pi r^2$ — Two circles.
3) $2 \times 3.14 \times 3^2 = 56.5$ — The lid and the base.
4) $\text{total} = 188.4 + 56.5 = 244.9 \text{ cm}^2$ — All three pieces.
