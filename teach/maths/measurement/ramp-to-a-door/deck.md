## Three parts

$$\text{run} = 0.45 \times 14 = 6.3$$

1) $\text{ramp} = \sqrt{0.45^2 + 6.3^2} \approx 6.32$ — Pythagoras: the ramp is the hypotenuse.
2) $\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{0.45}{6.3}\right) = 4.1°$ — Rise is opposite, run is adjacent.

## Which tool

**Which tool gives the angle of the ramp from the rise and the run?**

- tan⁻¹(rise ÷ run)
- The scale factor
- Pythagoras

## Find the mistake

**Ali finds the ramp's angle. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The gradient is 1 in 14, so rise ÷ run = 1 ÷ 14.
 2  That means the angle is 14°.
 3  So the ramp is 14° to the ground.
```

* Answer: line 2

<!-- Speaker note: Line 2: 1 in 14 is a ratio (rise ÷ run). The angle is tan⁻¹(1 ÷ 14) = 4.1°, not 14°. -->
