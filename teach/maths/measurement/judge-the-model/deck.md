## Different pace

$$\text{rule: } 8 \div 5 + 450 \div 600 = 2.35$$

1) $\text{group: } 8 \div 4 + 450 \div 600 = 2.75$ — Slower pace for full packs.
2) $2.75 - 2.35 = 0.4$ — 24 minutes longer.
3) $\text{report: about 2.4 to 2.8 hours, depending on pace}$ — Give a range and the reason.

## Exit ticket

**Why report a range, not one number, from a model?**

* The model is an estimate; the range shows how much depends on its assumptions.
