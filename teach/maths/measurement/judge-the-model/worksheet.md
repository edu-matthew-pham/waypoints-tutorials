:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students test a rate model against a different case and report its answer with its limits (AC9M9M05, node 3).

## Test the assumption


:::keep-together

### Different pace

1. $\text{rule: } 8 \div 5 + 450 \div 600 = 2.35$ — Fit walkers, good track.
2. $\text{group: } 8 \div 4 + 450 \div 600 = 2.75$ — Slower pace for full packs.
3. $2.75 - 2.35 = 0.4$ — 24 minutes longer.
4. $\text{report: about 2.4 to 2.8 hours, depending on pace}$ — Give a range and the reason.

**At $4$ km/h the distance takes $2$ hours and the climb adds $0.75$ hours. What is the total? (hours)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 2.75

2 + 0.75 = 2.75 hours.

:::


:::keep-together

### The difference

**How many hours longer is that than the rule's $2.35$? (hours)**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 0.4

2.75 − 2.35 = 0.4 hours, about 24 minutes.

:::


:::keep-together

## Report the answer


:::keep-together

### Another pace

**At 4 km/h on the flat, a 12 km walk with no climb takes how many hours?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 3

12 ÷ 4 = 3.

:::


:::keep-together

### The report

**Which is the best report for a trip time?**

☐ Exactly 2.35 hours

☐ Probably less

☐ About 2.4 to 2.8 hours: the rule says 2.35 for fit walkers, and a group with packs is slower

☐ No idea

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** About 2.4 to 2.8 hours: the rule says 2.35 for fit walkers, and a group with packs is slower

A good report gives a range and says what it depends on.
