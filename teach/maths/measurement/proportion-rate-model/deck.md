## Two rates

$$\text{time} = \dfrac{\text{distance (km)}}{5} + \dfrac{\text{climb (m)}}{600}$$

1) $\dfrac{8}{5} = 1.6$ — Hours for the distance.
2) $\dfrac{450}{600} = 0.75$ — Hours for the climb.
3) $1.6 + 0.75 = 2.35$ — Total hours: about 2 hours 21 minutes.

## Find the mistake

**Kai works out the lookout walk's time. Click the line where his working goes wrong.**

```
 1  The track is 8 km with a 450 m climb. The rule is 5 km/h plus 600 m per hour of climb.
 2  450 m is 0.45 km, so the distance is 8 + 0.45 = 8.45 km.
 3  Time = 8.45 ÷ 5 = 1.69 hours.
```

* Answer: line 3

<!-- Speaker note: Line 3: the climb is metres of height, not kilometres of walking. It has its own rate: 600 m per hour. -->
