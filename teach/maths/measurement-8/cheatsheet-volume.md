# Volume

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**volume and capacity**: A cubic metre is 100 × 100 × 100 = 1 000 000 cm³: convert each of the three lengths.

$$1\ \text{cm}^3 = 1\ \text{mL}, \quad 1000\ \text{cm}^3 = 1\ \text{L}, \quad 1\ \text{m}^3 = 1000\ \text{L}$$

:::
