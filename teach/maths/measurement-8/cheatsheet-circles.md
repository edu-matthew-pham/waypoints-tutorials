# Circles

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**area of a circle**: Slices of a circle make a near-rectangle πr long and r high. The area is between 2r² and 4r². Do not mix it up with C = 2πr.

$$A = \pi r^2$$

_24 slices laid top to tail: nearly a rectangle πr by r_

:::

:::keep-together

**radius from area**: Divide by π, then take the square root. A semicircle is half the area; its perimeter is half the circumference plus the diameter.

$$r = \sqrt{A \div \pi}$$

:::
