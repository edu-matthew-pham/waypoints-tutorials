# Area

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**composite area**: Cut the shape into pieces you know and add their areas, or take the missing pieces away from a big rectangle. Each piece uses its own lengths.

$$A = 8 \times 6 - 5 \times 3 = 33\ \text{m}^2$$

_An L-shape with its side lengths_

:::

:::keep-together

**estimating an irregular area**: Count the full squares, add half the part squares, multiply by the area of one square. A finer grid gives a closer estimate.

$$A \approx (\text{full} + \tfrac{1}{2} \times \text{part}) \times \text{one square}$$

:::
