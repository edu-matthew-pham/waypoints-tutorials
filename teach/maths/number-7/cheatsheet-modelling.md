# Modelling

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**known, unknown, relationship**: The first step in modelling: say what you know, what you need, and how they are linked. Then calculate.

$$\text{known} \to \text{unknown}$$

:::

:::keep-together

**profit as a percentage**: A percentage profit or change is out of the original amount, the cost, not the selling price.

$$\dfrac{\text{profit}}{\text{cost}} \times 100\%$$

:::
