# Area

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**perpendicular height**: The distance from the base to the opposite side or corner, at a right angle (∟) to the base. A slanted side is longer, and is not the height.

$$h \text{ at } 90^\circ \text{ to } b$$

:::

:::keep-together

**area of a parallelogram**: Cut the triangle off one end and move it: a rectangle with the same base and height.

$$A = b \times h$$

_A parallelogram cut and rearranged into a 6 by 4 rectangle_

:::

:::keep-together

**area of a triangle**: Two copies make a parallelogram with the same base and height; the triangle is half.

$$A = \tfrac{1}{2} \times b \times h$$

_A triangle and its turned copy make a parallelogram_

:::

:::keep-together

**composite area**: Split the shape into rectangles and triangles and add the parts, or subtract a missing part from a bigger shape. Put every length in the same unit first.

$$24 + 9 = 33 \text{ m}^2$$

:::

:::keep-together

**square units**: Area counts squares: cm², m². A 1 m square is 100 cm by 100 cm.

$$1 \text{ m}^2 = 10\,000 \text{ cm}^2$$

:::
