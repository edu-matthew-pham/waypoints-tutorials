# Modelling

> The words and methods from this unit, with an example of each.

:::keep-together

**scale**: A ratio of the drawing (or model) to the real thing. Each 1 unit on the drawing is 50 units in real life.

$$1 : 50 \Rightarrow 3\text{ cm} \to 150\text{ cm}$$

:::
