# Where they meet

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** Two ways to save: one starts ahead, one starts behind. When does the one behind pull level?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Students find where an exponential curve and a line meet from a graph, state the coordinates, and refine the answer by zooming in or by halving the gap in a table (AC9M10A05, node 2).

**Students should be able to say:** I can read where two graphs meet and say what the coordinates mean. I can narrow a crossing down by zooming in or by comparing values in a table. I can say which plan is better before and after the crossing.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Zoom in


:::keep-together

### Which window?

Read the window before you decide.

1. $2^x \text{ and } 10 + x$ — Where do the curve and the line meet?
2. $x = 3:\; 2^3 = 8,\; 10 + 3 = 13$ — The line is ahead.
3. $x = 4:\; 2^4 = 16,\; 10 + 4 = 14$ — The curve is ahead. The swap is between 3 and 4.
4. $x = 3.5:\; 11.3 \text{ and } 13.5$ — The line is still ahead, so the swap is between 3.5 and 4.
5. $x = 3.8:\; 13.9 \text{ and } 13.8$ — The curve is just ahead, so the swap is between 3.5 and 3.8.
6. $x \approx 3.8$ — Halve the gap again if you need more places.

Now try one on paper: $3^x$ against $20 + x$. At which whole number of $x$ is the curve first ahead? Type it.

**In this window the plans meet between which two whole years?**

☐ At exactly 8 years

☐ Between 6 and 7 years

☐ Between 8 and 9 years

☐ Between 9 and 10 years

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Between 8 and 9 years

At 8 years the line is ahead (4,400 against 4,300). At 9 years the curve is ahead (5,160 against 4,700). The swap happens between them.

:::

### Find the mistake

**Jo compares the plans at 10 years. Click the line where her working goes wrong.**

```
 1  Plan A is 2000 + 300x. Plan B is 1000(1.2^x). Which is the better plan?
 2  At 10 years, A = 2000 + 3000 = 5000 and B = 1000 x 1.2^10 = 6192.
 3  B is bigger at 10 years, so B is the better plan.
 4  B is the better plan, whatever the number of years.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 4

Line 4: one comparison says who is ahead at that time, not at all times. At 6 years, B is worth about 2,986 and A about 3,800: A is ahead. The plans swap where they cross, at about 8.2 years.

:::


:::keep-together

### Closer to which year?

**Zoom in once more in your head: at 8 years the plans differ by $4,400 - 4,300 = 100$ dollars (A ahead), and at 9 years by $5,160 - 4,700 = 460$ dollars (B ahead). Is the crossing closer to 8 or to 9? Type the closer whole number.**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 8

The gap at 8 years is 100 and at 9 years is 460, so the plans are much closer at 8 years. The crossing is just after 8.

### Between which years?

**At 5 years Plan A is worth $4,000$ and Plan B is worth $3,200$. At 6 years A is worth $4,300$ and B is worth $4,000$. At 7 years A is worth $4,600$ and B is worth $5,000$. Between which two years do they meet?**

☐ Between 6 and 7 years

☐ Between 5 and 6 years

☐ At 6 years

☐ After 7 years

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Between 6 and 7 years

A is ahead at 6 years and B is ahead at 7 years, so they swap between 6 and 7.

:::


:::keep-together

### Which is ahead?

**A table shows $2^x$ and $x + 12$. At $x = 3$ they are $8$ and $15$. At $x = 4$ they are $16$ and $16$. At what $x$ do they meet?**

Answer: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** 4

At x = 4 both are 16.

### A curve overtakes

**At 2 years $2^x$ is 4 and $3x$ is 6. At 3 years they are 8 and 9. At 4 years they are 16 and 12. Which statement is right?**

☐ The curve is always below the line

☐ The curve overtakes the line between 3 and 4 years

☐ They meet at exactly 3 years

☐ The curve overtakes the line between 2 and 3 years

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The curve overtakes the line between 3 and 4 years

The line is ahead at 3 years and the curve is ahead at 4 years, so the swap is between.

:::

## Make: back to the plans


**How far ahead?.** Back to the plans. Work it out on paper first, then type your answer.

**Which plan for 5 years?.** The decision is the crossing. The 20% a year is made up and much higher than a real savings account, so that the crossing is within a few years.

Sources: the two plans and the 20% a year are made up for this lesson.

**Also used in**

- **Phone plans.** A plan with a low fee and a high price per gigabyte meets a plan with a high fee and a low price per gigabyte, and the crossing says which to pick.
- **Science.** Where a measured line meets a predicted curve is where the model agrees with the data.
