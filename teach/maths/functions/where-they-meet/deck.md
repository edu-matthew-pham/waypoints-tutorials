## Why it matters

> Two ways to save: one starts ahead, one starts behind. When does the one behind pull level?

## Which window?

Read the window before you decide.

Now try one on paper: $3^x$ against $20 + x$. At which whole number of $x$ is the curve first ahead? Type it.

$$2^x \text{ and } 10 + x$$

1) $x = 3:\; 2^3 = 8,\; 10 + 3 = 13$ — The line is ahead.
2) $x = 4:\; 2^4 = 16,\; 10 + 4 = 14$ — The curve is ahead. The swap is between 3 and 4.
3) $x = 3.5:\; 11.3 \text{ and } 13.5$ — The line is still ahead, so the swap is between 3.5 and 4.
4) $x = 3.8:\; 13.9 \text{ and } 13.8$ — The curve is just ahead, so the swap is between 3.5 and 3.8.
5) $x \approx 3.8$ — Halve the gap again if you need more places.

## A curve overtakes

**At 2 years $2^x$ is 4 and $3x$ is 6. At 3 years they are 8 and 9. At 4 years they are 16 and 12. Which statement is right?**

- They meet at exactly 3 years
- The curve overtakes the line between 2 and 3 years

* Answer: The curve overtakes the line between 3 and 4 years
