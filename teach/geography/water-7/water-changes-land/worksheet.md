# How moving water changes the land

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A river runs through a valley. Does that explain how the valley became deeper?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Explain erosion, transport and deposition as processes that change a place, rather than merely naming a landform.

**Students should be able to say:** I can explain how flowing water removes and carries material. I can distinguish erosion, transport and deposition. I can explain a physical change instead of just naming a landform.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: a process, not a location


:::keep-together

### Build the process chain

**Which order explains Kerrin’s storm?**

☐ Downhill flow → deposition → erosion → transport

☐ Downhill flow → transport → deposition → erosion

☐ Downhill flow → erosion → transport → deposition

☐ Deposition → downhill flow → erosion → transport

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Downhill flow → erosion → transport → deposition

Gravity pulls runoff downhill. Flow removes soil, carries it and deposits some where it slows.

:::


:::keep-together

### Find Nib’s slip

1. $\text{Energy}$ — Gravity pulls runoff down a bare slope in a made-up park.
2. $\text{Erosion}$ — Water removes loose soil.
3. $\text{Transport}$ — Flow carries the soil along a shallow channel.
4. $\text{Change}$ — The slope loses soil and the channel becomes deeper.

**Nib explains the case. Click the first line where the reasoning goes wrong.**

```
 1  The river flows through a valley.
 2  The question asks how water can make the valley deeper.
 3  Nib says: "It is deeper because the river is in a low place. That is the whole explanation."
 4  Nib decides naming the location is enough.
```

Line: {{field-15}}

Why is it wrong?

{{lines-2}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** line 3

Line 3 gives the river’s location as the whole explanation. Explain how erosion and transport remove material over time.

## Explain the change


:::keep-together

### Which process?

**In these made-up observations, which process is the main focus?**

Groups: Erosion, Transport, Deposition

- A stream removes soil from its bank: {{field-15}}
- Sand travels along the streambed: {{field-15}}
- Silt settles on a flooded field as water slows: {{field-15}}

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** A stream removes soil from its bank → Erosion; Sand travels along the streambed → Transport; Silt settles on a flooded field as water slows → Deposition

:::


:::keep-together

### Explain, rather than locate

**Which explanation tells how a valley can become deeper?**

☐ The river lies in a low place between areas of higher ground

☐ A valley is a low area with a river flowing through it

☐ The valley is below a hill and the river is at its bottom

☐ Repeated flows erode the bed and carry loosened material away

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Repeated flows erode the bed and carry loosened material away

Erosion and transport can deepen a channel over time.
