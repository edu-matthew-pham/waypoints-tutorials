# Water on the move: how it joins places

**Name:** {{field-24}} **Date:** {{field-15}} **Class:** {{field-15}}

> **Key question:** A town has had no rain, but its river turns muddy. How could a change somewhere else reach it?


:::teacher-note

**In one sentence:** Trace water through a catchment, distinguish its forms, and explain what it carries between places.

**Students should be able to say:** I can distinguish surface water, soil moisture and groundwater. I can trace one pathway between two places. I can explain what water carries and why another place is affected.

**Width:** the unmarked parts are the floor every student does (predict, run, investigate and make). Typical adds the modify practice; wider adds the real-world contexts. They add contexts, not difficulty. Students work in the online tutorial alongside this sheet.

:::

## Worked example: more than a direction


:::keep-together

### What reaches the town?

1. $\text{Start}$ — Rain washes litter into a made-up school gutter.
2. $\text{Pathway}$ — Water flows along the gutter towards a creek.
3. $\text{Transfer}$ — Flow carries litter from the school to the creek.
4. $\text{Connection}$ — A change at school can affect water quality in the creek.

**In Kerrin, rain washes loose soil off the farm. Why might the town downstream care?**

☐ Every place on the river must have identical rain

☐ Soil cannot travel beyond the farm boundary

☐ The town can only be affected by its own land

☐ The river can carry soil to the town’s water intake

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** The river can carry soil to the town’s water intake

The farm and town are connected by surface water carrying soil, with a consequence for water quality.

:::


:::keep-together

## Explain what travels


:::keep-together

### An underground connection

**In a made-up case, a spill soaks into rock beside a village. Groundwater moves towards a spring at another village. Why might the second village care?**

☐ Underground water is always safe to drink

☐ Groundwater stays still because it is underground

☐ Only a shared road can connect the villages

☐ Dissolved pollution could travel to its spring

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Dissolved pollution could travel to its spring

A connection can be underground. Name the pathway and what it carries.

:::


:::keep-together

### More than downhill

**Which explanation goes beyond saying water flows downhill?**

☐ The river flows from high land to low land through the catchment

☐ There is water in the river and a lake farther downstream

☐ The river and the lake are both stores of surface water

☐ Runoff carries fertiliser from a field to a lake, affecting water quality

:::


:::teacher-note

**Answer:** Runoff carries fertiliser from a field to a lake, affecting water quality

Name the starting place, pathway, material and receiving place.
