# Evidence methods: a quick reference

| Evidence or method | Helps investigate | A limit |
|---|---|---|
| Stratigraphy | Relative order in an undisturbed sequence | Depth alone does not give an exact date |
| Ancient DNA comparison | Biological relationships and ancestry | Needs surviving usable DNA and context; does not determine cultural identity |
| Radiocarbon dating | Estimated age of suitable organic material | A sample date is not automatically an event date |
| Ancient writing | What was recorded, ordered or claimed | Damage, translation and purpose affect interpretation |
| Conservation | Keeping evidence available for future questions | Saving objects alone can lose their context |

Ask: **What is the question? What supports this claim? What remains uncertain?**
