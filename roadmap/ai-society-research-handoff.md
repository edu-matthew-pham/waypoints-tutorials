## What the course is (agreed so far)
- **Title:** *Who Does AI Affect?* About AI systems and other people; *Does AI Help Us Learn?* is about your own learning, so leave that out.
- **Place:** parallel to Does AI Help Us Learn?. Both follow AI Foundations; neither is a prerequisite for the other.
- **Level:** core ideas at Year 7, with wider steps (`tier: wider`) for older students. The society and policy material from a former Years 9–10 candidate (AI policy at school and in society) folds into its wider steps.
- **Candidate topics** (cases, not lectures): what you give away (data and privacy, a real consent screen); spotting generated media; automated decisions (who gets the job); running costs (energy, water); hidden labour (who labels the data).
- **Boundaries:** privacy basics (digital footprint, the Australian Privacy Principles) are already taught in Digital systems › Cyber safety and privacy; the new course links there instead of re-teaching. Bias as a technical idea is now in AI Foundations (`model-mistakes`, "when the missing group is people"); this course takes up fairness and who is affected.
- **Matthew's preferences:** borrow good existing resources rather than reinvent; plain words for Year 7; platform-agnostic; no phones in class; students own their working, light checks; not too much AI encouragement.

## The brief: three parts
1. **Curriculum fit.** The exact ACARA v9 Digital Technologies content descriptions (codes and wording) on ethical, social, privacy and impact considerations for Years 7–10, from the official ACARA site. Then two coverage checks: AI4K12's "Societal impact" big idea and UNESCO's AI competency framework for students (2024). Map each candidate topic to them; note gaps and topics with weak curriculum fit.
2. **Sources per topic.** For each topic, one to three sources a lesson could rest on, each with the specific claim it supports. Prefer peer-reviewed research, official bodies (for example the IEA for energy) and established investigative reporting. Note what is contested. Energy and water figures change fast and vary widely: dated estimates with ranges, never one confident number.
3. **Existing resources.** What Day of AI (MIT RAISE), Code.org's AI units, Common Sense Education and similar already offer for these topics (age level, format, quality), and each one's licence (can it be adapted, linked only, or attributed how?).
