# Rules: each with its reason, scope, source and weight

The one full list of rules for every chat and agent (set up 1 Nov 2026, Matthew said go; Project doc `claude/handoff-rules-audit.md`, step 1). `AGENTS.md` carries the short version and `CLAUDE.md` the working detail; subject roadmaps point here and list only their own rules. Add a rule here first, then a short line where it is used.

**Why this file:** rules got shortened each time they were copied and lost their reason and scope; agents then over-applied the short version (counted English write boxes became "no write boxes" everywhere; "wants are unlimited" was taught as fact). Matthew's intent is almost always a default with a reason, not a blanket rule (`roadmap/shared.md` › Proposed: rules carry their reason).

**Weights.** **Firm**: don't depart without asking Matthew (First Nations content; no real brands or living people in made-up scenarios; no AI marking; safeguarding, including privacy and contained ethical hacking; repo safety). **Default**: use judgement; if you depart, say why in your roadmap file. Everything not marked firm is a default.

**When Matthew decides something,** write its reason, scope, who and when, and its weight here in the same commit (W6).

## Register

Decided = where the decision is recorded. "None found" = no record of Matthew deciding it; kept as a default by Matthew on 1 Nov.

| T7 | **Ethical hacking, contained.** Firm: hands-on attack techniques run only on systems built for it (our pretend boards, or an isolated, school-owned lab the teacher has signed off, no internet); never against real systems or accounts students don't own or aren't authorised to test (school network, real websites, classmates' accounts); nothing that works outside the lab (no working malware, no credential-stealing pages for real services, no evading detection or keeping access). | T8 | **Match how sure we sound to how strong the evidence is.** Three levels: well established (Earth orbits the Sun, atoms, evolution, expansion of the universe) is said plainly, since hedging it would mislead; models (shells, tidal bulges, the particle model) are named as models; open questions (did time begin at the big bang?) and contested views (wants are unlimited) are said to be open or a view. That science changes with new evidence is taught head-on (Science as a Human Endeavour), not sprinkled as caveats. **Contestable or simplified ideas are labelled where students meet them** ("in economics…", "one view is…", "this model leaves out…"); keep the descriptor's terms; settled facts stay plain; a short phrase is enough, not a disclaimer on every line. Distractors are real errors, not disagreements with an assumption. Classification schemes (kingdoms, kinds of energy, rock types, text types) are taught as one useful way of sorting, firm on what every scheme agrees, never marking another valid scheme wrong (model: biology-7/kingdoms; Matthew, 1 Nov). | Honesty: ACARA tells us what to teach, not how sure to sound ("wants are unlimited" was taught as fact). | Every subject; science models included. | Matthew 30 Oct (`fab91a8`, widened `a362055`) | Default (judgement) |
| T8a | **Curriculum terms:** plain words first, then name the curriculum's term once where students meet it ("this is called a **rhetorical question**"), then mostly use the term, with the plain words as an occasional reminder.
