# ICT skills (`content/ict/`)

Started 25 Oct 2026 (Matthew asked). Plan and decisions: the Project doc `claude/digital-literacy-pathway-plan.md`; handoff: `claude/handoff-digital-literacy-pilot.md`.

## What it is
General ICT skills for students and for teachers of any subject, as its own subject (not Digital Technologies, so tool training is not taught as DT). Mapped loosely to the ACARA Digital Literacy general capability, which is not in Kit's node data (checked 25 Oct): at pathway level only, showing "main focus" and "touches", no per-lesson tags and no node derivation for now. Overlap with Digital Technologies nodes is named in text (for example AC9TDI6P07, AC9TDI8P11, AC9TDI8P01 and AC9TDI8P02, AC9TDI6P10 and AC9TDI8P14).

## Decisions (Matthew, 25 Oct 2026)
- Name: ICT skills.
- Concept first, tool second; core functions only, nothing that changes year to year (no pivot tables, macros, Copilot buttons); tool-agnostic with "in Excel / in Sheets" notes.
- Borrow or link existing resources (Microsoft Learn, Google Applied Digital Skills, typing tools) rather than rebuilding; touch typing is a link-out only.
- Pathways, in order: Working with data (spreadsheets, Years 6 to 8), documents and presentations, files and systems, investigating and judging information (workshop-style short lessons), staying safe and well (workshop-style).
- Pilot first: one lesson, tried with a colleague. If it works, continue with lessons 2 onward and the other pathways, proposing each pathway's plan for approval before building. If the widget or navigation does not work, stop and propose changes.

## Built (25 Oct 2026)
- `widget: sheet` (`playground-board/widgets/sheet.js`; AUTHORING › board widgets; `node tools/check-sheet.mjs` tests the formula engine and the copy, sort, filter and chart logic). With `table:` and `tools:` it also does Copy, Paste and Fill down (references shift like a real app's), Sort, Filter and a Chart.
- `content/ict/` (subject page, order 7) with the course `ict/data` (Working with data, Years 6 to 8), nine lessons, none with clips (no `app:`), so nothing to record:
  - Cells and formulas: **A formula keeps up** (cells have names, a formula uses them and keeps up, a typed answer does not), **Copy a formula** (a copy moves its names; fill down), **A cell that stays put** (`$` fixes a shared cell, such as g).
  - Summing up: **Add up a column** (SUM, a range is a colon), **Typical, smallest and biggest** (AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, ROUND, a typed 0 against an empty cell), **Count and decide** (IF, COUNTIF, quotes round words, `>=` against `>`).
  - Show the data: **Put the rows in order** (sort moves whole rows, filter hides), **Choose a chart** (column, line, scatter, pie; titles and axis titles).
  - Challenge **Report on an investigation** (beans in dark, shade and sun: summarise with formulas, chart the averages, pick a conclusion that matches the evidence).
  - Every lesson has the same shape: Predict, Try, Worked example (one planted mistake), Vary, Practise, Make and a `tier: typical` step in the school's own app. Data comes from Year 7 science (bean growth, weight and g, rainfall, animals, cooling water).
- Misconceptions: `typed-answer-stale`, `row-before-column`, `same-formula-every-row`, `fixed-cell-moves`, `comma-not-colon`, `zero-for-missing`, `words-need-quotes`, `sort-one-column`, `pie-for-change`.
- Tested 25 Oct: validate, build, `check-sheet.mjs`, every board check driven in a headless browser (each ticks with its intended actions), no sideways scroll at 390 px, `check-decks ict/`, `check-blockers` on the ICT pages.

## Built (26 Oct 2026, overnight, Matthew approved building this pathway)
- `widget: page` (`playground-board/widgets/page.js`; AUTHORING › `widget: page`): a small document or slide deck. Styles and a headings panel, by-hand formatting against styles, lists and tables with a header row, page layout, slides (one idea each, breaks, titles), pictures drawn by the widget (no photos), alt text and credit lines, slide colours with a measured contrast ratio, and text that runs off a slide.
- `content/ict/documents/` (second pathway in `content/ict/index.md`), course `ict/documents` (Documents and slides, Years 6 to 8), nine lessons in five units, no clips: Structure (**Headings say what a part is**, **Change the style, not every line**, **Lists and tables**), Room to read (**Room to read**), Slides (**One idea per slide**, **Pictures that help**, **Readable from the back**), Whose is it? (**Whose is it?**: who, where and the licence, with pretend source cards and `.example` addresses), then the challenge **Report the investigation** (a one-page report, then three slides, on the bean results from Working with data). Same lesson shape as Working with data; each ends with a `tier: typical` step in the school's own app.
- Misconceptions: `fake-heading`, `restyle-by-hand`, `order-needs-numbers`, `list-for-table`, `cram-the-page`, `slide-is-a-page`, `decoration-picture`, `low-contrast`, `no-credit-needed-online`.
- Tested 26 Oct: validate, build, `check-sheet.mjs`, all 34 board checks driven in a headless browser (each ticks with its intended actions and none is true at the start), no sideways scroll at 390 px (pages and boards), `check-decks ict/documents`, `check-blockers` (also `--wide`).
- Known limits: the checks are rough counts (headings, steps, slides, words, contrast, alt text of three or more words, a credit with three parts); real apps check none of these, and the lessons and teacher notes say so. Contrast is the standard WCAG ratio; the 24-point rule is a rule of thumb. The credit lesson is general practice, not legal advice, and tells teachers to fit it to the school's own rules. Slides on a phone are small (the model scales to the width).

## Decisions (Matthew, 26 Oct 2026): lessons and cards
- ICT skills is its own subject with its own entry point: light courses plus cards. Other subjects may link in optionally (a lesson or card that says "needs this ICT skill"); maths, science and code lessons are left alone for now.
- A skill has a **lesson** (counts toward progress) and a **card** (reference: no ticks, one screen: the idea, steps that work in any app, a note for each common app such as Word, Excel, Docs, Sheets, a "watch for" line, and a link to the lesson). Both exist; search finds both.
- Each ICT course has a Reference page listing its cards (`<course>/reference/`, the Reference chip on the course page). Cards live in `content/ict/<course>/cards.yml` (`tools/ict-cards.mjs`, AUTHORING › ICT cards). Core functions only; app notes name menus that rarely change and always say other apps work the same.
- Files and systems is cut to about four habit lessons (approved by Matthew 26 Oct 2026, built the same day: see Built, files) (names and folders, copy/move/delete, versions and backup, fix it first) with no new widget for now; they link to the concept lessons in How computers work.

## Built (26 Oct 2026): Staying safe and well
- `content/ict/safe/` (fifth pathway in `content/ict/index.md`, `years: "5-8"`), course `ict/safe`, approved by Matthew 26 Oct 2026 from the Project doc `claude/ict-safe-and-well-plan.md`. Eight `shape: 5e` workshops in five units, no clips (no `app:`), all on made-up stories with invented names and no real apps or people: **A day's trail** (three questions: can I take it back, does it say where I am, is it about someone else), **Who can see this?** (the audience you chose and the one you might get), **Pause before you post** (no face and no voice; the pause; what a bystander can do), **Who is on the other end?** (friendly is not known; five requests that mean check with a trusted adult first), **Screens and me** (notice how it feels, then choose a habit; no targets or hours), **Pressure and tricks** (hurry, a prize you didn't enter, everyone has it; stop, check, ask), **When something goes wrong** (stop, keep it, tell a trusted adult, block and report, look after yourself; a Mermaid `explainDiagram` and step diagram, `diagrams.mmd#plan` inside lesson.md), then **Our class agreement**.
- Lesson 8 is a short teacher-led lesson rather than a class-plan-only session: it keeps the course order and the course's end visible, gets a guide and deck like the others, and gives students a made-up example (Room 12) to come back to; the class writes its agreement in the room, and the site stores nothing it writes.
- Checks are predict, sort, match, question, bug (each lesson's planted mistake in `working.txt`) and reflect. Each lesson has a `tier: typical` Elaborate step for Years 7 and 8 (Years 5 and 6 get `?width=minimum`, said in the course `teacher.md` and every teacher.yml). Three typical steps reuse boards: `cyber` footprint `set: settings` (who-can-see-this), `cyber` inbox `set: easy` (pressure-and-tricks), `feed` `mode: design` (screens-and-me). No new widget.
- Seven cards in `content/ict/safe/cards.yml`; app notes name kinds of place (group chats, games, any app), not real apps. Misconceptions (own block at the end of `misconceptions.json`): `delete-takes-it-back`, `private-means-private`, `joke-is-fine-if-meant`, `friendly-means-trusted`, `rush-means-real`, `tell-means-trouble`. Class plans for all eight. `data/curriculum/ict-digital-literacy.yml`: `safe` set to built.
- Safeguards (plan › Required safeguards): every teacher.yml starts with a **⚑ Wellbeing review** note and an **If a student discloses something** note (follow the school's child-protection procedure, don't probe, never promise secrecy), plus ground rules (never ask about students' own experiences, apps or hours; private thinks are not written or collected). The course `teacher.md` (course guide) and the course summary say the wellbeing team should read it before use; so does `content/ict-teachers.md` and the pathway's `about:`. No laws or age rules are stated anywhere; students and teachers are pointed to the eSafety Commissioner. Links checked 26 Oct 2026 with WebFetch: esafety.gov.au, esafety.gov.au/kids, esafety.gov.au/report and kidshelpline.com.au (1800 55 1800, ages 5 to 25). eSafety's young-people and educators pages could not be fetched (robots), so they are named without a URL. Lessons 3, 4 and 7 carry extra review notes (bullying policy; grooming signs taught without naming; the one line on not saving or forwarding an image that shouldn't be shared).
- Links rather than repeats: Your digital footprint and Spot the phish (How computers work › Cyber safety and privacy), Designed to keep you and A fake about someone (AI in your life).
- Tested 26 Oct: validate (no errors or warnings for the course), build (`WP_NO_MAKECODE=1`, diagrams drawn with `WP_ONLY=diagrams render-pictures`), headless Chromium on the course page, reference cards, subject and pathway pages, ICT teachers page, all eight lessons and their teacher guides at 1100 and 390 px (no page errors, no sideways scroll, both diagram SVGs load). Every static check (predict, sort, match, question, bug, reflect: 51 in all) answered as a student would: each sort, match, question and bug ticks only with the intended answer and not after a wrong one. The three board checks were driven by clicking the board's buttons: none is true at the start, each ticks with its intended actions, and no board scrolls sideways at 390 px. `check-decks ict/safe` (all slides fit), `check-blockers` on 13 pages (clean). The test found bug-line lists pushing pages sideways at 390 px (long lines of prose); fixed in `tools/lesson-view.mjs` (roadmap/shared.md). Not clicked through: the worksheets and decks in Prepare itself, and the lessons in a real classroom.
- Known limits: not read by a wellbeing team and not tried with a class. The Typical (Years 7 and 8) scenarios are graded by situation, not checked against any age-banded framework. The board steps borrow the Coding Club app (Ava, Sam), so older students may meet the same boards in How computers work. Kids Helpline's number and eSafety addresses should be re-checked each term. The Digital Literacy levels for Years 5 to 8 are still unverified, so no level is claimed.

## Built (26 Oct 2026): Investigating and judging information
- `content/ict/information/` (fourth pathway in `content/ict/index.md`, between Files and Staying safe; `years: "5-8"`), course `ict/information`, approved by Matthew 26 Oct 2026 from the Project doc `claude/ict-investigating-information-plan.md`. Eight `shape: 5e` workshops and a challenge in six units, no clips (no `app:`): **Search like you mean it** (strong words; read the results page for Ad, date and address before clicking), **Who made it, and why?** (who, why: inform, sell, persuade, entertain; when), **Two pages, one question** (compare checkable clues, not looks; a third source), **Check the claim** (read sideways; copies are not checks; nothing found means unchecked, not false), **Fact, opinion or advert?** (could I check it?), **Numbers and charts that mislead** (axis, whole, how many; links to Working with data › Choose a chart), **Pictures out of place** (caption, date, crop; a picture is a source; links to AI in your life › Can you spot a fake?), **What did the AI say?** (an AI answer is a lead; asking the same AI is not checking; links to AI Foundations › Check what AI tells you), then the challenge **Answer it from three sources** (rate, agree, hedge, credit; links to Documents and slides › Whose is it?).
- Everything is made up: a town called Riverbend, `.example` addresses, invented makers, drawn pictures (no photos, no real people), and scripted AI replies labelled "not a real AI". No real site, outlet or person is judged, and no AI runs on the page. Practice claims are small and checkable (library hours, a school netball league, beans, a skate park). Every teacher.yml has a "Contested topics" paragraph on steering back to method (who made it, why, how would you check?) without settling the topic.
- Workshop shape: short student text, a sort or a rating, a planted mistake (`working.txt`, bug check), then the debate in the class plan (`inquiry`, a discussion activity and a class question for each). Sort checks use clear cases only; debatable cases (a review site that earns from sales, a page with two purposes) are in class plans, and `reasoning` checks (unmarked, two model answers, "a different view that also works") carry the written judgements. Width: floor plus Practise for Years 5 and 6; `tier: typical` and `tier: wider` steps add contexts for Years 7 and 8 (said in each teacher.yml). `smaller:` lead-ins on four planted mistakes and the axis board.
- Widgets: reused `sheet` (lesson 6) and `page` (lesson 7, drawn pictures and the credit box). `source.js` was read and not used: it is a page-source leak finder for the CTF, not a source-judging tool. One small addition to `sheet.js`: `tools: chart axis` adds a "Vertical axis starts at" box (`chart ymin:` presets it; `ch_ymin`, `ch_zero` for checks), because a cut-off axis could not be drawn before (columns always started at zero). Default behaviour is unchanged; `tools/check-sheet.mjs` tests the scale (five new cases); AUTHORING › `widget: sheet` documents it.
- Nine cards in `content/ict/information/cards.yml`. Misconceptions (own block in `misconceptions.json`): `top-result-is-best`, `pages-just-inform`, `looks-mean-trust`, `many-copies-agree`, `sure-sounding-is-fact`, `shape-not-numbers`, `picture-is-proof`, `ai-checks-itself`, `one-source-is-enough`. `data/curriculum/ict-digital-literacy.yml`: `investigating` set to built. ICT teachers page, its search entry and the subject page updated.
- Fixes found by testing (shared, small): parsons pieces in science-language lessons now wrap (`pre-wrap`, `min-width:0` in `tools/lesson-view.mjs`), since long prose steps pushed the page 254 px sideways at 390 px; sheet fields fit a phone (inline in `sheet.js`, since the board's `style.css` is in every board clip's hash), which also fixes an 8 px sideways scroll on Working with data › Choose a chart.
- Tested 26 Oct: validate (no errors or warnings for the course), `check-sheet.mjs`, build (`WP_NO_MAKECODE=1`), headless Chromium on 24 pages (subject, course, pathway, reference, ICT teachers, course guide, nine lessons with `?width=wider`, nine teacher guides) at 1100 and 390 px: no page errors, no sideways scroll. All 80 static checks answered as a student would: none done at the start, each sort, match, question, bug and parsons check stays unticked after a wrong answer (questions show feedback) and ticks with the intended one; predict, reflect and reasoning tick when used. The three board checks (two axis boards, one credit board) are false at the start and tick with their intended actions at both widths, with no sideways scroll inside the board. `check-decks ict/information` (all slides fit), `check-blockers` on 15 pages (clean). Not clicked through: decks and worksheets in Prepare itself; the course in a classroom.
- Known limits: not tried with a class. Source cards are Markdown blockquotes (plain, like the AI courses), not a widget; a `sources` board with About pages and links out was not needed for these workshops, but could make lesson 4 more hands-on. The made-up gardening figures in the challenge are loose on purpose and the teacher notes say so. Reverse image search is named (Lens, Bing Visual Search, TinEye) but not used. The Digital Literacy levels for Years 5 to 8 are still unverified, so no level is claimed.
- Publish: a new course, the subject page, `sheet.js` and `lesson-view.mjs` need the full publish; no clips re-record (no clip uses the sheet widget, and lesson-view is outside clip hashes) (or quick publish for pages; the widget and CSS changes ride on a whole-site run).
